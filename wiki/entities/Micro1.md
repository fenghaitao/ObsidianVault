## Definition

Micro1 is a talent-sourcing platform that transformed technical hiring by conducting 3,000+ AI-powered interviews daily using Claude.

## Key Information

- Reduced recruitment costs by 85% compared to traditional methods.
- Achieved 5x higher pass rates for AI-screened candidates reaching human interviews.
- Its platform is used by [[LegalSoft|Legal Soft]] to streamline interviewing operations.
