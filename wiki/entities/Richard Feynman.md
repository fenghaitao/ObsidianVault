## Definition

Richard Feynman is a physicist who, with Yuri Manin, proposed quantum computation in the 1980s.

## Key Information

- In the 1980s Feynman (and Manin, among others) suggested letting algorithms use quantum-mechanical operations.
- The original motivation was simulating quantum systems directly rather than building larger apparatus for simulations such as turbulence.
