## Definition

"More or Less" is a podcast co-hosted by Sam Lessin, Dave Morin, Brett Morin, and Jessica Lessin. The name is a play on "Lessin" and "Morin."

## Key Information

- The name is clever: a play on "Lessin" (Sam and Jessica) and "Morin" (Dave and Brett)
- Sam describes the dynamic: Sam and Jessica are "contrarians and hate everything by default," while the Morins "think everything is amazing"
- Jessica Lessin (who runs The Information) prompts the group with what's actually going on in the world
- The podcast is deliberately unfiltered — "kind of just like talking to your friends for an hour a week"
- They would do it even if no one was listening; it's primarily for their own enjoyment
- Despite having no growth strategies and not being paid for it, Sam says it's "weirdly useful" — listened to by people they care about in the industry
- Sometimes leads to serendipitous business connections when entrepreneurs hear them complain about something and offer better ideas
