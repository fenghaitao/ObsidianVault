## Definition

Stripe is a technology company that builds economic infrastructure for the internet, providing payment processing APIs and tools for online businesses. It integrates with Claude as a remote service connector, enabling payment-related workflows within Claude-powered applications.

## Key Information

- Listed as a remote service connector for [[Claude]], enabling one-click payment processing integration via OAuth.
- Featured in Anthropic's tool directory launch (July 2025) as a connector partner, alongside [[Notion]] and [[Canva]].
- Additional Stripe integration was noted as in development during the May 2025 [[Integrations]] launch.
- Integrates with Claude via the [[ModelContextProtocol|Model Context Protocol (MCP)]] as a remote connector, exposing payment data and actions to Claude.
- One of the notable companies launched by [[YCombinator]], founded by Patrick and John Collison.
- Provides the payment infrastructure behind thousands of internet businesses, processing hundreds of billions of dollars annually.
