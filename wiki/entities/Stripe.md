## Definition

Stripe is a financial technology company providing payment processing infrastructure. Mentioned as the company where David Singleton became CTO after serving as Site Director of Google London.

## Key Information

- David Singleton, former Site Director of Google London, became Stripe's CTO
- Singleton's advice to Philip Su about landing teams came from his Google experience, before his time at Stripe

### David Singleton's Stripe Tenure
- Mission: "increase the GDP of the internet" — help entrepreneurs start and scale companies, create jobs, and move money
- Singleton joined after a recruiting conversation that began as "asking for advice" with CEO Patrick Collison; he cared about the leadership-team seat more than the title
- Operating principles (e.g. "users first," "meticulous in your craft") are concrete, distilled from the most effective Stripes' behaviors, and periodically revisited
- Reliability: the 2020 pandemic demand spike prompted an ops review modeled on Amazon's, plus testing and gradual deployment that let them move both fast and safely; they passed five nines (later six nines)
- Hiring replaced whiteboard interviews with laptop pair-programming exercises designed to select meticulous, inquisitive engineers; leaked questions had to be constantly refreshed
- Singleton continued "engineeration" quarterly and used friction logs to steer developer-productivity investment
- Polished error messages (e.g. "not found, but an object with that ID exists in the other mode") are remembered by customers and exemplify "meticulous in your craft"

### Laurent's Stripe Tenure ([[summary-20260119 - Airbnb Staff Eng： Untold Rules of Calibrations and How To Not Get Stuck at Senior]])
- Joined as tech lead of build/test/tooling, scaling the organization from ~35 to ~140 engineers.
- Had a strong data/metrics culture; Laurent reframed measuring developer output from lines-of-code/PR count to the "journey of a change" (time and phases from branch to merge).
- Influenced by asking questions rather than dictating, embedded in teams to build credibility, and scaled further through office hours.
