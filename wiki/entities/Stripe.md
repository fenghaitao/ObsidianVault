## Definition

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform that processes payments and provides billing, checkout, and other financial services. It processed just over $1.4 trillion in a recent year, representing about 1.3% of global GDP.

## Key Information

- Patrick Collison is committed to retaining top talent
- Helped Lenny Rachitsky combat fraud rings during the Product Pass launch — worked with Lenny and Substack to shut down exploits
- The fraud rings (mostly in China) found vulnerabilities in the API that Lenny's team built to distribute free subscriptions
- Jeff Weinstein is a PM at Stripe who is cited as the epitome of "thinking like a CPO" — constantly thinking about Stripe as a business, not just his product area
