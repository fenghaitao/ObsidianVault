## Definition

History of Burning is a historical fiction novel that tells the story of Indian indentured servants brought to Uganda to build the British railroad. Jessica Fain recommends it as a "window" into an unfamiliar part of history.

## Key Information

- Historical fiction about Indian indentured servants brought to Uganda to build the British railroad
- Recommended by Jessica Fain in the lightning round as an example of historical fiction that provides a window into a less familiar history
- Part of Jessica's favorite genre: multigenerational historical fiction
