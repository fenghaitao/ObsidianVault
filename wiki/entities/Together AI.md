## Definition
Together AI is building the AI-native cloud — providing reliable compute for model training and inference at scale. It serves over a million developers and works closely with hundreds of companies, including Cursor and Deck.

## Key Information
- AI-native cloud platform for training and inference
- 1M+ developers on the platform
- Works with hundreds of companies including [[Cursor]]
- Acquired [[Refuel]] in 2025
- Has a dedicated voice AI team led by [[Rishabh Bhargava]]
- Provides inference infrastructure for speech-to-text models (P90 ~100ms transcription latency)
- Emphasis on co-location of models for reduced network latency
