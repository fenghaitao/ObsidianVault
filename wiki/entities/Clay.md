## Definition
Clay is a CRM (or "friend relationship management" system, as Šimon Podhajský describes it) used as one of the data sources in the Fulan read-only AI system for cross-source queries about who in your network might be interested in discussing your recent reading.

## Key Information
- Used as a contact/relationship management data source in Fulan
- Referred to as both a CRM and an FRM (friend relationship management) system
- Integrated via MCP (Model Context Protocol) for querying contact profiles and interests
- Queries can be slow — "the Clay MCP takes forever to run"
- Stores profile data including people's interests (AI, European tech, education) used for matching with reading topics
