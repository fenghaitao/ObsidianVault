## Definition

Clay is an AI-powered sales and data enrichment platform. Jason Lemkin notes it starts at around $100K/year, making it a significant investment compared to the $50-80K entry point for most AI GTM agents.

## Key Information

- Pricing starts at approximately $100K/year — higher than the typical $50-80K entry point for AI GTM agents
- Represents the upper end of the AI GTM agent pricing spectrum
- The high price point creates a barrier: junior salespeople won't have access to $100K budget to experiment with it
- The advice to "pick a tool and deploy it yourself" is implicitly aimed at VPs and leaders who control budget, not individual contributors
- There are "no $99/month products" in AI GTM that auto-train well yet — though this is expected to change
