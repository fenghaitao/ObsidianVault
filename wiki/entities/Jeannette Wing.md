## Definition

Jeannette Wing is the computer scientist who co-authored with Barbara Liskov the paper on behavioral subtyping, the formal definition behind the Liskov substitution principle.

## Key Information

- Co-authored the behavioral subtyping paper with Liskov, giving the formal definition of the Liskov substitution principle.
