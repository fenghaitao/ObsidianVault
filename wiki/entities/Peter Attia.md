## Definition

Peter Attia is a physician and author of the book "Outlive," which focuses on personalized medicine and optimizing healthspan.

## Key Information

- Tomer Cohen recommended "Outlive" as one of his top book recommendations.
- The book discusses "Medicine 3.0" — the notion of personalized medicine, which Cohen believes will become incredible in the world of AI.
- The book covers fitness, diet, and the biggest health factors to optimize for longevity.
