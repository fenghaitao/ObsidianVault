## Definition

Learn Prompting is a prompt-engineering education company founded by [[Sander Schulhoff]], home of the first public guide to prompt engineering on the internet.

## Key Information

- Schulhoff wrote the guide shortly before ChatGPT's public release; it has since been used to teach prompt engineering to millions of people.
- Co-teaches (with [[HackAPrompt]]) an AI security/red-teaming course on Maven, hosted at hackai.co, aimed largely at people with little to no AI background.
