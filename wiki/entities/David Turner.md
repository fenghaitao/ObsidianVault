## Definition
David Turner is the computer scientist who described translating lambda calculus programs into SK combinators.

## Key Information
- Wrote a paper describing how to translate lambda calculus programs into SK combinators — a translation expressible "in half a dozen lines."
- His S-K reduction scheme became the conceptual basis for the SKI/SKIM hardware machine.
