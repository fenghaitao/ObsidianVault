## Definition

Media/community property founded by [[Nikhyl Singhal]] for heads of product and CPOs, named after his career philosophy of optimizing for the job after next (the "skip" opportunity) rather than the immediate next move. Includes a curated community (Skip Community, ~125 heads of product), a podcast/newsletter (Skip Show / theskip.substack.com), a broader-audience mailing list (Skip Coach), and a forthcoming AI Q&A product trained on ~50 community leaders (Skip Help, built with Superme).

## Key Information

- The core Skip Community started ~5 years before this episode with ~20-30 members and one founder among them; has grown to ~125 members, 14 of whom have since become founders themselves — used by Singhal as evidence that product leaders increasingly move into founder/CEO/C-level roles outside traditional product management.
- Monthly in-person meetups (San Francisco) feature member show-and-tell of personally-built AI tools, which Singhal cites as evidence of the "builder joy" driving the industry's current renaissance. See [[Builders Vs Information Movers]].
- Skip Help (launching around this episode) lets users ask career/product questions answered by AI agents trained on the collective wisdom of ~50 Skip community leaders, not just Singhal himself.
