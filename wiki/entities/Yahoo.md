## Definition

Yahoo is the company that acquired James Everingham's startup Luminate; he committed to one year there before joining Instagram.

## Key Information

- Acquired Luminate (an "Instagram shopping" product for publisher sites).
- James committed to only one year at Yahoo; when that year was up, Elliot Shrag (a former board member) reached out to bring him to Instagram.
