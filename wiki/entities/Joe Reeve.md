## Definition
Joe Reeve works in the growth organization at ElevenLabs. He built the viral "statue app" that lets users take a picture of a statue and talk to it via AI, demonstrating the power of vibe coding by stitching together ElevenLabs and OpenAI APIs in two hours.

## Key Information
- Works in the growth organization at ElevenLabs, an audio AI foundation model company
- Built the statue app in 2 hours on a Sunday using Cursor, stitching together OpenAI deep research and ElevenLabs Voice Design API
- The app went viral: 50K impressions on day 1, 1.5M on day 2
- His viral post emphasized that vibe coding enables surprisingly big outcomes from rapid prototyping
- Had a CEO find his WhatsApp number and call him asking how he built what a team of 10 had been working on for a year
- The statue app's success led to the creation of "Eleven Hacks" at ElevenLabs
- Previously built a Fruit Ninja clone on Facebook Instant Games API that got 15 million users in Vietnam
- Learned video creation through political campaigning work before joining ElevenLabs
- Edits videos on mobile using CapCut; the statue video took ~25 minutes to edit
- Advocates for music, captions, and hooks in the first 6-12 seconds for viral videos
- Commissioning a physical statue with embedded technology (speaker, phone, microphone) for direct interaction without screens
- Built a red phone booth experience where users talk to an agent voiced as Sir Michael Caine
