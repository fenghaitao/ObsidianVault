## Definition
Calum Beer is the Chief AI Officer at the UK Department for Science and Technology (DST), responsible for systemic changes to make government more effective at building and nurturing high-performing technical teams. Described by Eoin Mulgrew as doing "great work" to turn the government oil tanker.

## Key Information
- Chief AI Officer at the UK Department for Science and Technology
- Works on systemic change to improve government's ability to attract and retain technical talent
- Described as turning the "oil tanker" of government — addressing the systemic challenges beyond any one team's remit
- Closed out the aiDotEngineer conference where this talk was given
