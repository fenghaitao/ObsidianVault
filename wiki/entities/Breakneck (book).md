## Definition

"Breakneck" is a book by Dan Wang about US-China relations, published in the last year. It uses the analogy of the US as a "lawyerly society" and China as an "engineering society."

## Key Information

- Written by Dan Wang
- Published in the last year
- Central analogy: the US is a "lawyerly society" while China is an "engineering society," each with pros and cons
- Sherwin Wu: "does seem like we're run by lawyers in the US"
