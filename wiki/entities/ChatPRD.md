## Definition

ChatPRD is an AI product management company founded by Claire Vo. It provides AI tools for product managers, with a significant enterprise customer base. Claire runs it mostly as a solopreneur.

## Key Information

- Founded by Claire Vo, a three-time Chief Product Officer
- AI product management tool
- Significant enterprise customer base — many inbound leads from larger organizations
- Product-led growth (PLG) that converts into enterprise opportunities
- Claire uses an OpenClaw agent named Sam to handle sales: daily CRM sweeps, identifying decision-makers, sending soft outreach emails
- Sam handles international deals end-to-end, while Claire personally takes San Francisco-based high-growth tech startups
- Claire previously paid someone 10 hours/week for sales work that Sam now handles
- Website: chatprd.ai
