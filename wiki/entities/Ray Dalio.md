## Definition

Ray Dalio is the founder of Bridgewater Associates and author, cited by Stewart Butterfield for his story about Michael Jordan learning to ski and viewing every mistake as a "gem" to collect and improve from.

## Key Information

- In his book, tells the story of Michael Jordan learning to ski: every time he messed up, he wanted the instructor to tell him exactly what he was doing wrong because each mistake was a "gem" he could collect to become a better skier
- Butterfield uses this as an analogy for building a culture where direct criticism is motivational and people are grateful for feedback
