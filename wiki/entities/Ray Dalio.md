## Definition

Ray Dalio is the founder of Bridgewater Associates, cited by [[Stewart Butterfield]] for an anecdote (from Dalio's book) about Michael Jordan wanting his ski instructor to tell him exactly what he was doing wrong, treating each correction as a valuable "gem" toward becoming a better skier.

## Key Information

- Butterfield uses the anecdote to argue that direct, critical feedback is genuinely motivational when there's enough trust in an organization — comparable to Toyota-style continuous-improvement culture (Kaizen, Six Sigma).
