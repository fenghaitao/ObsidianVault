## Definition
Richie Mini is a small robot acquired and powered by Prince Canuma using MLX Audio and MLX VLM for on-device perception (camera, audio) and voice interaction with real-time voice cloning of the Jarvis (Iron Man) voice.

## Key Information
- Acquired by Prince Canuma in 2025
- Powered entirely by on-device MLX: MLX Audio for speech/audio, MLX VLM for vision
- Uses Marvis TTS for real-time voice responses with voice cloning
- Voice cloning demo: responds in Jarvis (Iron Man) voice
- Demonstrates that agents can "hear, see, and sound just like you or your loved ones" running on-device
- Runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac, or embedded in a robot
- Represents the convergence of on-device vision, audio, and robotics
