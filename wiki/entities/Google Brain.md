## Definition
Google Brain was Google's deep-learning research team, which merged with DeepMind in 2023 to form Google DeepMind; Vlad Feinberg did pure research there early in his career.
## Key Information
- Vlad was "super focused" on maximizing first-author papers at NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR while at Google Brain.
- His manager Rohan Aneja pushed him and the team to get involved in launching the first Bard instead of just writing papers.
- Vlad's small SFT contribution to Bard, done in part by hyperparameter tuning and "golfing the XLA compiler" to fit old TPUs, earned a spot bonus from Jeff Dean.
- That engagement blossomed into more LLM-side investment and led to his current Google DeepMind role.
