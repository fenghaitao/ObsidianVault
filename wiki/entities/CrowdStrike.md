## Definition

CrowdStrike is a cybersecurity company whose platform is trusted by more than 60% of the Fortune 500. It is a [[ClaudeSecurity|Claude Security]] technology partner, building the Frontier AI Readiness and Resilience Service on [[Claude4.7Opus|Claude Opus]].

## Key Information

- **Frontier AI Readiness and Resilience Service**: pairs Claude Opus with CrowdStrike's AI Red Team Services and proprietary agent frameworks to continuously hunt for latent zero-days in customer applications, validate findings, and accelerate remediation before new code reaches production.
- **Technology partner (April 2026)**: named among the technology partners embedding Claude Opus into their own security tools as part of Claude Security's public beta launch.
- Quote: *"Frontier models like Anthropic's Claude Opus are giving defenders a capability advantage that didn't exist a year ago, pushing vulnerability management all the way to the left."* — Mark Manglicmot, Global VP of Consulting Services, CrowdStrike.
