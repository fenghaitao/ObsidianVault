## Definition
Ergo Labs is a company co-founded by Liad Yosef focused on human agentic interfaces. It is the company behind the co-creation of MCP Apps, the first official MCP extension for UI.

## Key Information
- Co-founded by Liad Yosef, co-creator of MCP Apps
- Focused on human agentic interfaces — the intersection of human interaction and AI agents
- Liad Yosef presented from Ergo Labs at aiDotEngineer 2026
- Part of the team that partnered with Anthropic and OpenAI to standardize MCP Apps
