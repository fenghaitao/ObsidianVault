## Definition

Elon Frank is a product leader who was Jessica Fain's boss at Slack. He is now CPO of Checker. He was known for always having a customer anecdote in his back pocket.

## Key Information

- Was Jessica Fain's boss at Slack
- Now CPO of Checker
- Always carried a customer anecdote — "I was just on site last week with so-and-so and they said..." — bringing his domain expertise to bear
- Many executives at Slack were not from a strong enterprise background, so Elon brought that expertise to the forefront
- Example of a product leader bringing domain expertise to executive conversations
