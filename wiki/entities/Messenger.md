## Definition

Facebook Messenger is Meta's messaging platform with a mature ecosystem for cross-platform video calling. Rahul Pandey's major IC6 project at Meta involved migrating Portal's video calling infrastructure back to Messenger's shared RTC (Real-Time Communication) infrastructure.

## Key Information

- Already had a mature ecosystem and infrastructure for cross-platform video calls between desktop, mobile (Android, iOS) with different codecs and qualities
- The Portal team initially forked off from Messenger to do their own thing as an experimental project
- Once Portal launched with hundreds of thousands of daily users, there was a need to reintegrate with Messenger's infrastructure
- The RTC (Real-Time Communication) team at Messenger was constantly making improvements that Portal needed to stay current with
- Rahul's main project was migrating Portal's core infrastructure back to Messenger's shared infrastructure while maintaining compatibility with Portal's custom features
