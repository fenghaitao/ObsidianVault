## Definition

Eros Resmini is the former CMO of Discord who was a close advisor to Amol Avaisary when Amol was a founder. His advice — "sometimes in life you just have to go for it" — became a life motto for Amol.

## Key Information

- Former CMO of Discord
- Was a very close advisor to Amol Avaisary when Amol was a founder
- Would push Amol with the phrase: "just go for it"
- Amol adopted this as a life motto: "Sometimes in life you just have to go for it"
