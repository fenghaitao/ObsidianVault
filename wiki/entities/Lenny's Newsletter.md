## Definition

Lenny's Newsletter is a popular publication by Lenny Rachitsky about product management, growth, and technology. Annual subscribers receive a "Product Pass" with free access to numerous products.

## Key Information

- Website: lennysnewsletter.com
- Annual subscribers get a year free of 19 products via "Product Pass": Devin, Lovable, Replit, Bolt, Linear, Superhuman, Descript, Whisper Flow, Gamma, Perplexity, Warp, Granola, Magic Patterns, Drag Cast, Chapard, D Mobin, Post Hog, and Stripe Atlas
- Dr. Becky is "an avid listener" of Lenny's content
