## Definition

Founder associated with [[TBH]], cited by [[Mark Pincus]] as an example of spotting a "proven" feature buried and underused inside someone else's product (an Arabic-only version of an existing app), then building a standalone hit around it.

## Key Information

- Pincus: "Nikita saw his perfect product buried inside an Arabic-only version [of his product], and he said, 'Oh my god, they nailed it.'" — parallel to Pincus's own story of finding offline browsing buried in Netscape/Internet Explorer to build [[Freeloader]].
- Previously a guest on Lenny's Podcast (referenced by the host during this episode).
