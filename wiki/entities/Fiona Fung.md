## Definition

Fiona Fung is an engineering leader who grew to senior director by rising through the ranks of Microsoft and Meta, and now supports the Claude Code team at Anthropic.

## Key Information

- First job out of college used Visual Studio to build Visual Studio, where she learned dogfooding
- Spent 11.5 years at Microsoft, working on the Visual Studio editor and debugger and, finally, JavaScript and TypeScript; she stayed to help ship TypeScript 1.0
- Became a manager of managers at Microsoft, supporting the manager of the TypeScript team (whom she calls one of the best compiler engineers she has worked with)
- Left Microsoft in 2015 to join Facebook, recruited by a former Visual Studio coworker who became her first Facebook manager, to build commerce (which became Facebook Marketplace)
- At Meta she later worked on RL/VR products — shipping Quest 2 during COVID, incubating Ray-Ban Stories (later Ray-Ban Meta), and working on Horizon OS — and on Instagram
- Deeply practiced dogfooding her teams' products as a leader, calling the hands-on debugging her "maker time"
- Her badge-post sign-off: "in a world where you can be anything, be kind"
- Joined Anthropic after using an internal Sonnet-based tool and being drawn to its mission-driven culture ("build AI that benefits humanity with safety first")
- At Anthropic she supports the Claude Code team, describing its idea-build-launch-feedback loop as even faster than her Facebook Marketplace days
