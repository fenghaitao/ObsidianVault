## Definition

Fiona Fung is an engineering leader at [[Anthropic]] who leads the teams behind [[Claude Code]] and [[Claude Cowork]], overseeing [[Boris Cherny]] and [[Cat Wu]]. She has been an engineer for over 25 years, previously leading Visual Studio and TypeScript at [[Microsoft]], founding and launching [[Facebook Marketplace]] at [[Meta]], contributing to Meta's first smart-glasses product and its Orion AR-glasses project, and later leading infrastructure, growth, integrity, and safety teams at Instagram (an org of 500+ people).

## Key Information

- Career path: started at IBM (operating system services team on DB2, using Vim/terminal debugging, no IDE); joined Microsoft in the early 2000s on the Visual Studio editor team (her first exposure to an IDE); spent 11 years at Microsoft; moved to [[Meta]]/Facebook where she started and scaled [[Facebook Marketplace]] from idea to launch; worked on Meta's first smart glasses and the Orion AR glasses project; later led Instagram infrastructure, growth, integrity, and safety orgs (500+ people); joined [[Anthropic]] to lead the [[Claude Code]] and [[Claude Cowork]] teams.
- At Meta, she interviewed as a manager but spent roughly her first quarter as an individual contributor to learn what it's like to ship as a Meta engineer before taking on people management.
- At Anthropic, she maintains a standing [[Claude Code]] remote session with access to all repos and Slack channels, which she uses (often monthly, alongside reports) to review what shipped, catch quality issues, and turn shipping activity into coaching conversations rather than just generating PRs.
- Coined/uses the internal [[Bad Vs Sad Framework|"bad vs. sad"]] quality framework and the phrase "make new mistakes" (encouraging learning through action rather than zero-mistake caution).
- Champions [[High Agency High Accountability|high agency paired with high accountability]] as a core team value on Claude Code/Cowork.
- Instituted the practice that new managers on her team start as individual contributors first, and continue doing small PRs part-time afterward as a "player-coach," both to build rapport with the team and to stay personally in touch ("in the flow") with fast-changing tools.
- Introduced pair-programming lunches and hackathons on the Claude Code team after noticing that working mostly with an individual agent was becoming a lonely experience.
- Practices [[Dogfooding]] and direct-feedback ("[[Latent Demand|latent demand]]") observation as her primary way of staying grounded in the product, citing examples from Facebook Marketplace (a Chile launch blocked by poor LTE performance, discovered on a small research trip; a new resale-scam vector she personally encountered as a seller) and from Anthropic (using Claude Cowork for her own travel expensing led directly to the "Claude for Small Business" bundle).
- Shifted her team's planning process from six-month roadmaps to monthly "[[Just In Time Planning|just-in-time (JIT) planning]]" on a lightweight spreadsheet, revisited weekly.
- Credits a [[Growth Mindset|growth mindset]] and the practice of asking "what is within my control?" for helping her adapt across major career-stage transitions (Microsoft to Meta, and again into the AI-tooling era); illustrates this with her own biography — born in Hong Kong, immigrated to Ontario, Canada as a child not speaking English, worked as a bank teller through the 2000 dot-com downturn to help fund her engineering degree via Ontario's OSAP assistance program.
- Personal details: taught to knit by her grandmother at age eight; known on the team for knitting during meetings (per [[Boris Cherny]]); dreams of eventually opening a yarn store in her grandmother's name; favorite recommended books include Margaret Atwood, Haruki Murakami, and "The Little Prince"; favorite films include Amélie and the Studio Ghibli films Spirited Away and Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind (cites Nausicaä as a formative influence on her leadership principles).
- States that what "keeps her up at night" is maintaining team culture and a "one team" mentality (open, honest reporting of what isn't going well) as the Claude Code/Cowork org scales rapidly.
- Cited in a separate Lenny's Podcast episode (with [[Adam Mosseri]], Head of Instagram) as describing the people she hires now as falling into two camps: "builders with great taste that can take an idea from end to end" and "people with deep expertise in a very specific domain." Mosseri independently corroborates this same split in his own hiring philosophy for Instagram's "[[Pods|pods]]" and "[[Product Staff|product staff]]" model, agreeing strongly that "[[Taste|taste]] matters a lot."
