## Definition
Omar Sanseviero is a presenter from Google DeepMind who delivered a talk on Gemma, Google DeepMind's family of open models, at the AI Engineering conference, covering Gemma 4's capabilities, architecture, licensing, and community ecosystem.

## Key Information
- Works at Google DeepMind
- Presented on Gemma 4 just 7 days after its release
- Covered Gemma's evolution from Gemma 1 through Gemma 4
- Emphasized on-device capabilities, Apache 2.0 license change, and the "Gemmaverse" community ecosystem
- Highlighted multilingual capabilities (140+ languages) and the Gemini-based tokenizer
- Demonstrated on-device agentic demos running in airplane mode on phones
