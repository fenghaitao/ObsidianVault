## Definition
Maggie Appleton is a staff research engineer on the GitHub Next Labs team. She is a designer by background, from an era when design and engineering were separate disciplines. She works on experimental, high-risk projects exploring the future of software development, including ACE (Agent Collaboration Environment).

## Key Information
- Staff research engineer at GitHub Next (GitHub's experimental research team)
- Background as a designer, from when design and engineering were separate disciplines
- Works on the Labs team within GitHub Next, which she describes as "the Department of Fuck Around and Find Out"
- Focuses on shaping new developer agentic tools
- Created ACE (Agent Collaboration Environment), a research prototype for collaborative AI engineering
- Argues that current coding agent tools are fundamentally single-player and ignore the collaborative nature of software development
- Advocates for tools that bring planning, context gathering, decision-making, and development under one roof
- Personal website: maggieappleton.com
- Presented at the AI Engineer conference
