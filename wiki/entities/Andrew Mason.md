## Definition

Founder of [[Groupon]], who told [[Eric Ries]] the story of how the company's "one email a day" founding principle eroded into eight emails a day after going public, used in *Incorruptible* as a case study in incremental value-erosion under ROI pressure.

## Key Information

- Held firm to "one email a day" as Groupon's defining discipline even as executives pushed for more, until he agreed to run a data experiment on sending two emails a day.
- Once the two-email experiment proved more profitable, he "couldn't say no" to further incremental increases — frequency crept to three, then eventually eight emails a day, illustrating how ROI-based incrementalism defeats a stated principle one individually-justifiable step at a time.
- Per [[Keith Rabois]] (episode 15): credited with the line that phones should have two buttons, "I'm bored" and "I'm hungry" — Rabois cites this framing as the moment [[DoorDash]]'s opportunity clicked for him as an early investor.
