## Definition

*Don't Make Me Think*, by Steve Krug, is a classic usability book whose title [[Stewart Butterfield]] adopted as an internal mantra at [[Slack]], alongside his "[[Utility Curves]]" framework.

## Key Information

- Butterfield says it's been over a decade since he read it and no longer remembers its specific examples, but the title itself became a durable internal shorthand for prioritizing comprehension over friction-reduction. See [[Comprehension Vs Friction]].
