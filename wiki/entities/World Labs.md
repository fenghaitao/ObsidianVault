## Definition

AI company founded by [[Fei-Fei Li]], working on world models; raised in the episode as a candidate direction for the kind of physically-grounded AI models [[Caitlin Kalinowski]] believes are needed for real engineering/[[CAD]] work.

## Key Information

- [[Lenny Rachitsky]] mentions Fei-Fei Li was previously a podcast guest and connects her work at World Labs to Kalinowski's stated need for AI models that understand physical properties like friction, weight, and contact.
- Kalinowski says she is not familiar with the specifics of what Fei-Fei Li/World Labs is working on but is interested to learn more, and views world models generally as likely part of the solution (though not the entire solution) for AI-assisted CAD and physical engineering.
