## Definition
World Labs is Fei-Fei Li's company that takes a different approach to world model building compared to Google DeepMind's Genie 3, focusing on generating 3D environments with Unity/Unreal Engine-style asset generation rather than pixel-by-pixel 2D rendering.

## Key Information
- Founded by Fei-Fei Li
- Takes a different approach to world model building than Genie 3
- Focuses on 3D environment generation with game-engine-style assets
- Contrasted with Genie 3's approach of dynamic pixel-by-pixel 2D frame generation
- Paige Bailey noted that all model approaches may converge on multiple input/output modalities in the future
