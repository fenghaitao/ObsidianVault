## Definition
Andres (Andy) Marafioti leads multimodal research at Hugging Face. He is the creator and maintainer of the Hugging Face speech-to-speech pipeline and the driving force behind the Reachy Mini open-source robot project.

## Key Information
- Lead of multimodal research at Hugging Face
- Maintains the Hugging Face speech-to-speech pipeline (2+ years of development)
- Led development of the Reachy Mini robot project
- Spent 2 weeks optimizing Coqui 3 TTS for real-time performance: implemented streaming, static KV cache, and CUDA graph capture to achieve 5.8x real-time factor (from 0.8x)
- Released "Faster Coqui 3 TTS" as open source on Hugging Face
- Vision: democratize how people interact with robots before the experience is dominated by a few companies
- Presented at aiDotEngineer conference
