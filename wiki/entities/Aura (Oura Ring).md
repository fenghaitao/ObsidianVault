## Definition

Aura (Oura Ring) is a sleep and health tracking wearable ring. Claire Vo uses it as part of her "full stack Silicon Valley sleep stack" alongside an Apple Watch and Eight Sleep mattress.

## Key Information

- Sleep and health tracking wearable ring
- Part of Claire Vo's "full stack Silicon Valley sleep stack" (Aura + Apple Watch + Eight Sleep)
- Claire: "I charge my Aura so it can confirm to me I'm not sleeping"
- Claire works from 6am to 9pm and falls asleep in 21 minutes
