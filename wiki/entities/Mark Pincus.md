## Definition

Founder of [[Zynga]] and, before that, [[Freeloader]], [[Support.Com]], and [[Tribe]]. Author of the book "[[Life at the Speed of Play]]," which synthesizes his product philosophy, most notably the [[Proven Better New]] framework.

## Key Information

- Developed [[Proven Better New]] at Zynga: instincts are right ~95% of the time, ideas are wrong ~75% of the time; master what's "proven," refine it into "better," then add a small "new" element.
- Coined/uses "[[Kill Hope Before Hope Kills You]]" — the discipline of distinguishing evidence-based belief from unfounded hope when deciding whether to keep pursuing a product idea.
- Career arc: Freeloader (offline browsing) → Support.com → Tribe (social networking, failed from being too ambitious) → Zynga (deliberately started "embarrassingly small," a Facebook poker game) → Earth (his ~20-year, still-unfinished metaverse project; pulled the plug on the current version after 4 years and $25M).
- Advocates being *less* ambitious at the outset as the path to the biggest ideas — a luxury of startups (vs. incumbents like Meta/Zuck, who must swing at billion-dollar-scale bets only).
- At Zynga, obsessed over [[Day 365 Retention]] and invented the [[Active Social Network]] (ASN) metric, valuing retention over virality.
- Management philosophy: "[[Make Everyone A CEO]]" (give people a hill to take with full operating control), "[[Close To The Metal]]" (best product CEOs stay in the pixel-level details), and the "[[Teaching Hospital Model]]" for mentoring successors via a "tech assistant" role.
- Believes AI should be used as a "testing machine, failure machine" — building many cheap wrong products fast to get signal, rather than one slow, expensive, hope-driven bet.
- On distribution: argues AI chat apps (GPT, Claude) are not yet a true consumer platform (no app-store-like discovery layer exists yet), and that consumer/social has "lost the adrenaline"; proposes reinventing a social "[[Cocktail Party Distribution]]" moment, possibly brokered by AI agents (a "[[Social Membrane]]").
- Parenting philosophy in the AI era: teaches his five kids critical thinking over rote knowledge, "generative" rather than "consumptive" online behavior, and to question authority figures' agendas; keeps a running document of life philosophies for his children.
- Defines his personal "why" as building an "internet treasure" — a service so embedded in daily life people can't imagine living without it.
