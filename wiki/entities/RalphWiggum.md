## Definition
Ralph Wiggum is a character from The Simpsons, known for trying the same thing repeatedly until it works. The "Ralph loop" AI pattern is named after him.

## Key Information
- Character from The Simpsons
- Namesake of the Ralph loop AI pattern
- The connection: Ralph Wiggum tries the same thing over and over, and eventually it works — mirroring the Ralph loop pattern of re-running the same prompt until the AI catches all its mistakes
- Referenced by Chris Parsons and Matt Pocock in their AI workflow talks
