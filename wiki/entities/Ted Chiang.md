## Definition

Ted Chiang is a science fiction author. Edwin Chen's all-time favorite short story is "Story of Your Life" by Ted Chiang.

## Key Information

- "Story of Your Life" is about a linguist learning an alien language, which resonates deeply with Edwin's childhood dream of deciphering alien communication
- The story was adapted into the film "Arrival," which Edwin also loves
- Edwin rereads "Story of Your Life" every couple of years
