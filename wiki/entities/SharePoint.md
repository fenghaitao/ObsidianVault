## Definition
SharePoint is Microsoft's enterprise content management and collaboration platform. In the Demand-Driven Context methodology, it is identified as one of the sources of institutional knowledge that forms the enterprise knowledge monolith.

## Key Information
- Part of the institutional knowledge monolith alongside Confluence, Jira, Slack, and GitHub
- Contains documentation that may be outdated, unreliable, or duplicated
- The Demand-Driven Context approach can be applied to SharePoint-based knowledge
