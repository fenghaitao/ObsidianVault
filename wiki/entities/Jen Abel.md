## Definition

Jen Abel is a pricing and sales expert who has been a guest on Lenny's Podcast multiple times. Jason Cohen references her advice on enterprise SaaS pricing and positioning.

## Key Information

- Has been on Lenny's Podcast at least twice
- Known for pricing and sales advice, particularly for SaaS companies
- Key insight: enterprises' sweet spot for contracts is typically $75K–$150K; that's how they normally buy SaaS software
- If your product is priced at $1K–$2K/month, getting into the $75K–$150K range "everything just gets easier"
- Pricing advice includes how to reposition the way you're selling to command higher prices
- Active on Twitter; Lenny describes her as great on Twitter
