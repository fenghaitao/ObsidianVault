## Definition

Jensen Huang is the founder and CEO of NVIDIA, the leading manufacturer of GPUs that power AI training and inference.

## Key Information

- Founder and CEO of NVIDIA
- Was a guest at Cisco's 12-hour AI summit, interviewed by Jeetu Patel on stage
- Jeetu Patel described the summit as having an "insane assembling of AI thought leaders" including Jensen
- Stated: "Every company in the world needs to have a cloud strategy. OpenClaw is the new computer."
- Called OpenClaw "the fastest growing open source project in history" — surpassing Linux
