### In the Kubernetes Origin Story
- Brendan Burns notes Google talked to people at Twitter (and Facebook) about scale-out infrastructure, and "they were all building this stuff" — evidence that Google's orchestrator "secret" wasn't actually one.

### Martin Odersky on Scala's Twitter adoption
- Twitter was originally written in Ruby and became unreliable (garbage collection/memory management) when it was ~25 people; investors pushed for Java, but engineers who knew OCaml found Scala: "quite a lot like OCaml," and telling investors "we do Java" was "not a lie" because it ships JVM bytecode.
- Twitter was the first major Scala adopter; once they chose it, "the floodgates opened" as other teams migrating from Ruby/PHP/JavaScript followed.
