## Definition

Matt McGinness is the Chief Product Officer (CPO) at Rippling, having previously served as the company's longtime Chief Operating Officer (COO). He is known for his frameworks on leadership intensity, deliberate understaffing, and organizational entropy.

## Key Information

- Longtime COO at Rippling before transitioning to CPO in January 2025
- Previously spent 9 years as founder of Inkling (2009-2018), which was sold to a private equity firm
- Spent 7 years at Apple, learning under Steve Jobs during the "death march" era
- Known at Rippling for "McGinness's injured birds" — focusing on whichever function is in disarray
- Early investor in Notion, Clever, Rippling, Decagon, LangChain, and ~70 companies total
- Advocates for "extraordinary results require extraordinary efforts"
- Developed the "pickle" (Product Quality List / PQL) framework at Rippling
- Created the SPOTTAK hiring framework (Smart, Passionate, Optimistic, Tenacious, Adaptable, Kind)
- Former radio personality/DJ in high school
- Lenny references Matt's advice on when to quit a startup: if it's four or five years in and it's just not clicking, it's probably time to move on — even though some people succeed years in, most likely it won't be you
- Jeetu Patel referenced a conversation with Matt about storytelling: the importance of a direct "Ethernet Cat 5 connection" to the front lines — no packet loss, message gets to the intended audience
