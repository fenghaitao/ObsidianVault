## Definition

Google Calendar is the Google consumer product Michael Bolin worked on early in his career and the one that drew him to Google.

## Key Information

- Bolin was excited to work on Google Calendar specifically when entering the industry, drawn by Google's web-forwardness and engineering quality.
- Calendar was "still mostly consumer" at the time and also sold to enterprises, but Bolin's team was "probably a cost center" rather than the money-maker.
- Bolin recalls a funny early example of charging ahead: he wanted weather icons in Calendar, cobbled together an XML blob to store the data, and his tech lead asked why he didn't use protocol buffers.
