## Definition
Sandipan "Sandy" Bhaumik is a distributed systems engineer with 18 years of experience building and scaling data systems in the cloud. He has worked at AWS and Databricks, with deep expertise in production-grade distributed systems across multi-tenant SaaS platforms and regulated industries (financial services, healthcare). For the last 2 years he has been deploying multi-agent AI systems in production.

## Key Information
- 18 years building distributed data systems
- Background spans multi-tenant SaaS and regulated industries (financial services, healthcare)
- Former AWS engineer
- Currently at Databricks
- Has spent 2 years deploying multi-agent AI systems in production
- Key thesis: multi-agent AI systems are fundamentally distributed systems problems — adding agents is not like adding features, it's building a distributed system
- Teaches patterns for production-grade multi-agent systems: choreography vs orchestration, immutable state, circuit breakers, compensation patterns, data contracts
- Emphasizes that coordination complexity grows exponentially with agent count (5 agents = 25x complexity)
