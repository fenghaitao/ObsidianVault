## Definition
Prefect Technologies is a data automation and orchestration software company founded by Jeremiah Lowin, operating the full stack from data engineering to AI agent infrastructure.

## Key Information
- Founded by Jeremiah Lowin, who has been building the company for 7-8 years
- Originally started to graduate orchestration ideas from Apache Airflow into data science
- Today operates the full stack of data automation and orchestration software
- Produces FastMCP, the de facto standard Python framework for building MCP servers
- FastMCP sees ~1.5 million downloads per day
- The company has a commercial product line that offers opinionated features (like per-tool masking) distinct from the open-source FastMCP
