## Definition

Dan Gil is the Chief Product Officer at Carvana. He is credited by Matt McGinness with the phrase "extraordinary results require extraordinary efforts."

## Key Information

- Chief Product Officer at Carvana
- Originator of the phrase "extraordinary results require extraordinary efforts," which Matt McGinness uses as a core leadership principle
- McGinness notes that Carvana "doesn't get enough credit for how much of a tech company it actually is"
