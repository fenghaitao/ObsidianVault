## Definition

Omni is an AI analytics company that takes a semantic layer approach to embedded analytics. Instead of having LLMs guess at SQL in production, Omni's AI knows business definitions and validates reasoning before queries hit production.

## Key Information

- Provides AI analytics with a semantic layer built in
- AI knows business definitions, not just raw tables
- Features: test queries, validate reasoning, lock down permissions before production
- Companies using Omni: Perplexity, DBT, BuzzFeed
- Offers a free 3-week trial at omni.co/lenny
- Sponsor of this episode of Lenny's Podcast
