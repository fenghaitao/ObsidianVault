## Definition

Dwarkesh Patel (rendered "Dark Cesh" in this episode's transcript) hosts the Dwarkesh Podcast, referenced by [[Lenny Rachitsky]] for its episode with AI researcher [[Rich Sutton]] on "[[Bitter Lesson|the bitter lesson]]" and Sutton's view that LLMs may be a dead end for reaching AGI.

## Key Information

- [[Edwin Chen]], asked for his take on Sutton's LLM-skepticism, agrees something beyond current LLM architectures will likely be needed, framing the goal as building models that can mimic the many different ways humans actually learn.
