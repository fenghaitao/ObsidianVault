## Definition

Dieter Rams (referred to as "DA Roms"/"deer Rams" in the transcript) is the influential industrial designer whose viral video — walking around criticizing famous designed chairs — [[Max Schoening]] pinned to his Twitter/X profile and discusses as an illustration of [[Malleable Software]]'s underlying design philosophy.

## Key Information

- In the clip Schoening references, Rams walks around disapprovingly pointing his walking stick at famous museum-piece chairs, saying "this isn't good enough" — including a remark that one piece "is neither orderly nor properly chaotic."
- Schoening's takeaway: design should be first useful and then beautiful; many famous designer pieces are built for display/status rather than actual use, and Rams' willingness to say "no, this is stupid" about celebrated designers' work cuts through unearned reverence for status/reputation.
- Schoening connects this directly to [[Malleable Software]] and to [[Stewart Brand]]'s *How Buildings Learn*: a good test of whether something is genuinely useful is whether you can change and tweak it over time, rather than being handed a fixed, "finished" object by an authority/architect.
- Schoening jokes his only point of commonality with Rams is that they are both German.
