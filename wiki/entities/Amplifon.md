## Definition
Amplifon is the world leader in hearing care solutions, operating across 26 countries with over 20,000 people and more than 10,000 stores globally. The company is undergoing an AI transformation, launching the Amplify program in January 2025 to govern AI adoption at scale.

## Key Information
- **Industry**: Hearing care solutions (hearing aids, audiology services)
- **Scale**: 26 countries, 20,000+ employees, 10,000+ stores
- **AI Initiative**: Amplify Program (launched January 2025) -- a global, cross-functional program to set rules for AI adoption
- **Amplify Pillars**: Governance (regulatory alignment, strategy, guidelines), Platform (certified infrastructure for AI development), Factory (development teams delivering scalable, reusable AI solutions)
- **Operating Model**: Control Tower (central team defining security, legal, technology guidelines and strategy) plus Committee (running strategy in countries/corporate, prioritizing use cases)
- **Key Challenges**: Short LLM lifecycle management, governance/compliance across distributed teams, enterprise scaling with centralized governance across multiple teams and infrastructures
- **Registry System**: Built enterprise-grade MCP Registry, A2A Registry, and Use Case Registry with an AI Gateway for unified LLM access
