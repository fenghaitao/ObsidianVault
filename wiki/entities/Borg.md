## Definition

Borg is Google's internal cluster-management/orchestration system, the internal predecessor whose ideas influenced Kubernetes.

## Key Information

- Ryan Peterman framed Borg as a "secret infrastructure sauce" and competitive advantage for Google, raising the question of why Google would give any of it away.
- Burns argued it wasn't actually a secret: people at Facebook, Twitter, and other scale-out companies were all building similar systems (and Mesos already existed), so an open-source orchestrator was inevitable — the real question was whether Google could influence it.
