## Definition

Mercury is a fintech company offering banking services for entrepreneurs and startups. It provides radically different banking loved by over 300,000 entrepreneurs. Lenny switched from Chase to Mercury and praises its product experience.

## Key Information

- Fintech company, not an FDIC insured bank
- Banking services provided through Choice Financial Group and Column NA, members FDIC
- Loved by over 300,000 entrepreneurs
- Free to get started, no in-person visits, no minimum balances
- Flexes to all sizes of company from startups to large enterprises
- Features: fast wire setup, spending tracking, triggers to move money, invoicing, team access controls
- Lenny describes it as "an actual product person built a bank versus a banking person building a product"
- Episode sponsor of Lenny's Podcast
- Website: mercury.com

### Onboarding and Quality
- Amol Avaisary previously worked on the growth team at Mercury
- His team spent a full quarter fixing onboarding quality: "Forget metrics, forget growth, forget everything else. We're going to spend a whole quarter fixing quality."
- Mercury's onboarding for regulated banking was extremely complex (registered agent vs. legal address vs. physical address, beneficial ownership details)
- The quality-focused quarter became the single most impactful quarter of Amol's career up to that point
- "Quality drives growth" — a core principle Amol brought to Anthropic
- Mercury also tested breaking out form steps into multiple screens to reduce cognitive load, which performed well
