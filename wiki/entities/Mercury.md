## Definition

Mercury is a fintech banking platform for startups, presented in this episode as a sponsor of [[Lenny's Podcast]]. It is a fintech company (not itself an FDIC-insured bank); banking services are provided through Choice Financial Group and Column N.A., members FDIC.

## Key Information

- Serves over 300,000 entrepreneurs; markets itself as banking "built by product people, not by bankers."
- Recently launched "Command," a conversational/AI interface built into Mercury that acts as a financial operator — used in the ad read to transfer money, categorize spending, analyze cash flow, and answer natural-language questions like "how much have I made from a specific sponsor over the past year."
- Offers an API, a terminal-native CLI, and an "AI ready" MCP server for banking operations.
- Per [[Amol]] (episode 16): he worked on Mercury's growth team, where a quarter-long focus purely on fixing onboarding-flow quality (for complex regulated-banking signup steps, e.g. registered agent vs. legal vs. physical address fields) — with growth metrics deliberately set aside — became his most impactful growth quarter to date, and shaped his broader "quality drives growth" philosophy. See [[Good Friction]].
