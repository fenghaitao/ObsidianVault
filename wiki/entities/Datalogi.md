## Definition
Datalogi is a company specializing in generating high-quality synthetic training data for language models, a capability that only became viable when LLMs crossed a quality threshold enabling them to generate data good enough to train themselves on.

## Key Information
- Focused on generating high-quality synthetic data for model training
- Part of a new wave of companies enabled by LLMs becoming capable of producing training data that improves model performance
- Jack Morris highlighted them as an example of the synthetic data generation trend that enables weight-based knowledge injection
