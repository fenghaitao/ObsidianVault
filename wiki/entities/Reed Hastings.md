## Definition

Reed Hastings is the co-founder and longtime CEO of Netflix.

## Key Information

- Had a vision from the late 1990s that DVD-by-mail was a stepping stone to streaming video; he named the company "Netflix" rather than "DVDs by mail.com" because he saw DVD shipping as a means, not the end.
- Was not a video expert himself, but built a team of strong leaders and a small team of excellent engineers to solve the streaming problem.
- In the early days, personally held small one-on-one sessions with new hires (e.g., four at a time) to understand who they were and what excited them about Netflix.
- David Ronca describes him as "an incredibly visionary leader" who could also execute — someone who "makes you dream of sailing the seas, and not just cutting lumber for a ship."
