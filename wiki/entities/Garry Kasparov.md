## Definition
Garry Kasparov was the world chess champion who was defeated by IBM's Deep Blue in 1997, marking the first time a computer beat the best human chess player. This event is a landmark milestone in the intertwined history of chess and AI.

## Key Information
- Defeated by Deep Blue in 1997, a watershed moment for AI and chess computing
- His defeat demonstrated that Type A (brute force search) engines had surpassed human chess ability
- Referenced by the Play Magnus team as a key historical milestone in the evolution from early chess computers to modern AI chess coaching
