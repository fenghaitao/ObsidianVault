## Definition
The Tony Blair Institute (TBI) is a think tank cited by Eoin Mulgrew for its estimate that AI could deliver £40 billion in annual productivity gains for UK government, quantifying the scale of the public sector productivity crisis.

## Key Information
- Estimated a £40 billion annual prize from AI-driven productivity gains in UK government
- Cited by [[EoinMulgrew]] to quantify the public sector productivity crisis
- Provides policy research and recommendations on technology adoption in government
