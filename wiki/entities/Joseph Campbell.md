## Definition

Joseph Campbell was a mythologist and author. Rachel Lockett keeps a quote of his on her desk: "If you can see your path laid out in front of you step by step, it's not your path. Your own path you make with every step you take. That's why it's your path."

## Key Information

- Mythologist and author known for "The Hero's Journey" framework
- Rachel Lockett's favorite life motto is a Joseph Campbell quote
- The quote speaks to forging one's own unique path rather than following a predetermined one
