## Definition
Alessandro Cappelli is co-founder and chief customer officer at Adaptive ML, a company building an RL Ops platform for enterprise LLM deployment. He was previously part of the team that trained Falcon, one of the most widely adopted open-source models.

## Key Information
- **Role**: Co-founder and chief customer officer at Adaptive ML
- **Prior work**: Part of the team that trained Falcon, an early widely-adopted open-source LLM
- **Core thesis**: Reinforcement learning is not just another post-training algorithm — it is the essential algorithm for systematically bringing LLMs to production
- **Key concept**: The "myth of the last mile" — getting to MVP is the first mile; the real marathon is MVP to production and beyond
- **Argument**: 95% of GenAI pilots fail to reach production because they lack a systematic improvement mechanism; RL provides this
- **Platform vision**: RL Ops platforms should holistically handle observe, train, and serve, accelerating the full model lifecycle
- **On human-in-the-loop**: Humans should define rubrics and scenarios (minutes to hours), not run expensive annotation campaigns (weeks)
- **On agents**: RL was originally designed for training agents in environments, so it naturally fits agent training
