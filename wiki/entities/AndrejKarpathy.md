## Definition

Andrej Karpathy is an AI researcher and educator — former founding member of [[OpenAI]], former Director of AI at Tesla, prolific essayist on Twitter/X and YouTube. In the timeline of this knowledge base he matters for two contributions: **coining "vibe coding"** (early 2025) and **articulating the canonical definition of [[ContextEngineering]]** later that year.

## Key Information

### Why he matters in this corpus

Two terms central to the AI-coding discourse trace back to him:

1. **[[VibeCoding]]** (early 2025) — coined by Karpathy in a now-famous tweet describing letting the AI write code with minimal oversight. Went viral; defined the way most developers approached AI coding for several months.

2. **[[ContextEngineering]] (definition)** (mid-2025) — when the term "context engineering" emerged in opposition to vibe coding's failure modes, Karpathy responded with a definition that became canonical:

   > *"Context engineering is the art of providing all the context for the task to be plausibly solvable by the LLM."*

   He insisted he wasn't trying to coin a new word. [[ColeMedin]] in `summary-context-engineering-is-new-vibe-coding`: *"Andre, if you're watching this, I think it is too late."*

### The arc

Both terms are Karpathy's, even though the later one corrects the earlier one's excesses. The progression — vibe coding becomes mainstream → its limits become visible → context engineering emerges as the disciplined alternative — is essentially a Karpathy-flavored debate the rest of the field has organized around.

### Other context

- **Karpathy LLM Wiki pattern** — see [[KarpathyLLMWiki]]. Karpathy's own pattern for personal knowledge management with LLMs: compile rather than retrieve, build a persistent wiki. **This is the foundational pattern of *this* knowledge base** (see `CLAUDE.md`'s schema). Cole Medin's [[SecondBrain]] is an implementation of it. Full deep dive lands in batch C (`20260406` video).
- **Toby Lütke (Shopify CEO)** — was the Twitter interlocutor who framed Context Engineering as "treating context as an engineered resource requiring careful architecture." Karpathy replied affirming the framing.

### Adjacent influence

Karpathy's broader content (lectures on building neural networks from scratch, the "let's build GPT" series, etc.) is part of why his framings stick. When he names something, the AI engineering community pays attention.
