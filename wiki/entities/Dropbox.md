## Definition

Dropbox is a file-storage/collaboration SaaS company where [[Elena Verna]] worked in growth immediately prior to joining [[Lovable]].

## Key Information

- Verna contrasts her AI usage at Dropbox ("here and there," mostly ChatGPT) with the deeply AI-native, expectation-driven daily workflow at Lovable — she says she never made the same leap in AI adoption at Dropbox despite having access to similar tools, largely because AI-native behavior wasn't the surrounding cultural default there the way it is at Lovable.
- Cited alongside [[Miro]], SurveyMonkey, Netlify, and Amplitude as prior B2B SaaS companies she benchmarks Lovable's retention metrics against.
