## Definition
PFF is a sports data company that provides analytics to NFL and NCAA teams and operates a consumer arm for fantasy football and sports betting. The company served as a case study for building a post-engineer engineering organization using AI coding agents.

## Key Information
- **Industry**: Sports data and analytics
- **Customers**: NFL teams, NCAA teams, and consumers (fantasy football, sports betting)
- **Scale**: ~200 employees, ~20 engineers, 100 million page views annually, 9 million drafts annually
- **Engineering Team**: Fully distributed across India, Spain, and the United States
- **AI Adoption**: Ran a case study (January–March 2026) with two top engineers using [[ClaudeCode]], achieving 25x deployment frequency and 10x output vs. a 10-engineer team
- **Development Practices**: Trunk-based development with feature flags, service repository pattern for APIs
- **Engineering Culture**: Eliminated traditional Agile ceremonies (standups, sprint planning, retrospectives) in favor of bi-daily huddles and an autonomous, spec-driven development pipeline
- **Customer Satisfaction**: Post-AI quality scores averaged 8.6/10 (up from 7–7.5 before AI)
- **Notable Product**: Draft tool used by fantasy football players, timed around the NFL draft
