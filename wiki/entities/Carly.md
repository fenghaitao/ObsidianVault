## Definition

Carly (also referred to as Karly in other transcripts) is a Senior Manager and Lead Engineer (staff-equivalent IC) at Activision, working in security. She is a panelist on the "Getting To Staff" discussions.

## Key Information

- Panelist on the Sept 2023 "Getting To Staff" panel
- Works at Activision as both a Senior Manager and IC Lead Engineer (staff-equivalent title)
- Graduated grad school in 2017; moved up from Junior Data Scientist fairly quickly
- Started at Activision at the Senior level and took about two years to get to Lead
- Works in security at Activision, making video games
- Her promotion to Lead was triggered by a presentation to an executive leader and the CTO, demonstrating the ability to speak technically to a non-technical audience
- The CTO offered her a job after that presentation, leading to an internal move and promotion
- Emphasized that being able to speak eloquently to both technical and non-technical audiences is critical
- Plays Call of Duty
- Active on LinkedIn and Instagram
