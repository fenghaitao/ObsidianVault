## Definition

Spiral is [[Every]]'s writing app, run by PM [[Marcus]], cited by [[Dan Shipper]] as his central example of why he is "super bullish" on product managers in the AI era.

## Key Information

- Marcus, a PM by training (previously ran Axios's writing product to tens of millions in ARR), took a year off, became deeply AI-pilled, and taught himself to use [[Cursor]] and later [[Claude Code]] well enough to personally ship product for Spiral.
- Described by Dan Shipper as "lightly technical" (understands what a database migration is, can read code) but paired his product/writing sense with AI-driven building speed, ships faster than almost anyone else on the team, and no longer needs to organize a whole team to execute his ideas.
