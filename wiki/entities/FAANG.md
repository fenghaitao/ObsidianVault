## Definition

FAANG is an acronym for five major American technology companies: Facebook (Meta), Amazon, Apple, Netflix, and Google. The term is used to refer to the most prestigious and highest-paying tech employers.

## Key Information

- The entire panel was focused on getting to Staff Engineer at FAANG companies
- Panelists had experience across Meta, Amazon, Netflix, Google, and Airbnb (FAANG-adjacent)
- FAANG companies are known for having well-defined engineering career ladders with Staff Engineer as a distinct level
- Compensation at FAANG companies grows geometrically rather than linearly as you move up levels
- Titles outside FAANG are often inflated compared to FAANG leveling standards
