## Definition
Earendil is a company co-founded by Armin Ronacher and Cristina Poncela Cubeiro in October 2024, focused on making sense of AI engineering tools and building agent-legible development workflows.

## Key Information
- Founded in October 2024 by Armin Ronacher and Cristina Poncela Cubeiro
- Focus: making sense of AI engineering tools and agent-assisted development
- Has been building with/on agents for 12 months (as of April 2026)
- Developed a PyExtension for code review that separates mechanical bugs from human-judgment callouts
- Philosophy: friction is necessary for steering; agents need guardrails and human judgment at critical moments
- Promotes agent-legible codebase design with mechanical enforcement (linting rules, unique function names, erasable syntax TypeScript)
