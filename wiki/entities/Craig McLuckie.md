## Definition

Craig McLuckie is a product manager and co-creator of Kubernetes. In this transcript Brendan Burns refers to him by first name only ("Craig") and describes him as "a great product manager."

## Key Information

- One of the three people (with Brendan Burns and Joe Beda) who built the initial Kubernetes demo at Google.
- The product/business-oriented member of the founding trio, complementing Joe's API design and Burns's rapid prototyping.
- Along with Burns and Joe, he held the aesthetic of distributed, democratic ownership rather than a "benevolent dictator for life" model, which was later codified into Kubernetes governance.
