## Definition

AirOps is a growth marketing platform for AI search (optimizing brand visibility in ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, etc.) that evolved from a node-based workflow builder to an agent-based system using the Claude Agent SDK. The platform uses document-based playbooks (skill equivalents for marketers) with embedded human review gates and specialized sub-agents for content quality.

## Key Information

- **AI search optimization:** Helps brands see how they appear in AI search, identify gaps, take action, and measure impact.
- **Document-based playbooks:** Natural-language skill-like documents with slash commands for tools — familiar to marketers used to Google Docs.
- **Human review gates:** Assign specific users to approve outputs at each section; only assigned user can unblock the agent.
- **Sub-agent decomposition:** Compliance check, dedicated writer, brand kit fetcher, and custom sub-agents per customer.
- **Results:** 130% citation rate increase, 42% share of voice increase, enterprise customers going live in 1 week (vs. 1 month).
