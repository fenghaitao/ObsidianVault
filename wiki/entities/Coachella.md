## Definition

Coachella is a major music and arts festival held annually in California. Ricky referenced it as an example of the memorable life experiences that matter more than career achievements.

## Key Information

- Ricky cited Coachella as one of his happiest moments in his 20s: "The happiest times were like when I'm at Coachella and I'm really drunk and my shirt's off and I'm having fun with my friends"
- Used to illustrate that work is not everything — the most memorable moments come from life outside work
