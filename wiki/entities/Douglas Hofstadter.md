## Definition

Douglas Hofstadter is an author and cognitive scientist, best known for "Gödel, Escher, Bach." Edwin Chen recommends "Le Ton Beau de Marot" as his favorite Hofstadter book.

## Key Information

- "Le Ton Beau de Marot" takes a single French poem and translates it 89 different ways, discussing the motivations behind each translation
- Edwin loves how the book embodies the idea that "translation isn't this robotic thing" — there are a million different ways to think about what makes a high-quality translation
- This mirrors how Edwin thinks about data and quality in LLMs: there are many valid ways to produce high-quality output
- While "Gödel, Escher, Bach" is Hofstadter's more famous book, Edwin prefers "Le Ton Beau de Marot"
