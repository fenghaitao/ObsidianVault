## Definition

Buck is Meta's custom-built build system used for Android compilation. Rahul Pandey noted that Meta's use of Buck was one of the significant differences from standard Android development he had to learn when joining the Portal team.

## Key Information

- Meta's custom build system for Android
- Used for the Portal hardware device's Android compilation
- Part of the uniquely different build pipeline that made Portal development significantly different from standard Android or even core Facebook Android
- The Portal build process was so heavy that building all 30 apps would take 3-4 hours, so engineers would only build the specific app they were working on

### Adam Ernst's Buck

- Adam Ernst cites Buck among the systems whose "guts" he has learned by diving in whenever a problem blocks him, rather than delegating to a specialist team.
