## Definition

Jeffrey Moore is the author of "Crossing the Chasm," a foundational book on technology marketing and market strategy. Jason Cohen highly recommends reading the actual book, not just blog post summaries.

## Key Information

- Author of Crossing the Chasm
- Jason Cohen: "Everyone's heard of it, but no one's read it"
- Key concept: defining a market includes that people in the market respect the opinions of other people in the market
- Early adopters spread to other early adopters — they're looking for someone like them to say "this is great"
- Jumping to a different market is not impossible, but case studies alone won't work — you need market-specific validation
- Has been a guest on Lenny's Podcast
