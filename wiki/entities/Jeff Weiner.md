## Definition

Jeff Weiner is the former CEO of LinkedIn, described by Reed Hoffman as a "late-joining co-founder" — an executive who joined years after founding but had such transformative impact that he is effectively considered a co-founder. Jason Cohen uses this same term for Heather Brunner at WP Engine.

## Key Information

- Former CEO of LinkedIn
- Joined LinkedIn about four years after founding
- Reed Hoffman coined the term "late-joining co-founder" to describe him
- His impact on the company's success, culture, and direction was so significant that he's considered effectively a co-founder
- The term became the model for describing transformative later-joining executives
