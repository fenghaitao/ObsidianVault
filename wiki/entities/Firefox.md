## Definition

Firefox is Mozilla's open-source web browser, built on the Gecko engine. It broke Internet Explorer's dominance in the 2000s and remains the last independent browser engine.

## Key Information

- Began as Phoenix, then Firebird, then Firefox: a stripped-down "just a browser" fork of the Mozilla/Netscape suite built by junior contributors
- Took off by delivering what people wanted (tabbed browsing, popup blocking, user control) at a time IE was in maintenance mode
- Inherited roughly 10 million lines of largely unowned Netscape code; its culture rewarded people who claimed and fixed the hardest parts
- After Chrome launched, it fell behind on multiprocess architecture, Flash/YouTube stability, and a legacy extension API; the Firefox OS bet added further resource competition
- Firefox Quantum (with the Servo-derived parallel CSS engine) restored performance competitiveness and made its CSS engine the fastest in the market
- Today it competes as the only independent engine against WebKit and Chromium, and gives Mozilla its standards-table leverage
- The Speedometer 3 shared benchmark collaboration (Mozilla's involvement keeping it neutral) helped make the real web roughly 30% faster
