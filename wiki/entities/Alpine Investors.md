## Definition

Alpine Investors is a private equity firm that created the "People First Operating Rhythm," a framework for aligning vision, strategy, KPIs, annual goals, and quarterly goals on a single page. Their portfolio companies that implement this rhythm show higher returns.

## Key Information

- Developed the one-page plan concept and the People First Operating Rhythm
- Graham Weaver, founder/leader of Alpine Investors, was a guest on Lenny's Podcast
- The one-page plan puts vision and values in the first column, strategic intentions and KPIs in the second, annual goals in the third, and quarterly goals in the fourth
- Rachel Lockett works in concert with Alpine, coaching some of their portfolio CEOs
- Portfolio companies that institute the People First Operating Rhythm result in higher returns
