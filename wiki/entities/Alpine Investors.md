## Definition

Alpine Investors is a private equity firm (associated with [[Graham Weaver]], a prior [[Lenny's Podcast]] guest) that developed the "People First Operating Rhythm" — a one-page strategic-alignment tool [[Rachel Lockett]] adapted into her own coaching practice as the "[[One-Page Plan]]."

## Key Information

- Reports that portfolio companies implementing the People First Operating Rhythm show higher returns, which the firm cites as validation of the approach.
- [[Rachel Lockett]] works in concert with Alpine, coaching some of its portfolio-company CEOs directly on this rhythm.
