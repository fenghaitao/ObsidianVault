## Definition

Benjamin Verbeek is a Member of Technical Staff at Lovable, working on continuous learning at scale for AI coding agents. He has a physics background, having previously worked with satellites, particle physics, and fusion reactors.

## Key Information

- **Role**: Member of Technical Staff at [[Lovable]]
- **Focus**: Continuous learning at scale — making AI agents learn from every mistake so failures happen once and never again
- **Background**: Physics — previously worked with satellites, particle physics, and fusion reactors
- **Presented at**: [[aiDotEngineer]] conference, June 2026
- **Key contributions**: Designed and presented Lovable's two-pronged approach to self-improvement: the [[Lovable Stack Overflow]] (captured solutions with A/B evaluation) and the [[Agent Vent Tool]] (agent-to-creator feedback via Slack)
- Joined Lovable around mid-2025 when the platform had a few thousand users; now processes over 200,000 projects per day
