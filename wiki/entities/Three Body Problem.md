## Definition

The Three Body Problem is a science fiction trilogy by Chinese author Cixin Liu. It explores life beyond Earth, human decision-making under existential threat, geopolitics, and the value of abstract science to human progress.

## Key Information

- Three-book science fiction series by Cixin Liu
- [[Kiti Bottom]]'s favorite book series
- Explores themes of extraterrestrial life, human decision-making, geopolitics, and the hidden importance of abstract science
- Kiti connects it to AI: AI helping in scientific areas could be "extremely crucial" — the book shows what could happen when scientific progress is stopped
- Lenny notes the series "only got really good about one and a half books in" — readers should persist through the first book
- Also recommended by Lenny alongside [[A Fire Upon the Deep]] for AI-interested sci-fi readers
- Boris Cherny recommends the Netflix series adaptation; he also recommends Cixin Liu's short stories even more
- Boris: "I thought that was like a great rendition of the book series"
