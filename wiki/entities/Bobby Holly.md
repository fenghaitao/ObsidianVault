## Definition

Bobby Holley is the CTO of Mozilla Firefox. He joined Mozilla as an intern in 2008, became a principal engineer after leading the Quantum CSS project, and eventually CTO.

## Key Information

- His first internship was at NVIDIA; he chose Mozilla over a return because Mozilla's open-source culture had "no guardrails" and selected for self-directed, persistent contributors
- Manager Damon Sicore ran all of Gecko and told him to "get on IRC, get on Bugzilla, and figure some things out" — a stark contrast to the structured NVIDIA internship
- Started on the mobile browser frontend (Nokia Maemo), graphics engine, and image rendering; because so much Firefox code was unowned, he ended his first internship owning a large chunk of the browser
- His early hero and career model was Boris Zbarsky, one of Mozilla's first distinguished engineers
- Led the security effort "Slaughterhouse" to replace chrome object wrappers, and later led the Quantum CSS uplift of Servo's parallel CSS engine into Firefox
- Promoted to principal engineer after the successful launch of Firefox Quantum, then grew into the CTO role
- Chose the self-given title "negative entropy" in Mozilla's early choose-your-own-title culture
