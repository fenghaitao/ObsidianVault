## Definition
Genie 2 was Google DeepMind's second-generation world model that scaled from 2D platformers to interactive 3D game environments, but was not yet real-time and could not achieve real-world quality or high definition.

## Key Information
- **Capability**: Interactive 3D environments (scaled up from Genie 1's 2D platformers)
- **Limitations**: Not real-time — required slow interaction; could not achieve real-world quality or high definition
- **Training**: Scaled up data and improved method from Genie 1
- **Significance**: Intermediate step proving 3D world generation was feasible, leading to Genie 3
- **Lineage**: Between Genie 1 (2D, seconds) and Genie 3 (real-time, high quality, memory, real-time prompting)
