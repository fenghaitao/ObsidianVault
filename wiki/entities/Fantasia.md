## Definition

Fantasia is a classic Disney animated film. Its "Sorcerer's Apprentice" segment featuring Mickey Mouse is used by Sherwin Wu as the central metaphor for AI coding: powerful and high-leverage, but the agents (brooms) can go wild if you don't supervise them.

## Key Information

- Classic Disney animated film
- The "Sorcerer's Apprentice" segment features Mickey Mouse finding the sorcerer's hat and trying to cast spells
- Mickey sets the brooms off on a task and falls asleep — the brooms go wild, flooding everything
- Eventually the old sorcerer returns and cleans everything up
- Sherwin Wu uses this as the perfect metaphor for AI coding: extremely powerful, but you need to know what you're doing and supervise the agents
