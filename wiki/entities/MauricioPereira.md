## Definition

Mauricio Pereira is an MIT robotics graduate, founder of [[CoatRobotics]], and co-creator of [[CustomUniverse]], a winning project at [[Anthropic]]'s [[ClaudeOpus4.8]] Build Day hackathon (June 2026) that turns phone photos of objects into 3D models for robotics training data generation.

## Key Information

- Co-created [[CustomUniverse]] with [[JakeStevens]], a phone-photo-to-3D object pipeline for robotics labs needing [[SyntheticData|synthetic training data]].
- MIT robotics graduate; runs [[CoatRobotics]], his startup.
- Brought the robotics training data problem he knew firsthand to the hackathon; met teammate [[JakeStevens]] at the event.
- [[ClaudeOpus4.8]] built the project end-to-end and operated a remote [[NVIDIAH100]] throughout the hackathon.
- The project lets labs scan a machine from a factory floor, drop it into a scene, and generate data to fine-tune a robotics model for that exact environment.
- Custom Universe relies on open-source models and is free to use; users can run it on their own GPUs.
- Competed at the [[ClaudeOpus4.8]] Build Day hackathon on June 13, 2026, a 12-hour event with 310 builders.
