## Definition
Simon Marlow is a maintainer of the Glasgow Haskell Compiler (GHC), known for rigorous and exhaustive pull request review standards.

## Key Information
- Maintains the Haskell compiler (GHC), one of the repositories in METR's developer productivity RCT
- Known for engaging in lengthy PR review discussions, sometimes arguing for many hours longer than the contributor spent writing the code
- Represents the extremely high quality bar maintained on mature open-source projects
- His review style exemplifies why open-source maintainers have strong incentives to reject PRs that don't improve maintainability
