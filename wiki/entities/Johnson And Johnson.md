## Definition

Pharmaceutical/consumer-health company cited by [[Eric Ries]] in *Incorruptible* as an example of a company whose stated mission ("patient health") was betrayed by an actual operating mission of growth, optimization, and quarterly targets.

## Key Information

- Ries states that Johnson & Johnson product managers put asbestos in baby powder and covered it up, despite the company's stated mission statement being patient health — his illustration that a written mission statement is worthless unless backed by structural enforcement ("mission drive," not just "mission hopeful").
- Used alongside the Unilever "purpose of Hellmann's mayonnaise" anecdote to argue that even humble products have a purpose worth taking seriously, and that abandoning that purpose for small cost savings ("save 3 cents on the bill of materials") is where real harm originates.
