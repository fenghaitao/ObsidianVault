## Definition
Stefano Fiorucci is an AI and software engineer at Deepset, where he works on AI orchestration and develops Haystack, an open-source LLM framework. He is passionate about small language models, fine-tuning, and reinforcement learning.

## Key Information
- Works at Deepset on Haystack, an open-source LLM orchestration framework
- By night, tinkers with small language models, fine-tuning, and RL
- Created a free "LLM RL Environments" course covering RL environment engineering
- Conducted experiments transforming LFM-2 from a weak tic-tac-toe player into a master using SFT + RL with verifiable rewards
- Advocated for open-source RL environments to prevent open-source models from lagging behind due to lack of training playgrounds
