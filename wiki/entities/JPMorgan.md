## Definition
JPMorgan Chase is a multinational financial services firm cited as an example of enterprise AI innovation, specifically for productizing an internal productivity tool into an entirely new revenue stream.

## Key Information
- Started building something as an internal productivity tool using AI
- Successfully productized the internal tool to generate an entirely new revenue stream
- Example of emergent AI value — the product wasn't planned on a roadmap; it emerged from doing the work
- Illustrates the principle that AI can unlock capabilities that were previously economically impossible
- Represents the "AI achiever" category of companies seeing growth from doing entirely new things, not just cost cutting
