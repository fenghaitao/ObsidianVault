## Definition

Dharmesh (Dash) Shah is the co-founder and CTO of HubSpot. He is known for making a rule with his co-founder from the beginning that he would never have direct reports, allowing him to stay in his zone of genius.

## Key Information

- Co-founder and CTO of HubSpot
- From the beginning, made a rule with his co-founder that he would never manage people
- Has zero direct reports and no one-on-ones as CTO
- Example of designing a role around one's natural gifts and strengths
- Cited by Lenny as an example of someone who successfully structured their work to avoid depletion
