## Definition

Dharmesh Shah is co-founder and CTO of [[HubSpot]], cited by [[Lenny Rachitsky]] as an example of deliberate "zone of genius" career design: he made a rule with his co-founder from the outset that he would never have direct reports or hold one-on-ones.

## Key Information

- [[Rachel Lockett]] notes this kind of arrangement is healthy only if the resulting management gap for those reports is still deliberately addressed by someone else — not having reports doesn't mean no one manages them.
