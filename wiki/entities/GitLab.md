## Definition

GitLab is a software development platform that provides DevOps capabilities. It is an early customer of [[ClaudeEnterprise]], using Claude across a variety of team workflows.

## Claude Usage at GitLab

As noted by Taylor McCaslin (Product Lead for AI and ML Tech):
- Brainstorming for product and feature ideas
- Streamlining internal processes
- Creating and translating content
- Writing and reviewing code
- Acts as a virtual collaborator that "feels like an extension of their work and expertise"
- Enables teams to take on more complex tasks while protecting GitLab's intellectual property (IP)

## Key Quote

"Claude offers our team members a tool that feels like an extension of their work and expertise, allowing us to take on more complex tasks and deliver greater impact while ensuring GitLab's IP remains private and protected." — Taylor McCaslin, Product Lead for AI and ML Tech, GitLab
