## Definition

Arduino is an open-source electronics platform based on easy-to-use hardware and software. Jia Chen used Arduino in her VR hologram hackathon project.

## Key Information

- Jia used an Arduino with an OLED screen to project a glass prism hologram through cardboard VR glasses
- The project used AI transcription (Whisper AI) to project live transcription in a hologram through the VR glasses
- The entire project was built in 9 hours, allowing the team to sleep during the hackathon
- The Arduino was connected via Bluetooth for the hologram projection system
