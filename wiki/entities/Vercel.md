## Definition

Vercel is a front-end cloud platform that also offers an AI cloud (AI SDK, AI Gateway, Workflows, Fluid Compute) for building and deploying AI-native applications. The company practices "Vercel builds Vercel with Vercel" — dogfooding its own products extensively.

## Key Information

- Jeanne DeWitt Grosser is COO, overseeing marketing, sales, customer success, revenue ops, and field engineering.
- Offers a consumption-based business model, though initially bundled into a SaaS-like pricing; recently unbundled pricing (August change) to separate enterprise and pro SKUs, making enterprise features available self-serve for startups.
- Enterprise SKU had about half startups on it before the unbundling, suggesting startups wanted enterprise features.
- GTM engineers at Vercel build agents on Vercel's own AI cloud (Workflow SDK, AI Gateway, Fluid Compute).
- Lead agent for inbound SDR qualification costs ~$1,000/year to run on Vercel infrastructure.
- Sales engineers are all former front-end developers with CS degrees.
- Segmentation includes CrUX rank (Google traffic data), workload type (e-commerce, crypto, SaaS), and growth potential.
- Heavy Slack users — every customer has a dedicated Slack channel.
- Strong penetration in e-commerce; historically less in enterprise SaaS (companies built before Vercel existed), but AI cloud is changing that.
- Examples: OpenAI is a top-25 traffic site using Vercel.
- V0 (Vercel's AI web development assistant) enables PMs, designers, and developers to build prototypes, full-stack apps, and sync with GitHub without writing code.
- Janine from Vercel is cited as an example of a sales leader who successfully made the jump to the AI era — part of the ~20% who aren't panicking about AI.
- "Don't build it yourself unless you're Vercel" — Jason Lemkin's rule: if you have a full-time dedicated engineer who wants to build GTM agents internally, it can work; otherwise, buy from vendors.
- Sponsor of Lenny's Podcast.
