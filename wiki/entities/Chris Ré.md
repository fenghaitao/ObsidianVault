## Definition

Chris Ré (transcribed in the episode as "Chris Ray") is a Stanford professor whose lab Nimit Sohoni and the founders of Cartesia were part of.

## Key Information

- Nimit said the Cartesia founders "were actually all in Chris Ré's lab with me" during his Stanford PhD.
- This lab connection is what gave Nimit familiarity and trust in the Cartesia founding team before joining.
