## Definition

Sei Network is the crypto project Jay Jo co-founded after leaving Robinhood, building decentralized trading infrastructure on-chain.

## Key Information

- Its token grew to a market cap over $10 billion
- Origin: inspired by Robinhood's GameStop halt — a vision of "Robinhood, but decentralized" that avoids centralized settlement frictions
- Initially built a centralized limit order book exchange as an application, then pivoted to building the underlying infrastructure
- Raised $35M total ($5M seed + $30M strategic rounds)
- Team grew from ~5–7 people in 2022 to ~60 across entities, deliberately lean to preserve culture and speed
- Cites crypto's verifiability benefit and stablecoin rails (USDC/USDT) as the path toward a globally accessible "decentralized Wall Street"
