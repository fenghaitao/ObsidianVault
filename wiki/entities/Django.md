## Definition

Django is a Python web framework co-created by Simon Wilson. It powers major platforms including Instagram, Pinterest, Spotify, and thousands of other websites.

## Key Information

- Co-created by Simon Wilson, who is described as a "10x engineer for over 20 years"
- Powers Instagram, Pinterest, Spotify, and thousands of other platforms
- One of the most widely used Python web frameworks in the world
