## Definition

Think Big Analytics was a startup company where Zach Wilson began his data career as a data analyst. The company taught him Hadoop, which became a critical skill that helped him get hired at Facebook.

## Key Information

- Zach started his career at Think Big Analytics as a data analyst after pivoting from his dream of being a math professor
- The company taught him Hadoop around 2015, when Hadoop was still in its early adoption phase
- Zach credits this experience as the main reason he got hired at Facebook, since Facebook was looking for Big Data Technologies skills at the time
- At Think Big Analytics, Zach was using LinkedIn as early as 2015, which later became critical for his career — a Facebook recruiter reached out to him through LinkedIn
