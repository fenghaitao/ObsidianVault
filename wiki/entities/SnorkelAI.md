## Definition
Snorkel AI is a frontier AI data lab that builds datasets and environments to define and advance future AI capabilities. The company operates at the intersection of academic frontier research and real enterprise deployments.

## Key Information
- Focus: building the best datasets and environments to define and advance future AI capabilities
- Co-founders have academic labs at Stanford, UW (University of Washington), and Wisconsin
- Internal team includes deployed engineers and applied engineers
- Exposure to both academic frontier work (frontier labs) and real enterprise deployments (finance, insurance, healthcare)
- Launched the [[OpenBenchmarksGrant]]: $3M committed to funding open benchmarks
- Believes benchmarks are critical for shaping the field, not just measuring progress
