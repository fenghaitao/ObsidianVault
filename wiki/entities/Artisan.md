## Definition

Artisan is a Y Combinator-backed AI outbound sales platform that automates email-based SDR workflows. It went from near-zero to ~$10M in revenue in one year.

## Key Information

- YC-backed company that grew from near-zero to ~$10M annual revenue in one year
- Used by SaaStr for outbound email campaigns — sent ~60,000 emails with high response rates
- SaaStr chose Artisan because they were a sponsor at SaaStr Annual and offered the most help with deployment
- Key insight: Artisan was willing to do the work (training, onboarding) when other vendors demanded $100K upfront or were scared of bad PR if the deployment failed
- Jason Lemkin's vendor selection advice: the vendor's willingness to help with training and deployment matters more than raw feature comparisons
- All AI GTM agents are "more similar than different" under the hood (running on Cloud 4, mashing APIs together); the FDE support is the differentiator
