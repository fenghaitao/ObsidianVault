## Definition

Artisan is a Y Combinator-backed AI outbound-sales agent company ("Arty" is [[SaaStr]]'s nickname for its instance) that [[Jason Lemkin]] picked as SaaStr's first outbound-automation vendor, in part because it was a SaaStr sponsor and, critically, because it agreed to provide hands-on implementation help when other vendors wouldn't.

## Key Information

- Growth: went from "like nothing to $10 million" in revenue within a year, per Lemkin.
- Selection story: Lemkin didn't yet know if Artisan was the best tool, but chose it because it offered to help the most — one competing vendor demanded $100K upfront before providing help, and another declined to be SaaStr's first customer for fear of bad PR if the deployment failed.
- Deployment: used for outbound to SaaStr's ~400,000-contact database (re-engaging past attendees for SaaStr Annual); after training, SaaStr sent about 60,000 emails via Artisan with "pretty high" response rates.
- Cited as a case where "if you have nothing" (no existing automated outbound motion), even a modest, well-trained agentic tool delivers high ROI simply because it's an incremental win over doing nothing.
