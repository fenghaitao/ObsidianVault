## Definition

Cox Automotive is the world's largest automotive services and technology provider. It integrated Claude across its dealer-network products to personalize the car-buying experience.

## Key Information

- Integrated Claude across **VinSolutions CRM**, **Autotrader PSX**, and **Dealer.com**, selecting Claude for performance on latency, cost, and accuracy.
- Uses Claude Sonnet for complex tasks requiring deeper comprehension and Claude Haiku for high-volume, rapid-response scenarios.
- **Impact**: consumer lead responses and test-drive appointments more than doubled via VinSolutions CRM; AI-generated vehicle listings achieved 80% positive feedback from sellers; managed services platform compressed dealer website content creation from weeks to same-day, generating over 9,000 client deliverables to date.
- Quote: *"Claude consistently ranks among the most advanced generative AI models available, and continues to push innovation forward with each update."* — Ben Flusberg, Chief Data Officer.
