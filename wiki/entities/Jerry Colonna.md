## Definition

Jerry Colonna is an executive coach and prior [[Lenny's Podcast]] guest, cited by [[Lenny Rachitsky]] for a formative line: "how are you complicit in creating the conditions you claim you don't want?"

## Key Information

- [[Rachel Lockett]] connects this directly to her own advice that entering any conflict with humility means first identifying your own contribution to the dynamic, rather than assuming the other person is entirely at fault.
