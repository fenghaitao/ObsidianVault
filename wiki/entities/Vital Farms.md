## Definition

Pasture-raised/organic egg brand ("vital eggs" in the transcript) discussed by [[Lenny Rachitsky]] and [[Eric Ries]] as a viral real-world example of quality perceptibly declining after a change in ownership/investor pressure.

## Key Information

- Lenny recounts a viral social-media (Twitter/TikTok) complaint that Vital Farms eggs got noticeably worse in quality and showed elevated toxin levels, coinciding with reports that the company had come to be owned by BlackRock; Ries had not previously heard this specific example and calls it his "new favorite example."
- Neither Lenny nor Ries claims certainty about the underlying toxin allegations specifically ("I'm not exactly sure if the toxins are real... I haven't looked into it super deep"), but both cite the broader pattern (a beloved brand perceptibly degrading after a change in financial ownership) as extremely common — Ries connects it to a related, unnamed story about a natural-foods founder ousted by investors in pursuit of higher growth/margins, after which quality fell, customers and employees became unhappy, and market share began shrinking.
- Used to introduce the "restaurant taken over by private equity — you can taste it" anecdote and the idea that "the more golden the goose, the greater the temptation to butcher."
