## Definition

HTC is the phone manufacturer Facebook partnered with in the early-2010s plan to build a Facebook phone by forking Android.

## Key Information

- Facebook's doomed "this time it's really going to happen" phone project involved partnering with HTC to fork Android and ship an HTC-branded device.
- The Facebook phone project had a hard deadline unlike almost every other project — code had to be burned into HTC's phones by roughly March 1st, adding urgency to the bad build system that motivated Michael Bolin's Buck.
