## Definition
Prime RL is a training framework by Prime Intellect that integrates with Verifiers for reinforcement learning training of LLMs. It supports supervised fine-tuning and RL training with algorithms like CISPO.

## Key Information
- Integrates with Verifiers for RL environment-based training
- Supports SFT and RL training runs
- Used in the tic-tac-toe experiment to fine-tune LFM-2 with synthetic data and then train via CISPO
- Includes a simple trainer (Verifiers RL) for RL training
- Wiki search environment provided as a starting example in the Prime RL repository
