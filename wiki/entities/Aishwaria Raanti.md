## Definition

Aishwaria Raanti (Ash) is an AI researcher and product leader who was an early AI researcher at Alexa and Microsoft, has published over 35 research papers, and has led/supported over 50 AI product deployments across companies like Amazon, Data Bricks, OpenAI, and Google. She co-teaches the #1 rated AI course on Maven with Kiti Bottom.

## Key Information

- Early AI researcher at Alexa (Amazon) and Microsoft
- Published over 35 research papers
- Co-creator of the Continuous Calibration Continuous Development (CCCD) framework for building AI products
- Co-teaches the #1 rated AI course on [[Maven]] with [[Kiti Bottom]], teaching product leaders how to build successful AI products
- Has consulted for enterprises on AI transformation, training, and strategy
- Worked with the CEO of [[Rackspace]] (Gajen) as part of his AI advisory group
- Argues that building is cheap today; design, judgment, and taste are the real differentiators
- Believes multimodal experiences will be a major trend in 2026
- Married to [[Kiti Bottom]] for 4 years
- Favorite book: [[When Breath Becomes Air]] by [[Paul Kalaniti]]
- Life motto: "They told it couldn't be done, but the fool didn't know it, so he did it anyway" (from her father)
