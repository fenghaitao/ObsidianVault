## Definition
Sholto Douglas is a Google DeepMind engineer (transcribed "Sholto") whom Vlad Feinberg consulted about pipelining early in the Gemini Flash work.
## Key Information
- In an early conversation, Sholto argued dense models were "so flop bound" that pipelining would not change the prefill profile; Vlad tested it, confirmed he was right, and abandoned the idea at the time.
- The insight resurfaced later for mixture-of-experts models, where pipeline prefill became the key serving innovation for Flash 2.0.
