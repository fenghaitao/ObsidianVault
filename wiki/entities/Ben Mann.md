## Definition

Ben Mann is a co-founder of Anthropic. He nudged Boris Cherny to create a DAU chart for Claude Code early on, helped kick off the Anthropic Labs team, and was instrumental in Boris's decision to join Anthropic.

## Key Information

- Co-founder of Anthropic
- Suggested topics for this episode of Lenny's Podcast
- Nudged Boris to create a DAU (daily active users) chart for Claude Code early on, before Boris felt it was ready
- Kicked off the Anthropic Labs team (with Mike Krieger) for "round two"
- Was a big part of why Boris ended up at Anthropic
