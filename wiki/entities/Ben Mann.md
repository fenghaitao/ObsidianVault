## Definition

Co-founder of [[Anthropic]] and a prior [[Lenny's Podcast]] guest; discussed Claude's character/constitution as central to the product's success (referenced in episode 13), and (per [[Amol]], episode 16) articulated why Anthropic continues building powerful AI despite believing it's dangerous — better to be in the game shaping outcomes ("driving the race to the top") than to abstain and have no influence.

## Key Information

- Wrote (or is closely associated with) an internal 2021 document — written just months after Anthropic's founding, years before AI coding tools' commercial potential was obvious — arguing the company should focus deeply on AI coding, a bet Amol credits with much of Anthropic's later success. (Referenced in the transcript via a garbled name possibly rendering as "Dan Mad.")
- Cited by [[Cat Wu]] (episode 13) on the importance of Claude's character/personality to the product's success — not a superficial detail, but a major driver of user attachment.
- Cited by Amol (episode 16) for the "stay in the game to have influence" argument underlying Anthropic's decision to keep building frontier AI despite safety concerns, rather than abstain from the field entirely.
