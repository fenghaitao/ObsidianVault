## Definition

Apache Spark is the data-processing engine created by Matei Zaharia; Databricks orchestrates up to roughly a million Spark jobs at a time.

## Key Information

- Created by Matei Zaharia (a Stanford faculty member and Databricks co-founder).
- Scheduling ~a million Spark jobs at Databricks — where OS schedulers didn't scale — motivated putting scheduling data in Postgres, the seed of the DBOS idea.
