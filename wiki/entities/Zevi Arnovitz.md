## Definition

Zevi Arnovitz (also spelled Zevy Arnowitz) is a Product Manager at Meta, previously a PM at Wix. He has zero technical background (studied music in high school, no tech unit in the Israeli army) but has developed a sophisticated AI-powered workflow for building and shipping products using Cursor, Claude Code, and multiple LLMs.

## Key Information

- PM at Meta; previously PM at Wix in the editor (core product) team
- No technical background — studied music, no coding experience
- Started building with AI after watching YouTube videos about Bolt and Lovable when Sonnet 3.5 launched, while traveling in Japan
- Built StudyMate, a side-project app for students to create interactive quizzes from study materials, entirely with AI tools
- Also built the Vortex, a dictation-related product for the Israeli market
- Created a structured slash commands workflow: create issue → exploration → create plan → execute → review → peer review → update docs
- Pioneered multi-model peer review: has Claude, Codex, and Gemini review the same code and debate findings
- Uses AI for interview prep: built a Claude project as a Meta interview coach, created a Base44 quiz game for segmentation practice
- Described by Tal Rav as "the most hands-on vibe coding PM I know"
- His engineers at Meta ask him to teach them his AI workflow
- Advocates for gradual exposure to code: GPT Projects → Bolt/Lovable → Cursor light mode → full terminal
- Philosophy: "If people walk away thinking how amazing you are, you failed. If people walk away and open their computer and start building, you've succeeded."
- Favorite books: The Fountainhead (Ayn Rand), Shoe Dog (Nike story), Mindset (Carol Dweck)
- Favorite shows: The Pit, Severance
- Life mottos: "You can just do things" and "Nobody knows what the fuck they're doing"
