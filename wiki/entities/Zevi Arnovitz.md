## Definition

PM at [[Meta]] (previously at [[Wix]]) with zero technical background who, over roughly a year, taught himself to build and ship real production software — including his own side business, [[Studymate]] — entirely by directing AI coding agents rather than writing code. His episode lays out his personal [[Slash Command Development Workflow]] and [[CTO Persona Pattern]].

## Key Information

- Non-technical background: did music in high school, no coding education, did not serve in an Israeli army tech unit. Started building with AI after watching a YouTube video (by Greg Isenberg or Riley Brown) about Bolt/Lovable while traveling in Japan around the time Claude 3.5 Sonnet released — he describes running straight home and opening Bolt without unpacking his bags.
- Graduated tools over roughly a year as his needs grew: ChatGPT projects (conversational only) → Bolt/Lovable (opinionated, eager-to-code "vibe coding" builders) → Cursor with Claude Code (full control, no guardrails) — see [[Exposure Therapy Onboarding]].
- Built and monetized [[Studymate]], a platform where students upload study materials and get AI-generated interactive quizzes (via Gemini), entirely solo as a "company of one," including localizing it from Hebrew to English in two days and standing up a personal site from no domain to live in 90 minutes — experiences he calls [[Time Machine Moments]].
- Introduced to [[Lenny Rachitsky]] by [[Tal Riv]], who described him as "the most hands-on vibe coding PM I know," noting his own engineers at Meta ask him to teach them his workflow.
- Used AI extensively to prepare for his Meta PM interview: a [[Claude]] "coach" project instructed to give unvarnished feedback, a custom Base 44 quiz app to drill product-segmentation questions, and Perplexity's Comet browser to analyze a public interview-question bank for prioritization — while still rating live human mock interviews as the single most valuable prep input. Cites [[Ben Arez]]'s guest-post prompts as a starting resource.
- Rejects the "outsourcing your thinking" critique of AI-assisted PM work: frames the PM job as harnessing whatever gets the team fastest to the right solution for users, not performing visible unaided effort, and argues junior PMs specifically benefit by using AI to reason at a level of scope (company strategy, marketing) above their formal seniority.
- Failure Corner: bombed his first product review at Wix by trying to work alone and impress four senior PMs, then realized the actual expectation of him was to be [[10x Learner Vs 10x Doer|"a 10x learner," not a 10x doer]] — afterward deliberately mapping each teammate's individual strength and using them as targeted mentors.
- Believes titles and responsibilities are collapsing as everyone becomes a builder, and argues — contrary to "no junior roles left" narratives — that this is actually the best time in history to be a junior, since curious, hardworking, kind communicators now have outsized leverage versus raw years of experience.
- Side anecdote: cites his brother, an entrepreneur serving elderly clients on tech adoption, as independently replacing paid tools (Zapier, Airtable) with a self-built CRM/automation system using the same AI-agent approach.
- Entrepreneurial background predates AI: ran a thermal-clothing resale business in high school in Jerusalem (negotiated directly with an importer, wrote a promotional basketball-game chant with his phone number in it that people still remember by tune).
- Lightning round: recommends *[[The Fountainhead]]* (Ayn Rand), *[[Shoe Dog]]* (Phil Knight), *[[Mindset (book)|Mindset]]* (Carol Dweck); loves *[[Severance]]* and *The Pit*; recently discovered [[CAP]] (Loom alternative); mottos "you can just do things" and his brother's "nobody knows what they're doing."
