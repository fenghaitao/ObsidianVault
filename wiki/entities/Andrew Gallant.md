## Definition
Andrew Gallant (handle "burntsushi", garbled in the transcript as "burnt sushi") is an engineer at Astral and the author of ripgrep.

## Key Information
- Author of ripgrep (transcript "Rip Grap") "and a bunch of other things"; Charlie calls him "a really amazing engineer."
- Crafted a celebrated uv optimization: representing roughly 90%+ of package version numbers within a single u64 integer, eliminating expensive per-version allocations during resolution — "one of the coolest PRs I've read."
