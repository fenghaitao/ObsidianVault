## Definition

Hillary Gridley is credited by Claire Vo for the concept of "ramble mode" or the "Yappers API" — the idea that the highest bandwidth API for an LLM is simply chatting to it, rambling about what you need rather than trying to structure everything perfectly.

## Key Information

- Originator of the "ramble mode" / "Yappers API" concept
- "The highest bandwidth API for an LLM is just chatting to it"
- Advocated for using voice notes to onboard AI agents: just ramble about who you are and what you need, and the agent makes sense of it
- Also big on how much of the manager skill set translates to AI and agent management
