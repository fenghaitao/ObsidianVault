## Definition

Sentry is an error tracking and application monitoring platform. Its AI debugging agent, Seir, uses Sentry context to identify root causes, suggest fixes, and even open PRs. It was a sponsor of this episode.

## Key Information

- Error tracking and application monitoring platform
- Seir is Sentry's AI debugging agent that uses context to tell you the root cause, suggest a fix, and open a PR
- Seir also reviews PRs and flags breaking changes with fixes ready to go
- Sponsor of Lenny's Podcast episode 26
- Website: sentry.io/lenny
