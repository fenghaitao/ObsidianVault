## Definition

Jason Cohen is a four-time founder and entrepreneur who built two unicorns, including WP Engine (WordPress hosting). He is also a prolific writer who has been blogging at asmartbear.com for nearly 20 years, and is the author of the book Hidden Multipliers.

## Key Information

- Four-time founder, including two unicorns; one was bootstrapped, one was VC-funded
- Founded WP Engine, a WordPress hosting company
- Co-founded Smart Bear (the blog and company)
- Has invested in about 60 startups
- Has been writing at asmartbear.com for almost 20 years, with ~300-350 posts total (150-200 he's proud of)
- Writing philosophy: "I will only put out stuff if it's the best that I can do" — prioritizes quality over consistency
- Co-won Ernst & Young Entrepreneur of the Year 2017 for Central Texas with Heather Brunner, CEO of WP Engine
- Author of "Hidden Multipliers" (book): small changes with outsized impact on revenue and profit
- Known for his stalled growth diagnostic framework, elephant curve concept, and pragmatic approach to SaaS growth mechanics
- On Twitter and at asmartbear.com; all articles are free with no ads or courses

## Notable Frameworks

- **Stalled Growth Diagnostic Sequence**: (1) logo churn, (2) pricing/positioning, (3) NRR, (4) channel saturation, (5) do you need to grow?
- **Elephant Curve**: Marketing channels are not S-curves; they sag after saturation
- **Cancellation Math**: max customers = new customers/month ÷ cancellation rate
- **"What made you cancel?"** vs "Why did you cancel?" — phrasing matters for usable responses
- **Pricing selects the market**: raising prices can increase signups by entering a different market segment
