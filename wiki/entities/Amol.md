## Definition

Head of growth at [[Anthropic]] (surname transcribed variously as "Avaisary"/"Ezzat Sary"), a [[Lenny's Podcast]] guest in his own right (episode 16) and cited by [[Lenny Rachitsky]] in the [[Cat Wu]] (ep13) and [[Nikhyl Singhal]] (ep14) conversations for his contrarian view on the future of the PM role. Previously led growth at [[Mercury]] and [[MasterClass]]; before that, a founder and investment banker. Got the Anthropic job by cold-emailing [[Mike Krieger]] (then CPO) — the only PM Krieger has ever hired via cold email.

## Key Information

- His argument (per episodes 13/14): as engineers ship dramatically faster with AI, PMs and designers get squeezed trying to keep up with the volume of features/ideas/docs — so the industry needs *more* PMs, not fewer, doing higher-leverage staying-on-top-of-everything work rather than personally shipping code. [[Nikhyl Singhal]] refines this: the valuable PM leverage isn't building features alongside engineers, but building internal tools/systems that track and evaluate the fast-growing volume of AI-driven changes. See [[Builders Vs Information Movers]].
- Anthropic's growth: ~$1B ARR at start of 2025 to ~$19B ARR by ~14 months later (10x year-over-year for three straight years), despite starting as the least-funded, no-distribution, non-first-mover player in the space (behind Meta/Google's distribution and OpenAI's head start). Attributes this partly to deep focus (on coding/B2B) driven by leadership/Dario Amodei, and partly to sheer necessity — a resource-constrained company had to pick a narrow bet. See [[Freedom Through Constraints]].
- Describes growth work at Anthropic as ~70% "success disasters" (firefighting problems caused by extreme scaling success) and ~30% proactive bread-and-butter growth work — see [[Success Disasters]].
- Growth org is ~40 people, structured into horizontals (growth platform, monetization) plus audience-focused pods (B2B, Claude Code growth, knowledge-worker growth, API growth); unusually weighted toward large bets (50/50 or more) rather than the typical growth-team split (60-70% small/medium optimizations, 20-30% large bets) — justified by Anthropic's belief that AI-driven product value compounds exponentially (100-1000x in 2 years) rather than linearly (30-50% for a typical non-AI product). See [[Exponential Thinking]].
- Leads "[[CASE (Claude Accelerates Sustainable Hypergrowth)|CASE]]" (internally acknowledged as an awkward acronym), an initiative to automate growth experimentation itself using Claude — evaluated across four stages (identifying opportunities, building, testing/brand-quality-checking, analyzing results) with a human in the loop; only became viable with Opus 4.5/4.6. Currently performs at roughly a "junior PM" level.
- Central activation philosophy: "the right friction helps" — well-placed friction (e.g., onboarding questions that let a product recommend the right features) reliably out-performs naive "remove all friction" approaches, tested repeatedly across Mercury, MasterClass, and Anthropic. See [[Good Friction]].
- Instituted a "two-week rule" for PM involvement: if a project is under two engineering-weeks, the engineer is on the hook to act as their own mini-PM (including cross-functional coordination); above two weeks, a PM stays accountable. Product-minded engineers who can self-PM become disproportionately valuable under this model. See [[Two-Week PM Rule]].
- Averse to PRDs — estimates 60-80% of what his team ships has no PRD at all; favors Slack threads and prototyping over documentation, reserving real PRDs (via a personal Claude skill trained on past examples) for the ~20-30% of higher-stakes projects.
- Uses Claude/Cowork extensively for personal workflows: a scheduled agent that reviews ~20-25 dashboards each morning and flags what needs attention; automated meeting-room booking, inbox triage, and expense/reimbursement filing via Brex; a weekly "misalignment scan" across Slack; and a "soft coaching" use case where Claude, fed a manager's public writing plus internal Slack history, generates feedback on what that manager might say about Amol's own week. See [[Notebook Channels]] for the related internal-transparency practice this data comes from.
- On AI safety and growth trade-offs: frames every controversial growth test as either "in the safety/values red line" (don't even test it) or "uncomfortable but testable" (run it, but expect a higher bar for real impact before shipping) — and states Anthropic (as a [[Public Benefit Corporation]]) has repeatedly taken commercial hits for safety reasons, including not releasing an early Claude chatbot before ChatGPT launched.
- Personal story: founded and ran a mental-health-quantification startup for 3 years (7-10 employees, several million raised) before shutting it down — credits that failure with teaching him the PM/cold-email/growth skills that built his later career. Separately survived a severe traumatic brain injury (from a martial-arts sparring session) requiring 9 months of recovery (including relearning to walk) and a re-injury a month into his next job; credits meditation, regular breaks, and "freedom through constraints" thinking with helping him manage the ongoing effects.
- Lightning round: recommends *Joy of Living* (Yongey Mingyur Rinpoche), *Awareness* (Anthony de Mello), and *Thinking in Bets* (Annie Duke); enjoyed the movie *Marty Supreme*; loves a Japanese bead-filled travel pillow (Maruhachi Shinsui Pro Pillow); life mottos "she'll be right" and "just go for it" (the latter from [[Eros Resmini]], former Discord CMO and a mentor); is a Michigan Wolverines/49ers football fan.
