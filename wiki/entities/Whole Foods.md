## Definition

Grocery chain referenced by [[Eric Ries]] in *Incorruptible* as one of the grim case studies (alongside Vectura) that founders need to study and grapple with in order to understand how successful, mission-driven companies can still lose control of their own values.

## Key Information

- Cited briefly, in the same breath as Vectura, as a story founders should know before assuming this kind of corporate value-erosion "won't happen to them" — Ries doesn't elaborate on the specifics of "what happened to it" in this episode but treats it as a well-known grim case in the book.
