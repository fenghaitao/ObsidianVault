## Definition
The AI Engineering Code Summit is a single-track, intentionally intimate conference event focused on AI coding, held in New York in January 2026. It is part of the AI Engineering Conference brand, which also includes a larger World's Fair format.

## Key Information
- Held in New York in early January 2026 (2025 AI Engineering Code Summit)
- Intentionally smaller and single-track, designed to bring the best people together around a single theme
- Theme: AI coding — the patterns, systems, and products that make AI-assisted software development possible
- Featured a leadership track the previous day on how AI is transforming software organizations
- Presenting sponsor: Google DeepMind; Platinum sponsor: Anthropic; plus gold and silver sponsors with expo booths
- MC: Jed Borovik from Google DeepMind
- Part of the [[AIEngineeringConference]] brand, which has two formats: World's Fair (large) and Summit (small, focused)
