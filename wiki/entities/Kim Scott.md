## Definition

Author of *[[Radical Candor]]*; [[Jacob Warwick]] credits her book (alongside his wife's influence) with giving him the confidence to be more assertive in his career — one of the most frequently mentioned books on [[Lenny's Podcast]], second only to *High Output Management*.

## Key Information (episode 28 — Dr. Becky Kennedy)

- [[Lenny Rachitsky]] cites a story he recalls from Kim Scott (from *Radical Candor*) about someone she managed who was doing a bad job; she avoided giving him direct feedback to spare his feelings, and nine months later had to fire him — he then asked why she never told him along the way he wasn't meeting expectations. [[Dr. Becky Kennedy]] uses this as the connecting example for her [[Resilience Over Happiness]] framework: withholding hard feedback to avoid short-term discomfort causes greater harm long-term.
