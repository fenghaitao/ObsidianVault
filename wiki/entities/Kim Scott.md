## Definition

Kim Scott is the author of "Radical Candor," a management philosophy that advocates challenging directly while caring personally. Lenny references her in the context of connecting before correcting and giving honest feedback.

## Key Information

- Author of Radical Candor
- Lenny references a story from Kim Scott: she had an employee who was doing a bad job, didn't give feedback for 9 months because she didn't want to upset him, then had to fire him — the employee asked "Why didn't you tell me I wasn't doing what you wanted?"
- The story illustrates the parallel to parenting: avoiding short-term discomfort (upsetting someone) leads to worse long-term outcomes
- Connects to Dr. Becky's concept of [[Resilience over Happiness]] — optimizing for comfort builds fragility

- Jacob Warwick credits Radical Candor with giving him the confidence to be assertive in ways he never had been before: "My wife taught me that. Kim Scott embraced it in the corporate world and like those two pieces like really helped elevate my career."
- Radical Candor is the second most mentioned book on Lenny's Podcast (behind High Output Management)
