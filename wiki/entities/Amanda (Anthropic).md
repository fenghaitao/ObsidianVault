## Definition

Amanda is the person at Anthropic who molds Claude's character — defining who Claude should be, how it should interact, and what personality traits it should embody. Cat Wu describes this as an incredibly hard role because the task is so ambiguous.

## Key Information

- Molds Claude's character at Anthropic
- Role is described as "incredibly hard" because the task is so ambiguous — "even coding is easier because you can verify the success"
- Requires a very strong sense of conviction in who Claude should be
- Has an "incredible ability to not only mold the character, but also to articulate what the goals are, what's successful and what's not"
- Cat Wu trusts Amanda's judgment on model quality — she's one of the "handful of people who are much better than others at articulating what makes a specific model or model-harness combination good"
