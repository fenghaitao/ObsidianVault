## Definition

Smart Bear is both a company founded by Jason Cohen and the name of his blog at asmartbear.com, where he has been writing in-depth articles on startups, SaaS, and product strategy for nearly 20 years.

## Key Information

- Blog at asmartbear.com has been running for almost 20 years
- ~300-350 total posts, with 150-200 Jason is proud of
- Publishing philosophy: quality over consistency — "I will only put out stuff if it's the best that I can do"
- Some years only 1-2 posts; other years up to 40
- All articles are free, no ads, no courses, nothing commercial — the only paid product is the book Hidden Multipliers
- Jason also used Smart Bear as a company name; people would cancel and he tested cancellation survey design there
- Known for articles on: elephant curve, stalled growth, pricing, cancellation math, AB testing pitfalls
