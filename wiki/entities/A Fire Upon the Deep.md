## Definition

A Fire Upon the Deep is a science fiction novel by Vernon Vinge, recommended by Lenny as essential reading for AI-interested sci-fi fans. It explores themes of AGI, superintelligence, and galactic-scale civilizations.

## Key Information

- Written by Vernon Vinge
- Recommended by Lenny Rachitsky on the podcast, originally discovered via Noah Smith's newsletter
- Described as "incredible" and "so epic"
- Themes include AGI and superintelligence
- Has sequels but the first book is the standout
- Lenny notes: "no one's heard of it" despite its quality
- Boris Cherny also recommends it; notes "It's so interesting from a AI AGI perspective"
- Boris also recommends the sequel A Deepness in the Sky, which he thinks is the greater of the two
