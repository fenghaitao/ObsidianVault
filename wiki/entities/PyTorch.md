## Definition

PyTorch is a machine-learning framework and a Meta engineering org where John Myles White was a director of engineering. In the episode he contrasts its craft-focused culture with the promotion-driven culture of Meta's AI infra.

## Key Information

- John Myles White was a director of engineering on PyTorch and MSL.
- PyTorch was one of the few Meta teams where "people were just genuinely in it for the love of the craft... people loved engineering as engineering."
- It held a higher promotion bar: "our eights are like as good as you're going to get anywhere in this whole world... our eights probably are better than the tens in a couple of other teams."
- That high bar created a perception that PyTorch people were under-leveled ("he's a five, but really he's like a seven"), which slowed promotions but made external teams trust that PyTorch people were credible at their level.
- Many people wanted to join PyTorch for its craft-focused culture; hiring friction came from the perception of a tougher promotion bar.
