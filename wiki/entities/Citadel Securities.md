## Definition

Citadel Securities is the market-making/securities firm (part of the Citadel family) where Nimit Sohoni worked as a quantitative researcher after his Stanford PhD; he refers to it interchangeably as "Citadel."

## Key Information

- Nimit joined Citadel Securities after interning there the summer before graduating with his PhD; friends who had interned at Citadel Securities and other quant firms encouraged his curiosity.
- He described having "a pretty great work-life balance" as a quant there, attributing it to trading hours: US traders work around US trading hours, and that culture trickles down — people cluster around market hours and don't take work home.
- He described Citadel as a "quant forward" firm, contrasting with Jane Street, where very few people are formally called quants because traders implement a lot themselves.
- Quant work involved heavy coding, mostly C++ with some Python.
- Citadel is listed among the well-regarded larger quant firms with great technical talent and returns.
