## Definition

Trustable is a company focused on AI governance and compliance, helping organizations keep track of emerging AI legislation and regulations.

## Key Information

- Sander Schulhoff knows the founder and spoke with them about AI legislation before ChatGPT was released.
- Schulhoff highlights compliance and governance as a valuable space in AI security, as opposed to guardrail products.
- The company helps organizations navigate the growing body of AI legislation around how AI can and cannot be used.
- Represents the governance/compliance segment of the AI security industry, which Schulhoff considers legitimately useful.
