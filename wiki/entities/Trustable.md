## Definition

Trustable is an AI governance and compliance company, cited by [[Sander Schulhoff]] as doing good work helping companies keep up with fast-evolving AI legislation.

## Key Information

- Schulhoff knows the founder personally and recalls speaking with him even before ChatGPT's release, when the scope of coming AI legislation wasn't yet clear.
- Positioned by Schulhoff as an example of the governance/compliance segment of the AI security market, which he considers genuinely valuable — in contrast to guardrail and automated-red-teaming vendors, which he argues largely are not ([[Guardrails Do Not Work]]).
