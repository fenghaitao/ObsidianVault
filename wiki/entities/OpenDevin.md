## Definition
OpenDevin was the original name of the OpenHands project, launched in early 2024 as an open-source alternative to Cognition's Devin autonomous coding agent. It was later rebranded to OpenHands.

## Key Information
- Launched the day after Devin's demo video in early 2024
- Created as a community-driven, open-source alternative to proprietary autonomous coding agents
- Motivated by the belief that the future of AI-powered software engineering should be shaped by the community, not a black box
- Later rebranded to OpenHands
