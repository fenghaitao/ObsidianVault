# Mardu Swanepoel

## Definition

Mardu Swanepoel is the founder of [[FlinnAI]] and a speaker at [[aiDotEngineer]]. He presented "What the Best Agents Share" (2026-05-26), distilling four design patterns shared by the best AI agents: focus modes, transparent execution, personalization, and reversibility.

## Key Information

- **Organization:** [[FlinnAI]]
- **Conference:** [[aiDotEngineer]], Channel Only playlist
- **Talk Date:** 2026-05-26
- **Talk Title:** "What the Best Agents Share"
- **Key Contribution:** Identified four cross-cutting design patterns from studying [[Cursor]], [[ClaudeCode]], [[Harvey]], and [[Manifold]] that agent builders can adopt.
- **Notable Quote:** Opens the talk with Picasso's quote on stealing — deeply studying something, making it your own, and using that to create something better.
