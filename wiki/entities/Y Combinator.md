## Definition

Y Combinator (YC) is a startup accelerator. Sam Lessin taught an etiquette class specifically for YC founders, hosted at the Four Seasons, after Garry Tan got mad about it.

## Key Information

- Sam Lessin taught an etiquette class for YC founders, including certificates of completion
- The class was partly tongue-in-cheek, making fun of the fact that YC founders "come out a little bit like animals" — having been holed up coding for months
- Garry Tan (YC president) was "very mad" about the class, which motivated Sam to do it more
- [[Qasar Younis]] served as COO under president [[Sam Altman]]
- Qasar asked Sam Altman not to announce his COO role for a year — to test whether it would work before facing public scrutiny
- Qasar wore his "YC hat" seeing hundreds/thousands of startups, forming his views on early traction and founder success
