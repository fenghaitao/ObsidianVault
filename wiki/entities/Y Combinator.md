## Definition

Y Combinator (YC) is a startup accelerator that provides seed funding, mentorship, and networking. Rahul Pandey and his co-founder Alex applied to YC and went through the program to fund Taro.

## Key Information

- Rahul and Alex applied to YC to fund Taro after leaving Meta
- Rahul described YC as a no-brainer for first-time founders without an existing network — it provides credibility, money, and accountability
- For repeat founders or those with existing VC networks, YC may not be worth it
- The 2-3 month YC period was described as very intense: weekly group check-ins, pressure to perform, and the "shame" of not delivering
- Rahul learned a lot during the YC period because of the intense pressure and accountability
- YC makes sense if you have ambition to build a huge company, not if you just want to build small apps that generate modest income
