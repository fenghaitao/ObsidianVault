## Definition

Startup accelerator where [[Qasar Younis]] served as COO under president [[Sam Altman]]. Younis asked Altman not to announce his appointment for about a year, reasoning that if the partners ultimately didn't want him as COO, there would be no public scrutiny over a short, failed tenure.

## Key Information

- Younis draws on his YC experience (seeing "hundreds and thousands of companies") for his founder advice, particularly the "hard reset" heuristic for stalled startups. See [[Founder Reset Heuristic]].
- Per [[Sam Lesson]] (episode 32): [[Slow Ventures]] ran an in-person etiquette class for YC founders at the Four Seasons — certificates of completion, wardrobe consultants, caviar/wine tasting, plus core low-heart-rate/eye-contact/handshake material — done partly "tongue-in-cheek" because YC president [[Garry Tan]] reportedly "got very mad about this," which Lesson says only motivated him to formalize the class further (eventually into a book).
