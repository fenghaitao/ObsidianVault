## Definition

The Abel Prize is an international mathematics prize awarded annually by the Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters, one of the highest distinctions in mathematics.

## Key Information

- Avi Wigderson is introduced in the episode as a "Turing award and Abel Prize winner," marking him as honored across both computer science and mathematics.
- Winning both prizes underscores Wigderson's point that theoretical computer science lives in both mathematics and computer science.
