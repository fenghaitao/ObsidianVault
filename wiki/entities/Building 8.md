## Definition

Building 8 was Meta's hardware division that developed the Portal video calling device. It was later rebranded as Portal. Rahul Pandey joined Building 8 when he first arrived at Meta.

## Key Information

- The original name of Meta's hardware division that developed Portal
- Later rebranded or renamed to Portal
- Rahul joined this relatively unique part of Facebook when he started at Meta
- The division worked on a forked version of Android with custom hardware
