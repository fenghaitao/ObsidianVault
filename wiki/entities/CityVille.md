## Definition

Facebook city-building simulation game by [[Zynga]], alongside [[FarmVille]] one of the company's biggest hits by installs, engagement, and revenue, though it did not endure on mobile.

## Key Information

- Grouped with FarmVille as having Zynga's best installs/engagement/retention/revenue numbers among all its games.
- Did not make a durable transition to mobile the way [[Words With Friends]] and [[Zynga Poker]] did.
