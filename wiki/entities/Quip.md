## Definition

Quip was a productivity/collaborative-documents startup founded by [[Brett Taylor]]. [[Molly Graham]] joined a couple of months before launch and ran everything outside product and engineering; the company was later sold to [[Salesforce]].

## Key Information

- Graham joined right after leaving [[Facebook]] (post-IPO), specifically seeking the experience of "building something from nothing," which she describes as meaningfully different from the experience of scaling inside an already-fast-growing company.
- She credits Quip with teaching her the tools/skills needed to go "from 0 to 1 and then from 1 to 2," and how lonely building something from scratch can be compared to holding on inside a company already scaling around you.
- Eventually acquired by [[Salesforce]].
