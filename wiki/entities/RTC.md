## Definition

RTC (Real-Time Communication) is the video calling infrastructure team within Meta's Messenger organization. They maintained the shared infrastructure for cross-platform video calls that Rahul Pandey migrated Portal back to as part of his IC6 promotion project.

## Key Information

- Part of the Messenger organization at Meta
- Responsible for cross-platform video calling infrastructure (desktop, mobile Android, iOS, different codecs, audio/video codecs)
- Constantly making improvements to the video calling infrastructure
- The Portal team initially forked away from RTC's infrastructure, then needed to migrate back
- Rahul's migration project required navigating bureaucracy between the RTC team, Portal leadership, PMs, and getting everyone aligned
