## Definition

Ian Spalter (transcribed in the episode as "Ian Sber") is a designer (formerly Instagram's head of design) who, with Kevin Systrom, designed the vision for IGTV.

## Key Information

- Ryan Olson calls him "another amazing designer" who, together with Kevin Systrom, designed and laid out the IGTV long-form vertical video vision before its surprise end-of-year all-hands reveal.
