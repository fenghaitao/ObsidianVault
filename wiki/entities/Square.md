## Definition

Square (now Block) is a fintech company founded by Jack Dorsey. Rahul Pandey received a job offer from Square after his Facebook internship, alongside offers from Google, Facebook, Microsoft, and Twitter.

## Key Information

- Rahul received a job offer from Square as one of five big tech offers after his internship at Facebook in 2013
- The five offers were: Google, Facebook, Microsoft, Twitter, and Square
- This collection of offers gave Rahul confidence to take the risky startup path at Cos instead

### The Snapchat-director guest at Square
- Joined Square directly into a director offer (rather than being promoted into the role), leading a ~25-person org (grown to 50+ within a year) in machine learning and data-science infrastructure
- The smaller company was willing to give him the chance to perform at director level, and the experience taught him the difference between managing and directing
