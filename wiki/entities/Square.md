## Definition

Square is a financial services and mobile payment company co-founded by Jack Dorsey and Jim McKelvey. It provides payment hardware and software for businesses of all sizes.

## Key Information

- Keith Rabois was COO at Square starting September 2010
- Jack Dorsey ran the company from an iPad, which inspired Rabois to go computer-free
- The "smoothie test" story originated at Square: an intern (Taylor Francis) solved a logistics problem that the entire office team failed at, proving himself a "barrel"
- Square's board included Vinod Khosla, who taught Rabois "the team you build is the company you build"
- Two Square colleagues (Max Rhodes and Jeff Kolodny) went on to found Fair
- Now part of Block (formerly Square, Inc.)
