## Definition

Payments company where [[Keith Rabois]] served as COO, run by [[Jack Dorsey]], recruited from Rabois's post-Google "aqua-hire" period on the strength of Rabois being one of only a few people in the world who was both financial-services-literate and entrepreneurial.

## Key Information

- Dorsey ran the company primarily from an iPad, which led Rabois to convert to an all-iPad workflow in September 2010 and never touch a computer again.
- [[Vinod Khosla]] served on Rabois's board here and delivered the "the team you build is the company you build" lesson that became Rabois's core hiring philosophy.
- Site of Rabois's "smoothie test" hiring story: an intern (Taylor Francis) solved a persistent logistics failure (delivering cold smoothies to engineers at 9pm) within a day where the existing office team had failed repeatedly — Rabois's illustrative example of identifying a "barrel." See [[Barrels And Ammunition]].
