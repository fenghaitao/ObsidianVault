## Definition
Hamill Hussein is described as "probably the leading authority on AI evals." He is a notable user of Amp Code and, as of late 2025, stated that Amp was his favorite coding agent.

## Key Information
- Leading authority on AI evaluations (evals)
- Stated Amp Code was his favorite coding agent (as of a couple weeks before December 2025)
- Not affiliated with Amp Code — cited as an independent endorsement
- Co-authored the O'Reilly white paper "What We Learned from a Year of Building with LLMs" alongside five other practitioners
- Presented the virtuous cycle of improvement (evals → data → improvements) as the unifying framework for LLM application development, tracing its genealogy through MLOps, DevOps, Lean Startup, and Toyota's Kaizen
- Advocated that evals and data literacy can be developed with 4-6 weeks of deliberate practice
- Argued that the AI engineer role is often overscoped but underspecified, and that hiring should be stage-appropriate: app dev first, then platform/data engineering, then ML engineering only once data exists
