## Definition

Brian Kernighan is a computer scientist who taught CS50 the year David J. Malan first took it (1996).

## Key Information

- Taught CS50 in 1996 (with Margo Seltzer before him), the year Malan, then a sophomore, first took the class.
- Malan describes him as "this very well-reputed computer scientist."

- In Bjarne Stroustrup's telling (transcribed "Brian Kernan" / "Brian Mcnite"), Kernighan was "down the hall" at Bell Labs as a co-creator of Unix and C, alongside Dennis Ritchie.
- Bjarne still talks to him ("I was talking to him this Friday"); he cites their continued friendship as proof the "C vs C++ language wars" narrative is silly.
