## Definition

*One Song* is a Hebrew-language podcast named by [[Tomer Cohen]] as his favorite in the episode's lightning round.

## Key Information

- Takes a single (typically popular) song and goes deep into its origin, history, and the story behind its creation — including the choices behind specific lyrics or how they match the music.
- [[Lenny Rachitsky]] points out the English-language podcast *Song Exploder* as a similar concept for non-Hebrew speakers.
