## Definition

MATLAB is a numerical-computing language and environment that the Julia programming language was originally designed to replace.

## Key Information

- Julia was "really designed to get rid of MATLAB," made by MIT math people who wanted to eliminate it.
- Julia's syntax was pitched to look like Python or MATLAB.
