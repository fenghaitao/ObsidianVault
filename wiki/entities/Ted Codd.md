## Definition

Edgar F. "Ted" Codd (transcribed "Ted Cod") is the IBM researcher whose pioneering 1970 CACM paper on the relational model inspired Mike Stonebraker and Eugene Wong to build Ingres.

## Key Information

- Stonebraker: 1971 — the year after "Ted Codd wrote his pioneering paper in CACM" — was when he and Eugene Wong decided to "do something" together, choosing the relational model over Codasyl and IBM's IMS.
