## Definition

SAP is a large enterprise software company used as an example of complex legacy systems where AI agents won't become self-sufficient overnight.

## Key Information

- Used by Alexander Imbiricos as an example of a company with "many complex systems" where AI agents can't just become self-sufficient overnight
- The path to agent autonomy for such companies will involve slowly replacing or updating systems to allow agents to handle more work end-to-end
- Contrasts with startups building new apps on modern stacks, where agents can be much more self-sufficient sooner
