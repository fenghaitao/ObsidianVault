## Definition

Startup co-founded by [[Lenny Rachitsky]], built on top of Foursquare/Gowalla check-in data, letting users ask real-time questions of people currently checked in nearby (e.g. "is there a long line at this bar right now?"). Acquired by [[Airbnb]], which led directly to Lenny's multi-year tenure there.
