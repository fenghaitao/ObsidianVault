## Definition

Local Mind was Lenny Rachitsky's startup, built on top of Foursquare and Gowalla, that allowed users to ask questions of people checked in at any location worldwide. It was acquired by Airbnb.

## Key Information

- Sat on top of Foursquare and Gowalla (when those were popular location-based services)
- Allowed users to ask questions like "Is there a long line at The Mill?" or "What's the special today?"
- Lenny describes it as "amazing idea and not something anyone needs, really" — you need it about once a quarter
- Google later implemented similar functionality with "Is it busy?" features
- Sold to Airbnb, where Lenny then worked for 7 years
