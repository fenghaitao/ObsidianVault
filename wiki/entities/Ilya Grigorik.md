## Definition

Ilya Grigorik is a Distinguished Engineer (L10, described as VP-equivalent) at Shopify. He previously founded PostRank (acquired by Google) and spent years at Google, seesawing between IC and manager roles including Director of Developer Relations.

## Key Information

- Grew to Distinguished Engineer at Shopify, a role he frames as VP-equivalent
- Graduated from the University of Waterloo and credits its co-op program as a major lever for success
- Founded PostRank, a social-engagement ranking system ("a child of the web 2.0 era"), which Google acquired
- Joined Google as an engineering manager on Google Analytics after the acquisition; the team rebuilt its stack to operate at Google's scale ("more than half the internet")
- Turned down a clear path to Director at Google to be voluntarily down-leveled back to an IC in the "Make the Web Fast" group, trading career track for curiosity, self-direction, and time control
- Later became Director of Developer Relations at Google, driving adoption of web performance metrics (W3C and IETF standards work)
- Frames principal+ engineering as demonstrating "dynamic range," and advocates building a career by "being the only" (unique skill intersection) rather than "the best" specialist
- Cites Sir Ken Robinson as a major influence on his philosophy about education and creativity
- Regrets self-filtering: "Don't let the pressure of your success stop you from trying"; overcame a high school fear of public speaking by pitching products he was passionate about
