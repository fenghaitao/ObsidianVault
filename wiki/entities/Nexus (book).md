## Definition
Nexus is a book by historian Yuval Noah Harari that traces the history of human information networks from the Stone Age through the printing press, the internet, and to the current era of LLMs. It argues that LLMs are unique because they are the first non-human entities capable of producing human language, and that their internal thinking is so different from human cognition that AI should be called "alien intelligence" rather than "artificial intelligence."

## Key Information
- Author: Yuval Noah Harari (historian)
- Covers human information networks from Stone Age to modern LLMs
- Central thesis: LLMs are unique because they are the first non-human producers of human language
- Argues the "A" in AI should stand for "alien" not "artificial" because AI thinking is fundamentally different from human cognition
- Referenced by Daniel Szoke in his argument for deterministic guardrails in AI-assisted coding
- Szoke's takeaway: AI failure modes may be totally unexpected to human intuition
