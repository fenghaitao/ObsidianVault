## Definition

SentinelOne is a cybersecurity company providing AI-powered endpoint protection. It is a [[ClaudeSecurity|Claude Security]] technology partner building defensive cyber offerings on [[Claude4.7Opus|Claude Opus]].

## Key Information

- **Technology partner (April 2026)**: named among the technology partners embedding Claude Opus into their own security tools as part of Claude Security's public beta launch.
- **May 2026 update**: confirmed to be building a defensive cyber offering on Claude Opus, with details to be shared as they become available.
