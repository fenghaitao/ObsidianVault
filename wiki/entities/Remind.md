## Definition

Remind is a small company where Rachel Lockett worked running the UX research team before transitioning to a product manager role. It was here that her colleague Zach Abrams gave her the pivotal feedback about her zone of genius being people leadership rather than product strategy.

## Key Information

- Rachel Lockett ran the UX research team at Remind
- The CEO asked her to move into the product manager role for the core product team
- She managed a team of 12 senior engineers who were initially skeptical of her non-technical background
- Within a month, she had the team working well together — reviewing each other's code, having healthy disagreements, and feeling more connected to users
- Despite team success, she found herself stressing at night about product decisions
- Zach Abrams, a colleague at Remind, gave her the feedback that redirected her career toward coaching
- This experience was the catalyst for her transition into executive coaching
