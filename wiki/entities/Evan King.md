## Definition

Evan King is a software engineer who reached Staff Engineer (IC6) at Meta by age 25, one of the fastest promotion trajectories at the company. He is the co-founder of Hello Interview, a mock interview and interview preparation platform.

## Key Information

- Grew up in the Seattle area, took AP Computer Science in high school (felt late compared to peers with Microsoft parents), was a straight-A student but struggled with CS initially
- Played Division 1 soccer at Cornell University but quit junior year to focus on software engineering after realizing he wouldn't be a professional soccer player
- Founded the Cornell hacking club (Capture The Flag / CTF), growing it from a few founding officers to a core team of 25 and up to 200 attendees at weekly lectures
- Interned at Zillow, where his manager (a former Googler) told him he was the best intern he had ever worked with — a major confidence-building moment
- Joined Meta through Boot Camp, initially explored cyber security teams but chose the Content Integrity org's terrorism team instead
- Was the first engineer on the terrorism team in Seattle, which was being moved from the London office
- Received his first promotion (IC3→IC4) after 6 months without any self-advocacy — his manager proactively told him
- Was made tech lead of the graphic violence team as a junior engineer, leading three engineers and holding weekly one-on-ones
- Responded to the Christchurch mosque shooting (March 2019) as the technical lead on Facebook's response, working in a war room with the VP of Integrity and head of content policy
- Created and led the Realtime Integrity team (initially just him and a PM), tasked with detecting live-streamed atrocities in real time
- Built the Golden Set Recall evaluation system, which restreamed past atrocity content through Bots on real Facebook infrastructure to holistically test detection systems
- Improved live suicide detection recall from 9% to mid-90s by incorporating comment signals into models — a simple but overlooked solution
- Achieved IC6 (Staff) promotion through a pattern of increasing halves: 1 half (IC3→IC4), 2 halves (IC4→IC5), 3 halves (IC5→IC6)
- Left Meta after about 1.5 years as staff to co-found a Web3 startup with his former manager (Stephan), who he described as the most brilliant person he'd ever worked with
- The Web3 startup (social intelligence layer combining Web2 Twitter activity with on-chain data) grew to ~100,000 users and was acquired
- After the acquisition, spent 6-8 months trying various ideas before building a Figma plugin (paste any website as editable Figma frames), which was also acquired
- Co-founded Hello Interview, which provides in-person mock interviews with current senior engineers and managers from target companies, plus AI-guided learning resources
- Hello Interview is still run by just Evan and Stephan (2 full-time employees), leveraging AI tools like Claude to accelerate development
- Evan's biggest career regret: not being more inquisitive about how things actually worked, opting to ship and move on instead of building deep understanding
- His advice to his younger self: invest in relationships and life outside work — he was underinvested in friendships and romantic relationships while hyper-optimizing for career
