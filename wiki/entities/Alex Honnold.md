## Definition

Alex Honnold is a professional rock climber known for his free solo ascent of El Capitan in Yosemite National Park, documented in the film *Free Solo*. Cat Wu cites him as an inspiration for his purity of pursuit and mental focus.

## Key Information

- Subject of the documentary *Free Solo* — climbing El Capitan without a rope or harness
- Cat Wu: "It's just such a pure achievement to be able to climb this extremely challenging, dangerous route. And to be able to have the mental focus to do it knowing that if you make a single mistake, you die."
- Cat Wu notes that the more you know about climbing, the more impressive Free Solo becomes — "the kinds of moves he's doing on the wall are things that I don't think I will ever be able to do in my lifetime if it were set in a gym like 1 ft off the ground"
- Cat Wu is a rock climber herself and relates to the pursuit of singular engineering-like goals
