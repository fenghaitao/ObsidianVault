## Definition

Will Durant was an American historian and philosopher. Sam Lessin recommends his book "Lessons from History" as "probably the most approachable non-obvious book I love" and "one of my favorite books ever."

## Key Information

- Author of "Lessons from History" (short read, a few hours)
- Sam Lessin: "from a few hours of investment to intellectual return" — his top book recommendation
