## Definition

Historian and co-author (with Ariel Durant) of *[[The Lessons of History]]*, named "Alan Durant" in the transcript (a likely mishearing/misremembering of Will Durant), which [[Sam Lesson]] calls one of his favorite books ever.

## Key Information

- Lesson: "the one serious one I'll make a recommendation on is Lessons from History... by Alan Durant is like probably the most approachable non-obvious book I love... It's a short read... from a few hours of investment to intellectual return" it offers an outsized payoff.
