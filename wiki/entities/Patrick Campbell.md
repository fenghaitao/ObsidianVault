## Definition

Patrick Campbell is a pricing expert known for having extensive data on startup pricing. Jason Cohen quotes him: "Your prices are way too low because you just guessed and you haven't changed them."

## Key Information

- Has 4,200+ data points about startup pricing
- Famous quote: "Your prices are way too low because you just guessed and you haven't changed them."
- Highlights that most startups pick prices based on competitors or arbitrary adjustments, not data
- Jason Cohen references him to support the argument that pricing is almost always too low for early-stage companies
