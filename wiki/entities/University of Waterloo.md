## Definition

The University of Waterloo is a Canadian university known (per Ilya Grigorik) for producing extraordinarily strong engineering interns, largely due to its co-op program.

## Key Information

- Grigorik noted the best interns he ever worked with came from Waterloo, even compared to MIT, Caltech, and Tsinghua University in China
- Its differentiator is the co-op program: students alternate study and work terms every semester, giving at least six "shots on goal" at trying different roles
- The program removes the stigma of short stints, exposes students to a range of companies and industries, and builds pragmatic, hands-on engineering skills
