## Definition

Google's early AR wearable, cited by [[Caitlin Kalinowski]] alongside [[Oculus]]/VR as an example of the social-acceptance challenge facing face-worn hardware.

## Key Information

- Kalinowski attributes VR's failure to become mass-market partly to the social isolation of covering one's face — a lesson she sees mirrored in Google Glass's own social-acceptance struggles ("glassholes") as a face-worn device.
