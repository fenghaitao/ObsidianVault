## Definition

Terence Tao (transcribed "Terren St"/"terish") is a Fields Medalist mathematician who became an enthusiastic Lean user after the Liquid Tensor Experiment.

## Key Information

- Began using Lean after the Liquid Tensor Experiment drew wide attention from the mathematics community.
- Got "addicted" to the gameplay of interactive proving: after his first project he said he wouldn't do it again, then a week later started another Lean project on a new result he had.
- de Moura cites Tao (alongside Jeremy Avigad, Kevin Buzzard, and Patrick Massot) as one of the serious mathematicians who insist dependent type theory — not higher-order logic — is what works for abstract mathematics.
