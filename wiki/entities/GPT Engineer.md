## Definition

GPT Engineer was the original name and product that preceded Lovable. It existed before Lovable officially launched in the third week of November 2024.

## Key Information

- Predecessor to Lovable; the company existed as GPT Engineer before rebranding and launching as Lovable
- Lovable officially launched in November 2024, separating from the GPT Engineer identity
- The pivot to Lovable branding (pink, purple, heart, "lovable") was a key strategic move that embedded brand personality into the product
