## Definition

GPT-5.4 is OpenAI's latest model (as of mid-2026). Simon Wilson describes it as "on par with Claude Opus 4.6 and possibly even better" and notes it's cheaper to use, making him lean toward it more heavily. It also released in mini and nano variants with five configurable thinking levels.

## Key Information

- Released approximately 3 weeks before the podcast recording (mid-2026)
- "Very, very, very good" — on par with or better than Claude Opus 4.6
- Cheaper than Opus 4.6, which is a factor in Simon's usage
- Released in multiple variants: GPT-5.4, GPT-5.4 mini, GPT-5.4 nano, each with five thinking levels (low, medium, high, etc.)
- Simon created a grid of 15 pelican-riding-bicycle SVGs from the different variants and thinking levels; GPT-5.4 at X-high drew the best pelican
- Simon's prompting style has evolved to fit Claude's way of thinking, so GPT-5.4 "almost matches my taste, but not quite" — though this may be due to his Claude-centric experience
