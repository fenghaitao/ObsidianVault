## Definition
Luke Harries is the Head of Growth at ElevenLabs. He presented Voice Engine, a new ElevenLabs product for wrapping existing chat agents with voice capabilities, at AI Engineer Summit 2026.

## Key Information
- Head of Growth at ElevenLabs
- Presented Voice Engine at AI Engineer Summit 2026
- Advocated for upgrading chat agents to voice agents, citing voice as a more natural, faster, and accessible medium
- Demonstrated one-prompt conversion of an existing chat agent to a voice agent
- Previously focused on ElevenLabs text-to-speech models before the company was pulled into building full voice agent solutions
- Announced Voice Engine would be released within weeks of May 2026, with design partner opportunities
