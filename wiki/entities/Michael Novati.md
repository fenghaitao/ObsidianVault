## Definition

Michael Novati is a former Meta (Facebook) Senior Staff Engineer (IC7) who joined as an intern in 2009 when the company had ~220 engineers. He was a self-described "coding machine" and later co-founded Formation (interview and offer-prep).

## Key Information

- Joined in 2009 between undergrad and a planned PhD at University of Washington (HCI), and never went to grad school
- Interviewed onto a team building internal collaboration tools (diff review, tasks, discussion) — a perfect fit he wanted
- Worked with Mark Zuckerberg on the 2009 reactions hackathon (any emoji as a reaction) and made a lockdown bet with him about committing code
- Was one of the company's "five most prominent ripstickers" before the practice was banned
- Wrote the internal "weekly navati" blog; in retrospect he wouldn't repeat it because off-the-cuff posts on sensitive topics caused friction with executives and HR
- Became known for single-handedly completing codebase-wide cleanups (e.g., removing every "preparable" class), work a team of 4+ senior engineers wouldn't finish
- Argues taste and judgment — not raw output — are what carry an engineer from prolific coder to IC7
- Left Meta and co-founded Formation, where he advises people on interviews and offers
