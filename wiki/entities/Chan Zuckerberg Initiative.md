## Definition

The Chan Zuckerberg Initiative (CZI) is a philanthropic organization founded by Mark Zuckerberg and Priscilla Chan. Molly Graham worked with Zuckerberg on building CZI.

## Key Information

- Founded by Mark Zuckerberg and Priscilla Chan
- Molly Graham worked with Mark Zuckerberg on building the organization
- Zuckerberg brought his escalation philosophy from Facebook to CZI: "people don't escalate enough"
