## Definition

The Chan Zuckerberg Initiative (CZI) is the philanthropy founded by [[Mark Zuckerberg]] and [[Priscilla Chan]]. [[Molly Graham]] helped run its first two years of full existence after leaving [[Quip]].

## Key Information

- Graham joined when CZI had 30 people — the same week it acquired two companies — and grew it to 250 people within its first year, applying "every single tool in my toolkit" from her prior roles.
- Cited by Graham as a counterexample to the assumption that philanthropy work is calm: "philanthropy sounds calm... must be so peaceful over there" — in practice it was as chaotic and fast-scaling as her time at [[Facebook]] or [[Google]].
- [[Mark Zuckerberg]]'s "[[Escalation Is A Tool]]" rule (escalate together rather than "tattle" when two people of equal power are stuck) was carried over from Facebook and applied at CZI as well.
