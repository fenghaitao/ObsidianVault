## Definition

Good Fire is an AI interpretability company where Thariq Shihipar did an applied-research fellowship before joining Anthropic.

## Key Information

- Described by Thariq as "an interpretability AI company"; his fellowship focused on applied research — figuring out how to use interpretability to make better products.
- His work there produced an interpretability visualization, which he shared publicly (his first big Twitter post, ~500 likes).
- He framed sharing the project as mutually beneficial: it raised Good Fire's profile as a startup while demonstrating his own initiative.
- The shared project is his personal example of expanding "luck surface area" — it drew inbound DMs that led to an intro into a role at Anthropic.
