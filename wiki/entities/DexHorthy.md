## Definition
Dex Horthy is a thought leader on AI-assisted development and founder of Human Layer. He originated the "Smart Zone and Dumb Zone" concept describing LLM performance degradation within context windows, and is quoted by Brendan O'Leary for his insights on the relationship between research quality and code quality.

## Key Information
- Founder of Human Layer
- Originated the "Smart Zone and Dumb Zone" concept: LLMs perform best in the first ~100K tokens of a context window (Smart Zone) and degrade progressively beyond that (Dumb Zone) due to quadratic scaling of attention relationships
- This concept is foundational to Matt Pocock's AI coding workflow — tasks must be sized to stay within the Smart Zone
- Quoted: "A bad line of research can potentially be hundreds of lines of bad code" — emphasizing that poor upfront understanding cascades into poor implementation
- Quoted: "AI can't replace thinking. It can only amplify the thinking you've done or the lack of thinking you haven't done." — arguing that AI magnifies both good and bad human preparation
- Has spoken extensively on the subject of AI-assisted development, with videos available on YouTube
- His philosophy underpins the Research-Plan-Implement workflow: invest human time in research and planning to maximize AI's effectiveness in implementation
