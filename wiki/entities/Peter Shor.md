## Definition

Peter Shor is a mathematician who in 1994 discovered polynomial-time quantum algorithms for factoring integers and for discrete logarithms.

## Key Information

- His algorithms "created an earthquake or an avalanche," because factoring and discrete log are "the two most basic underpinnings of all security systems that exist."
- Shor's result set off billions in investment (by companies and governments) to build quantum computers and to find quantum-resistant assumptions.
- These problems are special: they reduce to finding periods in a signal, which connects to the Fourier transform, something quantum computers can exploit via interference.
