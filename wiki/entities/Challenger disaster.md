## Definition

The Space Shuttle Challenger disaster (1986) is used by Simon Wilson as a metaphor for the AI industry's normalization of deviance around prompt injection and AI safety. The disaster gave rise to the sociological concept of "normalization of deviance" — repeated successful launches despite known O-ring flaws led to institutional overconfidence, until the disaster occurred.

## Key Information

- 1986 Space Shuttle disaster caused by O-ring failure
- "Lots of people knew that those little O-rings were unreliable, but every single time you get away with launching a space shuttle without the O-rings failing, you institutionally feel more confident in what you're doing"
- Led to the sociological concept of "normalization of deviance" — a research paper from the 1980s
- Simon's prediction: "We're going to see a Challenger disaster of AI" — a catastrophic prompt injection event that will force the industry to take security seriously
- He has made this prediction every 6 months for 3 years; it hasn't happened yet, but the normalization of deviance continues
