## Definition

David Sacks was the COO of PayPal and a key member of the PayPal Mafia. He gave Keith Rabois critical feedback about leadership leverage that shaped Keith's career trajectory.

## Key Information

- COO of [[PayPal]] during Keith Rabois's tenure
- Had an equation for leadership leverage: "1 + 1 has to equal three or more" — for every incremental person you hire, you have to produce disproportionate, non-linear returns
- Told [[Keith Rabois]] he wouldn't get promoted again until he could demonstrate leadership leverage
- This feedback led Keith to recruit talented people from within PayPal to his own team — which was very successful and led to his promotion
- Was also a lawyer but never practiced
- [[Peter Thiel]] was also a lawyer (quit after 5 months and 4 days)
