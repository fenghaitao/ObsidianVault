## Definition

COO of [[PayPal]] during [[Keith Rabois]]'s tenure there; gave Rabois the formative feedback that he wouldn't be promoted again until he demonstrated hiring "leverage" — the trigger for Rabois developing his talent-evaluation philosophy.

## Key Information

- Coined (per Rabois) the leverage equation "1 + 1 has to equal three or more": every incremental hire must produce disproportionate, non-linear returns, not just add headcount.
- Never practiced law, unlike Rabois and [[Peter Thiel]] (who practiced only briefly) — cited by Rabois as evidence Sacks may have been "even smarter" for skipping law entirely.
