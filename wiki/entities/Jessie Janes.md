## Definition

Jessie Janes is an ex-founder and ex-acquired founder who homeschools her four young children. She is an OpenClaw power user who unlocked the mental model of separate machines for agents and inspired Claire Vo's approach to agent management.

## Key Information

- Ex-founder, ex-acquired founder
- Homeschools four young children
- OpenClaw power user — her concept of separate machines for agents unlocked Claire Vo's mental model
- Uses voice notes to Telegram to capture ideas while on the floor with her kids (e.g., "remind me to do this number blocks lesson tomorrow")
- As a mother of young kids, "literally doesn't have hands" — OpenClaw gives her the ability to get things done without being at a laptop
- Appeared on Claire Vo's "How I AI" podcast
