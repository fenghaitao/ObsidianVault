## Definition

The Bay Area (San Francisco Bay Area) is the primary hub of the technology industry in the United States, home to Silicon Valley and many of the world's largest tech companies.

## Key Information

- Ryan noted that "$200K is like minimum wage in the Bay Area," reflecting the region's extreme cost of living
- Ricky is from the Bay Area and attended Monta Vista High School, one of the region's intensely competitive public schools
- The Bay Area's high cost of living contextualizes the seemingly high compensation numbers in big tech
