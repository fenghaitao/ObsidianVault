## Definition

Socratica is a school program focused on building and showcasing projects like a science fair. Jenny Wen recommends this approach for aspiring designers: build actual things, share them with people, and find a community of others who do the same.

## Key Information

- Started at a school after Jenny graduated
- Whole philosophy is building stuff and showcasing it "almost like a science project"
- Examples: someone built a Claude robot project, someone put googly eyes on a bus in Boston
- Emphasizes agency ("yeah we can just do stuff") and community
- Jenny recommends this approach for new designers trying to break in
