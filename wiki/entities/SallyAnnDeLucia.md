## Definition
SallyAnn DeLucia is the Director of RISE at Arize, with a background in data science before transitioning to product. She presented the "Build a Prompt Learning Loop" workshop at the AI Engineer Summit.

## Key Information
- Director of RISE at Arize, the AI observability and evaluation platform.
- Started career in data science before moving into product roles, and continues to write code.
- Works on building Arize's internal agent "Alex" integrated into the platform, giving her firsthand experience with agent optimization pain points.
- Presented a hands-on workshop demonstrating how to build a prompt optimization loop using Arize's prompt learning SDK and OpenAI models.
- Emphasized that agents fail primarily due to weak instructions and environment, not weak models.
- Argued that overfitting in prompt optimization should be reframed as building expertise rather than a flaw.
