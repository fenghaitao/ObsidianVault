## Definition
SallyAnn DeLucia is the Head of Product at Arize, with a background in data science before transitioning to product. She is a core contributor to Arize's internal AI agent Alex and functions as a part-time AI engineer alongside her product role. She has spoken at the AI Engineer Summit on prompt learning loops and context management.

## Key Information
- Head of Product at Arize, the AI observability and evaluation platform.
- Started career in data science before moving into product roles, and continues to write code as a part-time AI engineer.
- Core contributor to Arize's internal agent "Alex," built over the course of nearly a year.
- Presented "Build a Prompt Learning Loop" workshop demonstrating prompt optimization using Arize's prompt learning SDK and OpenAI models.
- Presented "How we solved Context Management in Agents" sharing lessons from building Alex, including smart truncation with memory, long session evals, and sub-agent delegation.
- Emphasized that agents fail because of context, not prompts — context management is a product and UX problem, not just an engineering one.
- Argued that overfitting in prompt optimization should be reframed as building expertise rather than a flaw.
