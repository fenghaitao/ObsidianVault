## Definition

Facebook Marketplace is the commerce product Fiona Fung left Microsoft to help build at Facebook in 2015.

## Key Information

- Originated from a former coworker's observation that people were buying and selling products in Facebook Groups, and the idea to build a product around it
- Fiona's three mission reasons for joining: affordability/commerce for most of the world (a $5–10 price difference and used goods matter to most people), environmentalism (giving goods a second, third, or fourth life), and supporting small businesses (enabling them to start without a brick-and-mortar store)
- Operated with weekly updates/sprints in the early days; the first version ran on www.fas.com
- First test launches were in the Seattle area and Hawaii
- Had aggressive-growth "war rooms" (one lasting ~2 months) that launched 75 experiments at once
- Fiona dogfooded the product heavily, keeping an inventory of items to sell (to her husband's initial annoyance)
