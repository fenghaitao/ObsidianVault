## Definition

*Le Ton beau de Marot* (rendered "Letobo Dearo" in the transcript) is a book by Douglas Hofstadter, named by [[Edwin Chen]] as one of his most-recommended books — one he loves even more than Hofstadter's more famous *Gödel, Escher, Bach*.

## Key Information

- Takes a single French poem and translates it 89 different ways, discussing the motivations behind each translation.
- Chen connects its central idea — that translation isn't a robotic, single-correct-answer process but involves countless valid approaches — directly to how he thinks about data quality and language at [[Surge AI]].
