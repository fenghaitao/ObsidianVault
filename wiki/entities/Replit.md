## Definition

Vibe-coding platform [[Marc Andreessen]]'s 10-year-old son independently discovered (with no input from his father) and became "100% obsessed" with, building Star Trek-themed simulators and games.

## Key Information

- Cited by Andreessen as a real-time example of the "supermpowered individual" idea applied to a child: his son spends hours vibe-coding using Replit, including recreating *Star Trek: The Next Generation*'s in-universe "LCARS" UI design language for custom games.
- [[Dr. Becky Kennedy]] (CEO of [[Good Inside]]) separately mentions playing around with Replit (transcribed as "Replet") after her team showed it to her, alongside [[Lovable]] and Figma Make, as part of her workflow for turning product ideas into visual prototypes.

### Per episode 35 (Jason Lemkin)

- [[Jason Lemkin]] (founder/CEO of [[SaaStr]]) describes himself as a "top 1% user" of Replit despite being unable to code, having built 12+ apps over ~150 days used a combined "million million" times — including a startup-valuation calculator (used ~800,000 times in 90 days) and a pitch-deck reviewer (reviewed ~3,000 decks); explicitly none of SaaStr's actual GTM/sales agents were built on Replit or in-house — Lemkin's advice is "don't build it yourself" for GTM tooling, reserving self-built tools for side projects like these.
- Traces his own experience of the product's capability jump: didn't work well when he started (~170 days before the recording); improved with a "V2" release (hallucinations went away); then, with "V3" (~45 days before the recording), gained an agents-talking-to-agents architecture — when Lemkin hits an issue, the coding agent calls in a separate "architect" agent, and the two debate the right approach before responding — which he says caused him to "fall out of my chair" the first time he saw it.
- Cited as one of the desk-nickname agents in SaaStr's own GTM agent fleet ("Reply" is the office nickname for the agent that resembles Replit's branding), though the tool itself is used for internal SaaStr side-project tools, not sales.
- Cited alongside [[Vercel]] and [[ElevenLabs]] as a hyper-growth AI-native company facing so much inbound demand that "half our job is picking which [leads] to respond to."
