## Definition

Company (portfolio/advisory company of The Build Collective) using AI and cameras to sort recycling materials quickly and accurately.

## Key Information

- Uses AI plus cameras to figure out, in real time, what items belong in which recycling bin/stream.
- Tony Fadell notes the company has been working on this for several years, applying the same "AI plus hardware" approach used across his portfolio.
