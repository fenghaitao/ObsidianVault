## Definition

Amazon DynamoDB is AWS's scalable NoSQL database, which Marc Brooker names as a scalable backend to use instead of putting a cache in front of a database.

## Key Information

- Brooker's preferred alternative to caching: "use a scalable back end… or DynamoDB or whatever your favorite scalable database is, and keep your database vendor honest about getting to the scale and performance you need."
- Named in contrast to adding caches, which carry a metastable failure mode.
