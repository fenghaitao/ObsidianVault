## Definition
Tasker is an Android automation app that Kitze used ~15 years ago to manage text-file-based todos with contextual triggers — reminders when connecting to Wi-Fi, arriving at a destination, or biking.

## Key Information
- Android app for automation and contextual triggers
- Kitze used it to manage text-file-based todo lists before modern markdown tools existed
- Contextual reminders: Wi-Fi connect, location arrival, biking detection
- Part of Kitze's early productivity system evolution — always trying to offload thoughts and process them
