## Definition
Cerebras is a hardware company that partners with OpenAI to provide blazingly fast inference for specialized models. They power GPT-5.3 Codex Spark, a research preview text-only model deployed on Cerebras hardware.

## Key Information
- Hardware partner for OpenAI's GPT-5.3 Codex Spark model
- GPT-5.3 Codex Spark is described as "blazingly fast" and is a text-only research preview model
- Used as the model for the PR Explorer sub-agent persona due to its speed
- Partnership demonstrates OpenAI's strategy of using specialized hardware for specific model variants
- In Callosum's **heterogeneous recursion** benchmarks on ULong: 7x cheaper and 5x faster than GPT-5.2, representing the low-latency option vs SambaNova's lower-cost option
