## Definition
Stability AI is an AI research company and foundation model provider known for image generation models, whose models are available through Amazon Bedrock's managed service.

## Key Information
- One of the leading model providers integrated with Amazon Bedrock
- Available alongside Anthropic, AI21 Labs, Cohere, Meta, Mistral, and Amazon Titan on the Bedrock platform
- Accessible through the unified Converse API for standardized multi-model development
