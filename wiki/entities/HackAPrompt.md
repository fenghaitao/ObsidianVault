## Definition

HackAPrompt is the first and largest AI red-teaming competition/company, founded and run by [[Sander Schulhoff]].

## Key Information

- Originated as the first-ever generative AI red-teaming competition, sponsored by OpenAI, Scale, Hugging Face, and roughly 10 other AI companies; it collected and open-sourced the first and largest dataset of prompt injections.
- The associated research paper won Best Theme Paper at EMNLP 2023 out of ~20,000 submissions, and both the paper and dataset are now used by every frontier AI lab and most Fortune 500 companies to benchmark and improve their models' AI security.
- Continues running large-scale AI red-teaming competitions studying real-world defenses, including a joint study with OpenAI, Google DeepMind, and Anthropic finding that adaptive human attackers break every state-of-the-art guardrail tested in 10-30 attempts.
- Co-teaches (with [[Learn Prompting]]) an AI security/red-teaming course on Maven, hosted at hackai.co.
