## Definition

HackAPrompt is an organization founded by Sander Schulhoff that runs AI red teaming competitions and research. It produced the first and largest open-source dataset of prompt injections and is the leading platform for adversarial AI testing.

## Key Information

- Ran the first-ever generative AI red teaming competition, sponsored by OpenAI, Scale, Hugging Face, and about 10 other AI companies.
- The competition's paper won best theme paper at EMNLP 2023, one of the top natural language processing conferences.
- The paper and dataset are now used by every frontier lab and most Fortune 500 companies to benchmark their models and improve AI security.
- Continues to run AI red teaming competitions studying defenses including guardrails, finding them consistently broken by human attackers.
- Released a major research paper alongside OpenAI, Google DeepMind, and Anthropic on adaptive attacks against state-of-the-art defenses.
- Also runs an educational course on AI security at hackai.co in partnership with Maven.
