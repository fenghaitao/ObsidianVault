## Definition

Resonance Radar is Brian Casel's custom-built application for curating and planning content ideas. It extracts potential content ideas from internal sources (customer interviews, daily work logs, published content, audience surveys) and was expanded with Claude Fable to add external trend monitoring (YouTube, Reddit, X/Twitter).

## Key Information

- Original purpose: organize ideas pitched by Brian and his AI agents for review and greenlighting.
- Internal sources: customer interviews, daily work logs, newsletter content, tweets, YouTube videos, audience survey responses.
- Expansion (built with Claude Fable): external watch list monitoring YouTube channels, Reddit subreddits, X/Twitter feeds for trending topics.
- Trending algorithm: calculates magnitude (deviation from source norm) and velocity (speed of popularity growth).
- Follows the Night Shift pattern: custom app UI for Brian, API for agents, agent skills on recurring schedules.
- Part of Brian's broader content pipeline alongside [[SparkDrop]].
