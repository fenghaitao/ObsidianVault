## Definition

Ethan Evans is a former Amazon VP (a team of 800+ engineers) who was fired twice in the dot-com era, then rebuilt his soft skills, rose to senior leadership, and now coaches people on promotion hurdles.

## Key Information

- Grew up wanting to work at Apple; Apple's "we hire from five schools" reply steered him to Carnegie Mellon, then a master's at Purdue
- His pre-Amazon career was a chain of high-growth networking/streaming startups, where he was "made redundant" twice largely for being abrasive ("a loose cannon pointed at them")
- An interviewer's challenge — "why didn't any company keep you if you were that valuable?" — triggered his soft-skills rebuild and study of workplace psychology
- Amazon recruited him (he'd studied them as a Barnes & Noble competitor); hired as a senior manager, he rose to director then VP
- Led the Twitch integration as an advisor with no authority, after giving up an 800-person team to practice influence
- Now writes and coaches on promotion strategy as "Ethan Evans VP"
