## Definition

HeyGen (transcribed as "Hey Jen"/"Hen"/"hent" in the source) is an all-in-one video-generation platform and AI company where the Snapchat-director guest serves as CTO.

## Key Information

- Positioned as an "all-in-one video generation platform" that wants to make video generation and visual storytelling accessible to everyone
- Focuses on "human-centric video generation" with a top-tier technical stack for building hyperrealistic avatars
- Differentiates from other video-generation platforms on quality, consistency, and controllability
- Targets content professionals (marketers, salespeople, everyday corporate users) rather than creative professionals such as moviemakers in Hollywood
- The guest's (CTO's) dream: "make camera obsolete" — enable everyone to make videos in any language, at any time, without a camera
- He envisions future podcasts being produced by two interactive avatars talking to each other
