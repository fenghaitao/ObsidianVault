## Definition

Sir Ken Robinson was an author and speaker on education reform, known for TED talks about creativity in education. Ilya Grigorik cites him as a major career influence.

## Key Information

- Author of books on "finding your element" and creative schools
- His message: rethink education away from a factory-line, age-binned model, since people "spike in different ways at different points in our life"
- Grigorik recommends his TED talks as one of the highest-ROI ~20-minute investments
- Influenced Grigorik's "be the only" philosophy about combining and mixing skills rather than pigeonholing learners
