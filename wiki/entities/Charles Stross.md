## Definition

Author of *Accelerando*, recommended by [[Boris Cherny]] in the episode's lightning round as capturing the accelerating pace of the current AI moment better than any other book he's read.

## Key Information

- *Accelerando* spans from the start of a technological "lift-off" approaching a singularity to a collective consciousness orbiting Jupiter, over the span of a few fictional decades.
