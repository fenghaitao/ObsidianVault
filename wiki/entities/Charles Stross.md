## Definition

Charles Stross is a science fiction author. Boris Cherny recommends his novel Accelerando, saying it captures the essence of the current AI moment better than any other book.

## Key Information

- Author of Accelerando
- Boris: "Accelerando is just this incredible book... the pace gets faster and faster and faster and I just feel like it captures the essence of this moment that we're in more than any other book that I've read"
- The book starts as a technological lift-off begins, approaches the singularity, and "ends with this collective lobster consciousness orbiting Jupiter"
- The pace happens over the span of a few decades
