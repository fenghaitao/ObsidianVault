## Definition
Sierra is a customer support scaleup that built TauBench, a benchmark for evaluating real-world customer support AI agents. Their airline agent benchmark includes a complex policy-driven agent with access to multiple tools (reservation management, flight information, user information).

## Key Information
- Customer support scaleup company
- Created [[TauBench]], a benchmark for real-world customer support agent scenarios
- The airline agent benchmark features: complex policy rules for when to change reservations and provide information, multiple tool integrations (reservations, flights, user data), and 599 conversation traces with annotations
- The benchmark data has 62% compliant / 38% non-compliant distribution across multiple models and trials
