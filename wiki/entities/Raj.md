## Definition
Raj is a Staff Software Engineer at IKEA in the Delivery and Services domain, and the creator of the Demand-Driven Context methodology for transforming institutional knowledge bases into agent-usable context blocks.

## Key Information
- Staff Software Engineer at IKEA, working in the Delivery and Services domain (100+ engineers, 6 product teams)
- Interested in architecture, neuroscience, linguistics, and AI
- Presented the Demand-Driven Context workshop at the AI Engineer conference
- Published a preprint on Demand-Driven Context on RXP in March 2026
- Built 20+ MCP servers before realizing the approach doesn't work against monolithic knowledge bases
- Experienced the "fourth stage" of knowledge management: hand-writing domain context, which proved exhausting
- Implemented Demand-Driven Context using both Copilot (at work) and Claude Code (for the demo)
- Advocates fixing knowledge base quality before retrieval, not during operational use
- Recommends starting at the smallest team scope with scoped Jira tickets, incidents, and Confluence pages
- Found that per-domain knowledge bases average ~96K tokens, fitting easily in modern context windows
