## Definition
Joao Moura (also goes by "Joe") is the CEO and founder of CrewAI, a production-ready multi-agent AI orchestration framework. He is a Brazilian engineer who previously worked at Clearbit before founding CrewAI.

## Key Information
- Founder and CEO of CrewAI, which executes over 10 million agents per month and 100,000+ crews daily
- Previously worked at Clearbit for many years
- Originally from Brazil
- Built CrewAI initially to automate his own LinkedIn content creation after his wife encouraged him to share more online
- Started building CrewAI during an anniversary trip — working early mornings while on holiday
- Uses his own product (CrewAI) to run company operations: marketing, lead qualification, and code documentation are all handled by agent crews
- Advocates for early adoption of agents: "start simple, expand to low risk and high impact"
- Speaker at AI Engineer conferences
