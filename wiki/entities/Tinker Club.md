## Definition
Tinker Club is a community founded by Kitze for AI agent enthusiasts and tinkerers. It hosts weekly meetups focused primarily on OpenClaw and personal AI agents, though participation has declined as agent fatigue set in across the community.

## Key Information
- Founded by Kitze as a community for people building and tinkering with AI agents
- "Every person inside is copy and paste of everyone inside is hilarious to see"
- Weekly meetups focus mostly on OpenClaw discussions
- Kitze's recurring question: "Which use cases do you have?" then "Which ones of them you cannot do with Claude Code and Codex?" — immediately reduces by 90%
- Initial explosion of sign-ups → now ~5 people per meetup
- "Slowly turning into Open Claw Anonymous" — community experiencing agent fatigue
- Members include people who build their own pinball machines — hardcore tinkerers
- Even these tinkerers are "freaking tired of trying to make this thing work"
