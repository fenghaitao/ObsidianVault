## Definition
TensorRT is NVIDIA's inference-optimization toolkit; Vlad Feinberg cites demonstrations built with it as a strong signal for frontier-lab candidates.
## Key Information
- Candidates can build on TensorRT (or Dynamo) to show they understand and can improve low-level inference components.
- It sits alongside open-source serving stacks (vLLM, SGLang) in the ecosystem of LLM-serving tooling labs care about.
