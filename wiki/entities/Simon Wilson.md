## Definition

Simon Wilson (Simon Willison) is a software engineer, open-source developer, and AI researcher. He co-created Django (the web framework powering Instagram, Pinterest, Spotify, and thousands of other platforms), coined the terms "prompt injection," "AI slop," and "agentic engineering," and created Datasette, a data analysis tool used in investigative journalism. He has been blogging about AI and software engineering at simonwillison.net, sharing his real-time learnings as he transitions from traditional coding to AI-assisted development.

## Key Information

- Co-creator of Django, one of the most widely used Python web frameworks
- Coined the term "prompt injection" in 2022 (before ChatGPT launched), though he regrets the name because it misleadingly implies the problem is solvable like SQL injection
- Popularized the terms "AI slop" and "agentic engineering"
- Has over 100 open-source projects; created Datasette for data journalism
- 95% of his code is now AI-generated; writes much of it from his phone while walking his dog on the beach
- Currently writing a "not a book" (published chapter by chapter on his blog) about agentic engineering patterns
- Day job: building open-source tools for data journalism, with the goal of helping a journalist win a Pulitzer Prize using his software
- Has a unique consulting model called "zero deliverable consulting" — one-hour calls with no report, no code, just his time
- Lives near the world's second-largest mega roost of California brown pelicans, which inspired his "pelican riding a bicycle" AI benchmark
- Predicts a "Challenger disaster of AI" due to normalization of deviance around prompt injection
- Uses Claude Code and OpenAI Codex interchangeably, switching as models leapfrog each other
- Turns off AI memory features because "as an AI researcher, I need to see what everyone else sees"
- His blog recently started making money through sponsorship, transitioning from unpaid side project to financially supportive
