## Definition

Rachel Lockett is an executive coach and former longtime HR leader at [[Pinterest]] and [[Stripe]], working with CEOs, founders, and tech leaders on emotional/positive intelligence, resilience, courage, vision-setting, and building trusted, accountable teams.

## Key Information

- Career origin story: was a UX researcher, then promoted into a product-manager role at a small company called Remind; despite quickly improving team dynamics and cross-functional trust, found the strategist/roadmap parts of the job personally draining. Colleague Zach Abrams told her that her actual "zone of genius" was people, not product strategy — a comment she initially resisted but ultimately acted on, leaving product to become a trained coach and later an HR leader. Zach went on to lead product at Coinbase, Brex, and Bridge (acquired by Stripe), and remains her client a decade later.
- At Stripe, built a coaching/retention program for the company's top ~50 tech-side executives under CEO Patrick Collison's push to retain top talent, using engagement scores and coaching circles; observed widespread executive exhaustion even among highly capable, committed leaders.
- Champions the "[[Zone Of Genius]]" framework: people have more energy operating in their natural strengths, and burnout is often a slow leak from spending too much time outside that zone rather than simple overwork. Aspirational target: ~80% of work time in one's zone of genius.
- Teaches two core "leader coaching" skills instead of always advising: "[[Active Listening Three Levels|active listening]]" (levels 1-3, culminating in hearing what's beneath someone's literal words) and asking powerful, open-ended questions using the "[[GROW Model]]" (Goal, Reality, Options, Way forward).
- For difficult conversations, teaches Marshall Rosenberg's "[[Non-Violent Communication (NVC)]]" framework (observations → feelings → needs → request), framing the goal of any conflict as mutual understanding rather than convincing the other person they're wrong.
- Cites Stripe's talent bar, the "[[Enthusiastic Rehire Test]]" ("would you enthusiastically rehire this person for the same role?"), as a clarifying tool for talent decisions.
- Adapted Alpine Investors' "People First Operating Rhythm" into her own "[[One-Page Plan]]" coaching framework for company-wide strategic alignment (vision/values, strategic intentions/KPIs, annual goals, quarterly goals on one page), paired with a regular "balcony" reflection cadence.
- Specializes in coaching co-founder relationships (cites a stat that ~65% of startups fail due to co-founder conflict) — see "[[Co-Founder Relationship Practices]]": personality-typing tools (uses the Enneagram), explicit "co-founder vows," and dedicated relationship time outside of business operations. Co-authored a related article/template with First Round.
- Runs a women's organization hosting eight retreats a year; uses Granola for coaching-session notes and pattern-tracking across client relationships, ChatGPT for retreat-activity brainstorming, and is building (unshipped, at time of episode) an AI assistant giving her clients light-touch, between-session tactical support using full context from her coaching frameworks and session history.
- Life motto: a Joseph Campbell quote — "if you can see your path laid out in front of you step by step, it's not your path; your own path you make with every step you take."
- Recommends *The 15 Commitments of Conscious Leadership* and *Designing Your Life* (Bill Burnett); reads Roald Dahl (*Witches*, *Matilda*) with her two children (ages 5 and 7); enjoyed *K-pop Demon Hunters*; uses and recommends Loom for scaling human-feeling training content.
