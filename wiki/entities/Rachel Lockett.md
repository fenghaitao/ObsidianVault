## Definition

Rachel Lockett is an executive coach and former longtime HR leader at Pinterest and at Stripe. She works with CEOs, founders, and leaders at tech companies on both the emotional side (positive intelligence, resilience, courage) and the operational side (vision, strategy, prioritization, building trusted and accountable teams).

## Key Information

- Former HR leader at Pinterest and Stripe
- At Stripe, worked with Patrick Collison on top talent retention, creating a program for the top 50 executives on the tech side
- Website: lockcoaching.com
- Formerly ran UX research at a small company called Remind, then moved into a product manager role before transitioning to coaching
- Both parents are therapists; she describes herself as a "work therapist"
- Runs a women's organization with eight retreats per year
- Uses Granola (AI note-taking) and ChatGPT in her coaching practice
- Building an AI tool to support clients between coaching sessions
- Has a client example: an engineering manager at Coinbase who had a tattoo of a sun with a redwood grove to remind him of his core strengths and purpose; he went on to run Base, the largest Ethereum L2
