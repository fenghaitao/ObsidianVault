## Definition

"There Is No Antimemetics Division" is a science fiction book by qntm (an online author) about a government agency fighting things that make you forget them. Sherwin Wu devoured it in two days and highly recommends it.

## Key Information

- Written by qntm, an online author
- Science fiction/horror genre
- Plot: a government agency fighting "antimemes" — things that make you forget them
- Sherwin Wu describes it as "super well written, super fascinating"
- "Unintentionally hilarious" — meant to be sci-fi/horror but made Sherwin laugh
- "Only a couple hundred pages" — Sherwin finished it in two days
- Discovered via recommendations on X
