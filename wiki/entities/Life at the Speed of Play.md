## Definition

Book by [[Mark Pincus]], the product of roughly five years of writing, synthesizing his product philosophy including [[Proven Better New]] and [[Kill Hope Before Hope Kills You]]. Released around the time of this podcast episode.

## Key Information

- For three of the four years writing the book, Pincus called it "Proven Better New," but changed the title because that name felt "too dry" relative to the broader "life at the speed of play" concept.
- Praised in a cover quote by [[Sam Altman]] (co-founder of [[OpenAI]]), who said the only bottleneck to building great products today is knowing what to create.
- Described by both Pincus and the podcast host as a deliberately quick, easy read.
- Pincus's "metaverse" — a lifelong project he calls "life at the speed of play" — is defined (crediting [[Reid Hoffman]]'s definition) as blurring the lines between the virtual and the real.
