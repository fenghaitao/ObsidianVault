## Definition

DeepMind is an AI research lab that Nimit Sohoni cited alongside OpenAI as an example of a big lab with enormous resources.

## Key Information

- Nimit said big labs like OpenAI and DeepMind have "all the compute in the world" and many researchers, but can be more averse to out-of-the-box ideas and more susceptible to groupthink than a nimble startup.
- He framed this as a reason he chose the Cartesia startup over a big lab.
- DeepMind merged with Google Brain in 2023 to form Google DeepMind; there Vlad Feinberg (pre-training area lead) ships Flash and Flash-Lite and runs three verticals — distillation, inference co-design, and quantization.
