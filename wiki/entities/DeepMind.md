## Definition

DeepMind is an AI research company acquired by Google. Edwin Chen admired DeepMind as a model for how a research-focused company could be acquired and still continue doing amazing science.

## Key Information

- Edwin was "always a huge fan of DeepMind because they were this amazing research company that got bought and still managed to keep on doing amazing science"
- DeepMind was an inspiration for Edwin's vision of Surge AI as a research lab more than a traditional startup
- Edwin initially thought DeepMind was a "magical unicorn" — an example that seemed impossible to replicate
- Google DeepMind co-authored a major research paper with HackAPrompt, OpenAI, and Anthropic on adaptive attacks against state-of-the-art AI defenses
