# Pablo Picasso

## Definition

Pablo Picasso (1881–1973) was a Spanish painter, sculptor, and one of the most influential artists of the 20th century. In the context of AI agent design, [[MarduSwanepoel]] invokes Picasso's philosophy on stealing — studying something deeply, truly understanding it, making it your own, and using that to create something better and unique.

## Key Information

- **Relevance to AI:** Picasso's quote on stealing is used as a framing device for agent design: study the best agents deeply, understand what they do, and build something better.
- **Context:** Referenced in [[MarduSwanepoel]]'s aiDotEngineer talk "What the Best Agents Share" (2026-05-26).
- **Quote Interpretation (per Swanepoel):** Stealing is not about taking something physically and presenting it as your own. It is about studying deeply, understanding, making it your own, and then creating something better than you could have without that process.
