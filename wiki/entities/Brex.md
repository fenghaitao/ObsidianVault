## Definition

Brex is a financial platform for startups and businesses, offering corporate cards, banking, treasury, and AI-powered finance automation. It was a sponsor of this episode of Lenny's Podcast.

## Key Information

- Provides high-limit corporate cards, banking, high-yield treasury, and AI agents for finance tasks
- AI agents handle expense filing, transaction auditing for waste, and report generation
- One in three startups in the United States runs on Brex
- Website: brex.com
- Sponsor of this podcast episode
- Amol Avaisary uses Claude/Co-work to automatically file reimbursements and expenses through Brex — "I don't do any of my reimbursements and expenses. Claude will go to Brex and file the reimbursements."
