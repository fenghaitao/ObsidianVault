## Definition

Brex is an "intelligent finance platform for founders," presented as a sponsor of this [[Lenny's Podcast]] episode.

## Key Information

- Offers high-limit corporate cards, banking, high-yield treasury, and a team of AI agents that handle manual finance tasks (filing expenses, scouring transactions for waste, running reports) according to user-defined rules.
- Ad claims one in three US startups already runs on Brex.
