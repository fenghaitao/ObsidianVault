## Definition

Human-Robot Interaction (HRI) researcher from whom [[Caitlin Kalinowski]] learned how to design robots that feel "human" and non-threatening.

## Key Information

- Principles Kalinowski attributes to her: soft appearance, telegraphing intent, and acknowledging people entering a room — design choices that make [[Humanoid Robots]] feel safe rather than alarming.
- Kalinowski compares this design discipline to what [[Pixar]] and [[Disney]] do with character animation.
