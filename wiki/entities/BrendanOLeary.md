## Definition
Brendan O'Leary is a speaker and advocate for "agentic engineering" who works at Kilo Code. He previously spent many years at GitLab and has a background in engineering management and product management before moving into developer relations.

## Key Information
- Works at Kilo Code, an AI coding agent company
- Previously worked at GitLab for many years
- Early career included roles as engineering manager and product manager at a healthcare software company
- Coined/popularized the term "agentic engineering" — the paradigm of working with AI as a collaborator rather than merely using it as a tool
- Advocates for treating AI agents as "energetic, enthusiastic, extremely well-read, often confidently wrong junior developers"
- Promotes the Research-Plan-Implement workflow loop for effective AI collaboration
- Emphasizes context engineering as the critical skill for engineers working with AI agents
- Shared an anecdote about giving interns a Balsamiq wireframe with Comic Sans and emoji placeholders, which they faithfully implemented — illustrating the importance of providing the right context
