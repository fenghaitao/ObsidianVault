## Definition

Nassim Taleb (transcribed in the episode as "Nasim Talb") is an author Ryan Olson cited for the concept of "skin in the game."

## Key Information

- Ryan Olson says he "really likes" Taleb, and applies Taleb's "skin in the game" idea to argue that senior ICs and managers should keep coding so they share the consequences of their designs.
