## Definition

Scott Renfro is a high-level individual contributor at Meta whom Philip Su described as a "force multiplier" and a "slam dunk case" of someone deserving of seniority.

## Key Information

- Scott came to Meta London for a 6-month rotation, where Philip worked with him more directly
- Peer feedback about Scott was "super glowing" — he would review PRs with sensitivity, never publicly shaming anyone, always framing feedback constructively
- His peer feedback consistently said: "Scott takes time out of his day to make me great"
- He was a force multiplier for teams — having him on the team would make the entire team much better
- He later led huge infrastructure-wide changes at Meta, including something related to GDPR
- He was capable both personally of guaranteeing a huge scope of work and being a force multiplier for the teams he was on
- Philip described him as a "slam dunk case of someone who deserved to be rewarded well" for his work
