## Definition
Asbjorn Steinskog is an engineer at Play Magnus (Take Take Take) who co-presented "Building a Chess Coach" at the AI Engineer conference. He presented the technical deep dive into the game review pipeline and the autonomous agent feedback loop.

## Key Information
- Co-presented the Play Magnus AI chess coach system with Anant Dole
- Presented the sections on the history of chess and AI, why LLMs are bad at chess, the game review pipeline architecture, and the autonomous agent feedback loop
- Demonstrated the Cloud Code Channel integration for automated commentary triage via Slack
- Showed how users reporting bad commentary triggers an agent that investigates, modifies prompts/detectors, regenerates commentary, and submits PRs
- A good chess player who uses his own skill alongside Anant Dole to evaluate LLM commentary quality
