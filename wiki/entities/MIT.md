## Definition

MIT (Massachusetts Institute of Technology) is where Dwayne Reeves studied before joining Facebook, and whose name recognition gave him outsized recruiting access.

## Key Information

- Dwayne's education was "fantastic," but he stresses the credential's true value was opportunities, not ability
- MIT's name meant recruiters would talk to him as a freshman — an edge many equally capable engineers lacked
- His freshman-year Microsoft internship almost automatically led to an Apple internship offer
