## Definition

MIT (Massachusetts Institute of Technology) is where Dwayne Reeves studied before joining Facebook, and whose name recognition gave him outsized recruiting access.

## Key Information

- Dwayne's education was "fantastic," but he stresses the credential's true value was opportunities, not ability
- MIT's name meant recruiters would talk to him as a freshman — an edge many equally capable engineers lacked
- His freshman-year Microsoft internship almost automatically led to an Apple internship offer

- Barbara Liskov spent her faculty career at MIT, developing the CLU and Argus languages, co-designing a design/specification course with John Guttag, and advising students including Brian Oki and Miguel Castro.
- David J. Malan credits MIT's OpenCourseWare for inspiring CS50's open-courseware mission and notes MIT has a larger, richer CS course catalog than Harvard — he finds it silly not to lean on each other.

- Bjarne Stroustrup uses MIT as the benchmark for Bell Labs' scale at his arrival: Bell Labs "probably had twice as many computer scientists as MIT at the time."
- James Cowling did his PhD at MIT (his master's thesis covered Byzantine fault tolerance), where a rack of servers was available for distributed-systems benchmarking in the pre-cloud era.
