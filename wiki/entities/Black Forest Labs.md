## Definition
Black Forest Labs (BFL) is an AI research company and the team behind Stable Diffusion, Latent Diffusion, and the FLUX model family. Founded in August 2024, BFL operates as a research-first organization, releasing state-of-the-art models in the open while also working with enterprise customers. Their trajectory extends from image generation toward visual intelligence, world models, and physical AI for robotics.

## Key Information
- Team behind Stable Diffusion, Latent Diffusion, and FLUX models
- Over 200,000 academic citations across the team
- Headquarters in Freiburg, Germany
- Founded/launched August 2024 with Flux 1
- First operating principle: release state-of-the-art models with every release
- Research-first: publishes papers openly (e.g., Self Flow) to advance the field
- Enterprise customers: Microsoft, Adobe, Canva, Mistral
- Model evolution: Flux 1 → Flux Context → Flux 2 → Flux 2 Klein → Self Flow research
- Long-term vision: visual intelligence → world models → physical AI and robotics
- Flux 1 was the most liked model on Hugging Face at launch
- Combines representation learning and generation in Self Flow approach
