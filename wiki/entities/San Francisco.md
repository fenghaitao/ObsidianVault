## Definition

San Francisco (SF) is a major city in California and a global hub for technology and startups. Jia Chen moved to SF for her startup Sprint.dev, living in the Soma neighborhood.

## Key Information

- Jia Chen moved to San Francisco during her gap semester to work on Sprint.dev
- She lived in the Soma neighborhood, which she described as a "scrappy environment"
- She raised angel investment at a Luma hot pot event in SF and VC investment at another Luma event
- Her apartment in SF was robbed, but she and her co-founder continued working on the startup within the hour
- Jia emphasized the importance of environment placement — moving to SF to be in "startup land"
