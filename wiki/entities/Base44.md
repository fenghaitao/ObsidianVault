## Definition

Base44 is an AI-powered app building platform in the same category as Bolt, Lovable, and Replit. Zevi Arnovitz used it to build a quiz game for practicing PM interview segmentation questions.

## Key Information

- Part of the vibe coding platform bucket alongside Bolt, Lovable, Replit, and v0
- Zevi used Base44 to create a quiz game for practicing segmentation questions during Meta PM interview prep
- Zevi describes it as doing "an amazing job at basically taking all the complex guesswork out of building product"
- The tradeoff: it handles sign-in with Google, database setup, and other complex decisions automatically, but you lose control over those decisions
- Founded by Mayor, who also hosts the Basically Podcast
