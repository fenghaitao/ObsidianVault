## Definition
HCAST is a collection of software-based tasks used by METR for evaluating AI capabilities, requiring autonomy through tool interaction, environment interaction, and problem-solving beyond simple Q&A.

## Key Information
- One of three task distributions used in METR's time horizon measurements
- Tasks require interacting with tools and environments, thinking through problems — not just Q&A style
- Used alongside SWAR (atomic problems) and RE-Bench (challenging ML research tasks)
- Part of the benchmark-style evidence that shows rapid exponential progress in AI capabilities
