## Definition

Roelof Botha is a partner at Sequoia Capital and a former PayPal executive. He was on the board of Square and is cited by Keith Rabois for recognizing the exceptional operating tempo at Square.

## Key Information

- Partner at [[Sequoia]] Capital
- Former [[PayPal]] executive — part of the PayPal Mafia
- Led the Series B at [[Square]] and joined the board
- At Square, 6 months in (two board meetings), he told [[Keith Rabois]]: "I haven't seen this kind of tempo since our PayPal days" — he'd been a VC for 9 years at that point
- Defined tempo: "At board meeting X, you guys identify an opportunity or problem, and by the next board meeting, you've shipped solutions"
- This feedback highlighted speed as a key differentiator of successful companies
