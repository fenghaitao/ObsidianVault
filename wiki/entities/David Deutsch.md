## Definition

David Deutsch is a physicist and author of "The Beginning of Infinity," a book about the power of explanations and the potential for infinite progress through scientific understanding.

## Key Information

- Tomer Cohen recommended "The Beginning of Infinity" as one of his top book recommendations.
- Cohen described it as not an easy read but very powerful.
- The book emphasizes the idea of cause and effect and that only clear scientific understanding enables breakthroughs and infinite progress.
- Naval Ravikant frequently references this book.
