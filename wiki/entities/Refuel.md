## Definition
Refuel was an AI and machine learning infrastructure company co-founded by Rishabh Bhargava. It was acquired by Together AI in 2025.

## Key Information
- Co-founded by [[Rishabh Bhargava]], who served as CEO
- Focused on AI and machine learning infrastructure
- Acquired by [[Together AI]] in 2025
- Rishabh Bhargava transitioned to lead voice AI at Together AI after the acquisition
