## Definition
Arise is an AI company focused on evals (evaluations) for AI systems. RL Nabors joined as Principal Developer Experience Engineer. The company addresses the problem of CTOs accidentally breaking agentic flows through prompt changes or LLM migrations — evals are positioned as the solution to catch these regressions.

## Key Information
- Focus area: AI evals (evaluations) for agentic systems
- Key problem: CTOs can ruin agentic flows with slight prompt changes or LLM migrations without realizing it
- RL Nabors is Principal Developer Experience Engineer
- Positioned as helping teams validate that their agentic systems continue working correctly after changes
