## Definition
IBM is a multinational technology company that trains frontier AI models, builds agent harnesses, and develops enterprise AI solutions including the open-source Open RAG project.

## Key Information
- Trains frontier AI models (including Watson models)
- Has a lab focused on building AI harnesses
- Develops Open RAG — an open-source enterprise RAG project with a strong harness providing enterprise-level security for siloed data operations
- Employs AI Developer Advocates like Tejas Kumar
- Focuses on harness engineering to enable reliable AI in private, data-sensitive enterprise environments
- Open RAG performs RAG operations on teams, calls, PDFs, invoices, and other internal data
