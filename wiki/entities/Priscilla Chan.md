## Definition

Priscilla Chan, with her husband [[Mark Zuckerberg]], co-founded the [[Chan Zuckerberg Initiative]], which [[Molly Graham]] helped run and scale during its first two years.

## Key Information

- Named by Graham as the co-founder (alongside Zuckerberg) of the philanthropy she was recruited to help build from ~30 to 250 people in its first year.
