## Definition
Zach Blumenfeld is a research engineer at Neo4j, a graph intelligence company. He previously worked in technical marketing at Neo4j before transitioning back to his AI and machine learning roots in a research engineering role.

## Key Information
- Research engineer at [[Neo4j]], focused on AI and machine learning
- Previously worked in technical marketing at Neo4j
- Presented on context graphs and decision traces for AI agents at the AI Engineer conference
- Demonstrated how decision traces (past decisions with causal chains stored in a graph) enable agents to make better decisions than traditional document-based retrieval
- Showcased the [[Create Context Graph]] CLI tool and [[Neo4j Agent Memory]] package as practical ways to get started with context graphs
