## Definition

Genspark is an all-in-one AI workspace providing AI spreadsheets, slides, and documents for white-collar workers. Founded by Kay Zhu, the company philosophy emphasizes that there are no traditional moats — only team culture and execution speed matter in a fast-moving AI landscape.
