## Definition

CEO of [[Rackspace]], cited by [[Aishwarya Naresh Reganti]] (who worked with him before his CEO tenure) as a model of hands-on AI leadership within her "[[AI Product Success Triangle]]" framework.

## Key Information

- Blocks 4-6am every morning specifically for "catching up with AI," scheduling no meetings during that window; also runs weekend vibe-coding sessions.
- Reganti presents this not as a demand that leaders personally implement AI systems, but as a discipline of rebuilding intuitions built over 10-15 years of a career, which may no longer hold in the AI era — "you must be comfortable with the fact that your intuitions might not be right."
- Curates a small set of two or three trusted information sources rather than following every AI opinion, then bounces what he reads off a small group of AI experts (Reganti among them) before decisions get made — Reganti notes she is one of the people he consults.
