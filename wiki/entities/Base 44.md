## Definition

AI app-building ("vibe coding") tool, grouped by [[Zevi Arnovitz]] alongside [[Lovable]], Bolt, Replit, and v0 as an opinionated builder that abstracts away technical decisions (sign-in, database setup) in exchange for ease of use and reduced user control.

## Key Information

- Zevi built a custom quiz web app in Base 44 to drill product-management segmentation questions while preparing for his Meta PM interview, playing it on his phone during his commute.
- Cited approvingly, not dismissively, as an example of the "opinionated builder" category's real value: it "does an amazing job at basically taking all the complex guesswork out of building product," at the cost of not letting the user choose implementation details (e.g., which sign-in method or database to use).
