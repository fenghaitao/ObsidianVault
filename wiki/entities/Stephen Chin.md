## Definition
Stephen Chin is the VP of Developer Relations at Neo4j. He is a speaker and advocate for knowledge graphs, context graphs, and graph-based AI architectures.

## Key Information
- Runs the Developer Relations team at Neo4j
- Presented "Connecting the Dots with Context Graphs" at the aiDotEngineer conference (May 2026)
- Advocates for context graphs as a unified architecture combining knowledge graphs with short-term, long-term, and reasoning memory for agents
- Leads Neo4j's free education initiatives including GraphAcademy courses and free Aura instances
- Uses the "Matrix" metaphor (blue pill / red pill) to frame the choice between siloed enterprise knowledge and connected context graph reasoning
