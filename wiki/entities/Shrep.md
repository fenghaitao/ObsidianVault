## Definition

Shrep (Schrep) was the CTO of Facebook/Meta during Philip Su's tenure as Site Director of Meta London. He advised Philip on the promotion to E9 and the landing team size.

## Key Information

- Shrep was the CTO of Facebook when Philip was running the London office
- He advised Philip during his promotion to E9: when Philip expressed concern about maintaining E9 performance when eventually switching back to IC, Shrep said "let's worry about that problem when it happens — let's not not promote you because you think one day you're going to want to go back to coding"
- Shrep specified that the London landing team should have enough engineers for two full interview loops (~10-12 people)
- Philip described Shrep as giving "great advice and guidance"
