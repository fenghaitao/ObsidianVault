## Definition

Nick Turley is the head of ChatGPT at OpenAI and a former guest on Lenny's Podcast. He was instrumental in bringing Alexander Imbiricos and his company into OpenAI.

## Key Information

- Head of ChatGPT at OpenAI
- Described Alexander Imbiricos as "one of my all-time favorite humans I've ever worked with" and said bringing him and his company into OpenAI was "one of the best decisions we've ever made"
- His mantra is "are we maximally accelerated?" which has completely infiltrated all of OpenAI
- Was a former guest on Lenny's Podcast
- Suggested topics for this conversation along with Ed Bay and Dennis Yang
- Shares the philosophy that at AI companies, it's better to get products out quickly and see how people use them rather than spending time making them perfect
- The original name for ChatGPT was "super assistant"
