## Definition

Nick Turley is Head of [[ChatGPT]] at [[OpenAI]] and a prior [[Lenny's Podcast]] guest.

## Key Information

- Praised [[Alexander Imbiricos]] as "one of my all-time favorite humans I've ever worked with," crediting the decision to bring Imbiricos and his startup into OpenAI as one of the company's best.
- Per [[Lenny Rachitsky]]: in a separate prior interview, revealed that ChatGPT's original working name was something like "super assistant" — directly echoing Imbiricos's own framing of Codex/ChatGPT's end-state as a general-purpose super assistant.
- Imbiricos cites Turley's internal mantra, "are we maximally accelerated?", as a recurring, company-wide framing at OpenAI.
