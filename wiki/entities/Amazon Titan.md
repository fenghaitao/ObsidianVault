## Definition
Amazon Titan is AWS's own family of foundation models available through Amazon Bedrock, offered alongside models from third-party providers including Anthropic, AI21 Labs, Cohere, Meta, Mistral, and Stability AI.

## Key Information
- One of the model families available on Amazon Bedrock's fully managed service
- Part of AWS's strategy to offer model choice rather than a one-size-fits-all approach
- Available alongside leading third-party models via the unified Converse API
