## Definition

Former [[Microsoft]] Windows executive, later a colleague of [[Benedict Evans]] at [[Andreessen Horowitz]], quoted by Evans on how incumbents respond to disruptive new technology.

## Key Information

- Quoted line: incumbents always try to frame a disruptive new technology as "just a feature" of their existing product — "sometimes they're right, sometimes it's a feature."
- Cited in the context of Evans's Microsoft/Windows-vs-Netscape browser-wars analogy for thinking about AI foundation models' potential for platform-style leverage. See [[Model Commoditization]].
