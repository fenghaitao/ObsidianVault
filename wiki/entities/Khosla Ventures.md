## Definition

Khosla Ventures is a venture capital firm founded by Vinod Khosla, co-founder of Sun Microsystems. The firm focuses on early-stage and deep tech investments.

## Key Information

- Keith Rabois is a Managing Director at Khosla Ventures
- Vinod Khosla was on Square's board and taught Rabois "the team you build is the company you build"
- Known for investing in high-conviction, contrarian bets
