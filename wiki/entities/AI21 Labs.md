## Definition
AI21 Labs is an AI research company and foundation model provider whose models are available through Amazon Bedrock's managed service.

## Key Information
- One of the leading model providers integrated with Amazon Bedrock
- Available alongside Anthropic, Cohere, Meta, Mistral, Stability AI, and Amazon Titan on the Bedrock platform
- Accessible through the unified Converse API for standardized multi-model development
