## Definition

Jon Klassen is a children's book author and illustrator known for his beautiful, sweet, and darkly humorous books. Lenny and Michelle enjoy reading his books to their son Jude.

## Key Information

- Author of children's books that Lenny and Michelle love reading to their son
- His books are described as beautiful and sweet, but also dark — "they all end in death"
- Example: "I Want My Hat Back" — a bear book where the bunny gets eaten
- Jude, at nearly 3 years old, asks "He ate the bunny?" when reading the book
