## Definition
The AI Safety Institute (AISI) is the UK government's leading body for evaluating frontier AI models. It was the world's first such institute and represents a major win for UK AI policy. AISI evaluates models for safety risks including in cybersecurity contexts.

## Key Information
- UK's leading government body for evaluating frontier AI models
- Was the world's first AI safety institute
- Number 10 Data Science supported AISI from day one by placing fellows to help set up their cybersecurity work stream
- Dr. [[HarryCoppock]], an early 10DS fellow, led on the Inspect tool — a CFI isolated environment for testing what AI agents do when given autonomy and tools
- A major partner of [[Number10DataScience]]'s cross-government transformation work
