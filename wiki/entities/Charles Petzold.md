## Definition

Charles Petzold is the author of *Code* ("the secret language of hardware and software"), one of [[Max Schoening]]'s recommended books, given his observation that a surprising number of professionally employed programmers don't actually understand how computers work.

## Key Information

- Schoening: "so code by Charles Petzold... it's like do you know how computers actually work? It is actually surprising to me how many professionally employed programmers don't know how computers work." He notes the book doesn't contain a line of code until roughly chapter 27, calling it "exceptionally good."
