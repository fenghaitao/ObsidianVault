## Definition

CEO of [[WP Engine]] for the past 11 years; [[Jason Cohen]] considers her a genuine "late-joining co-founder" (a term he borrows from Reed Hoffman's description of Jeff Weiner at LinkedIn) given her impact on the company's success and culture.

## Key Information

- Co-won the 2017 Ernst & Young Entrepreneur of the Year award (Central Texas) with Cohen — a rare co-win Cohen says he can't recall another example of.
