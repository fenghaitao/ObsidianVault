## Definition

Heather Brunner is the CEO of WP Engine. Jason Cohen describes her as a "late-joining co-founder" — a term borrowed from Reed Hoffman's description of Jeff Weiner at LinkedIn — because her impact on the company's success and culture was so transformative that she is effectively a co-founder.

## Key Information

- CEO of WP Engine for 11+ years (as of the podcast recording)
- Co-won Ernst & Young Entrepreneur of the Year 2017 for Central Texas with Jason Cohen
- Co-winners are rare for the EY award
- Described by Jason Cohen as a "late-joining co-founder" — a term coined by Reed Hoffman for Jeff Weiner at LinkedIn
- Jason's test: "If I just told you that Heather was a co-founder, you'd say, 'Yeah, no kidding.'"
