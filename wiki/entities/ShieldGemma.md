## Definition
Shield Gemma is a family of safety/guardrail models released by Google as official Gemma variants, designed for production use cases where content moderation (filtering toxic images or text) is needed to enforce policy compliance.

## Key Information
- Official Gemma variant released by Google
- Family of models focused on content safety and guardrails
- Designed for production use cases requiring policy enforcement
- Can filter toxic images and text that don't match configured policies
- Part of Google's broader Gemma ecosystem of official variants
