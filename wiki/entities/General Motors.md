# General Motors (GM)

American automotive manufacturer. [[Qasar Younis]] worked as an engineer at GM early in his career, an experience he credits as formative.

## Key Information
- Qasar spent over a decade working in large organizations (100,000+ employees) like GM and [[Bosch]]
- Working in the "bowels" of these organizations taught him how bad it is to be an employee — bureaucracy, leadership disconnect, antiquated tools
- This experience directly informed his approach to leadership at [[Applied Intuition]]: creating policies and culture with the employee's perspective in mind
- Automotive engineering is fundamentally an exercise in quality — building complex machines at scale, producing a car every ~30 seconds
- Qasar: many founders who have never been employees at the bottom of the totem pole lack this perspective
