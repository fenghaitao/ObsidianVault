## Definition

StreamBase is the stream-processing engine company that grew out of Mike Stonebraker's academic project, cited in his "One Size Fits All" argument as a specialized engine unlike a relational database.

## Key Information

- Cited in the 2004 "One Size Fits All" paper: a stream processing engine "looks nothing like a relational database" and was an order of magnitude faster than the general-purpose alternative.
- Stonebraker described it in a September 2026 episode as "an academic project which was building what became StreamBase."
