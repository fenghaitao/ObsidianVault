## Definition

Whoop is a health tracking wearable that Jacob Warwick uses daily. He credits it with helping him lose almost 50 pounds by paying attention to sleep and recovery stats.

## Key Information

- Health tracking wearable (wristband)
- Tracks sleep, recovery, strain, and other health metrics
- Jacob Warwick: "I've lost almost 50 lb just paying attention to my Whoop stats and like trying to sleep"
- Jacob describes himself as "a pretty obsessive guy" and data "geeks me out"
- Used daily alongside MacroFactor and Claude Co-work
