## Definition

MLX is a machine learning framework made by Apple, optimized for Apple Silicon chips found in iPhones, iPads, and Macs. It enables efficient on-device inference for language models and has a growing ecosystem of companion libraries.

## Key Information

- Developed by Apple and optimized specifically for Apple Silicon (iPhone and Mac chips)
- Enables running LLMs on-device at high speed (e.g., Gemma 4 at 40 tokens/second on iPhone)
- Growing ecosystem includes:
  - MLX Swift LM — for iOS/macOS app integration
  - MLX VLM — for visual models (by Prince)
  - MLX Audio — for audio models
  - MLX Video — for image and video generation models
- Supports omni models, text-to-speech, and speech-to-speech
- Models are sourced from the MLX community on Hugging Face (4,000-5,000 models available)
- Also supported as an inference engine in LM Studio alongside Llama CPP
