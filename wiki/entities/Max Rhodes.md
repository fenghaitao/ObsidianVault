## Definition

Max Rhodes is the co-founder of Fair, a company Keith Rabois invested in from the seed round. He previously worked at Square under Keith. His story illustrates the importance of asking the right reference questions.

## Key Information

- Co-founder of [[Fair]]
- Former employee of [[Square]] under [[Keith Rabois]]
- Keith is a long-time investor from the seed round
- When VCs reference-checked Max, they asked "Was Max a good employee?" — the answer was mixed, causing some VCs (including talented ones) to pass on investing in Fair
- If they had asked "Is Max capable of being a world-class entrepreneur?" — the answer was definitively yes
- Many investors who passed on Fair regret it
- This illustrates the critical importance of framing reference questions correctly
