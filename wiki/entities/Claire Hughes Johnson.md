## Definition

Claire Hughes Johnson is the author of "Scaling People" and a former executive at Stripe. Molly Graham cites her phrase "strategy should hurt" as a key goal-setting principle.

## Key Information

- Author of "Scaling People"
- Originator of the phrase "strategy should hurt", which Molly Graham adopted as rule #4 of her Six Rules for Goal Setting
- Graham says this phrase is a much better way to explain the concept of setting non-goals and making painful trade-offs
