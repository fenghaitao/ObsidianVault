## Definition

Claire Hughes Johnson is the author of *Scaling People*, a former [[Stripe]] executive, and a prior guest on [[Lenny's Podcast]]. [[Molly Graham]] credits her with two phrases she has adopted into her own frameworks: "[[Strategy Should Hurt]]" and the "letter bomb" metaphor for broken promises (see "[[Do Not Promise What You Cannot Control]]").

## Key Information

- "Strategy should hurt" — Graham says she used to describe the same underlying idea as "set non-goals" but found Hughes Johnson's phrasing far more effective at communicating that real prioritization requires painful trade-offs. Now rule #4 of Graham's "[[Six Rules For Goals And Alignment]]."
- The "letter bomb" metaphor — a talk Hughes Johnson gave at [[Glue Club]] included the line that promises you can't actually control are "like letter bombs that you mail yourself that are going to explode in your face in like a year" — short-term comfort, long-term damage. Graham has since adopted this metaphor as her own.
- Per [[Jeanne DeWitt Grosser]] (episode 42, a Stripe colleague): quoted praising Grosser as "probably the best go-to-market person at connecting with product and engineering, deeply understanding the product and providing the most valuable input to her counterparts of any I've ever seen."
