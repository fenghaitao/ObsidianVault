## Definition

James Everingham is a former head of engineering at Instagram and an engineering leader whose ~30-year career spans Netscape, Yahoo, Meta, and his own companies. He is now co-founder/CEO of Guild. (The episode title uses "Ex-Head of Eng at Instagram"; the transcript names him "James Everingham.")

## Key Information

- Kicked out of Penn State at 18 (a 0.0 GPA for never attending class) and hired weeks later by the same university as full-time staff; took ~150 credits of advanced CS for free, never got a degree, and started his first company at 20.
- Worked at Borland, then Netscape (recruited by Lloyd Tabb), where he ran the Netscape browser during the browser wars.
- Later founded Luminate (an "Instagram shopping for publisher sites" product), sold it to Yahoo, then joined Instagram as head of engineering through network/timing.
- At Instagram he partnered with CTO Mike Krieger; engineering was hitting ~100 people ("a common place where teams start to break"), and he helped scale it through the break point.
- Recruited by David Marcus into Meta's Novi/Libra crypto project (which grew to ~3,500 people), then led Meta Dev Infra (building Dev Mate) before co-founding Lightspark and then Guild.
- Advice: go where the smart people are; offer help instead of asking for a mentor; speak up more; max out your 401(k) on day one; time (not money) becomes the scarce commodity.
