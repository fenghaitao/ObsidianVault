## Definition
Nicholas Kang (Nick) is a Product Manager at Kaggle Benchmarks within Google DeepMind. He runs the Benchmarks platform and focuses on agentic evaluation solutions. Originally from Singapore, he lives in the San Francisco Bay Area.

## Key Information
- Product Manager for Kaggle Benchmarks, building and running the Benchmarks platform
- Focuses on agentic eval solutions at Kaggle
- Originally from Singapore, based in the San Francisco Bay Area
- Presented at the AI Engineering conference on "Agentic Evaluations at Scale, For Everybody" alongside Michael Aaron
- Diagnosed three core problems with current AI evals: scattered/stale benchmarks, non-transparent results, and evals built by too few people
- Advocates for democratizing eval creation so everyone — not just AI researchers — can contribute benchmarks
- Emphasized that if something isn't evaluated, "we cannot hill climb on it"
