## Definition
Coursera is an online learning platform that hosts AI and machine learning courses, including the "Generative AI with LLMs" course developed by deeplearning.ai in collaboration with AWS.

## Key Information
- Hosts the "Generative AI with LLMs" course alongside deeplearning.ai
- Recommended by Antje Barth as a learning resource for aspiring AI engineers
- Platform for accessing structured AI education content
