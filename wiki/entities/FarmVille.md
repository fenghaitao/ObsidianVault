## Definition

Facebook farming-simulation game by [[Zynga]], one of the company's two biggest hits (with [[CityVille]]) by installs, engagement, and revenue, though it did not make the transition to mobile as durably as [[Words With Friends]] or [[Zynga Poker]].

## Key Information

- Cited by [[Mark Pincus]] as the example of defining ambition "in the eyes of your consumer" rather than peers: the target user was "nurses in Indiana," not fellow product-industry peers.
- An expansion pack ("English countryside") became a case study for [[Proven Better New]] / [[Kill Hope Before Hope Kills You]]: instead of spending a $10M ad budget externally, Zynga tested marketing/art variants directly on the live game board, generating both product signal and $19M in early-access "key" sales.
- Along with CityVille, had Zynga's best installs, engagement, retention, and revenue numbers, but is described as a "victim of its own success" — viral spread on Facebook annoyed non-playing friends.
