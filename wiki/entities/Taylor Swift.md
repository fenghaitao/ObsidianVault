## Definition

Referenced via an award-show acceptance speech (cited by [[Lenny Rachitsky]]) in which she described spending thousands of hours privately iterating on songwriting before ever exposing her work to feedback — used to introduce a discussion about the risk of exposing creative work to audience feedback too early.

## Key Information

- [[Keith Rabois]] connects her point to a broader pattern among musicians: early, non-data-driven creative success is often hard to replicate once an artist has an audience whose feedback (via labels/managers) pulls subsequent work toward more derivative, less inspired territory.
