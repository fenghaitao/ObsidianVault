## Definition

Dhanji R. Prasanna (rendered "John G Donji" in this episode's transcript — an ASR mis-hearing) is CTO of Block (formerly Square) and a prior [[Lenny's Podcast]] guest, where he discussed Block's internal AI agent, "[[Goose]]."

## Key Information

- Cited by [[Lenny Rachitsky]] recounting an anecdote from his own interview with Prasanna: an engineer at Block has Goose watch his screen and listen to his meetings all day, proactively shipping PRs, sending emails, and drafting Slack messages based on what it observes — offered as a real-world precedent for [[Alexander Imbiricos]]'s hypothetical "[[Chatter Driven Development]]" idea.
