## Definition

Jeremy Stoppelman is the co-founder and CEO of Yelp. He worked closely with Keith Rabois at PayPal and was promoted to Engineering Director and Vice President at PayPal before founding Yelp. He is cited as an example of an engineer with commercial instincts.
