## Definition
Nate Lidzén is a Google engineer who transferred from the search side to Vlad Feinberg's pre-training team and now owns much of its inference code design for Flash and Flash-Lite.
## Key Information
- Vlad cites him as a "really great example" of an internal transfer: he approached it by helping his product area adopt LLMs as effectively as possible.
- By becoming the person who serves and applies LLMs well inside his org, he naturally became the research side's partner — and then transferred.
- Vlad's advice for internal transfers: you are already doing cutting-edge work integrating the tech into a real product, whether or not you transfer.
