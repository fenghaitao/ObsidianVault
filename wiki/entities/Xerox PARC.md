## Definition

Xerox PARC (transcribed in the episode as "Xerox Park") is Xerox's Palo Alto Research Center, the birthplace of personal computing, the Ethernet, and distributed personal computing.

## Key Information

- The people who later formed DEC Systems Research Center came from Xerox PARC, having "invented personal computing."
- They also had the notion of distributed personal computing and invented the Ethernet for it.
- Butler Lampson and Chuck Thacker were two of the senior figures in that lab.
