## Definition
Ken Thompson is the co-inventor of Unix and the author of the Unix philosophy maxim "Write programs that do one thing and do one thing well." His philosophy was cited by Matthias Luebken as a guiding principle for agent system design.

## Key Information
- Co-inventor of Unix alongside Dennis Ritchie
- Author of the famous Unix philosophy: "Write programs that do one thing and do one thing well"
- This philosophy was cited at AIE Code Summit as applicable to agent design: build small, focused tools that agents can compose, rather than complex monolithic systems
- The principle supports the "make it easy for agents" architectural pattern: expose simple CLIs that do one thing well, and let the agent compose them
