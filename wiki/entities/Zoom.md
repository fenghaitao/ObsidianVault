## Definition
Zoom is a video conferencing platform. In the context of voice agents, Zoom calls represent one of the omni-channel interaction paradigms unlocked when chat agents are upgraded to voice agents.

## Key Information
- Video conferencing platform used for remote meetings
- Referenced by Luke Harries as an example of omni-channel voice: a PostHog voice agent could join a Zoom call and correct participants giving wrong stats
- Demonstrates how voice agents extend beyond web widgets to integrate with existing communication tools
