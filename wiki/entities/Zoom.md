## Definition

Zoom is a video-conferencing company that launched an [[ModelContextProtocol|MCP]] connector for [[ClaudeCowork|Claude Cowork]] in April 2026, bringing meeting intelligence into agentic Cowork workflows.

## Key Information

- The Zoom connector delivers AI Companion meeting summaries and action items, alongside transcripts and smart recordings, directly into Cowork.
- Enables teams to build agentic workflows in Cowork on top of their Zoom meeting conversations.
- Added from the connector directory in Claude's settings.
