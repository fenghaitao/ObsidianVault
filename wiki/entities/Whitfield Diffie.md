## Definition

Whitfield Diffie (transcribed in the episode as "Whit Diffy") is a cryptographer, co-author (with Martin Hellman) of the Diffie-Hellman key exchange paper, and a friend of Leslie Lamport.

## Key Information

- In a coffee-house conversation, Diffie described the open problem of building digital signatures that he and Hellman hadn't solved.
- Lamport "literally on a napkin" wrote out the first digital signature algorithm, which was impractical at the time (roughly 128 bits to sign one bit), but gave Lamport digital signatures in his toolkit.
- The Diffie-Hellman paper appeared around 1975; very few people knew about digital signatures then, which is partly why Lamport's Byzantine-generals solution with signatures got less attention.
