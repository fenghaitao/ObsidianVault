## Definition

Carey Nachenberg is a cybersecurity engineer who rose to Fellow (Symantec's senior-most technical rank), then worked at Google X, Lyft's self-driving team, and became a UCLA part-time lecturer.

## Key Information

- Joined the Peter Norton Group in 1992 as its first intern; left in 2016 as Symantec's senior-most engineer (Fellow, roughly four levels above staff)
- Early work included writing assembly and porting the first antivirus engines to C, making them ~5x faster with better algorithms (hash/binary search over linear search)
- Spent his whole Symantec career self-directed — never assigned work, he picked impactful projects where the company had gaps
- Recruited into Google X by a former Symantec COO, joining as a Principal Engineer after being down-leveled from a Symantec "level 10 / VP" (Google won't hire unseen fellows)
- At Google X / Chronicle he built the indexing core of Backstory, a petabyte-scale cybersecurity log search product
- At Lyft he designed an end-to-end neural-network driving architecture with a safety "guard rails" layer, arguing hand-coded robotics heuristics were a dead end
- Learned the hard way to claim credit after others took credit for the Lyft architecture
- Became a UCLA lecturer in 2001 after a last-minute "our lecturer bailed" call with two weeks' notice; has lectured for about 25 years
- Teaches with empathy for the median student, humor, and visual playfulness; allows LLMs for learning but not for projects
