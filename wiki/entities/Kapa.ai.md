## Definition

Kapa.ai is a technology company that helps organizations turn their technical knowledge bases into production-ready AI assistants. They specialize in knowledge base to AI assistant transformation with Claude.

## Key Information

- **Specialization**: Converts technical knowledge bases into functional AI assistants
- **Migration experience**: Successfully migrated critical AI workflows from other models to [[Claude3.5Sonnet]]
- **Tooling**: Uses Anthropic's [[PromptEngineering]] tools, including the prompt improver feature in the [[AnthropicConsole]]
- **Outcome**: The prompt improver streamlined their migration to Claude 3.5 Sonnet and enabled faster time-to-production deployment
- **Leadership**: Founded by Finn Bauer (Co-Founder)

## Use of Claude

Kapa.ai leveraged the [[AnthropicConsole]] prompt improver tool to:
- Adapt existing AI workflows to Claude 3.5 Sonnet
- Refine prompts using automated prompt engineering techniques
- Achieve faster production readiness
- Enable reliable AI-powered knowledge base assistants
