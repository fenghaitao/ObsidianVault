## Definition

Claude Opus 4.5, released by Anthropic in November 2025, was one of the two models (alongside GPT-5.1) that crossed the threshold where AI coding agents went from "mostly works" to "almost always does what you tell it." Simon Wilson identifies this as the inflection point for AI coding.

## Key Information

- Released in November 2025 alongside GPT-5.1
- Crossed a threshold: previously coding agents would "mostly work" but required close attention; Opus 4.5 made it so "almost all of the time it does what you told it to do"
- The improvement was "incremental but crossed a threshold" — a qualitative leap, not just a quantitative one
- Software engineers who tinkered with it over the holidays had a "moment of realization" that the technology actually works now
- Part of Anthropic's year-long focus on coding, using reinforcement learning and reasoning models
