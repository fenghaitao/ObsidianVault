## Definition
ASA (Anti-Social Social Agent) is an MCP app created by RL Nabors that lets users interact with Twitter and BlueSky side by side without visiting the sites or being subjected to their algorithms. It demonstrates the concept of agent-mediated social media consumption.

## Key Information
- Created by RL Nabors as an MCP application
- Allows side-by-side interaction with Twitter and BlueSky
- Users avoid visiting the actual social media sites
- Users avoid being subjected to platform algorithms
- Example of agent-mediated content consumption — agents fetch and present content on the user's terms
- Demonstrates the MCP Apps pattern of replacing direct website visits with agent-mediated interfaces
