## Definition

Chinese technology company [[Qasar Younis]] cites (via the book *House of Huawei*) as the clearest example of why Chinese tech giants shouldn't be compared directly to Western companies: roughly a quarter of its couple-hundred-thousand employees are Communist Party members, and its actual goal is not shareholder profit but serving as an extension of the Chinese state — literally, "Huawei" translates to "China's ambition." See [[China As State Extension]].

## Key Information

- Younis's analogy: comparing Huawei to Apple because both make phones is like assuming a company called "MAGA" makes normal products just because a quarter of its staff belong to a political party whose goal is that party's expansion, not profit.
- Used to argue that China's competition with the US isn't really company-vs-company (e.g., OpenAI vs. DeepSeek) but company-vs-state (OpenAI vs. the Chinese government).
