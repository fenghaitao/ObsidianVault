# Huawei

Chinese telecommunications and technology company. [[Qasar Younis]] described it as fundamentally different from Western companies — an extension of the Chinese state rather than a profit-driven enterprise.

## Key Information
- ~200,000+ employees, approximately 25% are members of the Communist Party
- The name "Huawei" literally means "China's ambition"
- Qasar: "It's not even a company anymore. It's something else."
- Goals are not profit or shareholder returns, but expansion of state interests
- This makes comparisons with [[Apple]] or other Western companies fundamentally misleading
- Recommended reading: "House of Huawei" (book)
