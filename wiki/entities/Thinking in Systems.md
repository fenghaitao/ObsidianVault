## Definition

*Thinking in Systems* is a book by Donella Meadows, recommended by [[Matt MacInnis]] in the episode's lightning round.

## Key Information

- Meadows died partway through writing the manuscript; fellow professors completed it posthumously.
- MacInnis calls it the best generic framework for understanding how systems work, and says readers will find themselves extrapolating its ideas to every aspect of life after reading it.
