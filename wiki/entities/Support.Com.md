## Definition

Support.com is the second company [[Mark Pincus]] founded, coming after [[Freeloader]] and before [[Tribe]] in his career arc leading up to [[Zynga]].

## Key Information

- Sits in Pincus's own retelling of his career arc: Freeloader (offline browsing) → Support.com → Tribe (social networking, failed from being too ambitious) → Zynga (deliberately started "embarrassingly small") → Earth (his ~20-year, still-unfinished metaverse project).
