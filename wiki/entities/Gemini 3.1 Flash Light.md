## Definition
Gemini 3.1 Flash Light is the smallest and most cost-effective model in Google DeepMind's Gemini 3.1 series, offering strong price-performance ratio and fast inference speed, gaining traction among users migrating from Gemini 2.0 Flash and 2.5 Flash.

## Key Information
- Smallest model in the Gemini 3.1 series
- Strong price-performance ratio with fast inference speed
- Gaining traction among users upgrading from Gemini 2.0 Flash and 2.5 Flash
- Supports multimodal inputs (video, images, audio, text, code)
- Can perform video analysis with YouTube URLs, processing ~27,600 tokens for 5 minutes of content
- Works with code execution, search grounding, and URL context tools in AI Studio
- Can analyze images and draw bounding boxes at well under a fraction of a penny per operation
