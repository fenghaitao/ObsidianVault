## Definition

Foursquare is a location-based social networking platform that was popular in the early 2010s. Lenny Rachitsky's startup Local Mind was built on top of Foursquare's API.

## Key Information

- Local Mind sat on top of Foursquare and Gowalla, using their check-in data
- Was very popular ("cool") at the time alongside Gowalla
- Allowed third-party developers to build location-based apps on top of its platform
