## Definition
Databricks Genie is a third-party GenBI (Gen AI + Business Intelligence) solution that enables natural language querying of data. It was mentioned as a competing product that Northwestern Mutual is evaluating against their internally developed GenBI system.

## Key Information
- A GenBI solution from Databricks that competes with internally built GenBI agents
- Northwestern Mutual is evaluating it as a potential alternative to their in-house GenBI system
- The incremental research approach at Northwestern Mutual produced benchmarks that can be used to evaluate third-party solutions like Databricks Genie
- Asaf Bord noted that third-party solutions may be harder to govern from the outside compared to internally built systems
- The existence of solutions like Databricks Genie reinforces the value of the incremental approach: if a third-party solution becomes good enough, the company can pull the plug on internal development and adopt it
