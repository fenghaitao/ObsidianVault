## Definition
AMD (Advanced Micro Devices) is a semiconductor company whose Ryzen Max Plus 395 CPU was used by Liquid AI as a target hardware platform for on-device profiling of small model architectures.

## Key Information
- AMD Ryzen Max Plus 395 used as a profiling target for LFM 2 architecture optimization
- On-device profiling on this CPU demonstrated LFM 2's short convolution advantage in inference speed and memory usage
- Part of Liquid AI's approach of optimizing for real hardware rather than theoretical benchmarks
