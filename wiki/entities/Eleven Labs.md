## Definition

Eleven Labs is a voice-AI company and the main competitor of Cartesia.

## Key Information

- Nimit described Eleven Labs as a very similar voice-AI company to Cartesia, with roughly an 18-month head start.
- He used to play with Eleven Labs long before Cartesia existed to make fun videos.
- Per Nimit, Eleven Labs wins on some languages while Cartesia wins on others; Cartesia currently leads on latency and cost and has better voice cloning.
