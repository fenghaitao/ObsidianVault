## Definition
The UR5 is a robotic arm from Universal Robots, used in robotics research.
## Key Information
- In a Physical Intelligence cross-embodiment experiment, a "thinking stage" expressed as images let a UR5 fold a t-shirt even though there was no t-shirt-folding data collected on the UR5.
- The image-thinking trick works because the model has seen the UR5 arm in many poses and shirts in many folding states, so it can "dream up" a synthesized next-milestone image and back out the required joint angles from it.
- Illustrates that cross-robot skill transfer can be facilitated by "thinking in the right modality," rather than by special morphology-aware model design.
