## Definition
Marvis is a custom text-to-speech (TTS) model developed by Neywa Labs, built on MLX for Apple Silicon. It generates high-quality audio in under 100 milliseconds, enabling real-time voice responses for on-device AI applications.

## Key Information
- Developed by Prince Canuma at Neywa Labs
- Built on MLX for Apple Silicon (Mac, iPhone, iPad)
- Generates audio in under 100 milliseconds latency
- Part of the MLX Audio modular speech pipeline
- Used in the Locally AI app to give the chatbot voice capabilities
- Powers the Richie Mini robot with real-time voice responses and voice cloning
- Supports both Python and Swift for integration into native apps
- Available as a component in the modular speech pipeline (selectable TTS model)
