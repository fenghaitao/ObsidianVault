## Definition

Kent Beck is a software engineer and author known for extreme programming and test-driven development; his "explore, expand, extract" (three X's) framework was cited by Stefan Mai to explain company lifecycles.

## Key Information

- Author of the explore/expand/extract lifecycle framework
- Explore phase: asymmetric upside — you pay a constant cost to search for breakout velocity, so move fast and avoid constraints
- Extract phase: upside is capped at ~1-2% improvements with catastrophic risk looming, so risk-averse discipline dominates
- Amazon's culture leans toward the lean/extract end, while early Facebook sat much closer to explore
