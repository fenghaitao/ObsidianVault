## Definition

Jia Chen is a college student, hackathon competitor, content creator, and startup founder. She won 21 hackathons with a 70% win rate and co-founded Sprint.dev.

## Key Information

- Won 21 hackathons with approximately a 70% win rate, starting from a non-technical background as a finance major
- Started hackathons after taking one Python class and getting a 4.0; lost her first hackathon but became hooked on the enthusiasm for building
- Secretly rented a car and drove to Ohio for her second hackathon (Hacks) without telling her parents, and won first place in the hardware category
- Attended Michigan State University, a non-target school, before taking a gap semester to work on her startup in San Francisco
- Balanced a 3.5+ engineering GPA, 15 hackathons in one semester, and growing to 50K followers on social media through time blocking
- Had a music career on Spotify in high school with 150K streams before transitioning to tech
- Co-founded Sprint.dev with Aiden Goolan — an app that helps people learn to enter hackathons; the MVP was shipped overnight, crashed from traffic, and later raised angel and VC investment
- Landed seven Y Combinator companies and 50 other companies as clients, reaching five figures MRR
- Turned down a return to college (MSU), considering transferring to a California school instead
- Created short-form content about hackathons, with a video about receiving angel checks blowing up to 500K views on Instagram
- Recommended building something people want to use, getting projects out of localhost, and developing a personal brand for leverage
