## Definition

Early peer-to-peer file-sharing service, cited by [[Mark Pincus]] as the origin point of his "[[Cocktail Party Distribution]]" framing — the first time people were connected to each other and the shared "lead" was a music file.

## Key Information

- Pincus: "That cocktail party for me it started with Napster when all of a sudden all of us were connected to each other and the great lead was a music file."
- Positioned as the first step in a lineage of "cocktail party" products that generate valuable connections/leads: Napster → [[Facebook]] → [[LinkedIn]] (each generating "better lead generation" / productivity for users' time).
