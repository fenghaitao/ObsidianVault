## Definition

Anker is a consumer electronics company known for power stations, docks, and recharging accessories. Jason Cohen recommends Anker products for their high quality and fast charging performance.

## Key Information

- Makes power stations, docks, and recharging accessories
- Jason Cohen: "Everything seems to charge twice as fast when plugged into an Anker thing"
- Praised for super high quality and reliable performance
- Lenny Rachitsky also uses Anker chargers, noting their displays showing percentage, wattage per outlet, and other advanced features
- Described as a company that asks "How do we make this more fancy and fun and charge more?"
