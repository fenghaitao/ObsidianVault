## Definition

Al Vermeulen was one of the early engineers at AWS, a major contributor to the design of Amazon S3 and many of AWS's database services, and CTO of Amazon for a period before deciding that role wasn't what he wanted.

## Key Information

- Early AWS engineer and a huge contributor to the design of S3 and AWS's database services over time.
- Was CTO of Amazon for a period, then realized that wasn't the job he wanted to do.
- Brooker admired his ability to work at every level — from arguing edge cases in the Paxos paper (~2010) to executive conversations about cloud strategy and what to build.
- Brooker explicitly says he wants to model his own career after that breadth of working "almost at every level."
