## Definition

Gong is a revenue intelligence platform that records, transcribes, and analyzes sales calls and customer interactions. It has become "meaningfully more interesting" with the ability to run AI agents against its transcripts.

## Key Information

- Jeanne DeWitt Grosser calls Gong "incredible" because you can now run AI agents against its transcripts.
- Vercel uses Gong transcripts to power the "Dealbot" and "Lostbot" AI agents:
  - Lostbot: Reviews lost opportunities and identifies true reasons for loss (e.g., flagged a deal attributed to "price" as actually lost due to failure to demonstrate value and connect with economic buyer).
  - Dealbot: Runs in real-time, feeding insights into Slack channels per customer — alerts on missing economic buyer engagement, call quality issues, and provides follow-up suggestions.
- Also used for continuous enablement: after product launches, agents analyze calls to find objection handling gaps, enabling weekly "bug fix" sprints on the GTM process.
