## Definition

Gong is a sales call-recording/transcription tool, cited by [[Jeanne DeWitt Grosser]] (COO, [[Vercel]]) as having become meaningfully more useful in the past year because it can now be fed into AI agents.

## Key Information

- Vercel dumps all of its Gong call transcripts into an internal agent ("dealbot") that analyzes lost deals, cross-referencing Gong calls with Slack and email interactions to surface the real reason a deal was lost — e.g., revealing that a deal reported lost "on price" was actually lost because the team never reached the economic buyer and failed to demonstrate ROI convincingly.
- The same underlying data pipeline was extended into a real-time, in-Slack deal-coaching agent that flags issues like "you're this far into the sales process and haven't spoken to an economic buyer yet."
