## Definition

Tal Rav (also spelled Tal Riv) is an AI-forward product manager, previous Lenny's Podcast guest, and collaborator who introduced Zevi Arnovitz to Lenny Rachitsky. He described Zevi as "the most hands-on vibe coding PM I know."

## Key Information

- Previous Lenny's Podcast guest and multiple-time collaborator
- Described by Lenny as "one of the most AI forward product managers that I know"
- Introduced Zevi Arnovitz to Lenny with high praise: "His engineers at Meta ask him to teach them how to do what he does. Every time we get coffee, I repeatedly get this feeling of everyone needs to be hearing this."
- Has a course on building a PM co-pilot using GPT Projects
- Told Zevi: "code is just words at the end of the day" — a framing that helped Zevi overcome fear of code
