## Definition

Spider-Man 2 (2004, starring Tobey Maguire) is referenced by Simon Wilson as a metaphor for Open Claw and AI assistants. The villain Doc Ock (Alfred Molina) has four AI-controlled mechanical claws grafted to his body, controlled by an inhibitor chip. When the chip breaks, the claws take control of him — "yeah, that's Open Claw."

## Key Information

- 2004 film starring Tobey Maguire as Spider-Man and Alfred Molina as Doc Ock
- Doc Ock's AI claws are controlled by an inhibitor chip in the back of his head
- When the inhibitor chip breaks, the AI claws start controlling him instead
- Simon Wilson: "yeah, that's Open Claw. It's the baddie from Spider-Man 2"
