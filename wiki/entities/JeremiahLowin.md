## Definition

Jeremiah Lowin is the CEO and founder of Prefect, and the author of FastMCP, a Python framework for building MCP servers. Has ~20 years in open source, always in ML/data with Pythonic interfaces.

## Key Information

### FastMCP

- FastMCP simplifies building MCP servers in Python
- Had to inline JSON Schema enums because Opus models couldn't handle schema references
- Battle-hardened through community feedback across many models and clients

### On AI Slop

- Most effective heuristic for filtering AI slop PRs: close PRs with overly long descriptions (LLMs love verbose explanations)
- Tried requiring issues before PRs — bots just opened issues 1 second before PRs
- Looking for "constructive friction" — like the email spam micro-computation tax idea
- Prefect opens its conference facilities to any Python/data meetup in Washington, D.C.
- Prefect is a member of the Open Source Pledge ($2,000/engineer/year)
