## Definition

Loom is a video recording and sharing tool. Rachel Lockett recommends it as a favorite product she recently discovered, using it to record trainings for clients at a scaled holding company.

## Key Information

- Rachel Lockett's favorite recently discovered product
- Used for recording trainings for clients
- Enables scaling training in a "human connected way" through async video
- Particularly useful for working with scaled holding companies
- Zevi Arnovitz became disappointed with Loom: "They were taking so much money and the product... I just didn't love it"
- Zevi switched to CAP, an open-source alternative, and Lenny also recommended Supercut as another Loom alternative
