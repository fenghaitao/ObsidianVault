## Definition

Founder of [[Craigslist]]; former neighbor and personal acquaintance of [[Mark Pincus]] (via Pincus's dog, Zynga, the dog after whom Pincus later named his company).

## Key Information

- Spent two years adding photos to Craigslist listings, obsessing over preserving the ability to quickly scan text, described by Pincus as the mark of a "world-class product maker."
- Pincus recounts a personal anecdote: Newmark was "friends with my dog, Zynga, more than he was friends with me," and it was through the dog that Pincus learned about the two-year photo project.
