## Definition

Google Spanner is Google's globally distributed database that shipped a conventional transactional system, marking Google's abandonment of eventual consistency and MapReduce.

## Key Information

- Stonebraker: Spanner "had a conventional transactional system"; after building it, Google "completely abandoned eventual consistency and completely abandoned MapReduce," crediting Jeff Dean.
