## Definition

Sarah Caldwell is described as "a big deal at [[OpenAI]]" and a former colleague/mentee of [[Molly Graham]]. She suggested topics/questions for this episode and named Graham's "[[J Curve Versus Stairs]]" framework as the single most impactful thing she's learned from her.

## Key Information

- Keeps a sticker on her notebook with three pieces of advice Graham gave her when she started at OpenAI: "get to know your customer, they have the answer," "be patient because everything is going to change," and "just keep trying."
- Graham expands on the "get to know your customer" advice as an anchor point amid organizational instability — the customer is one of the few things that "will never change," even as internal structure and roles shift constantly, which she says applies with particular force at OpenAI.
- Credited (along with Eric Antinau and Ashley Murphy) with suggesting topics/questions for this episode.
