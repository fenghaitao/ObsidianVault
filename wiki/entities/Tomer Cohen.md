## Definition

Tomer Cohen is the long-time Chief Product Officer at LinkedIn, where he served for 14 years before announcing his departure in 2026. He is the architect of LinkedIn's full-stack builder model.

## Key Information

- Joined LinkedIn before the Microsoft acquisition and helped integrate the company post-acquisition.
- Led the transformation of LinkedIn's product perception from a staid professional network to a dynamic, engaging platform.
- Pioneered the full-stack builder (FSB) model at LinkedIn, reimagining how product is built in the age of AI.
- Replaced LinkedIn's APM (Associate Product Manager) program with the Associate Full-Stack Builder (APB) program.
- Created a formal career ladder with the title "full-stack builder" within LinkedIn.
- Moved his direct leadership team from functional leaders (design, PM, BD) to product area leaders evaluated via 360-degree feedback across functions.
- Emphasizes five irreplaceable human builder traits: vision, empathy, communication, creativity, and judgment.
- Believes that "becoming is better than being" (growth mindset) and that organizations should focus on continuous progress rather than reaching an end state.
- Previously co-rebuilt the LinkedIn feed and led the desktop-to-mobile transformation at LinkedIn.
- Announced his departure from LinkedIn after 14 years, expressing excitement for new problem sets and the next decade of building.
