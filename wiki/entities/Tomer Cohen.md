## Definition

Tomer Cohen was longtime Chief Product Officer at [[LinkedIn]], announcing his departure after 14 years around the time of this episode. He led the design and rollout of LinkedIn's "[[Full-Stack Builder Model]]."

## Key Information

- Joined LinkedIn years after first learning about it at a Stanford lecture on professional social networks given by [[Reid Hoffman]] in 2008; had briefly started his own company in between.
- Co-rebuilt the LinkedIn feed from scratch roughly a decade-plus before this episode — a formative experience he explicitly connects to the current agent-era lesson that curated "golden examples" beat unrestricted data access when training a system (human or AI) to recognize quality.
- Core thesis: at LinkedIn's scale, the product-development process had accumulated enormous, individually-justified complexity (many research sources, multiple review types, many sub-specialized roles) that collectively slowed shipping dramatically; LinkedIn's own data shows the skills required for existing jobs will change by ~70% by 2030, which he treats as forcing a first-principles rebuild of how product work is organized.
- Architect of the "[[Full-Stack Builder Model]]": individual builders (any function) take ideas from research through launch, working in small cross-functional pods; preserves five human traits (vision, empathy, communication, creativity, judgment — especially judgment) while automating everything else via custom internal agents (trust, growth, research, analyst, coding, maintenance/QA, "product jammer").
- Replaced LinkedIn's traditional APM (Associate Product Manager) program with a new "Associate Full-Stack Builder" program teaching coding, design, and PM skills together from day one.
- Emphasizes that culture/change-management — not platform or tooling — was the hardest and most important part of the rollout: tying adoption to performance reviews and hiring criteria, showcasing visible pilot wins, running a deliberately gradual, non-top-down rollout.
- Notes counterintuitively that LinkedIn's top performers adopted the new tools/workflow fastest and most enthusiastically — suggesting AI tends to amplify already-strong builders rather than uniformly lifting weaker ones.
- Life motto: "becoming is better than being" — continuous growth/iteration valued over reaching any fixed end state.
- Personal: recommends *Why Nations Fail*, *Outlive* (Peter Attia), and *The Beginning of Infinity* (David Deutsch); enjoys the Hebrew podcast "One Song" (similar to *Song Exploder*); uses ChatGPT voice mode during commutes and Tesla's built-in [[Grok]] assistant.
