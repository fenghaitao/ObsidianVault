## Definition

Annie Pearl is a product leader and Jessica Fain's first PM manager at Box. She is known for the mantra "It's not my fault, but it is my problem." She was previously a guest on Lenny's Podcast.

## Key Information

- Jessica Fain's first PM manager at Box
- Former CPO at Calendly and Glassdoor
- Now at Microsoft
- Previously a guest on Lenny's Podcast
- Known for the mantra: "It's not my fault, but it is my problem" — a key philosophy for product leaders taking ownership of outcomes even when they're not directly responsible
