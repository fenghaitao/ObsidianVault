## Definition

Code with Claude is Anthropic's developer conference: a full-day event of live workshops, product demos, technical talks, and 1:1 office hours with the teams behind Claude, aimed at developers and founders building with Claude.

## Key Information

- Launched in San Francisco (2025); expanded to San Francisco, London, and Tokyo for spring 2026 — its first time running in London and Tokyo.
- Agenda includes technical sessions and talks from Anthropic engineers alongside developers at startups and enterprises shipping with Claude.
- In-person seats are limited across all three cities; attendees are selected by random drawing among applicants, with notifications in early April.
- Livestream access and session recordings are available to everyone, regardless of in-person selection.
- San Francisco 2026 dates: two days of keynotes/breakout sessions/workshops in May 2026; London follows May 20–21, 2026, and Tokyo follows June 5–6, 2026 — Day 1 keynotes and breakout sessions streamed live for both.

### London 2026 (May 20–21)

- [[BorisCherny]], Head of [[ClaudeCode]], kicked off the London keynote describing the "magic" of coding and how AI agents are collapsing the distance between "I have an idea" and "it runs."
- Breakout sessions covered going beyond the basics with [[ClaudeCode]], optimizing [[ExtendedThinking|thinking budgets and effort levels]] across Claude models, and scaling DevEx with [[ClaudeCode]].
- Customer sessions featured [[Spotify]] (scaling DevEx to teams and agents), [[Base44]] (scaling a hypergrowth engineering org with Claude Code), and [[Legora]] (what legal agents inherit from coding agents).
- Announced self-hosted sandboxes (public beta) and MCP tunnels (research preview) for [[ClaudeManagedAgents]], plus customer adoption from [[Amplitude]], [[Clay]], and [[Rogo]].
- Full session recordings available at claude.com/code-with-claude/london. See [[summary-2026-05-26 - Code w Claude London 2026 Rethinking how we build]].
