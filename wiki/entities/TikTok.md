## Definition

TikTok is a short-video social media platform owned by ByteDance, referenced throughout this interview as [[Instagram]]'s primary competitor in short-video and recommendation systems.

## Key Information

- [[Adam Mosseri]] says he has always been impressed by TikTok/ByteDance's ability to use exploration-based ranking to help small/niche creators break out and find an audience — an area Instagram has been catching up on. See [[Exploration Based Ranking]].
- TikTok's explosive growth is tied by Mosseri to the 2020 pandemic, when people had more free time at home and were more willing to have their phone sound on — timing that coincided with Instagram's early version of [[Reels]] being under-positioned (built on top of [[Stories]] rather than standalone).
- Mosseri believes that if Instagram had shipped its eventual Reels product a year earlier (summer 2019 instead of summer 2020), TikTok would likely be smaller today, though he still considers it big and important.
- Mosseri credits TikTok/ByteDance with inspiring much of Instagram's recent investment in ranking for originality, content breakout rate, and cultural recency; says Instagram believes it is now ahead of TikTok in some areas of recommendation quality and behind in others, with a path to being best-in-class.
- Mosseri explicitly says he's "glad they exist" — TikTok's growth is framed as having created real economic opportunity.
