## Definition

Sarah Guo is the founder of Conviction, an AI-focused venture fund, and co-host (with Elad Gil) of the podcast *No Priors*. Referred to in the transcript as "Sarah Gua," an AI-transcription rendering of her name.

## Key Information

- Cited by [[Matt MacInnis]], alongside [[Mike Vernal]], as one of the investors he considers most deeply focused on and knowledgeable about AI — he says his early skepticism of Conviction's narrow AI focus proved wrong in hindsight.
- MacInnis and [[Lenny Rachitsky]] both recommend her podcast *No Priors* to listeners interested in AI investing.
