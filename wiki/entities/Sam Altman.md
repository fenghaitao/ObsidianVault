## Definition

Sam Altman is the CEO of OpenAI. He is credited with coining the phrase "one-person billion dollar startup" and has reinforced OpenAI's philosophy as an ecosystem platform company from the top.

## Key Information

- CEO of OpenAI
- Credited with coining "one-person billion dollar startup" — the vision that AI leverage could enable a single individual to build a billion-dollar company
- Along with Greg Brockman, reinforces OpenAI's commitment to being an ecosystem platform company from the top
- OpenAI's mission under his leadership: build AGI and spread its benefits to all of humanity
- Former president of [[Y Combinator]] when [[Qasar Younis]] served as COO
- Qasar told Sam not to announce his COO role for a year — to test internally whether it would work

- Jacob Warwick had clients interviewing for C-suite roles at OpenAI who would walk outside the office with Sam Altman — a tactic OpenAI used to keep executives out of the chaotic office, which Jacob describes as "two midgets in a trench coat pretending to be a real company"
- Jacob: "Pushing Sam Altman around a little bit which I'm okay with"
