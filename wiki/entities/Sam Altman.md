## Definition

Co-founder of [[OpenAI]]. Provided a cover quote for [[Mark Pincus]]'s book [[Life at the Speed of Play]], stating that today the only bottleneck to building great products is knowing what to create.

- Per [[Eric Ries]] (Incorruptible): referenced, alongside Elon Musk, as one of the "dueling" personalities in the OpenAI governance saga that Ries calls "a really hard case study to learn from."

## Key Information

- Per [[Benedict Evans]] (episode 07, referring to him as "Sam Waltman"/"Sam Alman" in the transcript): quoted as having said OpenAI will be "selling AI intelligence on a meter like water or electricity" — Evans pushes back hard on this framing, arguing Altman may be underestimating how thin utility-style margin structures actually are (electricity/water utilities don't take a cut of what's built on top of their service).
- Evans jokes that if AI fully replaced human labor with no offsetting new jobs, "all the money will go to Sam[,] Waltman[,] and Mark [Zuckerberg] can buy himself another Gulf Stream" — used rhetorically to introduce his rebuttal (the historical "lump of labor fallacy" pattern) rather than as his actual prediction.
- Per [[Qasar Younis]] (episode 22): was president of [[Y Combinator]] during Younis's tenure as COO there; Younis asked him not to publicly announce the COO appointment for about a year, so that if it didn't work out there would be no public scrutiny of a short, failed tenure.
