## Definition
Sam Altman is the CEO of OpenAI, cited in the MCP Apps talk for stating that 800 million people use ChatGPT on a weekly basis (October 2025), a statistic used to illustrate the distribution opportunity for MCP Apps.

## Key Information
- CEO of OpenAI
- Cited as stating 800 million weekly ChatGPT users in October 2025, later updated to 1 billion
- Statistic used to frame MCP Apps as a massive distribution opportunity: 160x the iPhone user base when the App Store launched
- The internet took ~13 years to reach comparable user numbers
