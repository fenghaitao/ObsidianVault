## Definition

Alexander Imbiricos (Alex) is the product lead for Codex at OpenAI. He joined OpenAI about a year before this interview (circa 2025), previously ran his own startup for 5 years, and before that was a product manager at Dropbox.

## Key Information

- Product lead for Codex, OpenAI's coding agent that has seen 20x growth since GPT-5 launched
- Previously founded a screen-sharing pair programming startup that was acquired by / joined OpenAI
- Before that, was a product manager at Dropbox
- Described by Nick Turley (head of ChatGPT) as "one of my all-time favorite humans I've ever worked with"
- Described by Kevin Weil (OpenAI CPO) as "simply the best"
- His startup's number one value was "be kind and candid" — a philosophy similar to radical candor
- His phone turns black and white at 9 PM; uses social media only for work purposes
- Can be found on Twitter/X at @nbrico and monitors r/codex on Reddit
- Identifies more with the Greek poet Andreas Imbrios (his relative) than the shipping magnate George Imbrios
- His family originates from the Greek island of Andros
- His mother is Malaysian
- Originally came to America wanting to work on US aircraft, now works in software
- Enjoys combustion engines and cars, recently bought a Tesla
- Enjoys science fiction, particularly Ian Banks' "The Culture" series, and the anime Jujutsu Kaisen
- Part of the Codex team is growing and actively hiring engineers, PMs, and salespeople
