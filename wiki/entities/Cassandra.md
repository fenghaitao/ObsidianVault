## Definition

Cassandra is a distributed NoSQL database. Uber used it among its ~52 database technologies, and applying Odin to databases like Cassandra marked a major scope expansion for the platform.

## Key Information

- Cassandra was one of the online database technologies at Uber (alongside MySQL and Postgres)
- Odin's scope expansion to "standard MySQL and Cassandra" was the milestone that (with other work) took Joakim Recht to Principal Engineer
- Dedicated database-expertise teams (Cassandra, MySQL) still existed; Odin handled fleet management (kernel upgrades, decommissioning, etc.)
