## Definition

Dribbble is a design inspiration and community website where designers share their work. Lazar uses it to find mock designs and screenshots that guide AI tools toward better visual output.

## Key Information

- Used as a source of design references when prompting AI tools like Lovable
- Part of the parallel prototyping workflow: find a mock design to guide the AI's visual direction
- Helps vibe coders without design backgrounds reference quality design work
- One of the go-to sites for "exposure time" — deliberately exposing yourself to great design
