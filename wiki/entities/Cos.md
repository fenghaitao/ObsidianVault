## Definition

Cos was a startup spun out of Stanford University research, founded by professor Yuri Lusco and an industry veteran. Rahul Pandey joined as employee #2 right out of college. The company was acquired by Pinterest after approximately 8 months of operation.

## Key Information

- Spun out of academic research from Yuri Lusco's lab at Stanford
- Focused on recommendation systems and machine learning (called "big data" at the time)
- Had two founders: Yuri Lusco (professor) and an industry veteran with 20+ years of experience
- Rahul joined as employee #2, alongside one other new grad from the lab
- The company lasted only about 8 months before being acquired by Pinterest
- The acquisition was described as closer to a failure than a success — the company never had a chance to really fail or succeed
- Rahul had not vested any options because the company had only existed for 8 months
- The acquisition was an "opportunistic bet" on interesting IP and academic ideas, but there was no clear pathway to revenue or product
- Pinterest's pitch was pairing Cos's technology with Pinterest's existing product-market fit for a "1+1=3" outcome
- Rahul and others had to re-interview to get offers at Pinterest, which was a stressful 72-hour scramble
