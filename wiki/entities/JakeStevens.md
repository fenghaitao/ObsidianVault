## Definition

Jake Stevens is an RIT computer-vision graduate, founder of [[Luminal]], and co-creator of [[CustomUniverse]], a winning project at [[Anthropic]]'s [[ClaudeOpus4.8]] Build Day hackathon (June 2026) that turns phone photos of objects into 3D models for robotics training data generation.

## Key Information

- Co-created [[CustomUniverse]] with [[MauricioPereira]], a phone-photo-to-3D object pipeline for robotics labs needing [[SyntheticData|synthetic training data]].
- RIT computer-vision graduate; runs [[Luminal]], his startup.
- Brought the scene builder as a side project to the hackathon; met teammate [[MauricioPereira]] at the event.
- [[ClaudeOpus4.8]] built the project end-to-end and operated a remote [[NVIDIAH100]] throughout the hackathon.
- Advised using [[Claude]] to choose tools, not just to write code; the team used Claude for research on model selection and for integrating unfamiliar technologies like [[AppleRealityKit]].
- Custom Universe relies on open-source models and is free to use; users can run it on their own GPUs.
- Competed at the [[ClaudeOpus4.8]] Build Day hackathon on June 13, 2026, a 12-hour event with 310 builders.
