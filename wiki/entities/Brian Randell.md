## Definition

Brian Randell (transcribed "Brian Randelle") is a British computer scientist who, in a conference conversation with Dijkstra, helped prompt publication of the "go to" letter.

## Key Information

- Talked with Dijkstra at a conference in Tennessee (outside, in the "stuff at the bar" of modern conferences) about the problems with the go-to statement; Randell and others told Dijkstra he should publish it.
- Casey notes Randell doesn't get the same name recognition as Knuth but was a prolific author with many papers from that era and "not an obscure figure" to anyone reading computing history.
