## Definition

Company referenced in the episode as the originator (via Jeff Bezos) of the "Working Backwards" / press-release-first product development methodology, which Lenny compares to Tony Fadell's own advice.

## Key Information

- Associated with the "Working Backwards" methodology (writing the product's press release before building it), the subject of a well-known book on the approach.
- Jeff Bezos, Amazon's founder, is cited by Fadell as sharing his own belief in iterative failure as the path to product success.
- Per [[Benedict Evans]] (episode 07): used as his central e-commerce analogy for the [[Task Vs Job]] framework — Amazon is very good at getting you a known SKU (product you can already identify), but "knowing what SKU you want" (i.e., figuring out what to buy in the first place) is a separate, unautomated job; Evans extends this directly to AI ("the model can write you the code, but what code do you want?").
- Mentioned by Evans as one of the "big four" tech incumbents (with Google, Apple, Facebook) that is hard to see losing from the AI platform shift, though he notes mobile-era history shows some big incumbents can still be blindsided by a platform transition (e.g., Yahoo Mail failing to make the mobile jump).
- Per [[Aishwarya Naresh Reganti]] and [[Kiriti Badam]] (episode 33): cited as one of 50+ companies where the pair have led or supported AI product deployments, alongside [[Databricks]], [[OpenAI]], and [[Google]].
