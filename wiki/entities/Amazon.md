## Definition
Amazon is a FAANG technology company and the parent organization of AWS and Amazon Kiro. Al Harris is a Principal Engineer at Amazon working on the Kiro agentic IDE.

## Key Information
- Parent company of Kiro, an agentic IDE for spec-driven development
- Kiro was theoretically funded out of the org that supports Amazon Q Developer, but was purposefully built as a very different product suite
- Kiro team is small (3-4 people) despite being at Amazon scale
- Amazon's internal teams use spec reviews (via Kiro-generated specs) instead of traditional design doc reviews
- Kiro is described as "brought to you by AWS" but not deeply integrated with the rest of the AWS ecosystem
- Internal Amazonians gave feedback that they want to start specs from technical notes or refactoring ideas, not just requirements
