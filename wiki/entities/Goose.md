## Definition

Goose is Block's internal, open-source AI agent, overseen by CTO [[Dhanji Prasanna]].

## Key Information

- Cited by [[Lenny Rachitsky]]: an engineer at Block has Goose watch his screen and listen to meetings throughout the day, proactively shipping PRs, sending emails, and drafting Slack messages based on what it observes — used as a real-world precedent for [[Alexander Imbiricos]]'s hypothetical "[[Chatter Driven Development]]" idea.
