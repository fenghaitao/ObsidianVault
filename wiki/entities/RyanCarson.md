## Definition
Ryan Carson is the former founder of Treehouse (which taught over a million people to code) and now runs Amp Code's builder community, a group for people experimenting with coding agents.

## Key Information
- Former founder of Treehouse, an education startup that taught over a million people to code
- Runs Amp Code's community of builders — a group focused on experimenting with and pushing the frontier of coding agents
- Using Amp Code is not a requirement to join the community
- Community activities include regular interviews with people building interesting things with agents, and in-person events
