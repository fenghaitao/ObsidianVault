## Definition

Robotics company (portfolio/advisory company of The Build Collective) that builds a non-humanoid robotic platform to automate retail inventory counting.

## Key Information

- Not a humanoid robot; instead does automated inventory of retail stores using an AI-enabled robotic platform.
- Tony Fadell notes the company has been at it for roughly seven to eight years and is now "taking off," after solving a real pain point: retail workers hate manually counting inventory on every shelf.
- Cited by Fadell as an example of a deep-tech, atoms-plus-software company with durable, hard-to-replicate differentiation.
