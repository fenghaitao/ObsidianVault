## Definition

Flickr is a photo-sharing platform co-founded by Stewart Butterfield and Cal Henderson. It was famously a pivot from a video game project.

## Key Information

- Co-founded by Stewart Butterfield and Cal Henderson, who have now worked together for 23 years
- Was a pivot from a video game, making Butterfield known as the "king of pivots"
- One of the early Web 2.0 success stories
