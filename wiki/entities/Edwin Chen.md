## Definition

Edwin Chen is the founder and CEO of Surge AI, the leading AI data company that trains frontier AI models including ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. He has a background in math, computer science, and linguistics from MIT, and previously worked as a researcher at Google, Facebook, and Twitter.

## Key Information

- Founded Surge AI in 2020, one month after GPT-3 launched, after recognizing the need for high-quality data to train next-generation AI models
- Built Surge AI to over $1B in revenue in under 4 years with fewer than 100 people, completely bootstrapped with no VC funding
- Attended MIT, drawn by both its strength in math/CS and the presence of Noam Chomsky
- His childhood dream was to find an underlying theory connecting math, language, and computer science
- Had a fanciful dream of being the one to decipher alien communication if aliens ever visited Earth
- Previously worked at Google, Facebook, and Twitter as a researcher, where he kept encountering the same problem: it was impossible to get the data needed to train models
- Created the famous "soda vs. pop" map while at Twitter, showing regional language variations across the United States
- Views himself as a scientist at heart, not a traditional CEO; prefers hands-on data analysis to meetings and sales
- Believes companies are an embodiment of their CEO's values
- Recommends: "Story of Your Life" by Ted Chiang, "The Myth of Sisyphus" by Albert Camus, "Le Ton Beau de Marot" by Douglas Hofstadter
