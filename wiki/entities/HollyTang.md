## Definition

Holly Tang is a designer and co-creator of [[Tekton]], a winning project at [[Anthropic]]'s [[ClaudeOpus4.8]] Build Day hackathon (June 2026) that reconstructs historical buildings in 3D with evidence-chain verification. She prototyped the project's first single reconstruction on her own before teaming up with [[AustinBurgess]].

## Key Information

- Co-created [[Tekton]] with [[AustinBurgess]], a project that reconstructs historical buildings in 3D across 339 incremental construction states with every component traced to a documented source via [[EvidenceChain|evidence chains]].
- Served as the designer on the team; prototyped a single reconstruction independently.
- Met teammate [[AustinBurgess]] in line for coffee at a Code with Claude event roughly a month before the hackathon.
- Tekton was built entirely on [[ClaudeOpus4.8]] with independent verifier sub-agents in isolated context windows and self-correction loops until all 20 tests passed.
- The team plans to make Tekton open source for museums, historians, nonprofits, and governments.
- Competed at the [[ClaudeOpus4.8]] Build Day hackathon on June 13, 2026, a 12-hour event with 310 builders.
