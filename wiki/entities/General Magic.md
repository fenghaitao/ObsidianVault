## Definition

Legendary early-1990s company (Tony Fadell was part of the team) that attempted to build smartphone-like technology roughly 15 years before the iPhone; subject of the documentary *General Magic*.

## Key Information

- Fadell was part of the General Magic team; he developed virtual keyboard/handwriting software there in the 1990s, experience he later drew on for the iPhone keyboard debate.
- Described by Fadell as "we made the iPhone 15 years too early" — a classic case of building technically impressive things nobody yet needed, without the surrounding technology or customer context to make them useful.
- Subject of the documentary film *General Magic*, which Fadell recommends; he notes viewers may not recognize his much younger self in it.
- Referenced as a formative lesson in not overworking without the right market timing/context ("why now" alongside "what pain").
