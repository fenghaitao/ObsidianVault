## Definition

*Outlive*, by Peter Attia, is named by [[Tomer Cohen]] as one of his most-recommended books in the episode's lightning round.

## Key Information

- Introduces the idea of "Medicine 3.0" — personalized, preventive medicine — which Cohen believes will become far more powerful in the age of AI.
- Organizes advice around key life-longevity categories (fitness, diet, and other major health factors) to help readers optimize healthspan.
