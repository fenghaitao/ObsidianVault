## Definition
O'Reilly Media is a technology publisher that published the white paper "What We Learned from a Year of Building with LLMs" co-authored by six AI practitioners. The paper's overwhelmingly positive reception led to the keynote at the AI Engineer Summit.

## Key Information
- Published the white paper that formed the basis of the keynote
- The paper combined lessons across strategic, operational, and tactical levels of building LLM applications
- The paper received positive feedback from diverse audiences including Postgres contributors, venture capitalists, and tool builders
- The six co-authors collaborated to write what was initially planned as separate blog posts into a single 30-page O'Reilly publication
