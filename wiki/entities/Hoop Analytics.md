## Definition

Hoop Analytics is a product where you can upload a video of a kids' sports game and it will annotate the shots, shooting percentages, passing percentages, and assists. Claire Vo's 9-year-old takes that data into Claude to make Gamma presentations for his coach.

## Key Information

- Upload video of kids' sports game → get annotated stats
- Annotates: shots, shooting percentages, passing percentages, assists
- Part of Claire Vo's "full AI basketball stack" for her kids
- Her 9-year-old takes the data into Claude to make Gamma presentations for his coach on what to focus on in practice
- Used with a gimbal-style tripod to capture steady footage of games
