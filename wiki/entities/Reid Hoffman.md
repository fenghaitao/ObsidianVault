## Definition

Co-founder of [[LinkedIn]]. Credited by [[Mark Pincus]] as the source of the definition of "[[Metaverse]]" that Pincus uses: blurring the lines between the virtual and the real, rather than an escapist, separate immersive world.

## Key Information

- Referenced only in passing in episode 5 as the originator of this metaverse definition, which Pincus applies to his own long-running "Earth" project.
- Per [[Tomer Cohen]] (episode 41): gave a 2008 Stanford lecture on the power of professional online communities that first drew Cohen's attention to LinkedIn, several years before Cohen eventually joined the company as CPO.
