## Definition
Sesame AI is a voice AI company developing speech-to-speech voice models. Neil Zeghidour mentioned being a "huge fan" of their voice model, which competes in the advanced voice AI space alongside OpenAI's advanced voice mode and others.

## Key Information
- Voice AI company building speech-to-speech models
- Neil Zeghidour is a self-described "huge fan" of their voice model
- Competes with OpenAI advanced voice mode, ElevenLabs, and others
- Their model is still half duplex (as of May 2026) — cannot handle simultaneous speaking
- Mentioned in the context of the gap between current voice AI and true full-duplex conversation
