## Definition

Rome (addressed as "Rome" in the podcast outro; the episode intro renders the name as "Ryan") is an engineering executive whose career ran from machine-learning research to frontline manager at Facebook, director at Square, senior director of the camera team at Snapchat, and CTO of the AI video-generation company HeyGen.

## Key Information

- Earned a PhD (five years) in machine learning, then spent three years as an IBM Research scientist focused on computer vision and machine learning
- Chose CMU over offers from Princeton and Cornell on the strength of its computer science, against his father's suggestion to attend the better-known Princeton
- Left industrial research because the ~50/50 research/engineering model (IBM Research, Microsoft Research) felt unsustainable; chose software engineering over faculty or quant trading because engineering is a "first class citizen" that can influence more people
- Joined fast-growing Facebook around 2009 ("the early years") because it was engineering-first, fast-growing, and broadly influential
- Joined Square directly into a director role (a team of ~25, grown to 50+ within a year) leading machine learning and data-science infrastructure teams
- Joined Snapchat as Director of Camera (the first page of the app) over the more comfortable Director of Data, intentionally learning iOS and Android from scratch for about two months to inch toward his CTO north star
- Was roughly the 100th employee at Snapchat, which grew to ~3,000 people in two years
- His long-standing north star was to become a CTO of an AI company, which the host confirms he achieved
- Never proactively looked for new jobs — nearly all opportunities came inbound via personal connections or recruiter outreach, and he weights personal connection heavily so he can weather down-cycles with people he likes
- Now leads HeyGen, an all-in-one human-centric video-generation platform; his stated dream is to "make camera obsolete" and make visual storytelling accessible to everyone
