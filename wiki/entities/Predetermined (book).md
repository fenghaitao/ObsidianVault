## Definition

"Predetermined" is [[Jacob Warwick]]'s planned book about negotiation. The title is meant to include a question mark — "Predetermined?" — reflecting the core belief that no outcome is predetermined, no matter your circumstances.

## Key Information

- Author: Jacob Warwick
- Status: Planned / in progress (as of 2026)
- Title is "Predetermined" with a question mark — "Predetermined?"
- Core message: no outcome is predetermined; no matter your situation, it's not fixed
- Will include Jacob's personal story about his son's cystic fibrosis diagnosis, which was presented as "predetermined" by doctors but the family chose not to accept that framing
- Jacob: "None of these outcomes are predetermined. No matter what situation you are in your life, how you were raised, it could be more difficult for you, but it's not predetermined"
