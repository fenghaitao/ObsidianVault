## Definition
Amy Boyd leads the Foundry developer relations team at Microsoft, focused on helping developers build, evaluate, and monitor AI agents on the Microsoft Foundry platform.

## Key Information
- Leads Foundry developer relations at Microsoft
- Presented "Mind the Gap (In your Agent Observability)" at AI Engineer conference in London (2026-05-14)
- Co-presented with Nitya Narasimhan on the full agent observability lifecycle
- Demonstrated the Foundry portal quick-start: creating projects, deploying agents, enabling tracing with App Insights, selecting evaluation metrics in the playground
- Emphasized observability should be built in at a very early stage, not as an afterthought
- Advocated for starting in the portal for quick prototyping, then moving to SDK for programmatic control
