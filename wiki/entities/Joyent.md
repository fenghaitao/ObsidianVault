## Definition

Joyent is the cloud computing company where Bryan Cantrill worked after leaving Oracle, originally as VP of Engineering and later as CTO, competing head-on with AWS.

## Key Information

- Joyent had a public cloud and competed with Amazon/AWS, which is how Cantrill came to learn the real economics of the cloud business (great margins despite Amazon's relentless price cuts).
- Cantrill was brought in as VP of Engineering because Joyent already had a CTO; he later became CTO himself.
- He cared less about the title and more that the company did not hire the empty CTO role externally when its founding CTO left.
- At Joyent, Cantrill worked with Steve Tuck (who led go-to-market/sales), and together they later co-founded Oxide; Joyent was later acquired by Samsung ("after Samsung acquired Join[t]").
- Cantrill's "worst hire in human history" happened at Joyent — a hire under an assumed name who had just come off parole for violent felonies — a failure that drove him to rebuild Joyent's hiring process (a writing-intensive, intrinsic-motivation-focused process) that later became Oxide's.
