## Definition
Eric Schmidt is a philanthropist and former Google CEO who was one of the funders of Kyutai, the non-profit AI research lab that created Moshi and from which Gradium AI was spun off.

## Key Information
- Philanthropist who funded Kyutai, a non-profit AI research lab
- Former Google CEO
- Alongside Xavier Niel and others, provided funding for open speech AI research
- His funding enabled the development of Moshi (first full-duplex speech-to-speech model) and other open speech models
