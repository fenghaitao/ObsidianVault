## Definition
Dr. Harry Coppock was one of the early fellows placed by Number 10 Data Science into the AI Safety Institute (AISI) from day one. He led on the Inspect tool, a CFI isolated environment for testing what AI agents do when given autonomy and tools.

## Key Information
- Early fellow of the [[Number10DataScience]] fellowship program
- Placed into the [[AISafetyInstitute]] from day one
- Led development of the Inspect tool for testing AI agent behavior in isolated environments
- Worked on AISI's cybersecurity work stream
