## Definition

Venture capital firm (slow.co) where [[Sam Lesson]] is a partner alongside [[Kevin Colleran]]. Central to this episode as the professional context from which Lesson's etiquette teaching emerged (VC meetings, LP relationships, founder pitches).

## Key Information

- Kevin Colleran is described as "maniacal" about etiquette gestures when meeting with limited partners (LPs) — e.g., always offering to clear coffee cups or diet cokes and acknowledging any mess made, even when someone else will clean it up.
- Lesson notes he is frequently late to meetings because VC schedules are packed into "30-minute chunks back-to-back... especially on Zoom," which he cites as a real-world case for the episode's etiquette advice on graciously handling lateness (both giving and receiving it).
- Ran an in-person etiquette class for [[Y Combinator]] founders at the Four Seasons, including wardrobe consultants and caviar/wine tasting alongside core etiquette material.
- Sold a prior startup (Lesson's own, pre-Slow Ventures) to [[Airbnb]]; Colleran is credited with cultivating relationships with Airbnb's EA/office manager during that sale process to smooth the deal.
