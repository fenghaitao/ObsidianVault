## Definition

Slow Ventures is a venture capital firm where Sam Lessin is a partner. Their website is slow.co.

## Key Information

- Sam Lessin is a partner at the firm
- Kevin Collard is also a partner
- Focus includes investments in the cultural implications of AI and businesses that need to exist because AI is a force in the world (e.g., Sublime Security)
- Sam's investment thesis: avoid "AI companies" that are too capital-intensive for seed returns; invest in companies that use AI as a tool rather than as their identity
