## Definition

Jane Street is a quantitative trading firm and one of the most prestigious employers for software engineers, particularly known for its use of OCaml and high cash compensation.

## Key Information

- Jane Street was mentioned as one of the "quant firms" that were "really really big" at both Cornell and UCLA for recruiting
- Cornell had an OCaml class, which was relevant for Jane Street interviews ("OCaml was like the thing for Jane Street")
- Jane Street has always been known to "pay handsomely in cash"
- Along with other quant firms, Jane Street was consistently high on tier lists of desirable employers, and this has remained true into recent years
