## Definition

Robert Moses was a controversial urban planner who shaped much of New York City. He is the subject of The Power Broker, Robert Caro's 1,100-page biography, which Jenny Wen recommends as a nuanced study of a controversial figure.

## Key Information

- Controversial urban planner who shaped New York
- Subject of The Power Broker by Robert Caro
- Jenny describes him as "really controversial" and appreciates the nuanced view Caro provides
