## Definition

Fable is an [[Anthropic]] AI model referenced by [[Adam Mosseri]] as a smaller/cheaper model relative to frontier options like [[Mythos]].

## Key Information

- Mosseri frames Fable as likely sufficient (and likely to become cheaper still) for smaller side projects and small-scale programming work, contrasted with paying for "frontier tokens" on a top-tier frontier model.
- Referenced alongside the broader point that model intelligence-per-dollar keeps improving: what a given price point buys today is far more capable than what it bought a year prior, and that trend is expected to continue.
