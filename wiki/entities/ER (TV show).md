## Definition

ER is a medical drama TV show that originally aired from 1994, running for 15 seasons. Jason Cohen recommends it as a show that holds up well after 25+ years, noting that his 16-year-old Gen Alpha daughter also enjoys it.

## Key Information

- Medical drama, originally aired starting in 1994
- 15 seasons
- Jason Cohen is watching it with his 16-year-old daughter (as of recording, around season 13)
- His daughter says it holds up after 25+ years
- Jason: "If this is an era when shows were an hour long and seasons were forever, and she says it's great TV, it must be great TV"
- Lenny recommends "The Pit" (on Netflix) as a similar show for ER fans
- Lenny's cousin was a young girl patient on ER; it was the start of her acting career
