## Definition

Dev Agents is the AI startup David Singleton co-founded after leaving Stripe. It is building a "next-generation operating system" platform so AI agents and agentic apps can work for consumers at scale.

## Key Information

- Dev Agents is the company name, not the product name; the platform will be how people find and use agents and agentic apps built by developers and creators
- Singleton frames it as the same moment as early mobile: agents need a place to exist with user context, collaboration, privacy/security, and a two-sided marketplace of developers and people
- Positioned against enterprise agent-infrastructure players by focusing on "how can we apply this technology for everyone"
- The co-founders all came from the early mobile/GM wave: Singleton (Android), Hugo (early Android product team), and Nicholas (mobile UI + Chrome)
- Singleton ran weekly user-facing friction logs at Dev Agents as part of product development
- Early-access signup for creators/developers: sdsa.ai/build
