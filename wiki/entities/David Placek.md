## Definition

Professional product-naming expert, cited by [[Lenny Rachitsky]] for a technique of running three separate brainstorming teams against different metaphors for the same product (e.g., for an AI IDE product: name it as if it were literally what it is, then as if it were a boat, then as if it were a spaceship) — finding the best names often come from the non-obvious metaphors.
