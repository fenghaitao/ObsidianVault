## Definition

Visual Studio is the Microsoft IDE that Fiona Fung used to build Visual Studio itself in her first job out of college.

## Key Information

- Fiona's first job out of college was "using Visual Studio to build Visual Studio," which is where she learned the practice of dogfooding
- She later worked on the Visual Studio editor and debugger at Microsoft
- Visual Studio teams ran sprints that seemed short to her at ~4 weeks — until she joined Facebook and saw weekly sprints
- A former Visual Studio coworker later recruited her to Facebook, becoming her first Facebook manager
