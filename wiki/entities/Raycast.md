## Definition

Productivity/launcher app named by [[Kiriti Badam]] as one of his favorite recently discovered products, and also offered as part of [[Lenny's Podcast]]'s sponsorship "product pass."

## Key Information

- Badam, who describes himself as "a stickler for productivity," praises Raycast for the new shortcuts it exposes for opening different things and typing shortcut commands quickly.
- Also mentioned as included (one year free) in Lenny's newsletter "product pass" for annual subscribers.
- Badam separately names "caffeinate" (a tool that prevents a Mac from sleeping, letting him run long unattended [[Codex]] tasks for hours) as another recent productivity discovery.
