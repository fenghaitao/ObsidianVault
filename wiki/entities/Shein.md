## Definition
Shein is a Chinese fast-fashion e-commerce company cited as a disruptor that moves at machine speed, forcing traditional retailers like Walmart to adopt AI capabilities to compete.

## Key Information
- Referenced alongside Octopus and Klarna as companies that find traditional enterprise decision speed "insane"
- Represents companies that study industries, craft playbooks, and redefine the games themselves
- Walmart built a social media trend scanner and generative designer specifically to compete with Shein's speed-to-market
- Operates at machine speed in contrast to traditional enterprises that take 6+ months for equivalent decisions
- Example of how AI-native or AI-accelerated companies force traditional enterprises to transform
