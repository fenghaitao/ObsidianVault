## Definition

OpenAI is an AI research and deployment company. Ryan mentioned it as an example of a startup where early employees could achieve significant wealth through equity.

## Key Information

- Ryan cited OpenAI as a hypothetical example of how to achieve extreme wealth: "join OpenAI as employee number 50 and then you can probably make it" to being "50 mil rich"
- The context was explaining the high-variance nature of startup equity compared to the stable high expected value of big tech

- Nimit Sohoni cited OpenAI (together with DeepMind) as an example of a big AI lab with enormous compute and resources, but one that can be more risk-averse and susceptible to groupthink than a nimble startup
