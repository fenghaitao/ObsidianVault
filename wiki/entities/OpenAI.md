## Definition

OpenAI is the AI research organization behind ChatGPT and GPT models. It is one of the frontier AI labs that Surge AI provides training data for.

## Key Information

- GPT-3 was released in 2020, which directly inspired Edwin Chen to found Surge AI one month later
- OpenAI released Sora, a video generation model, which Edwin cites as an example of a product choice that reveals something about what kind of AI future a company wants to build
- ChatGPT is noted for its sycophantic behavior — constantly telling users they're right and amazing — which Edwin sees as an example of optimizing for engagement rather than truth
- OpenAI is one of Surge AI's customers for training data
- Had a "code red" moment when Gemini 3 launched, reportedly losing ~6% market share in a week, showing even the market leader is vulnerable
- ChatGPT has almost a billion monthly active users and is synonymous with AI globally
- OpenAI's culture is described as "truly, truly bottoms-up" with a fuzzy long-term aim and empirical short-term execution — the talent caliber and individual drive are dramatically higher than at typical startups
- The company operates with a "ready-fire-aim" approach: get products out quickly, see how users behave, then double down on what works
- Codex is now used by all technical staff at OpenAI, with designers vibe coding prototypes and landing PRs, product marketers making string changes from Slack, and research teams using it for training infrastructure
- Codex is now being used for its own training runs — babysitting training graphs, catching configuration mistakes, and being on call for training issues
- The Sora Android app was built in 18 days (to employees) and 28 days total (public launch) by 2-3 engineers using Codex, becoming the #1 app in the App Store
- Atlas (OpenAI's browser) was built as a first-party way to provide contextual AI assistance without relying on accessibility APIs or screenshots
- OpenAI was a sponsor of the first HackAPrompt generative AI red teaming competition, which produced the first open-source dataset of prompt injections
- OpenAI co-authored a major research paper with HackAPrompt, Anthropic, and Google DeepMind on adaptive attacks against state-of-the-art defenses
- GPT-5 is described as "decently well defended" but human attackers can still jailbreak it in 10-30 attempts
- Denise (formerly at Slack/Salesforce for 14 years) left to become CRO of OpenAI — Maggie in OpenAI leadership says they "just can't hire enough enterprise reps"
- OpenAI is a top-25 traffic site using Vercel
- ChatGPT is described as "our best therapist on planet Earth" — an example of AI being a "people person"

- Molly Graham's partner Sarah Caldwell works at OpenAI; Graham gave her advice for navigating the company's extreme pace of change: "Get to know your customer. They have the answer. Be patient because everything is going to change. Just keep trying."
- Graham describes OpenAI as "extra special" in terms of instability and change, telling people there to expect a different job and different boss every six months
- Graham advises OpenAI employees to "see the instability as stability" because it's the only thing that is definitely going to be true

- 95% of OpenAI engineers use Codex daily; 100% of PRs are reviewed by Codex (Sherwin Wu, 2026)
- Engineers who use Codex more open 70% more PRs, and the gap is widening
- A team within OpenAI is maintaining a 100% Codex-written codebase as an experiment, with no escape hatch to manually write code
- Codex reviews 100% of all PRs internally, reducing code review from 10-15 minutes to 2-3 minutes
- ChatGPT has 800 million weekly active users (~10% of the world and growing)
- OpenAI's mission: build AGI and spread its benefits to all of humanity — the API platform is a key expression of this mission
- The API was OpenAI's first product (launched ~2020), reflecting the platform-first philosophy
- OpenAI views itself fundamentally as an ecosystem platform company: every model released in a product gets released in the API, competitors are not blocked, and the platform remains neutral
- Sam Altman and Greg Brockman reinforce the ecosystem platform philosophy from the top
- The free tier of ChatGPT has gotten dramatically smarter over time (GPT-5 today vs GPT-3.5 in 2022), raising the floor globally
- For $20/month, users get essentially the same AI that billionaires use; for $200/month, the Pro model
- The API offers: responses API (low-level), agents SDK (mid-level agent orchestration), agent kit/widgets (UI components), and eval API (testing)
- Sherwin Wu is head of engineering for the API and developer platform
- Kevin Weil is VP of Science, coined "this is the worst the models will ever be"
- The Codex team is seeing tangible internal energy around how far the tools have gotten
