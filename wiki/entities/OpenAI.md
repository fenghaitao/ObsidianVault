## Definition

OpenAI is an AI research and deployment company. Ryan mentioned it as an example of a startup where early employees could achieve significant wealth through equity.

## Key Information

- Ryan cited OpenAI as a hypothetical example of how to achieve extreme wealth: "join OpenAI as employee number 50 and then you can probably make it" to being "50 mil rich"
- The context was explaining the high-variance nature of startup equity compared to the stable high expected value of big tech

- Nimit Sohoni cited OpenAI (together with DeepMind) as an example of a big AI lab with enormous compute and resources, but one that can be more risk-averse and susceptible to groupthink than a nimble startup

### Michael Bolin on OpenAI
- Joined at the end of 2023 to build with "the best model" and to work alongside the senior people he respected (Meta E8/E9-level talent); he likened it to starting at Google around 2000.
- Describes the research-led culture as "certainly an adjustment" — the model being good matters more than what product engineers build on top of it.
- Leads the open-source Codex repository: Codex CLI launched April 2025, Codex web/cloud followed, and growth inflected in August 2025 with GPT-5, a refreshed terminal UI, an open-weights model, and the VS Code extension.
- Estimates 80–90% of his own code is now model-generated, while he hand-writes the safety-critical Rust sandboxing in Codex's harness ([[summary-20260309 - OpenAI Codex Tech Lead： How His Career Grew And How He Uses Codex ｜ Michael Bolin]]).

### Austin McDonald on OpenAI's Hiring Signal
- OpenAI's behavioral signal includes "act with humility" (growth — do you respond well to feedback and improve yourself and others) and "feel the AGI" (optimistic and positive about what AGI/AI can do for the world).
- Candidates who are not AI-forward and excited about new AI domains read as a poor culture fit to OpenAI interviewers ([[summary-20260316 - Meta Hiring Lead： Avoid Downleveling, Liars, Passing OpenAI And Anthropic Interviews]]).

### John Myles White on OpenAI
- Cited OpenAI (with Anthropic) as a smaller frontier-lab startup that has had more layoff rounds than MSL.
- Notes Statsig (which he says is essentially Meta's Deltoid) was bought by OpenAI; he does not know its current state after the acquisition.

### David J. Malan on OpenAI
- CS50's virtual "rubber duck" tutor (cs50.ai) is built on top of OpenAI's APIs (and Microsoft Azure's web API service) and is deliberately less helpful than ChatGPT.

### Charlie Marsh on OpenAI
- Charlie's company Astral (Python dev tools: Ruff, uv, Red-knot) was acquired by OpenAI.
- He studied early OpenAI blog posts (e.g., DALL·E, transcript "Dolly") as a model for communicating ideas through images alone.
- OpenAI makes heavy use of Rust, reinforcing his bet on the language.

### Vlad Feinberg on OpenAI
- Vlad cites OpenAI (with Anthropic) as a peer frontier lab; people from both agreed with the advice in his "how to get a job at a frontier lab" post, indicating skill demands overlap across labs.

### Leonardo de Moura on OpenAI
- OpenAI proved the unit-distance conjecture (informally); an OpenAI contributor (transcribed "Boris Alexi") later produced the ~1-million-line formal Lean proof showing it was false.
