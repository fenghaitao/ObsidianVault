## Definition

Spotify is a global audio streaming platform and an [[Anthropic]] customer that uses [[ClaudeCode]] to scale developer experience (DevEx) across teams and agents. Spotify presented at Code w/ Claude London 2026.

## Key Information

- Presented a session at [[CodeWithClaude|Code w/ Claude London 2026]] titled "Coding is No Longer the Constraint: Scaling DevEx to Teams and Agents at Spotify."
- The session covered how Spotify scales developer experience and agent adoption across its engineering organization using [[ClaudeCode]].
- Spotify is also listed as a partner of [[Anthropic]] on the broader Claude platform.
