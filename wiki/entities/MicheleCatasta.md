## Definition
Michele Catasta is a leader at Replit working on autonomous coding agents, specifically the Replit Agent product line. He presented at the aiDotEngineer conference on the three pillars of autonomy for building coding agents for non-technical users.

## Key Information
- Works at Replit on the Replit Agent, a coding agent designed for non-technical users.
- Presented "The 3 Pillars of Autonomy" at aiDotEngineer, outlining frontier model capabilities, verification, and context management as the key pillars.
- Argues for a "Waymo experience" of autonomy where users don't need technical expertise, as opposed to the "Tesla FSD" model of supervised autonomy.
- Advocates for decoupling autonomy from long runtimes and for the core loop as orchestrator for parallel agents.
- Mentioned that Replit is always hiring.
