## Definition

Chris Voss is a former FBI hostage negotiator and author of "Never Split the Difference," a book that popularized negotiation tactics drawn from high-stakes law enforcement scenarios. His concepts are referenced extensively by Jacob Warwick throughout the podcast.

## Key Information

- Former FBI hostage negotiator
- Author of "Never Split the Difference"
- Key concepts referenced by Jacob Warwick:
  - "Never be so sure of your worth that you wouldn't accept more" — don't anchor yourself to a ceiling
  - "Never split the difference" — avoid lazy compromise; the middle ground is often not the optimal outcome
  - Tactical empathy — understanding the other party's motivations and emotions to create leverage
- Jacob Warwick cites Chris Voss's work as foundational but notes that framing negotiation through FBI hostage scenarios can feel disconnected from everyday workplace situations
