## Definition
Kent Beck is a software engineer and the creator of Extreme Programming and Test-Driven Development (TDD). He is known for his contributions to software design methodology and agile practices.

## Key Information
- Creator of Test-Driven Development (TDD): write a test first, make it pass, then refactor
- Author of the principle: "Invest in the design of the system every day"
- TDD is advocated by Matt Pocock as essential for AI-assisted development because it forces the AI to take small deliberate steps
- TDD addresses the AI's tendency to "outrun its headlights" — producing too much code at once without feedback
- His principle of daily design investment contrasts with the specs-to-code approach, which divests from system design
