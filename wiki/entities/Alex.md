## Definition

Alex is Rahul Pandey's co-founder at Taro. They met while working together on the Portal team at Meta in 2017.

## Key Information

- Worked with Rahul on the Portal team at Meta in 2017
- Was on the same exact team as Rahul when Rahul first joined Meta
- Switched teams relatively quickly, moving to Instagram
- Stayed in touch with Rahul — they met every week in person at Facebook pre-COVID
- Once COVID hit, they started talking almost every other day about different ideas and things to try together
- Before quitting Meta, they committed to working together for at least 6 months to a year
- They ran free webinars during COVID about career growth, which drew up to 800 attendees
- They went through Y Combinator together to fund Taro
- Rahul described finding a co-founder as "a huge unlock" — he finally found someone he trusted and enjoyed working with who had similar timelines
- The co-founder relationship is "like a marriage" — timing alignment is critical and difficult to find
