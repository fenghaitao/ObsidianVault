## Definition

Sam Blonde is a sales leader who was on Jason Lemkin's team at his previous startup (acquired by Adobe), later became CRO of Brex, and now sits on the board of Rippling. He is known for building cold calling capabilities and honest, learner-mindset GTM leadership.

## Key Information

- Was a sales leader on Jason Lemkin's team at the startup acquired by Adobe
- When the company was acquired, he inherited Adobe reps and was shocked by how bad their emails were
- Became CRO of Brex, where he built a strong cold calling culture
- Now on the board of Rippling, where he helped bring cold calling expertise
- Gave a SaaStr talk with Matt Plank called "Rippling Secrets to Hypergrowth" — praised for the fluency of two honest CROs with a learner's mindset
- Represents the "best of the best" in sales leadership who will thrive with AI augmentation
