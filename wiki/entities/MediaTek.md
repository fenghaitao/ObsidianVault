## Definition
MediaTek is a semiconductor company and hardware partner for Google AI Edge. Google has previously published models that work on MediaTek silicon, with broader NPU support expected as more smaller models become available.

## Key Information
- Semiconductor company and Google AI Edge hardware partner
- Google has previously published models optimized for MediaTek silicon
- Broader NPU support expected over the course of 2026 as more smaller models become available
- Part of Google AI Edge's multi-vendor hardware acceleration strategy
