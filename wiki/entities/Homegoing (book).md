## Definition

Homegoing is a multigenerational historical fiction novel about the west coast of Africa and the impact of the slave trade, following two split parts of a family. Jessica Fain recommends it as a "window" into an unfamiliar world.

## Key Information

- Multigenerational historical fiction set on the west coast of Africa
- Explores the impact of the slave trade and the ongoing life in Africa from two split parts of a family
- Recommended by Jessica Fain in the lightning round as an example of her favorite genre: multigenerational historical fiction
