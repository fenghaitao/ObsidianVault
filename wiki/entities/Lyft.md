## Definition

Lyft is the ride-share company whose self-driving (Level 5) division Carey Nachenberg joined after Google X, where he worked on the autonomous vehicle architecture.

## Key Information

- The self-driving stack used classic hand-coded robotics heuristics and hand-parameterized decisions, a style inherited partly from academia (Carnegie Mellon lineage)
- Carey helped design an end-to-end neural-network driving architecture wrapped in a safety "guard rails" layer that could override unsafe decisions (e.g., running a red light)
- He argued the hand-coded, multi-parameter approach was a "dead end," as Tesla and others later acknowledged by shifting to end-to-end networks
- The autonomous division was eventually sold to a Toyota subsidiary, which acquired the IP
