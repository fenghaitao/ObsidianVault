## Definition
Lyft is a ride-sharing company and Uber's primary competitor in the US market. In this talk, Lyft is used as an example of how quality becomes a competitive differentiator when two products reach feature and price parity.

## Key Information
- Uber's primary competitor in the US ride-sharing market
- Launched Lyft Pool as a competing product to Uber Pool at the same price point
- Tuomas Artman's theory: when two products have the same features and price, users gradually migrate to the higher-quality one
- This quality-driven migration happens slowly over time — no A/B test can measure it
- Example of how building a low-quality product opens a company to being overtaken by competition
