## Definition

John Lowry is the author of "Negotiation Made Simple," which Jacob Warwick describes as a potential "bible" for negotiation. Jacob was initially frustrated reading it because it was the book he wanted to write himself.

## Key Information

- Author of "Negotiation Made Simple"
- Jacob Warwick: "I was pissed off because I'm like I wanted to write a book a couple years ago and then I read his and said this is what I wanted to write"
- Jacob describes it as a book that "really breaks it down in an easy way"
- Jacob appeared on Lowry's podcast as a guest
