## Definition

Game studio (later acquired by [[Zynga]]) that, after an earlier fully-innovative game failed completely, pivoted to a strict [[Proven Better New]] approach and created the hit [[Draw Something]].

## Key Information

- Was "on their last dollar" after a totally innovative, never-before-tried game failed completely.
- Rebuilt around Proven Better New: took a proven, viral mobile pattern and "perfectly copied" the turn-based system from [[Words With Friends]], resulting in Draw Something becoming the #1 game and app in the App Store for 60 days in 2012.
- Zynga subsequently acquired OMG Pop.
