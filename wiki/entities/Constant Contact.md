## Definition

Email marketing company for small businesses, cited by [[Jason Cohen]] as an example of restarting stalled growth via a genuinely new (not incremental) channel: physically running in-person workshops in many cities teaching small-business owners (restaurants, dentists) how to do email marketing, using Constant Contact.

## Key Information

- Cohen notes this seemed obviously cost-ineffective for a ~$20/month product but worked well, partly because they recruited power users who were also marketing agencies to help run and scale the workshops.
