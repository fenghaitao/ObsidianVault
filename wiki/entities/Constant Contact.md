## Definition

Constant Contact is an email marketing platform for small businesses, cited by Jason Cohen as an example of creative channel expansion when traditional marketing channels are saturated.

## Key Information

- When growth stalled, they restarted it by physically going to cities and holding workshops teaching small businesses how to do email marketing
- Target audience included restaurants, dentists, and other small business owners
- Despite being a ~$20/month product, the in-person workshop model was cost-effective and solved their growth problem
- They were clever about execution: used power users who were also agencies to help scale the workshops
- Example of thinking beyond traditional marketing channels when channels are saturated
