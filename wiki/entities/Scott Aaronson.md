## Definition

Scott Aaronson is a theoretical computer scientist who started the Complexity Zoo, a website cataloging complexity classes.

## Key Information

- Wigderson references "a website called the complexity zoo... started by Scott Aaronson," once it became clear there was "a whole zoo" of complexity classes.
- The Zoo has "hundreds and hundreds of complexity classes of all types," reflecting how theorists classify problems by many different resources.
