## Definition

Parker Conrad is the founder and CEO of [[Rippling]].

## Key Information

- Per [[Matt MacInnis]], personally serves as Rippling's sole payroll administrator for all 5,200+ employees, with no exceptions, and personally installs and dogfoods every new internal application before wider rollout.
- His Slack avatar is a generic yellow egg icon, which became an internal shorthand/meme at Rippling ("Parker Conrad is modeling personal intensity") for the executive practice of injecting unfiltered intensity directly into product teams rather than letting it be buffered away by management layers.
- Told MacInnis "you don't really learn from your mistakes, you learn from your successes" — a line MacInnis cites as foundational to his own view that career growth compounds faster by joining winning teams than by learning from failure.
- Approved MacInnis's transition from COO to CPO in December of the prior year after two years of disarray in engineering/product leadership hiring; MacInnis describes Conrad "slumping down in his seat" and saying he'd have to run another executive search before MacInnis volunteered to take the role himself.
