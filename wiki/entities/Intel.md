## Definition
Intel is a semiconductor company and hardware partner for Google AI Edge. Cormac Brick previously worked at Intel where he led the architecture for the NPU (Neural Processing Unit) used in modern Intel laptops.

## Key Information
- Semiconductor company and Google AI Edge hardware partner
- Cormac Brick led NPU architecture at Intel before joining Google
- NPU goes into all modern Intel laptops
- Google AI Edge continues to optimize LiteRT-LM performance with Intel as a partner
- Optimization work ongoing with Intel for edge AI deployment
