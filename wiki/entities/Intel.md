## Definition

Intel is a semiconductor company where Igor worked briefly before joining Google.

## Key Information

- Igor worked at Intel before Google.
- He recalls that switching projects at Intel was "almost impossible" — "much easier to just quit and then reapply" — in contrast with Google's fluid internal project mobility.

- Bjarne Stroustrup: the Intel representative ("wasn't actually an Intel guy") was in the x86 corner of a deadlock with IBM (PowerPC) over low-level hardware/cache-coordination models during C++ standardization; Bjarne spent "a couple of hours literally doing shuttle diplomacy" between the two corners, and they reached an agreement that landed in C++ 11.
