## Definition

Brendan Burns is a software engineer and co-creator of Kubernetes, with a PhD in robotics. At the time of this conversation he worked at Microsoft (Azure) after a career at Google, and recounts how Kubernetes was built and sold to leadership.

## Key Information

- Co-created Kubernetes with [[Joe Beda]] and [[Craig McLuckie]] (referred to in the transcript as "Joe" and "Craig") at Google.
- Wrote a high-80s-percent share of the original Kubernetes code; built the initial MVP in under a week (he estimates four to five days).
- Says his career "absolutely benefited" from Kubernetes' success, including a promotion while at Google.
- Holds a PhD in robotics; credits the PhD and a stint teaching CS 101 with his persuasive writing/presentation skills and his ability to explain Kubernetes to newcomers.
- Core advice: "hide ~10% of your effort from management" for self-directed side projects, and "keep better notes."
- Famous line: "The inevitable trajectory of software is death."
