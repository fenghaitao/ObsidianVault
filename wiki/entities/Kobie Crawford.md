## Definition
Kobie Crawford is a Developer Advocate at Snorkel, the frontier AI data lab. He presented Snorkel's research on task fidelity scaling laws, demonstrating empirically that higher-quality agentic benchmark tasks produce dramatically better RL training outcomes.

## Key Information
- Role: Developer Advocate at Snorkel
- Presented at aiDotEngineer's Channel Only series on June 2, 2026
- Presentation focused on Snorkel's empirical research validating that data/task quality directly impacts model training outcomes
- Covered Snorkel's task quality framework (four criteria), failure mode analysis, and the 5x training uplift from high-quality tasks
- Discussed Snorkel's use of human experts, LLM judges, rubric-based evaluation, and inter-annotator agreement for quality assurance
- Also addressed benchmark noise from low-quality tasks and Snorkel's open benchmark grants program
