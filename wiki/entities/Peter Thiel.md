## Definition

Peter Thiel is a co-founder of PayPal, Palantir, and Founders Fund. He was a key figure in the PayPal Mafia and is known for marshaling incredible talent density. He taught Keith Rabois the philosophy of hiring undiscovered talent.

## Key Information

- Co-founder of [[PayPal]] and [[Palantir]]
- Along with [[Max Levchin]], marshaled an incredible density of talent at PayPal, which allowed PayPal to succeed and spawned generations of successful companies
- Hired "pretty much everybody else" (non-engineering) at PayPal, primarily through his network (Stanford connections)
- Taught [[Keith Rabois]] on the first day at PayPal: "You've got to find undiscovered talent" — the only way to scale against large incumbents with infinite money
- Was also a lawyer — smart enough to quit after 5 months and 4 days
- [[David Sacks]] was also a lawyer but never practiced
