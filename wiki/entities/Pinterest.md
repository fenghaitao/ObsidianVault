## Definition

Pinterest is a social media and image sharing platform. Rahul worked there for two and a half years prior to Meta, where he was rejected for promotion twice.

## Key Information

- Rahul worked at Pinterest for two and a half years before joining Meta
- He was rejected for promotion twice at Pinterest
- Prior to Pinterest, Rahul was at a startup that got acquired by Pinterest
- Rahul's experience at Pinterest gave him perspective on both sides of the promotion process — getting promoted and getting rejected
- Pinterest had just developed an engineering leveling system when Rahul joined, modeled after Google and Facebook: L3 (entry), L4 (mid-level), L5 (senior)
- Rahul came in at L3 and pivoted from recommendation systems to Android mobile development
- Pinterest had a "Base Camp" onboarding program (modeled after Facebook's Boot Camp) — a 2-3 week program where new hires learned about different teams and chose where to work
- The culture was influenced by ex-Facebook and ex-Google people, but was criticized for being "too nice" — people weren't direct enough about feedback and prioritization
- Pinterest had a code review culture requiring two reviewers and "herald rules" that automatically added blocking reviewers for specific parts of the codebase
- The Android app at Pinterest already had hundreds of thousands of lines of code when Rahul joined
- International growth was a major initiative for Pinterest in 2015, which made Android strategically important (Android is dominant outside the US)
- Rahul was rejected for L3→L4 promotion twice; the second time he won on appeal after clarifying that a 16-month project duration was misinterpreted as lacking urgency
- Pinterest had an appeal process for promotion decisions
- Pinterest had around 400 people when Rahul was there — bigger than a startup but smaller than big tech
- One of the reasons Rahul left for Meta was that he didn't see a clear pathway to career rocket ship growth at Pinterest
