# Ricoh USA

Ricoh USA offers workplace solutions and digital transformation services designed to manage and optimize the flow of information across businesses. It adopted generative AI to enhance its operations, particularly for training data generation.

## Key Information

- Type: Workplace solutions and digital transformation services
- Focus: Information flow management across businesses
- VP of Portfolio Development: Ashok Shenoy

## AI Adoption

In 2023, Ricoh USA selected [[Claude2]] on [[AmazonBedrock]] to infuse generative AI into its operations. Key use case:
- **Training data generation**: Producing high-quality training datasets for internal AI/ML workflows
- **Data integrity**: Preserving data integrity while generating synthetic or structured data
- **Compliance**: HIPAA and SOC II adherence were critical selection criteria

*"We wanted to integrate a large language model that could produce high-quality training datasets while preserving data integrity and customer security. We selected Claude 2 on Amazon Bedrock for its proficiency in generating this data, as well as its adherence to stringent standards including HIPAA and SOC II."* — Ashok Shenoy, VP of Portfolio Development
