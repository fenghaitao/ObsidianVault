## Definition
AI Core is Android's system-level GenAI service that provides a central foundation model (2-5B parameters) pre-loaded on premium Android devices, with APIs for summarization, prompting, and other AI capabilities available to developers.

## Key Information
- Android system service for system-level GenAI
- Provides a central foundation model pre-loaded on premium Android devices
- Model size typically in the 2-5 billion parameter range
- Available APIs include summarization, prompt API, and an expanding set of capabilities
- Customization primarily via prompting or skills
- Gemma 4 E2B and E4B models are on the AI Core roadmap for future integration
- Analogous to Apple Intelligence on the Apple side
- Part of the system-level GenAI trend: large models built into the OS rather than per-app
