## Definition

Dan Wang is an author who wrote "Breakneck," a book about US-China relations. Sherwin Wu recommends the book for its analogy of the US as a "lawyerly society" and China as an "engineering society."

## Key Information

- Author of "Breakneck" (published in the last year)
- The book describes the US as a "lawyerly society" and China as an "engineering society," with pros and cons to each
- Sherwin Wu: "I read it and I was like, hmm, yeah, does seem like we're run by lawyers in the US"
