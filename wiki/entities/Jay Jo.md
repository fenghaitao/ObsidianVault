## Definition

Jay Jo is a software engineer turned founder. He worked at Robinhood before co-founding Sei Network, a crypto project whose token reached a market cap over $10 billion.

## Key Information

- Secured a Facebook internship via a "LeetCode and Chill" session whose "number of islands" problem appeared verbatim in his onsite
- Interned at Pinterest the next summer as a Kleiner Perkins fellow, preferring its smaller San Francisco culture
- Joined Robinhood as a new grad (signed at Series C ~100 people, joined at Series D); about a third of his new-grad class later raised venture money
- Became jaded after a 5/5 performance rating at the 9-month mark came with no promotion or compensation change; started "playing the game"
- Stayed 3.5 years, constrained by a family death, a $400,000 option exercise cost, and waiting for the July 2021 IPO to remove his handcuffs
- Experienced the GameStop saga from inside Robinhood on Jan 28, 2021, which inspired him to build better financial rails
- Before Sei, tried an AWS cost-management startup in 2019; pivoted into crypto in 2021, then from an on-chain order-book exchange to Layer-1 infrastructure
- Raised $35M ($5M seed + $30M strategic), starting 3 weeks after Terra collapsed, with Multicoin as the tier-1 lead investor
- Framed 2022 (founding Sei) as his highest-growth year, with non-engineering skills mattering most
- Advises young engineers not to over-index on the risk of leaving a job
