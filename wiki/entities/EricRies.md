## Definition
Eric Ries is the entrepreneur and author who created the Lean Startup methodology. In the keynote, his Build-Measure-Learn loop is cited as a precursor to the iterative improvement cycles used in DevOps, MLOps, and now LLM application development.

## Key Information
- Author of "The Lean Startup," which introduced the Build-Measure-Learn feedback loop
- His methodology focused on iterative improvement of the entire business, not just the product
- The Lean Startup loop is presented as a direct ancestor of the virtuous cycle of improvement (evals → data → improvements) recommended for LLM applications
- The genealogy traced in the talk: Toyota Production System → Lean Startup → DevOps → MLOps → LLM application development
