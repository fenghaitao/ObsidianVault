## Definition
Paige Bailey is a DevRel (Developer Relations) lead at Google DeepMind, with a background in machine learning dating back to 2009. She contributed to early open-source scientific computing libraries like NumPy, SciPy, and scikit-learn, and has experience in product, engineering, design, and DevRel roles.
