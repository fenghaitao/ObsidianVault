## Definition

Pablo Galindo Salgado is a CPython core developer, primarily working on the compiler, parser, and profilers. Known for his emotional and candid talks about the open source maintainer crisis in the age of AI.

## Key Information

- CPython core developer focusing on low-level internals (compiler, C parser)
- Presented at PyAI London 2026 with David Hewitt on "Maintaining OSS in the age of AI"
- Diagnoses the AI slop crisis as an asymmetric DDoS on maintainer attention
- Notes that "our ability to care is a global resource that is depleting"
- Observes that the old contributor-to-maintainer pipeline may be gone forever
