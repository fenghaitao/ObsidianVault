## Definition

FTI Consulting is a global business advisory and consulting firm. In the legal-AI context, it co-produces the annual "General Counsel Report" with [[Relativity]], tracking corporate legal departments' technology and AI adoption.

## Key Information

- Co-authored the 2026 General Counsel Report (with [[Relativity]]), which found that 87% of general counsel now report generative-AI use within their teams — up from 44% the prior year and just 20% in 2023.
- Cited by Anthropic in its May 2026 legal-industry deployment guide as evidence that legal AI adoption is accelerating industry-wide, driven by more complex matters and faster client-turnaround expectations.
