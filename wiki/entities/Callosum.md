## Definition
Callosum is a company building infrastructure for heterogeneous intelligence -- the paradigm where different AI model architectures, sizes, and hardware types work together in coordinated multi-agent systems. It optimizes at three levels: hardware selection per agent, agent interaction workflows, and workflow construction.

## Key Information
- Founded to spearhead the heterogeneous intelligence paradigm
- Optimizes multi-agent systems at three levels: hardware, agent interaction, and workflow construction
- Developed **heterogeneous recursion**, extending recursive language models with multi-chip/model mapping for long-context tasks
- On ULong benchmark: 7x cheaper/5x faster (Cerebras) and 12x cheaper/3x faster (SambaNova) vs GPT-5.2
- Beat GPT-5.2 by 18% and Gemini 2.5 by 25% on Video Web Arena using heterogeneous video action language models
- Working with [[Aria UK Institute]] on a £3M grant for the UK's first heterogeneous collocated cluster
- [[Adrian Bertagnoli]] is a founding engineer
