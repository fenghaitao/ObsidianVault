## Definition
Human Layer is a company founded by Dex Horthy. Dex Horthy originated the "Smart Zone and Dumb Zone" concept describing LLM performance degradation within context windows.

## Key Information
- Founded by Dex Horthy
- Dex Horthy created the Smart Zone / Dumb Zone concept that Matt Pocock uses as a foundational principle in his AI coding workflow
- The concept describes how LLMs perform best in the first ~100K tokens of a context window (Smart Zone) and degrade progressively beyond that (Dumb Zone)
