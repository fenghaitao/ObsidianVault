## Definition

The Complexity Zoo is a website, started by Scott Aaronson, cataloging the hundreds of complexity classes known in computational complexity theory.

## Key Information

- Wigderson references the site when describing the "zoo of complexity classes" — "hundreds and hundreds of complexity classes of all types."
- The classes classify problems by resource usage (time, space, communication, randomness, parallelism), and the Zoo collects them, reflecting how rich the landscape of models is.
