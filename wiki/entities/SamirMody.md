## Definition

Samir Mody is the head of AI engineering at The Browser Company of New York, where he leads the development of AI features for the Dia browser.

## Key Information

- Head of AI engineering at The Browser Company of New York
- Led the transition from Arc to Dia, the company's AI-native browser
- Advocates for treating model behavior as a craft and discipline
- Emphasizes building AI tooling into the product itself for faster iteration
- Presented at AIE CODE 2025 on lessons learned building AI browsers
