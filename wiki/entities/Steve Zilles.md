## Definition

Steve Zilles was the MIT graduate student who co-authored Barbara Liskov's paper on data abstraction, which sketched abstract data types and how a programming language could support them.

## Key Information

- Co-authored the impactful data-abstraction paper with Liskov that sketched the notion of abstract data types.
