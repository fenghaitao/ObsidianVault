## Definition

Venture capitalist and author of *Hard Things About Hard Things*, recommended by [[Jeetu Patel]] for how it addresses managing one's own psychology under pressure. Also cited by Patel for his view that company culture is a set of behaviors, not stated beliefs.

## Key Information

- Patel cites Horowitz's "sandwich" critique — saying something nice, then something critical, then something nice again — as a pattern he deliberately avoids in favor of direct, adult communication.
- Also cited by Patel as source of the framing that culture is what people actually do when things go wrong, not a values statement.
