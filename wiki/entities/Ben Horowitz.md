## Definition

Ben Horowitz is a co-founder of Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) and author of "Hard Thing About Hard Things," a book about managing psychology when things get difficult.

## Key Information

- Co-founder of Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)
- Author of "Hard Thing About Hard Things" — recommended by Jeetu Patel as one of the most important books
- The book focuses on how to manage your psychology when things get hard
- Jeetu Patel references the "shit sandwich" technique from the book: praise, criticism, praise — but Jeetu disagrees with this approach
