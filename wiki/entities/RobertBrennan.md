## Definition
Robert Brennan is the co-founder and CEO of OpenHands, with over a decade of experience in open-source developer tooling and natural language processing. He advocates for agent orchestration as the next frontier in AI-assisted software engineering.

## Key Information
- Co-founder and CEO at OpenHands (formerly OpenDevin)
- Background spans open-source dev tooling and NLP, each for over a decade
- Started OpenHands (as OpenDevin) in early 2024 after the Devon demo, motivated by keeping AI-driven software engineering open and community-driven
- Describes the shift from writing code to delegating to agents as similar to the transition from IC to manager
- Advocates for ~90% automation with human review at intermediate steps, not full automation
- Emphasizes that most developers will use single local agents, while a small percentage of early adopters will orchestrate parallel agents for massive productivity gains
