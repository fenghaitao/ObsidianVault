## Definition

Prior [[Lenny's Podcast]] guest and product thinker cited by [[Lenny Rachitsky]] as the source of the framing that "most adults in the corporate environment are really just babies in disguise." Name as transcribed in the source; the raw auto-transcript may have mangled this name, so treat the spelling as uncertain pending cross-reference with the actual episode credits.

## Key Information

- Rachitsky: "I have a former podcast guest, Ryas Doshi. He's one of my favorite product thinkers. He's been on the podcast a couple times." He attributes to this person a "rant" that adults in corporate environments behave like babies in disguise — always wanting to be the center of attention, refusing to share resources/budget/people, getting irrationally upset when things don't go their way, engaging in power struggles, and expecting others to fix things for them.
- This framing is the episode's opening premise, which Kennedy reinterprets as: all humans need the same things regardless of age, and unmet needs produce ineffective behavior at any age (see [[Feelings Overpower Skills]]).
