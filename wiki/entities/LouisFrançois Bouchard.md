## Definition
Louis-François Bouchard is the CTO and co-founder of Towards AI, an educational company focused on AI engineering. He was previously a PhD student in AI before transitioning to full-time education after ChatGPT's release. He has been creating videos explaining research papers for seven years and developing in the AI engineering space since 2020.

## Key Information
- CTO and co-founder of Towards AI
- Former PhD student in AI who switched to education full-time after ChatGPT's release
- Has been creating videos explaining research papers for seven years
- Has been developing in the AI engineering space since 2020 at his first startup
- Co-presented a 2-hour workshop on building deep research agents and writing workflows
- Advocates for starting with the simplest solution (prompting) and only adding complexity (workflows, agents) when needed
- Emphasizes that AI products combine workflows, tools, and agents — never just one pattern
- Believes high-quality technical content is expensive to produce and that AI should augment writers, not replace them
- Uses the "autonomy slider" mental model: more autonomy means less control and higher cost
