## Definition

Scott McNealy was Sun Microsystems' co-founder and CEO, credited with resisting layoffs early in Sun's decline and with a concise epitaph for the company.

## Key Information

- McNealy famously did not want to lay people off, which Cantrill considers a mistake — he "waited too long"; Sun eventually ran ~35 rounds of layoffs over about eight years.
- Cantrill quotes McNealy's epitaph for Sun as "Kick butt, had fun, didn't she? Loved her customers, changed computing forever," which Oxide adopted as part of its own mission.
