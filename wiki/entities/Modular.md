## Definition
Modular is a company founded by Chris Lattner and world-class AI/compiler engineers that is rebuilding the AI stack from the bottom up for the generative AI era, creating the MAX framework and Mojo programming language.

## Key Information
- Staffed by engineers who built TensorFlow, PyTorch, LLVM, MLIR, and XLA
- Founded a couple of years before 2024, making it a fairly young company
- Core product is [[MAX]], an AI framework focused on inference across CPUs and GPUs
- Also built [[Mojo]], a Pythonic systems programming language to power MAX
- Business model: MAX AI framework is free and widely available; Modular makes money through managed services
- Goal is to enable users to "own your AI" — control data, deploy where you want, integrate into your own security
- Supports production deployment on Kubernetes and SageMaker
- About 200,000 developers and 20,000 Discord community members as of mid-2024
