## Definition
The Open Benchmarks Grant is a $3 million commitment by [[SnorkelAI]] to fund and accelerate the next wave of open benchmarks for AI agents. The program accepts applications from academic teams and builders.

## Key Information
- Funded by Snorkel AI with $3 million committed
- Launched a few weeks before June 2026
- Over 120 applications received from academia and industry labs
- Still accepting proposals at benchmarks.snorkel.ai
- Goal: accelerate benchmarks that steer and guide where the AI field is going
- [[VincentChen]] helps run the program and review applications
