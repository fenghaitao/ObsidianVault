## Definition

Sash is an Instagram staff engineer (IC6) who reached staff at Meta despite switching teams ten times in nine years and deliberately optimizing for fun and breadth over impact.

## Key Information

- Referred to only by first name in the episode
- Hired as an IC4 at Meta (Facebook) and promoted to IC6 without ever optimizing for career growth
- Career arc: "newsfeed delight" browser animations → experimental hardware (AOSP/Android) → Instagram blockchain/NFT → the Threads prototype → AI prototypes
- Became known internally as a prototyping person people reached out to, by repeatedly posting working prototypes on Workplace
- Left Meta to return to startups as a two-way-door decision
