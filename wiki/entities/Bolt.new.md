## Definition

AI coding co-pilot product founded by [[Eric Simons]] that "blew up" in popularity; cited by [[Mark Pincus]] as an admired example of "being less ambitious" leading to a big breakout.

## Key Information

- Originated from a web-stack virtual-machine project the team had open-sourced and toiled on in relative obscurity, barely sustaining commercial development.
- The breakout realization: combining their existing web-stack virtual machine with an AI coding co-pilot produced something better than anything else available — an example of a small, humble starting point (an open-source side project) leading to an outsized outcome.
- Pincus was impressed enough to cold-email the founder.

### Per episode 35 (Jason Lemkin)

- Per [[Jason Lemkin]]: a distant "number three" behind [[Replit]] and [[Lovable]] by his ranking, but grew from $0 to $50 million in revenue in about six months. A former SaaStr sales colleague who now runs sales there told Lemkin the team has so many inbound leads that "half our job is picking which ones to respond to," and that Bolt closed a seven-figure deal "stolen" from Lovable simply because Lovable never called the prospect back.
- Cited as an example supporting Lemkin's "[[Plays Vs Playbooks]]" framework: at extreme demand levels, picking and choosing prospects (rather than trying to serve everyone) becomes a viable, even necessary, GTM strategy.
