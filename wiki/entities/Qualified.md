## Definition

Qualified is an AI-powered inbound lead qualification platform founded by the former CMO of Salesforce. It uses a chat bubble (similar to Intercom) to instantly qualify website visitors, filter out bad-fit prospects, and book meetings — operating 24/7.

## Key Information

- Founded by the former CMO of Salesforce
- Used by SaaStr for inbound qualification on sasterannual.com
- Immediately worked for SaaStr: qualified a sponsor at 11 PM on a Saturday night who ended up sponsoring
- Handles qualification 24/7 — something no human was willing to do
- Saves enormous time by qualifying out bad-fit prospects automatically
- Jason Lemkin's selection criteria: Qualified paired with Artisan as the two vendors most willing to help with deployment
- Key use case: replaces the human BDR who would otherwise take 1-2 days to respond to a "Contact Me" form submission
