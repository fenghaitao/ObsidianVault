## Definition

Qualified is an AI inbound-lead-qualification agent company ("Quali" is [[SaaStr]]'s nickname for its instance), founded by a former CMO of [[Salesforce]], that [[Jason Lemkin]] deployed as SaaStr's second AI GTM agent to qualify inbound sponsorship/event leads 24/7.

## Key Information

- Deployed on sasterannual.com (SaaStr's event site) to replace the "contact me" form experience: it fully qualifies every website visitor without them realizing they're being qualified, then automatically books a meeting with a human, or waitlists/declines a poor fit.
- Immediate result cited by Lemkin: someone submitted interest in sponsoring at 11pm on a Saturday night, was engaged and sold on the spot by the agent — a response speed and availability no human SDR would match.
- Selected as the second vendor (after [[Artisan]]) because it was "such low-hanging fruit" — no human at SaaStr was willing to pick up the phone and qualify inbound leads at all hours — and because, like Artisan, Qualified's team did hands-on implementation work rather than just shipping software.
