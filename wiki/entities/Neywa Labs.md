## Definition
Neywa Labs is the company founded by Prince Canuma, focused on building on-device AI capabilities on Apple Silicon via the MLX ecosystem. Their products include MLX VLM, MLX Audio, MLX Video, and Marvis TTS.

## Key Information
- Founded by Prince Canuma
- Focused on on-device AI for Apple Silicon (iPhone, iPad, Mac)
- Key projects: MLX VLM (vision), MLX Audio (speech/audio), MLX Video (generation), Marvis (TTS)
- Works with frontier labs for day-zero model support on MLX
- Marvis TTS generates audio in under 100 milliseconds
- Supports both Python and Swift for native app development
- Community projects include grounded visual reasoning, on-device video generation, and robotics integration
- 1.5M+ downloads across MLX ecosystem projects
