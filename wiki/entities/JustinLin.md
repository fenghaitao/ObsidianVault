## Definition
Justin Lin is an AI researcher who published work on memory layers, demonstrating that they can learn close to as much as full fine-tuning while experiencing almost no catastrophic forgetting.

## Key Information
- Published research on memory layers as a method for injecting knowledge into models
- Showed that memory layers can be controlled by specifying exactly which parts get updated, keeping changes minimal
- His results showed memory layers basically don't forget at all while learning close to as much as full fine-tuning
- Jack Morris cited his work as evidence that memory layers may be the best approach for knowledge injection when preserving base model knowledge is critical
