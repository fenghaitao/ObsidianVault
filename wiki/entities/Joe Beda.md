## Definition

Joe Beda is a software engineer and co-creator of Kubernetes. In this transcript Brendan Burns refers to him by first name only ("Joe") and describes him as a great engineer who was "fantastic at API design."

## Key Information

- One of the three people (with Brendan Burns and Craig McLuckie) who built the initial Kubernetes demo at Google.
- The API-design specialist of the founding trio; Burns stereotypes the trio's split as "Craig was the product business guy and Joe is the… really good at design kind of person and I was basically [the] hack prototypes" one.
- Along with Burns and Craig, he shared the aesthetic of distributed, democratic ownership rather than a "benevolent dictator for life" model.
