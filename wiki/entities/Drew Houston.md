## Definition
Drew Houston is the founder of Dropbox. James Cowling discussed the S3 migration project with him as early as 2010, before joining the company in 2012.
## Key Information
- Referred to in the episode as "Drew, the Dropbox founder."
- There was a long-standing desire at Dropbox (from before Cowling arrived) to control the company's destiny by owning its storage system rather than depending on S3.
- The storage migration team had a "contract with the Dropbox founders" — run with no downtime, no data loss, and no incidents for six months before any S3 data would be deleted.
