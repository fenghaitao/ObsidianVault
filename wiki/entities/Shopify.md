## Definition

Shopify is cited by [[Dan Shipper]] as an early real-world example of the "one super agent per company" model — it runs a company-wide agent internally referred to as "River."

## Key Information

- Dan Shipper points to Shopify's internal agent (nicknamed "River") as evidence that companies are already converging on a single shared/company-wide agent rather than giving every employee their own personal agent — part of his broader prediction about the "[[Parallel Org Chart|death of org charts]]."
- Per [[Keith Rabois]] (episode 15): for over 2 years, Shopify has banned PMs from presenting static PowerPoint/Keynote product proposals — every product presentation must be a working demo, with executives refusing to review anything non-functional. Cited by Rabois as evidence that PM, design, and engineering are "merging together."
- Per [[Simon Willison]] (episode 17): cited (alongside [[Cloudflare]]) as hiring roughly 1,000 interns in 2025 partly because AI coding assistance collapsed the traditional month-long intern-onboarding ramp to about a week — evidence for his observation that AI disproportionately benefits senior engineers and new/junior engineers, squeezing mid-career engineers hardest.
