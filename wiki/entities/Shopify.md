## Definition

Shopify is a commerce/e-commerce technology company. Ilya Grigorik grew to Distinguished Engineer (L10, VP-equivalent) there.

## Key Information

- Ilya Grigorik became a Distinguished Engineer at Shopify, a level he frames as VP-equivalent
- At Shopify (as elsewhere), Distinguished Engineers are expected to be "parachuted into foreign terrain" and figure out where to apply their skills within the first seven days, with no prescription given
- Grigorik mentors principal engineers coming into Shopify, with the expectation that they discover problems and build alliances with directors/VPs themselves
