## Definition

Shopify deployed Claude to power Sidekick, an AI assistant that translates complex merchant requests in natural language into ShopifyQL queries that previously required technical expertise.

## Claude Opus 4.6 Early Access (February 2026)

Engineers fed the model into existing iterative planning loops. Staff Engineer Paulo Arruda gave a vague one-line instruction ("move something from one page into another menu item") and got back more than asked for — details he didn't know he wanted, anticipating his next request: "I found myself saying 'You're absolutely right' to the AI instead of the other way around." Staff Engineer Ben Lafferty had Opus 4.6 port a large TypeScript library to Ruby for an internal prototype in one shot, creating a test shim first and validating against the original spec: "This was one of the first early access periods where I haven't had substantial feedback to give... For me, Opus 4.6 is the first model from Anthropic that feels like a true collaborator."
