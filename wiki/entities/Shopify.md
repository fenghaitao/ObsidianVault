## Definition

Shopify is an e-commerce platform, used by Butterfield as an example of a product where the challenge is comprehension, not friction — most first-time store openers don't know about reports, how to tweak them, or which metrics matter most.

## Key Information

- The purpose of the service is generally clear to end users
- Most first-time store openers don't know they can get reports, don't know what kinds of reports are available, and don't know how to customize them
- This is an example of how comprehension, not friction, is the real challenge for most product features
- Lovable's growth team launched a Shopify integration to enable e-commerce use cases, allowing users to vibe code their storefronts
- Shopify's CTO revealed on Lenny's Podcast that their team of ~100 people uses holdout groups to verify AB test results — about a third of positive test results disappear over time, demonstrating the prevalence of false positives in AB testing
