## Definition

Oculus is Meta's virtual reality division, acquired by Facebook. Philip Su worked there as an individual contributor after stepping down from being Site Director of Meta London, and was re-leveled from E9 to E7.

## Key Information

- Philip Su switched from being Site Director of Facebook London to an individual contributor on Oculus, which led to his deliberate re-leveling from E9 to E7
- John Carmack worked at Oculus and was known for his prolific coding and ability to drop into any codebase and provide concrete technical advice
- Philip worked on Oculus 360 Photos, a "very tiny product" — Carmack randomly dropped into its codebase, tried it, and provided insights about distortion on photo corners and performance optimizations
- Oculus was the context where Meta had to invent the IC9 level to accommodate exceptional individual contributors like Carmack
