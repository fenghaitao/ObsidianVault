## Definition

VR hardware startup founded by [[Palmer Luckey]] and colleagues who met hacking PlayStations/Super Nintendos into portable backpacks; acquired by [[Meta]] (then Facebook), where [[Caitlin Kalinowski]] led VR hardware including the Rift and [[Quest 2]].

## Key Information

- Founded with a rapid-iteration hacker ethos that Kalinowski says was good DNA for a hardware team; had built the Crescent Bay prototype prior to the Meta acquisition.
- After acquisition, Meta's challenge was to professionalize the rapid-iteration culture: raise manufacturing yields, increase production volumes, and reduce cost, starting with the first Rift.
- VR under Oculus/Meta drove foundational technology (SLAM, depth sensing, spatial perception) that Kalinowski says is now directly reused in robotics and autonomy.
- Kalinowski reflects that VR itself became a "niche" (partly due to the social isolation of covering one's face, as also seen with [[Google Glass]]) rather than the mass-market breakthrough originally expected.
