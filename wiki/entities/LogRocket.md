## Definition
LogRocket is a frontend monitoring and session replay platform. Raindrop is used alongside LogRocket and Sentry internally — Raindrop focuses on "fuzzy failures" (user frustration, refusals) while LogRocket handles traditional frontend observability.

## Key Information
- Frontend monitoring and session replay platform
- Used internally at Raindrop alongside Sentry
- Raindrop complements LogRocket by handling semantic/fuzzy failure detection that traditional observability tools don't cover
