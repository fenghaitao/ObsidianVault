## Definition
Mahmoud Mabrouk is the co-founder and CEO of Agenta AI, an open-source LLMOps platform. He has over 15 years of experience in machine learning, with a background in academia working on ML applied to computational biology and protein structure prediction.

## Key Information
- Co-founder and CEO of [[Agenta AI]]
- 15+ years experience in machine learning
- Previous academic background in ML applied to computational biology and protein structure prediction
- Current work focuses on sampling and auto-optimization workflows
- Presented a workshop on building calibrated LLM-as-judge evaluators using GEPA at aiDotEngineer
- Advocated for: calibrated LLM judges over generic hallucination detectors, binary evaluation metrics over numeric scales, custom reflection templates for GEPA optimization, and overfitting to training data as a first step
- Key optimization results: improved LLM-as-judge accuracy from 61% to 74% on TauBench airline agent data using GEPA
