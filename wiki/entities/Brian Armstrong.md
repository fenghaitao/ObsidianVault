## Definition

Brian Armstrong is the founder and CEO of Coinbase. Lenny cites him as an example of a founder whose unique background (economics, cryptography, engineering) created the perfect Venn diagram for starting Coinbase.

## Key Information

- Lenny draws a parallel between Brian Armstrong's unique background enabling Coinbase and Edwin Chen's unique background (math, CS, linguistics, research at major tech companies) enabling Surge AI
- Armstrong gave a talk about how his unique set of experiences allowed him to start Coinbase, which parallels Edwin's thesis that founders should build companies only they could build
