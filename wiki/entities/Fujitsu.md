## Definition

Japanese technology company whose "Horizon" accounting software was at the center of the UK Post Office scandal, cited by [[Benedict Evans]] as a cautionary precedent for the harms of blindly trusting buggy automated systems.

## Key Information

- The Horizon system contained bugs that produced false accounting shortfalls; hundreds of UK subpostmasters were wrongly prosecuted and some imprisoned as a result of institutions trusting the software's output over the humans it accused.
- Evans uses this case to argue that new technology creates genuinely new categories of serious harm (not just hype-driven fears), and that the right response is care and societal maturity, not blanket dismissal of new technology.
