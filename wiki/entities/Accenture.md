## Definition
Accenture is a global professional services and consulting firm specializing in digital transformation, technology, and operations for large enterprises. In the context of AI, Accenture's practitioners work on deploying agentic AI solutions at scale within the world's largest organizations.

## Key Information
- Serves enormous enterprises: telecoms, utilities, national infrastructure, government, healthcare, consumer products
- Research shows that only 12% of companies reach "AI achiever" status — achieving 50% higher revenue growth than peers
- 80% of companies are still stuck piloting and spending millions without meaningful returns
- Core insight from practitioners: the enterprise scaffold (governance, process, control layers) is the primary drag on AI value capture, not technology limitations
- Advises CFOs to think like VCs with portfolio-based AI investment rather than project-by-project business case justification
- Promotes progressive autonomy (shadow → advisory → controlled → full) for deploying AI agents into production
- Jess Grogan-Avignon and Jack Wang are Accenture practitioners who presented at AI Engineer Summit
