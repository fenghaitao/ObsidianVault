## Definition

Accenture is a global professional services and consulting firm. It is a [[ClaudeSecurity|Claude Security]] services partner, building Cyber.AI on [[Claude4.7Opus|Claude Opus]] to close the gap between finding and fixing vulnerabilities.

## Key Information

- **Cyber.AI**: an agentic platform that connects assets, identities, threats, and controls into a single operational model that Claude Opus reasons across, running detection, prioritization, and remediation as a continuous loop.
- **Internal validation at scale**: Accenture validated Cyber.AI internally first, taking security testing coverage from roughly 10% to over 80% across 1,600 applications and 500,000+ APIs, and cutting scan turnaround from 3–5 days to under an hour in their own global IT infrastructure.
- **Services partner (April 2026)**: named among the services partners (with BCG, Deloitte, Infosys, PwC) helping enterprises deploy Claude-integrated security solutions for vulnerability management, secure code review, and incident response, as part of Claude Security's public beta launch.
- Quote: *"Business leaders are navigating the fastest moving and most complex cyber threat landscape in history. We're partnering with Anthropic to deliver the tools clients need to stay ahead."* — Harpreet Sidhu, Global Lead, Accenture Cybersecurity.
