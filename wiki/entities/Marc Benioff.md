## Definition

Marc Benioff (rendered "Mark Beni off" in the transcript's ASR) is the founder/CEO of [[Salesforce]], discussed by [[Jason Lemkin]] regarding Salesforce's "Agent Force" AI product and its renewed hands-on, forward-deployed customer support model.

## Key Information

- Per Lemkin, Benioff talks a lot about Agent Force, Salesforce's AI agent product, which [[SaaStr]] deployed for lapsed-lead reactivation.
- Quoted (via Lemkin, secondhand) admiring [[Palantir]]: the "number one thing" Benioff says he envies about Palantir is not just its high deal sizes but that every Palantir customer can go live before they pay — something Benioff says he wishes Salesforce could do but can't yet, calling Salesforce "the biggest ship to turn" at $44 billion in revenue.
- Lemkin frames this as emblematic of an industry-wide shift away from the old SaaS sales playbook (avoid pilots, roll out slowly, get the signature before delivering value) toward AI having "upped the bar" on what customers expect before they pay.
- Runs roughly 2,000 people at Salesforce doing hands-on, forward-deployed customer implementation work, which Lemkin says mirrors how Salesforce treated customers 20 years ago (when Lemkin bought his first two seats) before that high-touch model faded.
