## Definition

Samsara is a technology company building products for the physical world, combining hardware, software, and edge AI. Their technology protects first responders, truck drivers, construction workers, and others who work in physical environments.

## Key Information

- Sponsor of Lenny's Podcast
- Builds products at the intersection of hardware, software, and edge AI
- Their AI doesn't just detect events; it reasons about intent — e.g., distinguishing between a truck driver braking abruptly due to distraction vs. a heroic act
- Works with enormous datasets and solves edge constraints at a planetary scale
- Operates in small teams, moving fast
- Mission: make the physical world safer and more efficient
