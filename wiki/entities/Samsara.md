## Definition

Samsara is a company building technology at the intersection of hardware, software, and edge AI for physical-world industries (first responders, trucking, construction), presented as a sponsor of this [[Lenny's Podcast]] episode.

## Key Information

- Pitched as protecting frontline physical workers via sensors/telemetry whose AI "reasons about intent" (e.g., distinguishing a truck driver braking abruptly due to distraction versus a heroic evasive action) rather than just detecting raw events.
- Recruiting pitch emphasizes working with large real-world datasets and small teams on physical-world safety/efficiency problems, including grounding LLMs in messy real-world telemetry and edge-constrained AI at scale.
