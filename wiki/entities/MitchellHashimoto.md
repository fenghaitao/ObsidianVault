## Definition
Mitchell Hashimoto is the founder of Ghosty, the open-source terminal emulator. He is a notable user of Amp Code, using it to drive development of Ghosty.

## Key Information
- Founder of Ghosty, an open-source terminal emulator
- Uses Amp Code to drive many of the changes he makes to Ghosty
