## Definition
NotebookLM is a Google AI-powered notebook product known for its podcast generation feature that creates natural-sounding two-person audio discussions from uploaded documents using text-to-speech technology.

## Key Information
- Google AI notebook product with document understanding capabilities
- Notable feature: podcast generation — creates AI-hosted audio discussions from documents
- Uses two distinct TTS voices for multi-character conversation
- The podcast feature inspired techniques for creating multi-character dialogue with TTS
- Guillaume Vernade demonstrated extending the two-voice concept to more characters using voice style control within a single TTS voice
