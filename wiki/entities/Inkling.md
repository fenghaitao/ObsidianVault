## Definition

Inkling was a mobile learning/content platform co-founded and led as CEO by [[Matt MacInnis]] from 2009 until it was sold to a private equity firm in 2018.

## Key Information

- MacInnis spent nine years on Inkling, riding what he calls "the curve of Silicon Valley coolness" up and then back down "into obscurity."
- He describes repeatedly believing the company had found product-market fit and, in hindsight, being certain it never truly had — a formative example he cites for his [[Binding Receptor Theory of Product-Market Fit|binding-receptor view of product-market fit]] and his argument that [[Never Quit Is VC Propaganda|persistence past ~4-5 years without clear PMF is usually a mistake]].
- He does not regret the experience — he says the chapter of his career he's in now (at [[Rippling]]) could not have happened without it — but frames it as a cautionary example of "trying until you die" rather than a success story.
