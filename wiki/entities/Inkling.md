## Definition

Inkling was a startup founded by Matt McGinness in 2009, which he ran for nine years before selling it to a private equity firm in 2018. The company experienced the full Silicon Valley hype cycle, rising up the "curve of coolness" and then descending into obscurity.

## Key Information

- Founded by Matt McGinness in 2009
- Sold to a private equity firm in 2018
- Experienced multiple pivots over nine years
- McGinness reflects that in hindsight, they should have quit after the second or third pivot (around year 4)
- McGinness views the experience as a lesson that they never truly had product-market fit despite thinking they did at various points
- The experience informed McGinness's later view that "learning from mistakes" is overrated compared to learning from successes
