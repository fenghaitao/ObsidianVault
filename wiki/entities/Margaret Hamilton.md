## Definition

Margaret Hamilton is the software pioneer who, after starting as a core programmer, managed the real-time software for the Apollo flight computer.

## Key Information

- One of the original core programmers on Apollo and manager of the whole real-time software that ran the Apollo flight computer — today widely heralded as a major real-time-systems achievement of that era.
- Had to write a defense of the Apollo guidance computer after people called the 1201/1202 alarms a failure without understanding that the computer "tripped" the alarms because it was used in an unplanned configuration — and, rather than crashing, went into backup modes and landed successfully.
- Casey cites her defense as a triumph of fault-tolerant engineering and an early example of a pattern that persists today: successful work gets "slandered" and sparking a flame war, then the engineer must respond.
