## Definition

The first VR headset shipped by [[Meta]] (then Facebook) after acquiring [[Oculus]], built from the Crescent Bay prototype; [[Caitlin Kalinowski]] led its hardware development, professionalizing the startup's rapid-iteration culture into a production-ready manufacturing process.

## Key Information

- Meta's post-acquisition challenge was to raise manufacturing yields, increase production volumes, and reduce cost relative to the prototype-stage hardware Oculus had built.
- Preceded [[Quest 2]] in Meta's VR hardware lineage; both drove foundational SLAM, depth-sensing, and spatial-perception technology later reused across robotics and autonomy.
