## Definition

Artist and author, wife of [[Lenny Rachitsky]]; creator of the viral chart-based book *Am I Overthinking This*, and author of the children's book *Charts for Babies* (published around the same time as this episode). Interviews Lenny in this role-reversal episode.

## Key Information

- Her charts are frequently shared without attribution across social media and turned into unlicensed merchandise (pillows, mugs) — she says she's stopped tracking this since it no longer bothers her as much as her own work has grown.
- Describes her creative process: charts succeed when they make *her* laugh or feel something even after she's already seen the idea in her own head; favors simple, quickly-digestible concepts over overly clever multi-layered ones (Lenny notes he sometimes has to push her to simplify). Ideas come from closely observing everyday life and (per her own account) partly from insights surfaced during meditation.
- *Charts for Babies* grew out of a notebook she'd originally started (from the front) as a "charts for new parents" project — she found herself writing children's-book-style rhymes in the back of the same notebook and ultimately filled it from both directions before committing to the children's book idea.
- Cites reading extensively to her son and her own father's early habit of showing her math patterns/fractals as childhood influences behind her visual, pattern-based thinking style.
- Personal creative-process detail (offered half-jokingly): a "Ballmer Peak"-style optimal caffeine window (a single-shot latte, roughly a 2-hour window with an external deadline) as her ideal condition for generating new chart ideas.
