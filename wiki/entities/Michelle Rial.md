## Definition

Michelle Rial is an author, chart artist, and Lenny Rachitsky's wife. She is known for creating viral charts that synthesize human experiences and emotions into simple, shareable visuals.

## Key Information

- Author of "Am I Overthinking This" -- a book of charts about overthinking everyday situations
- Author of a children's book called "Charts for Babies"
- Working on "charts for parents" content
- Her charts often go viral but are frequently detached from her attribution (people cut out her name, sell on merchandise)
- Creative process: ideas come from "living life and noticing," observing her own thinking (especially during meditation)
- Knows a chart is ready when it makes her laugh or feel something deeply even though she created it
- Works best when she's out living life, not when focused too narrowly on work
- Started a children's book in the back of a notebook while working on a different project in the front -- following intuitive pull
- Her charts succeed because they're simple, quick to digest, and show something the viewer hasn't thought of before
