## Definition
Alonzo Church was the mathematician and logician who developed the lambda calculus, a model of computation with no notion of mutation.

## Key Information
- The episode recounts that Alan Turing did his PhD at Princeton under Church's supervision, working on the Turing machine at the same time Church worked on the lambda calculus.
- The lambda calculus executes programs "just by reduction" and is "the essence of functional programming."
- Church's untyped lambda calculus was later given a static type system as System F (by Girard), which GHC's Core language implements.
