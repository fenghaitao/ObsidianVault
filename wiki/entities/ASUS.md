## Definition
ASUS is a hardware OEM that manufactures the Nvidia Spark (DGX Spark) desktop AI system, as Nvidia typically partners with OEMs rather than selling hardware directly to consumers.

## Key Information
- Manufactures the Nvidia Spark (DGX Spark) as an OEM partner
- Nvidia typically partners with OEMs rather than selling hardware directly; the Spark Founders Edition is an exception
- The ASUS Spark costs approximately $4,000
- Alex Cheema demonstrated heterogeneous prefill-decode disaggregation using an ASUS Spark paired with a MacBook
