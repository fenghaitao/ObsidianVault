## Definition

Flight-tracking mobile app cited by Tony Fadell as his prime example of "luxury software" — a well-crafted, well-architected original product that could be partially cloned or vibe-coded, but whose original craft and architecture couldn't be replicated by AI alone.

## Key Information

- Fadell calls the original Flighty "luxury software," contrasting it with cheap, throwaway "fast fashion" AI-generated software.
- He argues that while an AI coding agent might now be able to "vibe code" a copycat version-two of Flighty (since it exists as a reference), the original version-one product — including its overall architecture and pixel-level craft — required real human design taste and could not have been produced by AI alone.
- Fadell describes himself as one of Flighty's biggest word-of-mouth promoters among people he knows who fly frequently.
- Used as the positive counterexample to the episode's warning about AI-driven technical debt and "cognitive surrender."
