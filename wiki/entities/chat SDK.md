## Definition

chat SDK is a personal project by Malte Ubl (CTO of Vercel) that connects AI agents to chat applications like Slack, Telegram, and WhatsApp. It is part of his "vibe coding stack."

## Key Information

- Creator: Malte Ubl, CTO of Vercel
- Purpose: Hook AI agents to any chat app (Slack, Telegram, WhatsApp, etc.)
- Part of Malte's "vibe coding stack" alongside just bash
- Mentioned in the opening keynote of the first AI Engineer conference in Europe
