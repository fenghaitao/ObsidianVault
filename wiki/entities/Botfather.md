## Definition

The Botfather is Telegram's bot creation tool. Setting up OpenClaw communication through Telegram requires messaging the Botfather to create a bot that the agent can use to send and receive messages.

## Key Information

- Telegram's official bot creation interface
- Required step for setting up OpenClaw communication through Telegram
- Claire Vo: "You do have to talk to this guy called the Botfather on Telegram... just close your eyes and message the Botfather and don't think too hard about how weird that is"
- Always present in Claire's Telegram alongside her agent bots
