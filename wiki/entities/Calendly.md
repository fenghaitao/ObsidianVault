## Definition

Calendly is a scheduling tool that Sam Lessin famously hates. He went on a viral diatribe about how disrespectful it is, generating millions of engagements that meaningfully drove Calendly's growth for a month.

## Key Information

- Sam Lessin is "fairly famous for hating Calendly"
- Sam's viral rant about Calendly generated so much engagement that it meaningfully drove their monthly growth and he got messages from their board
- Sam's criticism: Calendly implies a flat hierarchy where all meetings are treated equally, which doesn't reflect real-world power dynamics
- Sam believes the default should not be Calendly — the less senior/less busy person should let the other person tell them when they're free
- Lenny Rachitsky notes a flaw: someone once figured out his Calendly URL and booked a meeting without permission
