## Definition

Molly Graham is a leader and operator known for her frameworks on rapid career growth, scaling companies, and organizational culture. She worked at Google, Facebook, the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative, and Quip, and now leads Glue Club, a community for leaders in fast-growing companies.

## Key Information

- Early employee at Google where she experienced the "two PhD students paradise" culture
- Spent five years at Facebook in HR and mobile roles, working closely with Mark Zuckerberg and Sheryl Sandberg
- Worked with Mark Zuckerberg on building the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative (CZI)
- Scaled Quip with Bret Taylor before it was sold to Salesforce
- Created the "Giving Away Your Legos" framework for career growth in scaling companies
- Developed the "J-Curve vs Stairs" career growth model, based on advice from Chamath Palihapitiya
- Originated the Waterline Model for diagnosing organizational problems
- Created the "Bob" concept for managing the emotional inner voice during change
- Six Rules for Goal Setting framework
- Runs Glue Club (glueclub.com), a community for leaders operating in changing, growing companies
- Has a Substack called "Lessons"
- Key advice: "Get to know your customer. They have the answer. Be patient because everything is going to change. Just keep trying."
- Core belief: all you take away from a company is people who want to work with you again and what you learned
