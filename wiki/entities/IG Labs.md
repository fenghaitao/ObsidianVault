## Definition

IG Labs was a small, high-talent-density team at Instagram co-founded by Ryan Olson (initially with designer Vivian Wong) to pursue new product initiatives that didn't slot cleanly into the existing org.

## Key Information

- Began as just Ryan and a designer (Vivian Wong); conceived as a "Delta Force"-like group of high talent density working on new initiatives.
- Motivation: as Instagram grew, projects not slotting into an org were underinvested; IG Labs would span across focus areas (e.g., location/maps/places ideas under the Instagram brand).
- Notable output: collaborative posts (multiple authors on one post), on which Ryan holds a patent.
- Its concentrated talent later seeded Threads when that initiative emerged.
