## Definition

University of California, Berkeley — a leading research university. Its researchers co-authored a paper with Data Bricks finding that 74-75% of enterprises cite reliability as their biggest barrier to deploying AI products.

## Key Information

- Researchers (including Mate Zahara Stoker) co-authored a paper with [[Data Bricks]] on enterprise AI adoption
- The paper found ~74-75% of surveyed enterprises cite reliability as their biggest problem with deploying AI products
- This reliability concern explains why many AI products today focus on productivity tools rather than end-to-end autonomous agents
- The paper supports the thesis that starting with high control/low agency is the right approach for AI product development
