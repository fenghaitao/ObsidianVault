## Definition

UC Berkeley (the University of California, Berkeley; referred to simply as "Berkeley" in both episodes) is where Mike Stonebraker built Ingres as a young professor and where Barbara Liskov did her undergraduate work.

## Key Information

### Mike Stonebraker
- Hired as an assistant professor; Eugene Wong mentored him from 1971 and they started Ingres in 1972, earning Stonebraker tenure in 1976.
- About a hundred universities ran the free Ingres on Unix; Arizona State's failed student-records attempt (no COBOL on Unix) led to Ingres Corporation in 1980.

### Barbara Liskov
- Rejected by Princeton's graduate program ("We do not admit women"), she attended Berkeley, where she had done her undergraduate math degree.
- Berkeley was co-ed and more open — the idea that "a door was shut and women couldn't do things" was not how Berkeley operated.
