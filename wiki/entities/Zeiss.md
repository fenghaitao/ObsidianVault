## Definition

German optics company (founded 1885), cited by [[Eric Ries]] in *[[Incorruptible (book)|Incorruptible]]* as a parallel "industrial foundation" governance case study to [[Novo Nordisk]].

## Key Information

- Academic research cited by Ries: companies with this industrial-foundation structure (Novo Nordisk and Zeiss) are six times more likely to survive to year 50 than conventional counterparts, and show superior return on invested capital.
