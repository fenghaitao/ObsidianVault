## Definition

Chamath Palihapitiya ("Chamath," rendered "Chimath"/"Chimoth" in the transcript) ran growth and mobile at [[Facebook]] during [[Molly Graham]]'s tenure there. He recruited her from HR onto Facebook's mobile-phone hardware project, an experience that became the origin story for her "[[J Curve Versus Stairs]]" framework.

## Key Information

- Recruited Graham with a characteristically blunt pitch: "you're useless, what are you doing in HR... this is stupid, you should come work for me" — which Graham describes as his signature move of insulting and complimenting someone in the same sentence.
- Asked her to help build a Facebook-branded mobile phone despite her having no relevant background; gave her, at the end of a six-month ramp-up, "the lowest performance rating I've ever gotten," per Graham.
- Drew Graham the original whiteboard picture contrasting predictable "stairs" careers (promoted every two years) with "J-curve" careers (a period of falling followed by a climb far beyond what stairs could reach) — the direct origin of her "[[J Curve Versus Stairs]]" framework.
