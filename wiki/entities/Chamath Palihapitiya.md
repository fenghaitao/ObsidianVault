## Definition

Chamath Palihapitiya is a former Facebook executive who ran growth and mobile. He is the originator of the "J-Curve vs Stairs" career growth metaphor that Molly Graham popularized.

## Key Information

- Ran growth and mobile at Facebook during Molly Graham's tenure
- Recruited Graham from HR to the mobile team, which was a radical career pivot for her
- Drew a picture on a whiteboard explaining the J-Curve vs Stairs career model: "The way a lot of people do careers is a set of stairs. Just walk up the stairs and you'll get promoted every 2 years. But that is boring. The much more fun careers are like jumping off cliffs."
- Gave Graham the lowest performance rating she ever received in her first six months in the mobile role, but she later became an expert
- The Facebook phone project was a "massive, costly failure" for Facebook but was not a failure for Graham personally
