## Definition

[[Mark Pincus]]'s first company, built entirely around an offline-browsing feature that was buried as a minor power-user feature inside Netscape and Internet Explorer.

## Key Information

- Pincus found "offline browsing" buried in Netscape/Internet Explorer as a niche feature and built an entire standalone product around it, because bandwidth was slow enough at the time to make that one feature valuable on its own.
- Cited as an example (alongside [[Nikita Bier]]/[[TBH]]) of spotting a "proven" feature buried and mishandled inside someone else's product — "when we can see something that is proven already in someone else's product but they have the wrong idea around it, that's gold."
- Started with a "very small, humble premise," per Pincus's "being less ambitious" principle, and had bigger success than he expected.
