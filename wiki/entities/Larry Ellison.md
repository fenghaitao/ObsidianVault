## Definition

Larry Ellison is the founder of Oracle, whom Mike Stonebraker describes as a "fabulous salesman" who competed against Ingres through shady business practices.

## Key Information

- Stonebraker: Ellison "made present tense and future tense indistinguishable" and effectively lied to customers, shipping software that didn't work and having early customers help debug it — "unconscionable" behavior.
- Oracle wrote two manual pages defining referential integrity ("not yet implemented"), while Ingres Corporation had it implemented.
- Ryan Peterman notes that someone from Sun Microsystems shared a similar view that Ellison was "a little bit shady."
