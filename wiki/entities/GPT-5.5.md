## Definition

GPT-5.5 is an [[OpenAI]] model that [[Dan Shipper]] cites as the first model to score meaningfully higher on his "[[Senior Engineer Benchmark]]," and which he says writes most of his email and documents by the time of this episode.

## Key Information

- On Dan Shipper's Senior Engineer Benchmark (rewriting the vibe-coded [[Proof]] codebase from first principles), every model up through the one before GPT-5.5 scored around 30/100 versus a human senior engineer's high-80s/low-90s; GPT-5.5 scored 62/100 (60 of those points using an "Opus 4.7 plan" alongside it) — the first roughly 30-point jump Dan has seen.
- The reason for the jump, per Dan Shipper: GPT-5.5 is the first model with enough "agency and confidence" to actually rip out and rewrite bad code from first principles when told to, rather than papering over problems with small patches around the edges (which he says every other coding model, including this one a year from now by his prediction, will still default to when just told "fix the issues").
- Also credited with writing "most" of Dan Shipper's email and many of Every's quarterly strategy documents (in combination with Codex/Notion agents) — part of his argument that AI-generated writing is becoming normal and preferred once a human takes responsibility for the content.
- Roughly three or four months before this episode, OpenAI shifted its coding-model direction (around the time it launched "5.3") toward being better at general-purpose knowledge-work tasks rather than being narrowly technical, a shift Dan Shipper says led into the [[Codex]] desktop app.
