## Definition

Figma is a collaborative web-based design tool. Evan King and Stephan built a product that allowed users to copy any website and paste it directly into Figma as fully editable frames.

## Key Information

- Evan and Stephan built a plugin that allowed users to go to any website, click one button to copy it, and paste it directly into Figma as fully editable frames
- The product was a "great tool for designers" with significant technical sophistication
- Stephan did the majority of the reverse engineering required to make the paste functionality work, since Figma's paste format was encrypted
- Other companies in the space wanted the technology and acquired it after only a couple of months
- This was the second of Evan and Stephan's startups to be acquired
