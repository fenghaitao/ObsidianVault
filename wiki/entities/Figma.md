## Definition

Figma is a collaborative design platform and maker of Figma Make, an AI-powered tool that turns prompts and designs into functional prototypes and apps.

## Key Information

- Figma Make enables users to create code-backed prototypes and apps from prompts within the Figma environment.
- LinkedIn is working with Figma to integrate its design systems so AI can reason over them effectively.
- Different teams at LinkedIn gravitate toward different design tools (Figma, Subframe, Magic Patterns), creating a convergence challenge.
- Figma was a sponsor of this podcast episode.

- Dr. Becky Kennedy is "obsessed" with Figma Make and uses it to prototype her product ideas: "I can vomit my ideas and I have so many ideas... I want to see the UX design or I want to see the whole email flow"
- [[Andrew Hogan]] (Figma insights lead) is a "big Good Inside member" and connected Dr. Becky with the Figma team
- Jenny Wen, former director of design at Figma (led FigJam and Slides), still uses Figma extensively as a designer
- Jenny says Figma is still important because: (1) exploring many different options (8-10 directions) is critical, and coding tools are too linear for this, (2) fine visual and interaction details like typography or styles are best explored on a canvas
- Jenny: "When I use Figma, I'm like, yes, this is what I should be using and it still fills a very good gap for me"
- "Figmates" is what Figma employees are called
- Anthropic uses Figma extensively — Amol notes they use Figma "a ton" and don't see that changing in the immediate future, despite all the talk of AI replacing SaaS tools
