## Definition

Deep-tech investment and advisory firm founded by Tony Fadell (buildc.com), investing in hardware, software, and chemical technologies aimed at unseating incumbents through fundamentally different technology.

## Key Information

- Invests in "deep technology" — hardware, software, software-plus-hardware, or chemical — focused on environment, societal benefit, and health, with a portfolio that has at times exceeded 200 companies.
- Portfolio/advisory companies mentioned in the episode include Simbe Robotics (retail inventory robotics), Great Parrot (AI-driven recycling sorting), Recursion (AI drug design), Groq, and Cerebras (AI chips).
- Beyond capital, the firm advises portfolio companies on product management, operations, financing, org development, and especially marketing/communications and storytelling.
- Philosophy: invest in technology that can fundamentally change a market or product category (not just compete on features or marketing), even if it takes longer to scale and adopt.
- Fadell notes the firm invested in some of these companies years before their categories became hyped, at pre-hype valuations, which he considers necessary for real venture returns.
- Website (buildc.com) is highlighted by Fadell as an example of marketing built for storytelling/education rather than direct e-commerce conversion.
