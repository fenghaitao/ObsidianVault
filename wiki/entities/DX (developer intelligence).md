## Definition

DX (getdx.com) is a developer intelligence platform designed by leading researchers to help organizations thrive in the AI era. It provides data and insights on which AI tools are working, how they're being used, and what's actually driving value.

## Key Information

- Companies using DX include Dropbox, Booking.com, Adion, and Intercom
- Provides deep understanding of how AI is providing value to developers and what impact AI is having on engineering productivity
- Sponsor of Lenny's Podcast
