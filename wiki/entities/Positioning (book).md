## Definition

*Positioning* is a classic, short marketing book recommended by [[Stewart Butterfield]] as directly informing his "[[We Don't Sell Saddles Here]]" memo and philosophy.

## Key Information

- Central argument Butterfield cites: it's almost impossible to implant a genuinely new idea in someone's head — it's far easier to combine ideas people already hold (e.g., "it's Jaws meets Star Wars," "Uber for pets"). Any product meaningfully different from existing alternatives isn't just creating a product, it's creating the surrounding market/mental category too.
