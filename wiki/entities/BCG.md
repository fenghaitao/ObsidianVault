## Definition

BCG (Boston Consulting Group) is a global management consulting firm. It is a [[ClaudeSecurity|Claude Security]] services partner building defensive cyber offerings on [[Claude4.7Opus|Claude Opus]].

## Key Information

- **Services partner (April 2026)**: named among the services partners (with Accenture, Deloitte, Infosys, PwC) helping enterprises deploy Claude-integrated security solutions for vulnerability management, secure code review, and incident response, as part of Claude Security's public beta launch.
- **May 2026 update**: confirmed to be building a defensive cyber offering on Claude Opus, with details to be shared as they become available.
