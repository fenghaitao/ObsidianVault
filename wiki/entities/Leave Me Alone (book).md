## Definition

Full title: *Leave Me Alone: A Good Inside Story About Deeply Feeling Kids*. [[Dr. Becky Kennedy]]'s second children's book, published under [[Good Inside]], about kids whose core fear is being "too much."

## Key Information

- Pre-orders were open at time of this episode; scheduled release February 2026, with Kennedy planning a related tour.
- Subject: "[[Deeply Feeling Kids]]" — a temperament pattern Kennedy calls her "passion project within the passion project that is Good Inside," describing them as the most misunderstood kids, often labeled dramatic or oppositional, whose actual core fear is being too much rather than a loss of control.
- Title explanation: "leave me alone" is what a deeply feeling kid often screams when completely out of control — Kennedy frames this as their deepest fear (being pushed away/confirmed as "too much"), not their actual wish; the book is intended as a companion for the kid to see themselves, paired with the app as the companion for the parent.
- Kennedy notes the pattern resonates for adults too — several people told her the title gave them insight into a partner or coworker's fear of being "too much," where big, explosive feelings push away the very people they need, confirming the fear in a self-reinforcing cycle.
