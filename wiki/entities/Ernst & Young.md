## Definition

Ernst & Young (EY) is a global professional services firm that runs the "Entrepreneur of the Year" award program. Jason Cohen and Heather Brunner co-won the 2017 Central Texas award.

## Key Information

- Runs the Entrepreneur of the Year award program
- Jason Cohen and Heather Brunner co-won for Central Texas in 2017
- Co-winners are rare for this award — Jason couldn't recall another example
- The award recognized both Cohen (founder) and Brunner (CEO) for their joint leadership of WP Engine
