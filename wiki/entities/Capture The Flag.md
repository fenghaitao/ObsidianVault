## Definition

Capture The Flag (CTF) is a cybersecurity competition where participants solve security-related challenges. Evan King founded a CTF club at Cornell and competed in CTFs through Reddit teams before starting the club.

## Key Information

- Evan King had done CTF competitions himself and entered teams through Reddit before founding the Cornell club
- The Cornell CTF club was founded by Evan by putting up flyers around the engineering quad, talking to ~25 people, and selecting a few as founding officers
- The club grew to a core team of ~25 people who competed in CTFs, with up to 200 people attending weekly lectures on hacking topics
- The club was advised by Emin Gun Sirer (now CEO of Avalanche blockchain)
- Evan noted that CTF and competitive coding (like TopCoder) are associated with "really really good technically brilliant engineers"
- The hacking and CTF experience set Evan up well for Meta, though he ultimately chose content integrity over cyber security
