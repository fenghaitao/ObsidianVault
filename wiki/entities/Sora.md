## Definition

Sora is OpenAI's AI video generation model. Edwin Chen uses it as an example of a product choice that reveals something about what kind of AI future a company wants to build.

## Key Information

- Edwin questions whether building Sora is the right thing to do, asking "which companies would build Sora and which wouldn't"
- The answer to this question "maybe reveals certain things about what kinds of AI models those companies want to build and what direction and what future they want to achieve"
- The steelman argument for Sora: it's fun, people want it, it generates revenue, and it trains data in interesting ways
- Edwin's counter: the path you take matters — "would you sell tabloids in order to fund some other newspaper?" — and the path may have negative consequences that harm the long-term direction
- The Sora Android app was built in 18 days from zero to employee launch, and 28 days total to public GA, by 2-3 engineers using Codex
- The app became the #1 app in the App Store, built with a handful of engineers in a handful of weeks
- The engineers used Codex to look at the iOS app, produce plans of work, and implement the Android version by looking at iOS and Android simultaneously
- Alexander Imbiricos called this "one of the most mind-blowing examples of acceleration" at OpenAI
