## Definition

Peak Salvation is a podcast miniseries by Philip Su about his time working at Amazon's flagship warehouse over peak season (Black Friday to Christmas). It explores concerns about automation, the income gap in America, and technical unemployment.

## Key Information

- Philip hired Rebecca Ambrose as a professional editor for the podcast, learning a lot from her feedback
- The podcast explores Philip's concerns about automation, what it means for society, and the rising income gap in America
- Philip believes Americans need to "get together and figure out how we help the average person" through the AI/automation transition
- Philip recommends the podcast to people interested in technical unemployment and societal adaptation to automation
