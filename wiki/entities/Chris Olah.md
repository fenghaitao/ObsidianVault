## Definition

Anthropic researcher credited by [[Boris Cherny]] with inventing the field of mechanistic interpretability — studying an AI model's internal neurons directly (analogous to studying neurons in a biological brain) to understand what concepts they encode and how the model reasons.

## Key Information

- Cherny suggests Lenny have him on the podcast as the industry expert on this topic.
- His field underlies the first of Cherny's "[[AI Safety Three Layers]]" — allowing Anthropic to detect, for example, when a neuron associated with deception is activating, and to understand phenomena like superposition (a single neuron encoding multiple concepts at once as models scale).
