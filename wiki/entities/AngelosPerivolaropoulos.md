## Definition
Angelos Perivolaropoulos is a research engineer who leads the speech-to-text team at ElevenLabs. He trains new models, works on inference, and also contributes on the product side. His team built Scribe V2, currently the best transcription model on public benchmarks.

## Key Information
- Leads the speech-to-text team at ElevenLabs
- His team built Scribe V2, the best transcription model on public benchmarks
- Currently working on training real-time transcription models for agents
- Describes himself as someone who "really likes to go straight into research and do train models and make things that are state of the art and very powerful"
- Also handles product-side responsibilities including client communication
- Delivered a hands-on workshop at aiDotEngineer on training an LLM from scratch locally using only PyTorch and basic libraries
- The workshop was inspired by Andrej Karpathy's nanoGPT project
- Built a ~1.8M parameter GPT-2-style transformer trained on Shakespeare, covering tokenizers, architecture, training loops, and inference
