## Definition
Marvin is an agent framework developed by Jeremiah Lowin that served as his entry point into AI developer experience before he created FastMCP.

## Key Information
- Developed by Jeremiah Lowin a few years before his FastMCP work
- Described by Lowin as "not wildly popular, but it was my leg into the world of AI, at least from a developer experience standpoint"
- Lowin learned a lot from the Marvin project that informed his later work on FastMCP and MCP best practices
