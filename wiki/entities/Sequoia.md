## Definition

Sequoia Capital is a prominent venture capital firm. Brian Shrier, a partner at Sequoia, served on Matt McGinness's board at Inkling and recommended the book "Conscious Business."

## Key Information

- Brian Shrier of Sequoia was on the board of Inkling (Matt McGinness's previous company)
- Shrier recommended the book "Conscious Business" by Fred Kaufman to McGinness
- McGinness regrets not taking the book recommendation sooner
