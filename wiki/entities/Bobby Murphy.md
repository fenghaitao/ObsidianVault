## Definition

Co-founder of [[Snapchat]] with [[Evan Spiegel]]; has a computer-science/mathematical/computational-science background but a strong personal interest in design, forming a generalist design-engineering dialogue with Spiegel that later became the template for Snap's whole design-team culture.

## Key Information

- Created Snap's Gen AI lab roughly a decade before this episode (well before the current generative-AI wave); credited with the real-time on-device machine-learning transformation behind Snapchat's early face-swap lens.
- His and Spiegel's original working relationship — one design-oriented, one engineering-oriented, both with mutual respect for the other's discipline — is cited by Spiegel as the model later scaled into Snap's broader [[Loonshots]]-style design/engineering dialogue.
