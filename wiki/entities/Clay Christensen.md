## Definition

Late management theorist quoted by [[Eric Ries]] in *Incorruptible*: "it's easier to do the right thing 100% of the time than 98% of the time." Author of *[[Innovator's Dilemma]]*, which [[Jeetu Patel]] calls "the Bible in tech."

## Key Information

- Ries cites this line to explain why mission-aligned companies feel easier to operate in day-to-day: once a principle is 100% non-negotiable, there is no need for debate/spreadsheets each time a tempting exception arises, because the answer is already known.
- Jeetu Patel recommends re-reading *Innovator's Dilemma* and its sequel *Innovator's Solutions* every few years.
