## Definition

Adam Simpkins is a Facebook/Meta engineer who worked with Michael Bolin and Wes Furlong on the Eden virtual file system project.

## Key Information

- One of the "tremendous engineers" Brian O'Sullivan gathered to build the virtual file system.
- Bolin says he thought of himself as "the worst engineer on the [team] for quite a bit," a compliment to Simpkins and Furlong's caliber.
