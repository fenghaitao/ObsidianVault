## Definition
Arvind is the computer scientist who led MIT's dataflow group and designed the Monsoon dataflow machine.

## Key Information
- Led the MIT dataflow group ("the MIT data flow group led by Arvind"), which built the Monsoon machine to execute dataflow (functional) programs.
- Monsoon was based on a token store, matching, and tokens flowing through a graph: when inputs arrive at a node, the node can fire.
