## Definition

Founder of [[Oculus]] (VR) and later founder of a defense-technology/robotics company building military hardware ("war robot" building, per the transcript). Described as a friend of [[Caitlin Kalinowski]]'s.

## Key Information

- Started Oculus, which was acquired by [[Meta]]/Facebook; Kalinowski notes it's striking that the same person who built consumer VR (seen at the time as "just playing games") now leads a company building military technology.
- Kalinowski says she and Luckey "don't agree on everything" but share the view that the U.S. needs to invest much more in drones than in aircraft carriers, framing this as a shift from legacy military platforms to fast-iterating, AI/software-driven warfare (citing Ukraine's rapid drone iteration with 3D printing as the model).
- Kalinowski has personally chosen not to work for companies that create lethal technology, but says she thinks "it's good to have people willing to do that" and that it takes a range of people to build the future.
