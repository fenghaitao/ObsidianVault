## Definition

*Heated Rivalry* is a six-episode TV series about two closeted, secretly-in-love rival hockey players, named by [[Matt MacInnis]] as his favorite recent show in the episode's lightning round.

## Key Information

- Airs on HBO Max and Crave; MacInnis, who is gay, calls it "smutty but delightful" and appreciates seeing gay characters represented as normal.
- Reportedly in negotiations for a Netflix acquisition at the time of the episode.
