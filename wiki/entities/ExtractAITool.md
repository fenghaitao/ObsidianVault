## Definition
Extract is an AI tool developed by the UK Incubator for AI in collaboration with DeepMind, built on Gemini. It digitizes large swaths of the planning application process, including handwritten documents and hand-drawn maps, and is being rolled out to every local authority in England.

## Key Information
- Collaboration between [[IncubatorForAI]] and [[GoogleDeepMind|DeepMind]]
- Built on Google's Gemini model
- Digitizes planning application processes, including handwritten content and hand-drawn maps
- Unveiled by the UK Prime Minister at London Tech Week
- Being rolled out to every local authority in England
- Addresses the problem that only 1 in 5 planning applications are currently decided on time
- Aims to enable more planning applications to be decided by AI automatically
- Impacts economic growth by speeding up planning decisions
