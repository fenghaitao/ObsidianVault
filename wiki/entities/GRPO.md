## Definition
GRPO (Group Relative Policy Optimization) is a fine-tuning method that DSPy's prompt optimizers have been shown to match or exceed in performance, according to research by Chris Potts.

## Key Information
- GRPO is a fine-tuning approach that requires infrastructure setup and model training.
- Research by Chris Potts indicates DSPy's Jeepa optimizer can achieve performance on par with or exceeding GRPO.
- The comparison supports the argument that prompt optimization can be a viable alternative to fine-tuning for certain tasks.
- Unlike GRPO, DSPy optimizers work by iteratively tweaking prompts rather than modifying model weights.
