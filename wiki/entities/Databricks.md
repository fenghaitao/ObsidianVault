## Definition

Databricks (transcribed "data bricks") is the company co-founded by Matei Zaharia, the creator of Spark, which orchestrates Spark jobs at cloud scale.

## Key Information

- Matei Zaharia, a Stanford faculty member and Databricks founder, created Spark.
- At Databricks, "at any given time we might be orchestrating a million Spark jobs"; OS schedulers couldn't scale, so scheduling data went into a Postgres database — the seed of the DBOS idea.
