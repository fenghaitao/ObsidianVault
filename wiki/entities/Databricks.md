## Definition
Databricks is a data and AI platform company. Phil Hetzel led Slalom Consulting's global Databricks business unit for 8 years, helping enterprise clients with data and AI implementations including generative AI proofs of concept.

## Key Information
- Data and AI platform (Apache Spark-based, with lakehouse architecture)
- Phil Hetzel led Slalom's global Databricks practice
- Clients built GenAI POCs on Databricks but struggled to reach production — a gap that motivated Hetzel's move to Braintrust
- Referenced in the context of enterprise AI implementation challenges
