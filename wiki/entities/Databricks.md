## Definition

Data/AI infrastructure company cited by [[Aishwarya Naresh Reganti]] and [[Kiriti Badam]] as one of the companies where they have deployed AI products, and as the home of researchers behind a survey Reganti cites on enterprise AI reliability concerns.

## Key Information

- Named alongside [[Amazon]], [[OpenAI]], and [[Google]] as companies across which Reganti and Badam have led or supported 50+ AI product deployments.
- Reganti cites a paper from "a bunch of folks at UC Berkeley... basically Mate Zahara, Stoker, and the folks at Databricks" finding that roughly 74-75% of surveyed enterprises identified reliability as their biggest obstacle to deploying AI products to end users — the names are garbled in the transcript's ASR but almost certainly refer to Databricks co-founders/UC Berkeley researchers [[Matei Zaharia]] and [[Ion Stoica]].
- This reliability finding is cited as the explanation for why most current AI products skew toward internal productivity use cases rather than customer-facing, end-to-end autonomous agents.
