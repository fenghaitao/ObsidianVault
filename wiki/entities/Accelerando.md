## Definition

Accelerando is a science fiction novel by Charles Stross. Boris Cherny says it captures the essence of the current AI moment better than any other book, with its accelerating pace culminating in a collective lobster consciousness orbiting Jupiter.

## Key Information

- By Charles Stross
- Boris: "Captures the essence of this moment that we're in more than any other book that I've read"
- The pace gets faster and faster as the story progresses
- Starts as technological lift-off begins and approaches the singularity
- "Ends with this collective lobster consciousness orbiting Jupiter"
- Takes place over the span of a few decades
- Boris's favorite genre is sci-fi
