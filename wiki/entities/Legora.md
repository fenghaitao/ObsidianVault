## Definition

Legora is a unified workspace where legal work happens — whether by humans, agents, or in collaboration. Claude models, especially Opus, excel at fine legal drafting by matching document and lawyer-specific styles. Legora designs its system to improve with every new model release.

## Key Information

- Presented a session at [[CodeWithClaude|Code w/ Claude London 2026]] (May 20–21) titled "What Legal Agents Inherit from Coding Agents: Lessons from Legora," exploring how patterns from agentic coding translate to the legal domain.
- Legora's design philosophy treats Claude as a drafting collaborator that adapts to individual lawyer styles rather than a generic automation tool.
