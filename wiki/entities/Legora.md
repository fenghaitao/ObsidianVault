## Definition
Legora is a vertical AI company that builds a collaborative AI workspace for law firms. It serves over 1,000 customers across more than 50 markets and is growing extremely fast.

## Key Information
- Vertical AI company focused on the legal industry
- Collaborative AI workspace for law firms
- Over 1,000 customers across more than 50 markets
- Has raised significant funding; described as growing "maybe the fastest in history"
- Hiring engineers in London
- CTO is Jacob Lauritzen
- Product features include: document collaboration with agent highlighting and commenting, tabular review (a known primitive where agents flag items needing human input), and domain-specific high-bandwidth artifacts
- Philosophy: agents should collaborate with humans through persistent, domain-specific artifacts rather than chat interfaces
- Applies Verifier's Rule to legal tasks: checking contract definitions (easy to verify), writing contracts (hard — only a judge can truly verify), litigation strategy (impossible — no objective truth)
