## Definition

The Great Post Explorer was an internal tool at Facebook (Meta) built by Philip Su to rank and expose people to the most commented-on and reacted-to posts of all time within Facebook's internal network (Workplace). It served as a crowd-sourced, categorized catalog of timeless internal content.

## Key Information

- Built by Philip Su inside Facebook's internal network (Workplace)
- Allowed users to discover the most commented-on or most reacted-to posts of all time
- Posts were categorized by crowdsourcing, enabling navigation by categories
- Philip's motivation: he benefited from serendipitously discovering great posts by people who came before him, and wanted these cataloged for others
- The tool addressed the problem that posts fade temporally, but some posts have timeless (evergreen) value
- Ryan L. Peterman described it as a "curated set of quality posts where all of Meta was helping you curate"
- The tool was still alive as of ~2 years before the interview (2023) but was taken down by the time of the interview (2025)
