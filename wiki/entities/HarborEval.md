## Definition
Harbor is an evaluation infrastructure and harness shipped with [[TerminalBench]] 2.0. It has become a de facto standard for teams building AI agents, providing standardized tooling for running and evaluating models against benchmarks.

## Key Information
- Shipped with Terminal Bench 2.0
- Became a de facto evaluation harness for agent builders
- Provides standardized infrastructure for running models against benchmarks
- Cited by [[VincentChen]] as an exemplar of [[Researcher UX]] — prioritizing the builder experience
