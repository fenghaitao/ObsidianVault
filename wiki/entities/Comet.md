## Definition

Comet was Meta's rewrite of facebook.com as a single-page web app. Boris Cherny co-built it with Facebook Groups as an early adopter.

## Key Information

- Core contributors included Tom Occhino (now at Vercel) and Jing Chen
- Boris volunteered Facebook Groups as the "guinea pig," letting the product team influence the direction of an infra project — unusual for its time
- Co-built abstractions reused by other teams (e.g., relay mutations — queuing mutations as "consistency at the cost of reliability")
- The migration began with 12 engineers and grew to roughly 20–30 engineers over about two years
- Boris estimates modern AI tools would shrink that to ~5 engineers for ~6 months
