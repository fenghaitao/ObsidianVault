## Definition

Comet is an ML/AI evaluation platform where Vincent Koc works as an eval researcher. The company runs benchmarks and evaluations for large enterprises including Uber, Netflix, and UK banks, and collaborates with universities on eval research.

- **Research**: Collaborates with universities on evaluation research
