## Definition

AWS Lambda is AWS's serverless compute service. Marc Brooker was on the Lambda team in 2020, where customer conversations about serverless and containers led him to his later database work.

## Key Information

- Brooker worked on Lambda in 2020 and talked to many customers who were excited to build on serverless and containers but found relational data didn't fit the paradigm.
- That signal sent him to the Aurora team, producing Aurora Serverless and Aurora D SQL.
- Some Lambda traffic-management and burst-handling algorithms draw on Erlang's roughly century-old telephony call-center queueing work.
