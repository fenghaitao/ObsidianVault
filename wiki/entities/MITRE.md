## Definition

MITRE is the company where Barbara Liskov worked as a researcher before and after graduate school; it is where she invented her notion of modularity that became data abstraction.

## Key Information

- Working at Mitre after grad school with a small team building a complicated system, Liskov invented a more complete notion of modularity ("I wanted to keep us out of trouble") that later let her see data abstraction.
- After her PhD she had no good faculty offers, so she returned to Mitre for four years, using the time to transition from AI to systems work without the pressure of students and teaching.
