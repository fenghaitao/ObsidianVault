# Windsurf

## Definition

Windsurf is an AI coding tool mentioned by Jason Warner as one of the engineering assistant products in the ecosystem that could potentially consume Poolside's models.

## Key Information

- **Category**: AI-powered coding assistant / engineering tool
- **Context**: Listed alongside Cursor, Cognition (Devin), and Replit as engineering assistants that could integrate Poolside's models
- **Ecosystem Role**: Part of the growing landscape of AI coding tools that Poolside aims to serve as a model provider
