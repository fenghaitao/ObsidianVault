## Definition

Outeré is a Seattle-based nonprofit founded by Philip Su that builds free software for global health. It was funded by the Gates Foundation and deployed in Nigeria, Kenya, and South Africa for malaria rapid testing.

## Key Information

- Founded by Philip Su in Seattle after leaving Facebook
- Funded by a grant from the Gates Foundation
- Builds free software for global health
- Deployed in Nigeria, Kenya, and South Africa
- Specifically assists with rapid testing for malaria
- Philip founded it because he felt "very fortunate and lucky to have been chosen to get a grant" and building free software for global health "seemed like a great idea"
