## Definition

The Greek Theatre is an outdoor amphitheater on the UC Berkeley campus in Berkeley, California. It's an iconic music venue where attendees can sit on the grass with blankets.

## Key Information

- Outdoor amphitheater on UC Berkeley's campus
- Known for great music performances
- Attendees can sit on grass with blankets
- Jessica Fain lives nearby and has a poster of Jimi Hendrix playing at the Greek
