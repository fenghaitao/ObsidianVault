## Definition

Agentforce is Salesforce's AI agent platform, heavily promoted by CEO Marc Benioff. It enables organizations to deploy AI agents for sales, service, and other CRM workflows, plugging into the Salesforce ecosystem as a hub for AI GTM agents.

## Key Information

- SaaStr is one of the earliest organizations of its size to deploy Agentforce
- Used by SaaStr to reactivate lapsed leads that human salespeople deemed "not worth their time" — achieved a 70% response rate
- SaaStr trained other agents first, then captured the best prompt and gave it to Agentforce — it was "pretty good" in a day
- Marc Benioff has deployed ~2,000 people at Salesforce doing forward-deployed engineer-style work to help customers succeed with Agentforce
- Salesforce is experiencing a renaissance as the "hub" or "database" for AI GTM agents — all the AI agent tools plug into it
- Benioff's stated envy of Palantir: high deal sizes and the ability to get customers live before they pay
- Agentforce reflects a return to Salesforce's early days of hands-on customer success, like when a rep would help a 2-seat customer get on the AppExchange
