## Definition

Lightspark is the Bitcoin/Lightning payments company James Everingham co-founded with David Marcus. (The transcript mis-transcribes it once as "Lightspeed Arc.")

## Key Information

- Co-founded with David Marcus after both left Meta; David wanted to build it in LA.
- James commuted ~3 days a week for 2 years, formed the team and built it, then stepped back as the team reached its next scaling stage.
- Mission: drive the cost and latency of moving value globally toward zero, so money moves "like packets of information."
