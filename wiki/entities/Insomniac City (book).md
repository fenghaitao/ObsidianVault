## Definition

Insomniac City is a memoir by Bill Hayes about his partner, the scientist Oliver Sacks, during Sacks' final days. Jenny Wen describes it as a beautiful, ethereal memoir about their love story that makes you think about mortality, love, and life.

## Key Information

- Written by Bill Hayes, partner of Oliver Sacks
- Covers the period around Oliver Sacks' death from cancer
- Jenny Wen calls it "one of my favorite books"
- Described as a "beautiful, kind of ethereal memoir" of Sacks' last days and their love story
- Jenny says it has "very little to do with the stuff on your podcast" but is just a book she really loves
