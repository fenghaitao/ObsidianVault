## Definition

Matt MacInnis is Chief Product Officer and former longtime Chief Operating Officer at [[Rippling]], the $16B+ workforce-management platform. He is the guest of [[Lenny's Podcast]] episode 36.

## Key Information

- Joined Rippling as COO in 2019; transitioned to CPO in January 2025, roughly 11 months before this episode, after Rippling had struggled through repeated hiring mistakes in engineering and product leadership. He described the product org he inherited as "locally optimized but globally incoherent" (invoking [[Conway's Law]]).
- Before Rippling, spent nine years (2009-2018) as co-founder/CEO of [[Inkling]], a mobile learning/content platform that never achieved durable product-market fit and was sold to a private equity firm in 2018.
- Spent roughly eight years at [[Apple]] under [[Steve Jobs]], where he learned the engineering "death march" — no break between shipping one product version and starting the next.
- Angel investor in roughly 60-70 companies, including early stakes in [[Notion]], Clever, Zenefits, and Rippling itself (before joining), plus more recent investments in [[Decagon]] and [[LangChain]]; also invested in numerous companies that went to zero (Macro, Debrief, Verb Data), which he cites deliberately to counter survivorship bias in "who I've invested in" answers.
- 45 years old at time of episode; Canadian; DJ'd under a radio persona in high school/college.
- Signature frameworks: [[Deliberate Understaffing]], [[Alpha Beta Hiring And Process Framework]], [[SPOTAK Hiring Framework]], [[The Pickle (Product Quality List)]], [[Binding Receptor Theory of Product-Market Fit]], [[Compounding Plus Power Law Plus Entropy]], [[Never Quit Is VC Propaganda]], [[Escalations Are A Gift]].
- Runs a "Conscious Business reading club" with his product leadership team at Rippling, handing out physical copies of *[[Conscious Business]]* "like candy."
- Life motto (from his father): "Nothing's ever so bad in life that it couldn't get worse."
- Name is transcribed as "Matt McInness" in some episode transcripts' auto-generated ASR (episode 34's transcript, for instance); correct spelling per public sources is MacInnis.
- Quoted (via [[Lenny Rachitsky]], episode 34) making a point that echoes [[Molly Graham]]'s frameworks: "if you're ever comfortable at work and feel like 'I got this,' you're making a huge mistake" — resonating with Graham's own comfort-is-a-warning-sign philosophy (see [[J Curve Versus Stairs]] and [[Bob The Monster]]); also cited there for advocating deliberate under-resourcing of teams ([[Deliberate Understaffing]], echoing Graham's [[Fifty Percent Growth Rule]]) and for wanting immediate escalation rather than people staying quietly stuck (echoing Graham's [[Escalation Is A Tool]]).
