## Definition

John Carmack is a legendary software engineer known for his work in gaming (id Software) and virtual reality (Oculus/Meta). Philip Su observed his extraordinary technical abilities while working at Oculus.

## Key Information

- Carmack was so prolific at coding that Meta eventually had to invent the IC9 (Distinguished Engineer) level partly to accommodate someone of his caliber
- His standout ability: he could drop into a codebase and product after not looking at it for six months and give actual, concrete, actionable technical advice
- Philip witnessed this firsthand: Carmack randomly dropped into the Oculus 360 Photos codebase (a "very tiny product"), tried it out, and provided insights about distortion on photo corners and performance optimization ideas — all unprompted
- No one on the team had thought of the things Carmack pointed out
- Philip described him as "obviously legendary"
