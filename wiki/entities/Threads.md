## Definition

[[Meta]]'s text-based social app, cited by [[Evan Spiegel]] as one of the only social products launched in the last 15 years to gain real traction — but only because it "sits on top of Instagram" and inherited Meta's existing distribution, not because of product novelty.

## Key Information

- Contrasted with [[TikTok]], which Spiegel says solved distribution differently — by spending billions of dollars subsidizing both sides of its video marketplace (paying creators, acquiring viewers) rather than leveraging an existing platform.
- Used as Spiegel's central evidence for his broader claim that distribution, not product-market fit, is the hardest unsolved problem in consumer technology today.
