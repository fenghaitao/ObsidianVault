## Definition

Peter Scholze (transcribed "Per") is a Fields Medalist mathematician whose "Liquid Tensor Experiment" was formalized in Lean.

## Key Information

- Had an unpublished result he himself was unsure about — one he considered among the most important of his career — and wanted assurance it was correct before publishing; it became the Liquid Tensor Experiment (2020; transcribed "liquid stencil experiments").
- The formalization was done manually (before modern AI), and was a landmark partly because the team formalized and even *simplified* the proof without fully understanding it, guided step-by-step by Lean's Info View.
- The project "attracted lots of attention from the math community" to Lean and, de Moura says, led Terence Tao to start using Lean.
