## Definition

Investor at Sequoia, described by [[Keith Rabois]] as a "product/market-driven" investor (a different investing paradigm from Rabois's own founder-driven approach). Coined the "chef" analogy for what remains valuable in product/design work as AI eats execution: a famous chef isn't valued for personally cooking every dish, but for defining the restaurant's value proposition, differentiation, pricing, and positioning.
