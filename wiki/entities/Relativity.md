## Definition

Relativity is a legal technology company best known for e-discovery and litigation-support software. It co-produces the annual "General Counsel Report" with [[FTIConsulting|FTI Consulting]], tracking corporate legal departments' technology and AI adoption.

## Key Information

- Co-authored the 2026 General Counsel Report (with [[FTIConsulting|FTI Consulting]]), which found that 87% of general counsel now report generative-AI use within their teams — up from 44% the prior year and just 20% in 2023.
- Cited by Anthropic in its May 2026 legal-industry deployment guide as evidence that legal AI adoption is accelerating industry-wide, driven by more complex matters and faster client-turnaround expectations.
