## Definition
David Allen is the author of Getting Things Done (GTD), whose weekly review methodology inspired the weekly reflection feature in Šimon Podhajský's Fulan read-only AI system.

## Key Information
- Author of Getting Things Done (GTD), a widely influential productivity methodology
- The GTD weekly review practice involves systematically reviewing commitments, projects, and next actions
- Fulan's weekly reflection feature is described as "a David Allen style getting things done like spin on the weekly reflection"
- The AI-synthesized reflection covers themes of the week, tensions and conflicts, commitments and relationships, notable moments, and reflection questions
