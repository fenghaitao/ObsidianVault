## Definition
AI Singapore is a research group that trains AI models for Southeast Asian languages, building on open models like Gemma to advance multilingual capabilities for the region.

## Key Information
- Focused on training models for Southeast Asian languages
- Builds on open models including Gemma to push state-of-the-art multilingual capabilities
- Conducts research with open models to advance language coverage for underrepresented languages
- Part of the broader Gemma community ecosystem ("Gemmaverse")
