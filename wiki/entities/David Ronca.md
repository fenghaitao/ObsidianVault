## Definition

David Ronca (transcribed in the episode as "David Rumpka" and "David Rona") is a retired engineering director who led video encoding at Netflix and video processing at Meta, and an early figure in content-based and convex hull encoding.

## Key Information

- Was an engineering director at both Netflix and Facebook/Meta; retired after a ~36-year career.
- Co-developed content-based encoding (with Ioannis Katsavounidis), which became the industry-wide approach to video encoding, and wrote an early patent on it.
- Worked at Innervideo before Netflix, where a 24/7 culture and a stage-3 colon cancer diagnosis reshaped his view of work-life balance.
- At Netflix he led the encoding technology team from one person to ~55 people; later led Meta's ~55-person video processing team starting in 2019.
- Holds a contrarian hiring view: foundational engineering ability matters more than LeetCode performance, and "you interview to the person, not to the process."
- Credits "12 Secrets to Microsoft Success" as the book that most shaped his leadership thinking.
- Active on LinkedIn offering career, leadership, and financial-planning guidance to younger engineers.
