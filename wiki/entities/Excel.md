## Definition
Excel is Microsoft's spreadsheet application; SPJ calls it his second-favorite programming language because its formula language is the world's most widely used functional language.

## Key Information
- The formula language is functional: it has no side effects and programs entirely with values (cells evaluate their dependencies, not "do this then do that").
- "The world's most widely used programming language by three orders of magnitude" — hundreds of millions of users versus millions for imperative languages.
- A weak functional language: no way to define new functions and very limited data types (flat arrays, numbers, strings).
- After ~20 years of effort, Microsoft added LAMBDA to Excel, making the formula language a Turing-complete, "proper grown-up functional language."
- Search "lambda Excel" for blog posts and YouTube videos on the feature.
