## Definition

Boris Cherny is the creator of Claude Code at Anthropic and a former Meta principal engineer (IC8). He started companies as a teenager and authored a TypeScript book before joining big tech.

## Key Information

- Built "chats and groups" at Facebook (bridging Messenger and Facebook), which earned his senior-engineer promotion; later led the "public groups" project to IC7 and was promoted to IC8
- Built Undux, a React state-management framework, and evangelized it internally via ~20–40 team tech talks
- Wrote a book about TypeScript and started what he calls the world's largest TypeScript meetup in San Francisco
- Studied economics, dropped out to do startups, and learned coding practically; recommends practical learning before theory
- Moved to Instagram's Tokyo office for his partner; ran the Python-to-Hack migration and delegated it to Jake Bolam
- Joined Anthropic for mission and safety, motivated by the societal stakes of AI
- Works roughly 9–6 and types with only two fingers, crediting automation and common sense for high output
