## Definition
Juan Herreros Elorza is the team lead for the cloud native technology team at Banking Circle, where he works on platform engineering. He advocates for designing internal developer platforms that serve both human developers and AI coding agents.

## Key Information
- Team lead for cloud native technology at Banking Circle, a global cross-border payments fintech
- Works on Banking Circle's internal developer platform called Atlas, which includes sub-platforms for compute (Kubernetes), infrastructure, messaging, and observability
- Presented at AI Engineer Europe (aiDotEngineer) on making platforms ready for AI agents
- Advocates for self-service, API-first, local-first, well-documented, and observable platforms as essential for AI agent productivity
- Argues that best practices like self-service and good documentation were always important but become critical when AI agents enter the workflow
- Recommends measuring platform success using DORA metrics, reliability metrics, support request volume, and SPACE framework
- Strategic advice: leverage organizational AI interest to implement platform best practices that previously faced resistance
