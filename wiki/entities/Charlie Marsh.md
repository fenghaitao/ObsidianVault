## Definition
Charlie Marsh is the founder of Astral, the Python developer-tools company behind Ruff, uv, and the Red-knot type checker, which was acquired by OpenAI.

## Key Information
- Career arc: worked at Khan Academy after school, then became the second engineer at Spring Discovery (a computational biology drug-discovery company) with no bio or ML background, building ML software systems across Python, Go, and Rust.
- The cross-ecosystem experience exposed him to the limitations of Python tooling; seeing native tooling take off in the JavaScript ecosystem (esbuild, SWC, Bun, Deno) prompted the question "why can't we have that in Python?"
- Created Ruff as a prototype to test that Python tooling could be much faster, and capitalized on the initial Hacker News attention by aggressively triaging and fixing issues within a day.
- Believes many great GitHub projects go undiscovered because they don't know how to market themselves; his philosophy is to communicate honestly and fast (a visual hook, a strong tagline, assume ~10 seconds of attention) without clickbait.
- Chose Rust for Ruff partly for hype, but now considers it "an extremely good bet" for its cargo tooling, opinionated defaults, performance, and memory safety; he would not start a net-new project in C or C++ and finds Zig and Go interesting alternatives.
- On agents: "the cost of putting up a plausible PR has gone to zero, while the cost to review has remained the same"; his teammates told him they now must review his agent-generated PRs far more closely, and he self-reviews each PR line-by-line in the GitHub UI.
- Strong software engineers are more valuable than ever at wielding agents; he remains concerned about the "garbage" he'd generate without significant software-engineering experience.
- Fundraising was preemptive and investor-initiated (seed, series A, series B — A/B never announced); he optimized for a few trusted investors and ran Astral remote-first.
