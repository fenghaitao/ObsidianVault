## Definition

LM Arena (LMSYS Chatbot Arena) is a popular online leaderboard where random users vote on which AI model response is better. Edwin Chen is highly critical of it as a metric for model quality.

## Key Information

- Edwin describes LM Arena as "terrible" — users skim responses for 2 seconds and pick whatever looks flashiest, without fact-checking
- The easiest way to climb LM Arena is to add crazy bolding, double emojis, and triple response length — even if the model starts hallucinating and getting answers wrong
- Frontier labs are forced to pay attention to LM Arena because enterprise customers use it to decide which model to buy
- Researchers at AI labs tell Surge AI that "the only way I'm going to get promoted at the end of the year is if I climb this leaderboard, even though I know that climbing it is probably going to make my model worse in accuracy and instruction following"
- Described as "literally optimizing your models for the types of people who buy tabloids at the grocery store"
