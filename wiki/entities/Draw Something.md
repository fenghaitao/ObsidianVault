## Definition

Drawing/guessing mobile game by [[OMG Pop]] that became the #1 App Store game and app for 60 days in 2012, built by perfectly copying [[Words With Friends]]'s turn-based mechanic — a case study for [[Proven Better New]].

## Key Information

- Created after OMG Pop's earlier, fully original game failed completely, prompting a shift to a proven-first strategy.
- Contrasted directly with [[Angry Birds]] (45-attempts, pure-innovation path) as the lower-variance, proven-first route to a similarly massive hit.
