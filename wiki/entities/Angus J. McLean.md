## Definition
Angus J. McLean is the AI Director at Oliver, an AI-first advertising startup. He works on the strategy side of the agency, focusing on audience insight, trends analysis, competitor analysis, and performance optimization using agents. He presented at aiDotEngineer on "Bounded Autonomy" — the space between free will and determinism in agent design.

## Key Information
- AI Director at Oliver, a startup with 3,000 staff across 46 countries, serving 200+ brands and generating ~4,000 assets per day.
- Works on the strategy side: audience insight, trends analysis, competitor analysis, and performance optimization — all with different agents.
- Oliver operates in a high-paced, high-risk environment as a customer-facing agency with significant media spend (20K to a few million per campaign).
- Uses agents primarily for speed (faster iteration and testing) and secondarily for scale (deeper research on personas and territories).
- Argued that LLMs are fundamentally closed boxes that don't understand data, and that most tools are band-aids around model constraints.
- Advocated for slowing down, keeping things simple, working within constraints, and using multiple representation structures.
- Believes context windows will never be enough because world knowledge doubles every ~12 hours.
- Demonstrated an analytics cluster processing 50,000 tweets into strategy insights almost instantly.
