## Definition
John Deere is a 200-year-old agricultural and manufacturing company whose VP of engineering reported that two-pizza teams are becoming one-pizza teams, partially due to AI tools.

## Key Information
- 200-year-old company that makes tractors and agricultural equipment
- VP of engineering reported that two-pizza teams are now just one-pizza teams
- The shift is partially attributed to AI tools
- Example of AI-driven team size reduction in traditional (non-tech) industries
