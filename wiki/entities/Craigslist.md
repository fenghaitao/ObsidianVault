## Definition

Classifieds website founded by [[Craig Newmark]], cited by [[Mark Pincus]] as an example of a "world-class product maker" perfecting a small, non-obvious improvement rather than a flashy new feature.

## Key Information

- Craig Newmark spent two years adding photos to Craigslist listings — not because photos themselves were hard, but because he wanted to preserve users' ability to quickly scan listing text (avoiding "the text below the fold" problem a less careful product maker might have caused.
- Used as an illustration that products people rely on daily generate ownership and resistance to change; even improvements can feel jarring if they disrupt users' learned pattern recognition.
