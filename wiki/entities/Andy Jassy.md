## Definition

Andy Jassy is Amazon's CEO (formerly of AWS) and, alongside Jeff Bezos, one of the executives Ethan Evans reported to across his Amazon career.

## Key Information

- Ethan reported into Andy Jassy during the Twitch integration, where Andy wanted the acquired business to reach profitability quickly
- As a non-founder "paid leader," Andy can't bet the company the way a founder can — he must partner with the board and with Jeff
- Ethan found Andy more probing and wait-and-see ("it's your plan, you're on the hook") versus Jeff's more enthusiastic "we'll do it together" style

### From the Corporate Politics Episode
- Running AWS, Jassy said of principal engineers: "we just need them solving the hardest problems" — he absolved a few of advising/mentoring duties.
- He tolerated a high-level employee others criticized for "not working that hard" because "he's in a role where we need him… we're just going to have to live with it" — essential skill as leverage over politics.
