## Definition

Switch is a book about change management. It is one of the only business books Jessica Fain has liked. The book introduces the concept of "shrinking the change" — making a scary or overwhelming change feel smaller and more manageable.

## Key Information

- One of the only business books Jessica Fain has liked
- About change management
- Introduces the concept of "shrinking the change": if something seems scary and overwhelming, make it smaller — an experiment, a one-week proof of concept
- This concept is a key tactic for building momentum and trust with executives
