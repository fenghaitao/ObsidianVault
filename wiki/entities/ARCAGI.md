## Definition
ARC-AGI (Abstraction and Reasoning Corpus for Artificial General Intelligence) is a benchmark designed to measure a type of efficiency or capability that humans have but AI models lack. Created by the [[ARCPrizeFoundation]], it is intentionally designed to remain unsaturated and expose the gap between human and machine reasoning.

## Key Information
- Designed to represent human-solvable tasks that models struggle with
- Remained unsaturated for several years
- When the o1-style reasoning push arrived (circa 2024-2025), there was a massive leap in capabilities that correlated with the benchmark's design
- ARC-AGI 2 was the long-running version; ARC-AGI 3 launched just weeks before June 2026
- ARC-AGI 3 launched with frontier models scoring under 1%
- Every task is human-solvable to some degree
- Cited by [[VincentChen]] as the exemplar of [[Model Headroom]] in benchmarks
