## Definition

Chuck Robbins is the CEO of Cisco and Chair of the Business Roundtable. He is known for his vision of transforming Cisco into "not just an iconic company, but an iconic and innovative company."

## Key Information

- CEO of Cisco
- Chair of the Business Roundtable
- Vision: wants Cisco to be "not just an iconic company, but an iconic and innovative company"
- Jeetu Patel describes him as deeply confident and knowing what he's good at and not good at, masterful at assembling teams around him
- Jeetu's key lesson from Chuck: "If you don't care about who gets the credit, you just go a lot farther in life"
- Jeetu and Chuck talk 4-5 times a day across the day
