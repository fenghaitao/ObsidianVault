## Definition

CEO of [[Cisco]] and chair of the Business Roundtable; close friend and mentor to [[Jeetu Patel]], who credits Robbins with hiring him and giving him the agency to drive Cisco's AI-first transformation.

## Key Information

- Wants Cisco to be not just an iconic company but an "iconic and innovative" one.
- When press coverage gave Patel outsized credit for a company achievement, Robbins was unbothered — his line: "if you don't care about who gets the credit, you just go a lot farther in life," which Patel cites as core to Robbins's confidence and self-awareness about what he's good/not good at.
- Patel and Robbins speak multiple times a day (starting and ending each day in contact), which Patel attributes to years of established trust.
