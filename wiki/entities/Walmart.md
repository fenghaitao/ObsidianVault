## Definition
Walmart is a multinational retail corporation cited as an example of enterprise AI innovation, specifically for building a social media trend scanner and generative designer to compete with fast-fashion disruptors.

## Key Information
- Built a social media trend scanner combined with a generative designer to identify and respond to fashion trends rapidly
- This AI capability allows Walmart to compete in entirely new ways with fast-fashion disruptors like Shein and Temu
- Example of an enterprise unlocking entirely new capabilities (not just cost cutting) through AI
- Illustrates the principle that AI enables things that were previously economically impossible for large organizations
- Represents the "AI achiever" category — companies achieving 50% higher revenue growth through AI
- Has very aggressive anti-bot systems including click-and-hold captcha challenges — Bright Data's MCP can bypass these by holding the button for 30+ seconds
- Used as a demo target in Bright Data's scraper-building talk: agent scraped Walmart headphones search results while bypassing the "robot or human" verification screen
