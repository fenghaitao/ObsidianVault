## Definition

Metronome is a real-time usage-based billing infrastructure company, presented as a sponsor of this [[Lenny's Podcast]] episode.

## Key Information

- Positioned as turning raw product-usage events into accurate invoices and keeping engineering, finance, and customer-facing billing in sync in real time, for companies running usage-based pricing or enterprise contracts.
- Cited as used by OpenAI and Anthropic, among other fast-growing companies, to run their billing.
