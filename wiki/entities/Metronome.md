## Definition

Metronome is a real-time billing infrastructure platform that turns raw usage events into accurate invoices. Used by OpenAI and Anthropic.

## Key Information

- Sponsor of episodes of Lenny's Podcast
- Provides real-time billing infrastructure for usage-based pricing, enterprise contracts, and AI services
- Used by OpenAI and Anthropic for their billing
