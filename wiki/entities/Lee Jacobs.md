## Definition

Lee Jacobs is a venture capitalist and friend of Lenny Rachitsky who gave him early encouragement to pursue writing.

## Key Information

- Encouraged Lenny to double down on his newsletter when Lenny was unsure about the path
- Pointed out the rare Venn diagram overlap: Lenny enjoyed writing and people valued it — "How often do you enjoy something and people value it?"
- Suggested that there might be a way to make money from it in the future
- This advice was a pivotal moment in Lenny's decision to pursue the newsletter full-time
