## Definition
Manus AI (now Meta Superintelligence) is a company building a general AI agent platform that executes tasks, automates workflows, and extends human reach across multiple interfaces including web, Slack, API, iOS, mail, Microsoft 365, and browser.

## Key Information
- Describes itself as "the action engine that goes beyond answers to execute tasks, automate workflows, extend human reach"
- Launched Manus 1.5 with re-architected models, improved quality, speed, and user satisfaction
- Scales to millions of daily conversations
- Built a general AI agent first, then added web development capabilities on top
- Ships with a full Docker sandbox per chat, enabling arbitrary package installation
- Supports Stripe webhooks, Redis, Chroma embeddings, and any pip-installable package
- Offers connectors for Gmail, Notion, and other platforms out of the box
- Has a 48-hour auto-deletion policy for uploaded files
- Hiring as of late 2025
- Privacy-focused: cannot read user chats unless explicitly shared for debugging; all data housed in the US
