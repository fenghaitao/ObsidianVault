## Definition

The Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF) is the Linux Foundation organization to which Kubernetes was donated to give it independent, democratic governance.

## Key Information

- Created about a year into Kubernetes' life; received the project, logos, trademarks, and legal assets.
- Independence was critical to adoption: partners wouldn't take a dependency on a single company's roadmap, so Kubernetes had to be an industry standard, not a Google standard.
- Governance rules were written in 2016 to prevent any one company from taking control of the community.
