## Definition
Phil Hetzel leads solutions engineering at Braintrust, an agent quality platform. He ensures customers get maximum value from the platform as quickly as possible, giving him broad exposure to the state of the art in evals and agent observability across many organizations.

## Key Information
- Leads solutions engineering at Braintrust (joined ~2025 after being a user first)
- Solutions engineering team ensures customers get maximum value from the platform as quickly as possible
- Previously spent 12 years in consulting and systems implementation
- Worked at KPMG for 4 years
- Worked at Slalom Consulting for 8 years, where he led the global Databricks business unit
- Motivated to join Braintrust after noticing clients were great at building GenAI POCs but none reached production
- Was an adjunct professor before his industry career
- Plays chess (self-described as "very bad at it") and has a dachshund named Pistol Pete
- Argues that GenAI agents do not exclusively belong to data scientists — the ideal approach is cross-functional teams with domain experts, engineers, and data scientists all contributing
