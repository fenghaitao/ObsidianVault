## Definition
Slalom Consulting is a business and technology consulting firm. Phil Hetzel worked there for 8 years, where he led the global Databricks business unit, helping clients with systems implementations including generative AI proofs of concept.

## Key Information
- Phil Hetzel spent 8 years at Slalom Consulting
- He led the global Databricks business unit
- During his tenure, he noticed clients were great at building GenAI POCs but none reached production — this motivated his move to Braintrust
- Part of his career path: KPMG → Slalom Consulting → Braintrust
