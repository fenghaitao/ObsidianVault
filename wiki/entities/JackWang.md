## Definition
Jack Wang is a practitioner at Accenture who works on deploying AI solutions at large enterprises. He co-presented "Most Enterprise Agentic Projects Are Doomed, Here's Why" at the AI Engineer Summit.

## Key Information
- Works with Accenture on enterprise AI deployments
- Shared a firsthand story: an agentic solution took 2 weeks to build but 12 months to reach production due to enterprise governance processes
- Described enterprise AI delivery as like Google Search results being reviewed by three teams with legal sign-off before appearing — with 2-week delays during quarter-end change freezes
- Emphasizes that the IT crowd is not the problem — the organization needs to learn the language of agentic delivery
- Advocates for hypothesis-driven delivery with statistical confidence instead of traditional milestone-based programs
- Argues that agentic delivery requires teams comfortable with ambiguity who can translate statistical numbers into stakeholder confidence
- Co-presented five enterprise tensions: speed, value, delivery, trust, and moat
