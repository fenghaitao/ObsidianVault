## Definition

Sora is [[OpenAI]]'s AI video-generation app; its Android version is cited by [[Alexander Imbiricos]] (Codex product lead) as one of the most striking internal examples of [[Codex]]-driven engineering acceleration.

## Key Information

- The Sora Android app was built to internal-employee readiness in 18 days, then reached full public (GA) launch 10 days later — 28 days total — built by only 2-3 engineers.
- Achieved #1 ranking in the App Store shortly after public launch.
- The team's approach leaned on Codex's strength at porting existing logic across platforms: engineers had Codex examine the existing iOS app, produce implementation plans, and then execute them for the Android build, working from both platforms' code simultaneously.
