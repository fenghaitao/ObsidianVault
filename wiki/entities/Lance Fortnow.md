## Definition

Lance Fortnow is a theoretical computer scientist and author of the book "The Golden Ticket: P, NP, and the Search for the Impossible."

## Key Information

- Ryan Williams recommends the early chapters of Fortnow's The Golden Ticket as a gentle, imagination-first introduction to complexity theory.
- Williams praises the book's central exercise: "trying to imagine what a world would be like if P equaled NP" — an exercise he enjoys and recommends.
