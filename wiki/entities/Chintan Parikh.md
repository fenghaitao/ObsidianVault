## Definition
Chintan Parikh is a product manager for Lite RT at Google AI Edge, Google DeepMind. He presented on accelerating AI on edge devices at the AI Engineering conference.

## Key Information
- Product manager for Lite RT, part of Google AI Edge at Google DeepMind
- Presented at the AI Engineering conference on deploying AI on edge devices
- Demonstrated Gemma 4 Edge models (E2B, E4B), the Lite RT framework, and the Google AI Edge gallery app
- Showcased on-device agent capabilities: function calling, structured JSON output, chain-of-thought thinking mode
- Presented cross-platform deployment coverage: Android, iOS, macOS, Linux, Windows, web, IoT, Raspberry Pi
- Demonstrated a live Raspberry Pi robot running Lite RT LLM on CPU for on-device inferencing
- Covered NPU acceleration partnerships with Qualcomm and MediaTek
