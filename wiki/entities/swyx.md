## Definition
swyx (Shawn Wang) is the organizer of the AI Engineer Summit and a prominent voice in the AI engineering community, known for coining and popularizing key concepts around AI development quality and best practices.

## Key Information
- Organizer of the AI Engineer Summit series, which has evolved through multiple themes: the rise of AI engineers, leadership, model labs, expanding TAM to include AI PMs and AI designers, and curation/coding
- Popularized the term "AI Engineer" which has become the defining job title for the community. Hamel Hussein gave "much props to swyx for popularizing this term" during the 2024 keynote.
- Runs AI News as a side project, described as "the only newsletter that tells you not to read it when there's nothing going on"
- Declared "war on slop" in his keynote, arguing that both humans and AI can produce low-quality content
- Formulated "swyx's Law of Anti-Slop": the amount of taste needed to fight slop is orders of magnitude larger than that needed to produce it
- Advocates using AI to fight slop through better prompting, code maps, computer use, sub-agents, and modularity
- Worked on Semi-Sync Value of Depth framework and Code Maps for scaling codebase understanding
- Chatted with Greg Brockman about modularity as a key design principle
