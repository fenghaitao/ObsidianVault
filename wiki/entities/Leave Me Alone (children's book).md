## Definition

"Leave Me Alone: A Good Inside Story About Deeply Feeling Kids" is Dr. Becky Kennedy's second children's book, releasing February 2026. It focuses on deeply feeling kids — children whose core fear is being "too much."

## Key Information

- Full title: "Leave Me Alone: A Good Inside Story About Deeply Feeling Kids"
- Dr. Becky's second children's book
- Release date: February 2026
- Title comes from what deeply feeling kids often scream when out of control — "Leave me alone!" — which is actually their deepest fear (being left alone), not their wish
- Deeply feeling kids are described as: labeled dramatic, oppositional, "too much"; their core fear is being "too much" and they act it out, getting it confirmed by the world
- These kids don't fear loss of control — they fear being too much
- The book serves as the "connection moment" for a kid to see themselves represented, while the Good Inside app serves as the companion for the parent
- Available for pre-order as of the episode recording
