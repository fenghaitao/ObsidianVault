## Definition

Co-founder and CEO of [[Cloudflare]], cited by [[Eric Ries]] in *Incorruptible* as the origin of the "let's figure it out" leadership response and of Cloudflare's emergent "make a better internet" mission.

## Key Information

- Came out of Harvard Business School reportedly wary of "consulting BS" — mission statements, values statements — and ran Cloudflare for years without a formal mission statement.
- When a junior engineer challenged him ("wouldn't a better internet be an encrypted internet? So why are we charging for SSL?"), rather than shutting the idea down as a distraction from strategy, Prince responded "let's figure it out" — driving the team to make free universal SSL encryption sustainable rather than abandoning the idea or bankrupting the company.
- Eventually agreed to formally adopt "make a better internet" as Cloudflare's mission statement after hearing employees independently describe the company that way; the company's first formally adopted value was "be principled."
