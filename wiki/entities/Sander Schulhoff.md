## Definition

Sander Schulhoff is an AI researcher specializing in adversarial robustness, prompt engineering, and AI red teaming. He is the CEO of HackAPrompt and co-author of the first comprehensive guide on prompt engineering (Learn Prompting).

## Key Information

- Has been doing AI research for approximately seven years, with much of that time focused on prompt engineering and AI red teaming.
- Wrote the first guide on the internet on learn prompting, which led him into AI security.
- Ran the first-ever generative AI red teaming competition, with sponsors including OpenAI, Scale, Hugging Face, and about 10 other AI companies.
- The competition produced the first and largest open-source dataset of prompt injections, and the resulting paper won best theme paper at EMNLP 2023 out of approximately 20,000 submissions.
- The paper and dataset are now used by every frontier lab and most Fortune 500 companies to benchmark their models and improve AI security.
- Runs the MATS (ML Alignment and Theory Scholars) AI research incubator program.
- Teaches the leading course on AI red teaming and AI security (hackai.co on Maven).
- Core thesis: AI guardrails do not work; the AI security industry is selling solutions that don't solve the fundamental problem.
- Known for the phrase "You can patch a bug, but you can't patch a brain."
