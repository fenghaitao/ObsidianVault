## Definition
Northwestern Mutual is a Fortune 100 financial services company specializing in life insurance and wealth management, founded over 160 years ago. The company is characterized by a risk-averse culture rooted in its motto of "generational responsibility."

## Key Information
- Financial services company focused on life insurance and wealth management
- 160+ years old, emphasizing long-term stability and generational responsibility
- Risk-averse culture: selling decades-long commitments to clients requires extreme reliability
- Has significant data, funding, use cases, and access to top talent — making it both a great and challenging place to do Gen AI
- Hosted the GenBI research initiative led by Asaf Bord
- The company's BI team (~10 people) spent roughly 20% of capacity just finding and sharing the right reports with stakeholders
- Runs a parallel semantic layer initiative to model metadata for human data interaction
- Evaluating third-party GenBI solutions like Databricks Genie alongside internal development
