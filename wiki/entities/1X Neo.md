## Definition

Humanoid robot made by 1X, cited by [[Caitlin Kalinowski]] as a good example of safety-conscious design among current humanoid robots.

## Key Information

- Kalinowski cites 1X Neo as having made significant safety considerations in its design, notably "pulling mass inwards," which reduces the impact energy if the robot's arm or body strikes a person — "softer robots is safer."
- Discussed alongside [[Optimus]] and [[Figure]] as one of a small number of companies "very ahead" in humanoid robotics, though Kalinowski views all current humanoids as advanced prototypes, not yet ready for at-scale (hundreds of thousands to millions of units) deployment.
