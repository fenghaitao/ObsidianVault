## Definition
Decagon is an AI company and Modal customer that has articulated a key insight about fine-tuning: frontier labs optimize their models to win on everything, while companies should optimize their models to win at their specific business logic.

## Key Information
- Mentioned by Benjamin Cowen as a Modal customer with a clear perspective on model specialization
- Core philosophy: frontier labs don't share your exact goals — they want their models to perform well across all benchmarks, but you want your model to excel at your specific product offering
- Exemplifies the business case for moving from frontier APIs to fine-tuned, domain-specific models
