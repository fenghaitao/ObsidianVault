## Definition

Decagon is a company whose Director of Product, Bihan Jiang, described an AI-accelerated product workflow built around Claude tools.

## Key Information

- Bihan Jiang, Director of Product: workflow starts in [[ClaudeCowork|Claude Cowork]] pulling context from Slack, the codebase, and docs, then moves into [[ClaudeCode|Claude Code]] to produce a demo-able prototype in a couple of hours — versus weeks previously.
- Quote: "Claude has raised the ceiling on what good product teams can build, and dramatically shortened the distance between idea and prototype... What has [changed] is how many more high-quality ideas we can actually put through the loop."
