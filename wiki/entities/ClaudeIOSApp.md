# Claude iOS App

The Claude iOS App is a free mobile application from [[Anthropic]] that brings Claude's AI capabilities to iPhone and iPad users, enabling access to Claude from anywhere at any time.

## Key Information

- **Launch date:** May 2024
- **Cost:** Free to download
- **Platform:** iOS (Apple iPhone and iPad)
- **Distribution:** [[AppleAppStore]]
- **Creator:** [[Anthropic]]
- **Positioning:** "Frontier intelligence in your back pocket"

## Features

### Primary Use Cases
- **Brainstorming:** Ideation and creative thinking on the go
- **Quick answers:** Fast responses to questions and information requests
- **Image analysis:** Analyzing scenes and images from the real world using the device camera

### Capabilities
- Full access to Claude's frontier AI model capabilities in mobile form
- Offline-friendly design for users on the move
- Integration with device camera for image capture and analysis

## Strategic Significance

The iOS app represents [[Anthropic]]'s strategy to expand Claude's accessibility beyond desktop:
1. **Platform diversification:** Reaching users where they are (mobile devices)
2. **Use case expansion:** Enabling new on-the-go workflows not possible on desktop alone
3. **Market reach:** Expanding Claude's user base through the app store distribution channel
