## Definition
Nano Banana Pro is a product launched by Google DeepMind in early January 2026, released alongside Gemini 3 during the same week as the 2025 AI Engineering Code Summit. It is referenced as an image generation model that can be chained with other models via the Interactions API's content blocks.

## Key Information
- Launched by Google DeepMind in early January 2026
- Released the same week as Gemini 3 and the AI Engineering Code Summit
- Announced by Jed Borovik during his opening remarks at the Summit
- Can be used via the Interactions API to generate images, chainable with other models (e.g., Flash for text, Lyra for audio)
