## Definition

Max Levchin is a co-founder of PayPal and Affirm, and a key member of the PayPal Mafia. He was responsible for hiring all technical talent at PayPal and is cited by Keith Rabois as an example of an engineer with "commercial instincts on steroids."

## Key Information

- Co-founder of [[PayPal]] and Affirm
- Hired all the technical talent at PayPal
- Together with [[Peter Thiel]], marshaled an incredible density of talent that made PayPal successful
- Described by [[Keith Rabois]] as having "commercial instincts on steroids" — an example of the "ultimate unicorn": an engineer who is also very business-minded
- Keith cites Max as one of the best engineers he's worked with who also had commercial instincts
- Part of the PayPal Mafia that spawned 25+ years of successful companies
