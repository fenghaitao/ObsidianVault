## Definition

Co-founder of [[PayPal]], responsible for hiring nearly all of the company's technical talent — largely through his personal network — contributing to the unusually high "barrel" density [[Keith Rabois]] credits for the "PayPal Mafia" phenomenon.

## Key Information

- Cited by Rabois as an example of an engineer with strong commercial instincts "on steroids," alongside [[Jeremy Stoppelman]].
