## Definition

Tesla is an electric vehicle and clean energy company known for its self-driving software. Alexander Imbiricos cites Tesla's self-driving UX as a master class in mixed initiative systems.

## Key Information

- Alexander Imbiricos recently bought a Tesla after previously only owning old sports cars
- He finds Tesla's self-driving software "quite inspiring" as an example of mixed initiative design
- The self-driving feature allows the human to adjust without turning off autonomy: you can accelerate, turn a knob to change speed, or steer slightly, and the car listens
- He calls it "a master class in building an agent that still leaves the human in control"
- This aligns with Nick Turley's mantra of "are we maximally accelerated?" at OpenAI
- **Tesla/FSD approach (L2++):** Fewer sensors, no HD maps, cheaper compute. Available everywhere. "L2 plus plus"
- [[Lenny Rachitsky]] uses Tesla FSD all the time — says it got very good recently
- Contrast with [[Waymo]]'s L4 approach: many sensors, HD maps, geo-fenced
- Every car company on the planet is now working on a product like Tesla FSD
- In 5-7 years, L2++ will become near-free and ubiquitous, like nav systems became free with CarPlay/Android Auto
