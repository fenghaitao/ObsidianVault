## Definition

Claude Co-work is Anthropic's agentic AI product that lets Claude act on behalf of users — using tools, browsing the web, and performing tasks. Jenny Wen leads design for Co-work. It was labeled a "research preview" at launch to set expectations, and was built on the principle of shipping early and iterating rapidly based on user feedback.

## Key Information

- Jenny Wen leads design for Claude Co-work
- Labeled a "research preview" at launch — acknowledging it's early and will improve
- Built on the principle of shipping early and iterating based on user feedback
- Was not built in 10 days from scratch — there were many internal explorations and prototypes before the final 10-day build sprint
- Jenny's favorite use case: introspection — analyzing personal notes, interview notes, and memos to extract insights
- Example: Jenny fed it interview notes and design craft notes to create a rubric for evaluating design craft in interviews
- Jenny describes it as "Claude with hands" or "Claude that's really good at taking all your garbage and turning it into something nice"
- Features include: to-do lists, multiple choice questions, teaching users use cases
- The homepage is being iterated to feel like a shared to-do list between user and Claude
- Jenny has shifted all her Claude chat use cases over to Co-work

- Jacob Warwick uses Claude Co-work daily and names it as his favorite product

### Amol Avaisary's Usage
- Amol uses Co-work extensively for daily automations at Anthropic
- **Data monitoring**: Co-work runs on a schedule, looks at 20-25 hex charts every morning, and flags concerning trends and interesting insights
- **Life admin**: Co-work books meeting rooms, clears inbox (first pass), files reimbursements and expenses via Brex
- **Manager lens**: Co-work looks at direct reports' work, OKRs, discussion transcripts, and suggests key takeaways and feedback
- **Self-coaching**: Co-work models Amol's manager (Amie Vora) and provides weekly feedback from her perspective
- **Misalignment detection**: Co-work scans Slack weekly to find areas of potential misalignment across projects — "does a really, really good job"
- Amol describes the quality as "hit or miss right now" — decent on some things, occasionally catches something crucial
- The Chrome extension that underpins Co-work use cases was built by the growth team
