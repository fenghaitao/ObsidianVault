## Definition
Hex is a collaborative data science and analytics platform. In the keynote, it is cited by Bryan Bischof (who identifies as being from Hex) as an example of a company shipping LLM-powered features in beta backed by evals, demonstrating the approach of getting products in front of users to collect real interaction data.

## Key Information
- Bryan Bischof identified himself as being from Hex during the keynote
- Cited as an example of shipping AI features in beta with evals, alongside Apple Intelligence and Adobe Photoshop
- Represents the strategy of releasing imperfect but working LLM features to begin collecting binary human feedback
