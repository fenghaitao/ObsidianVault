## Definition
Rohan Aneja is Vlad Feinberg's former manager at Google Brain who encouraged him to join the first Bard launch instead of focusing only on papers.
## Key Information
- He pushed the team to get involved in the Gemini program, giving Vlad the motivation to "roll up sleeves" on hyperparameter tuning and engineering work.
- That small engagement was recognized by Jeff Dean and, Vlad says, blossomed into his later LLM investment and current role.
