# LexisNexis

LexisNexis Legal & Professional is a leading global provider of information and analytics, serving customers in more than 150 countries across law firms, corporate, government, and academic markets. It is committed to responsibly developing AI solutions with human oversight.

## Key Information

- Type: Information and analytics provider
- Market: Legal, corporate, government, academic — 150+ countries
- AI approach: Responsible development with human oversight
- CTO: Jeff Reihl (Executive Vice President & Chief Technology Officer)

## AI Adoption

In 2023, LexisNexis selected [[Claude2]] on [[AmazonBedrock]] as part of its AI strategy to power legal AI capabilities. Key selection criteria:

- **Long context windows** for processing detailed legal documents
- **High-quality technical analysis**
- **Data security** — importance of partner data security and best-in-class LLM training
- **Fast outputs** for legal workflows

*"It's important to understand AI's capabilities and limitations, and to work with partners that deliver data security and provide best-in-class training of large language models."* — Jeff Reihl, EVP & CTO
