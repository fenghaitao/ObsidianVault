## Definition

Yaron Minsky is a computer scientist and Jane Street leader who, as a PhD student on Cornell's Ensemble project, was among OCaml's early systems users and later built Jane Street's trading infrastructure in OCaml.

## Key Information

- Was a PhD student working on the Ensemble project (late 1990s reliable-multicast network stack at Cornell) when it rewrote its C code in OCaml.
- Went on to Jane Street, bringing OCaml with him and implementing its trading infrastructure in the language.
- Jane Street values OCaml for automatic trading because it must be fast, reliable, free of long GC pauses, and readable by non-programmers (financial engineers and quantitative analysts).
- Jane Street continues to develop OCaml tooling, including experiments like "Oxidized OCaml" (stack allocation inspired by Rust).
