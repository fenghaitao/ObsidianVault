## Definition

Google Analytics is Google's web analytics product. It absorbed Ilya Grigorik's PostRank team after Google acquired the company.

## Key Information

- The acquisition began with a conference conversation led by Google Analytics lead PM Phil Moody
- Google wanted PostRank's talent and infrastructure to "speedrun" social analytics and show how Google+ was driving engagement
- Grigorik joined as an engineering manager on Google Analytics, and the team rebuilt its stack from scratch to operate at Google's order of magnitude ("more than half the internet")
