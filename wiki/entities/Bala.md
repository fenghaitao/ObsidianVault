## Definition

Bala is Simon's long-time engineering manager at Meta, credited with guiding Simon from IC3 to IC6 across two teams and a London relocation. He later transitioned into a product manager role.

## Key Information

- Simon's first and only manager for roughly 5.5 years, even as the team switched responsibilities and relocated from California to London
- Was recently converted from IC6 to manager when he met Simon, so he offered deep technical mentorship plus product sense
- Lured Simon onto the ads payments team by asking "what's more impactful than revenue?"
- Drew a whiteboard plan of Simon's proposed IC3→IC5 trajectory and delivered it
- Known for strong roadmapping, upward/downward communication, and an unusual product instinct (he later switched to PM)
- Celebrated each report's promotion by taking them out to dinner, announced via a mysterious lobby meetup
