## Definition
Talos represents the extreme end of hardware specialization — chips built specifically for a particular model architecture, as opposed to general-purpose GPUs like Nvidia RTX or H100.

## Key Information
- Positioned at the extreme end of the hardware specialization spectrum: general-purpose GPUs → Cerebras/Groq → Talos (model-specific chips)
- Currently not economically viable because frontier models change too quickly (every ~3 months)
- May become viable if model architectures stabilize or if diminishing returns on intelligence mean specific models become "good enough" for long-term deployment
- Alex Cheema notes the math doesn't work today — by the time a specialized chip is built, the frontier has moved
- Could become practical in a future where 20 specialized smaller models replace one large monolithic model
