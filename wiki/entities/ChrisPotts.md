## Definition
Chris Potts is a researcher who has compared DSPy optimizers (Jeepa) to fine-tuning methods like GRPO, finding them competitive or superior in certain scenarios.

## Key Information
- Presented research showing DSPy's Jeepa optimizer is on par with or exceeds the performance of GRPO, a fine-tuning method.
- Active in the prompt optimization research community alongside Omar Khattab.
- His work supports the argument that prompt optimization is a viable alternative to fine-tuning for improving model performance on specific tasks.
