## Definition

Microsoft is a multinational technology company and one of the world's largest enterprise software and cloud providers. In the AI space, Microsoft has integrated large language models across its product suite under the Copilot brand.

## Key Information

- Creator and operator of [[Microsoft365Copilot]], the enterprise AI productivity platform.
- Creator of [[CopilotStudio]], the low-code agent-building platform.
- As of September 2025, partnered with [[Anthropic]] to make [[Claude4Sonnet]] (Claude Sonnet 4) and [[Claude4Opus]] (Claude Opus 4.1) available as model options in Microsoft 365 Copilot.
- Runs the **Frontier Program** — an opt-in early-access program for Microsoft 365 Copilot licensed customers to access new AI capabilities.
- Microsoft 365 admin center is the enterprise administration hub for enabling third-party model access (e.g., Anthropic models).
