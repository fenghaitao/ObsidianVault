## Definition

Midjourney is an AI company focused on image generation. It is an early customer of [[ClaudeEnterprise]], using Claude as a virtual collaborator across research and operations workflows.

## Claude Usage at Midjourney

As noted by Caleb Kruse (Chief of Staff):
- Summarizing research papers
- Conducting Q&A with user feedback notes
- Iterating on moderation policies
- Acts as a collaborator to support company growth and exploration of new domains

## Key Quote

"Claude has been an incredible virtual collaborator for Midjourney. We use Claude for everything from summarizing research papers, to doing Q&A with user feedback notes, to iterating on our moderation policies. We're excited to keep working alongside Claude as we grow and explore new domains." — Caleb Kruse, Chief of Staff, Midjourney
