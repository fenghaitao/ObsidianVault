## Definition

AP Computer Science is an Advanced Placement course offered in some US high schools. It was Evan King's first exposure to computer science, though he noted it was not universally available.

## Key Information

- Evan King took AP Computer Science in high school, which he acknowledged was "certainly early" compared to most students
- Despite taking it early, he felt "late" because most students in his Seattle-area class had parents at Microsoft and had been programming since they were very young
- Evan struggled in the class initially — he "knew nothing" and found it "incredibly complex," which was stressful for a straight-A student
- Ryan Peterman's high school in Orange County did not offer AP Computer Science at all
- The contrast between their experiences shows that AP CS availability varied significantly by region and that early exposure was not necessary for career success
