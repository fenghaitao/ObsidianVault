## Definition
GitHub Next is GitHub's experimental research team that works on more speculative, high-risk projects than the rest of the organization. The Labs team within GitHub Next describes itself as "the Department of Fuck Around and Find Out." GitHub Next is responsible for prototyping the future of software development, including ACE (Agent Collaboration Environment).

## Key Information
- GitHub's experimental research and prototyping team
- Works on riskier, more speculative bets than the rest of GitHub
- Labs team within GitHub Next is self-described as "the Department of Fuck Around and Find Out"
- Building ACE (Agent Collaboration Environment), a multiplayer agent coding environment
- Many people internally at GitHub Next believe the PR and the issue are not the future of software development
- Exploring what comes after current development paradigms
- Website: githubnext.com
- Maggie Appleton is a staff research engineer on the Labs team
