## Definition
Silicon Valley is Mike Judge's HBO comedy about a fictional startup, notable for its creepily accurate portrayal of the tech industry. James Cowling served as a technical consultant from season 2 onward.
## Key Information
- Cowling had nothing to do with season 1; after it was a hit, the writers needed a compression/storage expert to figure out where the story could go, which drew in Cowling.
- The writers cared far more about technical accuracy than viewers would expect — down to what a data-center rack or wall diagram should look like.
- Many of the show's stories were collected from real companies and woven into the series; its characters are based on real people and archetypes, which is why it can feel "creepily accurate" and hard to watch for people in the industry.
- Cowling was paid $400 for the consulting work (a Hollywood union requirement); he initially didn't want payment because it complicated his visa.
