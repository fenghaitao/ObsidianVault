## Definition

HBO tech-satire series named by [[Aishwarya Naresh Reganti]] as a favorite recent rewatch, which she finds "eerily similar to everything that's happening right now with the AI wave."

## Key Information

- Reganti recommends anyone who watched it years ago rewatch it now, describing the show's cycles ("everything is repeating all over again") as timeless.
- Lenny notes the show's fictional startup was built around a compression algorithm, joking it's "maybe a precursor to LLMs in some small way."
