## Definition

Jack Dorsey is the co-founder and former CEO of Twitter and Square (now Block). He inspired Keith Rabois to switch to iPad-only computing in 2010.

## Key Information

- Co-founder and former CEO of [[Square]] (now [[Block]]) and Twitter
- Was running Square off an iPad when [[Keith Rabois]] joined in September 2010
- Keith immediately converted to iPad-only computing and hasn't used a computer since September 2010
- Everything Keith does is done from phone, watch, or iPad
- Referenced in the context of reference checking — when VCs called Jack for a reference on [[Max Rhodes]] (Fair), they asked the wrong question ("Was Max a good employee?" instead of "Is Max capable of being a world-class entrepreneur?")
