## Definition

Land Man is a TV show about the oil and energy industry that Sam Lessin recommends as his favorite recent show. It has become so popular among Silicon Valley types that a founder in Midland, Texas noted that "all these Silicon Valley people now think they understand the energy industry because they watched Land Man."
