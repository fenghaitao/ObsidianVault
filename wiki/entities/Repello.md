## Definition

Repello is an AI security company that offers automated red teaming and guardrails, along with a notable product that discovers what AI systems are running within a company.

## Key Information

- Sander Schulhoff was initially skeptical because Repello offered automated red teaming and guardrails, which he considers ineffective.
- However, Schulhoff highlighted a Repello product that scans a company's internal systems to discover what AI systems are actually running — often revealing many more than the CISO is aware of.
- Schulhoff found this "shadow AI" discovery product genuinely useful, as companies often deploy AI systems that get forgotten about but continue running.
- Represents the segment of AI security companies that are expanding beyond guardrails into more practical security tools.
