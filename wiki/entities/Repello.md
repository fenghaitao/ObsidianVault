## Definition

Repello is an AI security company cited by [[Sander Schulhoff]] as having shifted from purely guardrails/automated red-teaming (which he was "not particularly pleased to see") toward more genuinely useful products.

## Key Information

- Highlighted product: an AI-discovery tool that audits a company's actual AI footprint — e.g., telling a CISO who believes they run "three chatbots" that they actually have 16 chatbots and five other AI systems running, often forgotten deployments still burning compute/credits.
- Schulhoff frames this kind of AI-asset-discovery/governance tooling as a genuinely valuable niche within the broader AI security market, distinct from (and more useful than) guardrails or automated red-teaming.
