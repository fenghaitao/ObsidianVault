## Definition
Aria is a UK research institute partnering with Callosum on a £3M grant to operate the first heterogeneous collocated cluster in the United Kingdom, advancing the co-evolution of hardware and AI workloads.

## Key Information
- UK-based research institute
- Partnering with [[Callosum]] on a £3M grant
- Operating the first heterogeneous collocated cluster in the UK
- Advancing the heterogeneous intelligence paradigm through hardware-software co-evolution
