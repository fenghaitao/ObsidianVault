## Definition

Taylor Francis was an intern at Square who proved he was a "barrel" by solving the smoothie problem that the entire office team couldn't. Keith Rabois later gave him almost everything to do.

## Key Information

- Intern at [[Square]] during [[Keith Rabois]]'s tenure
- On his second day of work, he solved the "smoothie test": delivering healthy, delicious, cold smoothies at 9:00 PM to engineers
- The office team and EAs had repeatedly failed at this task — smoothies weren't cold, didn't arrive at 9:00 PM, etc.
- Taylor said "I'll solve it" and within a day, cold smoothies arrived at 9:00 PM on the standing desk table where engineers congregated
- Keith's reaction: "Oh my god, I found a barrel" — and later gave him almost everything to do
- This story is the canonical example of the [[Barrels and Ammunition]] framework from Keith's YC lecture in 2014
