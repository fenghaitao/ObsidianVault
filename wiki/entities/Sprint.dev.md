## Definition

Sprint.dev is a hackathon platform co-founded by Jia Chen and Aiden Goolan that helps students and developers of any age enter hackathons. It provides free tools, API credits, and Discord support to make hackathons more accessible.

## Key Information

- Co-founded by Jia Chen and Aiden Goolan
- Started when Jia shipped an MVP overnight (9:00 PM to 9:00 AM), posted it on Instagram, and it crashed from too much traffic
- The idea came from all the comments Jia received on Instagram about hackathons — she wanted to help people learn how to get into hackathons
- Raised angel investment at a Luma hot pot event in San Francisco, where an angel investor was intrigued by their story and product-market fit
- A second angel investor overheard at the same table and also put in a check
- Jia posted a video about receiving the angel checks, which got 500K views on Instagram, leading to more angel checks
- Landed seven Y Combinator companies and 50 other companies as clients
- Reached five figures in monthly recurring revenue (MRR)
- Provides API credits and Discord support to help newcomers feel less afraid to attend hackathons
- Looking for "best builders" defined by creativity, execution, collaboration, and shipping things people love — not by technical ranking
