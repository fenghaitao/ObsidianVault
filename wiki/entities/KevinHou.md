## Definition
Kevin Hou leads the product engineering team at Codeium and presented at aiDotEngineer on how embedding-based retrieval is hitting a ceiling for AI code generation. He introduced M-Query, Codeium's vertically integrated retrieval system that runs thousands of parallel LLM calls over codebase items to achieve best-in-class context retrieval.

## Key Information
- Leads product engineering at Codeium
- Presented "How Codeium Breaks Through the Ceiling for Retrieval" at aiDotEngineer (July 2024)
- Previously worked in the autonomous driving industry before joining Codeium
- Drove the product-led evaluation approach at Codeium: building eval datasets from real PR commit messages mapped to modified files
- Advocates for product-driven (not research-driven) development and using production signals like thumbs up/down to measure AI feature quality
- Argues that embedding-based retrieval has plateaued and that throwing more compute via vertical integration is the path forward
