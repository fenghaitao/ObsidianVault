## Definition

The Browser Company of New York is a startup founded in 2019 with the mission to rethink how people use the internet by building a browser that reflects modern internet usage patterns. They are the creators of Arc (2022) and Dia (2025), an AI-native browser.

## Key Information

- Founded in 2019 by Josh Miller (CEO)
- Shipped Arc in 2022, a browser focused on personalization, organization, and craft
- Shipped Dia in early 2025, an AI-native browser with an assistant that works alongside users
- Core philosophy: build tools, process, platform, and mindset to iterate faster than everyone else
- Invests heavily in internal AI tooling built directly into their own product
- Created a dedicated "model behavior" team to shape LLM personality and behavior
