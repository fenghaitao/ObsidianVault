## Definition

Reed Hoffman is the co-founder of LinkedIn and a prominent venture capitalist and thought leader in technology and social networks.

## Key Information

- Tomer Cohen first learned about LinkedIn deeply when attending a lecture at Stanford in 2008 where Reed Hoffman spoke about the power of professional communities online.
- Hoffman's vision of professional communities online inspired Cohen and ultimately led to him joining LinkedIn.
- Cohen described Hoffman's vision as "incredible" and aligned with his own purpose.
- Hoffman coined the term "late-joining co-founder" to describe Jeff Weiner, who joined LinkedIn four years after founding but had such transformative impact that he was effectively a co-founder. Jason Cohen uses the same term for Heather Brunner at WP Engine.
