## Definition

President of [[Y Combinator]] (referred to as "Gary Tan"/"Gary's Tang" in the transcript). Cited by [[Sam Lesson]] as reacting with visible annoyance to Lesson's YC-founder etiquette class, which in turn motivated Lesson to formalize it further.

## Key Information

- Per Lesson: "YC Gary's Tang got very mad about this. I'm like, 'Well, now I have to do it, right?'" — Tan's irritation is described as one of the catalysts that pushed the etiquette class from a one-off into an ongoing project (eventually a book).
- Lesson says he doesn't fully understand the source of Tan's reaction ("I don't really understand what makes Gary Tan mad and it's fine") but speculates it may reflect a view that etiquette coaching is the wrong thing for YC founders to focus on relative to building.
- Lesson frames the class itself as gently "tongue-in-cheek," poking fun at the fact that YC founders — having been "holed up in a room coding" for months — often show up unaware of basic social/etiquette norms (e.g., leaving a coffee cup on a desk without asking where to put it).
