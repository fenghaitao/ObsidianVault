## Definition
The Pragmatic Engineer is the number one paid technology newsletter for 3 years, founded by Gergely Orosz after leaving Uber. It covers software engineering in depth, with insider stories from Big Tech and startups, anonymized from real incidents and examples.

## Key Information
- Founded by Gergely Orosz about a year after leaving Uber (during COVID)
- First article was about Uber's platform/product split — never written about publicly before
- Felt product-market fit immediately: 100 paid subscribers ($100/year) before publishing anything; 1,000 in 6 weeks
- Number one paid technology newsletter for 3 years
- Eventually overtaken by SemiAnalysis
- Strategy: said no to all interviews, collaborations, podcasts for 2 years — focused entirely on writing
- Started a podcast about 1.5 years ago
- Grew the team with researchers (Ellen, Jessica)
- Known for anonymizing insider stories from real incidents at major tech companies
