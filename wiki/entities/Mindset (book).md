## Definition

Book by Carol Dweck, who coined the term "[[Growth Mindset]]," named by [[Zevi Arnovitz]] as a life-changing lightning-round recommendation.

## Key Information

- Zevi: "it kind of sounds like a self-help book, but then you understand that it's completely psychological and is based on research" — credits it with shifting him from what he describes as a fixed mindset toward deliberately cultivating a growth mindset.
