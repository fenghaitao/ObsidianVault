## Definition

"Mindset: The New Psychology of Success" is a book by psychologist Carol Dweck that introduced the concept of growth mindset vs. fixed mindset. Zevi Arnovitz says it completely changed his life.

## Key Information

- Written by Carol Dweck, psychologist
- Introduces the concepts of growth mindset and fixed mindset
- Zevi: "That book completely changed my life"
- Zevi notes it may sound like a self-help book but is actually based on psychological research
- Connects to Zevi's philosophy of being a "10x learner" rather than a "10x PM"
