## Definition

Campfire is a modern accounting software provider using Claude to accelerate accounting teams' monthly close process.

## Key Information

- Cited as an example of a document-processing capability (bank reconciliation) that can be built once and reused across departments — the same system also handles invoice processing.
- Cuts 3 days off the monthly close timeline, with 90% less time spent on bank reconciliation and 50% faster reporting through its Ember AI chat interface.
