## Definition
Madison Faulkner is a Partner at NEA (New Enterprise Associates) investing in infrastructure and developer tools. She was formerly a Meta AI researcher who led data and AI teams before moving into venture capital to address infrastructure challenges from the investment side.

## Key Information
- Partner at [[NEA]], focused on infrastructure and developer tools investments
- Formerly a Meta AI researcher who led data and AI teams
- Transitioned to venture capital out of frustration with the state of infrastructure, aiming to address it "from the top down"
- Presented at AIE Code Summit 2026 on why CI/CD is dead and the emergence of Continuous Compute
- Framed the agentic software evolution as moving from monolithic agents to microservices with agents
- Highlighted GitHub activity data showing unprecedented spikes in commits and code volume from agents
- Described the fragmented agentic software lifecycle spanning new IDEs, autonomous engineering solutions, and traditional DevOps
- Referenced Mitchell Hashimoto's vision for fixing GitHub by evolving it for AI-first users
- Advocated for hardware-software co-design with caching as the orchestration layer
