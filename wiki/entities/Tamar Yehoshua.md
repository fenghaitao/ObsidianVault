## Definition

Tamar Yehoshua is a product leader who succeeded April Underwood as CPO of Slack. She is now CPO and Head of AI at Atlassian. Jessica Fain served as her Chief of Staff at Slack.

## Key Information

- Succeeded April Underwood as CPO of Slack
