## Definition

Succeeded [[April Underwood]] as CPO of [[Slack]]; [[Jessica Fain]] served as her chief of staff. A prior [[Lenny's Podcast]] guest, now CPO and Head of AI at Atlassian.

## Key Information

- Cited by Fain for a formative moment of frustration: after asking the same question ("I want to see the top 10 use cases on X") in four separate meetings without it ever being documented, she pointed out the team wasn't picking up on how much she cared about that request — an example of failing to "follow the thread" of subtle executive feedback.
