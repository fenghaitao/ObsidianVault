## Definition

Medium is a blogging and publishing platform where Lenny Rachitsky first began writing online. His first post about what he learned at Airbnb was featured by Medium and widely shared.

## Key Information

- Lenny's first post on Medium was about what he learned at Airbnb
- Medium featured the post, giving it wide distribution
- Brian Chesky shared it with the entire Airbnb company
- Lenny wrote several more posts on Medium before eventually moving to Substack for his weekly newsletter
