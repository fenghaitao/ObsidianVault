## Definition
Peter Steinberger is the creator of OpenClaw, the fastest-growing open-source project in GitHub history. He joined OpenAI to work on bringing agents to everyone while also founding the Open Claw Foundation as a vendor-neutral home for the project.

## Key Information
- Creator of OpenClaw, which reached ~30K commits, ~2K contributors, and ~30K PRs in its first 5 months
- Joined OpenAI to work on agent technology; part of his role is "to help them not make the same mistakes again" that were learned at OpenClaw
- Founded the Open Claw Foundation, inspired by Ghosty's model, to ensure OpenClaw remains vendor-neutral
- Describes running the foundation as "like running a company on hard mode" — all the responsibilities with volunteers you can't direct
- At peak coding workflow: running ~10 agent sessions simultaneously; now ~5-6 as loops got faster
- Famous for a viral photo showing his multi-window agent workflow and massive token spending at OpenAI
- Emphasizes "taste" as the key skill in AI-assisted development — the ability to distinguish good output from AI-generated slop
- Created the soul.md concept for agent personality after noticing Claude Code's default personality didn't fit WhatsApp conversations
- Advocates for iterative development over fully automated "dark factory" approaches — "the way to the mountain is usually never a straight line"
- Vision: ubiquitous agents that follow you everywhere (home, work, glasses, earbuds) like Star Trek's computer
- Believes OpenClaw "would have never been able to come out of an American company" — would have been killed in legal review
- On building OpenClaw initially: "What's the worst that can happen? It could exfiltrate my token, my emails... I can live with that risk"
- Key skills for engineers in AI age: taste, system design, saying no, big picture thinking
- Instagram account described by swyx as "underfollowed" with "good stuff"
- **Malte Ubl's perspective**: Cited Peter Steinberger and OpenClaw as evidence of Europe's leadership in AI engineering innovation. Peter was on stage at the same conference an hour after Malte's keynote.
