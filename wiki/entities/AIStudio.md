## Definition
AI Studio is Google's AI prototyping platform offering cloud-hosted access to Gemma 4's larger models (31B and 26B) for immediate development, agentic workflow building, and function calling testing.

## Key Information
- Cloud-hosted option for Gemma 4's 31B and 26B models
- Enables immediate prototyping without self-hosting infrastructure
- Supports building agentic workflows and testing function calling
