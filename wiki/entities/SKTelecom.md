# SK Telecom

SK Telecom is one of the largest telecommunications operators in South Korea. The company has adopted fine-tuned Claude models to improve customer support operations and internal processes.

## Claude Adoption

SK Telecom trained a custom Claude model for customer support workflows using [[AmazonBedrock]]'s fine-tuning capability for [[Claude3Haiku]].

### Results

- **Agent Feedback**: 73% increase in positive feedback for support agents' responses
- **Task Performance**: 37% improvement in key performance indicators (KPIs) for telecommunications-related tasks
- **Automation**: The fine-tuned model efficiently generates:
  - Topics from customer interactions
  - Action items for follow-up
  - Summaries from customer call logs
  - Breakdown of complex customer issues into manageable steps

## Strategic Impact

By embedding a fine-tuned Claude model in customer support operations, SK Telecom measurably improved internal processes and overall customer satisfaction.
