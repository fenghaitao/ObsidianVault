## Definition

iGent AI is a London-based AI company advancing autonomous software development through Maestro, an AI partner that transforms natural language conversations into executable code.

## Key Information

- Headquartered in London, UK.
- CEO and Co-founder: Sean Ward.
- Product: **Maestro** — a software engineering agent capable of multi-day sessions on real-world codebases.
- Maestro uses [[Claude4Sonnet]] with 1M token [[ContextWindow]] to enable "true production-scale engineering."
- The 1M context window unlocked autonomous, multi-day coding sessions — described by Sean Ward as "a new paradigm in agentic software engineering."
- iGent AI frames this as "supercharged autonomous capabilities" for Maestro.
