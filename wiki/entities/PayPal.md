## Definition

PayPal is an online payment system founded in 1998 that became one of the most talent-dense companies in Silicon Valley history, spawning the "PayPal Mafia" of founders and investors.

## Key Information

- Had 254 people in Mountain View at acquisition, with 12-17 "barrels" (Keith Rabois framework)
- Peter Thiel and Max Levchin "marshaled an incredible density of talent"
- Hiring was primarily through first or second-degree connections to the engineering team or Peter Thiel through Stanford
- Keith Rabois was EVP of Business Development and Policy
- Rabois learned talent identification by "stealing" promising people from other teams within PayPal
- David Sacks was COO and gave Rabois feedback about leadership leverage (1+1 must equal 3+)
- The talent density at PayPal enabled the creation of dozens of subsequent successful companies
