## Definition
Mauro Luchetti is the AI Center of Excellence Manager at Quantyca. He presented the technical architecture of Amplifon's enterprise registry system at the aiDotEngineer conference, covering the AI Gateway and the three-registry design.

## Key Information
- **Role**: AI Center of Excellence Manager at [[Quantyca]]
- **Presentation**: "One Registry to Rule them All" at [[aiDotEngineer]], co-presented with Sonny Merla and Mattia Redaelli
- **Key Technical Topics**: AI Gateway architecture (unified LLM access, Entra ID authentication, budgeting), MCP Registry (private enterprise extension with enterprise metadata), A2A Registry (agent card standard, CICD auto-publishing), Use Case Registry (connecting agents, tools, and models to business use cases)
- **Enterprise Metadata Framework**: Defined five enterprise metadata dimensions for registry enrichment -- ownership, environment, authentication model, cost attribution, and use case linkage
