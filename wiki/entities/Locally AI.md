## Definition

Locally AI is a fully native iOS chatbot app that allows users to run on-device language models on iPhone using MLX. It is also available on iPad and macOS. The app was acquired by LM Studio.

## Key Information

- Fully native iOS chatbot for running on-device models with MLX
- Available on iPhone, iPad, and macOS
- Supports Apple Foundation models and many MLX-compatible models including Gemma 4, Qwen, and small LM models from Hugging Face
- Can run models as small as 350M parameters (Liquid models) for shortcuts and automation
- Free to use on the App Store; users download models (typically 1-3 GB)
- Curated model selection ensures only models that work correctly on iPhone are available
- Acquired by LM Studio (announced at the talk)
