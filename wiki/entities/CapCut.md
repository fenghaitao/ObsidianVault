## Definition
CapCut is a video editing application available on mobile and desktop. Joe Reeve used the mobile version to edit the viral statue app video in approximately 25 minutes.

## Key Information
- Mobile video editing app used by Joe Reeve for the statue app viral video
- The statue video was edited entirely on a phone in ~25 minutes
- Joe Reeve finds the desktop version "three times harder" to use than mobile
- Desktop subscription is also "three times more expensive" than mobile
- Used with a DJI Bluetooth lapel mic for better audio quality
- Joe Reeve's editing process: add captions, front-load the hook, add music (often generated with ElevenLabs music generation)
- Represents the "80/20 rule" of video editing — getting to a good standard is relatively easy, but going beyond requires desktop tools
