## Definition

Pioneering management theorist, author of *The Principles of Scientific Management* (1911), referenced by [[Eric Ries]] in *Incorruptible* as a contrast to his lesser-known but far more prescient contemporary, [[Mary Parker Follett]].

## Key Information

- Taylorism was, per Ries, one of the most popular management fads of all time — debated at the U.S. Supreme Court, front-page news, and the subject of movies about him.
- Introduced by Ries mainly to set up the point that his contemporary Mary Parker Follett, writing at the same time, produced work far more prescient and durable than Taylor's, yet was erased from history for most of the 20th century.
