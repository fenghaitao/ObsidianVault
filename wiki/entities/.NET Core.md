## Definition

.NET Core is the cross-platform, modern, open-source rewrite of .NET that David Fowler co-architected at Microsoft. It was the project that drove his promotion to principal engineer.

## Key Information

- Fowler was pulled off Kudu (an Azure git-push deploy technology) to help start .NET Core, chosen alongside another principal engineer as its architects
- The team rebuilt the package manager, runtime, and libraries essentially from scratch with ~30 engineers
- Fowler initially tried to code review every single change, burned out within a year, and learned to trust people, delegate, let people fail, and separate urgent from non-urgent
- It was the longest project of his career (~10 years) and taught him the long-term impact of early decisions — a core part of his "deferred impact" and judgment
