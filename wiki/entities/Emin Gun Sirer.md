## Definition

Emin Gun Sirer is a computer scientist, Cornell professor, and the CEO and founder of the Avalanche blockchain platform. He was the adviser of Evan King's hacking club at Cornell and a TA for Evan.

## Key Information

- Was the adviser of the Cornell hacking club (Capture The Flag) that Evan King founded
- Was a TA for Evan at Cornell
- Was influential in the crypto community, working on Bitcoin and other similar projects
- Had research assistants at Cornell working on crypto projects
- Is the current CEO and founder of Avalanche, one of the larger blockchain platforms
- Through his influence, Evan gained early exposure to crypto and blockchain technology
