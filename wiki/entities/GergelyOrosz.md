## Definition
Gergely Orosz is the author of The Pragmatic Engineer newsletter and @pragmaticengineer on social media. A former Uber engineer (where he worked alongside Tuomas Artman), he is a prominent voice on software engineering practices, engineering management, and the evolving role of engineers in the age of AI.

## Key Information
- Author of The Pragmatic Engineer, a widely-read newsletter on software engineering
- Former Uber engineer, worked with Tuomas Artman during Uber's hypergrowth period
- Interviewer in this talk with Tuomas Artman at the aiDotEngineer conference
- Shared Uber experience: revenue was the golden metric, quality was hard to measure and easy to ignore
- Sat in on one of Linear's Quality Wednesdays and described it as "awesome" and "aspirational"
- Pushed back on Tuomas's claims to draw out nuance: challenged whether feature factories existed before AI, questioned whether the full-week hiring trial is practical
- Referenced the 2000s evolution of the programmer role: single language → multiple languages → QA → DevOps → now PM and customer support
