## Definition

Ivan Illich is the author of *Tools for Conviviality* ("Tools of Conviviality" in the transcript), recommended by [[Max Schoening]] for its contrast between technology/tools that let users exercise human ingenuity and autonomy versus tools built at industrial scale that become destructive to human autonomy.

## Key Information

- Schoening: "it's sort of the contrast between... the history of technology and tools that let users exercise human ingenuity and autonomy versus tools that are more at industrial scale that almost have become destructive to human autonomy." He calls it a "weird" recommendation relative to his other picks.
- Thematically resonant with Schoening's [[Malleable Software]] argument (user ownership and adaptability vs. vendor/industrial control).
