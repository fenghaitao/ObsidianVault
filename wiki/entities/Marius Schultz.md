## Definition

Marius Schultz is a Meta Senior Staff engineer (IC7) who specializes in frontend web engineering. He reached IC7 by "redefining expectations" three times.

## Key Information

- Developed a passion for the web in 9th grade learning HTML; famously read a CSS book ("Little Boxes") in one sitting on Christmas Eve
- Studied computer science in Munich while working at a small agency building web applications
- IC4→IC5: greatly exceeded for multiple halves, but one promo was blocked by an "expectations miss" — too pushy and intense pushing his ideas rather than collaborating
- Earned "redefines expectations" as an IC4 by leading a project classified as IC6-level (10+ engineers plus PMs and designers)
- Earned a second RE at IC5→IC6 by running a hard-deadline web project and flexing into a "product hybrid" role through ambiguity
- Joined Instagram web (recruited by Jake Bolam), rewrote the notifications panel, and worked in product infrastructure (error boundaries, reliability)
- Was the first engineer on Threads web (6–8 weeks solo), then hired and led the team as TL for both logged-out and logged-in clients
- Shipped ~2,000 diffs in the year Threads launched, contributing to his IC7 promotion
- After IC7, moved to a large Instagram server backend project team led by Jake
- Not actively pursuing IC8; not considering the management track
