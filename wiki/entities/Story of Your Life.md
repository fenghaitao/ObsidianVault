## Definition

*Story of Your Life* is a short story by Ted Chiang, named by [[Edwin Chen]] as his all-time favorite short story in the episode's lightning round; it is the basis for the film *Arrival*.

## Key Information

- Centers on a linguist learning an alien language — Chen says he rereads it every couple of years and connects it directly to his own childhood dream of deciphering an alien language, which shaped his academic path (math, linguistics, MIT) and later his approach to data/language quality at [[Surge AI]].
