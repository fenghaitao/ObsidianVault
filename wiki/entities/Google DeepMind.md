## Definition
Google DeepMind (GDM) is the AI research lab formed in 2023 from the merger of DeepMind and Google Brain; it builds the Gemini family of models and is where Vlad Feinberg leads pre-training.
## Key Information
- Vlad Feinberg is a Google DeepMind Distinguished Engineer (L9) and pre-training area lead.
- Internally abbreviated "GDM"; has distinct focus areas and standalone teams for classical LLM research (pre-training, post-training) alongside product-integration teams like the one wiring Gemini into search results.
- Vlad rejects a sharp applied-vs-research split: even "applied" work (grounded, citation-backed answers; assessing source quality and filtering sarcasm/jokes) is hard research, and pure-research teams still own delivering models that train stably.
- His team delivers Flash and Flash-Lite (AI Overviews, AI mode, ads, YouTube, 1P models) and is technical POC for the Google-Apple partnership; it also contributes pre-training research to the Pro series.
- Three research verticals under him: distillation, inference co-design, and quantization.
- Recruiting view: skill demands are similar across frontier labs; the advice in Vlad's post was endorsed by people at OpenAI and Anthropic.
