## Definition

[[Google]]'s AI research lab, led by [[Demis Hassabis]]; referenced by [[Aishwarya Naresh Reganti]] as combining image-model, LLM, and world-model ("Genie") work in a direction she cites as evidence for her prediction that multimodal AI will make a major leap in 2026.

## Key Information

- Reganti mentions its world-model product by the name "Genie" (noting some uncertainty about the exact name in the transcript: "I think is what it's called"), alongside its image-model and LLM work, as the combined direction Hassabis has discussed publicly.
- Cited as part of the broader argument that better multimodal understanding (not just generation) is needed to unlock messy real-world data — e.g., handwritten documents and low-quality PDFs that current models still can't reliably parse.
