## Definition

Hadi Partovi is the engineer — transcribed in the episode as "Hody Partovi" — who, while running Internet Explorer, placed the giant "E" logo on Netscape's front lawn during the browser wars.

## Key Information

- Transcription variant: the transcript renders his name "Hody Partovi"; the correct spelling is Hadi Partovi.
- He ran Internet Explorer while James Everingham ran the Netscape browser; the IE-team lawn prank in San Francisco is attributed to him.
- He and James later worked together at Tellme Networks ("I reported in initially to Hody").
