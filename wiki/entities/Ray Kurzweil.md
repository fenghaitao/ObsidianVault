## Definition

Futurist and author (working on a book about living long enough to achieve indefinite lifespan), met years ago by [[Jeetu Patel]] while Patel was a chief scientist at Google.

## Key Information

- When Patel asked him about the societal impact of indefinite human lifespans (housing, agriculture, transportation all needing to change), Kurzweil's response — that humans underestimate exponential change because they only think exponentially along a single dimension at a time — became a recurring frame for how Patel thinks about compounding technological change, including AI.
