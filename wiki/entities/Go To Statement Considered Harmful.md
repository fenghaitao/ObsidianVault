## Definition

"Go To Statement Considered Harmful" is Edsger Dijkstra's influential 1968 letter arguing against the go-to statement.

## Key Information

- Originally titled "A Case Against the Go-To Statement," it grew out of a conference conversation with Brian Randell.
- Editor Niklaus Wirth retitled it and ran it as a letter to the editor to skip refereeing, which drew angry letters.
- The letter became a flashpoint of the structured-programming movement.
