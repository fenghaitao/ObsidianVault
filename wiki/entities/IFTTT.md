## Definition
IFTTT (If This Then That) is an automation service that connects different apps and devices. Kitze used it with Google Home to split voice commands — when saying "tell my assistant to [task]," IFTTT would take the second half and send it to any connected service.

## Key Information
- Used by Kitze with Google Home for voice-based todo logging
- Supported splitting Google Assistant commands: "tell my assistant to" → IFTTT takes the second half
- Enabled storing voice todos before AI was smart enough to process them natively
- Part of Kitze's early productivity system evolution
