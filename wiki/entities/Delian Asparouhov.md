## Definition

Delian Asparouhov is a venture capitalist who was Keith Rabois's chief of staff. He wrote a blog post on "lessons learned from Keith" and had the same reaction as Roelof Botha to Fair's exceptional execution pace.

## Key Information

- Former chief of staff to [[Keith Rabois]]
- Wrote a blog post: "Lessons learned from Keith" — including the hiring principle about value creation vs. value preservation
- Shadowed Keith at the second [[Fair]] board meeting and said: "If there's one company in Silicon Valley that would cause me to leave being a VC, it'd be Fair"
- His reason: "The pace of execution" — same as [[Roelof Botha]]'s observation about [[Square]]
- "There is something slightly off, and by the next board meeting, not only have they identified the root causes, but they've shipped and reacted and measured their impact"
