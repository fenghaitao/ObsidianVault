## Definition

Kalshi is a regulated prediction market platform that allows users to trade on the outcomes of real-world events, including elections, economic indicators, and cultural events. It serves as a benchmark for evaluating the forecasting accuracy of AI-driven simulation models.

## Key Information

- Tracked by [[SimFrancisco]]'s synthetic population model as a forecasting accuracy benchmark during the [[ClaudeOpus4.8]] Build Day hackathon (June 2026).
- [[SimFrancisco]]'s 10,000-resident synthetic electorate tracks prediction markets like Kalshi within a couple of percentage points.
- Regulated by the US Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) as a derivatives exchange, offering legally traded event contracts.
- Allows trading on event outcomes spanning elections, economic data releases, weather, and cultural events.
- Used alongside [[Polymarket]] as a real-world validation signal for [[SyntheticPopulation|synthetic population]] forecasting models.
