## Definition

Annie Duke (referred to as "Andy" in the transcript's ASR) is a decision-making author whose book *Quit: The Power of Knowing When to Walk Away* uses [[Stewart Butterfield]]'s pivot from Glitch to [[Slack]] as a case study.

## Key Information

- Butterfield cites her framing of his own pivot decision as an expected-value calculation — essentially a "smart fold": once the expected value of continuing had diminished enough, an alternative path became more attractive, similar to poker decision theory (Duke's other book is *Thinking in Bets*).
