## Definition

Sarah Novotny was the Kubernetes community leader credited by Brendan Burns with assembling the bootstrap committee that wrote Kubernetes' governance rules.

## Key Information

- Burns credits her with "a ton of credit" for pulling together the roughly seven-to-eight-person bootstrap committee that wrote Kubernetes governance in 2016.
- The committee represented the whole community, aligned rather than infighting, which let it codify democratic rules.
