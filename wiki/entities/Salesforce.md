## Definition

Salesforce is the CRM company founded in the 1990s, led by CEO [[Marc Benioff]]. In [[Jason Lemkin]]'s account, its "Agent Force" AI-agent product became one of [[SaaStr]]'s 20 GTM agents, and Salesforce itself has become the de facto data hub ("database") that most competing AI go-to-market agents plug into. Separately, it acquired [[Brett Taylor]]'s startup [[Quip]], where [[Molly Graham]] was an early operating executive (episode 34).

## Key Information

### Per episode 34 (Molly Graham)
- Named only as the eventual acquirer of [[Quip]], the pre-launch collaborative-documents startup founded by [[Brett Taylor]], where Molly Graham ran everything outside product and engineering.

### Per episode 35 (Jason Lemkin)
- SaaStr deployed Agent Force specifically to re-engage prospects human reps had deprioritized ("not enough commission," too busy with bigger deals); after training it on a single distilled prompt, it achieved a 70% response rate — "so good," per Lemkin, because those leads were "dying to interact" but had been ignored by humans.
- Lemkin describes SaaStr as "one of the only organizations of our size" running Agent Force this deeply, and shares a "cheat code": after months of learning, the team collapsed three separate agent workflows down to a single well-crafted prompt handed to Agent Force, and it performed well within a day.
- Lemkin frames Salesforce's current forward-deployed, hands-on support model as a return to how the company operated ~20 years ago when he bought his first two seats ("I'm here for you, I'm going to help you get on the app exchange") — a level of hands-on service that had faded and is now "kind of back," with Benioff running roughly 2,000 people in this function.
- Quotes Benioff (rendered "Mark Beni off" in the transcript) admiring [[Palantir]]'s ability to have customers live before they pay, calling it the "number one thing" he envies about Palantir (alongside its high deal sizes) — Salesforce, being "the biggest ship to turn" at $44B, can't yet offer that.
- Despite being "founded in the 90s," Salesforce has become — per Lemkin — the central hub/database that most competing AI GTM agent vendors integrate with, a claim he connects to his "hive mind" framing (see [[Hive Mind]]).
- Cited as an example (alongside HubSpot) of an "old school B2B SaaS" company at billions in revenue that still has great inbound leads it fails to call back.
