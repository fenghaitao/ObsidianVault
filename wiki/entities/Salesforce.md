## Definition

Salesforce is a $44B cloud-based CRM and enterprise software company. It acquired Slack in one of the largest tech acquisitions in history. In the AI era, Salesforce is experiencing a renaissance as the "hub" or "database" for AI GTM agents, with its Agentforce platform and 2,000-person forward-deployed engineering team.

## Key Information

- Acquired Slack in one of the biggest tech acquisitions at the time
- Stewart Butterfield left Salesforce 2.5 years after the acquisition (as of the podcast recording)
- Marc Benioff has deployed ~2,000 people doing forward-deployed engineer-style work for Agentforce customers
- Agentforce is Salesforce's AI agent platform; SaaStr is one of the earliest organizations of its size to deploy it
- Salesforce is becoming the hub/database for AI GTM agents — all the AI agent tools plug into it, creating a renaissance for the platform
- Benioff envies Palantir's ability to get customers live before they pay and wishes every Salesforce customer could do the same
- SaaStr used Agentforce to reactivate lapsed leads, achieving a 70% response rate
- The "old Salesforce" (20 years ago) was hands-on with every customer; this FDE-driven approach is a return to that ethos
- Denise (formerly at Slack/Salesforce for 14 years) left to become CRO of OpenAI
- The Qualified platform was founded by the former CMO of Salesforce

- Acquired Quip, the productivity software company founded by Bret Taylor and scaled by Molly Graham
