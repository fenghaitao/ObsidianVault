## Definition
Salesforce is a CRM and enterprise software company that has implemented AI token usage tracking with minimum spend targets, contributing to the "token maxing" phenomenon. Also a contributor to the Open Claw Foundation.

## Key Information
- Has a tool where anyone can search and see how many dollars each person at the company spent on AI-related tokens
- Implements a minimum AI spend target per month (~$175 between various tools)
- Engineers token max at the beginning of each month to hit the minimum spend target
- Part of the Open Claw Foundation alongside Nvidia, Microsoft, Telegram, Tencent, ByteDance, and others
- **n8n Integration**: Mentioned as an example of an integration target in n8n workflows — "if this is a personal contact, Google contacts, business contact, go right into Salesforce"
