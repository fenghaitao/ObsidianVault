## Definition
Symbolics was the company that built the Lisp machine, commercial hardware designed to execute Lisp programs.

## Key Information
- Designed the Lisp machine, "a piece of hardware… designed by Symbolics to execute Lisp programs."
- An example of the 1980s enthusiasm for building hardware matched to functional programming.
