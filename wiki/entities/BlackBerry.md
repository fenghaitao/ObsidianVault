## Definition

Physical-keyboard smartphone/messaging device that was the dominant competitor Apple's iPhone team debated matching (or not) during iPhone development.

## Key Information

- Had only an estimated 1-2% market penetration at the time of iPhone development, prompting internal Apple debate about whether to chase BlackBerry's loyal user base or target the other 98% of mobile users with something different.
- Central to the iPhone team's "most heated" internal debate: whether to include a physical keyboard (BlackBerry-style) or go all-in on a virtual/multi-touch keyboard.
- Described by Fadell as fundamentally "just a texting machine and nothing else," lacking the technology convergence (multi-touch, Wi-Fi, cameras, video) that made the iPhone possible.
- The "BlackBerry" movie (referenced by Lenny) portrays BlackBerry's founders initially dismissing the iPhone as not serious due to its lack of a physical keyboard.
