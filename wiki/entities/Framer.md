## Definition

Framer is a website builder that works like a design tool, with real-time collaboration, CMS, SEO, and integrated A/B testing. It was a sponsor of this episode of Lenny's Podcast.

## Key Information

- Sponsor of Lenny's Podcast episode 31
- Sponsor of Lenny's Podcast episode 28 (Framer Pro free for Product Pass subscribers)
- Used by companies from early-stage startups to Fortune 500s including DoorDash, Zapier, Perplexity, and 11 Labs
- Changes go live in seconds with a single click, no engineering help needed
- Offers programs for startups, scaleups, and large enterprises
- Positioned as turning a .com from a "formality into a tool for growth"
