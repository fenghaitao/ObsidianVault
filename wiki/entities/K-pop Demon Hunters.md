## Definition

Animated movie named by [[Dr. Becky Kennedy]] as something she "loved so much" that she wrote a whole guide on it, in the lightning-round segment of [[Lenny's Podcast]] episode 28.

## Key Information

- Kennedy: "I really loved K-pop Demon Hunters so much. I wrote a whole guide on it cuz I think there's it's just such an opener for really good deep connected moments with our kids."
- Mentioned alongside the Netflix show *Secrets We Keep* in response to "Is there a recent movie or TV show you have really enjoyed?"

- Per [[Rachel Lockett]] (episode 43): also named as a favorite, having watched it with her daughter, whom she dressed as a character from it for Halloween.
