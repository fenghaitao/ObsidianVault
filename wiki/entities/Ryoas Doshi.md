## Definition

Ryoas Doshi is a product thinker and former guest on Lenny's Podcast. He is known for his "babies in disguise" rant about corporate adults.

## Key Information

- Former Lenny's Podcast guest (appeared a couple times)
- Described by Lenny as "one of my favorite product thinkers"
- Known for the observation that "most adults in the corporate environment are really just babies in disguise"
- Examples: wanting to always be center of attention, not wanting to share resources/budget/people, getting irrationally upset about things not going their way, needing people to fix things for them
- Dr. Becky reframes this as: "all humans need the same things. Whether we're one or five or 45 or 85"
