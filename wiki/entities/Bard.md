## Definition
Bard was Google's conversational AI assistant that preceded Gemini; Vlad Feinberg contributed supervised fine-tuning to one of its first releases.
## Key Information
- At the start of the Gemini program, Jeff Dean gave spot bonuses to people who jumped in and launched the first version of Bard.
- Vlad's contribution was small: supervised fine-tuning (SFT) for an early Bard version, involving hyperparameter tuning and fitting the model to old TPUs.
- His manager Rohan Aneja encouraged the Google Brain team to get involved rather than stay focused on first-author papers.
- The lesson Vlad drew: unglamorous work (hyperparameter tuning, compiler golfing) on high-value projects compounds into bigger investments and opportunities.
- Bard is the predecessor to the Gemini model family.
