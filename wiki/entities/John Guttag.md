## Definition

John Guttag is the MIT colleague with whom Barbara Liskov jointly developed a class on program design, modularity, data types, specifications, and reasoning about correctness.

## Key Information

- The joint MIT class taught how to design around modularity and data types and how to write specifications and reason about correctness.
- Its emphasis on "think about the meaning of things first" (implementation hidden inside) contrasted with the West Coast's implementation-focused classes/subclasses view — a difference that shaped the Liskov substitution principle.
