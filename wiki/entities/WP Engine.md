## Definition

Managed WordPress hosting company founded by [[Jason Cohen]]; one of his two unicorn outcomes (VC-funded, versus his earlier bootstrapped unicorn [[SmartBear]]). Led as CEO by [[Heather Brunner]] for the past 11 years.

## Key Information

- A significant share of WP Engine's business is sold through agencies that build WordPress sites for clients, rather than direct-to-customer — cited by Cohen as an example (alongside HubSpot's agency channel) of a genuinely new distribution channel, not an incremental tweak to an existing one.
