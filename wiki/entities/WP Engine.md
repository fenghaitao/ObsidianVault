## Definition

WP Engine is a WordPress hosting company founded by Jason Cohen. It became a unicorn and is one of two unicorns Cohen built. Heather Brunner joined as CEO and is described as a "late-joining co-founder" for her transformative impact.

## Key Information

- Founded by Jason Cohen (one of his two unicorns)
- Heather Brunner has been CEO for 11+ years and is described as having co-founder-level impact
- Jason Cohen and Heather Brunner co-won Ernst & Young Entrepreneur of the Year 2017 for Central Texas
- Co-winners are rare for this award
- A significant portion of websites are sold through agencies that create WordPress sites — an example of an indirect channel that dramatically changed growth rate
- Used as an example of creative channel expansion: selling through agencies instead of only direct
