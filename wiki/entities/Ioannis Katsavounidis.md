## Definition

Ioannis Katsavounidis (transcribed in the episode as "Jannis Katzanetus," also called "Giannis") is a Greek signal-processing engineer and encoding scientist David Ronca calls the strongest engineer he has ever worked with.

## Key Information

- Was working as a signal-processing professor in Greece when David Ronca brought him in during a summer break around 2010 to work on parallel-encoding ideas.
- Their work gave birth to content-based encoding, which became the industry-wide approach to video encoding (with an early patent written by Ronca and him).
- Later joined Netflix and took the work further into convex hull encoding (transcribed "convex all encoding"), which mathematically maximizes quality for a given bandwidth — the model now used by both Netflix and Meta.
- Developed a cost-benefit codec-evaluation model (energy spent vs codec efficiency gained) that brought academic theory into practical, large-scale decision-making.
- Worked with Ronca at Innervideo, Netflix, and Meta; Ronca describes him as "an engineer's engineer" and one of the most brilliant people he has known.
