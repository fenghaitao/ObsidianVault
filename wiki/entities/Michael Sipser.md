## Definition

Michael Sipser is a theoretical computer scientist and author of the textbook "Introduction to the Theory of Computation."

## Key Information

- Ryan Williams recommends Sipser's Introduction to the Theory of Computation as "a fairly concise and very well-written textbook" for learning complexity theory.
- It is listed among his suggested readings for going deeper into the field.
