## Definition

Peter Naur (transcribed "Peter Nauer" / "Peter Now") is the Danish computer scientist who is the "Naur" in Backus-Naur form and a major figure in ALGOL standardization.

## Key Information

- The "N" in Backus-Naur form (BNF), the notation heard in the context of context-free grammars and parsing.
- A major figure in standardizing the language ALGOL and writing up its standard.
- Casey recounts a Hoare note: Naur told Hoare "making multipass compilers is easy. I just wrote a nine-pass one," which Hoare records as the reason he "decided not to pursue" an idea that, in hindsight, resembled static single assignment form.
- Casey muses Naur may have inadvertently set compiler science back ~15-20 years with that dismissal.
