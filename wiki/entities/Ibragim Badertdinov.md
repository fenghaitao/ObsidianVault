## Definition
Ibragim Badertdinov is a researcher at Nebius who leads the SWE-rebench coding agent evaluation leaderboard. He has a non-traditional background as a dentist by training before transitioning to AI research.

## Key Information
- Researcher at [[Nebius]], working on coding agent evaluation
- Creator and maintainer of [[SWE-rebench]], a monthly-refreshed coding agent leaderboard
- Former dentist — has papers on NeurIPS and ICML about RL and test-time scaling alongside dentistry publications
- Draws parallels between dental pain and infrastructure pain: "both will not let you sleep at night"
- Published on Google Scholar spanning both AI (RL, test-time scaling) and medical domains
- Advocates for practical, infrastructure-focused evaluation of coding agents
- Active on X (Twitter) and the Local LLaMA subreddit, incorporating community feedback into model selection
- Released SWE-Rehab (~30K RL environments) and SWE-Rehab V2 (20 programming languages) as open-source training datasets
