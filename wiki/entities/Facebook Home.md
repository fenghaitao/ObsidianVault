## Definition

Facebook Home was a fork of Android at the operating-system level, built together with an HTC hardware device. It was [[Adam Mosseri]]'s first project as a PM (he declared himself PM after the original PM quit), predating his move to [[Instagram]].

## Key Information

- Described by Mosseri as "a spectacular failure," but one that taught him more in a year to a year and a half than perhaps any other point in his career, because he had to learn carrier relationships, OEM partnerships, certification processes, and Android/operating-system internals from scratch.
- Mosseri ultimately ended the project himself, reasoning that sometimes the best thing you can do is bring a long-running idea to a conclusion in order to determine definitively whether the idea had product-market fit.
