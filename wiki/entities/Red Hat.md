## Definition

Red Hat is an open-source software company whose OpenShift platform was one of the earliest Kubernetes adopters and partners.

## Key Information

- OpenShift (Red Hat's platform-as-a-service offering) was "trying to build a platform as a service" and became an early Kubernetes partner.
- Burns notes partners like Red Hat, Azure, and AWS could "take a bet on Kubernetes" because of its independent governance under the Linux Foundation.
