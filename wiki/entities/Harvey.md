## Definition

Harvey is an AI legal-tech product cited by [[Jason Lemkin]] as an example of "everyone in market at once": law firms adopting AI for contract/document review aren't a scattered few early adopters, but nearly the entire category simultaneously.

## Key Information

- Lemkin's point: "It's not just one law firm looking at Harvey. It's everyone" — used as the central illustration for his [[Everyone In Market At Once]] framework, where in-market adoption for a hot AI category has jumped from a historical ~3-5% of prospects per year to "north of 50%."
- Cited alongside unnamed contract-review competitors as tools firms are picking specifically because "we need to automate how we review contracts and documents with AI now."
