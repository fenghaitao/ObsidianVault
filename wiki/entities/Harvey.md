## Definition

Harvey is a legal AI company that gave [[Claude4.6Opus|Claude Opus 4.6]] early access testing, bringing in experienced lawyers to evaluate it on real legal tasks against BigLaw Bench, their benchmark for real-world legal work.

## Key Information

- Opus 4.6 scored 90.2% on BigLaw Bench — the first Anthropic model to break 90%, with 40% of tasks receiving perfect scores.
- An internal lawyer's qualitative reaction after a single query: the output felt "smart and analytical, like it's actually thinking."
- **May 2026**: cited (alongside [[SolveIntelligence|Solve Intelligence]]) as an example of legal-tech vendors building their own products on Claude — not just using it as an assistant — as part of Anthropic's broader legal-industry connector/plugin expansion. See [[LegalAI]].
- **Dreaming customer (May 19, 2026)**: Harvey uses [[ClaudeManagedAgents]] to coordinate complex legal work like long-form drafting and document creation. With dreaming, their agents remember what they learned between sessions — including filetype workarounds and tool-specific patterns — rather than relearning them each time. Completion rates went up ~6x in Harvey's tests, corroborating the same figure cited in [[AgenticMemory]]. See [[summary-2026-05-19 - New in Claude Managed Agents dreaming, outcomes, and multiagent orchestration]].
