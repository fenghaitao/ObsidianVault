## Definition
Priscila Andre de Oliveira is a Senior Software Engineer at Sentry, maintainer of the Verdaccio open-source NPM registry, and co-organizer of Vienna JS. She describes herself as an "agent manager" — since December 2025, she has stopped writing code and only prompts AI agents to produce it. Her talk at aiDotEngineer revealed that 67% of her AI usage is comprehension, not generation.

## Key Information
- Senior Software Engineer at Sentry (6+ years)
- Self-described "agent manager" — has not coded since December 2025, only prompting
- Maintainer of [[Verdaccio]], an open-source NPM registry
- Co-organizer of [[Vienna JS]], a JavaScript meetup in Vienna, Austria
- Brazilian-based in Vienna, Austria
- Mother of a 2-year-old toddler
- Tracked her AI usage across 116 sessions: 67% comprehension, 2% code generation
- Built the [[Catch Me Up Skill]], a custom Claude skill with six exploration modes (Architecture, Convention, Feature Trace, Syntax, Testing, History)
- Argues that understanding AI research is a critical missing step in the [[ThreePhaseApproach]]
- Advocates for shipping "keynote code" (high quality) rather than slop code
- Quoted: "AI is the teammate who never gets tired of your questions. It's the cheapest senior engineer out there."
- Works on Sentry's frontend; is a visual learner who prefers structured outputs like organograms and flow diagrams
