## Definition
Xavier Niel is a philanthropist and French billionaire who was one of the funders of Kyutai, the non-profit AI research lab that created Moshi and from which Gradium AI was spun off.

## Key Information
- Philanthropist who funded Kyutai, a non-profit AI research lab
- French billionaire and technology investor
- Alongside Eric Schmidt and others, provided funding for open speech AI research
- His funding enabled the development of Moshi and other open speech models
