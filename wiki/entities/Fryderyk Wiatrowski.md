## Definition
Fryderyk Wiatrowski is the co-founder of Viktor, an AI employee platform that lives in Slack. He presented Viktor's story and architecture at the AI Engineer conference (aiDotEngineer) in May 2026.

## Key Information

- **Role**: Co-founder of Viktor
- **Presented at**: AI Engineer conference (aiDotEngineer), May 2026
- **Talk**: "Viktor: AI Coworker That Lives in Slack" — covered Viktor's evolution from web agents to email agents to Slack-based AI employee
- **Key insights shared**:
  - Viktor launched February 2026 as an experiment with zero growth expectations; achieved immediate product-market fit
  - The team's journey: JCAI (web agent, 2023) → Jace (email agent, 2024) → Viktor (AI employee, 2026)
  - Design philosophy: Viktor is "not a tool, it's a hire" — should be treated like a human employee
  - Three pillars for great AI coworkers: helps get work done, knows the company, is friendly
  - Personality matters: users rejected GPT 5.4 in favor of Opus 4.6 because of personality
  - Proactivity must be earned: rolling out proactive features on day one triggers security team backlash
  - Context isolation and multi-user memory are the hardest scaling challenges for company agents
  - Shared context (one person connects, whole team uses) is the key advantage over personal agents
  - Customer lesson: admin connected personal Gmail as team integration — taught the need for integration scoping
  - Tied the AI employee vision to Gottfried Leibniz's 17th-century calculator ambition
- **Notable quote (Leibniz)**: "It is unworthy of excellent men to lose hours like slaves in the labor of calculation. Let us leave that to machines."
