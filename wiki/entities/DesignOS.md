## Definition

Design OS is Brian Casel's free open-source guided design process for AI-first development. It fills the gap between product idea and codebase with three phases: product planning, section design, and export of production-ready components with implementation prompts. Built on React/Vite/ShadCN/Tailwind.

## Key Information

- Free and open-source at buildermethods.com/designos.
- Three phases: (1) Product Planning (vision, data model, design tokens, shell); (2) Section Design (requirements, sample data, screens); (3) Export (components + prompts).
- Standalone project separate from app codebase.
- Two implementation approaches: incremental (milestone-by-milestone) and one-shot.
- Companion to [[AgentOS]]: Design OS for greenfield design, Agent OS for legacy standards.
