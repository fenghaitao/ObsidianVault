## Definition

JetBrains is an IDE maker (IntelliJ, PyCharm, and related tools) whose products bundle Anthropic's [[ClaudeCodeSkills|claude-api]] skill (April 2026), including its Junie coding-agent product, giving developers Claude API expertise directly inside the IDE.

## Key Information

- [[ClaudeCode]] already ships a JetBrains IDE plugin (installable from the JetBrains Marketplace) — see [[ClaudeCode]] for that integration.
- As of April 2026, JetBrains and its Junie product additionally bundle the open-source `claude-api` skill from `anthropics/skills`, alongside [[CodeRabbit]], [[ResolveAI|Resolve AI]], and [[Warp]].
