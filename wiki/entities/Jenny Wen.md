## Definition

Jenny Wen is the head of design for Claude at Anthropic, now leading design for Claude Co-work. She was previously director of design at Figma where she led the design teams behind FigJam and Slides. She has also worked as a designer at Dropbox, Square, and Shopify. She gave a talk called "Don't Trust the Design Process" arguing that the traditional design process is dead.

## Key Information

- Head of design for Claude, now leading design for Claude Co-work at Anthropic
- Joined Anthropic as an IC (individual contributor) to stay close to the work; moved back to IC after a stint managing
- Former director of design at Figma, led design teams behind FigJam and Slides
- Also worked at Dropbox, Square, and Shopify
- Gave a talk at a conference in Berlin (September 2025) called "Don't Trust the Design Process" — the talk was her most resonant ever but also received backlash from designers invested in the traditional process
- Worked on the team that built Claude Code (the agentic product for non-coding tasks)
- Boris credits Jenny, Felix, and Sam as the team that built Claude Code
- Believes the traditional design process is dead; design work is stratifying into execution support and vision/direction
- Spends time in code implementing polish and last-mile details alongside engineers
- Still uses Figma extensively for exploring multiple design options (coding tools are too linear)
- Uses Claude Code in VS Code for frontend tweaks and via Slack for remote fixes
- Shifted all chat use cases to Claude Co-work
- Identifies three designer hiring archetypes: strong generalist, deep specialist, craft new grad
- Management philosophy: "low leverage" tasks become high leverage when a senior leader does them
- Uses Co-work for introspection — analyzing personal notes to extract insights about herself
- On Twitter/X at jenny_wen; not active on LinkedIn
- Favorite book: The Power Broker by Robert Caro; also recommends Insomniac City by Bill Hayes
- Life motto: "It is what it is" (not defeatist — brings levity to move forward)
