## Definition

Sonnet 3.5 (Claude 3.5 Sonnet) is a version of Anthropic's Claude model. Its release was the trigger for Zevi Arnovitz's AI building journey — he watched YouTube videos of people building apps with it while traveling in Japan.

## Key Information

- The release of Sonnet 3.5 was the pivotal moment that sparked Zevi's AI building journey
- Zevi was in Japan when it launched and watched YouTube videos of people building apps with Bolt and Lovable
- The experience felt like "someone came up to me and said, 'Hey, you have superpowers now'"
- Zevi immediately opened accounts and started building upon returning from Japan
- Boris Cherny notes that Sonnet 3.5 could only run 15-30 seconds before going off the rails; very different from Opus 4.6 which runs 10-30 minutes unattended
- Boris: "It's not Sonnet 3.5 anymore. The new models are just completely different"
