## Definition

Sonar is a company building AI-coding quality tooling, organized around an "agent-centric development life cycle" framework of three pillars: guide, verify, and solve. It acquired [[Gitarr]] and sponsored [[ColeMedin]]'s video on AI coding autonomy levels.

## Key Information

- **Positioning**: argues traditional code review and quality gates weren't built for the volume/speed at which AI now writes code, letting bugs and compounding errors slip through.
- **Reported result**: teams using Sonar are 44% less likely to hit production outages caused by AI-generated code.
- **[[Gitarr]]** fits into Sonar's "verify" pillar specifically.
