## Definition

iA Writer is a minimalist writing application. Jenny Wen uses it for personal notes, one-on-one notes, random thoughts, memos, interview notes, and other writing collected over years.

## Key Information

- Jenny Wen uses iA Writer for a folder of local notes spanning all different topics
- Used as a personal knowledge repository over years
- Jenny uses Claude Co-work to analyze her iA Writer notes for introspection and insight extraction
