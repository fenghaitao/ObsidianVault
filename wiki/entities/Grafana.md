## Definition

Grafana built a Claude-powered intelligent assistant that lets users extract insights from observability data through natural language, without requiring PromQL/LogQL query expertise.

## Key Information

- Users can ask questions like "What's causing latency spikes in the checkout service?"; Claude identifies relevant metrics, constructs appropriate PromQL/LogQL queries, correlates data across systems, and presents actionable insights.
- Cited as an example of agentic coding/AI democratizing access to specialized development and analysis capabilities.
