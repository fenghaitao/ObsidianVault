## Definition

Investor in [[Applied Intuition]] and friend of [[Qasar Younis]]; described as viewing fame itself as a powerful tool — if a founder lacks an existing network, building a public following is an effective way to recruit employees, investors, and customers. Cited by Younis, alongside [[Marc Andreessen]], as an influence in his decision to start posting publicly on X.
