## Definition

Ivan Zhao is a co-founder of [[Notion]], credited by [[Max Schoening]] (referred to as "Ivan" in the transcript) alongside [[Simon Last]] with the early intuition that AI would meaningfully change how Notion (and product-building generally) should operate.

## Key Information

- Schoening: "I think both Ivan and Simon had the intuition of hey this is going to change a lot of things" — cited as a key reason Notion moved on AI ahead of the broader industry rush to be "AI native."
- [[Matt MacInnis]] (an early Notion investor) credits Zhao's personal "absolute insistence on craftsmanship" as the specific founder idiosyncrasy underlying Notion's rare, roughly four-year path to product-market fit — cited as a "narrative violation" (see [[Jeff Lewis]]) rather than a replicable strategy, since MacInnis argues every company's success traces back to its founder's particular idiosyncrasies.
