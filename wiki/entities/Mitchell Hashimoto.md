## Definition
Mitchell Hashimoto is co-founder of HashiCorp (transcript garbles it as "Hashiore") and creator of the Ghostty terminal (transcript "Ghosty").

## Key Information
- Charlie cites Hashimoto's recent post about a deliberately "terrible" renderer: an LLM optimized it to ~10x faster, but his hand-written version was ~100x faster — a caution against shipping accumulated "slop" instead of reasoning from first principles about how fast a system should be.
- Used as evidence that agents are good at local micro-optimizations but need human prompting and collaboration to reach larger redesigns.
