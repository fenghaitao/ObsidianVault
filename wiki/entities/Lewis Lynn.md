## Definition

Lewis Lynn is the creator of a free, always-updating online question bank of real PM interview questions. Zevi Arnovitz used this resource combined with AI analysis to prioritize his interview preparation.

## Key Information

- Created a free online question bank of real PM interview questions that is constantly updated
- Zevi used Comet (Perplexity browser) to run analyses on the question bank to identify the most frequently asked questions
- This data-driven approach helped Zevi prioritize which question types to focus on during mock interviews
