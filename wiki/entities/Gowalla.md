## Definition

Gowalla was a location-based social networking platform that competed with Foursquare in the early 2010s. Lenny Rachitsky's startup Local Mind was built on top of both Foursquare and Gowalla.

## Key Information

- Competed with Foursquare in the location-based check-in space
- Local Mind used both Foursquare and Gowalla's APIs to aggregate location data
- Was popular at the time alongside Foursquare
