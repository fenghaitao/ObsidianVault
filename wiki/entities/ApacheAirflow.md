## Definition
Apache Airflow is an open-source workflow orchestration platform. Jeremiah Lowin was a member of its Project Management Committee (PMC) before founding Prefect Technologies.

## Key Information
- Workflow orchestration platform for programmatically authoring, scheduling, and monitoring data pipelines
- Jeremiah Lowin served on the Apache Airflow PMC before founding Prefect
- Prefect Technologies was originally started to graduate orchestration ideas from Airflow into data science
- Represents the "things that are really good at doing orchestration" that Lowin contrasts with LLM agents, which are "right in the middle" for orchestration capability
