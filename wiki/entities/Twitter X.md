## Definition

Twitter/X is a social media platform identified as one of the two key organic marketing channels for B2B companies (alongside LinkedIn), where founders and employees build in public to drive growth.

## Key Information

- One of two key B2B social platforms alongside LinkedIn
- Primary channel for founder socials and employee socials at Lovable and similar AI companies
- Anton Osika (Lovable CEO) uses X to share growth numbers, new features, and lessons learned
- Part of the shift from SEO to social as organic marketing strategy
- Content must show personality and humanity — corporate-scrubbed AI-generated copy doesn't work
