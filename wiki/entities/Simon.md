## Definition

Simon (surname not stated) is a Meta staff engineer originally from Sweden who grew from new grad (IC3) to Staff Engineer (IC6) in three years, earning two rare "redefines expectations" ratings and two discretionary equity grants.

## Key Information

- Returned to Meta as an intern-turned-new-grad; pre-learned React/TypeScript-style technologies and skipped tool onboarding thanks to his prior internship
- Set an original goal of IC5 in 3 years, then overachieved by reaching IC6 in 3 years
- Worked on the ads payments growth team focused on revenue, which made impact easy to prove
- His first-half IC3→IC4 promo came from projects bringing in 3x the team's half revenue goal
- Earned RE ("redefines expectations") twice — as an IC4 and during his IC4→IC5 half
- Led a pod of engineers as an IC4, was intern manager and intern peer simultaneously, and later an intern director managing ~10 intern managers
- His IC5→IC6 growth was about moving from delegation to accountability ("delegation is not abdication") and building systems (people + project breakdowns) to surface problems early
- Briefly became a manager, then returned to IC after a chaotic stretch (manager in an accident, skip on leave, London exit, and overpromising two IC5→IC6 reports); open to management again in 3-5 years
