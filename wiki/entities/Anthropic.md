## Definition

Anthropic is the AI lab behind the Claude model family and Claude Code.

## Key Information

- Boris joined for mission and safety after being "blown away" by ChatGPT, describing LLMs as an "alien life form that we get to nurture"
- Culture prizes generalists — product managers, data scientists, and researchers all code
- Everyone holds the title "member of technical staff," regardless of function
- Safety is core: the company has held up model releases until they were confident they were safe, citing rising risks at the Opus 4 scale (e.g., designing bioweapons)
- Claude Code's productivity effect: ~70% per-engineer productivity growth even as headcount tripled

### Fiona Fung on Anthropic
- Fiona joined after using an internal Sonnet-based tool to build work tools, seeing firsthand how transformative AI already is for how people work
- She was drawn to how mission-oriented everyone was — "build AI that benefits humanity with safety first" — which reminded her of why she joined Facebook Marketplace in 2015
- Onboarding confirmed the mission is core to the culture and DNA, "not just a slogan"
- The Claude Code team's loop — idea → build → launch internally → gather dogfooding feedback → launch publicly — is a "rapid iteration loop" that she says may feel even faster than her Facebook Marketplace days

### Austin McDonald on Anthropic's Hiring Signal
- Anthropic's behavioral interview assesses a signal called "hold light and shade": the ability to understand both the positive and negative implications of AI for the future.
- When telling a story about leveraging AI to Anthropic, candidates should mention how they considered potential negative implications of the project and how they went about assessing and mitigating them — without that, interviewers won't feel comfortable hiring you ([[summary-20260316 - Meta Hiring Lead： Avoid Downleveling, Liars, Passing OpenAI And Anthropic Interviews]]).
- John Myles White cited Anthropic (with OpenAI) as a smaller frontier-lab startup that has had more layoff rounds than Meta Superintelligence Labs.
- Vlad Feinberg cites Anthropic (with OpenAI) as a peer frontier lab whose people agreed with his "how to get a job at a frontier lab" post, indicating overlapping skill demands.

### Thariq Shihipar on Anthropic's Engineering Culture
- Anthropic treats "figure out how to work at a higher abstraction level" as part of the job; the culture tolerates spending a day trying to automate something even if it fails, because the failure is data ("Claude is not good at this — how do we make it better").
- The internal-external perception gap is real: Thariq hears of large enterprise customers who "haven't typed a line of code themselves in 6 months," and many engineers are barely in the IDE anymore.
- The team's goal is for Claude to absorb the "glue work" (e.g., translating a Figma file into code) so humans concentrate on unique work; there is a ton of demand for unique thinking.
- "Claude Code is really good at the implementation of code, and Claude Tag is for the rest of the software development life cycle" — feedback, code review, CI/CD, incidents.
- Internal essays arguing direction are expected to stay human-written; AI-generated writing should be disclosed (routine data readouts are fine, novel thought is not).
