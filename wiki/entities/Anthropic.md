## Definition

Anthropic is the AI lab behind the Claude model family and Claude Code.

## Key Information

- Boris joined for mission and safety after being "blown away" by ChatGPT, describing LLMs as an "alien life form that we get to nurture"
- Culture prizes generalists — product managers, data scientists, and researchers all code
- Everyone holds the title "member of technical staff," regardless of function
- Safety is core: the company has held up model releases until they were confident they were safe, citing rising risks at the Opus 4 scale (e.g., designing bioweapons)
- Claude Code's productivity effect: ~70% per-engineer productivity growth even as headcount tripled
