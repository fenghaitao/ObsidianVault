## Definition

Zillow is an online real estate marketplace company. Evan King interned there before joining Meta.

## Key Information

- Evan King interned at Zillow before joining Meta, working on the rentals team
- He finished his internship project quickly and spent his extra time building a fun internal game where employees could "catch" colleagues who left their computers unlocked, featuring a leaderboard and graphs
- Hung out with the cyber security team and they took him to Defcon in Las Vegas, despite security not being his assigned team
- At the end of the internship, Evan's manager (who had worked at Google for a long time) told him he was the best intern he had ever worked with — a major confidence-building moment that Evan carried into Meta
- The key trait his manager noticed: Evan was inquisitive and didn't feel bounded by what he was told to do
- At Zillow, Evan built a personal hosted search engine (variant of Lucene) where he stored markdown notes of everything he learned, so he never had to solve the same problem twice
