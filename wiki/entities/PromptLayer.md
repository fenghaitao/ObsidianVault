## Definition
PromptLayer is an AI engineering workbench and platform for prompt management, evaluation, logging, auditability, and governance. Founded by Jared Zoneraich and based in New York, it processes millions of LLM requests per day and serves as a collaboration platform for teams building AI products.

## Key Information
- Founded approximately 3 years before December 2025
- Based in New York City with a small team
- Core thesis: rigorous prompt engineering and agent development with involvement from both product and engineering teams
- Believes domain experts (lawyers for legal AI, etc.) should be involved alongside engineers
- Provides prompt management, auditability, governance, logging, and evals
- Processes millions of LLM requests per day
- Has an eval product that doubles as a batch runner for running prompts through columns of data
- Founder dogfoods the product extensively for building agents
- Many insights in Jared's talk come from customer conversations about building coding agents
- Has a mascot called "Prompt Wrangler"
