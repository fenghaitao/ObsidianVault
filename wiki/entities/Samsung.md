## Definition
Samsung is a consumer electronics company whose Galaxy S25 Ultra smartphone was used by Liquid AI as a target mobile hardware platform for on-device profiling of small model architectures.

## Key Information
- Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra used as a mobile profiling target for LFM 2 architecture optimization
- On-device profiling on this phone demonstrated LFM 2's short convolution advantage in inference speed and memory usage on mobile hardware
- Represents the mobile/phone deployment target for edge models
