## Definition

Anton Osika is co-founder and CEO of [[Lovable]].

## Key Information

- Cited by [[Elena Verna]] (Head of Growth) as a driving force behind Lovable's "build in public" strategy — personally posting on social media about product progress and growth numbers, in the same vein as other founder-led public-narrative strategies (e.g., Gamma's [[Grant Lee]]).
- Verna recounts Osika reacting to a bad bug ("this is not lovable") as the internal cultural signal that triggers an immediate, no-queue fix — illustrating the company's product-quality bar being enforced from the top.
