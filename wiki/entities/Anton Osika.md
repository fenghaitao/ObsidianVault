## Definition

Anton Osika is the CEO and co-founder of Lovable, the AI-powered app builder that hit $200M ARR in under one year. He is known for building in public and sharing Lovable's growth journey on social media.

## Key Information

- CEO and co-founder of Lovable (originally launched as GPT Engineer)
- Leads Lovable's building-in-public strategy, regularly tweeting about growth numbers, new features, and lessons learned
- Elena Verna notes she sometimes has to say no to Anton to protect her personal time and family commitments
- Under his leadership, Lovable grew from 30 to 100 employees in 6 months, with plans to quadruple by end of year
- His social media presence is a core growth lever for Lovable, alongside employee socials
