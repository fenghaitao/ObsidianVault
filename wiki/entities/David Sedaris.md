## Definition

David Sedaris is a humorist and essayist known for his autobiographical stories. Lenny referenced him in the context of needing to live life to have material to write about.

## Key Information

- Lenny references Sedaris's approach to generating material: he says yes to everything he's invited to because he needs experiences to keep pumping out stories
- "Sure, let's go to Vegas right now. Let's do it."
- This philosophy aligns with Michelle's creative approach — ideas come from living life, not sitting and trying to generate them
