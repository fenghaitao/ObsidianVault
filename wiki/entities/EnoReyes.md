## Definition
Eno Reyes is a speaker from Factory AI who presented at aiDotEngineer on making codebases agent-ready. He advocates for investing in automated validation criteria as the key enabler for AI coding agents.

## Key Information
- Works at Factory AI, a company whose mission is to bring autonomy to software engineering
- Presented "Making Codebases Agent Ready" at aiDotEngineer (December 2025)
- Argues that the primary limiter for coding agent effectiveness is organizational validation criteria, not agent capability
- Emphasizes that organizations should invest in making their codebases agent-ready rather than spending excessive time comparing coding tools
- Promotes specification-driven development as the emerging paradigm for software engineering with AI
- Believes software developers will remain heavily involved, but their role shifts to curating the validation environment
