## Definition

A Sentimental Value is a Norwegian film by the same director who made The Worst Person in the World. It's a family drama about a family and the house they lived in their entire lives, where the house itself is sort of a character. Jenny recommends it for its subtle pacing, writing, and character relationships.

## Key Information

- Norwegian film by the director of The Worst Person in the World
- Family drama about a family and their lifelong home
- The house is described as "sort of a character" in the film
- Jenny watched it on a plane ("how directors want you to watch their films")
- Praised for subtle pacing, writing, and character relationships
