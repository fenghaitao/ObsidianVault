## Definition

Albert Gu is a co-founder of Cartesia and the researcher behind Mamba (state space models); he was a PhD labmate of Nimit Sohoni in Chris Ré's lab at Stanford.

## Key Information

- Nimit knew Albert "pretty well" — he was a good friend, and they had worked closely together in Chris Ré's lab.
- Albert showed, via Mamba, that sequence modeling was not a "solved problem" to be handled by scaling transformers alone — state space models provide computational-efficiency and even raw-quality advantages, and hybrid models can combine both.
- Albert's analogy (from a blog Nimit references): SSMs are "like a brain" (a fixed-size state), while transformers are "like a database" (recalling anything in context).
