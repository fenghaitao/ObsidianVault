## Definition

Jender is an engineering manager who was Zach Wilson's direct manager at both Facebook and Netflix. He played a pivotal role in Zach's career by recognizing his potential early and enabling his transition from Facebook to Netflix as a Senior Engineer.

## Key Information

- Was Zach's manager on the Growth team at Facebook — specifically the Core Growth and Notifications sub-team
- Zach described him as an "entrepreneurial startup-y guy" and one of his best friends to this day
- Built strong trust with Zach early when Zach demonstrated he could solve full-stack problems even as a data engineer (fixing a notification framework that required learning XHP/React)
- Left Facebook during Zach's second year there, which contributed to Zach's promotion to E5 being delayed (three different managers in one year disrupted the promotion case)
- Later became a hiring manager at Netflix and recruited Zach as a Senior Data Engineer, overriding the typical level requirements because he had already seen Zach's work firsthand
- Was fired from Netflix in 2019 when Netflix collapsed the entire Data Engineering org into Data Science, eliminating the VP, director, and manager chain
- Zach's decision to leave Netflix was heavily influenced by Jender's departure — Zach joined Netflix specifically to work with Jender
- Zach still consults with Jender on entrepreneurship topics
