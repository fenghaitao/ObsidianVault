## Definition

PostRank was a company founded by Ilya Grigorik that built a "better version of PageRank" for the web 2.0 era, ranking posts by social engagement (links, comments, likes) rather than links alone. It was acquired by Google.

## Key Information

- Founded after Grigorik deferred his University of Toronto graduate work to pursue investor conversations
- Core insight: links and social interactions are a "social currency" and a feedback signal on content quality; disconnected pages "are not worth much"
- Launched July 8; early servers "melted down" due to demand and early poor code, later optimized out over a couple of weeks
- Acquired by Google, which cared more about the team's talent and infrastructure than the PostRank product; the product was effectively shelved and rebuilt inside Google Analytics
- The acquisition was "closer to [an aqua-hire]" — the IP was used, but the product was rebuilt from scratch at Google scale
