## Definition

Terrence Rohan is a venture capitalist and seed investor, co-researching a project with [[Edwin Chen]] (CEO, [[Surge AI]]) interviewing early employees who spotted generational companies before they were obviously big.

## Key Information

- The project interviewed five people who joined companies like OpenAI and Stripe as early employees before either was widely recognized as a major success, looking for patterns in how they identified those opportunities early.
- Chen reports the pattern found aligns closely with his own founder philosophy: outsized ambition and conviction in a specific idea, rather than iteratively chasing product-market fit wherever it leads.
