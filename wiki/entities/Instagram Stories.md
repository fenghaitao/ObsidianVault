## Definition

Instagram Stories is the ephemeral, full-screen, 24-hour sharing format Instagram launched to compete with Snapchat; Ryan Olson led its core iOS team.

## Key Information

- The creation team had churned through prior product directions; Ryan joined as lead iOS engineer and cut the team down to a core of two iOS engineers (with borrowed help, two Android engineers, and a half-time server engineer).
- Built in 2–3 months working 16–18 hour days, 7 days a week.
- Goal: get everyday people comfortable sharing again — normal-people sharing was evaporating to Snapchat's ephemeral format.
- Instagram's advantage: people already had their friend graph (they just lacked the right outlet); it also added touches Snapchat lacked (tap-left to navigate back, hold-to-pause).
- Ryan's North Star on it: "if you want to go fast, go small" — full ownership and fewer decision-makers.
- His PM on the project (Nathan Sharp) later became his co-founder.
