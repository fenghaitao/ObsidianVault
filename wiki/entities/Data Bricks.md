## Definition

Data Bricks is a data and AI platform company. It was mentioned in the context of a research paper co-authored by folks at UC Berkeley and Data Bricks that found 74-75% of enterprises cite reliability as their biggest problem with deploying AI products.

## Key Information

- Co-authored a research paper with [[UC Berkeley]] researchers (Mate Zahara Stoker and others) about enterprise AI adoption challenges
- The paper found that ~74-75% of enterprises surveyed cited reliability as their biggest barrier to deploying AI products to end users
- This reliability concern is why many AI products today focus on productivity (low autonomy) rather than end-to-end agents replacing workflows
- [[Aishwaria Raanti]] and [[Kiti Bottom]] have led/supported AI product deployments involving Data Bricks
