## Definition

Notion is cited by [[Dan Shipper]] as the tool [[Every]] used, via its built-in agents, to run its entire end-of-2025 quarterly planning process.

## Key Information

- Every ran quarterly planning almost entirely through Notion agents: employees talked to an agent about what happened, their goals, and their metrics; the agent pushed back and connected each person's answers to the overall company strategy, producing AI-generated strategy reports per team that Dan Shipper could then use to identify which teams needed to coordinate and which plans were high vs. low quality.
- Cited as a concrete example of Dan Shipper's prediction that AI-generated planning documents and writing will become normal, useful, and preferred over human-handwritten equivalents, as long as the responsible human stands behind the content.
- [[Matt MacInnis]] (CPO, [[Rippling]]) cites Notion's roughly four-year path to product-market fit — including a period the founders spent in Japan reworking their approach — as a rare, unrepeatable "narrative violation" ([[Jeff Lewis]]'s term): success driven by the specific idiosyncrasies of [[Ivan Zhao]] and the founding team's insistence on craftsmanship, not a playbook other founders should copy. MacInnis was an early investor in Notion.
