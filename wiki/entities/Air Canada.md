## Definition

Airline cited by [[Kiriti Badam]] as a cautionary real-world example of unconstrained agent autonomy: one of its chatbots hallucinated a refund policy that was not part of the company's actual playbook, and the company was legally held to honor it.

## Key Information

- Cited as motivation for the episode's staged-autonomy design philosophy (see [[Agency Control Trade-off]] and [[Continuous Calibration Continuous Development]]) — Badam pairs it with his own experience shutting down a customer-support product due to unmanageable hot-fixing as two examples of what happens when an AI system is given more decision-making latitude than it has earned.
