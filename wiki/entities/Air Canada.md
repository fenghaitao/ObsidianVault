## Definition

Air Canada is a Canadian airline that was cited as an example of an AI agent failure: one of their chatbots hallucinated a refund policy that was not in their actual playbook, and the company was legally forced to honor it.

## Key Information

- AI agent (chatbot) hallucinated a refund policy that was not part of the company's actual playbook
- Due to legal reasons, Air Canada was forced to honor the hallucinated policy
- This is cited as an example of why building AI products with high control and low agency initially is critical — to avoid dangerous autonomous decisions
- Part of a pattern of "really scary incidents" with AI agents making unauthorized decisions
