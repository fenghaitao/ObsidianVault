## Definition

Ian Banks (Iain M. Banks) is a Scottish science fiction author best known for "The Culture" series, which depicts an optimistic future with AI. Alexander Imbiricos recommends the series as a way to think about what kind of world AI can help usher in.

## Key Information

- Author of "The Culture" series, described as "relatively recent writing about a future with AI, but it's an optimistic future with AI"
- Alexander Imbiricos loves the series because most sci-fi is dystopian, but the Culture is a "gay space communist utopia" (as the Culture subreddit jokes)
- Imbiricos uses the Culture as a framework to think about "what kind of world can we usher in and what decisions can we make today to help usher in that world"
