## Definition

Steven Willmott is the CEO of SafeIntelligence and a co-author of the OpenAPI specification. He has a long background in computer science and API infrastructure.

## Key Information

- CEO of SafeIntelligence, a 3-year-old company specializing in formal verification for ML models and agent testing
- Co-author of the OpenAPI specification (formerly Swagger), a widely used standard for describing REST APIs
- Previous company worked in API infrastructure
- Presented at aiDotEngineer on spec-driven testing for AI agents
- Advocates for an open, version-controlled specification format for agent testing, analogous to OpenAPI for APIs
- Draws on decades of computer science experience to bridge formal verification and modern ML/LLM validation
