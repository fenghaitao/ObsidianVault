## Definition

Oliver Sacks was a scientist, neurologist, and author. He was the partner of Bill Hayes and the subject of the memoir Insomniac City, which chronicles his final days before dying of cancer.

## Key Information

- Scientist, neurologist, and author
- Partner of Bill Hayes
- Subject of Insomniac City, which covers his final days
- Died of cancer
