## Definition
Magnus Carlsen is widely considered the best chess player in the world and the founder of Play Magnus (Take Take Take), a chess application featuring an AI-powered game review coach.

## Key Information
- Founded Play Magnus, which built an AI chess coach that analyzes games and generates grounded commentary
- Commented on an LLM chess tournament from the Play Magnus office in Oslo, observing that LLMs "don't really know how to play chess"
- The LLM chess tournament was organized by Kaggle when they launched their game arena benchmark
- His company employs Anant Dole and Asbjorn Steinskog, who presented the chess coach system at AI Engineer conference
