## Definition

The Linux Foundation is the nonprofit software foundation that hosts the Cloud Native Computing Foundation, to which Kubernetes was donated.

## Key Information

- Kubernetes was donated to the CNCF under the Linux Foundation about a year after the project's creation, including logos, trademarks, and legal assets.
- Independent ownership was critical so partners could take a dependency without betting on a single company's roadmap.
- Burns notes the Linux Foundation can supply case law showing that open-source contribution liability worries don't hold water legally.
