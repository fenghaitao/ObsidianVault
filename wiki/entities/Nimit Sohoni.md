## Definition

Nimit Sohoni is a Stanford PhD, an AI researcher at the voice-AI company Cartesia, and a former quantitative researcher at Citadel Securities. (The transcript once renders his name as "Amit" in the episode's closing sign-off.)

## Key Information

- Completed his PhD at Stanford in Chris Ré's lab, where he did AI research for 4–5 years and knew the future Cartesia founders (including Albert Gu).
- Interned at Citadel Securities the summer before graduating and joined full-time as a quant, drawn by curiosity about a new problem domain and a career combining math and computation.
- After "a couple years" at Citadel, joined Cartesia as an early employee, motivated by returning to AI, personal/technical growth, and shaping a small startup; he noted he graduated "right before ChatGPT came out."
- Believes a PhD helps but is not required for either AI research or quant; the skill a PhD actually builds is "research taste" — finding the right problems.
- Argues quant can offer better work-life balance than AI research, and that finance is secretive because an alpha's value depends on not being shared.
- His advice: build deep technical fundamentals so opportunities come to you, and don't spread yourself too thin.
