## Definition

Texas grocery chain cited by [[Eric Ries]] in *Incorruptible* as a real-world "culture bank deposit" example: during an ice storm power outage, a store manager let customers take their groceries home for free because the point-of-sale system was down.

## Key Information

- When the power went out during an ice storm and the point-of-sale system stopped working, an H-E-B store manager let customers take their groceries home without charge.
- Ries uses this to illustrate that the manager's action wasn't isolated individual courage but the product of deliberate training: H-E-B teaches employees that doing the right thing in moments like this is "making a deposit in the culture bank," not an exceptional act of heroism.
