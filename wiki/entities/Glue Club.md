## Definition

Glue Club is a community for leaders operating in changing, growing companies, founded by Molly Graham. It provides support, sanity, and practical guidance for leaders navigating the "tornado" of scaling organizations.

## Key Information

- Founded and led by Molly Graham
- Website: glueclub.com
- Designed for leaders inside "crazy companies that are changing all the time"
- Graham says she gets energy from "unsticking people and helping people feel supported and seen and helping them grow"
- Focuses on the "real stuff" — practical leadership problems, not theoretical frameworks
- Graham also runs a Substack called "Lessons" as a parallel community
