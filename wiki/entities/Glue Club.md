## Definition

Glue Club (glueclub.com) is the community [[Molly Graham]] founded and runs for leaders operating inside fast-growing, rapidly changing companies who want to develop themselves as quickly as their companies are scaling.

## Key Information

- Represents the culmination of Graham's post-Facebook career pivot away from chasing C-suite titles ("LinkedIn crushes") toward what she describes discovering she actually loves: building safe spaces for leaders to learn, grow, and find "sanity and connection in a world that's kind of insane."
- Graham teaches several of her core frameworks inside Glue Club, including the "[[Waterline Model]]" and "[[Serve The Business Not The People]]."
- [[Claire Hughes Johnson]] gave a talk at Glue Club that included the "letter bomb" promises metaphor Graham has since adopted (see "[[Do Not Promise What You Cannot Control]]").
- Positioned by Graham as a resource especially relevant to leaders at fast-changing AI-era companies like [[OpenAI]].
