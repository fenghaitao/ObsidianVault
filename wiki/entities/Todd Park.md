## Definition

Founder of [[Devoted Health]], cited by [[Eric Ries]] as the originator (via [[Howard Schultz]]) of the "[[Culture Bank]]" rule: only make deposits, never intentionally make withdrawals, from an organization's trust/culture account.

## Key Information

- Described by Ries as "one of my favorite founders."
- Learned the "culture bank" framing from Howard Schultz, who built [[Starbucks]]; the rule states that principled sacrifices are "deposits" into an organization's trust, and greedy/self-interested shortcuts are "withdrawals" — the discipline is to only ever make deposits, and never intentionally make a withdrawal (accidental mistakes will happen, but should never be intentional).
- Ries reports that everyone who first hears the "Todd Park rule" says it sounds impossible, but frames it as genuinely achievable discipline rather than aspiration.
