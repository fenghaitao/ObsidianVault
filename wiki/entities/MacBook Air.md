## Definition

[[Apple]] laptop where [[Caitlin Kalinowski]] was technical lead, including the wedge-shaped mass-production version, during her 2007-2012 tenure at Apple.

## Key Information

- One of several Apple hardware projects (alongside [[MacBook Pro]] and Mac Pro) where Kalinowski credits Apple's culture of scrutinizing "why we're building it this way" for training a generation of hardware leaders now spread across the industry.
