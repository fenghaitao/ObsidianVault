## Definition
Trossen Robotics (transcribed "Trusson Robotics" in this transcript) is a maker of low-cost hobbyist robot arms used in robotics research.
## Key Information
- Its ~$7,000 arms formed the low-cost bimanual hardware for the ACT / ALOHA paper.
- The arms were set up in a leader-follower configuration for teleoperated demonstration collection.
- Representative of Levine's point that reliable low-cost hardware matters for a healthy robotics ecosystem, and that much of that hardware currently comes from China.
