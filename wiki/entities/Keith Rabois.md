## Definition

Keith Rabois is a managing director at Khosla Ventures, member of the PayPal Mafia, and former COO of Square. He is known for his frameworks on talent identification, team building, and operating discipline.

## Key Information

- Part of the PayPal Mafia where he was EVP of Business Development and Policy
- COO at Square (joined 2010, inspired by Jack Dorsey's iPad-only workflow)
- VP of Corporate Development at LinkedIn
- Early investor in Stripe, Palantir, Airbnb, YouTube, DoorDash, Ramp
- Has not used a computer since September 2010 -- runs everything from iPad/iPhone/Watch
- Known for "barrels and ammunition" framework for team building
- Believes high performance organizations don't have psychological safety -- they're about winning
- Philosophy: push harder when winning (offset complacency), be supportive when losing
- Key hiring advice: find undiscovered talent, do 20+ references, ask "If you were CEO, what would you have done differently?"
