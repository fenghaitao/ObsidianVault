## Definition
FGN (Functional Generative Network) is Google DeepMind's latest weather model that directly predicts cyclones — including categorization, trajectory, wind speed, and eye formation — within the network itself rather than requiring post-processing cyclone detection on top of general weather predictions.

## Key Information
- **Direct cyclone prediction**: Incorporates cyclone categorization, recognition, trajectory, wind speed, and eye formation directly into the network training
- **No post-processing**: Unlike predecessors that required separate cyclone detectors as post-processing steps
- **Adoption**: Already used by the US National Hurricane Center, with expected worldwide use in coming years
- **Advantage**: Much better cyclone prediction than general weather models with bolt-on detection
- **Lineage**: Successor to GraphCast and GenCast in DeepMind's weather prediction series
