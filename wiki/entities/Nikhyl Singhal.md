## Definition

Long-time product executive at [[Meta]] and [[Google]], former CPO at [[Credit Karma]], four-time founder, and founder of the [[Skip]] community for heads of product/CPOs and its associated podcast/newsletter. A repeat [[Lenny's Podcast]] guest (his first appearance was one of the show's first 20-30 episodes), regarded by Lenny as a top source of blunt, non-sugarcoated product-career advice.

## Key Information

- Central argument: product management is bifurcating into "builders" (who thrive, since AI removes the tedious information-moving parts of the job and lets them ship directly) and "information movers" (whose core skill — moving information between organizational layers — is being obsoleted). See [[Builders Vs Information Movers]].
- Predicts (12-24 months) mass "shedding and rehiring": companies laying off large numbers of legacy staff while hiring smaller, AI-first replacement cohorts — see [[Shed And Rehire]].
- Argues the industry currently has the most open PM roles globally in 3+ years, contradicting the "AI kills the PM role" narrative — because judgment (deciding what's worth building, evaluating whether a change is good) becomes more valuable, not less, as the cost/pace of building explodes.
- Names "[[Crossing The Threshold]]" as the number-one piece of career advice: finding the courage to reinvent one's working style despite the exhaustion and identity-loss that entails.
- Describes the "[[Shadow Superpower]]" paradox: people who most thoroughly mastered the old way of working are often the most resistant to recognizing that a new way is needed, since their success reinforces exactly the instincts that no longer apply.
- Argues personal brand/pedigree (e.g., "I worked at a top company for years") is losing career signal value compared to demonstrated current-tool fluency — see [[Modernity Over Brand]].
- Warns that the current wave will set back workplace diversity and geographic breadth, since the pace of change favors people who can spend nights/weekends on AI tools, disadvantaging (for example) parents in demanding caregiving years.
- His own tool stack: mostly Claude over the last 3 months (previously heavy Codex use for high-reasoning tasks); builds internal tools for his Skip community (member-matching agents, job-board automation, content-analysis agents) and habitually "vibe codes" while watching plot-driven TV.
- Names the "Skip" career philosophy (source of his community's name): optimize not for your next job, but for the job after that ("the skip").
- Lightning round: recommends the novel *James*; enjoys *Paradise* and *Lioness*; daily Tesla full-self-driving user (noticed it eliminated driving anxiety he didn't know he had); life motto is Einstein's "genius is 1% inspiration, 99% perspiration" (reinterpreted for the AI era as: AI now supplies the perspiration, so human inspiration/judgment is what remains scarce).
