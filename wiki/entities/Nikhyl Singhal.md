## Definition

Nikhyl Singhal is a former Meta and Google executive, four-time founder, and CPO at Credit Karma. He founded the Skip community for heads of product and CPOs, and is widely regarded as the top source of career advice for product managers.

## Key Information

- Long-time executive at Meta and Google
- CPO at Credit Karma
- Four-time founder
- Founded the Skip community (CPOs/heads of product) and The Skip Coach (broader tech professionals)
- 30 years of building consumer products at scale
- Key thesis: "information mover" PMs are becoming dinosaurs; builders are in a renaissance
- Predicts massive shedding and rehiring in next 12-24 months as companies go AI-first
- Argues that "judgment" is the paramount remaining skill for PMs
