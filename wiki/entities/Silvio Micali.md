## Definition

Silvio Micali is a cryptographer, co-author of the Goldwasser–Micali–Rackoff paper on interactive/zero-knowledge proofs and of the GMW zero-knowledge result with Oded Goldreich and Avi Wigderson.

## Key Information

- Co-authored the paper defining interactive proofs and zero-knowledge proofs.
- Co-authored (with Goldreich and Wigderson) the paper showing every provable statement has a zero-knowledge interactive proof.
- With Manuel Blum, wrote the fundamental paper that treats randomness as a function of the observer's computational power (the coin-toss thought experiment).
