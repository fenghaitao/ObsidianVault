## Definition

Dr. Becky Kennedy is a clinical psychologist, author, and CEO of Good Inside, one of the most popular parenting books, podcasts, communities, and apps. She is known for translating child psychology principles into practical, actionable frameworks for parents and leaders.

## Key Information

- Clinical psychologist by training, initially trained in reward/punishment, timeout, sticker chart models
- Founded Good Inside after realizing the methods she was teaching parents were "at complete odds with everything I knew was helping all the adults in my practice change their lives"
- Posted her first Instagram post on February 28, 2020, two weeks before NYC shut down for COVID
- Her company Good Inside has ~65 employees and is profitable
- Has three children, one of whom she describes as a "deeply feeling kid" (0% people-pleasing)
- Second children's book "Leave Me Alone" (February 2026) focuses on deeply feeling kids
- Presents herself as a reluctant self-promoter: describes her initial Instagram posts as "vomiting them out" of compulsion rather than strategic intent
- Uses AI tools including Figma Make, Replit, Lovable, Claude, and ChatGPT to prototype ideas
- Describes herself as "allergic to ideas without action" — always wants to combine insight with practical action
- Believes parenting is "the last area in life that we glorify instinct alone"
- Considers the idea of "maternal instinct" as a form of gaslighting parents
- Believes what feels "natural" in parenting is simply how we were parented
- On Lenny's Podcast: "You're like a major celebrity to me and my whole team knows it"
- Recommends books: The Power of Moments, Messy Middle, Creativity Inc.
- Life motto: "This feels hard because it is hard, not because I'm doing something wrong"
- Surprised that "a company is just people" and that therapy skills translate to company leadership
