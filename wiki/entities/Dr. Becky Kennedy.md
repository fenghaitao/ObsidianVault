## Definition

Clinical psychologist, author, and CEO/co-founder of [[Good Inside]] — a parenting philosophy, book, podcast, app, and community. Guest on [[Lenny's Podcast]] episode 28, where she argues the principles she teaches for raising resilient kids apply directly to leading adults at work.

## Key Information

- Trained initially in a reward/punishment/timeout/sticker-chart clinical model, which she says worked cleanly on paper but contradicted what actually helped the adults in her private practice change their lives — the gap between the two is what led her to develop the "good inside" approach.
- Core thesis: all humans (age one to eighty-five) need the same things; when needs aren't met, people express themselves in ineffective ways — bad behavior at any age is "feelings that overpower skills," not a moral failing. See [[Feelings Overpower Skills]].
- Frameworks she teaches, each translated from parenting into workplace leadership in this episode: [[Power Of Repair]], [[Connecting Before Correcting]], [[Behavior Vs Identity]] ("good inside"), [[Most Generous Interpretation]] (MGI), [[Sturdy Leadership]], [[Boundaries Vs Requests]], and [[Resilience Over Happiness]].
- Describes two temperament patterns she sees recur in both kids and adult coworkers: [[Resilient Rebels]] (core fear = loss of control) and [[Deeply Feeling Kids]] (core fear = being "too much").
- Posted her first Instagram post on February 28, 2020 (about two weeks before New York City's COVID shutdown) without a business plan in mind — says it felt like "vomiting out" ideas she was compelled by; the community that formed around it, not the content itself, is what she says she'd bet on if she had to choose one.
- Runs Good Inside with a co-founder (a fellow psychologist she's known for years); the company has ~65 employees, is profitable, and raised outside funding "a couple years ago" toward a stated ambition to build a broader "Disney-type ecosystem" around Good Inside's IP, stories, and characters in 2026–2027.
- Her second children's book, *Leave Me Alone: A Good Inside Story About Deeply Feeling Kids*, is available for pre-order and scheduled for February 2026 release; centers on kids whose deepest fear is being "too much," who push away the people they need most in the exact moments they need them.
- Describes her personal AI workflow: she uses Figma Make (and has team members show her [[Replit]] and [[Lovable]]) to turn ideas into full visual prototypes (UX flow, tone, copy) rather than pitching verbally, because "I don't like to have an idea I can't fully see out." She frames effective prompting as "vomiting" unstructured thoughts rather than pre-organizing them, and says she models this live for her team using tools like [[ChatGPT]] and [[Claude]].
- Product/company built around a "drip" model: users share what's going on, get 5-minute insight-plus-action content, access an AI chatbot named "Dr. GG," and can join live coaching/community events (2–3 per month) with trained coaches.
- Credits her understanding-people skills from clinical practice as a directly transferable leadership skill as a first-time company CEO: "a company is just people."
- Lightning-round recommendations: books *The Power of Moments*, *The Messy Middle*, *Creativity, Inc.*; TV/movies *Secrets We Keep* and *K-pop Demon Hunters*; product discovery: Liberty puzzles (thick hand-cut wooden puzzles); life motto: "this feels hard because it is hard."
