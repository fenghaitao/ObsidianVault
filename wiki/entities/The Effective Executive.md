## Definition

*The Effective Executive* is a classic 1960s management book, recommended by [[Matt MacInnis]] in the episode's lightning round.

## Key Information

- MacInnis notes it's dated in some respects (era-specific gendered pronouns for "the secretary" and "the executive") but says it's full of simple, enduring advice on leading teams that has stayed in print for 70 years.
