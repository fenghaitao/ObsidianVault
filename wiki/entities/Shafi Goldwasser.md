## Definition

Shafi Goldwasser is a cryptographer and co-author of the Goldwasser–Micali–Rackoff paper that defined interactive proofs and zero-knowledge proofs.

## Key Information

- The original zero-knowledge definition "came in a paper... of Goldster Micali and Rakov," which also contained the definition of interactive proofs.
- The key conceptual leap: proofs don't have to be written down like mathematical papers — a prover and randomized verifier can have a discussion to convince the verifier of a claim.
