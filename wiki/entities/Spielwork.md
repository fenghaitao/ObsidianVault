## Definition
Spielwork is a mobile app for vibe coding games with a TikTok-style swiping interface, representing one of the early experiments in consumer vibe coding.

## Key Information
- Mobile app focused on vibe coding games
- Uses TikTok-style swiping as the interaction pattern for browsing/discovering games
- Cited by Joe Reeve as one of the few examples of people "doing consumer vibe coding well"
- Part of the emerging consumer vibe coding space alongside a London-based game vibe coding tool
- Represents the attempt to bring vibe coding to mobile-first, non-developer audiences
- Joe Reeve noted that games may not be the ideal vehicle for consumer vibe coding due to their complexity
