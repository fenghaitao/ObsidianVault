## Definition

Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) is a leading venture capital firm co-founded by Marc Andreessen and Ben Horowitz, investing across stages from seed to growth in technology companies.

## Key Information

- Co-founded by Marc Andreessen
- Py McCormack wrote what Marc describes as "the best piece ever written about us" — the best explanation of what a16z does and how they think
- Has a YouTube channel (youtube.com/@a16z) that Marc says has "a lot of great stuff" and will be "very exciting in the next year"
- Putting significant effort into video content and content production with a growing team
- [[Qasar Younis]] credits [[Marc Andreessen]] as a mentor and heavy influence — Marc pushed him to go online and join Twitter/X
- a16z was one of the pioneers of individual VC brand-building, transforming venture capital from anonymous PE-style firms to personality-driven firms
