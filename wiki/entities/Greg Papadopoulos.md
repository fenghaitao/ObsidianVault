## Definition

Greg Papadopoulos was Sun Microsystems' CTO, and the executive who chartered the Fishworks group and presented Bryan Cantrill's Distinguished Engineer case.

## Key Information

- Chartered the Fishworks group in San Francisco that built Sun's new storage product.
- Presented Cantrill's DE case to the committee, reportedly saying he expected no one to vote against it; the vote was unanimous.
- Cantrill is "very grateful to Greg" because Papadopoulos handled a political process Cantrill wanted nothing to do with.
