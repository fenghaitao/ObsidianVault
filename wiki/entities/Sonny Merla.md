## Definition
Sonny Merla is the Global Data Science and AI Manager at Amplifon, the world leader in hearing care solutions. He presented Amplifon's enterprise-grade registry system for MCP and A2A agents at the aiDotEngineer conference.

## Key Information
- **Role**: Global Data Science and AI Manager at [[Amplifon]]
- **Presentation**: "One Registry to Rule them All" at [[aiDotEngineer]], co-presented with Mauro Luchetti and Mattia Redaelli of [[Quantyca]]
- **Key Topics**: Amplifon's Amplify program, AI governance at enterprise scale, registry system for MCP and A2A agents, operating model (Control Tower + Committee)
- **Perspective**: Focused on the business and organizational challenges of scaling AI adoption across a global enterprise with dozens of teams on three continents
