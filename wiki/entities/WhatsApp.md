## Definition

WhatsApp is a messaging platform acquired by Facebook (Meta) for $19 billion in 2014. Rahul referenced it as the quintessential example of a blockbuster acquisition, contrasting it with the much smaller acqui-hire of his own startup Cos by Pinterest.

## Key Information

- Acquired by Facebook for $19 billion in 2014
- Rahul used this as an example of the most famous type of acquisition, where "everyone became filthy rich"
- Contrasted with the other end of the spectrum: startups that effectively failed but call it an acquisition to save dignity
