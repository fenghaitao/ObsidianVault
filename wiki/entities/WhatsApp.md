## Definition
WhatsApp is a messaging platform used as an initial chat interface for personal AI agents. Radek Sienkiewicz started his OpenClaw journey with WhatsApp as his first single-channel integration before migrating to Telegram and then Discord.

## Key Information
- Radek Sienkiewicz's first OpenClaw integration: "just WhatsApp, just one ability to do to just like chat"
- Served as the starting point for his incremental adoption approach — one simple channel, one simple workflow
- Later migrated to Telegram, then Discord as his needs grew and he wanted more organizational capabilities
- Represents the simplest starting point: a single messaging channel with no complex setup
