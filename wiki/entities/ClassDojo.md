## Definition

ClassDojo is an education platform serving 45 million users across 90% of US elementary schools, using Claude to power its Sidekick AI teaching assistant.

## Key Information

- Sidekick AI compresses admin work that used to take teachers 20-30 minutes per class roster down to seconds.
