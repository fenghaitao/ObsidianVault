## Definition
Todd Lipkin is a mentor of Vlad Feinberg's who first got him into computer science and exemplifies being "the kind of person other people want to see succeed."
## Key Information
- Vlad describes him as a "very dear friend and mentor" who first got him into computer science.
- He models kindness, teachability, and success-through-helping-others, which Vlad says stands in contrast to cynical or Machiavellian workplace behavior.
- Vlad's advice to his younger self is essentially to be like Lipkin: help people succeed and design projects that let others' complementary skills shine.
