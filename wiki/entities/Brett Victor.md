## Definition

Brett Victor (referred to as "Bret Victor" in the transcript) is the designer/researcher whose talk "Stop Drawing Dead Fish" is cited by [[Max Schoening]] as the direct inspiration for [[Notion]]'s shift from designing chat interfaces as static [[Figma]] mockups to prototyping them in a small, LLM-friendly codebase.

## Key Information

- Schoening's framing of the talk's relevance: a static image of a chat interface is essentially a "dead fish" — you have to "feel" the AI/dynamic behavior to actually understand and design it, which a static mockup can't convey.
- This insight directly led Schoening and another designer to build Notion's first "playground" codebase for prototyping chat UIs — the origin of designers and PMs at Notion learning to prototype (and later ship) in code.
