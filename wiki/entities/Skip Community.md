## Definition

The Skip Community is a curated group of ~125 heads of product and CPOs created by Nikhyl Singhal. Members gather monthly in San Francisco to share best practices, discuss what's changing in product management, and collectively reinvent the craft. The community grew from ~20-30 members five years ago to over 125 today.

## Key Information

- Founded by Nikhyl Singhal about 5 years ago
- ~125 heads of product and CPOs as members
- Monthly meetups in San Francisco
- In the first 2-3 years, there was only 1 founder among members; now there are 14 founders
- Members share what they're building internally — show-and-tell format at meetups
- Community members are building chief of staff apps, agent systems, and productivity tools for their organizations
- Tightly curated with an "infinite wait list" — Nikhyl grows it slowly to maintain trust
- One member interviewed for a CHRO (Chief HR Officer) position because the company wanted a product manager background
- Related properties: Skip Coach (broader mailing list), Skip Show (podcast), Skip Help (AI agents)
- Skip Help: AI agents trained on ~50 community leaders to answer career questions, powered by writ (super me)
