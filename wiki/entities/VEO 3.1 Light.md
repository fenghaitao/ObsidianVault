## Definition
VEO 3.1 Light is Google DeepMind's video generation model that creates stock footage with audio at 720p resolution. It is a paid-tier model requiring an API key and supports configurable duration and aspect ratios.

## Key Information
- Video generation model from Google DeepMind with audio output
- Output resolution: 720p (not 4K, which requires higher-tier models)
- Configurable aspect ratios: 16:9 (landscape) or 9:16 (mobile/portrait)
- Configurable video duration (4, 6, or 8 seconds)
- Paid-tier model requiring API key attachment in AI Studio
- Demonstrated generating vegan basketball-themed food truck stock footage
- Part of the VEO video generation model series
