## Definition

Google Chrome is Google's web browser, launched in 2008 and built on the open-source Chromium engine. It displaced Firefox's market share through heavy distribution spending and architectural advances.

## Key Information

- Launched with V8's method JIT, a multiprocess architecture, a robust plugin model, and early native video playback — structural advantages that created real gaps Firefox had to close
- Google spent hundreds of millions (reaching into the billions when counting its own homepage ads) on Chrome distribution, targeted at IE but with Firefox as "collateral damage"
- Bobby Holley describes a "year of 100 oopses": Google repeatedly framed actions that hurt Firefox (like telling Firefox users to "Download Chrome") as unintentional
- Today most "AI browsers" (Edge, Opera, Perplexity) are Chromium frontends rather than independent engines; Chrome is aggressively integrating Gemini and pushing proprietary on-device LLM APIs
