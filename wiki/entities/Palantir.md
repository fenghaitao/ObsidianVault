## Definition

Palantir is described by Jason Lemkin as "the single most successful public B2B company at the moment." It pioneered the concept of forward-deployed engineers — embedding technical teams on-site with customers for months to ensure software deployment success.

## Key Information

- "The single most successful public B2B company at the moment" (as of the recording)
- Marc Benioff (Salesforce CEO) envies two things about Palantir: (1) high deal sizes, and (2) the ability to get every customer live before they pay
- Pioneered the forward-deployed engineer model — embedding teams on-site for ~6 months to get software working
- Palantir's FDE model involves nine-figure deals, which is different from what most startups need — but the concept of ensuring deployment success before payment is transferable
- The "go live before you pay" model is a dramatic shift from traditional enterprise software where customers pay first and deployment takes years
