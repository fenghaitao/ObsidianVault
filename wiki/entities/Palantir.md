## Definition

Palantir is a data analytics and software company known for its work with government and defense agencies. It was considered a "S-tier" company for tech recruiting at Cornell and UCLA around 2017.

## Key Information

- Palantir was considered an "S-tier" company to work for around 2017, especially at Cornell and UCLA
- Evan King interviewed at Palantir and wanted the job "more than anything" — it was the company that was "so cool"
- Evan was convinced he crushed his Palantir onsite interview and was in the first group of names called — which he interpreted as being hired (the company said if your name is called early, either you crushed it and are hired, or you failed)
- He waited two weeks and was told he didn't get the job — he was "so heartbroken"
- The experience was a major disappointment that "shook him up" and he still remembers it to this day
- Evan noted that Palantir is no longer considered as prestigious as it was in 2017
