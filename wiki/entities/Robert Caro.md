## Definition

Robert Caro is a journalist and biographer, best known for The Power Broker, his 1,100-page biography of Robert Moses. Jenny Wen highly recommends the book.

## Key Information

- Author of The Power Broker, a biography of Robert Moses
- Known for exhaustive, deeply researched biographies
- The Power Broker is approximately 1,100 pages
