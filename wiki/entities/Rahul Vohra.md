## Definition

Founder of [[Superhuman]] and a prior [[Lenny's Podcast]] guest, referenced by [[Lenny Rachitsky]] as having gone through a transition where VC/leadership pressure pushed him away from hands-on product work, before he found his way back to doing the work he loved.

## Key Information

- Raised by Lenny as a comparison point when asking [[Evan Spiegel]] whether he experienced a similar CEO-role transition; Spiegel says his own experience is only partly similar — he actively enjoys the CEO role's strategic/leadership responsibilities, not just the product work.
- Per [[Lenny Rachitsky]] (episode 43): cited alongside [[Dharmesh Shah]] as an example of deliberate "[[Zone Of Genius]]" career design — Vohra hired a president to take on operational/execution work he wasn't well-suited for.
