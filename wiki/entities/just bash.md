## Definition

just bash is a bash interpreter written in TypeScript by Malte Ubl (CTO of Vercel) that provides a sandbox with sub-nanosecond startup time for AI agents. It is part of his "vibe coding stack."

## Key Information

- Creator: Malte Ubl, CTO of Vercel
- Written in TypeScript
- Provides a sandbox with sub-nanosecond startup time
- Designed for AI agents that "love bash"
- Part of Malte's "vibe coding stack" alongside chat SDK
- Addresses the broader trend of giving agents a computer without giving them a computer
