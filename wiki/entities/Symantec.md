## Definition

Symantec is a cybersecurity and software company (home of Norton antivirus) where Carey Nachenberg spent ~24 years and became its senior-most engineer (Fellow).

## Key Information

- Acquired the Peter Norton Group (Norton Utilities, Norton Commander) and rebranded an acquired antivirus product as Norton Antivirus
- In Carey's early years it had little formal engineering culture ("the wild west"): no agile process and little unit/integration testing
- Career ladder roughly tracked Google/Meta levels (junior → senior → staff → distinguished engineer → fellow)
- Distinguished engineer and fellow promotions were decided quarterly by a CTO-led committee of technologists, to keep the bar consistent company-wide
- Carey became Fellow without applying — after the Veritas merger leveled in a "fellow" role, he was nominated without his knowledge
