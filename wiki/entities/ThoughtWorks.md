## Definition

ThoughtWorks is a global IT consultancy that hosted an offsite with engineering VPs from various companies to discuss AI's impact on software engineering. They identified the "mid-level engineer squeeze" — AI most benefits senior engineers (amplifies expertise) and junior engineers (accelerates onboarding), but mid-career engineers are in the most trouble.

## Key Information

- Hosted an offsite about a month before the podcast recording (mid-2026)
- Brought together engineering VPs from different companies to discuss AI's impact
- Key finding: AI is really good for experienced engineers (amplifies skills) and new engineers (solves onboarding), but mid-career engineers face the most disruption
- Cloudflare and Shopify both hired ~1,000 interns in 2025 because AI reduces onboarding costs from a month to about a week
