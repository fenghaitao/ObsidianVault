## Definition

Hacker News (HN) is a tech-focused social news aggregator run by Y Combinator. Simon Wilson uses it as a barometer for regional attitudes toward AI, noting that European commenters (midnight to 8am Pacific time) are more AI-skeptic than Americans.

## Key Information

- Run by Y Combinator
- Simon Wilson: "I spent way too much time on Hacker News"
- Regional differences visible in comment patterns: "a conversation that starts at midnight Pacific time and goes until 8:00 a.m. has a very different tone because it's the Europeans"
- Europeans are "a lot more AI skeptic than the Americans are generally"
