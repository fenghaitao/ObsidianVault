## Definition

Noam Chomsky is a renowned linguist and cognitive scientist at MIT. Edwin Chen cites Chomsky's presence at MIT as one of the reasons he chose to attend the university.

## Key Information

- Edwin went to MIT "because it's obviously one of the best places for math and CS, but also because it's the home of Chomsky"
- This reflects Edwin's deep interest in the intersection of linguistics, mathematics, and computer science
