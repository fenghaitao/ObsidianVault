## Definition
Apple is the technology company behind iPhone, iOS, Siri, and Apple Silicon (M-series chips). Its hardware strategy with powerful on-device compute (GPU, Neural Engine) enables local AI via frameworks like MLX and Core ML. Kitze predicts Apple may win the AI agent race because local models are getting "insanely good."

## Key Information
- Creator of Apple Silicon: M1 through M5 series chips with powerful GPUs and Neural Engine
- MLX uses the GPU for on-device AI inference, not the Neural Engine
- Core ML is needed to access the Neural Engine, but has poor developer experience due to private API limitations
- Apple may be merging Neural Engine components into the GPU (hints in M5 series)
- WWDC expected to potentially address Core ML API issues and Neural Engine accessibility
- Kitze's prediction: "it would be the funniest thing if Apple wins all of this"
- Local models getting "insanely good" and will get better
- Most normies will be fine with local Siri agent with tool capabilities from installed apps
- Advantages: no wasting credits, data doesn't go anywhere, phone magically does things
- iOS limitations for agents: "it can maybe send you a push notification and if Tim Cook allows" — Kitze switched to Android for agent control
