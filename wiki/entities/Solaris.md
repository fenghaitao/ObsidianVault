## Definition

Solaris is Sun Microsystems' Unix-based operating system, where Bryan Cantrill spent his early Sun years in kernel development, and one of the seminal technologies that came out of Sun.

## Key Information

- Cantrill worked in Solaris kernel development at Sun beginning in 1996, in what he calls the moment when every other computer company was "mortgaging its future to Microsoft and Windows."
- By early 1996 Sun was, in his telling, the only company truly invested in its own operating system, during "Unix's darkest hour."
- Solaris was among the seminal technologies (alongside Spark, Java, and others) that Cantrill says emerged from Sun's passion and independence.
