## Definition
Yuval Noah Harari is a historian and author known for his books on human history and the future, including Sapiens, Homo Deus, and Nexus. His concept of AI as "alien intelligence" rather than "artificial intelligence" was referenced by Daniel Szoke in arguing for deterministic guardrails in AI-assisted coding.

## Key Information
- Historian and author of Sapiens, Homo Deus, and Nexus
- In Nexus, argues that LLMs represent the first time a non-human entity can produce human language
- Proposes calling AI "alien intelligence" instead of "artificial intelligence" because AI thinks fundamentally differently from humans
- His framing was used by Szoke to argue that AI failure modes may be unpredictable to human intuition
- This unpredictability makes deterministic safety nets (like Rust's compiler) essential for AI-generated code
