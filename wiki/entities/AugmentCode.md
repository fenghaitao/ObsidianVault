## Definition

Augment Code is an AI coding tool built on Claude via Google Cloud's [[VertexAI]], cited as a customer example of agentic coding's development-speed gains.

## Key Information

- Documented an enterprise customer completing a project in two weeks that their CTO estimated would take four to eight months with traditional development.
- Guy Gur-Ari, Chief Scientist: "Tasks that would take weeks for a developer to learn can now be completed in a day or two." Also noted that [[Claude4.5Sonnet|Claude Sonnet 4.5]]'s intelligence "makes better use of Augment's codebase context, handles longer-horizon tasks, and opens up new agentic possibilities."
