## Definition

Ubiquiti is a home networking and security camera company praised by Sherwin Wu as "the Apple of home networking" for its beautiful hardware, excellent software, and great mobile app experience.

## Key Information

- Home networking products: wireless routers requiring Ethernet wiring throughout the house
- Security cameras that integrate into the Ubiquiti ecosystem with an "incredible" mobile app, Apple TV app, and iPad app for live feeds
- Described as "the Apple of home networking" — beautiful products with excellent software
- "A little pricey, but not that pricey"
- Sherwin Wu fully converted from Eero to Ubiquiti
