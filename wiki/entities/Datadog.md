## Definition

Datadog is an observability and monitoring platform that also houses EPO, a leading experimentation and feature flagging platform. It was a sponsor of this episode.

## Key Information

- Observability and monitoring platform
- Home to EPO, the leading experimentation and feature flagging platform
- EPO was built by the same team that built experimentation at Airbnb
- Product analytics: PMs can watch replays, review funnels, dive into retention, and explore growth metrics
- Goes beyond numbers with session replay, heat maps, and scroll maps
- Feature flags are tied to real-time data for safe rollouts and precise targeting
- Sponsor of Lenny's Podcast episode 26
- Website: datadog.com/lenny
