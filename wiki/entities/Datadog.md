## Definition

Datadog is an observability/monitoring company whose Senior Product Manager, Kai Xin Tai, described AI-native PM practices used in building its Bits AI SRE agent.

## Key Information

- Kai Xin Tai, Senior Product Manager: studies the Bits AI SRE agent's strengths and failure modes through offline evaluation on real-world production incidents, and designs tight feedback loops that refine the UX to surface where the agent struggles.
- Quote: "a PM's craft has shifted from defining certainty upfront to accelerating discovery."
