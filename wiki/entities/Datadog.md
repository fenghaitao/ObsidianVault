## Definition
Datadog is an observability and monitoring platform company that invested in Arize's Series C funding round, alongside Microsoft.

## Key Information
- Investor in Arize's Series C round.
- Datadog provides traditional software observability (logs, traces, metrics) while Arize focuses on AI-specific observability and evaluation.
- Aman Khan noted that Datadog traces are not contextually aware of human/AI interaction patterns, which is a gap Arize fills.
- Braintrust is a self-described happy Datadog user for traditional website uptime and error monitoring, even though Braintrust itself is an agent observability platform — demonstrating that traditional and agent observability are complementary, not competing.
