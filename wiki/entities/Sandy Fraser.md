## Definition
Sandy Fraser was Bjarne Stroustrup's boss at Bell Labs, working in networking.

## Key Information
- Told Bjarne on his visit that he'd "come at the wrong time" (no jobs open), then changed his mind after Bjarne gave a talk to a development group the next day.
- A "great guy" working with networking.
- Hired Bjarne into the research group, where he stayed "for the next couple of decades."
- Part of the informal Bell Labs hiring approach: talk to people, then recommend the good ones to the director.
