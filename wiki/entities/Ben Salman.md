## Definition

Ben Salman is a data scientist who co-built "Project Rosalind" with [[Jeanne DeWitt Grosser]] at [[Stripe]] around 2017 — an early, largely unsuccessful attempt at AI-powered personalized outbound sales email.

## Key Information

- Later worked at ZoomInfo, then founded his own AI-native go-to-market startup, productizing the "company universe" concept from Project Rosalind with modern AI layered on top.
- His stated long-term thesis: AI will eventually make outbound prospecting largely unnecessary by directly matching companies to relevant products/vendors.
- Grosser notes with amusement that several of her Project Rosalind collaborators now work at OpenAI and Anthropic doing GTM work, while Salman independently started an AI-native GTM company and she is separately rebuilding a similar concept at [[Vercel]].
