## Definition
Richard Hamming was a mathematician and computer scientist known for his famous talk "You and Your Research," in which he posed the question: "What's the most important problem in your field, and why aren't you working on it?"

## Key Information
- Author of the influential talk "You and Your Research"
- His framing question was used by Jed Borovik to open the 2025 AI Engineering Code Summit
- The question challenges researchers and engineers to focus on the highest-impact problems in their field
- Borovik applied this framing to argue that AI is the most important problem in technology and code is the most important problem in applied AI
