## Definition

Dan Shapiro authored the blog post that introduced the five-levels-of-AI-coding-autonomy framework ([[AICodingAutonomyLevels]]) — spicy autocomplete → coding intern → junior developer → developer → engineering team → dark factory — which [[ColeMedin]] adopts and maps onto his own [[HarnessEngineering]] system in a dedicated video.

## Key Information

- Framed the levels using the analogy of the 2013 SAE five-levels-of-vehicle-automation scale, mapping degrees of driving automation onto degrees of AI-coding delegation.
