## Definition
Dan Shapiro is an author who published a blog post (January/February 2026) describing six levels of AI coding autonomy, from spicy autocomplete to the "dark factory."

## Key Information
- Published a framework of six stages of autonomy for automating software
- The levels range from Level 1 (spicy autocomplete) through Level 6 (dark factory / software factory)
- Eric Zakariasson used this framework as the foundation for his presentation on building a software factory
- The dark factory concept describes a black box where agents autonomously ship, test, and build code
