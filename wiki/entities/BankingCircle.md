## Definition
Banking Circle is a global cross-border payments provider that offers banking services on top of accounts and liquidity management. It is a fintech company processing over 1 trillion euro per year and serving over 700 regulated financial institutions.

## Key Information
- Global cross-border payments provider
- Processes over 1 trillion euro per year
- Serves over 700 regulated financial institutions
- Over 250 people building technical systems including client APIs, core banking systems, internal tools, data science/engineering, and clearing scheme integrations
- Established a platform engineering team to build an internal developer platform (Atlas) that abstracts away infrastructure and cloud complexity
- Atlas includes sub-platforms for compute (Kubernetes), infrastructure (blob storage, databases, secrets), messaging, and observability
- Fintech operating in a regulated environment where reliability is a primary concern
