## Definition
YouTube is a video sharing platform. Referenced in the Demand-Driven Context workshop as a source of rapidly evolving AI content — "by tomorrow morning on YouTube, somebody would have already posted something differently, better than me."

## Key Information
- Used as a metaphor for the rapid pace of AI innovation and content creation
- In the era of AI, no single approach or thesis remains unchallenged for long
- The aiDotEngineer channel hosts workshop and presentation content on YouTube
