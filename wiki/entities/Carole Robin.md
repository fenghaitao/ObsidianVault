## Definition

Carole Robin is a former Stanford Business School professor known for creating the popular "Touchy Feely" (Interpersonal Dynamics) course. She was a guest on Lenny's Podcast. She created a movement around interpersonal skills, particularly for founders.

## Key Information

- Taught the "Touchy Feely" course at Stanford Business School (Interpersonal Dynamics)
- Was a guest on Lenny's Podcast
- Created a movement around interpersonal skills, especially for founders
- Her framework uses the concept of "the net" — you can talk about your feelings and reactions on your side of the net, but as soon as you cross to blaming or making assumptions, the other person becomes defensive
- The concept aligns with Non-Violent Communication principles
