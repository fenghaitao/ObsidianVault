## Definition

Carole Robin is associated with Stanford Business School's "Touchy Feely" interpersonal-dynamics course and a prior [[Lenny's Podcast]] guest, cited by [[Rachel Lockett]] as a parallel influence to her "[[Non-Violent Communication (NVC)]]" framework.

## Key Information

- The course/model Lockett references teaches a "net" metaphor: you can be bold and honest about your own feelings and reactions on your side of the net, but as soon as you cross it into blaming or assuming things about the other person, they become defensive.
- Many founders reportedly take Robin's model to heart, per Lockett.
