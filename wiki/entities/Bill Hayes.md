## Definition

Bill Hayes is an author and memoirist, best known for Insomniac City, a memoir about his partner Oliver Sacks' final days. He was Sacks' partner when Sacks died of cancer.

## Key Information

- Author of Insomniac City
- Partner of Oliver Sacks during Sacks' final days
- Wrote a memoir about their love story and Sacks' last days
