## Definition

MATS ("ML Alignment & Theory Scholars," per [[Sander Schulhoff]] rendered informally as "ML alignment and theorem/theory scholars") is an AI-safety research-mentorship program that Schulhoff has been involved in running.

## Key Information

- Hosts researchers working on a range of AI safety/security topics beyond core alignment, including sabotage, eval awareness, sandbagging, and — the subfield Schulhoff highlights as most relevant to AI guardrails/security — "control": the study of how to safely operate and constrain a potentially malicious or misaligned AI system, framed by Schulhoff as "given a malicious AI, how do you keep it from hurting you."
