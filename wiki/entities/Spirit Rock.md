## Definition

Spirit Rock is a meditation retreat center. Both Amol Avaisary and Lenny Rachitsky have done meditation retreats there. Amol goes at least once a year and credits meditation with helping him manage the intensity of his role at Anthropic.

## Key Information

- Meditation retreat center
- Amol Avaisary does a retreat there at least once a year
- Lenny Rachitsky has also done a retreat at Spirit Rock
- Amol credits deep meditation with helping him maintain a "level head" and manage the relationship between awareness and reality
- "That relationship between awareness and reality, that's where you have choice"
