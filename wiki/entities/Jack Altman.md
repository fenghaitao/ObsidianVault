## Definition

Jack Altman is the co-founder and CEO of Lattice. Keith Rabois once asked him how many barrels are at Lattice, and the answer was two — a more common answer than PayPal's 12-17.

## Key Information

- Co-founder and CEO of [[Lattice]]
- Brother of [[Sam Altman]]
- When [[Keith Rabois]] asked him on a podcast how many barrels are at Lattice, the answer was two
- This is described as a more common answer for a "very good company" — compared to PayPal's unusually high 12-17 barrels
