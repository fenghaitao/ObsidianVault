## Definition

Johan Håstad is a theoretical computer scientist whose significant strengthening of the PCP theorem proved tight hardness of approximation results.

## Key Information

- Wigderson credits Håstad ("hustad") with the strengthening that, for constraints on three variables, satisfying 7/8 + ε of the constraints is already NP-hard.
- Random guessing satisfies 7/8 of such constraints, so the result shows even the slightest ε improvement over trivial is as hard as finding the optimum.
