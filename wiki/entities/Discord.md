## Definition
Discord is a community communication platform used both for community building (CrewAI's 8,000+ member community) and as a makeshift UI for personal AI agents. Kitze critiques it as not designed for Life OS purposes — users are "just molding them into something, but they'll never be the right UI for you to manage your life fully."

## Key Information
- CrewAI's Discord community has over 8,000 people
- Serves as a primary community channel for the open-source project alongside GitHub and Reddit
- Represents organic community growth around AI agent tools
- **Agent UI usage**: Kitze uses five Discords, each with many channels, threads, and forum posts to organize conversations with specialized AI agents
- Kitze's critique: "Discord and Telegram were not meant for Life OS" — "just molding them into something, but they'll never be the right UI"
- People started using Discord topics, Slack, and other platforms to organize agent conversations after finding one-on-one chat with a single agent insufficient
- Wolfer deliberately does not support Discord — forces use of its own purpose-built UI
