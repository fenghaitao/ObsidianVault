## Definition

Communication platform whose founders, per [[Mark Pincus]], concluded they needed to become an "inverted pyramid" organization — a case study for the "[[Close To The Metal]]" management principle.

## Key Information

- Discord's founders told Pincus they realized they had been outsourcing/delegating the most important product decisions (UX) to their least experienced people, doing this every day — and decided to invert that, with founders taking the "first and last mile" of the product.
- Grouped with [[Slack]] as an example of a game-company pivot (Discord also began as a game-related project before becoming a communication tool).

- Former CMO [[Eros Resmini]] was a close advisor to [[Amol]] (Anthropic's head of growth) during Amol's founder years.
