## Definition

Discord is a messaging and community platform. Lovable uses Discord as its community hub with hundreds of thousands of members.

## Key Information

- Lovable's community runs on Discord with hundreds of thousands of members
- The community is described as "very lively" with community managers ensuring questions get answered
- Lovable has an ambassador program running through Discord
- Community is cited as a key growth lever: amplifies word-of-mouth, social posting, and retention
- Community was started very early on at Lovable and runs on standard Discord (nothing custom-built)
- Eros Resmini is the former CMO of Discord, who was a close advisor to Amol Avaisary
