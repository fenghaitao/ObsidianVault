## Definition

Will Bailey is the Android counterpart and tech lead for the Instagram Stories project, whom Ryan Olson credits as a model product-focused engineer.

## Key Information

- Was an IC8 and the tech lead for the Stories project; he "really set the example" for how to succeed as a product engineer.
- Brought Ryan into the rooms where Stories decisions were made with Mike Krieger and Kevin Systrom.
- During the project's "dark days" (when it was spinning in circles), he wrote a document outlining the full product vision — which Ryan looked back on five years later and found it "nailed" the roadmap.
- Also part of the IGTV team.
- Exemplified the Instagram culture that prioritized product engineering (contrasted with Facebook's bias that "real engineers work on infrastructure").
