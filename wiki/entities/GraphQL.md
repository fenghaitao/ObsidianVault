## Definition

GraphQL is a data query and manipulation language for APIs, co-invented by Lee Byron at Facebook/Meta, that lets clients request exactly the data they need.

## Key Information

- Co-invented by Lee Byron, who was Adam Ernst's manager.
- ComponentScript was built to be based on GraphQL for data consistency, but at the time (2014-era) GraphQL tooling was slow and "janky," and building on the native GraphQL stack slowed the framework down.
- A competing server-driven UI approach skipped GraphQL (and thus data consistency) entirely — a trade-off Adam Ernst and his team found horrifying but that gave the rival a big speed advantage.
- Adam Ernst built broad knowledge by diving "eight levels deep" into GraphQL codegen whenever it blocked his code.
