## Definition

Tanmayi Priya Dasari is an electrical engineering and computer science major at [[UCBerkeley]] and co-creator of [[SimFrancisco]], a winning project at [[Anthropic]]'s [[ClaudeOpus4.8]] Build Day hackathon (June 2026) that models San Francisco with 10,000 synthetic residents drawn from US Census data for polling and prediction-market forecasting.

## Key Information

- Co-created [[SimFrancisco]] with [[TejasPrabhune]], modeling San Francisco with 10,000 synthetic residents each with their own demographics, personal history, and worldview.
- Electrical engineering and computer science major at [[UCBerkeley]]; met her hackathon teammate Tejas through the university's Machine Learning club.
- Sim Francisco forecast the 2024 presidential vote at 81.3% Democratic (actual 83.8%) and San Francisco's March 2024 Prop A at 70% (actual 70.38%).
- The team used a verifier and an adversarial agent alongside [[Claude]] to build a backend that reproduced the city's real demographic distributions.
- [[ClaudeOpus4.8]] wrote the entire front and back end of the project.
- Competed at the [[ClaudeOpus4.8]] Build Day hackathon on June 13, 2026, a 12-hour event with 310 builders.
