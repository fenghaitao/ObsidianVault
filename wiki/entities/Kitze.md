## Definition
Kitze is a developer and creator known for Sizzy.co, Benji, and Wolfer. He is a prominent figure in the personal AI agent community, founder of the Tinker Club, and an advocate for self-hosted, locally-controlled AI assistants. He has been obsessed with productivity tools since age 10 and has built multiple to-do/life-OS applications over two decades.

## Key Information
- Creator of Sizzy.co (browser for responsive web development)
- Creator of Benji, a "life OS" app with 60+ features (todos, habits, calendar, food tracking, routines) — still unshipped after 3-4 years
- Creator of Wolfer, a personal agent framework built on Codex with nested topic context injection
- Founder of Tinker Club, a community for AI agent tinkerers
- Created the Open Claw lobster logo at 2am; wore lobster suits to meetups
- Self-describes as having ADHD, which drives cycles of intense focus followed by abandonment of projects
- Went "full hipster mode" — moved all data off cloud to self-hosted infrastructure for agent access
- Switched back to Android from iOS to give agents full phone control (notifications, app install/uninstall)
- Critiques model personality degradation: "talking to GPT-5... feels like talking to a box of oats"
- Predicts the end of consumer apps — AI will prompt humans, not the reverse
