## Definition

[[Zynga]]'s mobile word game, a "Proven Better New" derivative of [[Scrabble]] with mobile polish ("better") and Facebook-graph-based asynchronous play with friends ("new").

## Key Information

- Held up by [[Mark Pincus]] as the textbook example of [[Proven Better New]]: proven = Scrabble; better = mobile-native polish and UX; new = playing asynchronously against friends already on your Facebook graph.
- Reached 14 million daily active users (DAUs) — evidence that the "better + new" combination, not the base Scrabble mechanic alone, drove the massive adoption.
- Along with [[Zynga Poker]], proved more enduring than [[FarmVille]]/[[CityVille]] because it successfully made the transition to mobile.
- The turn-based mechanic pioneered here was later copied by [[OMG Pop]] for [[Draw Something]].
