## Definition

Jamie Zawinski (also known as "JWZ") is the early Netscape/Mozilla engineer who originally suggested open-sourcing the Netscape browser.

## Key Information

- Suggested giving away the browser to "invite the world to help" against Microsoft's much larger resources.
- Saved the censored code from the open-source cleanup and later released it ("you can go out there and find all of the censored code").
