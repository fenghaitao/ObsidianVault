## Definition

Cornell University is an Ivy League university in Ithaca, New York. Evan King attended Cornell, where he played Division 1 soccer and founded the hacking club.

## Key Information

- Evan King played Division 1 soccer at Cornell before quitting his junior year to focus on software engineering
- Evan founded the Cornell hacking club (Capture The Flag / CTF), starting with flyers around the engineering quad, growing to a core team of 25 competitors and up to 200 attendees at weekly lectures
- Emin Gun Sirer was the adviser of the hacking club and a TA for Evan; he is now the CEO and founder of Avalanche blockchain
- Meta came to Cornell's campus to interview, which is how Evan got his initial interview opportunity
- Cornell had an OCaml class, which was relevant for Jane Street interviews
- Evan graduated from Cornell in 2017
