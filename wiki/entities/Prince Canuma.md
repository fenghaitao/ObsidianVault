## Definition
Prince Canuma is the founder of Neywa Labs and a major contributor to the MLX ecosystem. He has built MLX VLM, MLX Audio, MLX Video, and Marvis TTS. His work on on-device AI was originally motivated by his father's blindness in 2020 and a promise to restore his ability to read and navigate the world.

## Key Information
- Founder of Neywa Labs
- Major contributor to MLX, with 1.5M+ downloads and 4,000+ models ported
- Created MLX VLM (vision-language models on MLX)
- Created MLX Audio (audio framework for speech-to-text, text-to-speech, speech-to-speech on MLX)
- Created MLX Video (video generation on MLX)
- Created Marvis TTS (custom text-to-speech model with <100ms generation latency)
- Implemented Turbo Quant publicly within 30 minutes of paper release, achieving 4x KV cache reduction
- Works with frontier labs for day-zero MLX support of open source models (e.g., Gemma 4)
- Powers a Richie Mini robot with MLX vision + audio + voice cloning
- Personal motivation: father went blind in 2020; on-device AI enables accessibility without cloud dependency
- Active on Twitter sharing MLX community projects
