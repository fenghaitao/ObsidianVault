## Definition

BeReal (transcribed in the episode as "Bal") is a friends-first social app known for its viral "It's time to be real" notification prompt.

## Key Information

- Ryan Olson referenced BeReal as an example that a friends-only social network can grow, citing its built-in viral mechanic — the "It's time to be real" notification that sparked conversation.
