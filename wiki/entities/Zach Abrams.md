## Definition

Zach Abrams is a product leader who worked with Rachel Lockett at Remind. He gave her the pivotal feedback that her zone of genius was not product strategy but people leadership, which led her to transition into coaching. He went on to lead product at Coinbase, Brex, and Bridge (acquired by Stripe).

## Key Information

- Was a colleague of Rachel Lockett at Remind, a small company
- He was a great product manager who observed Rachel's natural gift for motivating teams and influencing executives
- Told Rachel: "Your zone of genius is not being a product strategist, but you have gotten the team more motivated than I ever could and you've influenced the entire executive team behind your ideas. You're a people person."
- Went on to lead product at Coinbase and Brex
- Most recently led Bridge, which was acquired by Stripe
- Remains a client of Rachel Lockett's coaching
- Both have been "honing their gifts" over the last decade
