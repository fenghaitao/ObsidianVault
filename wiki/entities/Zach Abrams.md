## Definition

Zach Abrams is a product leader who, as a colleague of [[Rachel Lockett]] at a company called Remind roughly a decade before this episode, told her that her true "zone of genius" was working with people, not product strategy — a comment that prompted her career pivot into executive coaching.

## Key Information

- Went on to lead product at Coinbase, Brex, and Bridge (later acquired by [[Stripe]]).
- Remains one of Lockett's coaching clients a decade later; the two have tracked their respective career pivots into their own "zones of genius" over that time.
