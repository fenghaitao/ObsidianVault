## Definition

Lee Byron is a former Meta senior engineer and co-inventor of GraphQL who was Adam Ernst's manager when he prompted the creation of ComponentKit.

## Key Information

- Co-invented GraphQL and had done a lot of early work with React on the web when it was still new.
- Prompted Adam Ernst to think through how React's concepts could work in an iOS-first way, which led to ComponentKit.
- Was deeply involved in the mediation conversations that helped get ComponentKit adopted amid pushback from iOS engineers.
- Was one of the senior engineers who carried significant weight at Facebook and helped convince others.
