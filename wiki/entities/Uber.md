## Definition

Ride-hailing company cited by [[Benedict Evans]], alongside [[Airbnb]], as one of Marc Andreessen's classic examples of a company that "redefines the question" rather than just doing "the old thing but more" — disrupting an industry (taxis) without selling software to the incumbents.

## Key Information

- Evans contrasts Uber's large, clearly measured market impact on taxis with Airbnb's comparatively marginal measured impact on hotels — used to illustrate that platform-shift disruption narratives are never as uniform as they first appear ("it depends").
