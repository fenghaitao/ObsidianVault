## Definition

Uber is a ride-sharing app, cited by Butterfield as an example of excellent product simplicity: the app's main screen was just "Where would you like to go?" and "Other," with everything else behind "Other."

## Key Information

- At one point, the Uber app opened to just "Where would you like to go?" and "Other" — "Other" contained everything else (change payment method, set pickup location, etc.)
- Butterfield calls this "perfect" because almost all of the time, people just want to choose where they want to go
- This is an example of the principle: present the 2-3 most common things people want to do, put everything else behind "Other"
- The app no longer works this way (as of the podcast recording)
