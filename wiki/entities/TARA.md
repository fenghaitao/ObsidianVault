## Definition

TARA is a tool built by Kyeyune Kazibwe, formerly of Uganda's Ministry of Works and Transport and a winner of Anthropic's "Built with Opus 4.6" Claude Code hackathon, that turns dashcam road footage into a complete road-investment appraisal — replacing traditional feasibility studies that cost $1–4 million and take 9–14 months.

## Key Information

- Uses [[Claude4.6Opus|Opus 4.6]]'s vision capabilities to analyze every dashcam frame, identifying surface conditions, distress patterns, and roadside activity (pedestrians, cyclists, market stalls).
- Segments roads into condition sections, auto-populates intervention costs, and generates a full economic appraisal (NPV, cash-flow projections, sensitivity analysis) plus an equity score assessing who benefits from the investment based on nearby facilities and areas of concern.
- Demonstrated on real dashcam footage from Uganda's Kira–Matugga Road (then under construction); generates a complete PDF report — condition assessment, economic analysis, equity findings, sensitivity interpretation — in about five hours versus weeks previously.
