## Definition

Persona is a verified-identity platform, presented as a sponsor of this [[Lenny's Podcast]] episode, helping organizations onboard users, fight fraud, and build trust.

## Key Information

- Positioned as automated user/business/employee verification to combat AI-enabled fraud (impersonation, account takeover, money laundering) without adding friction for legitimate users.
- Cited customers: Etsy, LinkedIn, Square, and Lyft.
