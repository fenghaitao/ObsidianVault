## Definition

Persona is a verified identity platform that helps organizations onboard users, fight fraud, and build trust through automated user, business, and employee verification.

## Key Information

- Provides automated user, business, and employee verification
- Helps combat AI-enabled fraud including money laundering, identity takeover, and business impersonation
- Used by platforms including Etsy, LinkedIn, Square, and Lyft
- Sponsor of Lenny's Podcast
- Offers 500 free verifications per month for one year to Lenny's listeners
