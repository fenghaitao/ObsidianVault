## Definition
BCPL is an early low-level systems language with only one data type ("the word"), used by Bjarne Stroustrup to rewrite his PhD simulator.

## Key Information
- "A language that will make C look like a high-level language," having only one data type.
- Bjarne rewrote his Simula simulator in BCPL for the CAP computer at Cambridge: "a very painful exercise," and he jokes he "lost half [his] hair debugging."
- The result ran roughly 50x faster, and he got his PhD.
- The pain convinced him never again to attempt a problem with such inadequate tools — the seed that produced C++.
