## Definition

Novi was Meta's cryptocurrency wallet project (also referred to as the Libra project) that James Everingham helped lead. It never shipped amid political and regulatory pressure.

## Key Information

- Also called the Libra project; James and Kevin Weil were recruited by David Marcus.
- The org grew to ~3,500 people; governments were wary of Meta "rewriting currency."
- The team kept re-architecting for regulatory compliance for years until, even with a "platinum version of stablecoin," regulators still wouldn't allow release.
- Regret: "we probably should have just released it and asked for forgiveness."
