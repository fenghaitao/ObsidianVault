## Definition

Jason Lemkin is a two-time founder, founder and CEO of SaaStr (the world's largest community for B2B founders), and a prominent voice on sales, go-to-market, and AI in SaaS. He is also an investor who has deployed almost $200 million into founders from the SaaStr community.

## Key Information

- Two-time founder who sold his last startup to Adobe, then started writing a blog in 2012 about the mistakes he made
- SaaStr started as meetups in 2015, grew to events with 10,000+ attendees annually, and now does eight figures in revenue
- Has invested almost $200 million (~10x lifetime) into founders from the SaaStr community
- Replaced SaaStr's ~10-person human sales team with 20 AI agents and 1.2 humans, achieving roughly equivalent performance
- Built 12 apps on Replit in 150 days as a top 1% user despite not being able to code
- Known for being direct and articulate about sales and GTM, with a "learner's mindset"
- Built tools on saster.ai including a valuation calculator (used 800,000 times in 90 days) and a pitch deck reviewer (reviewed ~3,000 decks)
- Believes the classic email-based SDR and human BDR roles will be "mostly extinct" within 12 months
- Advice for salespeople: pick an agentic tool, train it yourself, get it into production — this makes you "hyper employable"
- Anecdote from [[summary-33 - Why most AI products fail： Lessons from 50+ AI deployments at OpenAI, Google & Amazon]]: one of his salespeople quit when an AI agent started tracking everyone's Salesforce updates automatically — the agent exposed that this person wasn't actually doing any work, illustrating that AI makes it harder to "sit around and twiddle your thumbs"
