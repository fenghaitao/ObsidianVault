## Definition

Personal AI news aggregator built by [[Sam Lesson]] (named "letter meme" / "lettermeme.com" in the transcript, exact spelling uncertain due to transcription), which converts newsletters he doesn't have time to read into a daily grid of AI-generated cartoons summarizing what's happening in the world.

## Key Information

- Lesson's answer to the episode's "AI Corner" segment: "I built a little personal news aggregator called letter meme that basically takes all the newsletters I don't have time to read and turns them into daily cartoons... I have a grid of like what's going on in the world as a front page in cartoons."
- Users can pipe in their own set of trusted newsletters to get a personalized daily email digest, continuously updated.
- Built in two phases: Lesson built the first end-to-end version himself via vibe coding, citing [[Cursor]], DigitalOcean, and [[Cloudflare]] as the tools that "will get you a long way in terms of just like building this stuff on the fly." He later brought in engineer friends to "uplevel" the parts he ran out of time to improve — cited as an example of vibe coding not fully scaling past a certain point on its own.
- Directly inspired the cartoon illustrations used in Lesson's etiquette book/materials, per the episode's framing.
