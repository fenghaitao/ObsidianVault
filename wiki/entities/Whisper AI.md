## Definition

Whisper AI is an automatic speech recognition system developed by OpenAI. Jia Chen used Whisper AI in a hackathon project to provide live transcription projected as a hologram in VR glasses.

## Key Information

- Used in Jia's favorite hackathon project: a VR system built from cardboard and glass with an Arduino
- Whisper AI provided live transcription of a speaker (e.g., a professor), which was then projected as a hologram in the top right of the VR glasses
- The transcribed notes were also summarized in a companion web app
- The entire project was built in 9 hours, allowing the team to sleep during the hackathon
