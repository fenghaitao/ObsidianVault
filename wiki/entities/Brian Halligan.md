## Definition

Brian Halligan is the founder and chairman of HubSpot. He worked at Sequoia and helped Deli (the digital clone platform) early on, creating his own digital clone that Jason Lemkin found more compelling than LennyBot in some ways.

## Key Information

- Founder and chairman of HubSpot
- Worked at Sequoia and helped the Deli platform early on (Sequoia-backed)
- Created a "Digital Brian" clone on Deli that Jason Lemkin preferred over LennyBot because it was more purely Brian (vs. LennyBot which synthesizes all of Lenny's guests too)
- The contrast between LennyBot (synthesizing many voices) and Digital Brian (one voice) helped Lemkin decide where to position his own Digital Jason
