## Definition

Brian Halligan is the founder and chairman of [[HubSpot]], cited by [[Jason Lemkin]] as a key inspiration for SaaStr's own AI "digital clone" agent: Halligan built his own AI clone on [[Delphi]] before Lemkin did.

## Key Information

- Backed by Sequoia (Halligan worked at Sequoia, which helped him get the project going early on).
- Lemkin says what distinguished Halligan's Delphi-based clone from Lenny's "Lennybot" was that it wasn't just Halligan's own voice/knowledge, but synthesized the collective voices of people Halligan had worked with/interviewed — the design choice Lemkin says made Lennybot powerful and that he tried to replicate for SaaStr's own general-purpose agent.
