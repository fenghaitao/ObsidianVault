## Definition

Prime Video (formerly Amazon Video) is Amazon's video streaming service. Lee worked on the Prime Video Android app at Amazon, where he solved the Android method limit problem by writing a custom class loader.

## Key Information

- Lee worked on the Amazon Video app (now Prime Video) at Amazon
- The app combined with Shopping, App Store, and other Amazon apps exceeded Android's method limit
- Lee wrote a custom class loader to allow multiple DEX files in an Android application before Google officially supported it
- This was Lee's most technically complex IC work and was a key part of his staff-level technical credibility
- The project demonstrated solving a hard technical constraint rather than forcing engineers to "code dumb" around the limitation
- Steve Huynh was reorged into Prime Video after Amazon Tickets was shut down; he worked on delivering live sports on the Prime Video platform
- The catalog work in Prime Video that Steve took on was the scope that ultimately led to his promotion to Principal Engineer (L7)
- The work sat in the gaps between principal engineers' assigned areas (playback, clients, catalog), requiring an end-to-end solution that no single principal was responsible for
