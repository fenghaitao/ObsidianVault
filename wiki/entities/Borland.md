## Definition

Borland was a 1990s software tools and languages company (Borland C++, Turbo Pascal) where James Everingham worked early in his career — "sort of like where all the good engineers were in the valley."

## Key Information

- Was "probably the main competitor" to Microsoft in the early 90s as a languages/developer-tools company.
- Recruited James because of his open-source developer tools.
- James was recruited from Borland to Netscape by colleague Lloyd Tabb; lost market share to Microsoft's Visual Studio, with "layoff after layoff."
