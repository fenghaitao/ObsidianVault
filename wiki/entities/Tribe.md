## Definition

Early social-networking company (Tribe.net) founded by [[Mark Pincus]], started around the time LinkedIn was launching. Cited as his cautionary example of failing from excessive ambition.

## Key Information

- Pincus saw the size of the social-networking opportunity and "tried to do everything" instead of picking one use case — despite multiple ideas inside Tribe working (e.g., "urban tribes"), the lack of focus led to failure.
- D30 retention was reportedly terrible ("sinking speedboat"); Pincus says that with a Proven-Better-New mindset of testing more ideas and looking around for market heat, he "would have massively changed [his] odds of success."
- The "Tribes" concept inside the product is described as a precursor idea to what became Reddit / listings-industry products.
- Failure at Tribe led to a period Pincus calls "the abyss," after which he started Zynga deliberately small (a poker game).
