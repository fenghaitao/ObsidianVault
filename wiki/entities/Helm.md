## Definition
Helm (Holistic Evaluation of Language Models) is a standardized modular harness for reproducible evaluation of AI models, developed by the Stanford [[CRFM]] (Center for Research on Foundation Models). It pioneered the approach of evaluating different scenarios and models against a standard test bed.

## Key Information
- Built by the Stanford CRFM team
- Pioneered a standardized modular harness for reproducible evaluation
- Evaluates different scenarios as well as models against a standard test bed
- Cited by [[VincentChen]] as an exemplar of [[Researcher UX]] in benchmarks
- Several years old as of 2026 but foundational to evaluation infrastructure
