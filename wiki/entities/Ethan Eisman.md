## Definition

Ethan Eisman was the head of design at Slack during the period when the product principles were being codified. He led the initiative to write Slack's product principles.

## Key Information

- Head of design at Slack
- Led the initiative to codify Slack's product principles
- Worked with Noah Weiss, Jessica Fain, and other early Slack folks to capture the ideas that made Slack great from a product principles and craft perspective
