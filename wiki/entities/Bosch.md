# Bosch

German multinational engineering and technology company. [[Qasar Younis]] worked at Bosch as an engineer, part of his decade-plus experience in large organizations.

## Key Information
- One of the large organizations (100,000+ employees) where Qasar worked before founding [[Applied Intuition]]
- This experience contributed to his understanding of what it's like to be an employee at the bottom of a large hierarchy
- Qasar credits this perspective as essential to becoming a better leader
