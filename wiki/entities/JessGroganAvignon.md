## Definition
Jess Grogan-Avignon is a practitioner at Accenture who works on deploying AI solutions at large enterprises. She co-presented "Most Enterprise Agentic Projects Are Doomed, Here's Why" at the AI Engineer Summit.

## Key Information
- Works in the world of enormous enterprises: telecoms, utilities, serving entire nations, government, healthcare, consumer products
- Emphasizes that at enterprise scale, actions have real consequences — bad deployments can take down critical national infrastructure
- Argues that the enterprise scaffold built for control, process, repeatability, and governance is now the primary drag on AI value capture
- Co-presented five enterprise tensions: speed, value, delivery, trust, and moat
- Stresses that the day you ship is not the finish line — it's when the race begins
- Advises that the real moat is living memory (customer interaction signals), not transactional memory (ERP, CRM, SOPs)
