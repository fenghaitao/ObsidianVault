## Definition
The Science Museum in London is a major museum exploring interactive technology for visitor experiences. Its co-CEO met with Joe Reeve about the statue app technology, and the museum is known for experimenting with interactive exhibits like large tablets for children.

## Key Information
- Co-CEO met with Joe Reeve (ElevenLabs) to discuss the statue app technology
- Known for being "really good at going and trying stuff" — early adopter of interactive museum technology
- Has large tablets that kids can interact with, though Joe Reeve noted this can feel like "sticking technology onto" exhibits rather than being a core part
- Part of the broader museum interest in AI-powered visitor experiences alongside the V&A and Sainsbury Centre
- Represents the institutional demand for making collections and exhibits conversational through AI
