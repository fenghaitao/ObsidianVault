## Definition

Physicist cited by [[Marc Andreessen]] for having spent decades unsuccessfully obsessed with alchemy — specifically the "philosopher's stone," a hypothetical process for transmuting common lead into rare gold — a quest Andreessen argues AI has finally, literally achieved (turning common sand/silicon into rare thought). See [[Philosopher's Stone Analogy]].
