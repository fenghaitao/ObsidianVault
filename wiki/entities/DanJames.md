## Definition
Dan James is a former fellow of the Number 10 Data Science fellowship program who went on to found Just AI, a team within the UK Ministry of Justice that deploys forward-deployed engineers into prisons and the criminal justice system.

## Key Information
- Former fellow of the [[Number10DataScience]] fellowship program
- Founder of [[JustAI]] within the Ministry of Justice (MOJ)
- Leads a team deploying AI engineers into prisons to work with parole officers and prison wardens
- Applying AI to stop drug flow into prisons, improve security, and find efficiencies in manual processes
