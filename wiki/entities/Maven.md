## Definition

Maven is an online course platform hosting cohort-based courses. Aishwaria Raanti and Kiti Bottom teach the #1 rated AI course on Maven, focused on building enterprise AI products.

## Key Information

- Hosts the #1 rated AI course taught by [[Aishwaria Raanti]] and [[Kiti Bottom]]
- The course teaches product leaders key lessons about building successful AI products, focusing on a problem-first approach rather than tool-first or hype-first
- The course covers the Continuous Calibration Continuous Development (CCCD) framework
- Also offers free resources and free sessions alongside the paid course
- Claire Vo launched a Maven course for executives on transforming engineering, product, and design organizations, co-taught with Zack
- Claire and Zack built the entire course using Claude Code and used an OpenClaw agent named Sage to project-manage the course development
