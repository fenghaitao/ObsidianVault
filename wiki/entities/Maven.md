## Definition

Online cohort-course platform hosting [[Aishwarya Naresh Reganti]] and [[Kiriti Badam]]'s "number one rated AI course," on building enterprise AI products.

## Key Information

- Reganti describes the course as teaching product leaders "unlearning mindsets" and a [[Problem First Approach]] rather than a tool-first or hype-first approach to building AI products.
- The guests also maintain a free ~20K-star GitHub repository of AI learning resources, and offer free sessions/writing alongside the paid course.
