## Definition

Eminem is a rapper whose song "Lose Yourself" (with the line "one shot, one opportunity") is referenced by Sam Lessin as emblematic of the scarcity mindset that etiquette helps overcome. Sam says: "Every once in a while before a big presentation, you got to listen to it and pump yourself up."
