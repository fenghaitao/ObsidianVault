## Definition

Bangaly Kaba is the author of the Adjacent User Theory, which describes how product-market fit expansion happens through capturing users just outside the core user base.

## Key Information

- Wrote a well-known article on Adjacent User Theory "many years ago"
- Theory states that the biggest opportunity for growth when in no-growth stages is to capture adjacent users — those just outside the core user base with somewhat similar needs but different geo, use case, or requirements
- Elena Verna references this theory to explain the challenge AI companies face: they're constantly recapturing pioneers rather than reaching adjacent users/latent majority
