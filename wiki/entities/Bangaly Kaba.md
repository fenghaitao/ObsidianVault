## Definition

Bangaly Kaba (transcribed "Bengali" in the source) is a growth executive — former VP of Growth at Instagram and Instacart — credited by [[Elena Verna]] as the originator of "[[Adjacent User Theory]]," a framework developed while scaling Instagram's growth team past 1 billion users.

## Key Information

- Adjacent User Theory: identifies users who are aware of and have tried a product but aren't successfully retained, due to remaining barriers in current positioning/experience — argued to be the cheapest, highest-opportunity growth lever available to a company with an established core user base, since it requires no new market or expanded marketing budget.
- Verna applies the theory to worry that the AI/vibe-coding category is entirely focused on retaining early "pioneer" adopters, at the expense of converting the much larger "adjacent user"/"latent majority" segment.
