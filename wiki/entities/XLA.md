## Definition
XLA is Google's accelerator compiler for machine learning; Vlad Feinberg recalls "golfing the XLA compiler" to fit a model into limited TPU memory during the first Bard launch.
## Key Information
- Compiler golfing (squeezing programs to fit memory) was part of the unglamorous engineering that earned him a Jeff Dean spot bonus.
- It sits in the low-level/hardware tooling cluster alongside kernel DSLs like ThunderKittens and CuTe.
