## Definition

Fiona Fung is the Director of Engineering for [[ClaudeCode]] and [[ClaudeCowork]] at [[Anthropic]]. She leads engineering and product for both products and delivered the talk "Running an AI-native engineering org" at Code w/ Claude SF 2026.

## Key Information

- Director of Engineering for Claude Code and Claude Cowork at [[Anthropic]].
- Started her career in the early 2000s working on [[VisualStudio|Visual Studio]] at Microsoft, where software shipped on CD-ROMs with hard manufacturing deadlines.
- Joined the Claude Code team before it adopted agentic coding as the default way of working, and led the organizational transformation as coding throughput ceased to be the bottleneck.
- Authored a six-month roadmap upon joining that was out of date by month three due to Claude Code's own acceleration of development speed.
- Introduced just-in-time (JIT) planning on the team, shifting away from design docs toward discussions in PRs and prototypes.
- Indexes hiring on two profiles: creative builders with product sense, and engineers with deep systems expertise — de-emphasizing raw coding throughput.
- Instituted a policy that every manager on the Claude Code team must start as an individual contributor first.
- Manually summarized customer feedback channels every morning until automating it as a [[ClaudeCodeRoutines|Claude Code routine]].
- Advises engineering leaders to pick their noisiest workflow, ask whether it still serves its purpose, and automate or drop it.
