## Definition

Mike Krieger is a co-founder of [[Instagram]] (referred to in the transcript, with a transcription error, as "Mike Kger"). [[Adam Mosseri]] took over as Head of Instagram from Krieger and [[Kevin Systrom]] roughly 8 years before this interview. Later became Chief Product Officer at [[Anthropic]].

## Key Information

- Co-founded Instagram with [[Kevin Systrom]].
- Handed leadership of Instagram to [[Adam Mosseri]] roughly 8 years prior to this 2026 interview.
- As Anthropic's CPO, hired [[Amol]] as head of growth after Amol cold-emailed him — the only PM Krieger has ever hired via cold email. Amol notes Krieger's team wasn't formally hiring for a growth role at the time, but was just beginning to consider building one.
- Cited by Amol (via [[Ben Mann]]) for his experience rebuilding onboarding at Mercury/similar products to be "magical" — emphasizing that helping users understand what an AI tool is capable of is critical to activation and retention.
- Per [[Jenny Wen]] (episode 23): cited as an example of her "low-leverage tasks are secretly high-leverage" management principle — she recalls Krieger personally submitting PRs himself, which signals to a team that no task is beneath a senior leader and builds credibility/closeness to the actual product.
- Referenced (as "Mike Greger") by [[Lenny Rachitsky]] alongside [[Kevin Weil]] in episode 40 (Edwin Chen): Lenny recalls asking both CPOs how much longer dedicated product teams will be needed given how capable AI models are becoming.
