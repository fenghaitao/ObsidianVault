## Definition

Mike Krieger is the co-founder and CTO of Instagram, which Facebook acquired in September 2012.

## Key Information

- Co-founder and CTO of Instagram at the time the company was acquired by Facebook (September 2012).
- Was still building Instagram when Adrien Friggeri met him in early 2013; the core Instagram team was small (around 30 people) with Facebook staff helping on safety and data.
- Friggeri pitched the idea of building A/B testing for Instagram to Krieger (and others), who said the team had "bigger fish to fry" while Instagram was migrating from AWS to Facebook infrastructure — an idea Friggeri pursued anyway.
