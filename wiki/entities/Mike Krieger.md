## Definition

Mike Krieger is the co-founder and CTO of Instagram, which Facebook acquired in September 2012.

## Key Information

- Co-founder and CTO of Instagram at the time the company was acquired by Facebook (September 2012).
- Was still building Instagram when Adrien Friggeri met him in early 2013; the core Instagram team was small (around 30 people) with Facebook staff helping on safety and data.
- Friggeri pitched the idea of building A/B testing for Instagram to Krieger (and others), who said the team had "bigger fish to fry" while Instagram was migrating from AWS to Facebook infrastructure — an idea Friggeri pursued anyway.

- Ryan Olson calls Mike Krieger "something of a hero." He models a "lead from the front" style — never putting himself above the team and jumping in wherever help was needed (finishing the Stories "neon brush" himself, reviewing diffs at 2 a.m., working the same insane hours).
- On the White Out redesign, Krieger said "don't worry about trying to A/B test this thing... build it and ship it" (Facebook leadership later overrode this the night before launch).

### CTO vs Head of Engineering
- When engineering hit ~100 people, Krieger ("Mikey") brought in James Everingham and handed over people/execution, keeping technology, roadmap, and the external engineering brand for himself.
- James: Mikey was "actually a really gifted manager too, but he just didn't want to do that… he wanted to focus on the technology and even coding."
- They wrote and published their job descriptions to the org to remove confusion about who to ask for what.
