## Definition

One of Denmark's first credentialed female doctors, later diagnosed with then-fatal diabetes; co-founder (with husband [[August Krogh]]) of the Nordisk Insulin Laboratorium, predecessor to [[Novo Nordisk]].

## Key Information

- Learned of a new Canadian technology to isolate insulin during a 1920s North American lecture tour with August; the couple negotiated to commercialize it in Denmark.
- Worried from the outset about the temptation to "harvest what others plant" — i.e., extract monopoly profits from desperate, dependent diabetic patients — a concern that shaped the company's founding governance structure. See [[Ethos And Integrity]].
