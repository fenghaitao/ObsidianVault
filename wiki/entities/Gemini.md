## Definition

[[Google]]'s AI model/chatbot product, discussed by [[Benedict Evans]] as a case study in [[Distribution Moat|distribution beating product differentiation]] — and reportedly a candidate to power a future version of [[Apple]] Intelligence.

## Key Information

- Evans: "for a normal person," there's no discernible product difference between Gemini and [[ChatGPT]] — so existing distribution (Android, Google I/O integrations, replacing Chromebooks with "Gemini books") is what wins users at the margin, not superior quality.
- Reportedly the model that could power a future Apple Intelligence, which Evans frames as a test of whether foundation models get leverage "up the stack" (Windows-style) or stay commodity infrastructure (AWS-style) regardless of whose logo is on the assistant.
- Also referenced as the AI product [[Meta]]'s own assistant is benchmarked against in usage-survey comparisons with ChatGPT.
- Per [[Zevi Arnovitz]] (episode 31): powers his app [[Studymate]]'s quiz-generation feature, and (as "Gemini 3") is praised for being unusually strong at front-end/UI work — he splits plans into back-end/front-end and routes front-end tasks to Gemini specifically for this reason. Used inside Google's Antigravity IDE, he personifies it in his [[Multi-Model Peer Review]] practice as a talented but erratic "crazy scientist" whose visible step-by-step reasoning (e.g., proposing to delete a dashboard mid-task, then reversing) is alarming to watch even though its design output is excellent.
