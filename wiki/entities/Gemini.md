## Definition

Gemini is Google's family of frontier AI models. It is one of the models trained using data from Surge AI.

## Key Information

- Gemini 3's launch was cited as an example of how AI labs market their models by claiming #1 status on various benchmarks
- Edwin notes that when Gemini 3 launched, the marketing emphasized benchmark performance, which may not correlate with real-world usefulness
- Google is one of Surge AI's customers for training data
- Jason Cohen finds Gemini particularly good at converting charts and images into tabular data that can be pasted directly into Google Sheets — useful for doing custom analysis on data that's otherwise locked in images
- Zevi Arnovitz uses Gemini 3 for UI and frontend work, describing it as "unbelievable at UI"
- Zevi describes Gemini's personality as "a crazy scientist who's super artsy, super talented at designing"
- When using Gemini within Anti-gravity (Google's Cursor competitor), Zevi finds its thought process "terrifying": it will delete things, make mistakes, then produce beautiful results
- Zevi: "If you sit next to it and watch it work, it's terrifying. Like, you would fire that person instantly"
- Despite the scary process, Gemini is "very good at design" and Zevi uses it specifically for frontend tasks
- Zevi uses Gemini in his multi-model peer review alongside Claude and Codex
