## Definition
Gemini is Google DeepMind's family of large language models; Vlad Feinberg is its pre-training area lead.
## Key Information
- Vlad's team delivers the Flash and Flash-Lite Gemini models and contributes pre-training research to the Pro series.
- Gemini LLMs are integrated into search results (AI Overviews and AI mode), ads, YouTube, and other first-party products.
- The Gemini 2.0 report describes the series as mixture-of-experts models — a switch made viable by a serving-time (pipeline prefill) innovation.
- Vlad's first LLM engagement was supervised fine-tuning for an early Bard release, the predecessor to Gemini.
- Making Gemini models good for search requires hard research: grounded and factual answers, citation, and source-quality assessment.
