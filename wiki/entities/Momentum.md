## Definition

Momentum is an AI-powered RevOps tool (alongside competitor Attention) that automatically tracks every action a salesperson takes and records it in the CRM in real time, creating full transparency into sales activity.

## Key Information

- One of two similar RevOps AI tools SaaStr uses (the other being Attention)
- Automatically tracks every single thing a human does in real time — every communication, every action
- Creates complete transparency: when SaaStr rolled it out, one salesperson quit the same day because it revealed they hadn't done anything in 30 days
- The person had been showing up to standups claiming to do outbound work, but the AI exposed the truth
- Jason Lemkin's message: embrace the transparency — AI tracking everything you do is the future; fighting it is career suicide
- Similar tools have existed before but not with this level of comprehensiveness and real-time tracking
