## Definition

Google is a technology company. In the context of this wiki: Google DeepMind develops Gemini models, Google Antigravity is an AI coding tool, Google Cloud hosts AI Studio, and Google has published both an agentic-engineering masterclass and the Open Knowledge Format standard.

## Key Information

### Agentic-engineering masterclass (June 2026)

A 51-page article that [[ColeMedin]] describes as independently converging on most of what he teaches — the AI-driven SDLC, the harness-is-90%-of-the-system claim, the vibe-coding→agentic-engineering spectrum, static-vs-dynamic context, and conductor-vs-orchestrator modes. See [[AIDrivenSDLC]], [[StaticVsDynamicContext]], [[ConductorVsOrchestrator]], [[TokenEconomics]].

### Open Knowledge Format (July 2026)

A standard built on top of [[AndrejKarpathy]]'s [[KarpathyLLMWiki]] pattern for organizing and sharing personal/team knowledge bases across agents. See [[OpenKnowledgeFormat]].
