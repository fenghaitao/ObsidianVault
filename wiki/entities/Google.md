## Definition

Google is a technology company that Malte Ubl identifies as playing a key commoditizing role in the AI model market. With the cheapest infrastructure on the cost side, Google will always decide to make models cheaper, keeping prices low and benefiting the AI engineering application layer.

## Key Information

- **Role in model commoditization**: Has the cheapest infrastructure on the cost side, which means they will always push prices down
- **Malte Ubl's thesis**: In a commoditized model market, Google's cost advantage keeps prices where they should be — very low
- This benefits AI engineers by ensuring value accrues to the application layer rather than model providers
- Malte gave Google "props" for playing "an amazing role" in keeping model costs down
- Part of the three-way competition (OpenAI/Anthropic/Google) that drives model commoditization
- **n8n Integration**: Google services (Gmail, Google Calendar) are primary integration targets for n8n AI agents. OAuth authentication is one-click. Google Calendar API has a naming quirk where "summary" is the event title

- **Chrome DevTools for Agents**: Google ships Chrome DevTools MCP, a purpose-built DevTools experience for AI agents that returns semantic summaries instead of raw trace data. Led by Michael Hablich.
