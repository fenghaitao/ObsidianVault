# Thomson Reuters

Thomson Reuters is a global content and technology company serving professionals in legal, tax, accounting, compliance, government, and media sectors. The company is expanding its use of Claude models through fine-tuning in [[AmazonBedrock]].

## Claude Products and Features

Thomson Reuters has already seen positive results with [[Claude3Haiku]] and plans to leverage [[FineTuning]] in [[AmazonBedrock]] to further optimize solutions.

### CoCounsel - Legal AI Assistant

CoCounsel is Thomson Reuters' AI platform that helps legal and tax professionals synthesize expert knowledge and deliver comprehensive advice to clients. As of June 2025, CoCounsel leverages [[Citations]] to provide verifiable, trustworthy output:
- Enables citing and linking to primary legal sources
- Minimizes hallucination risk by grounding responses in provided documents
- Strengthens trust in AI-generated legal content for practicing attorneys
- Simplifies building, maintaining, and deploying citation functionality
- As of the 2026 State of AI Agents survey, CoCounsel gives lawyers minutes-level access to 150 years of case law and 3,000 domain experts, cited as a leading example of enterprise agent ROI.

### Strategic Objectives

- **Accuracy & Speed**: Improve accuracy and speed of AI-assisted results for professionals
- **Industry Expertise**: Optimize Claude around Thomson Reuters' industry-specific knowledge and requirements
- **Consistency**: Provide consistent user experiences across their solutions
- **Quality**: Deliver high-quality results at faster speeds

### Expected Outcomes

By fine-tuning Claude 3 Haiku with Thomson Reuters' domain expertise, the company anticipates:
- Measurable improvements in result quality
- Enhanced AI assistance tailored to specific professional domains
- Faster inference for time-sensitive professional tasks

### CoCounsel Rebuilt on Claude Agent SDK (May 2026)

At Anthropic's February 2026 "Briefing: Enterprise Agents" event, Thomson Reuters showcased CoCounsel rebuilt on the [[ClaudeAgentSDK|Claude Agent SDK]]. Anthropic's May 2026 legal-industry announcement notes the integration "now runs both ways" — Thomson Reuters is also an early contributor of legal skills, plugins, and style conventions to Anthropic's legal plugin ecosystem, alongside Box, Legal Quants, and Lawve AI. See [[LegalAI]].
