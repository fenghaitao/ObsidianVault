## Definition
Mayank Pant is a billing solution architect at Stripe who presented a five-step framework for AI pricing at the aiDotEngineer conference.

## Key Information
- Works at Stripe as a billing solution architect
- Presented research based on Stripe's two years of experience working with AI companies on billing and pricing
- Developed a five-step AI pricing framework: (1) Define Your Value, (2) Define Your Charge Metric, (3) Pick Your Pricing Model, (4) Build Guardrails, (5) Iterate
- Advocated for hybrid pricing (base subscription + usage) as the dominant model for AI companies
- Emphasized that iteration speed in pricing is a competitive advantage — the first price is a hypothesis, not a commitment
- Recommends abstracting features behind credits so pricing can evolve without confusing customers
