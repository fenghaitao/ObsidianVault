## Definition

Michigan State University (MSU) is a public research university in East Lansing, Michigan. Jia Chen attended MSU before taking a gap semester to work on her startup in San Francisco.

## Key Information

- Jia Chen attended MSU, which she described as a "no name state school" where starting a company was "pretty much unheard of"
- Only two or three people at MSU were very serious about startups
- Jia likely will not return to MSU, but may transfer to a school in California
- At MSU, she balanced a 3.5+ engineering GPA, 15 hackathons in one semester, and growing to 50K social media followers
- Her advice for non-target school students: become a decision maker in your area (leadership in clubs, big projects, personal brand) to stand out
