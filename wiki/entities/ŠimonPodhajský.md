## Definition
Šimon Podhajský is the Head of AI at Waypoint and the creator of Fulan, a read-only personal AI system that analyzes digital exhaust fumes across six data sources to provide cross-source insights without ever writing back to source systems.

## Key Information
- Head of AI at Waypoint
- Created Fulan, a read-only personal AI system that ingests six read-only data sources (email, journal, browser, task manager, CRM, etc.)
- Advocates for read-only AI as a distinct product category, not a stepping stone to agentic AI
- Coined the term "cognitive exhaust fumes" to describe digital activity as a byproduct of cognition
- Uses Claude skills, Python scripts, and the Anthropic API to power Fulan's weekly reflections and cross-source queries
- Presented at aiDotEngineer in April 2026
