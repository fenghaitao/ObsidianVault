## Definition
Eliyahu Goldratt is the author of "The Goal" (1984), which introduced the Theory of Constraints — the idea that in any system there is always one bottleneck, and optimizing anything other than that bottleneck is pointless or counterproductive.

## Key Information
- Author of "The Goal" (1984), a business novel that introduced the Theory of Constraints
- Theory states: in any system there is always one bottleneck; fix that first, then find where the bottleneck moves
- Chris Parsons applies this to AI adoption: some teams using AI go slower because they're not working on the constraint (e.g., release process, not coding speed)
- Parsons recommends reading The Goal for understanding how to optimize AI-driven development workflows
- The principle applies to Ralph loops: don't parallelize until the sequential bottleneck is addressed
