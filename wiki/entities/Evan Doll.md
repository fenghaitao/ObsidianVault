## Definition

Evan Doll (referred to in the episode as "Evan") is a co-founder of Flipboard and a former Apple iOS engineer who wrote UIViewController and taught Stanford's CS193P course.

## Key Information

- Worked at Apple on the core of iOS; "actually wrote UIViewController," the core UI class (per Ryan Olson).
- Taught Stanford's CS193P course (made available on iTunes); this is how Ryan learned iOS development.
- Called Ryan right away to recruit him to Flipboard as an intern after an investor friend made the connection; Ryan is glad he answered the phone.
