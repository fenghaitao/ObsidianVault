## Definition

Sebastian Ullrich is a Lean lead developer who co-built Lean and co-founded the Lean nonprofit with Leonardo de Moura.

## Key Information

- Co-founded the Lean nonprofit (established 2023) with de Moura; its current goal is to push Lean as a programming language for software verification.
- Co-built Lean with de Moura through the painful C++ → Lean self-host: reducing to bare-bones features and reconciling old and new implementations across ~100,000 handwritten lines.
- When Lean first compiled itself, de Moura recalls calling Ullrich in excitement ("Are you not excited? He said, 'Yes, I am, I am.'").
