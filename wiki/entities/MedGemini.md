## Definition
Med-Gemini is a multimodal medical model based on Gemma 3, released by Google as an official Gemma variant for medical tasks including radiology, chest X-ray understanding, and other healthcare applications.

## Key Information
- Official Gemma variant released by Google
- Based on Gemma 3 architecture with multimodal capabilities
- Designed for medical use cases: radiology, X-ray interpretation, chest X-ray understanding
- Open model that can be further fine-tuned for niche medical applications
- Part of Google's broader Gemma ecosystem of official variants
