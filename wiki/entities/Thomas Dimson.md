## Definition

Thomas Dimson (transcribed in the episode as "Thomas Dimpson" / "Thomas Simpson") is the original author of Instagram's content-ranking algorithms (feed, Stories, Explore), a principal engineer/director at Instagram from 2013–2020, later at OpenAI.

## Key Information

- Did the feed ranking / "basically started all the ranking at Instagram," which shipped around the same time as the White Out redesign.
- Ryan credits him with the insight that the ranked feed led people to create and share more (friends shown more often), which flipped Ryan's initial skepticism.
- Coined the phrase "wandering the impact desert" for stretches without a great project to work on.
- Ryan describes him as probably the smartest person he's ever met — highly intelligent but also socially skilled and understanding of people.
- Per Ryan, he started his own company, OpenAI bought it, and he is now a researcher there.
