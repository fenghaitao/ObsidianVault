## Definition

Steve Tuck is a technology executive and Bryan Cantrill's co-founder at Oxide. He met Cantrill at Joyent (where he led go-to-market/sales) and previously spent roughly a decade at Dell.

## Key Information

- Cantrill and Tuck worked closely together at Joyent; Cantrill concluded "this guy is amazing" and decided "whatever we do next, we do it together."
- Tuck came up on the go-to-market/sales side at Joyent; before that he spent about a decade at Dell.
- The two took long walks in San Francisco generating "bad idea after bad idea" for their next company before landing on the rack-scale machine vision that became Oxide.
- Cantrill credits Tuck as being as team-oriented as he is, and says that shared bond is part of Oxide's foundation.
