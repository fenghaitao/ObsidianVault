## Definition

Webflow is a no-code web design and development platform. Jessica Fain currently works there as a product leader. The company is focused on democratizing design and development skills so that "everyone can do design, everyone can ship code, everyone can write a PRD."

## Key Information

- No-code web design platform
- Jessica Fain is a product leader there
- CPO is Rachel Woolen
- Kev is head of design
- Has "office hours" for early-stage alignment conversations before formal one-pagers
- Redesigned their PM career ladder, led by Jessica Fain, emphasizing "product citizenship" as a trait of seniority
- Focused on empowering people to move as fast as their brains and tools will allow
- Working on enabling anyone in the organization to ship designs to production
