## Definition

Daniela Amodei is a co-founder of Anthropic. Along with Dario Amodei, she has invested heavily in building Anthropic's culture, which Amol Avaisary describes as the company's "secret sauce."

## Key Information

- Co-founder of Anthropic
- Along with Dario, has invested heavily in Anthropic's culture — "Daniela and Dario, they really believe in this a lot"
- Amol: "I think it's just created a very special culture"
