## Definition

Co-founder of [[Anthropic]], who left [[OpenAI]] together with her brother [[Dario Amodei]] to start the company, per [[Eric Ries]] in the *Incorruptible* episode of Lenny's Podcast.

## Key Information

- Left OpenAI with Dario Amodei ("two or three OpenAI crises ago," per Ries) to found Anthropic, before the generative-AI/ChatGPT boom, when the company was not yet considered hot and top venture funds were not participating in its round.
- Along with Dario, was advised by Eric Ries on governance structure before incorporation; the two of them wrote [[Public Benefit Corporation]] status and the right to later create a mission-oversight trust into Anthropic's charter from inception.
- Ries credits "all the credit" for Anthropic's success to Dario, Daniela, and the whole team, describing his own advisory role as very small.
