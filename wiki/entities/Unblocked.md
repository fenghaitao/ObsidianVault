## Definition

Unblocked is a company building context engines for AI agents. They create systems that supply agents with exactly the context they need (and none they don't) to avoid doom loops and make agents mergeable by default.

## Key Information

- Focuses on building context engines for enterprise codebases
- Builds social engineering graphs that map relationships between code, people, and organizational decisions
- Aims to reduce context overhead: tokens, time, and human attention bottlenecks
- Founded by Peter Werry
