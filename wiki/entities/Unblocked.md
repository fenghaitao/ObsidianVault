## Definition

Unblocked is a company building context engines for AI agents. They create systems that supply agents with exactly the context they need (and none they don't) to avoid doom loops and make agents mergeable by default. Their context engine ingests data across SaaS apps and systems of record, resolves conflicts, respects data governance, and delivers token-optimized context.

## Key Information

- Focuses on building context engines for enterprise codebases (customers with 20,000+ members)
- Builds social engineering graphs (social graphs) that map relationships between code, people, and organizational decisions
- Six key differentiators: unified system context, targeted retrieval, conflict resolution, data governance, personalized relevance, token optimization
- Aims to reduce context overhead: tokens, time, and human attention bottlenecks
- Founded by Peter Werry; Brandon Waselnuk is also an engineer at the company
- Open-sourcing a social graph component that procedurally generates expert graphs from code repositories
- Context engine sits in customer Slack channels (e.g., ask-engineering) answering questions automatically
