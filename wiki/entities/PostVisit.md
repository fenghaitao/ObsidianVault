## Definition

PostVisit is a suite of AI tools built by Brussels-based cardiologist Michał Nedoszytko, a winner of Anthropic's "Built with Opus 4.6" Claude Code hackathon, addressing what happens to patients after a medical appointment ends.

## Key Information

- Explains diagnoses in plain language, analyzes visit notes and AI-scribe transcripts, and surfaces relevant clinical evidence from scientific resources and full health records — with physician oversight maintained throughout.
- Gives patients a clearer picture of their own care and gives physicians visibility into how patients are doing between appointments; built around privacy, security, and clinical best practices.
- Nedoszytko previously built Previsit.AI, an AI patient-intake system deployed in Belgium, Greece, and Poland; PostVisit was the counterpart product he had wanted to build for two years.
- Built in about one week using [[ClaudeCode]] while road-tripping from Brussels to San Francisco for the hackathon.
