## Definition
RE-Bench is a collection of challenging, novel, open-ended machine learning research and engineering challenges used by METR to evaluate AI capabilities. Tasks are very difficult even for top human experts.

## Key Information
- One of three task distributions used in METR's time horizon measurements
- Contains tasks "really far beyond" Joel Becker's personal capabilities
- AI models are "having a good crack at them some decent percentage of the time"
- Used alongside HCAST (software tasks requiring autonomy) and SWAR (atomic problems)
- Represents the high-difficulty end of METR's evaluation spectrum
