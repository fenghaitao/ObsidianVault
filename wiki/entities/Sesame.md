## Definition

Sesame is an AI voice company that went viral for creating incredibly intimate, emotional voice experiences. Marc Andreessen cites it as an example of the exciting developments in AI voice technology.

## Key Information

- Went viral in 2025 for creating intimate, emotional AI voice experiences
- Marc Andreessen is a fan and cites Sesame as a standout in AI voice technology
- The company was recently sold (as of the podcast recording date)
- Part of the broader AI voice and wearables revolution that Marc believes is coming
