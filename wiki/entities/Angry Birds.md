## Definition

Mobile game by [[Rovio]], cited as an example of the pure-innovation ("blank whiteboard," non-derivative) path to a hit product, contrasted with [[Proven Better New]].

## Key Information

- Rovio made 45 different games before Angry Birds, each reportedly totally different from the last (not visibly building/learning from prior failures or market data).
- Pincus frames this as "wildcat drilling": innovative and eventually a hit, but with much worse odds than the Proven-Better-New path — contrasted directly with [[OMG Pop]]'s [[Draw Something]], which took the opposite (proven-first) route to a similar-scale outcome.
