## Definition
Aman Khan is an AI product manager at Arize, specializing in evaluation systems for AI applications. He previously worked on self-driving car evaluation at Cruise and on the machine learning platform at Spotify.

## Key Information
- Currently an AI PM at Arize, working on evaluation systems for AI agents and LLM applications.
- Previously worked on evaluation systems for self-driving cars at Cruise (2018-2019), giving him deep domain expertise in eval.
- Worked on Spotify's ML platform, including recommender systems (Discover Weekly, search embeddings).
- Has been at Arize for approximately 3.5 years as of late 2025.
- Collaborates with Lenny's Podcast on educational content for AI product managers.
- Advocates for "thrive coding" — data-driven development with evals — as an alternative to "vibe coding."
- Argues that evals should replace traditional PRDs as the new form of product requirements.
- Sold Arize to all of his previous managers (Cruise, Spotify).
