## Definition
Viktor is an AI employee/coworker platform that lives in Slack. It is a company agent (not a personal agent) with access to 3,000+ integrations, shared context across the organization, and the ability to build its own connections when integrations are missing. It launched in February 2026 and achieved immediate product-market fit.

## Key Information

- **Launched**: February 2026 as an experiment with zero growth expectations; immediate product-market fit and worldwide adoption
- **Interface**: Lives entirely in Slack — no web app. Interacts through DMs, channels, threads, emoji reactions, and message edits
- **Integrations**: 3,000+ tools accessible; if a needed integration doesn't exist, Viktor can build its own connections
- **Model**: Uses Opus 4.6 as its main model; tested GPT 5.4 but users preferred Opus's personality
- **Key Capabilities**:
  - Company-wide shared context: one person connects an integration, whole team benefits
  - Proactivity: can join conversations to suggest workflows, verify claims (e.g., statistical significance in A/B tests)
  - Cloud-based operation: no need for a computer to be open
  - Context isolation: prevents context leakage between different channels and teams
  - Integration scoping: personal vs shared integrations to prevent data leakage
- **Evolution**: Preceded by JCAI (web agent, 2023) and Jace (email agent, 2024). Originally believed browsers were the right interface for AI employees; shifted to Slack for async, human-employee-like interaction
- **Design Philosophy**: "Viktor is not a tool, it's a hire" — should be treated like a human employee with appropriate access scoping
- **Pricing**: Offers free credits for new users ($100 at the conference)
