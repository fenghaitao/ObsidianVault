## Definition

Leonid Levin is a computer scientist who independently co-discovered NP-completeness (the Cook–Levin theorem).

## Key Information

- Named alongside Stephen Cook ("cook and leaven") as having defined NP-completeness and proving that NP-complete problems exist.
- The first problem shown NP-complete in those original papers was satisfiability.
