## Definition

Niklaus Wirth (transcribed "Nicholas V") is the computer scientist who invented Pascal and later Modula and Oberon, and the author of "Algorithms + Data Structures = Programs."

## Key Information

- Inventor of Pascal, and later Modula and Oberon.
- Author of "Algorithms + Data Structures = Programs," which Anders Hejlsberg calls the "it book" for him when younger — light on math and symbolism, rich in instructive examples and explanations of data structures.
- The book taught Hejlsberg hash tables (he previously only knew linked lists); switching Turbo Pascal's symbol tables from linked lists to hash tables roughly doubled compile speed.
- The book also explains how to construct a simple compiler, including error recovery.
