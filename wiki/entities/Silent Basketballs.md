## Definition

Silent basketballs (and silent soccer balls) are foam sports balls the same size as regulation basketballs and soccer balls. They can be bounced and played with indoors without making noise, solving the problem of sports-oriented kids in small living spaces.

## Key Information

- Foam construction — same size as regulation basketballs/soccer balls
- Some versions are as heavy as real basketballs
- Can be dribbled and shot indoors without noise
- Designed for sports-oriented kids who don't have enough outdoor play space
- Claire Vo uses them with her three boys in their San Francisco home
- She has painter's tape on a tall ceiling so her kids can shoot baskets indoors
- Praised by Claire as a "niche product that solves a little thing in my life" — an example of great product-market fit discovery
