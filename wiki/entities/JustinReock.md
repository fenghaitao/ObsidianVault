## Definition
Justin Reock is a speaker and thought leader at DX, a developer productivity measurement company acquired by Atlassian. He presented on AI-assisted engineering leadership at the aiDotEngineer conference.

## Key Information
- Works at DX (acquired by Atlassian), a company focused on measuring developer experience and productivity
- Co-authored an "AI strategy playbook for senior executives" available as a PDF guide
- Presented data-driven findings on GenAI adoption volatility across organizations
- Advocates for psychological safety, proper metrics, and SDLC-wide AI integration over top-down mandates
- Contributed to the DXAI Measurement Framework, which organizes AI metrics into Utilization, Impact, and Cost dimensions
