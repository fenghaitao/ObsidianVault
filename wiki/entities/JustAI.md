## Definition
Just AI is a team within the UK Ministry of Justice (MOJ), founded by former Number 10 Data Science fellow Dan James. The team deploys forward-deployed engineers into prisons and other parts of the criminal justice system to apply AI to improve security, safety, and efficiency.

## Key Information
- Founded by [[DanJames]], a former [[Number10DataScience]] fellow
- Sits within the Ministry of Justice (MOJ)
- Deploys forward-deployed engineers (FDEs) into prisons and the criminal justice system
- Takes a similar approach to 10DS but embeds engineers with parole officers and prison wardens instead of policy teams
- Key use cases: stopping the flow of drugs into prisons, finding efficiencies in manual processes, improving prison security and safety
- One FDE, Will, dropped out of Harvard, did Y Combinator, and joined to work in HMP Wandsworth
