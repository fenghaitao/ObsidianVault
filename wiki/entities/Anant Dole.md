## Definition
Anant Dole is an engineer at Play Magnus (Take Take Take) who co-presented "Building a Chess Coach" at the AI Engineer conference. He is a strong chess player who uses his own skill to evaluate the quality of LLM-generated chess commentary.

## Key Information
- Co-presented the Play Magnus AI chess coach system with Asbjorn Steinskog
- Presented the sections on what Play Magnus is, the game review feature, latency vs quality trade-offs, and LLM evaluation
- A good chess player who personally evaluates LLM commentary quality by comparing it against how he would calculate and play
- Emphasized the importance of separating data pipeline from language generation and closing the loop with autonomous agents
- Hosted a chess simul (simultaneous exhibition) at the conference, playing multiple opponents at once
