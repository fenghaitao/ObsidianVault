## Definition

Adrien Grondin is the developer of Locally AI, an iOS chatbot app for running on-device language models with MLX. He is active on Twitter sharing content about on-device AI.

## Key Information

- Developer of Locally AI, a fully native iOS chatbot for running on-device models

- Active on Twitter sharing on-device AI content
- Locally AI was acquired by LM Studio
