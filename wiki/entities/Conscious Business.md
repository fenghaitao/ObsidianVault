## Definition

*Conscious Business* is a book by Fred Kaufman, recommended by [[Matt MacInnis]] in the episode's lightning round as one of his most-recommended books.

## Key Information

- MacInnis describes it as effectively "a user manual for human beings," especially valuable for younger leaders/managers/executives (people in their 20s and 30s) first learning to lead.
- It has reportedly been used at LinkedIn as a leadership-training framework.
- MacInnis was introduced to the book by Brian Shrier at Sequoia, a board member at his previous company ([[Inkling]]), and says he wishes he'd read it sooner.
- Rippling runs an internal "Conscious Business reading club" for MacInnis's product leadership team, and he hands out physical copies "like candy."
