## Definition
Pardeep is a co-founder of RunPod, the cloud AI infrastructure company. Together with Zenon, he started RunPod in 2022 by repurposing failed crypto-mining GPU rigs in their basement and launching via Reddit for community feedback.

## Key Information
- Co-founded RunPod in 2022 with Zenon
- Originally had GPU rigs for crypto mining; after that failed, prototyped what became RunPod's foundations
- Launched on Reddit offering free GPU access in exchange for feedback
- RunPod has been revenue-generating since inception and grew to 500,000+ developers and $120M ARR
