## Definition

Arvid Kahl is an indie founder and SaaS builder whose product Podscan is 98% coded by Claude. He is known for his build-in-public journey, his podcast, and his systematic approach to AI-powered development including the concept of "comprehension debt."

## Key Information

- Creator of Podscan (podscan.fm), a podcast monitoring platform.
- 98-99% of his code is AI-written; hand-touches 1-2 lines per day.
- Uses PHP Storm with Claude Code terminal, browser, logs, and scheduler in one view.
- CLAUDE.md is "sizable" with code examples and the Augment library for system prompts.
- Video-to-docs workflow: records himself using the app, Claude transcribes and creates documentation.
- "Theory of coding": Claude builds a temporary internal theory — don't interfere manually mid-session.
- "Comprehension debt": must actively maintain mental model of AI-written codebase.
- "Super delayed TDD": writes tests 2 days after deploying to production. Podscan has 3,500 tests.
- Steps through every Git change manually to maintain understanding.
- Even for one-line fixes, prefers asking Claude — it knows all locations where a change applies.
