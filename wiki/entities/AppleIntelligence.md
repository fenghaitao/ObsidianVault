## Definition
Apple Intelligence is Apple's suite of AI-powered features integrated across its operating systems. In the keynote, it is cited as an example of a major company shipping LLM-powered features in beta that were "good enough" — validating the strategy of getting products in front of users early to collect real interaction data.

## Key Information
- Cited as an example alongside Adobe Photoshop and Hex of companies shipping beta AI features backed by evals
- Used as evidence that if major companies can ship AI features in beta, smaller teams should also feel comfortable starting with imperfect but working products
- The mention was part of the argument that binary human feedback from real user interactions is more valuable than waiting for perfection
