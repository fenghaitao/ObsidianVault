## Definition
Gemma 3 is the previous generation of Google DeepMind's open-source model family, which introduced vision support for the first time and paved the way for Gemma 4's native multimodality.

## Key Information
- Predecessor to Gemma 4
- Introduced vision support across all model sizes for the first time in the Gemma family
- Gemma 3N model launched open-sourced audio, vision, and text capabilities
- Used pan-and-scan approach for variable resolution images (splitting into square sequences), which Gemma 4 improved upon with native variable aspect ratios and resolutions
- Significantly outperformed by Gemma 4 across agentic capabilities, coding, multimodal, and multilingual benchmarks
- Used as base for Med-Gemini (multimodal medical model) and cancer therapy pathway discovery research
- Models ranged from 1B to 27B parameters, designed to fit on a single consumer GPU
