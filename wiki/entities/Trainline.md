## Definition

Trainline is a train ticketing platform that aggregates multiple rail carriers across Europe and the UK into a single app. It serves 27 million active users and processes 6.3 billion ticket bookings annually, and has deployed both classic ML models and multi-agent generative AI systems.

## Key Information

- Aggregates train carriers across Europe and the UK into one platform
- 27 million active users, 6.3 billion ticket bookings annually
- Runs both classic ML models (train disruption prediction) and generative AI multi-agent systems
- Travel assistant is a multi-agent system that handles refunds, ticket changes, and proactive support
- Uses Braintrust for AI observability and evaluation
