## Definition

Outdoor apparel company founded by [[Yvon Chouinard]], cited by [[Eric Ries]] in *Incorruptible* as an example of a **[[Perpetual Purpose Trust]]** governance structure and of a founder driven by an uncompromising, non-ROI notion of quality.

## Key Information

- Chouinard, more famous today for environmental activism, is described by Ries as having been "a quality zealot" who believed quality was an objective function and that every product had a quality level it deserved — a belief most people think is "insane," which Ries says is exactly why Patagonia succeeded.
- Patagonia is governed via a perpetual purpose trust, which (unlike a purely economic foundation) has no economic dimension of its own; it includes trustees plus a distinct "purpose protector" role whose job is to be able to sue the trustees themselves if they ever deviate from the mission — a checks-and-balances structure Ries compares to a government.
- Cited as one of the "spiritual holding company" examples alongside Novo Nordisk's foundation structure and Anthropic's Long-Term Benefit Trust.
