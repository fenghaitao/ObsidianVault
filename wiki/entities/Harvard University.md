## Definition

Harvard University is the institution where David J. Malan teaches CS50 (across its College, Extension School, and online versions).

## Key Information

- Malan studied government as a Harvard concentrator before switching to computer science via CS50.
- Harvard Extension School is its continuing-education program, where Malan first taught in 1999.
- Malan returned to Harvard for a five-year CS PhD and took over CS50 in 2007.
- He notes the value of Harvard is credentialing, networking, and life experiences — academics are "often secondary."
- There was on-campus resistance to leaning on MIT's larger CS course catalog, which Malan sees as a missed opportunity.
