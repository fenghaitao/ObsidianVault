## Definition

IBM Watson is an AI platform developed by IBM, known for being one of the earliest and most scaled enterprise AI deployments. It encompasses natural language processing, machine learning, and question-answering capabilities deployed across industries.

## Key Information

- Described by Dr. Satya Nitta as one of the "most scaled AI deployments on the planet."
- Dr. Nitta and members of the Emergence team previously worked on the IBM Watson platform at IBM Research.
- Represents early enterprise AI infrastructure that predates the current wave of LLM-based AI agents.
