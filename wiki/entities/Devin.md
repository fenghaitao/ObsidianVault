## Definition

Devin, an early autonomous AI coding agent, is cited by [[Dan Shipper]] as one of the companies that tried a cloud-based coding-agent environment before the "put the agent on your own computer" paradigm (pioneered by [[Claude Code]]) won out.

## Key Information

- Dan Shipper: "there were people before them [Anthropic] like Devin who I think had the big cloud environment," and OpenAI tried something similar too — but real adoption, in his account, only took off once coding agents ran directly on the user's own computer with full local access (the Claude Code model).
