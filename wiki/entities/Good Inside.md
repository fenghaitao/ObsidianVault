## Definition

Good Inside is a parenting company founded by Dr. Becky Kennedy and her co-founder (also a psychologist). It encompasses a book, podcast, app, community, and live events built around the philosophy that all people are "good inside" regardless of their behavior.

## Key Information

- Founded by Dr. Becky Kennedy and a co-founder, both clinical psychologists
- ~65 employees, profitable, raised venture funding a couple years ago
- The app provides personalized content, an AI chatbot named "GG" (Dr. Gigi), live events, coach-led support sessions, and a parent community
- Dr. Becky does 2-3 live events per month
- The app combines insight (understanding) with action (practical scripts) in 5-minute sessions
- Has a free potty learning course at goodinside.com (Google "good inside potty")
- Website: goodinside.com
- Second children's book "Leave Me Alone" (February 2026) focuses on deeply feeling kids
- Dr. Becky describes the vision as a "Disney type ecosystem" built around IP, story, feeling, and connection
- Has more physical products and "exciting things" planned for 2026-2027
- The AI chatbot helps identify common themes across different parenting struggles and suggests proactive approaches
- Community is described as "expert driven but also just parent community"
- Dr. Becky sees the podcast and Instagram as "flags" — entry points — while the app provides the "ground" (framework and approach)
