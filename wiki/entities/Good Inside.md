## Definition

Parenting company founded and led by [[Dr. Becky Kennedy]], encompassing a book, podcast, Instagram/social presence, app, coaching community, and underlying set of principles for understanding human behavior in any relational "system" (family, workplace, marriage, siblings).

## Key Information

- Kennedy describes Good Inside as "a set of core principles that help us better understand human beings, our self, the core relationships we're in, why we do the things we do" — applicable to any system, not just parenting, because the same principles govern human behavior across contexts.
- Core frameworks associated with the brand: [[Behavior Vs Identity]] ("good inside" — separating identity from behavior), [[Most Generous Interpretation]] (MGI), [[Sturdy Leadership]], [[Boundaries Vs Requests]], [[Resilience Over Happiness]], [[Connecting Before Correcting]], and [[Power Of Repair]].
- Origin: Kennedy's first Instagram post went up February 28, 2020, roughly two weeks before New York City's COVID-19 shutdown, without any business plan behind it; growth accelerated once the community that formed around the content became the primary value, more than any single post or live workshop.
- Users reportedly began calling it "parent school" — Kennedy frames the company's purpose as filling a gap where parenting is "the last area in life that we glorify instinct alone," unlike other skills (gardening, cooking, Greek history) that people study deliberately.
- Product model: users share what's going on with them; Good Inside provides short (~5 minute) insight-plus-action content drips, an AI chatbot named "Dr. GG" ("GG"), and access to live coaching sessions/events (2–3 per month) run by trained coaches, plus an active parent community.
- Offers a free "potty learning" course (deliberately not called "potty training," reflecting the framework that toddlers need to learn a skill, not be disciplined into one) at goodinside.com, framed as addressing one of the few domains (what goes in/out of their body) where young kids have full autonomy.
- Company size and financials (as of this episode): ~65 employees, profitable, raised outside funding "a couple years ago"; stated 2026–2027 ambition to expand into a broader IP/story ecosystem (Kennedy analogizes to Disney) beyond the current digital-product core.
- Products/IP: the original "Good Inside" parenting book; a second children's book, *Leave Me Alone: A Good Inside Story About Deeply Feeling Kids* (pre-order open, releasing February 2026), about kids whose core fear is being "too much"; a podcast; an Instagram/social presence; a subscription app/community.
- Website: goodinside.com.
