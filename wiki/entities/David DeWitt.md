## Definition

David DeWitt (transcribed "Dave Dwit") is a database researcher who, with Mike Stonebraker and others, understood distributed databases and co-authored the 2011 paper showing they beat Hadoop.

## Key Information

- Stonebraker: "Dave DeWitt and others who were involved in our 2011 paper, we understood distributed databases and understood that you could beat the heck out of Hadoop."
