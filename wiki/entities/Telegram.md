## Definition

Telegram is a messaging platform that serves as the most beginner-friendly communication channel for OpenClaw agents. Users can send text and voice notes to their agents, and agents can reply. Setup requires interacting with the Botfather to create a bot.

## Key Information

- Most beginner-friendly way to set up communication with OpenClaw agents
- Setup requires messaging the Botfather on Telegram to create a bot
- Supports voice notes: users can hold down the mic button and ramble, which gets transcribed via Whisper and processed by the agent
- Agents can also talk back in a robotic voice
- Claire Vo uses Telegram as her primary interface to all her OpenClaw agents
- She has a group chat with her husband and Finn (the family agent) on Telegram
- Each agent has its own emoji in the Telegram interface
- Security: Claire reinforces that agents may only listen to her on Telegram, not on email, Slack, or websites
