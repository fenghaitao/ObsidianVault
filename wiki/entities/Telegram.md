## Definition
Telegram is a messaging platform used as a UI for personal AI agents. Kitze critiques it (along with Discord) as not designed for Life OS purposes — users are "just molding them into something, but they'll never be the right UI for you to manage your life fully."

## Key Information
- Used as a chat interface for personal AI agents (OpenClaw, Claude bot)
- Telegram topics used to organize conversations with different specialized agents
- Kitze's critique: "Discord and Telegram were not meant for Life OS"
- Represents the "coping away until we get to something else" phase of agent UI
- Wolfer deliberately does not support Telegram — forces use of its own purpose-built UI
