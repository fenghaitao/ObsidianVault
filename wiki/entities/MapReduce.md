## Definition

MapReduce is the programming model and white paper from Google that helped spark the big-data revolution — and, in Brendan Burns' telling, the cautionary tale for why Google needed to open-source Kubernetes.

## Key Information

- Google wrote the original MapReduce white paper but "had nothing to do with" Hadoop, the open-source reimplementation, and got no credit for it.
- The lesson: publishing papers is not enough to stay "in the driver's seat"; people need something runnable, which became an argument for open-sourcing Kubernetes.
