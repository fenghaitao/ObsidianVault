## Definition

Igor is a software engineer (first name only given in the episode) who was a senior staff engineer (L7/E7) at Google, Cruise, and Meta, and who publicly pursued a demotion and returned to Google at L6.

## Key Information

- Has been programming since age 12; spent ~14.5 years at Google, progressing from L3 up to L7 (senior staff).
- Before Google, worked briefly at Intel; between Google and Meta, worked at Cruise.
- At Meta, asked management for a demotion from E7, which Meta said it could not process for an IC staying in the same job family.
- Returned to Google at L6 (one level down), accepting lower compensation; Google had no standard process for a down-level return but made it possible, including a coding-interview exception.
- Believes senior (E5/L5) has the best quality of life and that he would be comfortable at E6 rather than E7.
- His Google L7 promotion came from leading ads machine-learning training infrastructure onto first-generation TPUs.
- Shared the demotion story publicly because of his personal position of privilege (no visa dependence, spouse's health insurance, grown kids, no mortgage).
