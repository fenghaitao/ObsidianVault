## Definition

Stanford University is a top-tier research university in Silicon Valley. Rahul Pandey attended Stanford for 5 years completing both an undergraduate and master's degree, and Stanford's culture of faculty spinning out companies directly led to his first job at the startup Cos.

## Key Information

- Rahul spent 5 years at Stanford doing an undergrad and master's degree
- He stayed for the fifth year partly because he felt he hadn't made faculty connections and wanted to take part in the groundbreaking research
- He was a research assistant (RA) in a lab with professor Yuri Lusco, a rising star
- Stanford is known for being generous and willing to allow faculty to go out and start companies, with a revolving door between academia and industry
- The lab work Rahul did at Stanford led to the spinout company Cos
- Having Stanford on his resume was a major form of social proof that gave Rahul confidence to take risks early in his career
- Rahul noted that at Stanford, "everyone has a vague aspiration to start a company"
- At the UCLA talk, Ryan and Ricky discussed that Stanford and Ivy League schools are startup-oriented colleges — startup recruiters reported that UCLA students overwhelmingly prefer big tech, while Stanford students are more willing to join startups, possibly because "they're rich, so they don't need to make money immediately after college"

- Nimit Sohoni completed his PhD at Stanford, in Chris Ré's lab alongside the future founders of Cartesia (including Albert Gu); Stanford also supplied the network that seeded his curated research feed on X/Twitter
- Martin Hellman earned his PhD at Stanford under information theorist Tom Cover and returned to the faculty in 1971 after time at IBM and MIT; Stanford "worried about inbreeding," so he left for a few years first.
- Stanford general counsel John Schwartz advised Hellman on the 1977 ITAR/NSA matter and recommended Hellman present the Cornell papers himself rather than his students.
- Creative Initiative, which Hellman credits with saving his marriage, was founded by Stanford professor Harry Rathbun.
- Hellman recruited Ralph Merkle from Berkeley to Stanford for a PhD (as a research assistant with tuition covered).
