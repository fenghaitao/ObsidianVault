## Definition

Marshall Rosenberg was the creator of the Non-Violent Communication (NVC) framework, a four-step process for conflict resolution and connection: Observations, Feelings, Needs, and Requests. He authored a book on the topic.

## Key Information

- Creator of the Non-Violent Communication (NVC) framework
- The NVC framework has four steps: Observations (facts), Feelings (emotions without blame), Needs (universal human needs), Requests (small, achievable olive branch)
- The framework helps people stay "on their side of the net" — sharing their own experience without crossing into blame or assumptions
- Rachel Lockett recommends this framework for difficult conversations
- The framework acknowledges that professionals have feelings, which are often ignored in tech environments
