## Definition

Atoms is Travis Kalanick's company (referenced in the transcript as Cloud Kitchens) where Brian Attwell is CTO. It aims to make food cheaper and more convenient through robotics and supply-chain optimization.

## Key Information

- Company thesis: "no one should cook their own food" — if meals could be made cheaper, people would outsource cooking (as they already do in lower-labor-cost economies)
- Second insight: there is "a ton of avoidable waste in the food supply chain"
- Built robots that reduce labor costs in real restaurants by 50%; a lunch logistics network cutting meal cost 30–40%; lower-cost real estate; and Otter software (processes 18% of the world's orders)
- Americans spend ~$2.5 trillion/year on food; redesigning the supply chain puts ~$5 trillion "on the table"
- Operated in stealth: no corporate website, employees discouraged from posting their employer on LinkedIn, and no PR people
- Stealth reasoning: going public helps fundraising/hiring/ego (which Travis doesn't need), but invites employee distraction (post-Cambridge Analytica negativity) and copycat competitors (they faced ecosystems of clones after public launches in Korea and the US)
- Recruiting-event attendees knew it as "Cloud Kitchens"; the podcast title refers to the company as "Atoms"
- Runs largely B2B, so individual products (Picnic, Otter) can be marketed while the core company stays quiet
