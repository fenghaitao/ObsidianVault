## Definition
Red Hat is an enterprise open-source software company, known for Red Hat Enterprise Linux, OpenShift, and other infrastructure products. It contributes to the Open Claw Foundation, helping with security and dockerization.

## Key Information
- **Industry**: Enterprise open-source software
- **Products**: Red Hat Enterprise Linux, OpenShift, Ansible, JBoss
- Previous employer of Patrick Riley (Auth0 presenter, Duasio team)
- Contributor to OpenClaw: helping with security and dockerization as part of the Open Claw Foundation
- Part of the vendor-neutral foundation alongside Nvidia, Microsoft, Telegram, Salesforce, Tencent, ByteDance, and others
