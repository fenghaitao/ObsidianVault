## Definition

Star Trek is a long-running science fiction franchise spanning multiple TV series and films. Marc Andreessen and his son are fans, and the franchise's LCARS (Library Computer Access and Retrieval System) design language from Star Trek: The Next Generation is referenced as a prompt target for vibe coding.

## Key Information

- Marc Andreessen and his 10-year-old son watch Star Trek together, including the original series, The Next Generation, and the new Starfleet Academy
- Marc's son uses Replit to vibe code Star Trek simulators using the LCARS design language from The Next Generation
- The LCARS UI design language from The Next Generation is a recognizable aesthetic that can be prompted in AI coding tools: "give me a Star Trek Next Generation user interface"
- Marc describes the new Starfleet Academy as "actually quite good"
