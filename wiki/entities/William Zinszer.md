## Definition

William Zinszer is the author of "On Writing Well," a classic book on writing craft. Jason Cohen recommends it as the book that most helped him write, saying "on my best day I write like that."

## Key Information

- Author of On Writing Well
- Jason Cohen's most-recommended book for writing
- Core lesson: "Cut. Cut more and more of your stuff. There's always more to cut."
- Anecdote: Zinszer was on a panel with an amateur writer who said "I never knew writing could be hard." Zinszer replied: "I never knew writing could be easy."
- This exchange captures the struggle of being a serious writer
