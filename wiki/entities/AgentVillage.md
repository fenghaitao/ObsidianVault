## Definition
AI Village (agentvillage.ai) is a research project where multiple AI agents live in a simulated village, occasionally interacting with humans, and attempt to accomplish fuzzy real-world goals using computer use capabilities.

## Key Information
- Agents attempt tasks like organizing events at a park, running human subjects experiments, or operating a merch store
- Tasks are not clearly specified — they are "fuzzy goals" rather than benchmark-style tasks
- Becker reports that "basically all the time the models fall on their faces and suck"
- Uses computer use (GUI) capabilities, which Becker notes are considerably worse than CLI/text-based capabilities
- Becker is interested in adapting this approach to text-only with better elicitation to qualitatively study where models fail on real-world objectives
- Represents a third evidence source for triangulating AI capabilities alongside benchmarks and RCTs
