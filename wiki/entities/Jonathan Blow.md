## Definition

Jonathan Blow is a game designer and programmer best known for the puzzle game The Witness.

## Key Information

- Casey Muratori contributed to The Witness (the walk system) and calls the game "absolutely fantastic."
- Casey credits Blow's design as good even though parts of his own implementation were "janky."
