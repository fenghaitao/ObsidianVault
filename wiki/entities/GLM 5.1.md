## Definition
GLM 5.1 is an open source large language model that ranks at the top of the SWE-Bench leaderboard on Hugging Face Benchmark Datasets as of May 2026. Merve Noyan of Hugging Face strongly recommends it as the best open model for use with Hermes Agent.

## Key Information
- Top-ranked open model on SWE-Bench according to Hugging Face Benchmark Datasets (as of May 2026)
- Recommended by Merve Noyan as the model to use with Hermes Agent
- Successfully used to autonomously fix a Slack integration failure in Hermes Agent — "it fixed on its own"
- Merve describes it as "a very good model" and "absolutely crashing it"
- Used personally in Merve's coding setup
- Cited as evidence that open source models have caught up to closed models (per the 2026 AI Index)
