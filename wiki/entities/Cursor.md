## Definition

Cursor is an AI-powered code editor company that sponsored Ryan L. Peterman's podcast as he worked to make it self-sustaining.

## Key Information

- Ryan credited Cursor (along with WorkOS) as one of the "great sponsors" that signed on and "brought some money in," which helped the podcast sustain itself.
- Taking sponsors at all was a reluctant step for Ryan: he had avoided monetization because finding sponsors felt like time taken away from the fun parts of creating content.
