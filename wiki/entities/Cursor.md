## Definition

Cursor is an AI coding tool/company discussed by [[Dan Shipper]] as a strong technical competitor in the agentic coding space that has deliberately stayed focused on programmers rather than expanding into general knowledge work like [[Codex]] and [[Claude Cowork]].

## Key Information

- Dan Shipper says Cursor's cloud/cloud-agent implementation is technically more advanced than either OpenAI's or Anthropic's, but Cursor has more distinctly chosen to stay "a lane" for programmers, which he thinks may cap how far it expands beyond that (even as the definition of "programmer" broadens).
- Reported (per Dan Shipper) to have been essentially, though not fully, acquired by SpaceX around the time of this episode.
- Also named (in the WorkOS sponsor read) as a customer of [[WorkOS]], alongside OpenAI, Anthropic, Vercel, Replit, and Sierra.
- One of Marcus's (Every PM, [[Spiral]] lead) formative tools before he moved primarily to Claude Code — described as having been "extremely cursor pilled" for a long time.
- [[Boris Cherny]] briefly joined Cursor (impressed by the team and product) after leaving Anthropic, but returned to Anthropic about two weeks later, realizing he missed Anthropic's safety mission specifically.
- Per [[Lazar]] (episode 27, professional vibe coding at [[Lovable]]): cites Cursor, alongside Lovable, as a tool supporting per-project persistent "rules"/"agent.md" instruction files that keep an AI coding agent oriented across sessions without re-explaining context each time — central to his [[Token Budgeting]] approach.
- Per [[Lenny Rachitsky]] (episode 27, recalling a prior interview with [[Michael Truell]], Cursor's CEO): Truell described a vision of Cursor becoming "what comes after code" — a layer above raw code where people no longer need to think about code directly; Lenny connects this to Lazar's description of the agent-conversation layer (reading agent output, responding in English) as that emerging layer.
- Per [[Zevi Arnovitz]] (episode 31): runs [[Claude Code]] directly inside Cursor as his primary tool, having graduated there from Bolt once he needed more control (e.g., adding payments); praises its built-in "Composer" model for being extremely fast on less-complex execution tasks, and personifies Claude-in-Cursor as a communicative, opinionated "dev lead" in his [[Multi-Model Peer Review]] practice. Positioned as the final, full-control stage of his [[Exposure Therapy Onboarding]] path.
- Per [[Sam Lesson]] (episode 32): named in this episode's [[WorkOS]] sponsor ad-read customer list ("OpenAI, Cursor, Perplexity, Vercel, Plaid"); also cited (alongside DigitalOcean and [[Cloudflare]]) as one of the tools Lesson used to vibe-code the first version of [[Lettermeme]], his AI newsletter-to-cartoon aggregator.
