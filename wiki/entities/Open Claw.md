## Definition

Open Claw is an open-source personal digital assistant that gives an AI agent access to your email, calendar, and other personal tools to take actions on your behalf. It became a viral sensation, going from first line of code (November 25, 2025) to a Super Bowl ad (February 2026) in just ~3.5 months, and inspired a generic term "claws" for this category of AI assistants.

## Key Information

- First line of code written November 25, 2025; by February 2026, a white-labeled Open Claw hosting provider ran a Super Bowl ad
- "Has there ever been a project that got that level of success in that much time?" — Simon Wilson
- Over 1,000 people committed code to it
- Catastrophic from a security standpoint: people have lost Bitcoin wallets and other assets
- Demonstrates enormous demand for personal digital assistants — hundreds of thousands of people set it up despite the complexity and security risks
- Simon Wilson describes it as "almost exactly the thing I most argue against existing" — a system with access to private data, exposed to malicious instructions, and capable of exfiltration (the lethal trifecta)
- Despite security risks, Claude Opus mostly spots unsafe instructions and refuses them — but not 100% of the time
- Simon Wilson compared it to a Tamagotchi: "a digital pet, and you buy the Mac mini as an aquarium"
- Inspired the generic term "claws" (Nano Claw, etc.) — Simon predicts "building your own claw" will be the new "Hello World" of AI engineering
- Spider-Man 2 reference: Doc Ock's AI-controlled claws with an inhibitor chip — when the chip breaks, the claws control him
