## Definition

Silicon Valley is a region in the southern San Francisco Bay Area known as the global center for technology and innovation. Jia Chen emphasized the importance of placing herself in Silicon Valley when building a startup.

## Key Information

- Jia applied the concept of environment placement to her startup journey by going to Silicon Valley to be in "startup land"
- She drew a parallel between attending office hours in person for learning efficiency and being physically present in Silicon Valley for startup building
- The move to the Bay Area was a deliberate strategy to maximize second-order effects of being in the right environment
