## Definition
Flux 2 Klein is Black Forest Labs' interactive image generation and editing model, released January 2025, achieving sub-second generation (300ms) and editing (500ms) while maintaining quality on par with or better than competing open-source models.

## Key Information
- Released January 2025 as a step toward interactive editing and generation
- 500ms for image editing, 300ms for image generation — effectively real-time
- Available in 4B and 9B parameter sizes
- On par or better than other open-source models (Kwen at ~15-20s for same tasks)
- Multi-reference editing still under 1 second (vs. Kwen ~20s)
- Enables real-time mock-up rendering, interactive visual engines for gaming/films
- Represents BFL's push toward real-time visual intelligence
