## Definition
Gradium AI is a voice AI model provider spun off from Kyutai, the non-profit research lab. Their mission is to unlock the unrealized potential of voice AI by training voice models (speech-to-text, text-to-speech, speech-to-speech) as building blocks for voice agents and solutions. They do not work on orchestration or specific verticals — they are a pure model provider.

## Key Information
- Spin-off from Kyutai, a non-profit AI research lab
- CEO: Neil Zeghidour
- Mission: unlock the unrealized potential of voice AI
- Provides: speech-to-text, text-to-speech, speech-to-speech (transformation, translation, dialogue)
- Not working on orchestration or verticals — pure model provider for voice
- Key product: Gradium Phonon, an on-device TTS model running on smartphone CPU (<100M parameters)
- Voice cloning from ~10 seconds of audio
- Streaming STT and streaming TTS with voice cloning
- Semantic VAD (voice activity detection)
- Focused on science and engineering to solve the "last mile" of voice AI
- Open to private beta for Gradium Phonon
