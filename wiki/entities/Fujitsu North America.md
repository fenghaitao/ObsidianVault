## Definition

Fujitsu North America is the North American subsidiary of Fujitsu, a global technology and IT services company. Within the AI domain, the organization has worked on contact center Voice AI solutions that apply generative AI to extract structured business intelligence from audio streams.

## Key Information

- **Parent**: Fujitsu (global technology corporation)
- **AI Focus**: Emerging data technologies and AI architecture for enterprise applications
- **Notable Project**: A four-stage low-latency pipeline for contact centers that reduces after-call work time by ~50% through automated call summarization, intent recognition, and CRM integration
- **Domain Expertise**: Contact center operations, speech-to-text, generative AI orchestration, PII security in audio streams
