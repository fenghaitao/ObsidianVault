## Definition

Blade Runner is a science fiction film. Sam Lessin references the Voight-Kampff test from the film — the test used to determine whether someone is a real human or a replicant — as a metaphor for the AI detection problem: "are you a real or are you a bot?"

## Key Information

- Sam Lessin references the Voight-Kampff test as a framework for thinking about AI detection companies
- He invests in companies around the theme of "the Voight-Kampff test from Blade Runner" — determining real vs. bot
