## Definition

Science-fiction film referenced by [[Sam Lesson]] via its "Voight-Kampff test" — the fictional interrogation test used to distinguish humans from replicants — as an analogy for a real investment theme he's pursuing: distinguishing "real" from "bot" in an AI-saturated world.

## Key Information

- Lesson, describing what he looks for in AI-adjacent (not AI-branded) investments: "whether it's sublime security or outtake... things that are basically all around the theme of the Voight-Kampff test from great Blade Runner, which was the test they basically ran: are you real or are you a bot? ... that's a huge problem."
- Used to frame a category of startups addressing the societal/business implications of AI (authenticity verification, bot detection) as distinct from "AI-branded" startups he is skeptical of. See [[Terra Nova Narrative]].
