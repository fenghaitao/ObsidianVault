## Definition
The Vasa is a 17th-century Swedish warship that sank on its maiden voyage in Stockholm harbor, used by Bjarne Stroustrup as a cautionary tale for the C++ standards committee.

## Key Information
- Ordered by the Swedish king to be "the best and most beautiful battleship around," initially with one gun deck.
- Mid-build, the king ordered a second gun deck and more ornate statues, making the ship top-heavy without improving the foundation.
- Stability testing (the crew running side to side) was reportedly stopped after 7 of 14 runs because it looked dangerous.
- It sailed out, caught a gust of wind halfway across the harbor, capsized, and sank; preserved in low-oxygen water, it was recovered and is displayed in Stockholm.
- Bjarne's lessons for the standards committee: improve the foundation rather than piling on features; never compromise testing; professionals sometimes must say "no" to high management.
