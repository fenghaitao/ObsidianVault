## Definition

Succeeded [[Tamar Yehoshua]] as CPO of [[Slack]]; a prior [[Lenny's Podcast]] guest, worked at Slack since its early days.

## Key Information

- Kept a personal notebook (in his bag) of things he'd learned from [[Stewart Butterfield]] over the years, purely for his own genuine learning — later became key source material when Slack formally codified its product principles. Cited by [[Jessica Fain]] as a model example distinguishing genuine learning from "politicking." See [[Politics Vs Influence]].
- Credited (episode 44) as one of the people who suggested topics/questions for [[Lenny Rachitsky]]'s dedicated interview with [[Stewart Butterfield]].
