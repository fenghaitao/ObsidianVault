## Definition

Noah Weiss is a product leader who succeeded Tamar Yehoshua as CPO of Slack. He was an early Slack employee and previously a guest on Lenny's Podcast.

## Key Information

- Succeeded Tamar Yehoshua as CPO of Slack
- Early Slack employee
- Previously a guest on Lenny's Podcast
- Kept a small notebook with a section dedicated to "things I learned from Stuart [Butterfield] over the years" — used for genuine learning and growth, not for showing off
- This notebook later helped codify Slack's product principles when writing them with Ethan Eisman and Jessica Fain
- Example of learning from leaders with genuine curiosity rather than politicking
