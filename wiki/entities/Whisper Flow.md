## Definition

Whisper Flow is an AI-powered voice-to-text tool that Elena Verna uses extensively because she feels she "has no time to type anymore." She talks to her phone and laptop to get things done.

## Key Information

- Used by Elena Verna at Lovable for voice-to-text input
- Elena: "I feel like I have no time to type anymore. So I just talk to my phone and talk to my laptop all the time."
- Available as a free one-year subscription for annual subscribers of Lenny's Newsletter
- Part of the AI-native toolkit alongside ChatGPT, Granola, and Lovable
- Jason Cohen also recommends Whisper Flow for dictation, praising the keyboard shortcuts for use across all software
- [[Aishwaria Raanti]] recommends it as a "conceptual transcription tool" — when used with Codex, it identifies variables and seamlessly switches between transcription and instruction (e.g., saying "add three exclamation marks" actually adds the punctuation instead of writing the instruction)
- Zevi Arnovitz used Whisper Flow during his live demo on Lenny's Podcast to dictate feature requirements to Claude
- Marc Andreessen uses Whisper Flow and praises its ability to understand when you're giving it instructions ("bullet points over there") vs. dictating content — it distinguishes between commands and transcription
- Marc: "it's like a voice transcription function, but you can actually talk to the AI model while you're doing voice transcription"
