## Definition

Voice transcription app [[Marc Andreessen]] uses and praises, which understands spoken meta-instructions (e.g. "I want bullet points over there") as instructions rather than transcribing them literally.

## Key Information

- Andreessen cites it as a strong example of the emerging "voice input" product category he's bullish on, alongside AI voice-output products like [[Sesame]] and Grok's "Bad Rudy" character.
- Per [[Lazar]] (episode 27, professional vibe coding at [[Lovable]]): mentions Whisper Flow, alongside Lovable's own built-in voice-dictation function, as an option for starting a new project with a spoken "brain dump" prompt rather than typing.
- Per [[Jason Cohen]] (episode 30): his favorite recently-discovered product, praised for its keyboard shortcuts and everyday dictation use; a year of it is included in Lenny's newsletter "product pass" for annual subscribers.
- Per [[Aishwarya Naresh Reganti]] (episode 33): her favorite recently discovered product; praises it as a "conceptual transcription tool" that identifies context (e.g., variable names when dictating into Codex) and interprets spoken meta-instructions as instructions rather than transcribing them literally (e.g., saying "add three exclamation marks" actually adds them instead of writing out the phrase). Also included in Lenny's "product pass" for annual newsletter subscribers.
