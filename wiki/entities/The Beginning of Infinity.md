## Definition

*The Beginning of Infinity*, by David Deutsch, is named by [[Tomer Cohen]] as one of his most-recommended books in the episode's lightning round — one he calls a hard but powerful read.

## Key Information

- Central argument: genuine, sustained progress only becomes possible once you have a real causal explanation for why something happens, not merely a correlation or a description — and once you have that explanation, essentially unbounded ("infinite") further progress becomes possible on top of it.
- Cohen connects this directly to product-building: finding a true explanation for cause and effect in a product problem, rather than a surface-level pattern, is what allows genuine iteration and breakthroughs afterward.
- [[Lenny Rachitsky]] notes the book is frequently recommended by Naval Ravikant and that he found it a difficult read himself.
