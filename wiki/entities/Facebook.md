## Definition

Facebook (now Meta) is a social media company. Molly Graham describes its culture as a "19-year-old hacker's dorm room" focused on shipping above all else, deeply shaped by Mark Zuckerberg's personality.

## Key Information

- Culture described as "19-year-old hacker's dorm room" — shipping above all else
- Had three company goals for the entire five years Graham was there: growth (monthly active users), engagement, and revenue
- Engagement was the goal that "won in a fight" — it was the most important priority
- Goal-setting was done on a six-month cycle; annual goals were set but reset every six months
- The Facebook phone project was a "massive, costly failure" for the company
- Graham worked there for five years in HR and mobile roles
- 80% of the culture was defined by Mark Zuckerberg's personality
- Contrasted with Google's "two PhD students paradise" culture
- Facebook Marketplace was born from latent demand: 40% of posts in Facebook groups were buying and selling
- Facebook Dating was born from a similar observation: 60% of profile views were opposite-gender non-friends
- Boris Cherny was responsible for code quality across all of Meta's codebases (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp)
