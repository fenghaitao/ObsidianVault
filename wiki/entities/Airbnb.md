## Definition

Airbnb is a travel and accommodation marketplace. Lenny Rachitsky worked there for 7 years before leaving to start his newsletter. Sam Lessin also sold one of his companies to Airbnb.

## Key Information

- Lenny Rachitsky worked at Airbnb for 7 years as a software engineer and product manager
- Lenny's first Medium post was about what he learned at Airbnb; Brian Chesky shared it with the whole company
- Lenny left Airbnb with four plans (A-D), none of which included the newsletter
- When COVID hit, Airbnb's future looked uncertain ("RIP Airbnb all over Twitter"), which motivated Lenny to monetize his newsletter
- Local Mind (Lenny's startup) was acquired by Airbnb
- Sam Lessin sold a company to Airbnb
- When selling the company, Sam's advisor made a point of building a good relationship with the EA and office manager at Airbnb because "if they like you, it helps you get the thing"
