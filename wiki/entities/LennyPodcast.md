## Definition
Lenny's Podcast is a popular product management podcast and newsletter hosted by Lenny Rachitsky. Aman Khan collaborates with Lenny on educational content for AI product managers.

## Key Information
- Aman Khan has been working with Lenny on content for AI product managers.
- The podcast hosted a conference where both OpenAI CPO Kevin Weil and Anthropic CPO Mike Krieger spoke about LLM hallucinations and the importance of evals.
- Widely known among the PM community — a show of hands at the talk confirmed most attendees listen to it.
