## Definition

TV show about the oil/energy industry (Midland, Texas), named by [[Sam Lesson]] as his favorite recent show in the episode's lightning round.

## Key Information

- Lesson: "recent I think Land Man is fabulous... I'm like really into it."
- Used as a jumping-off point for a broader observation: tech/Silicon Valley's recent surge of interest in the energy industry, which Lesson attributes partly to the show's popularity. He recounts a founder based in Midland, Texas joking that "all these Silicon Valley people now think they understand the energy industry because they watched Land Man" — a dynamic Lesson says he's observed firsthand among venture capitalists.
