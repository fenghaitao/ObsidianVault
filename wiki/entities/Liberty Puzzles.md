## Definition

Liberty Puzzles makes thick, hand-cut wooden puzzles where the pieces are uniquely shaped — you never know what's an edge piece. Dr. Becky recommends them as a monotasking activity that provides grounded energy.

## Key Information

- Hand-cut thick wooden puzzles
- Tricky design: pieces don't clearly indicate edges, colors connect across pieces in unexpected ways
- Dr. Becky uses them as a monotasking activity: "you truly cannot multitask"
- Provides "grounded energy" — a contrast to constant multitasking
- Dr. Becky and her husband do puzzles together at night instead of working on the couch
- Similar to Stave Puzzles
