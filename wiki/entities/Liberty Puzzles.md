## Definition

Brand of thick, hand-cut wooden jigsaw puzzles that [[Dr. Becky Kennedy]] named as a recently discovered product she really likes, in the lightning-round segment of [[Lenny's Podcast]] episode 28.

## Key Information

- Kennedy: pieces are hand-cut with irregular, non-obvious edges (colors that connect can be the end of one color and start of another), which makes them tricky.
- She values them because puzzling forces monotasking — something she finds hard to do generally despite it giving her "grounded energy." She and her husband now do them together at night instead of each being on their laptops on the couch.
