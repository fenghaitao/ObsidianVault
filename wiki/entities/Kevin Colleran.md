## Definition

Partner at [[Slow Ventures]] alongside [[Sam Lesson]] (transcribed as "Kevin Collard" / "Kevin" in the episode). Cited as a model of exacting etiquette practice, especially around respecting the people who serve founders and LPs.

## Key Information

- Described by Lesson as "maniacal" about a specific gesture: no matter who is in the room, if meeting with a limited partner (LP), he takes the coffee cup or diet coke away himself, or at minimum makes a point of asking where to put it and acknowledging the mess — even when someone else will clearly clean up after them.
- Framed as the embodiment of the episode's broader "respect the gatekeepers/staff" principle — treating servers, EAs, and office managers as part of the team rather than invisible labor.
- Credited (alongside an unnamed advisor helping with the sale) for building a strong relationship with the EA and office manager at [[Airbnb]] during the sale of one of Lesson's companies to Airbnb, which Lesson says materially helped the deal go smoothly.
