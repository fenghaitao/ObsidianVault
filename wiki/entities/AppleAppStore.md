# Apple App Store

The Apple App Store is Apple's primary distribution platform for iOS and iPadOS applications. It serves as the central marketplace where users discover, download, and manage applications for their Apple devices.

## Key Information

- **Owner:** Apple Inc.
- **Platform:** iOS and iPadOS
- **Launch:** 2008
- **Primary function:** Distribution, discovery, and management of applications for Apple mobile devices

## Distribution Hub

The App Store is the official channel through which developers publish applications to reach Apple's user base. All iOS apps distributed through the App Store undergo Apple's review process for quality and security standards.
