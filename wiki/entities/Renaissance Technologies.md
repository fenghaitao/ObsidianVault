## Definition

Renaissance Technologies ("RenTech") is a legendary quantitative trading firm that Nimit Sohoni cited as the "gold standard" of the space.

## Key Information

- Nimit called RenTech "one of the mythical firms in the space — you can't really argue with their returns"; historical returns over a 20–30 year period are "pretty insane."
- He described it as "maybe the gold standard, depending on who you ask," before listing larger well-regarded firms and elite smaller firms that follow its profile.
