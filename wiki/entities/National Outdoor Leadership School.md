## Definition

The National Outdoor Leadership School (NOLS) is where [[Molly Graham]] took her first job out of college, leading 75-day wilderness expeditions in Patagonia and Alaska for mostly college-age students. It's also where she first learned the "[[Waterline Model]]."

## Key Information

- Teaches leadership and communication skills through wilderness expeditions — Graham led groups of peers she didn't previously know across multi-month trips.
- Source of Graham's earliest exposure to the "[[Waterline Model]]," which she later brought into her corporate scaling/management work and now teaches inside [[Glue Club]].
