## Definition

Amazon Alexa is Amazon's voice-controlled AI assistant platform, deployed at massive scale across consumer devices and enterprise applications. It is cited as one of the most scaled AI deployments globally.

## Key Information

- Described by Dr. Satya Nitta as one of the "most scaled AI deployments on the planet."
- Members of the Emergence team were previously involved in building and deploying Alexa at Amazon.
- Represents large-scale distributed AI systems experience that informs Emergence's approach to enterprise AI agents.
