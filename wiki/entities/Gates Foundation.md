## Definition

The Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation is a philanthropic organization that funded Philip Su's nonprofit Outeré, which built free software for global health deployed in Nigeria, Kenya, and South Africa for malaria rapid testing.

## Key Information

- The Gates Foundation funded Outeré, Philip Su's Seattle-based nonprofit
- Outeré built free software for global health, specifically assisting rapid testing for malaria
- The software was deployed in Nigeria, Kenya, and South Africa
- Philip felt "very fortunate and lucky" to have been chosen for a Gates Foundation grant
