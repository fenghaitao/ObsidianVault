## Definition
Stadia was Google's cloud game streaming platform, launched in 2019 and shut down in 2023. Guillaume Vernade worked on Stadia before moving to Google DeepMind.

## Key Information
- Cloud game streaming service by Google
- Launched November 2019, shut down January 2023
- Allowed playing games via streaming without dedicated gaming hardware
- Guillaume Vernade worked as a video game producer on Stadia before joining DeepMind
- Referenced as an example of Google's pattern of launching and killing products
- Vernade's background in game production informs his interest in real-time adaptive music (Lyria Real-Time) for game soundtracks
