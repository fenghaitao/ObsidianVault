## Definition
Armin Ronacher is the creator of Flask, the popular Python web framework. He has spoken about the paradigm shift in working with AI, articulating that "we're no longer just using machines — we're now working with them."

## Key Information
- Creator of Flask, a widely-used Python web framework
- Quoted by Brendan O'Leary: "We're no longer just using machines. We're now working with them." — capturing the shift from tool-use to collaboration with AI
- Reports gaining more than 30% of time in his day because AI handles much of the work
- Attributes his productivity gains to knowing what to hand off to AI and what to keep for himself — directing the work rather than blindly accepting suggestions
- His framing of "working with" vs "using" AI is central to the agentic engineering paradigm
