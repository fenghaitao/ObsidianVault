## Definition
Armin Ronacher is the creator of Flask, the popular Python web framework, and co-founder of Earendil. With 20 years of software development experience (mostly in open source), he has become a prominent voice in the AI engineering space, advocating for intentional friction, agent-legible codebases, and the importance of human judgment in AI-assisted development.

## Key Information
- Creator of Flask, a widely-used Python web framework
- 20 years of software development experience, mostly in open source
- Co-founder of Earendil (started October 2024), a company making sense of AI engineering tools
- Previously worked at Sentry (left April 2024), which coincided with the rise of Claude Code and agent engineering
- Quoted by Brendan O'Leary: "We're no longer just using machines. We're now working with them."
- Reports gaining more than 30% of time in his day because AI handles much of the work
- Advocates for intentional friction: "Without friction there's no steering"
- Argues that agents optimize for making progress, not correctness, creating brittle systems
- Promotes agent-legible codebase design: modularization, no hidden magic, mechanical enforcement via linting rules
- Built a PyExtension for code review that separates mechanical bugs from human-judgment callouts (database migrations, permission changes, dependency additions)
