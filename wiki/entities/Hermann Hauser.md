## Definition

Long-time colleague of Tony Fadell (acquainted since 1987); creator of Acorn Computer (the "Apple II of the UK" in the 1970s) and co-founder of ARM (Acorn RISC Machine), the processor architecture spun out of Acorn.

## Key Information

- Founded Acorn Computer, described as the UK's equivalent of the Apple II in the 1970s.
- Co-founded ARM (Acorn RISC Machine — "Acorn RISC" gives ARM its name), the low-power processor architecture later used in the iPod and countless mobile devices.
- Posed (via Lenny) the question that framed a major part of the episode: "How do you decide what is worth building?"
- Tony Fadell's answer, attributed to lessons learned partly through this relationship: always start from pain, then ask whether new technology exists to solve that pain in a genuinely different way.
