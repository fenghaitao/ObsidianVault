## Definition

Muay Thai is a type of martial arts. Amol Avaisary practiced Muay Thai for many years until he suffered a traumatic brain injury from a kick to the head during sparring in early 2022.

## Key Information

- A type of martial arts
- Amol Avaisary practiced it for many years "never had a problem"
- He suffered a traumatic brain injury in early 2022 from a kick to the head during a normal sparring session — "the wrong hit to the head in the wrong way"
- The injury led to 9 months of recovery, during which he couldn't walk, look at screens, or listen to music for extended periods
