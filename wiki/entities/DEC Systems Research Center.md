## Definition

DEC Systems Research Center (transcribed in the episode as "deck circ") is Digital Equipment Corporation's research lab, which Leslie Lamport joined in 1985, and where he developed the Paxos algorithm.

## Key Information

- Founded by people who came from Xerox PARC and had invented personal computing and the Ethernet.
- Built one of the first distributed operating systems, with all computers on a single Ethernet network sharing common storage.
- Had code (in the operating system) for maintaining consistency of that storage, but no stated algorithm — Lamport set out to prove it impossible and instead ended up deriving Paxos.
