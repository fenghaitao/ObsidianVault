## Definition
Firecrawl is a web scraping service used for extracting content from dynamic websites and sites that may block traditional scraping. It was used alongside Apify in the Towards AI deep research agent for scraping web pages provided as research links.

## Key Information
- Web scraping service for dynamic and anti-scraping websites
- Used in the Towards AI deep research agent alongside Apify
- Part of the content ingestion pipeline: scrape provided links before deep web search
- Chosen for its ability to handle websites that block traditional scraping approaches
