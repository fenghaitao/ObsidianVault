## Definition
The Sainsbury Centre is a museum and gallery located in Norwich, UK. It is run by Jago, the former head of the Americas at the British Museum. The building is known as the filming location for the Avengers headquarters in the Marvel Avengers films.

## Key Information
- Run by Jago, former head of the Americas at the British Museum
- Jago is leading an academic process to determine what inanimate objects should sound like when given AI voices
- The process considers material origins (where the rock came from), crafting location (where it was carved), and exhibition history (where it has lived)
- Example: a statue from Chinese mountain rock, carved in Vietnam, displayed in Britain for 200 years — what accent should its AI voice have?
- This philosophical work is important for ElevenLabs and the broader industry as voice AI extends to everyday objects like elevators
- The building served as the Avengers headquarters in the Marvel films
- Represents the cultural/academic perspective on voice design for objects
