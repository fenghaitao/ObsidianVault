## Definition
Ryan Lopopolo is a member of technical staff at OpenAI who coined the term "Harness Engineering." He is a self-described "token billionaire" who spent 9 months building software exclusively with agents, banning his team from touching their editors.

## Key Information
- Member of technical staff at OpenAI
- Coined "Harness Engineering" — the discipline of building systems and guardrails that enable agents to do the full job of software engineering
- Self-described "token billionaire" — spends over a billion output tokens per day (~$1,000+)
- Banned his team from touching their editors for 9 months, requiring all code to be produced through agents
- Built internal tools at OpenAI to improve co-worker productivity, with localization and internationalization from day one
- Authored the article "Harness Engineering" and appeared on the Latent Space podcast
- Presented at AIE CODE 2026 in London
- His team operates a 750-package pnpm workspace structured for agent efficiency
- Advocates for "Garbage Collection Day" — a weekly practice of systematically eliminating slop patterns
- Believes every "continue" typed to an agent is a failure of the harness
- Future vision: take a token budget and a quarter/half/year of work, give it to machines, and have them continually advance the product
