## Definition

Vincent Koc is an AI evaluation researcher at Comet and a core contributor to OpenClaw. He self-describes as "the friendly can car" — someone who lives on the edge of technology, experimenting with emerging tools before they are mainstream. He works with universities and benchmarks evals for large organizations including Uber, Netflix, and UK banks.

## Key Information

- **Role**: Eval researcher at [[Comet]], working on AI evaluation and benchmarking
- **OpenClaw contribution**: Core contributor to the [[OpenClaw]] agent harness project
- **Research focus**: Malleable evals — adaptive evaluation methodologies for self-optimizing AI agents
- **Keynote speaker**: Presented at AI engineering conferences on the evolution from static benchmarks to intent-based evaluation
- **Methodology**: Works with universities on eval research; runs benchmarks for enterprise organizations
- **Philosophy**: Advocates for treating evals as living, self-optimizing agents rather than static datasets; emphasizes that the "janky, weird" edge of technology is where real learning happens
