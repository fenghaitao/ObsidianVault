## Definition

Vincent Koc is an AI evaluation researcher at Comet and a core maintainer at OpenClaw. He self-describes as "the friendly can car" — someone who lives on the edge of technology, experimenting with emerging tools before they are mainstream. He works with universities and benchmarks evals for large organizations including Uber, Netflix, and UK banks. At OpenClaw, he is one of the most prolific contributors, hitting close to 3,000 commits/day at peak velocity.

## Key Information

- **Role**: Eval researcher at [[Comet]], working on AI evaluation and benchmarking
- **OpenClaw contribution**: Core maintainer of the [[OpenClaw]] agent harness project; one of ~10-15 core maintainers
- **Research focus**: Malleable evals — adaptive evaluation methodologies for self-optimizing AI agents
- **Dark Factory methodology**: Runs 5-20+ parallel agent "swim lanes" simultaneously — treating engineering as factory management
- **Velocity**: Hit ~3,000 commits/day at peak; sustained velocity of hundreds of commits daily
- **Infrastructure**: Uses Codex with Git worktrees (70-80 active daily), custom session management to handle the load
- **Skills approach**: Maintains open-source ".skills" (like dotfiles) on GitHub for agent workflows, technical documentation
- **Management background**: Previously managed 30-40+ people in large organizations (airlines, etc.) — directly applies people management skills to agent management
- **Philosophy**: "2025 was about token maxing. 2026 is about not wasting them. It's about token efficiency. It's about agent in the loop."
- **Intuition**: Developed ability to "feel the reasoning tokens" — can detect when an agent is going off-track by how it explains itself, not just what it does
