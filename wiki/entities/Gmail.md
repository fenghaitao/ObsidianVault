## Definition

Gmail is Google's email service. Claude Code (Co-work) can integrate with Gmail to read and respond to emails on behalf of users.

## Key Information

- Co-work can use Gmail to send and read emails
- Boris uses Co-work to respond to his top emails automatically
- Part of Co-work's tool integrations for non-technical task automation
