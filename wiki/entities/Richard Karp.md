## Definition

Richard Karp is a computer scientist famous for NP-completeness reductions — showing that many natural problems are NP-complete.

## Key Information

- Wigderson recalls meeting Karp ("Darp") at his first conference: a first-year graduate student was introduced to Karp, who "invented... all these complete things," and Karp immediately asked what he was working on and was "actually interested."
- The anecdote illustrates Wigderson's point that the field has no hierarchies and is remarkably accommodating to young people.
