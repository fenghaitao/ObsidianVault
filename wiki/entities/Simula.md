## Definition
Simula is the pioneering general-purpose language that introduced the class concept and object-oriented programming, created by Ole-Johan Dahl and Kristen Nygaard.

## Key Information
- Bjarne learned Simula at Cambridge and wrote his PhD distributed-systems simulator in it.
- "Very good" at simulation, but "misnamed" — it was a general-purpose language, and the bad name hurt adoption.
- The simulator worked on small examples but a full-scale run needed too much mainframe time, so he was kicked off the machine.
- Simula was proprietary and never ported to the CAP computer, forcing a painful rewrite in BCPL.
- Its class concept was the key idea Bjarne took into C: "take the class concept from Simula and stick it into C."
- C++ "follows Simula's model of defining types, defining classes, and defining class hierarchies."
