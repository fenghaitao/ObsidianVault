## Definition

"The Mythical Man-Month" is a classic software engineering book by Fred Brooks (published in the 1970s). Sherwin Wu draws on its "surgeon" metaphor for software engineering, where one expert surgeon does the core work while everyone else supports them.

## Key Information

- Written by Fred Brooks, published in the 1970s
- Predicted that software engineering might move toward a "surgeon" model: one person doing the core work while others support (like nurses, residents, fellows in an operating room)
- Sherwin Wu uses this metaphor as his management philosophy: treat engineers like surgeons and the manager's job is to hand them scalpels before they ask
- The book's prediction hasn't fully played out in practice (software engineering is more collaborative), but the metaphor remains powerful for management
