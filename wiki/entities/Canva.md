## Definition

Canva is a graphic design platform, mentioned by Lenny as an example of perseverance — CEO Melanie Perkins went through a hundred investor rejections before finding investment.

## Key Information

- CEO Melanie Perkins was rejected by a hundred investors before someone invested
- Butterfield distinguishes this from pivoting: Perkins always believed in the product and vision; she was just trying to find the right articulation to get investors
- Jason Lemkin contrasts Canva's old workflow ("fire up Canva and try to create things or even worse, wait four days for someone to do it for you") with modern AI imaging tools like Reeve that generate images instantly from prompts
