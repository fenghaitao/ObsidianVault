## Definition
Canva is a graphic design platform and one of Black Forest Labs' enterprise customers, using BFL's image generation and editing models.

## Key Information
- Enterprise customer of Black Forest Labs
- Uses BFL's FLUX models for image generation and editing capabilities
- Part of BFL's enterprise customer portfolio alongside Microsoft, Adobe, and Mistral
