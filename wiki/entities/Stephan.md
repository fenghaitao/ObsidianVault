## Definition

Stephan is the co-founder of Hello Interview and Evan King's former manager at Meta. He was described by Evan as the most brilliant person he had ever worked with and the biggest influence on his career.

## Key Information

- Was Evan King's first manager at Meta — the manager who proactively created opportunities for Evan (tech lead of graphic violence, the Realtime Integrity team)
- Evan described him as an "incredible leader" whose path up the chain was fast and whose influence was fantastic
- Had a significant role in advocating behind the scenes for Evan's promotions and the creation of the Realtime Integrity team
- Moved to a different team at Meta as an M2 before reaching out to Evan about Web3 and crypto ideas
- Co-founded the first Web3 startup with Evan (social intelligence layer), which grew to ~100,000 users and was acquired
- Co-founded a second product (Figma plugin to paste websites as editable frames), which was also acquired
- Did the majority of the reverse engineering work to decrypt Figma's paste functionality
- Co-founded Hello Interview with Evan, currently the only two full-time employees
- Evan described their relationship as "partners" — Stephan was respectful of Evan's needs and never pressured him to leave Meta
