## Definition

The Christchurch mosque shooting occurred on March 15, 2019 in Christchurch, New Zealand, where a gunman livestreamed the shooting of a mosque, killing 51 people. This tragedy was the catalyst for Meta creating the Realtime Integrity team.

## Key Information

- Happened on March 15, 2019 — a gunman livestreamed the shooting of a mosque in Christchurch, New Zealand, killing 51 people
- The livestream was on Facebook for 1.5 minutes before being detected — Meta "blew it" and "missed it"
- Copies of the video spread rapidly across the platform as people downloaded and re-shared it
- Evan King was the technical lead in Facebook's response, working in a war room with the VP of Integrity and head of content policy
- The response involved many nights of work, training models overnight, adjusting content detection algorithms, and updating photo/video banks
- The incident led to the realization that Meta was underinvested in real-time detection of the "worst of the worst atrocities on live video"
- This directly led to the creation of the Realtime Integrity team, with Evan King and a PM tasked with building it from scratch
- Mark Zuckerberg was "breathing down the neck" of leadership about preventing another such incident
