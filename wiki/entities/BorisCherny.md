## Definition

Boris Cherny is the Head of Claude Code at [[Anthropic]]. He leads the team building Anthropic's agentic coding tool and has spoken at [[CodeWithClaude]] developer conferences about the philosophy behind AI-assisted software development.

## Key Information

- Head of Claude Code at [[Anthropic]].
- Delivered the keynote at Code w/ Claude London 2026 (May 20–21), where he framed the event's theme around collapsing the distance between "I have an idea" and "it runs."
- In his keynote, he described the first time he felt the "magic" of coding: in secondary school, he wrote TI-83 programs that solved his math homework and tests, and taught himself HTML to make his eBay listings for Pokémon cards sell better. He learned by tinkering, and when something ran, it was exciting.
- He argued that programming got complicated over time — compilers, typecheckers, build systems — with each layer pushing the distance between idea and execution further out. With AI agents, that distance is collapsing again: you describe a problem, and the program shows up. "It's the calculator feeling, except the calculator can write a distributed system."
