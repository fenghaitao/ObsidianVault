## Definition

Owner.com is an "AI for restaurants" company, crossing $100 million in revenue, whose CEO Kyle sits on a board with [[Jason Lemkin]]. Cited as a concrete example of AI-driven sales-rep productivity gains.

## Key Information

- Has roughly 100 salespeople; CEO Kyle is targeting $3-5 million in revenue per rep with AI assistance, versus what Lemkin estimates would have been $300-500K per rep for a comparable company three or four years ago — "an order of magnitude more efficiency," per Lemkin, while still needing more reps to hit next year's growth target (i.e., AI efficiency gains don't necessarily shrink total headcount at fast-growing companies).
