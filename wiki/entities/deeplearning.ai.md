## Definition
deeplearning.ai is an AI education platform founded by Andrew Ng that offers courses on generative AI, large language models, and related topics, including a course on "Generative AI with LLMs" developed in collaboration with AWS.

## Key Information
- Founded by Andrew Ng
- Offers the "Generative AI with LLMs" course, which AWS colleagues collaborated on with Andrew Ng's team
- Course is available on both deeplearning.ai and Coursera
- Recommended by Antje Barth as a resource for understanding generative AI fundamentals
