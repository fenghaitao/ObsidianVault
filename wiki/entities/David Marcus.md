## Definition

David Marcus is the executive who led Messenger and then Meta's crypto project (Novi/Libra), and later co-founded Lightspark with James Everingham.

## Key Information

- Ran Messenger at Meta; recruited James into the crypto mission ("the mental virus" — "it takes about 24 hours for it to infect people").
- Led Novi/Libra; the org grew to ~3,500 people but never shipped under political and regulatory pressure.
- Left Meta for his "entrepreneurial roots" and co-founded Lightspark with James.
