## Definition

ALGOL (ALGOrithmic Language) is an early, influential family of programming languages that became a foundation for language design and structured programming.

## Key Information

- Peter Naur was a major figure in standardizing ALGOL and writing up its standard, and is the "Naur" in Backus-Naur form (BNF).
- ALGOL's notation and ideas fed later language design, making it a recurring touchstone in structured-programming history.
