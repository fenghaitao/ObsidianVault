## Definition

Microsoft 365 Copilot is Microsoft's enterprise AI productivity platform that integrates large language models with Microsoft 365 applications (email, chats, meetings, files, and more) to deliver AI-powered work assistance at scale.

## Key Information

- Enterprise platform bringing AI capabilities to millions of Microsoft 365 licensed customers.
- Offers model choice: as of September 2025, supports Claude Sonnet 4 and Claude Opus 4.1 alongside existing model options, giving organizations flexibility to select the right AI for their needs.
- Two primary integration points for Claude:
  - **Researcher agent**: A reasoning agent for complex, multistep research tasks — can reason over web, third-party data, emails, chats, meetings, and files. Powered by Claude Opus 4.1 (user-selectable).
  - **[[CopilotStudio]]**: Enables building, orchestrating, and managing enterprise-grade custom agents with multiagent systems and prompt tools.
- Access to Anthropic models initially rolls out via the **Frontier Program** — Microsoft's opt-in early-access program for M365 Copilot licensed customers.
- Anthropic models are hosted outside Microsoft-managed environments and subject to Anthropic's Terms and Conditions.
- Organization admins enable Anthropic model access through the Microsoft 365 admin center.
