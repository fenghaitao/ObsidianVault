## Definition

Marc Brooker (transcribed in the episode body as "Mark Brooker," though the title spells it "Marc") is a Distinguished Engineer at AWS who works on databases and distributed systems; he was on the AWS Lambda team and led the Aurora Serverless and Aurora D SQL work.

## Key Information

- Spent roughly two decades at AWS and estimated he read between 3,000 and 4,000 industry postmortems and Amazon COEs over his career.
- Stayed on-call for ~15 years because on-call and deeply analyzing postmortems/COEs is where most of his in-practice distributed-systems knowledge came from.
- On the Lambda team in 2020, heard customers excited about serverless/containers but frustrated that relational databases were still operationally "serverful"; this customer signal plus technical trends led him to join Aurora and build Aurora Serverless and then Aurora D SQL.
- Argues caches carry a metastable failure mode and that he prefers teams avoid caching where possible.
- Writes a blog (including a series on how AI may impact software engineering) to scale the reach of his expertise and to force mental clarity.
- Recommends Martin Kleppmann's Designing Data-Intensive Applications, Hennessy & Patterson's computer architecture, and quantitative systems design books.
- Advice to his younger self: be bolder about changing teams/organizations when learning and impact taper — he changed organizations ~4 times and thinks ~5–6 would have been optimal.
