## Definition
Alex Stepanov is the creator of the C++ Standard Template Library (STL).

## Key Information
- Transcribed in the episode as "Alex Stefanov."
- Bjarne credits him (and the STL) with rescuing C++ from the absence of a standard library: "we got saved by Alex Stefanov with the STL ... that was real luck."
