# Lonely Planet

Lonely Planet is one of the world's most trusted sources of travel information, known for its travel guides covering destinations globally. Its engineering team adopted generative AI to help customers access its decades of travel content.

## Key Information

- Type: Travel information and media company
- Known for: World-class travel guides and destination content
- SVP of Engineering and Data Science: Chris Whyde

## AI Adoption

In 2023, Lonely Planet adopted [[Claude2]] on [[AmazonBedrock]] to:
- Unlock decades of accumulated travel content
- Help customers plan memorable trips
- Integrate generative AI in a scalable, reliable, and secure manner

*"We've turned to Claude 2 on Amazon Bedrock to integrate generative AI in a scalable, reliable, and secure way, making it easier than ever for our customers to access our world-class travel content when and how they want."* — Chris Whyde, SVP of Engineering and Data Science
