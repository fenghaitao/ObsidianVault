## Definition

Mythos is an incredibly powerful AI model built by Anthropic, described as being so capable that "people are a little afraid of what it can do." As of the podcast recording, it was still in preview.

## Key Information

- Still in preview at time of recording — not yet publicly launched
- Described as "incredibly powerful" — so powerful that people are "a little afraid of what it can do"
- Anthropic uses the models internally, which has increased their rate of shipping "a little bit"
- Not the primary driver of Anthropic's shipping velocity — they've been moving fast for several quarters before Mythos
- Cat Wu: "We are very lucky to be able to work with the frontier models"
