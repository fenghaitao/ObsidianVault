## Definition
Mythos is a frontier AI model that was anticipated but not yet released at the time of Chris Parsons' workshop (early May 2026). Parsons referenced it as a potential game-changer that could make skills unnecessary — "Maybe we won't even need skills. Maybe we'll just say, 'Write a newsletter.' And it'll do it."

## Key Information
- Referenced as an upcoming model that was expected to be released around the time of the workshop but was delayed
- Parsons noted he was "quite glad they didn't actually release Mythos yesterday because I don't think it would be working today if they did"
- Some people who have used it say "it's good, but it's mostly marketing"
- Parsons speculates it could change the need for skills and structured prompts entirely
- Represents the uncertainty in AI tooling — what works today may not be needed tomorrow
