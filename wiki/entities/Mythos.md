## Definition

Mythos is an [[Anthropic]] frontier AI model referenced extensively by [[Adam Mosseri]], who spent significant time with it in the week of this interview.

## Key Information

- Mosseri describes it as the first model that made him feel like he was "just talking to a much more technical, much smarter engineer" than himself.
- Praised for its willingness to push back rather than act as a "pleaser" — Mosseri says Mythos has "gotten really good at being like, I can't do this, let's move on," and explicitly recommends picking a model that pushes back when using AI for strategy work, since a model that just agrees isn't useful for real strategic thinking.
- Contrasted with [[Fable]], a smaller/cheaper model Mosseri considers likely sufficient for side projects.
