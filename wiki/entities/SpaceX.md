## Definition

Aerospace company associated with [[Elon Musk]], cited by [[Sam Lesson]] as an example of narrative-driven valuation in his Contrarian Corner argument about AI startup investing.

## Key Information

- Lesson, discussing his skepticism of seed investing in "AI companies": "if the storytelling of Elon allows SpaceX, which by the way I love — I think SpaceX is an awesome company — but if all of a sudden that actually can be worth 1.4 trillion dollars to the public market, guess what? The money powers are going to do great with all this narrative-driven... religion."
- Used as a real-world proof point that belief/storytelling alone can sustain extremely high valuations for a genuinely great company, distinct from (but structurally similar to) the "AI god narrative" he's skeptical of for most seed-stage "AI companies." See [[Terra Nova Narrative]].
- Per [[Dan Shipper]] (in an earlier, separate episode of [[Lenny's Podcast]]): reported to have essentially, though not fully, acquired [[Cursor]] around that episode's timeframe.
