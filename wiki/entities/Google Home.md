## Definition
Google Home is Google's smart speaker. Kitze used it with Google Assistant and IFTTT to voice-log todos around the house before modern AI agents existed.

## Key Information
- Used by Kitze for voice-based todo logging via Google Assistant + IFTTT
- IFTTT supported splitting the command: "tell my assistant to [task]" → second half sent to any IFTTT service
- Part of Kitze's early journey toward offloading thoughts to a system
