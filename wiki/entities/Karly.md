## Definition

Karly (also referred to as Carly in the transcript) is an engineering manager at a FAANG company who manages Staff Engineers. She has experience managing approximately 44 engineers under her.

## Key Information

- Panelist on the "Getting To Staff" discussions (Sept 2023 and Feb 2024)
- Works at Activision (not a FAANG company) as a Senior Manager while also holding an IC title equivalent to Staff (called "Lead Engineer" at Activision)
- Finished grad school in 2017; moved up from Junior Data Scientist fairly quickly; started at Activision at the Senior level and took about two years to get to Lead
- Works in security at Activision, making video games (Call of Duty)
- Manages staff engineers as part of her role
- At Activision, has 44 engineers under her, though not all of them are direct reports
- Her promotion to staff was triggered by a presentation to an executive leader and the CTO, where she demonstrated the ability to speak technically to a non-technical audience
- The CTO offered her a job working for him after that presentation, leading to an internal move to staff
- Reported that 50% of the time when approached about staff promotion, she felt the person wasn't ready; the other 50% she felt they were ready long ago
- Believes it's better to ask too early than too late for promotion, as long as you can take feedback
- Emphasized that a manager has no incentive to gatekeep promotion — if you're ready, it's in their best interest to fight for you
- Highlighted that how you handle feedback is as much a part of whether you're ready for promotion as anything else
- Argued that if you take feedback and get combative, you're proving the point that you're not ready
- Posts on LinkedIn and Instagram, pivoting toward humor and levity content
- Also known as Carly in the Sept 2023 transcript
