## Definition

AI researcher who wrote "The Bitter Lesson" (~2019 blog post, referenced by [[Boris Cherny]] as ~10 years old), arguing that more general models/methods reliably outperform more specific, hand-engineered ones over the long run. See [[Bitter Lesson]].

## Key Information

- Per [[Lenny Rachitsky]] (episode 40, referencing a separate [[Dwarkesh Patel]] podcast appearance): argued that LLMs may be something of a dead end and that progress toward AGI will plateau because of fundamental limits in how they learn — a view [[Edwin Chen]] (CEO, [[Surge AI]]) says he substantially agrees with, believing something beyond current LLM architectures will likely be needed.
