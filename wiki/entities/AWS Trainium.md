## Definition
AWS Trainium is Amazon Web Services' custom-built chip for AI training and inference, mentioned as part of a trend toward specialized heterogeneous compute in data centers.

## Key Information
- AWS's custom AI chip for training and inference workloads
- Cerebras is noted as doing similar heterogeneous pairing work with Trainium chips
- Part of the broader industry trend toward specialized, heterogeneous hardware for different parts of the AI workload
- Alex Cheema cites this as evidence that the same heterogeneous approach will come to local inference
