## Definition
Cruise is a self-driving car company where Aman Khan previously worked as a PM for evaluation systems (2018-2019), before moving to Spotify and then Arize.

## Key Information
- Aman Khan started his career as an engineer at Cruise working on self-driving cars.
- He became a PM for evaluation systems at Cruise, building expertise in how to evaluate nondeterministic AI systems.
- The eval development loop at Cruise followed a pattern: start with simple scenarios (straight roads), curate datasets of hard examples (left turns, pedestrians), and iterate until performance meets thresholds.
- This self-driving eval experience directly informed his approach to LLM agent evaluation at Arize.
