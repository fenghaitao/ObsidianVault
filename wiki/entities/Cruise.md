## Definition

Cruise (transcribed "Cruz" in the episode) is the self-driving/autonomous-vehicle company, backed by General Motors, where Igor worked between Google and Meta.

## Key Information

- Igor found his first year at Cruise difficult to ramp up, but eventually found a relative "comfort zone" where he could code, mentor, and act as a (uber) tech lead.
- The company went "bad" after an accident, followed by layoffs and steady attrition, which prompted Igor to leave.
- Per Igor, Cruise's smaller size meant less infrastructure to learn and a less crowded senior space, making it easier to carve out scope than at Meta.
