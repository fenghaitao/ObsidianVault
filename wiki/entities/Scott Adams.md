## Definition

Creator of *Dilbert*, cited by [[Marc Andreessen]] (noting his recent passing) for career advice about combining skills: the "additive effect" of being good at two things is more than double, and at three things more than triple.

## Key Information

- Adams's own framing: he wasn't the world's best cartoonist or the world's best business person, but being a cartoonist who deeply understood business made him "spectacularly great" at making Dilbert — a combination neither a pure cartoonist nor a pure businessperson could have produced.
- Cited by Andreessen as the origin of what he calls the [[Skill Combination Premium]] — you become a "super relevant specialist" in the intersection of domains, worth more than the sum of being competent in each alone.
