## Definition

Lovable is a platform that lets anyone create software through conversation. Founded by Anton Osika, it focuses on unlocking new economies by empowering every human who wants to create something. The partnership with Anthropic evolved from customer-vendor to collaborative building.

## Claude Opus 4.6 Early Access (February 2026)

The models/evals team ran structured benchmarks while engineers booked "vibe check" time building apps with the new model. Engineering lead Alexandre Pesant's own stress test — a subway-mapping/itinerary side project that had stalled on previous models — pushed further with max effort turned up than he expected: "It went further than others." He also noted a broader autonomy shift from the model's ability to use the browser and test its own work. Co-founder Fabian Hedin: "Claude Opus 4.6 is an uplift in design quality. It's more autonomous, which is core to Lovable's values. People should be creating things that matter, not micromanaging AI."
