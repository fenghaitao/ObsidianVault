## Definition

Lovable is an AI-powered app builder (vibe coding platform) that lets users build working apps and websites by chatting with AI. It hit $200M ARR in under one year with ~100 employees, making it one of the fastest-growing companies in history.

## Key Information

- Hit $200M ARR in under one year; 8 months to $100M, then 4 months to $200M; over 8 million users
- Originally existed as GPT Engineer before officially launching as Lovable in November 2024
- Based in Stockholm, Sweden; ~100 employees (tripled from 30 six months prior)
- Raised a Series B at a $6 billion valuation
- Key use cases: founders building startups (non-technical founders), employees building internal tools and prototypes, personal projects
- "Lovable" branding drives internal culture: if something isn't lovable, it gets fixed immediately
- Internal tools are built on Lovable (dogfooding); specs include Lovable prototypes
- Community runs on Discord with hundreds of thousands of members
- Growth strategy: building in public, giving product away for free, influencer marketing, shipping velocity
- Growth team spends 95% on innovation, 5% on optimization (inverse of traditional companies)
- Sponsor of Lenny's Podcast
- Product is pink/purple/heart branded; ~20% women users
- Competes in the AI coding/vibe coding space with Replit and Bolt; Bolt is described as "a distant number three behind Replit and Lovable"
- Example of lead-rich AI company: Bolt closed a seven-figure deal "stolen from Lovable because no one called them back at Lovable" — even the hottest companies can't service all their demand
- Represents the "hypergrowth AI-era company" end of the bifurcated B2B market
- Zevi Arnovitz started his AI building journey with Lovable and Bolt after watching YouTube videos about them while traveling in Japan
- Zevi's key insight about Lovable and Bolt: their system prompt makes them "super eager to write code" — they start coding immediately instead of planning first
- This eagerness is fun at the start but causes problems with complex features (payments, database changes) where planning is critical
- Zevi eventually graduated from Lovable to Cursor with Claude Code for more control over planning and architecture
- Zevi: "I don't know if you can't build really amazing production apps using Bolt or Lovable now, but if you want the most cutting edge abilities... it's probably best to be on one of these tools [Cursor/Claude Code]"
- First company to officially hire a "vibe coding engineer" (Lazar) as a full-time role
- Has a voice input function for brain-dumping prompts
- Agent output labels mistakes in orange with a "try to fix" button
- Built-in version control and project knowledge settings (rules.md equivalent)
- Supports exporting code to GitHub; allows code ownership
- Integrates with Shopify; Lazar built the Shopify integration templates and the Lovable merch store
- Community manager built a video generation feature inside Lovable before it was an official feature
- S&P 500 companies and enterprises in healthcare, finance, telecom use Lovable on enterprise plans
- 2025 motto: "Demo Don't Memo" — build prototypes instead of writing documents
- Design team includes Felix (runs newsletter on design prompting), Nad, and Abby
- About 20% women users; runs "She Builds" women-only hackathons
