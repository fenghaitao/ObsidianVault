## Definition
Clojure is a functional programming language created by Rich Hickey. Jake Nations mentions being "outed as a Clojure guy" at the speaker dinner, referencing the language's community and its creator's philosophy on simplicity.

## Key Information
- Created by Rich Hickey
- Functional programming language
- Hickey's philosophy of simplicity (from his "Simple Made Easy" talk) is closely associated with the Clojure community
- Mentioned in the context of distinguishing between "simple" and "easy" in software design
