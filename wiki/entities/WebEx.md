## Definition

WebEx is Cisco's video conferencing and collaboration platform, one of the products most commonly associated with the Cisco brand by the general public.

## Key Information

- Cisco's video conferencing and collaboration product
- Lenny notes that most lay people think of Cisco as "WebEx and maybe some routers"
- Part of Cisco's broader portfolio beyond networking hardware
