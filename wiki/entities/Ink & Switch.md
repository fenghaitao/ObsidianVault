## Definition

Ink & Switch is a research lab, cited by [[Max Schoening]] (Head of Product, [[Notion]]) as being "at the forefront" of thinking about how to make software more malleable while preserving real-time collaboration and security. [[Jeffrey Litt]], with whom Schoening works closely, is a researcher there.

## Key Information

- Schoening: "this is a thing that the folks at Ink & Switch are obviously... at the forefront [of]... how would we make software more malleable so that we feel more ownership over it without going back a long time and not having real-time collaboration and sort of the security aspects and so on."
- Cited directly in the context of [[Malleable Software]] — the idea that software should adapt to users' interests rather than a vendor's fixed design.
