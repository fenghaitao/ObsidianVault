## Definition

Ray-Ban Stories is the smart-glasses product from Meta's partnership with Ray-Ban, released in September 2021 (with the later "Ray-Ban Meta" successor). Adrien Friggeri worked on its growth/data and later led "experiences" on an unreleased device in the same line.

## Key Information

- Ray-Ban Stories was released in September 2021; after launch it had quality and growth issues that Friggeri was asked to diagnose on his return to Meta.
- Vincent Hardy was the engineering director behind Ray-Ban Stories (per Friggeri).
- Friggeri spent roughly eight months to a year on Ray-Ban Stories growth and data work before pivoting to product/hardware scope.
- Examples of "experiences" on a device like Ray-Ban Meta: on-device AI, music listening, photo capture, and companion-app features.
- Friggeri's later IC8 scope was leading all user experiences on a specific, then-unreleased hardware line within the same smart-glasses/wearables program.
