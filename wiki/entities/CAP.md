## Definition

CAP is an open-source alternative to Loom for screen recording and video sharing. Zevi Arnovitz discovered it after becoming disappointed with Loom's pricing and product quality.

## Key Information

- Open-source alternative to Loom
- Zevi describes it as "really well-crafted" with attention to detail
- Zevi was disappointed with Loom ("taking so much money and the product... I just didn't love it")
- Represents the trend of open-source alternatives to established SaaS tools
