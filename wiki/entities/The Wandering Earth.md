## Definition

The Wandering Earth is a short story collection by Cixin Liu (author of Three-Body Problem). Boris Cherny recommends it even more than Three-Body Problem, noting it offers a Chinese sci-fi perspective different from Western sci-fi.

## Key Information

- Short story collection by Cixin Liu
- Boris actually likes Liu's short stories even more than Three-Body Problem
- Boris: "Quite interesting to see Chinese sci-fi because it has a very different perspective than Western sci-fi"
- Boris: "Just beautifully written"
