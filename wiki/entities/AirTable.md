## Definition

AirTable is a no-code database/spreadsheet platform. Zevi Arnovitz's brother, a non-technical entrepreneur, replaced AirTable (along with Zapier) by building his own CRM and automation system entirely with AI.

## Key Information

- Zevi's brother was paying for AirTable and Zapier before replacing them
- He built a custom replacement for both using AI tools, without any technical background
- Demonstrates how AI enables non-technical people to replace off-the-shelf SaaS with custom solutions
