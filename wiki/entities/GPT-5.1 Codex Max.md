## Definition

GPT-5.1 Codex Max is an [[OpenAI]] model, shipped the week before episode 39 of [[Lenny's Podcast]], powering [[Codex]].

## Key Information

- Per [[Alexander Imbiricos]] (Codex product lead): roughly 30% faster than GPT-5.1 Codex at completing a given task, and meaningfully smarter at its higher reasoning levels.
- Specifically credited with carrying [[Andrej Karpathy]]'s widely-cited praise ("give it your gnarliest bugs, let it run for an hour, and it solves them") — Imbiricos calls it "definitely carrying that mantle" of tackling the hardest bugs.
- Supports "compaction," letting Codex work continuously for very long stretches (users report 24+ hour runs) by recognizing when it's approaching its context-window limit and preparing a compressed handoff into a fresh context window.
- The version shipped the week of the episode was also the first Codex model to natively understand PowerShell, supporting the team's Windows-port effort for [[Atlas]].
