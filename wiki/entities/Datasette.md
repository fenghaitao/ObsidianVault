## Definition

Datasette is an open-source data analysis tool created by Simon Wilson, designed to help journalists and others explore and publish data. It has become a staple of investigative journalism, allowing users to query datasets with SQL and share findings.

## Key Information

- Created by Simon Wilson as part of his day job building open-source tools for data journalism
- Has been under development for over 5 years
- Aimed at helping journalists tell stories with data, but valuable to anyone who needs to interrogate data
- Simon is now integrating AI capabilities: feeding in PDFs of police reports, extracting key details, building database tables, and helping run SQL queries
- Simon's goal: "I want somebody in the world to win a Pulitzer Prize where my software was like 3% of what they used"
- The AI integration is challenging because AI hallucinates — but "journalists deal with untrustworthy sources all the time," so they're better equipped to work with AI than most professions
