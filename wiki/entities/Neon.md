## Definition
Neon is a serverless Postgres database provider. Phil Hetzel mentioned it as an example of a database teams might adopt when graduating from spreadsheet-based evals to a proper persistence layer for their eval platform.

## Key Information
- Serverless Postgres platform with branching and scale-to-zero
- Mentioned as an example database for eval persistence in the "vibe-coded UI" maturity stage
- Represents the step up from Excel/Google Sheets to a real database for eval data
