## Definition

John Perlow is a senior Android engineer (ex-Google) at Facebook/Meta who was the first to support Michael Bolin's early work on Buck.

## Key Information

- Told Bolin "you're going to do it, just do it fast before anyone [objects]" and "I'll support you if you get this done."
- Was a very prolific coder genuinely happy for a faster dev cycle, and one of the first to give Buck kudos — borrowed credibility that helped Bolin's project gain traction.
