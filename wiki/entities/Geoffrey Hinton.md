## Definition

Geoffrey Hinton is a computer scientist and a pioneer of deep learning who won the Turing Award (2018). In this transcript his surname is transcribed "Jeff Hinton."

## Key Information

- Judea Pearl references Hinton's recent public statement that AI/LLMs are "in a dead end," contrasting it with the more optimistic timelines advanced by some leading AI companies.
- Reported as part of the broader disagreement among prominent figures over whether scaling LLMs will lead to AGI.
