## Definition

Activision is a major video game publisher known for franchises like Call of Duty. It is where Carly (Karly) works as a Senior Manager and Lead Engineer in security.

## Key Information

- Carly works at Activision as both a Senior Manager and IC Lead Engineer (staff-equivalent title)
- Activision's leveling for ICs uses "Lead Engineer" as the staff-equivalent level
- Carly works in security at Activision, making video games
- Carly's promotion to Lead was triggered by a presentation to an executive leader and the CTO at Activision
- The CTO offered her a job after the presentation, leading to an internal move and promotion
