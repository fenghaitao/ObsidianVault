## Definition
Hervé Bredin is the Chief Science Officer and co-founder of [[pyannoteAI]], a company focused on conversation understanding and speaker diarization. He was an academic researcher for most of his career before founding the company approximately two years ago. He is the creator of [[PyAnnote]], the popular open-source speaker diarization toolkit.

## Key Information
- Chief Science Officer and co-founder at [[pyannoteAI]]
- Former academic researcher — spent most of his career in academia before starting the company
- Creator of [[PyAnnote]], an open-source toolkit for [[Speaker Diarization]] (~10K GitHub stars)
- Built PyAnnote over many years; its popularity surged after [[OpenAI]] released [[Whisper]], as users combined the two for speaker-attributed transcription
- Presented at [[aiDotEngineer]] Voice & Vision session in June 2026
- Research focus: speaker diarization, conversation understanding, multi-speaker speech processing
- Demonstrated live diarization pipelines using PyAnnote Community One (open-source) and Precision-2 (premium) models
- Advocated for going beyond transcription to understand who said what, when, how, and to whom
