# Manifold

## Definition

Manifold is an AI coding agent referenced by [[MarduSwanepoel]] in his aiDotEngineer talk as an exemplar of [[TransparentExecution]]. It displays task progress, showing users what tasks are completed, what remains, what it examined, and what it made of those things.

## Key Information

- **Category:** AI coding agent
- **Notable Feature:** Transparent task progress — shows completed and pending tasks, along with what the agent examined and its interpretation.
- **Pattern Exemplified:** [[TransparentExecution]] — making agent thinking and actions visible to the user.
- **Context:** Studied alongside [[Cursor]], [[ClaudeCode]], and [[Harvey]] as one of the best agents.
