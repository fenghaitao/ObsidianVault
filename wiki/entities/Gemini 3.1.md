## Definition

Gemini 3.1 is Google's AI model released in mid-2026, notable for its video featuring an animated pelican riding a bicycle, referencing Simon Wilson's informal benchmark.

## Key Information

- Released a few weeks before the podcast recording (mid-2026)
- Their release tweet/video featured an animated pelican riding a bicycle, directly referencing Simon Wilson's benchmark
- Simon noted they also did "all of the other combinations" — giraffe in a tiny car, etc. — meaning they preempted his backup benchmarks
- Simon uses Gemini for image generation (via Nano Banana) primarily for fun and pranks
- Simon considers Gemini as a potential future coding model: "the next Gemini model comes out, might be the best coding model for a couple of months"
