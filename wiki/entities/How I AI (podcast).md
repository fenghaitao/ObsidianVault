## Definition

How I AI is a podcast hosted by Claire Vo that tells stories about how people are using AI in their work and lives. It is a sister podcast to Lenny's Podcast and is part of Lenny's podcast network.

## Key Information

- Hosted by Claire Vo
- Sister podcast to Lenny's Podcast
- Focuses on real stories of how people use AI
- Available on YouTube (youtube.com/@howiaipod), Spotify, Apple Podcasts
- Claire uses an OpenClaw agent named Howie to assist with podcast production: prepping guest bios, sending reminders, finding YouTube comments to reply to
- Claire was surprised by how much work goes into podcasting: "Man, this work is hard. There is so much that goes into the business of podcasting."
