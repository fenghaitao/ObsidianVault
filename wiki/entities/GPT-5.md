## Definition

GPT-5 is OpenAI's foundation model launched in August 2025, which served as the catalyst for Codex's explosive 20x growth. GPT-5.1 Codex Max is a specialized variant optimized for coding tasks.

## Key Information

- GPT-5 launched in August 2025, dramatically improving Codex's capabilities
- Codex saw 20x growth since the GPT-5 launch
- GPT-5.1 Codex Max shipped (as of the interview date in 2026), offering ~30% faster task completion and higher intelligence at higher reasoning levels
- The Codex models are now the most served coding models in OpenAI's API, adopted by major API coding customers
- The models are trained specifically for use in OpenAI's first-party harness with strong opinions about how the agent should work (using the shell, operating in a sandbox)
