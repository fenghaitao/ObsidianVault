## Definition

Brian Leven is a [[Notion]] employee cited by [[Max Schoening]] as a leading example of high [[Agency]] at the company: he blurs the engineering/design boundary and, beyond his formal role, acts as one of Notion's top informal recruiters.

## Key Information

- Schoening's example of "do you drive Notion like it's stolen" (his own self-directed advice): Brian already blurs engineering and design, but is also probably Notion's number one recruiter in practice — proactively identifying what the org needs and going out to find the right person for it, regardless of whether it's officially his job.
- Cited as an example of agency demonstrated "out of the day-to-day," motivated by wanting to affect change rather than fulfilling a defined role.
- Lenny Rachitsky notes Brian Leven was previously a guest on Lenny's sister podcast, "How I AI."
