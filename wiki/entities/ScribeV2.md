## Definition
Scribe V2 is a transcription model built by Angelos Perivolaropoulos's team at ElevenLabs, currently the best transcription model in the market on popular public benchmarks.

## Key Information
- Built by the speech-to-text team at ElevenLabs, led by Angelos Perivolaropoulos
- Currently the best transcription model on popular public benchmarks
- Angelos encourages people with transcription use cases to use it: "I think it's quite good"
- Referenced as "Scribe" in the Voice Engine product, where it serves as the speech-to-text component
- Combined with V3 text-to-speech and emotion-aware turn taking in Voice Engine
