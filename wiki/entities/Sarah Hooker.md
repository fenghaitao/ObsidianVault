## Definition
Sarah Hooker is an AI researcher formerly at Google Brain and Cohere, known for articulating the concept of the "hardware lottery" — the idea that AI research directions are biased toward what current hardware can efficiently compute, leaving many promising approaches unexplored.

## Key Information
- Previously at Google Brain and Cohere
- Authored the "hardware lottery" thesis, arguing that research success is determined by compatibility with available hardware, not necessarily by theoretical merit
- Alex Cheema cites her work as foundational to EXO Labs' thesis that there are many unexplored optimizations for inference hardware
- Her work suggests the current Nvidia GPU-centric hardware stack (optimized for training) leaves significant low-hanging fruit for inference-specific hardware and software
