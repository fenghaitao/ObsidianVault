## Definition

*Jujutsu Kaisen* is an anime named by [[Alexander Imbiricos]] as his favorite recent show in the episode's lightning round.

## Key Information

- He values it for its unusually kind, positive protagonist, contrasting it with an older wave of anime (e.g., *Evangelion*, *Akira*) whose protagonists were deeply flawed and unhappy despite being given outsized responsibility to save the world.
