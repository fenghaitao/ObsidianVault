## Definition

The iPhone is Apple's flagship smartphone product line, used as an example in the panel of a high-visibility product that makes promotion more likely.

## Key Information

- Ryan L. Peterman used the iPhone as an example of a core Apple product where the likelihood of promotion is "way way higher" compared to working on a less prominent product like GarageBand
- Used to illustrate the heuristic that team and product choice matters for promotion potential
- Ryan L. Peterman shot the Bjarne Stroustrup interview with two iPhones in Bjarne's study — an example of the scrappiness of recording every episode himself.
