## Definition

aiDotEngineer is an AI engineering conference and YouTube channel that hosts talks, workshops, and presentations on AI engineering topics including AI coding agents, identity for AI agents, MCP deployments, AI inference frameworks, and related technologies.

## Key Information

- **Format**: Conference with workshops and presentations, also a YouTube channel
- **Content**: AI engineering talks including agent identity, coding agents, prompt learning, workflow patterns, MCP gateways, AI inference frameworks (MAX), and more
- **Related Event**: AI Engineering Code Summit (AIECodeSummit)
- **Playlist**: "Channel Only" contains workshop and presentation content
- **Notable Speaker**: Chris Lattner (Modular) presented on MAX and developer productivity across CPU and GPU
