## Definition

"Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion" is a book by [[Robert Cialdini]] that explores the psychological principles behind why people say "yes" and how to apply these principles ethically. Jacob Warwick recommends it as his top book.

## Key Information

- Author: Robert Cialdini
- Jacob Warwick's #1 book recommendation
- Covers principles of persuasion including reciprocity, commitment, social proof, authority, liking, and scarcity
- Jacob applies the reciprocity principle in negotiation through the [[Reciprocity Imbalance]] technique
