## Definition

Steakhouse chain where [[Jeetu Patel]] had his first job (making $2.25/hour plus tips), cleaning toilets, washing dishes, and waiting tables.

## Key Information

- Patel credits the job with curing a childhood stutter (having to entertain customers for tips) and shaping his work ethic; his favorite dish there was the Malibu chicken.
- He believes everyone should work in hospitality when young for the life lessons it teaches.
