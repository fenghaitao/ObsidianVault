## Definition

Chronicle is an Alphabet-born cybersecurity company spun out of Google X and later folded into Google Cloud; its product Backstory indexed petabytes of enterprise security logs for fast investigation.

## Key Information

- Started inside X as the stealth "Project Lantern"
- Backstory ingested huge volumes of enterprise telemetry (firewalls, proxies, DHCP, email, client logs) and indexed it so queries took ~2 seconds instead of 30 minutes
- Brought Google-scale compute and storage to turn security log search into "a big data game"
- Was spun out under Alphabet (the "C" in Alphabet) before being reacquired by Google Cloud for integration
