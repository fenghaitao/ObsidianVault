## Definition

WorkOS is a B2B SaaS platform that provides drop-in APIs for enterprise features (SSO, SCIM, RBAC, audit logs). Described as "Stripe for enterprise features."

## Key Information

- Sponsor of Lenny's Podcast
- Provides APIs for single sign-on, SCIM, RBAC, audit logs, and other enterprise features
- Used by OpenAI, Cursor, Perplexity, Vercel, Plaid, and hundreds of other companies
- Offers Slack support with real engineers who answer questions quickly
- Described as "the fastest path to becoming enterprise ready and unlocking growth"
