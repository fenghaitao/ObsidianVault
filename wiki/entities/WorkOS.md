## Definition

WorkOS is a developer platform providing drop-in enterprise-readiness APIs (single sign-on, SCIM, RBAC, audit logs, etc.) for B2B SaaS products, presented in this episode as [[Lenny's Podcast]]'s season-presenting sponsor.

## Key Information

- Positioned as "Stripe for enterprise features" — turns common enterprise sales blockers (SSO, SCIM, RBAC, audit logs) into drop-in APIs.
- Customers/partners cited in the sponsorship read include [[OpenAI]], [[Anthropic]], Cursor, Vercel ("Versell" in the transcript), Replit ("Replet"), and Sierra.
- Marketed toward both seed-stage startups trying to land their first enterprise customer and larger companies ("unicorns") expanding globally.
- Reconfirmed as this season's presenting sponsor in the Eric Ries episode, with an expanded customer list read as "OpenAI, Anthropic, Cursor, Vercel, Replit, Sierra, Clay, and hundreds of other winning companies."
- Also read as the season-presenting sponsor in episode 07 (Benedict Evans), with the identical "OpenAI, Anthropic, Cursor, Vercel, Replit, Sierra, Clay, and hundreds of other winning companies" ad read and "Stripe for enterprise features" positioning.
- Also read as a sponsor in episode 27 (the professional vibe coding episode), with the same "OpenAI, Cursor, Perplexity, Vercel, Plaid" (as heard) customer list and "Stripe for enterprise features" positioning.
- Also read as a sponsor in episode 32 (the [[Sam Lesson]] etiquette episode), again with the "OpenAI, Cursor, Perplexity, Vercel, Plaid" customer list and "Stripe for enterprise features" framing.
