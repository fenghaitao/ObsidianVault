## Definition

Bob Kahn, with Vint Cerf, is considered a founder of the internet; his paper on a dream of distributed computing inspired Barbara Liskov to enter the field.

## Key Information

- Liskov: "I read a paper by Bob Kahn, who was with then Cerf considered to be the founders of the internet. Bob talked about his dream of distributed computing... nobody knew how to build those programs... I just thought, great problem."
