## Definition

Claude Cowork (also written "Co-work") is [[Anthropic]]'s AI agent product for general knowledge work, led by [[Fiona Fung]] alongside [[Claude Code]].

## Key Information

- Originated (per Fung's account here) partly from observing that many non-coders were already using Claude Code for non-coding tasks — Anthropic built Cowork to make that experience better for people who are not coders by trade.
- "Routines": a Cowork feature (launched roughly one to two months before this conversation) that lets users schedule recurring automations — e.g., "watch this feedback channel every morning and summarize the themes" — which then autonomously kick off agents to research and draft PRs asynchronously, ready for review when the user checks back in. Described by Fung as a further level of abstraction above manually writing and firing off individual prompts.
- Launched "Claude for Small Business," a bundle/toggle inside Cowork packaging plugins and workflows useful to small-business owners (e.g., invoice/PDF handling, expense management, competitive menu-pricing analysis). This emerged from Fung's personal use of Cowork for her own travel expensing and subsequent hands-on visits helping small-business-owner friends onboard.
- Example small-business use cases surfaced during onboarding visits: finding menus buried in a disorganized documents folder by giving Cowork directory access; running informal market/competitive-pricing analysis for a restaurant by asking Claude to compare cuisine style and prices in the area.
- Cited as an example of Anthropic "leaning into knowledge work" broadly, beyond the initial coding use case, as part of the company's pattern of identifying and going big on latent-demand opportunities (see [[Latent Demand]]) ahead of other AI labs.
