## Definition

Influential management theorist referenced twice by [[Eric Ries]] in *Incorruptible*: for proposing an inverse fiduciary ordering to [[Sol Price]]'s, and for calling [[Mary Parker Follett]] "the prophet of management."

## Key Information

- Argued the fiduciary ordering should be employees first, customers second, shareholders last — offered by Ries as an alternative to Sol Price's "customers first, employees second, shareholders last," with Ries emphasizing that the specific ordering matters less than the discipline of committing to one in writing.
- Called Mary Parker Follett "the prophet of management" after her work was rediscovered and republished in the 1990s.
