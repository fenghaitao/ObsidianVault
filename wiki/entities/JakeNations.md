## Definition
Jake Nations is a software engineer at Netflix who has spent recent years driving adoption of AI tools within the company. He is a speaker at aiDotEngineer and the creator of the "context compression" methodology (also called context engineering or spec-driven development).

## Key Information
- Works at Netflix, helping drive AI tool adoption across the organization
- Works on a codebase with approximately 1 million lines of Java, with the main service at ~5 million tokens
- Developed the three-phase approach (Research → Planning → Implementation) for working with AI code generation
- Advocates that developers must retain understanding of their systems rather than outsourcing thinking to AI
- Argues that pattern recognition and architectural instinct come from hard-won experience dealing with production failures
- Led a manual authorization migration at Netflix that revealed hidden constraints AI analysis could not surface
