## Definition
Neil Zeghidour is the CEO of Gradium AI, a voice AI model provider spun off from Kyutai, the non-profit AI research lab he co-founded. He previously led the development of Moshi, the first full-duplex speech-to-speech conversational model.

## Key Information
- CEO of Gradium AI, a for-profit spin-off from Kyutai
- Co-founded Kyutai, a non-profit AI research lab funded by philanthropists including Eric Schmidt and Xavier Niel
- Led development of Moshi, the first speech-to-speech model for conversation (released ~2 years ago)
- Also developed speech-to-speech translation and Pocket TTS at Kyutai
- Created Gradium to make voice models that can be used in production beyond open source
- Gradium's mission: unlock the unrealized potential of voice AI as a model provider for voice agents
- Argues voice AI is not a commodity — the last mile is the most difficult to solve
- Presented at AIE Code Summit (aiDotEngineer) in May 2026
