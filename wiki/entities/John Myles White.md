## Definition

John Myles White is a former engineering director at Meta who led engineering on PyTorch and Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL) and earlier worked on Meta's experimentation (A/B testing) tools. The episode title transcribes his name as "John White"; the transcript itself introduces him as "John Myles White."

## Key Information

- Was a director of engineering on PyTorch and MSL; quit recently, so he spoke freely.
- Joined Meta (then Facebook) into a "data science" team that split into core data science and data science infrastructure; he preferred the infrastructure side that owned the experimentation tools.
- Was one of the main developers of Deltoid 3 (now just "Deltoid"), Meta's A/B testing framework.
- Became de facto tech lead of the A/B testing tools after almost all senior engineers left within ~6 months, driving the vision for years.
- Before Meta, was a core contributor to the Julia programming language (taking a 6-month leave to work on it).
- Ran promotions in AI infra for a long time and observed its promotion-obsessed culture.
- Wrote popular tweets on grad students treating industry as a "safe position of last resort" and on favorite statistics books (Larry Wasserman; "Introduction to Agnostic Statistics").
- Regret: never attended his skip-level manager Javi's office hours.
