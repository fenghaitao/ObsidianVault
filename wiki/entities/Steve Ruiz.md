## Definition
Steve Ruiz is the founder of tldraw, a London-based startup that builds an online whiteboard, a whiteboard SDK, and experimental AI-on-canvas projects including Make Real and Fairies.

## Key Information
- Founder of tldraw, based in London
- Built Make Real (2023), one of the first AI projects to generate working prototypes from canvas drawings, predating Lovable and the term "vibe coding"
- Built Fairies (late 2024), a multi-agent canvas where AI agents appear as animated sprites with visible state and spatial awareness
- Built a tldraw desktop app (Electron wrapper) that exposes an HTTP endpoint for AI script injection, exploring the frontier of what agents can do with full canvas access
- Advocates for local-first, file-over-app philosophy as the ideal platform for maximizing AI agency while containing risk
- Drew all the fairy sprites himself
- Active on Twitter/X at @steveruizok
