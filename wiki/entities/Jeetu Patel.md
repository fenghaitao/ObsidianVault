## Definition

Jeetu Patel (also known as "G2") is the Chief Product Officer (CPO) and President at Cisco, overseeing approximately 30,000 people. He is known for leading Cisco's transformation from a traditional networking company into an AI-first platform company.

## Key Information

- Chief Product Officer and President at Cisco (~30,000 people under him)
- Previously worked at Box and EMC, building apps for end users
- Nicknamed "G2"
- First job was at Sizzler Steakhouse earning $2.25/hour plus tips — credits hospitality work for overcoming his stuttering and shaping his character
- Says he couldn't have done his CPO job without AI — had zero knowledge of many Cisco domains and used ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Grok to accelerate his learning
- Introduced a six-part framework for building great companies: Timing > Market > Team > Product > Brand > Distribution
- Believes "stamina trumps intellect" — hunger, curiosity, and persistence matter more than raw intelligence
- Counterintuitive leadership philosophy: debate and critique in public, build trust in private (opposite of "praise in public, criticize in private")
- Believes "innovation is a choice" — large companies can innovate just as much as small ones
- Hosted a 12-hour AI summit at Cisco featuring Jensen Huang, Sam Altman, Marc Andreessen, Fei-Fei Li, and many other tech leaders
- Active on LinkedIn where he shares work and non-work insights
- Believes everyone should work in hospitality when young
