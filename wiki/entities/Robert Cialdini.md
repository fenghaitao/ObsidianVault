## Definition

Robert Cialdini is a psychologist and author of "Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion," which Jacob Warwick recommends as his top book. The book explores the principles of persuasion and how they can be applied ethically.

## Key Information

- Author of "Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion"
- Jacob Warwick's #1 book recommendation
- Key principle referenced by Jacob: "How to Win Friends and Influence People" concept of giving first before asking — reciprocity
- Jacob applies Cialdini's principles in negotiation through the [[Reciprocity Imbalance]] technique
