## Definition

Martin Fowler is a software engineer, author, and thought leader known for his work on software architecture, refactoring, and agile development. He coined the term "semantic diffusion" in the 2000s.

## Key Information

- Coined the term "semantic diffusion" — the phenomenon where someone creates a term, everyone starts using it with their own definitions, and the original meaning gets lost
- The term was referenced by [[Aishwaria Raanti]] to describe what is happening to the word "evals" in the AI industry today
- Originally introduced the concept in the context of software engineering terminology
