## Definition

Classic tile-based word board game; the "proven" base mechanic that [[Zynga]]'s [[Words With Friends]] was built on top of, per [[Mark Pincus]]'s [[Proven Better New]] framework.

## Key Information

- Held up by Pincus as the textbook "proven" element in Words With Friends: proven = Scrabble's core mechanic; better = mobile-native polish and UX; new = asynchronous play against friends already on your Facebook graph.
