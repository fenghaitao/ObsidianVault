## Definition

Jeff Lewis is an investor described by [[Matt MacInnis]] as having "many angular views on things," credited with coining the term "narrative violations."

## Key Information

- "Narrative violations": Lewis's phrase for the idea that every outsized business success violates conventional wisdom/investment checklists in some important way — cited by MacInnis in his discussion of why [[Notion]]'s success (via [[Ivan Zhao]]'s idiosyncratic persistence) can't be replicated as a generic playbook.
