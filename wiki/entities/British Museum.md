## Definition
The British Museum in London is a major museum and the location where Joe Reeve demonstrated his AI statue app, which lets visitors take a picture of a statue and have a voice conversation with it.

## Key Information
- Location of the statue app demo by Joe Reeve (ElevenLabs)
- The app was demonstrated outside the museum, with the museum's iconic facade featured in the viral video
- Former head of the Americas at the British Museum, Jago, now runs the Sainsbury Centre
- Jago is leading an academic process to determine what inanimate objects (like statues) should sound like, considering material origins, carving locations, and exhibition history
- The museum's collections raise philosophical questions about voice design: a statue carved from Chinese mountain rock in Vietnam, displayed in Britain for 200 years — what accent should it have?
