## Definition

"Negotiation Made Simple" is a book by [[John Lowry]] that Jacob Warwick describes as a potential "bible" for negotiation. Jacob was initially frustrated because it was the book he wanted to write himself.

## Key Information

- Author: John Lowry
- Jacob Warwick: "I was pissed off because I'm like I wanted to write a book a couple years ago and then I read his and said this is what I wanted to write"
- Described as breaking negotiation down in an easy, accessible way
- Jacob appeared on Lowry's podcast as a guest
