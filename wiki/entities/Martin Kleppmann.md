## Definition

Martin Kleppmann is the author of Designing Data-Intensive Applications, whom Marc Brooker highly recommends to anyone building distributed systems.

## Key Information

- Brooker: "anybody who's building distributed system things, I highly recommend Martin Kleppmann's book" (Designing Data-Intensive Applications).
- Brooker notes a second edition of the book was coming out soon at the time of recording.
