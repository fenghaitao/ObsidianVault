## Definition

Rackspace is a managed cloud computing company. Its CEO Gajen was cited as an example of a leader who stays hands-on with AI, blocking 4-6 AM every morning for "catching up with AI."

## Key Information

- CEO Gajen blocks 4:00-6:00 AM every morning for catching up on AI news, podcasts, and information — no meetings during that time
- Gajen also has weekend white-coding sessions to stay technically sharp
- He maintains a curated list of 2-3 trusted AI sources and bounces questions off an AI advisory group (which included [[Aishwaria Raanti]])
- His AI engagement trickles down into company decisions — demonstrating the importance of top-down AI leadership
- Example of a leader rebuilding intuitions: after 10-15 years of building intuitions, leaders must be comfortable being "the dumbest person in the room" and relearning
