## Definition

Cloud infrastructure company whose CEO, [[Gajen Kandiah]], is cited by [[Aishwarya Naresh Reganti]] as an exemplar of hands-on AI leadership in the "[[AI Product Success Triangle]]" framework.

## Key Information

- Reganti describes working with Kandiah before he became Rackspace's CEO; his habit of blocking 4-6am every morning purely for "catching up with AI" (no meetings scheduled) plus weekend vibe-coding sessions is cited as a model of leaders staying hands-on and relearning their intuitions rather than delegating that work away.
- Reganti frames his behavior as instructive precisely because it isn't about implementing things himself, but about rebuilding intuitions that were built up over 10-15 years and may no longer be reliable in the AI era.
- Kandiah reportedly relies on a small, curated set of two or three trusted information sources rather than following every AI opinion, then discusses what he reads with a small group of AI experts (including Reganti) before it "trickles down into a bunch of decisions."
