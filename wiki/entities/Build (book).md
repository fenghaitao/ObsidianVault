## Definition

Book written by Tony Fadell for product builders, covering topics including data-vs-opinion decisions, micromanagement, the customer journey, storytelling, and the "three generations" pattern for building a real company.

## Key Information

- Popularized the term "builder" as an umbrella identity spanning product management, design, and engineering roles.
- Contains a chapter on "sweat the details" / good micromanagement, and a data-versus-opinion-based decision framework.
- Includes an updated version of the "Crossing the Chasm" adoption-curve graphic, adapted for how Fadell's teams approached customer segments across product versions.
- Contains a chapter on not overworking, informed by Fadell's General Magic experience.
- Advocates writing the product's press release before starting the project (echoing Amazon's "Working Backwards" methodology).
- Frames the "three generations" idea: make the product, fix the product, then fix the business.
