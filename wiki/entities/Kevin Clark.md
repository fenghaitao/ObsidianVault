## Definition

Kevin Clark is a software engineer at Sun Microsystems who, along with Jeff Bonwick, energized Bryan Cantrill during his Sun interview and helped convince him to join.

## Key Information

- Cantrill describes meeting Kevin Clark and Jeff Bonwick as "a bolt of lightning": energized, passionate people who saw the same potential for operating systems innovation.
- The encounter flipped Cantrill from "I don't particularly want to work for Sun" to "I'm absolutely going to go work there."
