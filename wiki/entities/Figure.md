## Definition

Humanoid robotics company mentioned by [[Caitlin Kalinowski]] as one of a small number of companies "very ahead" in humanoid robot development, alongside [[Optimus]] and [[1X Neo]].

## Key Information

- Grouped with Optimus (Tesla) and 1X Neo as leading humanoid robot programs in the current landscape.
- Kalinowski's overall view is that all current humanoids, Figure included, remain advanced prototypes rather than at-scale products, constrained mainly by safety validation and supply chain rather than by AI capability.
