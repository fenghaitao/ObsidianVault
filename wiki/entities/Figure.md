## Definition
Figure is a humanoid robotics company building general-purpose humanoid robots.
## Key Information
- Named by host Ryan L. Peterman alongside Tesla as one of the competitors building humanoid robots.
- Sergey Levine highlights Figure's live-streamed demo of a robot sorting packages as a genuine demonstration of generalization, in contrast to rehearsed acrobatic demos.
- Levine sees live demos and long time-lapses as a creative, useful way to make the otherwise-invisible property of generalization legible to viewers.
