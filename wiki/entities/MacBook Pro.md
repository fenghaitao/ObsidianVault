## Definition

[[Apple]] laptop where [[Caitlin Kalinowski]] served as thermal lead on the first unibody model (2007-2012 era).

## Key Information

- One of several Apple hardware projects (alongside [[MacBook Air]] and Mac Pro) where Kalinowski credits Apple's culture of scrutinizing "why we're building it this way" — even finishing unseen internal details — for training a generation of hardware leaders now spread across the industry.
