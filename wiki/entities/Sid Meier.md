## Definition

Veteran game designer ("the godfather of game design") whose social-civilization game on Facebook is used by [[Mark Pincus]] as a cautionary example within [[Proven Better New]].

## Key Information

- Released a Facebook social-civilization game that [[Zynga]] PMs predicted, within 10 minutes of launch, was "dead on arrival" — not because the game design was bad, but because the first-time-user-experience required too many clicks and wasn't a proven-quality onboarding flow.
- Cited as proof that even the most revered game designer can fail commercially by skipping the "proven" step of Proven Better New — great "new" design alone isn't sufficient if users never survive onboarding to see it.
