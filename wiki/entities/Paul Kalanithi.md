## Definition

Indian-origin neurosurgeon diagnosed with lung cancer at 31 or 32, author of the memoir *[[When Breath Becomes Air]]*, cited by [[Aishwarya Naresh Reganti]] as her most-recommended book.

## Key Information

- Wrote the memoir after his diagnosis; Reganti cites his reframing of Socrates's "the unexamined life is not worth living" as "if the unexamined life is not worth living, was the unlived life worth examining?"
