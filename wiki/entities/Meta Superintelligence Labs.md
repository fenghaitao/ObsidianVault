## Definition

Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL) is a Meta AI frontier lab where John Myles White served as an engineering director. It is central to the episode's discussion of Meta's employee experience and promotion culture.

## Key Information

- John Myles White was a director of engineering on MSL (and PyTorch).
- He is "very bullish" on Meta as a stockholder but "very bearish" on Meta as an employee; he says this tension is not unique to MSL.
- One thing that makes MSL more stressful than other frontier labs is the sense that it might have layoffs — so far fewer rounds (or perceived as fewer) than smaller startups like OpenAI and Anthropic.
