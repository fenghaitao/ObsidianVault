## Definition

*Am I Overthinking This* is Michelle Rial's first book, a collection of humorous charts that synthesize relatable life experiences and feelings. It has sold many copies and resonated widely.

## Key Information

- Michelle Rial's first book of charts
- Has sold many copies
- Contains charts about overthinking, life experiences, and feelings that people identify with
- Some charts from the book went viral after someone simply took a photo of a page and shared it
- Charts from this book are frequently shared on social media without attribution, and reproduced on merchandise (pillows, mugs, etc.) by unauthorized sellers
