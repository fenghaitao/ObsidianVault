## Definition
DeepSeek is the Chinese AI lab behind the open-source DeepSeek V3 model, which dominated a Wall Street Journal narrative around the release of Gemini's Flash 2.0 thinking model.
## Key Information
- DeepSeek V3 launched around the same time Vlad's team shipped Flash 2.0 thinking.
- A Wall Street Journal article framed China taking over AI with open-source models, showing "chat GPT and DeepSeek" near the top of the LMSYS Arena leaderboard and Gemini near the bottom.
- Vlad noticed the table did not match the live leaderboard; the article had "ill-written rows," and the corrected chart put Flash 2.0 thinking far ahead of DeepSeek V3.
