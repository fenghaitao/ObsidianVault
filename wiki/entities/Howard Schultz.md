## Definition

Builder of [[Starbucks]], credited by [[Eric Ries]] (via [[Todd Park]]) as the original source of the "[[Culture Bank]]" rule — treating principled sacrifices as deposits and greedy shortcuts as withdrawals in an organization's trust account.

## Key Information

- Todd Park learned the culture-bank framing from Schultz, who is credited with instilling the pattern of publicly recognizing employees who make a values-driven sacrifice as making a "deposit" in the company's culture.
