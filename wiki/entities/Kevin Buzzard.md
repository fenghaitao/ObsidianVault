## Definition

Kevin Buzzard is a mathematician (Imperial College London) prominent in formalizing undergraduate and research mathematics in Lean.

## Key Information

- Named by Leonardo de Moura, alongside Terence Tao, Jeremy Avigad, and Patrick Massot, as one of the serious mathematicians who insist that dependent type theory — not higher-order logic — is required for real mathematics.
- Part of the math-first community built around Lean and mathlib that made Lean popular initially with mathematics rather than software.
