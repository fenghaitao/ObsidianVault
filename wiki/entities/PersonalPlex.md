## Definition
PersonalPlex is a speech-to-speech model published by Nvidia, built based on Moshi's full-duplex architecture. It represents one of the few models to adopt Moshi's approach to handling simultaneous bidirectional conversation.

## Key Information
- Published by Nvidia
- Based on Moshi, the first full-duplex speech-to-speech model
- One of the only models (alongside Moshi) to implement full-duplex conversation
- Represents industry adoption of Kyutai's open research
- Mentioned as a recent development at the time of the talk (May 2026)
