## Definition

Retro is the friends-only, ad-free, subscription-based photo journal app built by Ryan Olson's product studio (co-founded with Nathan Sharp); it became popular in Taiwan.

## Key Information

- First product of a "world-class product studio" Ryan co-founded with Nathan Sharp (his Stories PM); the studio aims to let great builders do their best work.
- Friends-only by design: mutual friending, private accounts, a 250-friend cap, and no following model (you can't build an audience).
- No ad-based business model — Ryan argues ads plus an algorithmic feed misalign incentives to hijack attention; monetization is a premium subscription (e.g., video sharing requires it).
- Almost half of daily users post something, versus a much lower creation rate at Instagram — a sign it's built for creation.
- Reached number one on Taiwan's app store for a period and stays at the top of the photo category; "when it works, it works."
- Positioned as "feel good social media" that coexists alongside (rather than replacing) Instagram.
