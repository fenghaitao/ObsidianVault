## Definition

Deltoid is Meta's internal A/B testing framework; John Myles White was one of its main developers (Deltoid 3, later just "Deltoid").

## Key Information

- Lets you route one code path to A and another to B and measure downstream effects of a test.
- White was one of the main developers of Deltoid 3.
- He says Statsig's product is essentially Deltoid re-built externally ("almost the entire UI is the same, almost all the functionality is the same") and regrets not having built the Deltoid startup himself.
- Anecdote: someone blocked shipping Deltoid 3 because a holdout flipped from a "statistically significant win" to "not significant," which White saw as "cultural pathology" — treating "barely significant" as a win.
