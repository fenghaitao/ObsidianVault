## Definition

*Charts for Babies* is a children's book by Michelle Rial, published April 7th. It teaches early learning concepts through simple charts and illustrations. It is Michelle's third book and first children's book.

## Key Information

- Published April 7th (likely 2025)
- Age range: 2-4, but effectively 0-4
- Teaches early learning concepts: sizes, shapes, lines, numbers, directions, feelings, colors, amounts, sharing, concepts, relationships, opposites
- Written in rhyming cadence
- Simply illustrated with charts
- Came from the back of a notebook while Michelle was working on a different book (charts for new parents) — an example of following the pull
- Michelle's father, a geophysicist, taught her math and patterns from a young age, which influenced her approach to teaching concepts through charts
- The idea was inspired by reading many children's books with her son Jude and noticing how charts naturally combine early learning concepts
- Available at all major retailers and local bookstores
