## Definition

Phil Wadler is a computer scientist and one of the original designers of Haskell who collaborated with Martin Odersky on the Pizza language.

## Key Information

- Described by Martin Odersky as "one of the Haskell original designers."
- Collaborated with Odersky on Pizza, the predecessor to Scala, whose goal was an accessible functional language on the then-widespread JVM.
