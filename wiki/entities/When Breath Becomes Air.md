## Definition

When Breath Becomes Air is a memoir by neurosurgeon Paul Kalaniti, written after he was diagnosed with terminal lung cancer. It explores the meaning of life, mortality, and the balance between examining life and actually living it.

## Key Information

- Written by [[Paul Kalaniti]] after his terminal lung cancer diagnosis at 31-32
- [[Aishwaria Raanti]]'s favorite book, read during the COVID pandemic
- Contains the quote arguing against Socrates: "If the unexamined life is not worth living, was the unlived life worth examining?"
- Aishwaria's takeaway: people in the AI era who are constantly reinventing themselves need to "take a pause and live for a bit" — "stop evaling life too much"
