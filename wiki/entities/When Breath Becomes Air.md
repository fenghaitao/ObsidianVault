## Definition

Memoir by [[Paul Kalanithi]], named by [[Aishwarya Naresh Reganti]] as the book she finds herself recommending most to other people.

## Key Information

- Written by Kalanithi, an Indian-origin neurosurgeon diagnosed with lung cancer at 31 or 32, after his diagnosis; Reganti says she read it during COVID, when "all we ever wanted to do... is stay alive."
- Reganti cites a passage where Kalanithi argues against Socrates's "the unexamined life is not worth living," reframing it as: "if the unexamined life is not worth living, was the unlived life worth examining?" — meaning people can spend so much time examining their mission/purpose that they forget to actually live.
- Reganti applies this directly to people in the AI era who are "continuously going through this phase of reinventing themselves," urging them to "take a pause and live for a bit."
