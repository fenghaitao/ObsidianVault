## Definition

Substack is a newsletter publishing platform that Lenny Rachitsky uses to publish and monetize Lenny's Newsletter. It provides the infrastructure for paid subscriptions, including the paywall feature Lenny used to begin monetizing his writing.

## Key Information

- Lenny moved his newsletter to Substack after initially writing on Medium
- Substack provides the paywall and subscription infrastructure for Lenny's Newsletter
- Lenny worked with Substack to shut down fraud rings that were exploiting his Product Pass offer
- Lenny wonders about the long-term viability: "I don't think there's an answer to like Substack writer podcast life long term"
