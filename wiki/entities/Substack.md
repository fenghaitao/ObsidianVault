## Definition

Substack is a newsletter publishing platform used by multiple panelists for long-form content about career growth and software engineering.

## Key Information

- Ryan L. Peterman writes a Substack with content about software engineering career growth, described as "pure Solid Gold" by the panelists
- Zach Wilson writes a Substack at exactly.substack.com focusing on data engineering and software engineering content
- Lee recently started a Substack as a way to recycle and organize content previously published on LinkedIn, which is hard to search and discover old content on
- The panelists generally use LinkedIn for short-form and Substack for long-form content
