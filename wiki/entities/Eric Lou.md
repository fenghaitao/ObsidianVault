## Definition

Eric Lou is a [[Notion]] employee cited by [[Max Schoening]] as an example of high [[Agency]]: he deliberately reskilled from writing long strategy documents to prototyping directly in Figma and then in code, specifically to make himself more hireable at an earlier stage of a hypothetical startup.

## Key Information

- Schoening recounts Eric asking him: if Schoening ever started a startup, would he hire Eric? Schoening said not among the first 10 hires (implying he wouldn't need a dedicated product manager that early). Eric responded he'd work on his skills so he'd be hirable within the first five hires instead.
- This led Eric to shift from writing long PRDs to spending more time directly in Figma, and eventually to preferring to just build a working prototype and show it rather than write about it — cited by Schoening as a clear sign of proactively reshaping one's own role rather than waiting to be asked.
