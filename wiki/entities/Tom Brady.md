## Definition

Tom Brady is a legendary NFL quarterback. Jacob Warwick references Brady's Tampa Bay Buccaneers contract as an example of [[Performance-Based Compensation]] — a contract structured with milestone triggers rather than a flat salary.

## Key Information

- Legendary NFL quarterback
- Jacob Warwick uses Brady's Tampa Bay contract as an example of performance-based compensation:
  - Win 8 games → $500K bonus
  - Win 12 games → $1.5M bonus
  - Go to playoffs → extra bonus
  - Win the division → extra bonus
  - Get to the Super Bowl → additional bonus
  - Win Super Bowl MVP → $2.5M more
- This structure aligns incentives: the player gets paid more when the team succeeds
- Jacob recommends this approach for executive compensation: tie additional comp to measurable performance milestones
