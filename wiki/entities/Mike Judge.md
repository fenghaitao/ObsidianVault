## Definition
Mike Judge is the creator of the HBO series Silicon Valley (and of Beavis and Butt-Head and Office Space). He began his career as a software engineer, which shaped the show's startling accuracy.
## Key Information
- Started his career as a software engineer (Cowling recalls it was at Lockheed) — a fact many people don't realize.
- Silicon Valley was a throwback to the kind of engineering work Judge did; Office Space captured the dystopian cubicle era of tech.
- After the unexpected popularity of Silicon Valley's first season, the writers needed a compression/storage expert to figure out where the story could go, which led to James Cowling's consulting role.
