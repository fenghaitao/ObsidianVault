## Definition

Mirage (formerly called Captions) is a successful AI video company led by CEO Garav, known for its policy of shipping a marketable feature every week.

## Key Information

- Formerly called Captions; rebranded to Mirage
- CEO Garav implemented a policy of shipping a marketable feature every week
- This shipping velocity approach is cited as parallel to Lovable's strategy of daily shipping to maintain market noise
- Garav appeared on Lenny's Podcast to discuss this approach
