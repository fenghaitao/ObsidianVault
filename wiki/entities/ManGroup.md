## Definition

Man Group is an alternative investment manager managing over $200 billion in assets for pension funds, sovereign wealth funds, and large institutions. They use Claude Code and skills to research, backtest, and productionize systematic trading signals with AI.

## Key Information

- Manages real capital for teachers, metal workers, and other pension holders globally.
- Systematic trading division uses algorithmic capabilities across thousands of securities and hundreds of markets.
- AI now proposes investment ideas, gets data, runs backtests against 15+ years of history, writes strategy proposals, and productionizes signals -- with human review.
- Built a governed skills marketplace with managed (owned, tested, lifecycle-managed) and community skills, organized by business unit.
- Skills governance was the key enabler: without it, skills became local optimizations (e.g., hardcoded cost centers in expense report skills).
