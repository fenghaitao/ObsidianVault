## Definition
Jeffrey Hinton Lee is credited by Chris Parsons as the originator of the Ralph loop concept. Around June 2025, he proposed the idea that after an AI finishes a task, you should just give it the same prompt again — the AI would often review its own work and find things it missed.

## Key Information
- Originator of the Ralph loop concept (circa June 2025, described as "the ancient times in AI")
- Core idea: after AI finishes a task, re-run the same prompt. The AI often reviews its code and realizes it missed something
- The concept was named after Ralph Wiggum from The Simpsons — the character who tries the same thing over and over until it works
- The idea was initially important because AI coding tools frequently left work incomplete; modern models are much better at recognizing completion
