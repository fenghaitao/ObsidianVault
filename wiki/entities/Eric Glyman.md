## Definition

Eric Glyman is the co-founder and CEO of Ramp. Keith Rabois recommends his speech on hiring and notes that Ramp exemplifies speed, velocity, and promoting from within.

## Key Information

- Co-founder and CEO of [[Ramp]]
- Keith Rabois recommends his speech on hiring (similar to Keith's own Ramp talk on how to hire)
- Ramp's board meetings start with the "days" slide — counting the number of days since launch
- Ramp is known for extreme velocity: shipped cards in 3 months when industry standard is 9-12 months
- Ramp exemplifies promoting from within rather than hiring senior experienced people
