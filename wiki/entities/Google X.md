## Definition

Google X (later "X") is Alphabet's moonshot incubator, focused on creating viable, potentially world-changing businesses that can spin out. Carey Nachenberg joined its stealth cybersecurity project.

## Key Information

- Operated with less process than core Google so teams could move fast (one-page performance reviews at the Chronicle spin-out, vs. Google's lengthy ones)
- Carey joined as a Principal Engineer on "Project Lantern," a stealth cybersecurity effort that became Chronicle
- The environment had brilliant engineers ("a guy over 200 IQ"), but project taste varied as widely there as anywhere
- High-level recruiting is bespoke: Carey was brought in via a former Symantec COO and interviews that were mostly leadership/sales-pitch, not coding
