## Definition
Steve Kaliski is a Principal Software Engineer at Stripe who led the issuing team (physical and virtual credit cards) for four years and has spent the last two years exploring how to enable AI agents to spend money safely and how Stripe businesses can adapt to agent buyers.

## Key Information
- Principal Software Engineer at [[Stripe]]
- Spent first four years leading Stripe's issuing team — the product that lets developers create physical and virtual credit cards, historically for humans and increasingly for robots
- Last two years focused on agent payments: how robots spend money and how businesses receive money from robots
- Presented three Stripe primitives for agent payments: [[Shared Payment Tokens]], [[Machine Payments Protocol]], and [[Agent to Commerce Protocol]]
- Core thesis: discovery benefits from non-determinism, but credentials, payments, and checkout REQUIRE determinism
- Key collaborators: [[Tempo]] (Machine Payments Protocol), [[OpenAI]] (Agent to Commerce Protocol)
