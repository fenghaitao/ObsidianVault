## Definition

Smart Pricing is an Airbnb machine learning algorithm that automatically sets prices for Airbnb listings. Zach Wilson's pricing and availability data sets powered this algorithm, making it a key example of Master Data creating leveraged impact.

## Key Information

- Zach worked on the pricing and availability data sets that fed into Smart Pricing at Airbnb
- His data sets were Master Data — they were used by many teams and powered an ML algorithm
- Zach cut the size of these data sets by 90%, making them 10x smaller for the entire company to use
- This work was a key part of his staff-level impact at Airbnb
- Smart Pricing is an example of data engineering creating impact through automated ML decisions
