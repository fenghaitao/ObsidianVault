## Definition

Science-fiction book series [[Lenny Rachitsky]] mentions reading to his son, referenced during the dining-etiquette/tipping discussion as a whimsical parallel to the complexity of restaurant bill math.

## Key Information

- Lenny: "I'm reading one of my sons right now the third book of Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy," recalling that the "Bristoatics mathematics" that powers the improbability drive is framed as the most complicated math in the universe — the math of a restaurant bill — which he and Sam Lesson jokingly connect to the episode's tipping-etiquette confusion.
