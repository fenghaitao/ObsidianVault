## Definition

Clubhouse is a viral social-audio app that experienced explosive growth in late 2020 and early 2021, where Adrien Friggeri worked briefly building its data infrastructure.

## Key Information

- Went "crazy viral" with rapid growth in late 2020/early 2021; Friggeri joined when the company had roughly 10-11 people.
- Early Clubhouse had no machine learning and no ML-based feed ranking; it "spammed" users with notifications.
- Friggeri built A/B testing for Clubhouse and introduced machine learning to filter some of the spammy notifications.
- A large fraction of the early team was former Coinbase or former Facebook engineers — independent, self-driven, and hungry.
- Culture was described as very hands-on, with a strong desire to build an authentic social network.
