## Definition
Jed Borovik is a product engineering leader at Google DeepMind who serves as Gemini's assistant and leads the Jules coding agent product engineering team. He was the MC for the 2025 AI Engineering Code Summit in New York.

## Key Information
- Works at Google DeepMind as Gemini's assistant
- Leads the product engineering team for Jules, Google's coding agent
- Served as MC (Master of Ceremonies) for the 2025 AI Engineering Code Summit
- Opened the Summit by framing it through Richard Hamming's "You and Your Research" question
- Emphasized that AI is the most important problem in technology and code is the most important problem in applied AI
