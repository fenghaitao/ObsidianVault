## Definition

Barbara Mento was reportedly the first female consultant at McKinsey (circa 1950s). She developed the "Mento Pyramid" — a method for presenting information to executives that starts with the recommendation, then the options explored, then the evidence, rather than the traditional bottom-up approach.

## Key Information

- Reportedly the first female consultant at McKinsey in the 1950s
- Developed the Mento Pyramid method for presenting to executives
- The pyramid flips traditional presentation order: start with recommendation → options explored → supporting evidence
- This approach is effective because busy executives want the conclusion first, not the process
