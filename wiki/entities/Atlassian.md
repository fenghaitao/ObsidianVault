## Definition

Atlassian is an enterprise software company known for products like Jira, Confluence, and Trello. Tamar Yehoshua, former CPO of Slack, is now CPO and head of AI at Atlassian.

## Key Information

- Tamar Yehoshua is CPO and head of AI at Atlassian
- Tamar was previously CPO at Slack, succeeding April Underwood
