## Definition

*Why Nations Fail* is a book named by [[Tomer Cohen]] as one of his most-recommended books in the episode's lightning round; its authors won the Nobel Prize the year before this episode.

## Key Information

- Central argument: national success or failure comes down to whether a country's institutions are "extractive" (concentrating opportunity and benefits for a few) or "inclusive" (broadly shared participation and opportunity) — not the more common explanations of culture, natural resources, or religion.
- Cohen connects this framework directly to LinkedIn's mission around "opportunity" and to how he thinks about building products more broadly.
