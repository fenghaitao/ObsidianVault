## Definition

Herb Cohen is the author of "You Can Negotiate Anything," a book Jacob Warwick recommends for its simplicity and accessibility. Unlike many negotiation books focused on high-stakes corporate deals, Cohen's book uses everyday examples like negotiating for dishwashers.

## Key Information

- Author of "You Can Negotiate Anything"
- Jacob Warwick recommends it as an accessible introduction to negotiation
- Jacob notes the book is "a little dated" (written in the 1980s) and "doesn't suit all audiences," but values its simplicity
- The book's strength: it breaks negotiation down to simple concepts (apples and oranges) rather than complex corporate scenarios
