## Definition

Ryan Olson (transcribed in the episode as "Ryan Olsen") is an iOS engineer who grew to principal engineer (IC8) at Instagram, created the FLEX open-source debugging tool, and later co-founded Retro.

## Key Information

- Failed his 2011 Facebook internship interview from nerves (on a question that was later banned); later bounced back, used beta blockers for interview anxiety, and joined full-time at IC4.
- Interned at Flipboard after an investor friend (who founded Insight Partners) connected him to co-founder Evan Doll; there he built and open-sourced FLEX.
- At Instagram he led the White Out redesign, Instagram Stories (a core iOS team of two), IGTV, and co-founded IG Labs; his path was IC4 → IC5 → IC6 → IC7 → IC8.
- Felt underleveled at hire and explicitly asked his director to promote him from IC4 to IC6 in one cycle (the company declined but he seeded the faster path).
- Tried the rare tech lead director (TLD) role, believing senior ICs and managers should keep coding ("skin in the game").
- Left with Stories PM Nathan Sharp to found a product studio whose first product is Retro, the friends-first social app.
- Was a serious rock climber (on the US team, competed in World Cups) before sacrificing that for Stories.
