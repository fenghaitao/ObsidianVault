## Definition

Brandon Gall is COO of [[Every]], cited by [[Dan Shipper]] as the person who coined the term "[[Computer Errands]]" for personal (non-work) uses of AI agents.

## Key Information

- Coined "computer errands" to describe the territory of using personal agents for everyday personal tasks (e.g., ordering groceries) — a category Dan Shipper says will be huge, though Every's own focus is mostly on work use cases.
