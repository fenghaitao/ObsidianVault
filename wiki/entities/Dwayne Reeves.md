## Definition

Dwayne Reeves is a Meta senior staff engineer (IC7) who joined Facebook as a new grad from MIT and became tech lead for Hack, Meta's PHP-derived programming language.

## Key Information

- Initially interviewed at Facebook only as practice (he preferred Google and Twitter), but a surprise offer increase and CTO Bret Taylor personally meeting new grads changed his mind
- Joined a team building a PHP-companion DSL; after that was cancelled, moved through privacy infrastructure before going all-in on Hack's type system
- Typed a core API and found null-handling bugs in ~20% of call sites, which made Meta start taking Hack seriously
- Coined the "uncanny valley of type systems" to describe the transitional pain of partly-typed codebases
- Growth arc: IC5 (an RE rating while writing the least code of his career by enabling others), IC6 (handing off the project that "left orbit"), then a TLM stint before returning to IC
- Named mentors: Kendall Hopkins, Paul Biznet, and type-theory researcher Andrew Kennedy (HHVM)
- Stayed ~14 years by weighing temporary frustrations against being valued and doing unique language work
