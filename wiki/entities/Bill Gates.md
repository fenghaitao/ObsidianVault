## Definition

Bill Gates is the co-founder and former CEO of Microsoft.

## Key Information

- Per David Ronca's recollection of "12 Secrets to Microsoft Success," Gates forced the whole company onto the (then-broken) NT Server in the mid-1990s, which exposed its bugs to the entire company and got it fixed — an early, famous example of dogfooding.
- Instituted a company-wide hiring freeze during Microsoft's rapid growth, believing it was hiring too many people and funding too many pointless projects; this forced executives to kill low-value projects and reassign people.
- As a result, Microsoft effectively cut the bottom ~20% of work/people that "they should have cut anyway."
