## Definition

Actively AI (actively.ai) is a company that uses [[ClaudeManagedAgents]] to power cross-account agentic search for sales teams, with [[ScheduledDeployments|scheduled deployments]] refreshing answers regularly.

## Key Information

- Powers cross-account agentic search for sales teams using Managed Agents.
- Uses scheduled deployments to refresh answers on a regular cadence.
- Replaced scheduling infrastructure the team initially built themselves, simplifying their stack.
- Cited by Anthropic (June 2026) as an example of scheduled deployments eliminating the need for custom scheduler infrastructure.
