## Definition
Tuomas Artman is the CTO of Linear, the project management tool known for high-quality software. A former Uber engineer, he is a leading advocate for building tasteful, high-quality software in the age of AI, arguing that quality and craft become the key competitive differentiator when AI makes shipping trivially easy.

## Key Information
- CTO of Linear, a project management tool renowned for its design quality and performance
- Former engineer at Uber, where he experienced hypergrowth and the tension between shipping speed and quality
- At Uber, had a PR rejected because a UI element was "off by two pixels" — an experience that shaped his commitment to quality
- Created Linear's Quality Wednesdays practice after finding 35 problems in a single small UI menu
- Instituted Linear's Zero Bug Policy: all bugs fixed immediately, typically within 2-3 hours
- Believes AI agents have no taste — they can implement correct code but produce unnatural-feeling results
- Argues that as AI makes shipping trivially easy, saying no to feature requests becomes the critical skill
- Foresees that in four years, all engineers will need to become product engineers — mini-PMs who understand customers
- Linear's hiring process includes a full-week paid trial where candidates build a greenfield project
- Recommends Apple's Human Interface Guidelines as the best resource for learning good UX
