## Definition

Founder of [[Patagonia]], cited by [[Eric Ries]] in *[[Incorruptible (book)|Incorruptible]]* as a "quality zealot" driven by an uncompromising, non-ROI notion of product quality.

## Key Information

- More famous today for environmental activism, but Ries emphasizes his underlying belief that quality was an objective function and every product had a quality level it deserved — a belief most people think is "insane," which Ries argues is exactly why Patagonia succeeded. See [[Harder Is Easier]].
- Founded Patagonia's governance around a [[Perpetual Purpose Trust]] structure.
