## Definition
Crawlera is Bright Data's proxy and crawler product for web data collection. Rafael Levi notes that it sometimes leads users on "wild goose chases" and personally prefers CloudCrawl.

## Key Information
- Bright Data product for proxy-based web crawling
- Rafael Levi: "Crawlera does a great job as well, but I prefer CloudCrawl because it just gives me less headache. Crawlera sometimes takes me on a wild goose chase."
- Part of Bright Data's suite of web data access tools
- Alternative to CloudCrawl within the Bright Data ecosystem
