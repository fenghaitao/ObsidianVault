## Definition

Alex Schultz (transcribed "Alex Schulz") is an executive at Meta who became its chief marketing officer (CMO) and was head of analytics earlier in his tenure.

## Key Information

- Was head of analytics at the time Adrien Friggeri and his team pitched Bento as the first step toward fixing data-team pain.
- Friggeri describes going to Schultz and Brady Lattimer to surface data-team pain and win leadership buy-in for the data-tools effort.
