## Definition

Podcast hosted by [[Lenny Rachitsky]], focused on product management, growth, and engineering leadership, frequently featuring guests from top tech companies discussing how AI is reshaping their work. Episode 02 features [[Adam Mosseri]] on taste and judgment in an AI world; episode 05 features [[Mark Pincus]] discussing [[Proven Better New]] and related product philosophy; episode 06 features [[Tony Fadell]] discussing "taste," opinion-based decision-making, and AI's impact on product building; episode 07 features [[Benedict Evans]] discussing his "AI is eating the world" presentation and the case for "presuming radical uncertainty" about AI's economic and job impacts.

## Key Information

- Sponsors read across these episodes include WorkOS (B2B SaaS enterprise-readiness APIs), Vanta (compliance/security automation), and Mercury (banking for startups).
- Host references prior episodes with [[Eric Simons]] (Bolt.new) and [[Nikita Bier]] (TBH).
- Host promotes lennysnewsletter.com / lennyspodcast.com for subscriber benefits and past episodes, and "Lenny's product pass" (lennisproduct.com), a free year of access to notable AI products exclusive to newsletter subscribers.
- Episode 06 features [[Tony Fadell]] (co-creator of the iPod, iPhone, Nest thermostat; author of *Build*), described by Lenny as having been near the top of his guest wish list since starting the podcast.
- Episode 02 guests/references include [[Adam Mosseri]] (Head of Instagram), [[Fiona Fung]] (Anthropic, Claude Code & Cowork), [[Boris Cherny]] (Anthropic, Claude Code), [[Cat Wu]] (Anthropic, Claude Code), [[Benedict Evans]], and [[Kevin Weil]] (former CPO at OpenAI).
- Has recurring segments including "fail corner" (a guest shares a major professional failure) and "hot seat corner."
- Available on Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube, and other podcast platforms.
- Episode 07 sponsors: [[WorkOS]] (season-presenting sponsor) and [[Vanta]] (compliance/security automation), plus the recurring "Lenny's Product Pass" pre-roll offer.
- Episode 07 closes with the podcast's recurring "lightning round" segment (favorite books, movies/TV, recent product discovery, life motto, and a final personal question) — used here to ask Benedict Evans about his old-phone collection.
- Episode 28 features [[Dr. Becky Kennedy]] (clinical psychologist, CEO of [[Good Inside]]) on applying parenting frameworks — repair, connecting before correcting, "good inside" (behavior vs. identity), the most generous interpretation, sturdy leadership, boundaries vs. requests, resilience over happiness — to leading adults at work; also features the recurring "AI corner" segment (Kennedy's use of Figma Make, Replit, Lovable, Claude, and ChatGPT) and lightning round.
- Episode 27 features [[Lazar]], [[Lovable]]'s first official "vibe coding engineer," on the rise of full-time professional vibe coding as a career, prompted by a mention from prior guest [[Elena Verna]] (Lovable's head of growth). Sponsors read: [[Strella]] (AI customer-research platform) and [[Samsara]] (physical-world hardware/software/edge AI), plus the recurring "Lenny's Product Pass" pre-roll and a mid-roll for [[WorkOS]] (enterprise-readiness APIs).
- Episode 33 features [[Aishwarya Naresh Reganti]] and [[Kiriti Badam]] on why most AI products fail and their "[[Continuous Calibration Continuous Development]]" framework for building them successfully. Sponsors read: [[Merge]] (unified integrations API), [[Strella]] (AI customer-research platform, repeat sponsor), and [[Brex]] (finance platform for founders), plus the recurring "Lenny's Product Pass" pre-roll ([[Whisper Flow]] and [[Raycast]] both cited as included products).
- Episode 35 features [[Jason Lemkin]] (founder/CEO of [[SaaStr]]), his second appearance, on replacing SaaStr's sales team with 20 AI agents plus 1.2 humans. Sponsors read: [[DX]] (developer intelligence platform, repeat sponsor) and [[Vercel]]'s v0 (web-development assistant), plus [[Data Dog|DataDog]]'s EPO (experimentation/feature-flagging platform) as a mid-roll.
- Episode 32 is an unconventional, non-AI-focused episode featuring [[Sam Lesson]] (partner at [[Slow Ventures]], former VP Product at [[Facebook]]) covering Silicon Valley etiquette end-to-end (introductions, conversations, hygiene, dress, dining, scheduling, communication, meetings, exiting), organized around his "[[Low Heart Rate]]" and [[Abundance Mindset]] framing. Sponsors read: [[10Web]] (AI website building), [[DX]] (developer intelligence platform), and [[WorkOS]] (enterprise-readiness APIs, customer list "OpenAI, Cursor, Perplexity, Vercel, Plaid"). Closes with the recurring AI Corner (Lesson's newsletter-to-cartoon aggregator, [[Lettermeme]]) and Contrarian Corner (his thesis that seed VCs backing "AI-branded" startups will lose enormous money) and lightning round.
- Episode 34 is a "greatest hits" episode featuring [[Molly Graham]] (early [[Google]]/[[Facebook]]/[[Quip]] operator, ran the [[Chan Zuckerberg Initiative]]'s first two years, now runs [[Glue Club]]) collecting her career/management frameworks: "[[Give Away Your Legos]]," "[[Bob The Monster]]," "[[J Curve Versus Stairs]]," the "[[Waterline Model]]," "[[Six Rules For Goals And Alignment]]," "[[Serve The Business Not The People]]," "[[Escalation Is A Tool]]," the "[[Fifty Percent Growth Rule]]," and "[[Founder Personality Defines Culture]]." Sponsors read: [[DX]] (developer intelligence platform, repeat sponsor), [[Brex]] (finance platform for founders, repeat sponsor), and a mid-roll for GoFundMe Giving Funds (donor-advised fund product).
