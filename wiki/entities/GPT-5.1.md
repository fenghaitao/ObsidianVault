## Definition

GPT-5.1 was released by OpenAI in November 2025 and was one of the two models (alongside Claude Opus 4.5) that crossed the threshold where AI coding agents went from "mostly works" to "almost always does what you tell it." Simon Wilson identifies this as the inflection point for AI coding.

## Key Information

- Released in November 2025 alongside Claude Opus 4.5
- Crossed a threshold: previously coding agents would "mostly work" but required close attention; GPT-5.1 made it so "almost all of the time it does what you told it to do"
- Part of OpenAI's year-long focus on coding using reinforcement learning and reasoning models
- The O1 model (late 2024) was the first to exhibit reasoning; by GPT-5.1, all models had reasoning capabilities
