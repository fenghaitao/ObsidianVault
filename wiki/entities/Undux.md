## Definition

Undux is an open-source React state-management framework Boris Cherny created because Redux felt overly complicated.

## Key Information

- Built after Flux and Redux; Boris couldn't wrap his head around reducers and wanted a simpler way to update state
- First used at a nonprofit he volunteered for, then launched internally at Facebook after seeing repeated Redux frustration in a support group
- Grew adoption by scraping the support group to tally issues by team, then delivering roughly 20–40 team tech talks
- Became one of the most popular state-management frameworks at Facebook before being replaced by Recoil and later Relay
