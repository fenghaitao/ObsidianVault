## Definition

Nathan Sharp (referred to in the episode only as "Nathan") is the former PM on Instagram Stories and the co-founder (with Ryan Olson) of the product studio that built Retro.

## Key Information

- Was the PM on the Instagram Stories project; he and Ryan worked closely and stayed close friends.
- Later moved to the Facebook side, started Facebook Dating, then worked on some virtual-reality products.
- Co-founded the product studio with Ryan when their timing finally aligned; they met at a bar called Lone Palm to discuss building products differently.
- They pay themselves less than all of their employees (with more equity), which Ryan sees as the right structure.
