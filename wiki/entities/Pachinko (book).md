## Definition

Pachinko is a multigenerational historical fiction novel that tells the story of a Korean family over several generations, including their migration to Japan. Jessica Fain recommends it as a "window" into an unfamiliar world.

## Key Information

- Multigenerational historical fiction about a Korean family over generations, moving to Japan
- Jessica Fain's favorite genre: multigenerational historical fiction that provides a "window" into unfamiliar worlds
- Jessica notes how the book contrasts the modern pace of life with historical rhythms: in the 1600s-1700s, two wives in one generation spent their entire day cooking, going to market, and serving meals
- Recommended by Jessica Fain in the lightning round
