## Definition

Malte Ubl is the CTO of Vercel with over 25 years of software engineering experience, previously at Google. He formerly ran JSConfEU in Berlin and delivered the opening keynote at the first AI Engineer conference in Europe.

## Key Information

- CTO of Vercel
- Over 25 years of software engineering experience, including time at Google
- Former organizer of JSConfEU in Berlin (ran for 10 years, ended summer 2019)
- Delivered the opening keynote at the first AI Engineer conference in Europe (2026)
- Has been hacking on two personal projects: chat SDK (agent-to-chat-app connector) and just bash (bash interpreter in TypeScript)
- Calls these his "vibe coding stack"
- Argues that AI engineering is the legitimate successor to web development as a mainstream engineering discipline
- Believes there is still a place for engineers in the future; next generation will be even better at AI engineering
- Advocates that almost all current agent harnesses have the wrong architecture (combining harness runtime with code execution runtime)
- Sees Europe as the leader in AI engineering innovation
