## Definition

Sheryl Sandberg is the former COO of Facebook. Molly Graham describes working with her as "like going to business school without going to business school" and learned an enormous amount from her.

## Key Information

- One of the key leaders Molly Graham worked closely with at Facebook
- Gave advice used by Graham: growing more than 100% every year is a bad idea — the happiest growth rate is 50%, 100% is manageable, anything more than doubling is "a world of pain"
- Graham says she learned an enormous amount from Sandberg, comparing it to a business school education
- When Graham was considering the risky mobile phone role at Facebook, Sandberg told her "that project will be dead in two months, but you can do it because you'll still have a job here"
