## Definition

Sheryl Sandberg (spelled "Cheryl" in this episode's transcript) was COO of [[Facebook]] and [[Molly Graham]]'s manager/mentor there. [[Lenny Rachitsky]] separately recalls her from his own time at [[Airbnb]].

## Key Information

- Graham followed Sandberg (and Elliot Schrage) from [[Google]] to Facebook in 2008.
- When [[Chamath Palihapitiya]] recruited Graham onto Facebook's mobile-phone project, Sandberg told her "that project will be dead in two months, but you can do it because you'll still have a job here" — approving the risk while managing expectations, a formative moment in Graham's "[[J Curve Versus Stairs]]" story.
- Graham credits Sandberg with teaching her "an enormous amount... going to business school without going to business school."
- Per Lenny Rachitsky (from his own time at Airbnb): gave a fireside chat there framing fast growth and hiring pressure as "a good problem to have," compared to stagnation — cited as an example of her general leadership philosophy on scaling.
