## Definition

AI-powered search/answer company named in this episode's [[WorkOS]] sponsor ad read as one of the "winning companies" powered by WorkOS's enterprise-readiness APIs, alongside [[OpenAI]], [[Cursor]], [[Vercel]], and [[Plaid]].

## Key Information

- Cited purely as part of the WorkOS customer list in the sponsor read ("What do OpenAI, Cursor, Perplexity, Vercel, Plaid, and hundreds of other winning companies have in common?").
- Maker of [[Comet]], its AI-powered web browser, which [[Sander Schulhoff]] cites as the subject of a real-world indirect [[Prompt Injection]] data-leak incident: a malicious web page tricked Comet's AI into exfiltrating the logged-in user's account data.
