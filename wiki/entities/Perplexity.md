## Definition
Perplexity is an AI company mentioned by Kitze as one of the players trying to build cloud-based AI agents, alongside Cohere and OpenAI. Represents the "cloud agents" future — polished but limited compared to custom self-hosted agents.

## Key Information
- Mentioned as building a cloud agent offering
- Part of the "cloud agents" future: for the masses but "too nerfed" for tinkerers
- Kitze's framework: cloud agents won't satisfy people who want to self-host and own models
