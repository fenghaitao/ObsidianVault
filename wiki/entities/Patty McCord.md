## Definition

Patty McCord (transcribed sometimes as "Batty") was Netflix's chief talent/people officer in its early days, credited as an architect of the Netflix culture memo and "freedom and responsibility" philosophy.

## Key Information

- In Netflix's early days, she personally interviewed every candidate and "had to bless every hire."
- Told David Ronca in his interview that Netflix doesn't value 24/7 work: "if you want to impress us, blow us away with what you can do in an 8-hour day."
- Wanted people who lie in bed at night thinking about a problem they're trying to fix at Netflix.
- In a meeting, thanked an employee for working 24/7, then told them "don't do this again... figure out how to set your team up so that you don't have to work all the time."
- David Ronca says he learned more from her than from Reed Hastings about being a good leader and a good collaborative teammate.
