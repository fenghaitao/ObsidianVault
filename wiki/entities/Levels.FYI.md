## Definition

Levels.FYI is a website that provides leveling frameworks and compensation data across tech companies. It was referenced by Ryan L. Peterman at the start of the panel to establish the definition of Staff Engineer across different FAANG companies.

## Key Information

- Ryan Peterman shared a diagram from Levels.FYI to give context on what Staff Engineer means across companies
- The diagram showed: Google L5 = Senior, L6 = Staff; Meta E5 = Senior, E6 = Staff; Amazon SDE3 = Senior, no "Staff" title (next level is Principal)
- The diagram was used to align the audience on what "Staff Engineer" means across different company vocabularies
- The resource helped establish the common understanding that Staff Engineer is about influence and scope across multiple teams

- Ricky confirmed his Google compensation numbers aligned with Levels.FYI data: $180K (L3), $250K (L4), $350K (L5), ~$520K (L6), all roughly average for their respective levels
- At the UCLA talk, Ryan noted that Levels.FYI is "pretty accurate" except for fluctuations in equity, and showed that Meta's numbers were much higher than Google's at the time due to Meta's stock "ripping recently"
