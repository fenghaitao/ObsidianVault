## Definition
Rich Hickey is the creator of the Clojure programming language and author of the influential 2011 talk "Simple Made Easy," which distinguishes between simplicity (structural, one fold, no entanglement) and ease (proximity, low effort).

## Key Information
- Creator of the Clojure programming language
- Gave the talk "Simple Made Easy" in 2011
- Defined "simple" as meaning "one fold, one braid, no entanglement" — each piece does one thing without intertwining with others
- Defined "easy" as meaning "adjacent" — what is within reach, what you can access without effort
- Argued you cannot make something simple by wishing; simplicity requires thought, design, and untangling
- His framework is central to Jake Nations' argument that AI is the "ultimate easy button" that makes the easy path frictionless
