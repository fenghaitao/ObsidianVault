## Definition

Guild is the company James Everingham founded to build a control plane / governance layer for managing AI agents inside enterprises.

## Key Information

- Founded from the observation that Meta's Dev Mate agentic platform had overwhelming internal pull ("40,000 developers is fun, but it'd be fun to go do this for 40 million").
- Thesis: agents need identity/access control, observability, circuit breakers, and policy — "you want to treat them like employees."
- It is a deterministic layer on top of non-deterministic AI, needed "regardless of which model company wins" (Anthropic, OpenAI, Meta, or someone else).
- Gremlins analogy: agents multiplying unpredictably need to be "bubble wrapped" to provide predictability and control.
