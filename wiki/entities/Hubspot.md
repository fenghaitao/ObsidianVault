## Definition

Hubspot is a CRM and marketing/sales platform. It is a Claude customer that is rolling out [[EnterpriseManagedAuthorization|Enterprise-Managed Authorization (EMA)]] across its teams.

## Key Information

- **EMA rollout:** Hubspot was cited by [[Anthropic]] in June 2026 as one of the organizations deploying Enterprise-Managed Authorization for [[MCPConnector|MCP connectors]] across its teams.
- **Enterprise adoption:** Hubspot's rollout of EMA demonstrates adoption of centralized MCP connector management among large enterprise Claude customers.
