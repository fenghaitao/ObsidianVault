## Definition

Rebecca Ambrose is a professional editor whom Philip Su hired to edit his podcast miniseries "Peak Salvation." Philip learned a great deal about storytelling and structure from her feedback.

## Key Information

- Philip hired Rebecca Ambrose to edit his podcast "Peak Salvation"
- Philip wanted the words to be well-structured and the story told well, and knew a professional would do a better job at editing
- Philip learned "a ton" from Rebecca's editing feedback — her professional touch is evident in the podcast episodes
- Apart from the podcast, Philip has never had an editor for any of his written posts
