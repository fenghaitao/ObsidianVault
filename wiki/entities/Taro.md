## Definition

Taro is Rahul's company focused on career growth for software engineers. It sponsored the September 2023 "Getting To Staff" panel.

## Key Information

- Founded by Rahul, who previously worked at Meta and Pinterest
- Focuses on career growth content for software engineers, particularly around promotion to senior levels
- Sponsored the Sept 2023 "Getting To Staff (IC6) at FAANG" panel
- Rahul described Taro as his employer, noting he can speak openly about his experiences because he runs the company
- Steve Huynh quoted Rahul Pandey from Taro: "Bad news that's delivered early is just news; bad news that's delivered late is terrible news" — a principle about proactive communication with managers
- Rahul and co-founder Alex met on the Portal team at Meta in 2017, stayed in touch, and eventually committed to working together for 6 months to a year
- Before quitting Meta, they ran free webinars during COVID about career growth, which drew up to 800 attendees
- They went through Y Combinator to fund Taro — Rahul recommends YC as a no-brainer for first-time founders without existing VC networks
- Initially the idea was to build a dev tool, but the community angle proved more powerful; they pivoted into courses and now have a robust course platform with community elements
- The team is still small (3 full-time people) after two and a half years
- Website: jointaro.com
