## Definition

Charles Rackoff is a cryptographer and co-author of the Goldwasser–Micali–Rackoff paper defining interactive proofs and zero-knowledge proofs.

## Key Information

- Part of the "Goldster Micali and Rakov" paper that introduced interactive proofs and zero-knowledge proofs.
- The paper conceived of proofs as randomized discussions rather than written documents, allowing a small error probability that can be reduced arbitrarily.
