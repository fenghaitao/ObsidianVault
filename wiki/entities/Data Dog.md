## Definition

Data Dog (Datadog) is an observability/analytics platform, sponsor of Lenny's Podcast episode 35, promoted specifically for "EPO" (Eppo), its experimentation and feature-flagging product line, aimed at product managers connecting product analytics to engineering issues.

## Key Information

- Ad-read pitch: positions EPO as letting PMs watch session replays, review funnels, and explore retention/growth metrics, then run experiments to validate fixes, tying product analytics directly to bugs, UX friction, and business impact — explicitly framed as "the same platform their engineers rely on every day."
- Host connection: Lenny cites his own experience at [[Airbnb]], noting the same team that built Airbnb's internal experimentation platform built Eppo.
- Positioned as going beyond raw engineering metrics into session replay (heat maps, scroll maps) and real-time feature-flag-gated rollouts.
