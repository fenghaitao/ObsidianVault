## Summary

Former Amazon Principal Engineer (L7) who spent 18 years at the company. Entered Amazon through a non-traditional path: majored in English/Creative Writing at the University of Washington with a minor in Math and Applied Math. Had been programming since childhood (IBM XT 8088, Pascal, C++) and took AP Computer Science in high school. Got into Amazon via a networking referral from a high school friend, starting as a green badge (contractor) Support Engineer before transitioning to full-time SDE. Climbed from SDE1 to Principal Engineer over 18 years, working on Kindle, Amazon Tickets, Prime Video, and payments. Conducted thousands of interviews and trained thousands of people as an Amazon Bar Raiser trainer. Now runs the YouTube channel "A Life Engineered" and a weekly newsletter.

## Key Details

- 18 years at Amazon, over 20 different managers
- English/Creative Writing major, University of Washington
- Started as green badge contractor (Support Engineer), transitioned to blue badge full-time
- Promoted to Senior Engineer (SDE3) in 2012, promoted to Principal Engineer (L7) in 2020
- Thought he was ready for Principal in 2016; took 4 additional years and the promotion process changed 3 times during that span
- Worked on Kindle, Amazon Tickets, Prime Video (live sports), and payments
- Trained thousands of people as a Bar Raiser trainer at Amazon
- Collects vintage computers as a hobby
- Runs "A Life Engineered" YouTube channel and newsletter
