## Definition

Dylan Field is a Flipboard intern classmate of Ryan Olson who went on to co-found and lead Figma, which later went public.

## Key Information

- Was an intern at Flipboard during part of Ryan's summer; he ended his internship early and founded Figma.
- Ryan observed (with Devin Finzer) that Dylan was determined to start a company from the start, unlike classmates who just wanted big-tech jobs.
- His early insight: WebGL was a new technology that would enable new things on the web, and his co-founder Evan Wallace was early and skilled at it.
- They iterated on use cases (even a meme-generator app "for a week or something") before finding the designer audience.
- Figma went public at roughly a $50B market cap on the day before the episode (per Ryan Olson).
