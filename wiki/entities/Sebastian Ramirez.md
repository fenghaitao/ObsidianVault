## Definition
Sebastian Ramirez is the creator of FastAPI and an early, influential supporter of Charlie Marsh's projects (notably Ruff).

## Key Information
- He noticed Ruff early ("oh, this is interesting, I'd like to use it"); Charlie asked himself what it would take for Ramirez to adopt it, then built those things.
- Remains a great supporter of Astral's projects.
