## Definition
Omar Khattab is the founder and original developer of DSPy, a declarative framework for programming language models from Stanford.

## Key Information
- Created DSPy with a "systems mindset," focusing on encoding intent in a transferable way.
- Appeared on the A16Z podcast discussing DSPy's philosophy.
- Emphasizes that DSPy is not primarily an optimizer but a set of programming abstractions; optimization is an added benefit.
- Leads research on prompt optimization being competitive with fine-tuning methods like GRPO.
