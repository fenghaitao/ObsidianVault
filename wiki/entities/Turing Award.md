## Definition

The Turing Award (the A.M. Turing Award) is the highest distinction in computer science, sometimes called the "Nobel Prize of computing."

## Key Information

- Leslie Lamport is a Turing Award winner, recognized for his contributions to distributed systems.
- Lamport says Butler Lampson "really deserved his Touring award" for his understanding of building systems.
- Lamport reflected that the reason he "wound up getting a Touring award" was not that he was particularly smart, but that he had his gift of abstraction.
- The award is referenced throughout the episode as shorthand for the highest tier of computer-science achievement.
