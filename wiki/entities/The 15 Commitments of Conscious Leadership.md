## Definition

*The 15 Commitments of Conscious Leadership* is named by [[Rachel Lockett]] as one of her most-recommended books in the episode's lightning round.

## Key Information

- Contains the concept of taking 100% responsibility rather than operating from victimhood or blame — Lockett cites this as directly relevant to how leaders should approach interpersonal conflict, entering with humility about their own contribution to a dynamic rather than assuming the other party is entirely at fault.
