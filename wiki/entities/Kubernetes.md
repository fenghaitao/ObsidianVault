## Definition

Kubernetes is the open-source container-orchestration system that [[Max Schoening]] cites as having beaten [[Heroku]] in the market despite (in his view) offering a worse user experience — used as the basis for his prediction about how the SaaS market will evolve.

## Key Information

- Schoening: "Kubernetes was much more successful than Heroku even though I think from a user experience perspective it was much worse but the delta was Heroku was saying hey we're going to replace your ops team and Kubernetes was we're going to make your ops team superheroes and also we're not going to lock you into a cloud you can choose."
- Cited as evidence that businesses value optionality/avoiding vendor lock-in over pure UX quality — informing his broader prediction (see [[SaaS Apocalypse]]) that the software market will keep favoring multi-provider flexibility even as individual layers commoditize.
