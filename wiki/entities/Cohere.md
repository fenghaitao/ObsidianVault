## Definition
Cohere is an AI research company and foundation model provider whose models are available through Amazon Bedrock's managed service.

## Key Information
- One of the leading model providers integrated with Amazon Bedrock
- Available alongside Anthropic, AI21 Labs, Meta, Mistral, Stability AI, and Amazon Titan on the Bedrock platform
- Accessible through the unified Converse API for standardized multi-model development
- **Kitze's critique**: Tried Cohere's cloud agent for 5 minutes — "this is too nerfed. This is not an OpenClaw alternative. It cannot do like even 5% of the things that OpenClaw can do."
- Represents the "cloud agents" future: polished for the masses but insufficient for tinkerers who want to self-host and own models
