## Definition

Fair is a fintech company founded by Max Rhodes and Jeff Kolodny, both former Square colleagues of Keith Rabois.

## Key Information

- Keith Rabois was a long-time investor from the seed round
- Reference checking story: VCs asked "Was Max a good employee?" (wrong question) vs. "Is Max capable of being a world-class entrepreneur?" (right question)
- Many good VCs passed on Fair because they framed the reference question incorrectly
- Those who passed "regret it"
