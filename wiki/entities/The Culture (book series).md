## Definition

*The Culture* is a science-fiction series by Iain M. Banks, recommended by [[Alexander Imbiricos]] in the episode's lightning round.

## Key Information

- Imbiricos values it as relatively recent writing depicting an optimistic future with AI, in contrast to most dystopian AI science fiction — jokingly described (per the Culture subreddit) as a "gay space communist utopia."
- He uses the series as a lens for thinking about what kind of world advanced AI could help usher in, and what decisions made today could help get there.
- [[Lenny Rachitsky]] recommends *A Fire Upon the Deep* as a companion AI-themed sci-fi read.
