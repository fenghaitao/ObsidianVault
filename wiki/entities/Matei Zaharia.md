## Definition

Matei Zaharia (transcribed "Mate Haria") is a Stanford faculty member, a co-founder of Databricks, and the original creator of Spark.

## Key Information

- Created Spark; at Databricks, up to a million Spark jobs might be orchestrated at once.
- Noted that OS schedulers didn't scale, so scheduling data was put in a Postgres database — the insight that seeded DBOS.
