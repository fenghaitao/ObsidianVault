## Definition

Bento is Meta's internal notebook platform based on Jupyter, used across the company for data science and machine-learning work, first built by Adrien Friggeri and a small team.

## Key Information

- Originated from a personal library Friggeri wrote to run data pipelines inside the ML orchestration framework (FBLearner Flow), cutting boilerplate from ~300 to ~20 lines with sensible defaults and an escape hatch.
- The first version was "really dumb" — a script that set up Jupyter; it grew into a company-wide platform with on-demand instances.
- Adoption strategy was twofold: (1) target new-hire boot camp/data camp so network effects compounded with company growth, and (2) provide support and maintenance for legacy systems to build trust before encouraging migration.
- Friggeri transitioned into a Tech Lead Manager role and built a team by hiring people who were already building tooling on the side.
- By the time Friggeri left Meta, Bento was used by ~60% of people doing data work at the company.
