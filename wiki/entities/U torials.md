## Definition

U torials ("Tutorials for you, by you") was an early side project by Lenny Rachitsky and a friend. It was a platform for people to contribute how-to guides and tutorials, similar in concept to what TikTok and YouTube later became.

## Key Information

- Name stands for "Tutorials for you, by you"
- People contributed how-to guides on various topics — how to make eggs, how to take a quick shower
- Lenny describes it as "so ahead of its time"
- Predated Wikipedia (or was concurrent with it) and was conceptually similar to what TikTok and YouTube became
- Built with a friend during college
