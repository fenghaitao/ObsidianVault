## Definition

~1,100-page biography of Robert Moses by [[Robert Caro]], recommended by [[Jenny Wen]] as an "aggressive" but worthwhile recommendation in an era of short attention spans, for its long-arc view of one controversial person's entire career.

## Key Information

- Wen values it for showing how someone's views and methods change across decades, and for a nuanced portrait of a controversial figure — a corrective to short-term thinking.
- Wen notes fellow design leader Julie Zhuo was independently reading it around the same time.
