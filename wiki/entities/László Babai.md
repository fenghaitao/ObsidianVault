## Definition

László Babai is a mathematician and computer scientist who, in parallel with Goldwasser, Micali, and Rackoff, proposed the interactive proof system model.

## Key Information

- Wigderson notes the interactive proof model was suggested in a paper "of babai parallel [at] the same time from different motivations."
- Interactive proofs effectively generalize NP by allowing an interaction and (small, reducible) randomized error.
