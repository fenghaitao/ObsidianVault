## Definition

IGTV was Instagram's long-form, mobile-native vertical video product, pitched by Kevin Systrom and designer Ian Spalter; it largely failed but informed what became Reels.

## Key Information

- Kevin Systrom and Ian Spalter designed and revealed the vision at an end-of-year all-hands as a surprise; a small team (Ryan Olson, Will Bailey, Thomas Dimson plus per-platform leads) built it.
- It aimed to combine YouTube-style long-form content with vertical format — a mismatch: people weren't producing high-quality long-form vertical, and inventory never materialized.
- They tried AI to reformat landscape video to vertical (Ryan holds a patent on it), but creators were "horrified" at their content being reformatted.
- Instagram's assumption that its platform could change the industry didn't fully materialize ("a little bit of hubris"), but the effort informed Reels.
