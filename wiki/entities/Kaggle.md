## Definition
Kaggle is a data science and model hosting platform where Gemma 4 models are available for self-hosting and download.

## Key Information
- One of three self-host options for Gemma 4 models (alongside Hugging Face and Ollama)
- Google-owned platform for data science competitions and model sharing
