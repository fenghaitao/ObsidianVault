## Definition
Kaggle is the world's largest AI/ML community with 30+ million users, owned by Google. Beyond its well-known data science competitions, Kaggle has been building a suite of open-source GenAI evaluation platforms over the past two years: hackathons for community-driven benchmark creation, standardized agent exams, Game Arena (PvP unsaturable benchmarks), and a Benchmark platform for anyone to build, run, and share evals.

## Key Information
- World's largest AI/ML community: 30+ million users
- Historically known for data science competitions
- Over the past two years, has invested heavily in GenAI eval platforms
- One of three self-host options for Gemma 4 models (alongside Hugging Face and Ollama)
- Google-owned platform
- Has a points and medals system for community incentivization
- Kaggle simulation platform (originally an RL platform) powers Game Arena game runs
- LLM model proxy available on Colab for consistent model interaction

### Eval Products
- **Eval Hackathons**: Community-driven benchmark creation events with guardrails and creative freedom
- **Agent Exams**: Standardized agent testing — paste a prompt, get a score on a leaderboard
- **Game Arena**: PvP game-based unsaturable benchmark with Elo scores (Werewolf, Poker, Chess)
- **Benchmark Platform**: Build, run, and share evals using assertions, LLM judging, tasks, and aggregated benchmarks
