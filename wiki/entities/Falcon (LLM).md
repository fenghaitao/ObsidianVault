## Definition
Falcon is an open-source large language model that was one of the most widely adopted open-source models approximately 3 years ago. Alessandro Cappelli was part of the team that trained it, and the core founding team of Adaptive ML came from this effort.

## Key Information
- **Timeline**: Trained approximately 3 years ago (circa 2023)
- **Significance**: One of the most widely adopted open-source models at the time
- **Legacy**: The team's experience training Falcon revealed the gap between open-source models and proprietary frontier models — specifically, the lack of reinforcement learning
- **Connection to Adaptive ML**: The founding team's realization about the RL gap led to the creation of Adaptive ML
