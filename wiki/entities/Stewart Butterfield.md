## Definition

Stewart Butterfield (born Dharma Jeremy Butterfield) is a founder and product leader who co-founded Flickr and Slack, the latter of which was sold to Salesforce in one of the biggest tech acquisitions of its time. He is known for his product taste, craft orientation, and unique mental models for building products people love.

## Key Information

- Born as Dharma Jeremy Butterfield; changed his name to Stewart at age 12 to be "normal"
- Co-founded Flickr (originally a pivot from a video game)
- Co-founded Slack (originally a pivot from the game Glitch)
- Sold Slack to Salesforce for one of the largest tech acquisitions at the time
- Left Salesforce 2.5 years ago (as of the podcast recording); has a young daughter born 3 days after his last day
- Currently focused on family, philanthropic work, personal creative art projects, and supporting other artists
- Has worked with Cal Henderson (CTO of Slack) for 23 years, across both Flickr and Slack
- Rarely does podcasts; this was a notable appearance
- Known for the "We Don't Sell Saddles Here" internal memo and the mantra "In the long run, the measure of our success will be the amount of value that we create for customers"
- Made employees chant the customer value mantra at company all-hands
- Described himself as feeling Slack was "a giant piece of [__]" that "we should be humiliated to offer to the public" — a perpetual desire to improve
- Had "Hey Stuart, what do you think?" sessions where PMs would sit with him for 30 minutes to download his belief system and past experience before designs were done
- Known for the "Stuart plus two more" rule: teams would deliver exactly what he asked for plus two alternative versions they felt good about
- Noah Weiss kept a notebook section of "things I learned from Stuart" — used for genuine learning, not politicking
- April Underwood used a whiteboard-marker technique in strategy meetings with Stuart to extract and package his ideas
- Described as having "always had better ideas than we did" by Jessica Fain — but also a learner with a growth mindset
- Jessica Fain notes that the alumni of Slack (many of whom have been on Lenny's Podcast) is a sign of "an incredible team"
