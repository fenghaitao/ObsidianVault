## Definition
Raia Hadsell is VP of Research at Google DeepMind, overseeing approximately 1200 scientists and engineers across 10 labs. She has been at DeepMind for nearly 13 years and serves as a UK AI Ambassador bridging government, academia, and industry.

## Key Information
- **Background**: Undergraduate degree in philosophy of religion (1990s), PhD in computer science at NYU working with Yann LeCun on convolutional neural networks for robots (2000s)
- **DeepMind tenure**: Joined when DeepMind had 30-40 people (~2013), now VP running ~1200 people across 10 labs
- **Research areas**: Frontier AI, next architectures for Gemini, world models, weather prediction, embedding models
- **Philosophy**: Root-node research — find the deepest unsolved problems rather than wasting time on leaves
- **UK AI Ambassador**: Helps bridge gaps between government, academia, and industry
- **Notable projects**: GraphCast, GenCast, FGN (weather prediction), Genie 1/2/3 (world models), Gemini Embeddings 2
- Presented at AI Engineer Summit in London (April 2026)
