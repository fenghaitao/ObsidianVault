## Definition

American EV maker cited by [[Qasar Younis]] as the closest US equivalent to celebrated Chinese EV companies — makes good products but loses significant money, and is consequently valued far lower (possibly not even a top-50 or top-100 Bay Area company by valuation). Used to argue that Chinese EV makers only look superior because they aren't held to the same profit-driven business assessment as a normal Western company. See [[China As State Extension]].
