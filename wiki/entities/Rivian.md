# Rivian

American electric vehicle company. [[Qasar Younis]] uses Rivian as an example of the unfair comparison between American and Chinese EV companies.

## Key Information
- Makes great EV products but loses money on them
- The market punishes Rivian for not being profitable
- Qasar: "If you said top 50 or top 100 companies in the Bay Area, I'm not sure Rivian would even make that list"
- The EV business is fundamentally tough — not a reflection on Rivian's people or products
- Chinese EV counterparts are lauded but don't face the same profit constraints because they are state-backed
