## Definition

Marcus is a product manager at [[Every]] who runs the writing app [[Spiral]]. [[Dan Shipper]] cites him as the anecdotal case that convinced him he is "super bullish" on PMs in the AI era.

## Key Information

- Previously ran Axios's writing product as a PM, growing it to tens of millions in ARR; took a year off, became deeply AI-pilled, and taught himself to build well using [[Cursor]] (extensively, for a long time) and now primarily [[Claude Code]].
- Described by Dan Shipper as "lightly technical" — understands concepts like database migrations and can read code, but could not have been hired for an engineering role even a year earlier.
- Ships faster than almost anyone else on the Every team by pairing his product sense and eye for user feedback with AI-driven building speed, without needing to organize a team to execute — Dan Shipper says this makes him feel "liberated."
