## Definition
Jamie Turner is the co-founder of Convex with James Cowling, and a former Dropbox engineer who redesigned the desktop client and sync protocol.
## Key Information
- Referred to in the episode as "Jamie, my co-founder."
- At Dropbox, after the storage system shipped, "Jamie went and redesigned the sync protocol, the desktop client."
- Shares Cowling's disposition that they can only work on things they actually care about and believe in.
