## Definition

Workday is a major enterprise HCM (human capital management) and financial management software company. It is cited by Rippling's leadership as a competitor that has failed to build a broad general-purpose software platform despite its market success.

## Key Information

- Described by Matt McGinness as "terrible software" that "everybody agrees on"
- Criticized for integrating with other systems "through flat files via SFTP" — a major limitation for AI use cases
- McGinness argues Workday has failed to build a truly broad platform on the "people primitive" foundation
- Seen as a legacy competitor that will struggle to leverage AI effectively due to data integration limitations
- Anthropic uses Workday — Amol mentions it as one of the SaaS tools they still use heavily, noting that despite all the talk of AI replacing SaaS, Anthropic still uses Workday, Slack, Figma, and many other tools
