## Definition

Workday is a large HCM/enterprise-software company, cited by [[Matt MacInnis]] as a cautionary contrast to [[Rippling]].

## Key Information

- MacInnis calls Workday's software widely regarded as bad — a view he says Workday itself would agree with — and argues that despite the company's commercial success, it failed to build a broad, general-purpose business-software platform on top of the "people primitive," which Rippling is explicitly trying to do instead.
