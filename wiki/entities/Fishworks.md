## Definition

Fishworks was the Sun Microsystems group (inside the San Francisco mission) that developed a new storage product (a storage appliance) and gave Bryan Cantrill some of his standout team experiences.

## Key Information

- Chartered by then-Sun CTO Greg Papadopoulos, Fishworks developed a storage product that was "going gangbusters," though Cantrill says with hindsight it "wasn't as great as we thought it was."
- The product's success was central to Cantrill's Distinguished Engineer case; Greg Papadopoulos presented the Fishworks-derived materials to the DE committee.
- Cantrill cites Fishworks (alongside Sun's E-Tris, Joyent's El Ax and Triton) as one of the "incredible team" experiences where everyone thought the work impossible but pulled it off together — the feeling that motivated Oxide.
