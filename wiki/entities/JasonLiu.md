## Definition

Jason Liu is a machine learning engineer, independent consultant, and angel investor. Creator of Instructor (Python library for structured outputs from LLMs, 1M+ monthly downloads) which inspired OpenAI's structured output feature. Known for his talk "Pydantic is All You Need."

## Key Information

- Background in mathematical physics and computational mathematics
- Worked at Meta on content analysis for cyber crime, and at StitchFix on recommendation systems
- Created Instructor, a library for structured LLM outputs
- Teaches organizations to approach AI scientifically: measurement, rapid iteration, meaningful outcomes
- Framework for RAG improvement: segment queries by satisfaction and volume, prioritize high-volume/low-satisfaction segments
- Argues most RAG failures are inventory problems (missing data) or capability problems (missing metadata/filters), not AI quality issues
- Consults for companies like Zapier and HubSpot
