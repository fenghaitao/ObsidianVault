## Definition

LinkedIn is a professional social networking platform owned by Microsoft, with a unique view into the world of work through its data on jobs, skills, and professional communities.

## Key Information

- Has a unique view of the world of work, providing data on skills change, job market trends, and professional network dynamics.
- Under Tomer Cohen's product leadership, transformed from a desktop-focused company to a mobile-first platform.
- Is piloting the full-stack builder model to reimagine product development in the age of AI.
- Has specific trust vectors unique to its platform (e.g., "Open to Work" feature exposing job seekers to potential scams).
- Maintains an enormous internal graph used by the analyst agent for querying data.
- Has a sophisticated growth team and growth process with unique internal loops and funnels.
- Features like semantic people search and semantic job search were built using the new AI-assisted tools.
- Re-architecting its core platform with composable UI components so AI can reason over the codebase.
- Uses both Copilot Enterprise and ChatGPT Enterprise internally, with heavy customization.
