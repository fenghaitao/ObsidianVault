## Definition

LinkedIn is a professional social networking platform. Multiple panelists use LinkedIn as their primary channel for publishing career growth content and building professional visibility.

## Key Information

- Ryan L. Peterman primarily posts on LinkedIn and writes a Substack
- Zach Wilson posts on LinkedIn and notes that LinkedIn's algorithm rewards conciseness — a useful skill for technical communication
- Lee writes on LinkedIn about tech career and mental health topics
- Carly posts on LinkedIn, pivoting toward humor and levity content
- Rahul is active on LinkedIn
- Rahul encourages people to connect with him on LinkedIn as a way to get help with career growth
- Jia Chen recommended posting projects on LinkedIn as a way to stand out to recruiters and increase visibility
- Zach noted that posting daily on LinkedIn taught him to make dense information concise, which translated to better technical communication at work. A Facebook recruiter reached out to Zach via LinkedIn in 2015, which he describes as the turning point that got him into big tech. Zach started actively creating content on LinkedIn in December 2020 after leaving Netflix, and his account grew to 50K+ followers by September 2021. He credits LinkedIn as being critical for his entire career since 2015. He had a viral post about applying for jobs you do not think you are ready for — he applied to Airbnb Staff with 6 years experience when the job description required 10+.
