## Definition
Steve Jobs was the co-founder and CEO of Apple, renowned for his product philosophy and insistence on design quality. In this talk, his famous principle is invoked as the antidote to AI-driven feature bloat.

## Key Information
- Quoted by Tuomas Artman: "Great products come out of saying no to 999 things and yes to one thing"
- His philosophy of disciplined product focus is presented as more relevant than ever in the age of AI
- With AI making it trivially easy to ship every feature request, Jobs' principle of saying no becomes the critical skill for maintaining product coherence
- Apple's Human Interface Guidelines are recommended by Tuomas as the best resource for learning good UX
