## Definition

DX is a developer intelligence platform designed by leading researchers that helps organizations understand how AI tools are being used and what impact they have on engineering productivity. It was a sponsor of this episode.

## Key Information

- Sponsor of Lenny's Podcast episode 31
- Designed by leading researchers to help organizations adapt to the AI era
- Helps leaders answer questions like: which tools are working, how are they being used, what's driving value
- Used by companies like Dropbox, Booking.com, Adyen, and Intercom
- Provides data and insights on how AI provides value to developers and impacts engineering productivity

- Sponsor of Lenny's Podcast episode 26
- Website: getdx.com/lenny
