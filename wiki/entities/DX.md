## Definition

Developer-intelligence platform (getdx.com), presented as one of this episode's sponsors, positioned as helping engineering leaders measure which AI tools are working, how they're used, and what's driving value.

## Key Information

- Ad read describes it as "designed by leading researchers to thrive in the AI era," addressing organizations struggling to answer which tools are working, how they're used, and what's actually driving value.
- Named customers in the ad read: Dropbox, Booking.com, Adion, and Intercom — described as using DX to understand how AI is providing value to their developers and its impact on engineering productivity.
