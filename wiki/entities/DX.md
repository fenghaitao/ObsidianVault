## Definition
DX is a developer productivity and experience measurement company, acquired by Atlassian. It provides tools and frameworks for measuring developer productivity, including AI adoption impact.

## Key Information
- Described as "the developer productivity measurement company"
- Has large aggregate datasets used to analyze GenAI impact on engineering teams
- Developed the DXAI Measurement Framework, organizing AI metrics into Utilization, Impact, and Cost dimensions
- Created the "Core Four" metric set inspired by DORA and SPACE frameworks
- Conducted studies showing extreme per-company volatility in AI adoption outcomes
- Published an "AI strategy playbook for senior executives"
- Emphasizes effective surveys with 90%+ participation rates, treating developer experience as a systems problem
- Acquired by Atlassian
- Used by Intercom for developer surveys and productivity measurement as part of the 2x project
