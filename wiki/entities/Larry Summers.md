## Definition

Economist and friend of [[Marc Andreessen]], credited with the career-planning advice "don't be fungible" — don't be a replaceable cog by having only one narrow skill.

## Key Information

- Andreessen cites this as economics-speak for the same idea behind [[Scott Adams]]'s Dilbert career framing: combining rare skills makes you not just harder to replace, but "massively important," since you may be one of very few people who can do that specific combination.
