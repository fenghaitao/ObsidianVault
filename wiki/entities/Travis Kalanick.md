## Definition

Travis Kalanick is the co-founder and former CEO of Uber and the founder/CEO of Atoms (Brian Attwell's company). He is an "intense polymath" whose track record and standards drew strong engineers to his new venture.

## Key Information

- Built Uber; after leaving, founded Atoms (referenced in the episode as Cloud Kitchens), pulling many of Uber's strongest engineers
- Described by Attwell as an "intense polymath" with "intense dad energy" — patiently coaching people in backgammon and water skiing at offsites
- Reviewed Attwell and the head of Picnic's redesigned leveling structure, leaving comments on "how each clause can be better" and dismissing it as "lazy… over-indexing on scope… uninsightful"
- Gave Attwell permission to discuss the (stealth) company thesis on the podcast: "I could give you a bit more than we've given anybody before"
