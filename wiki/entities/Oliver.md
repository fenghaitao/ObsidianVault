## Definition
Oliver is an AI-first advertising startup that transitioned from traditional advertising to almost fully generative AI. With 3,000 staff across 46 countries, they generate around 4,000 assets per day for more than 200 brands, putting significant media spend (20K to a few million) behind their AI-generated creative work.

## Key Information
- Originally a traditional advertising agency, now almost fully gen AI.
- 3,000 staff across 46 countries.
- Generates ~4,000 assets per day for 200+ brands including Johnnie Walker.
- Unlike other gen AI content agencies, Oliver puts real media spend behind AI-generated assets, enabling performance measurement in the wild.
- This creates a strong feedback loop: large data volumes → faster iteration → deeper understanding of what actually works.
- Agency structure: accounts (client relationships), creative (ideation and content), strategy (insight, audience, direction).
- Both creative and strategy departments are increasingly agentic: ideation, copywriting, content production, audience insight, trends analysis, competitor analysis, and performance optimization all done with agents.
- Operates in a high-paced, high-risk environment — poor reception of AI-generated images can be as damaging as good ones are beneficial.
- Uses agents primarily for speed (faster iteration) and secondarily for scale (campaign and territory personalization).
- Localizes advertising for specific cities (e.g., New York vs. Miami) using deep research agents.
