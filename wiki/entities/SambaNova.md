## Definition
SambaNova is a hardware company providing AI inference chips. In Callosum's heterogeneous recursion benchmarks, SambaNova hardware delivered 12x cheaper and 3x faster performance compared to GPT-5.2 on the ULong benchmark.

## Key Information
- Hardware partner used by [[Callosum]] for heterogeneous intelligence workloads
- On ULong benchmark (heterogeneous recursion): 12x cheaper and 3x faster than GPT-5.2
- Tradeoff vs Cerebras: lower cost but slightly higher latency compared to Cerebras (7x cheaper, 5x faster)
- Represents the heterogeneous paradigm where different hardware is chosen based on task demands
