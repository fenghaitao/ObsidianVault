## Definition

Refactoring is Martin Fowler's book codifying the practice of restructuring code without changing its behavior; Casey Muratori critiques its treatment of performance.

## Key Information

- Casey groups Refactoring with Clean Code as guidance that defers or dismisses performance concerns.
- He argues such guidance prevents compilers from applying optimizations and should discuss the performance trade-off explicitly.
