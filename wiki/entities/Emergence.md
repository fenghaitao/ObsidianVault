## Definition

Emergence is an R&D AI company advancing the science and development of AI agents. Founded by Dr. Satya Nitta, the company builds infrastructural platforms enabling enterprises to deploy autonomous AI agents for complex workflows.

## Key Information

- Describes itself as a "distributed systems meets AI R&D team."
- Team drawn from top AI labs: IBM Research, Google Brain, Amazon Alexa, Meta, Microsoft, and others.
- Prior team experience includes building scaled AI deployments: IBM Watson platform, Amazon Alexa, recommendation engines for Amazon Prime, Amazon Video, and Twitter.
- Building two core platforms:
  - **Orchestrator Agent**: Launches August 2025 (early access live), orchestrates across multiple agents and LLMs for complex enterprise workflows. Completes the full agentic loop: plan → act → verify → remember → improve.
  - **Agent E**: Open-source web agent that controls web browsers autonomously. Currently tops the Web Voyager Benchmark.
- Core R&D focus areas: self-improving agents, AI planning and reasoning, agent-oriented programming.
- Target enterprise workflows: RPA, document processing, claims processing, access management.
- Developer dashboard provides prompt analysis, cost/latency optimization, custom model creation, and LLM migration support.
