## Definition
Nimrod Hauser is a founding engineer at Baz, an AI-powered code reviewer company. He has over 20 years of backend and data engineering experience, with a career spanning Salesforce, cybersecurity startups, crypto startups, and now developer tools.

## Key Information
- Founding engineer at Baz since the company was founded in 2023.
- Has been building AI-powered code reviewers for several years at Baz.
- Also works on other features to improve R&D productivity for developers, PMs, and other roles.
- Describes himself as someone who tinkers with anything agentic ("If it's Agentech, we're probably tinkering with it").
- Presented a framework of five best practices for optimizing third-party agentic tools at aiDotEngineer.
