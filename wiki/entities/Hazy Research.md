## Definition
Hazy Research is a Stanford University research group known for work on efficient AI, including the ThunderKittens project and recent research on "intelligence per watt" as a metric for tracking AI efficiency improvements over time.

## Key Information
- Based at Stanford University
- Known for ThunderKittens, a research project on efficient GPU kernels
- Recently published work on "intelligence per watt" (more accurately intelligence per joule), tracking how model quality per unit of energy is improving exponentially
- Their metric shows ~5x improvement in intelligence per joule over 2 years from hardware improvements alone, with an additional 3x from model improvements
- Alex Cheema cites their work as validation that local inference is on an exponential improvement trajectory
