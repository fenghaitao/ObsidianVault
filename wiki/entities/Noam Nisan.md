## Definition

Noam Nisan is a computer scientist who, with Avi Wigderson, developed the Nisan–Wigderson pseudorandom generator.

## Key Information

- Co-developed the NW generator, which starts from a few truly random bits and generates many pseudorandom bits whose indistinguishability rests on a hard function.
- Wigderson's analogy: hardness lets you "make your first million dollars" (one hard bit), and then "there are methods to make many more" — more than the bits you started with.
- The generator is at the heart of the hardness vs randomness paradigm.
