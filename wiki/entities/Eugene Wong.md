## Definition

Eugene Wong (transcribed "Gene Wong"/"Jean") is the Berkeley professor who mentored Mike Stonebraker and co-started the Ingres database project in 1972.

## Key Information

- Took Stonebraker "under his wing" in 1971 — "you're way ahead if you get adopted by a mentor who knows the ropes."
- Proposed they build a relational system after Ted Codd's 1970 paper, which became Ingres.
