## Definition

Tejas Prabhune is an electrical engineering and computer science major at [[UCBerkeley]] and co-creator of [[SimFrancisco]], a winning project at [[Anthropic]]'s [[ClaudeOpus4.8]] Build Day hackathon (June 2026). He is also exploring a post-training company, using Sim Francisco to test whether simulated personas can stay consistent enough to train models on long-horizon tasks.

## Key Information

- Co-created [[SimFrancisco]] with [[TanmayiPriyaDasari]], modeling San Francisco with 10,000 synthetic residents drawn from US Census data.
- Electrical engineering and computer science major at [[UCBerkeley]]; met his hackathon teammate Tanmayi through the university's Machine Learning club.
- Sim Francisco uses an evolutionary clustering algorithm (created by [[Claude]]) that batched 10,000 residents into ~300 representative personas, cutting inference cost 10-100x.
- Using Sim Francisco as a test bed for his post-training company, exploring whether simulated personas can maintain enough consistency to train models on long-horizon tasks.
- Competed at the [[ClaudeOpus4.8]] Build Day hackathon on June 13, 2026, a 12-hour event with 310 builders.
