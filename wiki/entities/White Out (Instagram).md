## Definition

White Out is Instagram's 2016 visual redesign — flat icon, white "chrome," semantic colors — that Ryan Olson co-led with a designer (transcribed "Joy Vincent"), and which essentially defines how Instagram looks today.

## Key Information

- A couple months into Ryan's tenure; goal was to strip color from the chrome so all focus sits on the content (photos/videos).
- Mike Krieger said not to A/B test it ("Build and ship it"), but a Facebook executive overrode that the night before launch, forcing A/B tests (a 2% holdout) and delaying the planned launch party.
- Built behind a feature flag with colors made semantic so it merged incrementally — avoiding the painful long-lived-branch merge (like the Direct Messaging feature had been).
- The icon was controversial and got "raked on Twitter" at launch, but data showed it materially increased daily app opens.
- The lead designer (transcribed "Joy Vincent") was "an incredible talent and the nicest guy ever," who passed away while at Instagram.
