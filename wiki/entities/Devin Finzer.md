## Definition

Devin Finzer (transcribed in the episode as "Devon Fin Fininser") is a Flipboard intern classmate of Ryan Olson who went on to co-found OpenSea.

## Key Information

- Host Ryan L. Peterman identifies him as the other Flipboard intern (alongside Dylan Field) who founded a multibillion-dollar company — OpenSea ("OpenC" in the transcript).
- Like Dylan Field, he was determined from early on to start a company rather than take a job.
