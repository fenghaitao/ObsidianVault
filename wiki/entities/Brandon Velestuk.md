## Definition

Brandon Velestuk was the creative director for product development at Slack, and co-creator of the "tilting your umbrella" metaphor with Stewart Butterfield.

## Key Information

- Was creative director for product development at Slack at the time
- Walking with Butterfield in Vancouver's Yaletown neighborhood in the rain, they noticed how few people would tilt their umbrellas to avoid poking others
- This observation became the "tilting your umbrella" metaphor for Slack's product philosophy: small courtesies and empathy create competitive advantage
