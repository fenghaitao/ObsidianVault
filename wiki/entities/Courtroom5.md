## Definition

Courtroom5 is a legal-tech company focused on access to justice, offering free/low-cost tools to self-represented litigants. Its tools are available to Claude users via an MCP connector as part of Anthropic's May 2026 legal-industry access-to-justice partnerships (alongside BoardWise, Descrybe, and the Free Law Project).

## Key Information

- CEO & Co-Founder: Sonja Ebron.
- Quote: *"Most people don't know they have legal rights until it's too late to use them. Claude can now meet them where they are — in the moment they're scared and searching for answers."*
- Cited alongside the Free Law Project and Justice Technology Association as part of Anthropic's legal aid / public-service push, which also includes discounted Claude for Nonprofits pricing for qualifying legal aid clinics, public defenders, and nonprofit legal services organizations.
