## Definition

Cixin Liu is a Chinese science fiction author best known for the Three-Body Problem trilogy. Boris Cherny recommends his short story collection The Wandering Earth even more than Three-Body Problem.

## Key Information

- Author of the Three-Body Problem trilogy
- Boris recommends his short stories even more than Three-Body Problem
- The Wandering Earth is one of his short story collections
- Boris notes Chinese sci-fi has a "very different perspective than Western sci-fi"
- Three-Body Problem was adapted into a Netflix series that Boris recommends
