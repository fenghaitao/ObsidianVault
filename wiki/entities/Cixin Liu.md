## Definition

Chinese sci-fi author best known for *Three-Body Problem*; [[Boris Cherny]] recommends his short-story collection *The Wandering Earth* even more highly, valuing its distinctly non-Western perspective.

## Key Information

- Per [[Kiriti Badam]] (episode 33): names the *Three-Body Problem* series as his favorite sci-fi, citing its blend of speculative science-fiction, life-outside-Earth themes, geopolitics, and the outsized impact of abstract science on human progress — which he sees as a useful analogy for AI's own long-run importance. [[Lenny Rachitsky]] agrees it's among his favorite series, noting it takes about one and a half books to become "mindblowing."
