## Definition

Elena Verna is Head of Growth at Lovable and a repeat guest on Lenny's Podcast (record four appearances). She is a growth leader with 15-20 years of experience at companies including Miro, Dropbox, SurveyMonkey, Netlify, and Amplitude.

## Key Information

- Currently Head of Growth at Lovable, which hit $200M ARR in under one year
- Has been at Lovable for ~6 months (as of recording); previously at Dropbox
- Holds the record for most appearances on Lenny's Podcast (four times)
- Claims only 30-40% of her 15-20 years of growth experience transfers to AI-native companies
- Spends 95% of time innovating on growth at Lovable vs. 5% on optimization (inverse of previous roles)
- Key growth strategies: building in public, giving product away for free, shipping velocity, influencer marketing, community
- Runs "She Builds" — a women-only hackathon initiative to address the gender gap in AI adoption
- Newsletter at elenaverna.com where she posts findings and patterns from her work
- Uses AI extensively: ChatGPT deep thinking mode, Granola for meeting summaries, Whisper Flow for voice-to-text, Lovable for prototyping
- Advocates for work-life balance through moment-by-moment prioritization rather than trying to achieve "balance"
- Believes vibe coding is a skill worth adding to resumes; non-technical vibe coders are becoming a new role
- Hired Lazar as the first official "vibe coding engineer" at Lovable; discovered him through his building-in-public on LinkedIn and YouTube
- Brought Lazar onto the growth team initially; his role expanded to serve all departments
