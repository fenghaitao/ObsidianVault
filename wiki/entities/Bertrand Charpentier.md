## Definition
Bertrand Charpentier is a speaker from Pruna who gave a talk at aiDotEngineer on rethinking what "state-of-the-art" means for AI models. He advocates for evaluating models along a Pareto frontier of quality vs. efficiency rather than picking a single "best" model from leaderboards.

## Key Information
- Works at [[Pruna]], a company building compressed, efficient "performance models"
- Argues that public leaderboards are inconsistent, not task-specific, and lack statistical significance
- Demonstrates that manual inspection introduces double bias (evaluator preference + sample selection)
- Shows that ChatGPT Image evaluations cost 20 days of compute vs. 7 hours with an optimized model
- Advocates for Pareto frontier plots (efficiency vs. quality) to identify multiple SOTA models
- Pruna's compression techniques: module-specific quantization, pruning, and denoising step reduction (distillation, caching)
- Open-sources compression algorithms through Pruna's package while also offering proprietary hosted models
