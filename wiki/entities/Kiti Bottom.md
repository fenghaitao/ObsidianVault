## Definition

Kiti Bottom (Kiti) works on Codex at OpenAI and has spent a decade building AI and ML infrastructure at Google and at Kumo. He has led/supported over 50 AI product deployments across companies including Amazon, Data Bricks, OpenAI, Google, and both startups and large enterprises. He co-teaches the #1 rated AI course on Maven with Aishwaria Raanti.

## Key Information

- Works on [[Codex]] at [[OpenAI]]
- Previously built AI/ML infrastructure at [[Google]] and Kumo
- Co-creator of the Continuous Calibration Continuous Development (CCCD) framework
- Helped build Codex's code review product which gained significant traction
- Describes Codex's approach to evals as balanced: evals for core product safety + heavy customer feedback monitoring + social media listening
- Believes multi-agent systems are misunderstood — peer-to-peer agent coordination is extremely hard to control; supervisor-sub-agent patterns are more successful
- Believes coding agents are still underrated despite chatter — penetration outside the Bay Area is very low
- Predicts 2025-2026 will be incredible for process optimization with AI
- Emphasizes persistence as the key differentiator — "pain is the new moat"
- Productivity enthusiast: uses [[Raycast]] and [[Caffeinate]] for long-running Codex tasks
- Favorite sci-fi: [[Three Body Problem]] trilogy
- Enjoys [[Expedition 33]] video game
- Married to [[Aishwaria Raanti]] for 4 years
- Life motto: "you can only connect the dots looking backwards" ([[Steve Jobs]])
