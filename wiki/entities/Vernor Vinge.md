## Definition

Vernor Vinge is a science fiction author of A Fire Upon the Deep. Both Lenny and Boris recommend the book, noting it's interesting from an AI/AGI perspective.

## Key Information

- Author of A Fire Upon the Deep and A Deepness in the Sky
- Lenny: "It's so interesting from an AI AGI perspective"
- Boris notes A Deepness in the Sky is also great; he thinks it's the greater of the two
- Boris: "I like how deepness in the sky also"
