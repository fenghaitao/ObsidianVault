Antje Barth is a speaker and AWS representative who presents on the transition from software developer to AI engineer, covering practical steps including foundation model fundamentals, AI developer tools, prototyping with Amazon Bedrock, and building agentic applications.

- Highlights AWS services including Amazon Q Developer and Amazon Bedrock
- Co-presented with colleague Mike Chambers who created product demos
