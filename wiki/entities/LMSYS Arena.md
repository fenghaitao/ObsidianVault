## Definition
The LMSYS Arena (Chatbot Arena) is a public LLM leaderboard that vindicated Gemini's Flash 2.0 thinking model when a Wall Street Journal table mis-ranked Gemini.
## Key Information
- A friend sent Vlad a screenshot of an "ill-written" Wall Street Journal table placing DeepSeek V3 and ChatGPT near the top and Gemini near the bottom.
- Vlad checked the live leaderboard and saw the just-released Flash 2.0 thinking model in the top-right corner, far ahead of DeepSeek V3.
- The episode frames this as an important, if understated, team accomplishment against an "open source narrative."
