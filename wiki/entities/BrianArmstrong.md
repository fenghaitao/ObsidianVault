## Definition
Brian Armstrong is the CEO of Coinbase, known for aggressively pushing AI tool adoption by requiring all engineers to use AI tools and firing an engineer who didn't comply within a week.

## Key Information
- CEO of Coinbase
- Sent an email requiring all engineers to "get on board and use AI tools"
- Threatened to have a conversation with anyone who didn't use AI tools within a week
- Fired an engineer a week later (on a Saturday) who didn't comply
- Engineers at Coinbase have base salaries of $300-400K plus equity
- The message was effective: "everyone just started to use it"
