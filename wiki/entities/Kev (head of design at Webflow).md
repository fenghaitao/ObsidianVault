## Definition

Kev is the head of design at Webflow. He is known for responding quickly to subtle executive cues and turning them into actionable frameworks.

## Key Information

- Head of design at Webflow
- When CPO Rachel Woolen mentioned design reviews would need rethinking, Kev had a Loom with a framework ready within an hour
- Framework covered: high-risk vs low-risk design changes, blast radius, release processes, and how to enable anyone in the organization to ship designs to production
- Exemplifies the skill of picking up on subtle executive threads and responding quickly
