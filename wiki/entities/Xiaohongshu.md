## Definition

Xiaohongshu (Little Red Book, also known as RED) is a Chinese social media and e-commerce platform. Ricky's mother used it to research his career level and finally validated his success.

## Key Information

- Ricky's mother, who had been skeptical of his career without an MBA, looked up his staff engineer level on Xiaohongshu and finally acknowledged: "Oh wait, that's kind of good"
- This illustrates how even immigrant parents who doubt a tech career path can be won over by the concrete prestige of FAANG leveling
