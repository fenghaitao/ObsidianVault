## Definition
Towards AI is an educational company that creates courses, videos, training, and technical content around AI engineering. Co-founded by Louis-François Bouchard (CTO), the company builds production-ready AI agent systems and teaches others how to build them through courses like the Agentic Engineering course.

## Key Information
- Educational company focused on AI engineering content: courses, videos, training, technical articles
- Co-founded by Louis-François Bouchard (CTO)
- Creates technical content requiring AI engineers, writers, and editors — an expensive, time-consuming process
- Built a deep research and writing automation system to augment (not replace) their content production pipeline
- The system was used to build a course that teaches how to build the system itself — a meta-educational loop
- Offers an Agentic Engineering course with 34 lessons, three end-to-end portfolio projects, certificate, and Discord community
- The course is rated 5/5 by 300+ students, with the first six lessons available for free
- Uses Gemini (Pro and Flash) as their primary LLM, citing the free tier as beneficial for students
- Employs Samridhi as a consultant and Paul Iusztin as a contributor
- Presented a 2-hour workshop at aiDotEngineer on building deep research agents
