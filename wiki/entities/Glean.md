## Definition

Enterprise search/data-integration platform [[Evan Spiegel]] uses to build a personal AI agent that scans [[Snapchat]]'s dashboards, documents, and leadership updates and surfaces what needs his attention.

## Key Information

- Described by Spiegel as the most secure way for him to access Snap's different documents and dashboards; some related agent work is also done in [[Claude]].
- Framed as enabling the flat, fast-moving leadership style Spiegel says he's always wanted for the company.
