## Definition
Benji is Kitze's "life OS" app — an all-in-one productivity tool combining todos, habits, calendar, planner, events, routines, food tracking, and 60+ other features. Named after Kitze's dog (also the mascot). Despite 3-4 years of development, it has never been properly shipped due to ADHD-driven feature creep.

## Key Information
- Started in 2022 as "an app to rule them all"
- 60+ features: todos, habits, planner, events, routines, food tracking, calendar, and more
- Named after Kitze's dog; the dog is the mascot
- Still not properly finished after 3-4 years: "Maybe one more feature. Maybe one more feature."
- No landing page — joked about on a podcast: "Very ambitious for someone who doesn't have a landing page for Benji"
- Kitze's vision: "Benji phone and a Benji OS"
- **Benji AI Assistant** (2023, unshipped): voice-to-agent feature — press a key, record with microphone, periodically cut speech and call Benji APIs to update calendar/todos live. Went viral on Twitter but never shipped.
- 2023 implementation was before models reliably returned JSON — had to "bully the models to return JSON"
- Others took one feature (food tracking via photo → calorie analysis) and "made multi-millions"
- "But I have 60 features. There's a lesson in there."
- Preceded by TodoDoIt (2016, priority-based todos) and Better (expanded to habits/planner/events)
