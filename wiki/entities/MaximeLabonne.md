## Definition
Maxime Labonne is the Head of Pre-training at Liquid AI, where he leads the development of small frontier models (Liquid Foundation Models) for edge deployment across text, vision, and audio modalities.

## Key Information
- Head of Pre-training at Liquid AI
- Works on edge models ranging from 350M to 24B parameters
- Released LFM 2.5 350M text model and VLM 450M vision-language model
- Advocates that small models have unique challenges distinct from scaled-down large models
- Developed techniques for reducing doom loops in small reasoning models using DPO and RL
- Believes small models excel at agentic tasks when paired with tools like web search and Python
