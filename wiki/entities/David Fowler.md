## Definition

David Fowler is a Distinguished Engineer at Microsoft who joined as an intern in 2006 and was promoted 11 times over 17+ years. He helped build NuGet, SignalR, and .NET Core.

## Key Information

- From Barbados; went to Florida Tech, switched from computer engineering to computer science, and had been coding since age 10
- Got his Microsoft internship by showing a game demo at a career fair rather than résumé prestige, then interned in 2006 and 2007 (one of the last intern groups to visit Bill Gates's house)
- Founding team on NuGet (first engineer); built SignalR as a nights/weekends side project that became an official product, his first real tech lead
- Pulled onto .NET Core as one of its architects alongside ~30 engineers; promoted to principal the same year as the first .NET Core design/build and the birth of his first child
- Reached Distinguished Engineer through Microsoft's peer-review process, the same level Guido van Rossum holds — a source of "maximum imposter syndrome"
- Lives by "code is currency" and permissionless agency: build a working prototype and show it, "you can just do things"
- Still codes daily by choice and blocks eight hours a week for IC work
- Mentors included Jeffrey Snover (PowerShell creator); they pushed him to ask decision-makers directly what the next level requires, and to amplify strengths rather than fix every weakness
