## Definition

Ruff is Astral's fast Python linter and formatter, written in Rust. Zanie Blue discussed how Ruff must evolve for agents: lower-confidence diagnostics (agents don't get fatigued), more unsafe fixes (agents can assess edge cases), and plugin support for agent-defined custom lint rules as a form of memory.
