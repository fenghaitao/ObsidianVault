## Definition

Box is a cloud content management and file sharing company. Jeetu Patel previously worked at Box before joining Cisco.

## Key Information

- Cloud content management and collaboration platform
- Jeetu Patel worked at Box before Cisco, operating in the "apps layer" building products for end users
- Jeetu left Box on emotional but good terms, maintaining a close relationship with the Box team
- Jessica Fain started her PM career at Box; her first PM manager was Annie Pearl
- Jeff Weinstein and Jessica Fain worked together at Box
