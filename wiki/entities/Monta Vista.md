## Definition

Monta Vista High School is a highly competitive public high school in Cupertino, California, in the Bay Area. Ricky attended Monta Vista, and Ryan joked about its intense academic culture.

## Key Information

- Ryan joked that Ricky's competitive drive came from attending Monta Vista, a famously intense Bay Area high school: "He's from the Bay Area. He went to one of those intense high schools"
- Ricky acknowledged he's been "trying to get the Monta Vista out of me" but that his competitive nature from that environment contributed to his drive for career acceleration
- Ryan noted that Ricky claimed he was "scammed" into being ambitious, but Ryan countered that Ricky had "something inside of him — he's pretty competitive"
