## Definition
Till Draw is an open-source project that closed its issue and pull request tracker due to being overwhelmed by AI-generated ("clanker") submissions. Mario Zechner cited it as an example of how AI agents are destroying open source.

## Key Information
- Closed down their issue and pull request tracker entirely
- Cited by Mario Zechner as evidence of "clankers" (AI agents) destroying OSS
- Part of a pattern: Open Claw's tracker similarly overwhelmed, half of Pi's tracker is Open Claw instances posting garbage
