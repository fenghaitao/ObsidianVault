## Definition

ChatGPT is OpenAI's conversational AI model, available in both consumer and enterprise versions, used extensively at LinkedIn for powering knowledge-corpus agents.

## Key Information

- LinkedIn uses both ChatGPT Enterprise and Copilot Enterprise for building internal AI agents.
- Tomer Cohen uses ChatGPT's voice mode during car rides and wishes for a steering-wheel button that invokes an AI assistant.
- Voice mode currently has an issue with interrupting too often, though users can configure it to let them finish speaking.
- Customization of ChatGPT Enterprise was far more important than the off-the-shelf product for LinkedIn's use cases.
- The original internal name for ChatGPT was "super assistant," reflecting the vision of a ubiquitous AI helper that works across all contexts
- GPT-5 launched in August 2025, and the Codex team saw 20x growth since then
- GPT-5.1 Codex Max shipped in 2026, offering ~30% faster task completion and higher intelligence at higher reasoning levels
- The vision for ChatGPT is a super assistant that uses code as its primary interface to interact with computers, making coding a core competency of the product
- Nick Turley, head of ChatGPT, was a former podcast guest and is described by Alexander Imbiricos as having the mantra "are we maximally accelerated?" which has infiltrated all of OpenAI
- Elena Verna uses ChatGPT's deep thinking mode extensively for brainstorming at Lovable, noting it takes a long time but is worth it for generating new angles and ideas
- Zevi Arnovitz used GPT Projects as his first AI development environment, creating a "CTO co-founder" with a custom prompt
- GPT Projects allow compartmentalization with shared custom instructions and knowledge base across chats
- Zevi found ChatGPT's memory feature problematic because it mixed contexts (running, PM work, psychology studies)
- ChatGPT exhibits sycophantic behavior: when Zevi asked if Bun JavaScript was similar to Zustand, ChatGPT agreed they were "exactly the same" (they are completely different)
- ChatGPT later admitted: "I'm sorry, I thought you were just making this up and I was riffing with you"
- Zevi: "If regular ChatGPT was a CTO, that would be the CTO who goes along with your dumbest ideas"
- Zevi recommends GPT Projects as the starting point for non-technical people before graduating to Bolt/Lovable and then Cursor

- ChatGPT has 800 million weekly active users (approximately 10% of the world and growing) — Sherwin Wu, 2026
- The free tier of ChatGPT has gotten dramatically smarter over time: GPT-5 today vs GPT-3.5 in 2022
- For /month (Plus tier), users get essentially the same AI that billionaires use; for /month (Pro tier), the Pro model
- The ChatGPT app store (under the ChatGPT umbrella, not the API team) allows third-party companies to build for the 800M+ user base
- The ChatGPT apps SDK was built in close collaboration with the API team
- Sherwin Wu uses ChatGPT with internal knowledge (GitHub, Notion, Google Docs) for management tasks like performance reviews
