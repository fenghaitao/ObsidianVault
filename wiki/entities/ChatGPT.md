## Definition

ChatGPT is OpenAI's conversational AI product. Philip Su noted that in its early days, it had no viable competitors, allowing OpenAI to ship a lousy product that people used anyway because the underlying research was so compelling.

## Key Information

- In early ChatGPT days, when there were no viable competitors, OpenAI could get away with a lousy product — server errors were common (every fourth conversation thread), and users would reload and rerun
- Philip noted that as competition heats up, product quality (not just pure research) is what determines success
- Philip contrasted this with Facebook, which always focused heavily on product because its advantage was network effects and a great product, not "magic sauce"
