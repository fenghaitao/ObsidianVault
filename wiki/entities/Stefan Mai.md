## Definition

Stefan Mai is a former Amazon and Meta senior manager (M2) who grew from a self-serve advertising engineer to leading a 30-40 person org, and now works on Hello Interview.

## Key Information

- Joined Amazon in 2012 in early advertising and experienced its "death march" grind first-hand
- Transitioned to management "prematurely" and spent ~18 months fixing his early soft-skills gaps
- Survived roughly seven managers in 3.5 years at Meta before his M2 promotion
- Conducted 500+ interviews across big tech and now helps candidates through Hello Interview (stefan@hello.com)
- Left big tech to start a business after realizing he'd been deferring it for more than a decade
