## Definition

Casa (getcasa.com) is a home services app that provides a household assistant. They take stock of every paint color, appliance, and light bulb in your home, and handle repairs, maintenance, package pickups, and donation collection. Jessica Fain is a loyal user and Lenny Rachitsky has also tweeted about them.

## Key Information

- Home services app: "What if everyone had a household assistant?"
- Takes stock of every paint color, appliance, and light bulb in your home
- When something breaks or needs repair, you message them and they handle it (e.g., sending light bulbs from Amazon, finding dishwasher replacements)
- Picks up packages every Friday and donations
- Does filter rotation — replaces air purifier filters, etc. every six months
- Provides handyman credits every month for tasks you've been putting off
- Currently only in the Bay Area, expanding to LA
- Domain: getcasa.com
- Jessica Fain: "They're just the nicest people"
- Lenny Rachitsky has also tweeted about being a Casa user
