## Definition

Household-management service [[Jessica Fain]] recommends in the episode's lightning round (getcasa.com): catalogs a home's appliances/paint colors/light bulbs and handles repairs, replacement ordering, package pickup, and recurring maintenance (filter rotation, etc.) via a dedicated household-assistant-style service. Based in the Bay Area, expanding to LA at the time of the episode.
