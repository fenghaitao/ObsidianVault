## Definition
Michael Aaron is a Software Engineer at Kaggle within Google DeepMind, working primarily on evaluations and benchmarks. He has been at Google for about a third of his life and at Kaggle for about half of his Google tenure.

## Key Information
- Software Engineer at Kaggle, focused on evaluations and benchmarks
- Presented at the AI Engineering conference on Game Arena and the Benchmark platform alongside Nicholas Kang
- Deep-dove into Game Arena's engineering pipeline: game design, prompt fairness iteration, OpenSpiel harness, Bradley-Terry pairwise scheduling, Elo scoring, and community engagement
- Identified key challenges: cost of running hundreds of thousands of games for statistical significance, keeping the community engaged as watching LLMs play each other gets repetitive, and maintaining fair comparisons as models and endpoints change over time
- Openly invited the community to contribute ideas on Game Arena challenges at the DeepMind booth and via email
