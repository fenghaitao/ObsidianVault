## Definition

Peter is the developer of OpenClaw (formerly Clawbot/moldbot), an open-source AI agent framework. He is a power user of Codex and is described as being in close touch with the Codex team, providing great feedback. OpenClaw has become the fastest growing open source project in history.

## Key Information

- Developer of OpenClaw (formerly called Clawbot, then moldbot)
- Power user of Codex (OpenAI's coding agent)
- In close touch with the Codex team, gives them great feedback
- Sherwin Wu: "not surprised that he uses it"
- Peter feels he can almost blindly trust Codex's output: "anytime it does things he just trusts that it has done the right job and he's just like almost certain he could just commit it to master and it'll be great"
- Sherwin notes that Peter is extremely barraged by asks, emails, DMs, and PRs — comparable to the OpenAI team after the ChatGPT launch
- Published a book recently where AI agents talk to each other, described by Sherwin as "Her happening in real life"
- Upcoming guest on Lenny's Podcast
- Jensen Huang called OpenClaw "the new computer" and noted it's the fastest growing open source project in history, surpassing Linux
- OpenClaw has been described as "personal by default" — designed for single-user trust relationships, not group chats
- The maintainers have done significant work to harden OpenClaw against prompt injection and security risks
- Claire Vo: "Peter and the maintainers have been very clear about this is open source, this is an experiment, this is not for everyone, this is yours to kind of grow and build and own"
- Peter cares about mental health and people's actual lives, not just metrics
- The onboarding experience is praised as model design: "Who am I? Who are you?" — agents conduct an interview to build their identity
- OpenClaw's soul file (identity.md) is pre-seeded with excellent concepts like "be helpful, have opinions, be resourceful before asking" and "remember you are a guest"
