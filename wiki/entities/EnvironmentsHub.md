## Definition
The Environments Hub is a community space for sharing reinforcement learning environments for LLMs, tightly integrated with the Verifiers library. It serves as a marketplace and discovery platform for RL environments.

## Key Information
- Community-driven space for sharing RL environments
- Tightly integrated with Verifiers — both are "different faces of the same intent"
- Aims to fight environment fragmentation in the LLM ecosystem
- Ensures open-source models have access to quality training playgrounds
- Provides discoverability for what others are building in the RL environments space
