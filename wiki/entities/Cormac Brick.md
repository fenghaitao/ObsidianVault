## Definition
Cormac Brick is the tech lead for Google AI Edge, focusing on bringing AI models to edge devices. He previously worked at Intel where he led the architecture for the NPU used in modern laptops, and has been working on edge AI since 2016.

## Key Information
- Tech lead at Google AI Edge, working on on-device and edge AI deployment
- Previously at Intel, led architecture for the NPU in modern laptops
- Started edge AI work in 2016 running GoogleNet on a USB key hardware accelerator plugged into a Raspberry Pi at NeurIPS 2016
- Has been focusing on edge AI for approximately 10 years
- Works closely with the Gemma team at Google DeepMind
- Presented on tiny LLMs (TLMs) and agent skills on edge devices at AIE Code Summit 2026
- Covered LiteRT-LM, Gemma 4 on-device models, agent skills architecture, and tiny model fine-tuning workflows
