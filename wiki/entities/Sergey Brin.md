## Definition

Sergey Brin is a co-founder of [[Google]]. [[Molly Graham]], an early Google employee, cites him (with [[Larry Page]]) as one of the founder-CEOs whose personality she says defined ~80% of the company's culture — see "[[Founder Personality Defines Culture]]."

## Key Information

- Graham describes Google under Brin and Page as feeling like "two PhD students' paradise" — a campus-like environment where ideas mattered more than what shipped, in contrast to the "19-year-old hacker's dorm room" feel of [[Facebook]] under [[Mark Zuckerberg]].
