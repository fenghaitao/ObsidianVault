## Definition
Klarna is a Swedish fintech company providing buy-now-pay-later services. It is cited as an example of a disruptor that moves at machine speed, redefining industry games while traditional enterprises take 6+ months to make equivalent decisions.

## Key Information
- Referenced alongside Octopus and Shein as a company that thinks traditional enterprise decision speed is "insane"
- Represents companies that study the industry, craft playbooks, and then go on to redefine the games themselves
- Contrasted with enterprises where "a decision you take in an afternoon could easily take an enterprise 6 months or more"
- Known for aggressive AI adoption including replacing significant portions of its workforce with AI tools
- Example of a company operating at machine speed rather than human pace
