## Definition

"You Can Negotiate Anything" is a book by [[Herb Cohen]] that teaches negotiation through simple, everyday examples rather than complex corporate scenarios. Jacob Warwick recommends it as an accessible introduction to negotiation.

## Key Information

- Author: Herb Cohen
- Published in the 1980s
- Jacob Warwick notes it's "a little dated" and "doesn't suit all audiences" but values its simplicity
- Uses everyday examples like negotiating for dishwashers rather than high-stakes corporate deals
- Jacob: "It's not complicated. It's like here's an apple and here's an orange and here's the concept of negotiation really spelled out simple"
