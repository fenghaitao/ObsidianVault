## Definition
The Incubator for AI is a team within the UK Department for Science and Technology (DST) that incubates new AI solutions for usage across the public sector. It is essentially a spin-out of the Number 10 Data Science fellowship program, with most of its original technical team being 10DS fellows.

## Key Information
- Sits within the Department for Science and Technology (DST)
- Incubates new AI solutions for the UK public sector
- Original founding technical team were mostly [[Number10DataScience]] fellows
- Collaborates with 10DS on scaling up incubated work
- Produced the [[ExtractAITool]], a collaboration with [[GoogleDeepMind|DeepMind]] built on Gemini
- Extract digitizes planning application processes, including hand-drawn maps, and is being rolled out to every local authority in England
