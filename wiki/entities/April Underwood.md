## Definition

April Underwood is a product leader who served as CPO of Slack. She was Jessica Fain's mentor and the first CPO Jessica served as Chief of Staff for. She is known for her strategic thinking and her approach to product leadership.

## Key Information

- Former CPO of Slack
- Jessica Fain pitched her to be her Chief of Staff while eight months pregnant, during a two-day overlap
- Taught Jessica the whiteboard technique for product strategy meetings — holding the whiteboard marker while extracting ideas from Stuart Butterfield
- Her approach: marrying the leader's instincts with the ability to package ideas in a translatable, actionable framework
- Previously a podcast guest on Lenny's Podcast
