## Definition

Jeanne DeWitt Grosser is the COO at Vercel, where she oversees marketing, sales, customer success, revenue operations, and field engineering. Previously, she was Chief Product Officer at Stripe, where she built the company's early sales team from the ground up, and earlier worked at Google (joining the Gmail team in 2004).

## Key Information

- Currently COO at Vercel, overseeing all customer-facing and revenue-generating functions (marketing, sales, technical sales, customer success, support, partnerships).
- At Stripe, served as CPO and built the first sales team from scratch; spent nine years at the company.
- At Google, worked on Gmail starting in 2004 (joined June 1, two months after Gmail's April 1 launch).
- Known for building sales organizations that engineers and product teams respect — her litmus test: "If I put you in front of 10 engineers at our company, it should take them 10 minutes to figure out you aren't a product manager."
- Believes the best GTM orgs are "equal parts revenue driving and R&D" — translating customer feedback into product roadmap signal.
- Advocates for treating GTM like a product, designing unique customer buying journeys.
- Pioneered the GTM engineer (GTMEN) role at Vercel, building AI agents for sales automation.
- Former competitive diver in college; credits the sport with teaching resilience and obsession with excellence.
- Life motto: "When the going gets tough, the tough get going" / "Where there's a will, there's a way."
