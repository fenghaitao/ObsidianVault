## Definition
DORA (DevOps Research and Assessment) is a research group that publishes benchmarks and metrics for software delivery performance, including research on the impact of AI adoption in engineering.

## Key Information
- Published research on AI adoption impact showing modest but positive-leaning industry averages: 7.5% increase in documentation quality, 3.4% increase in code quality with 25% increase in AI adoption
- Uses Bayesian posterior distributions to represent confidence in initiative impacts
- Identified clear AI policies and time to learn as top initiatives that move the needle most
- Industry benchmark for change failure rate is approximately 4%
- DORA metrics and SPACE framework inspired DX's measurement approach
