## Definition

METR (transcribed in the source audio as "I meter" — likely an auto-transcription artifact for "METR") is the AI evaluation organization referenced by [[Dan Shipper]] for its benchmark measuring how long a task an AI model can complete autonomously at a given accuracy threshold.

## Key Information

- Dan Shipper cites METR's benchmark showing the newest/largest Anthropic model at the time (referred to in the episode as "[[Mythos]]") could complete tasks autonomously for roughly 17 hours at 50% accuracy — used by Dan as evidence that raw model-capability progress is real and exponential, even while he argues this doesn't translate directly into full automation of human jobs.
- Framed as the kind of benchmark that can make an "AI job apocalypse" narrative look inevitable, which Dan Shipper explicitly pushes back on elsewhere in the episode (see [[AI Job Apocalypse]]).
