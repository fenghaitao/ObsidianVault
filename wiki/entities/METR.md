## Definition
METR (Model Evaluation and Threat Research) is an independent research nonprofit that seeks to inform the public, policymakers, and AI labs about the degree to which AIs might pose catastrophic risks to society.

## Key Information
- The "model evaluation" part seeks to understand AI capabilities and propensities
- The "threat research" part connects those capabilities and propensities to potential catastrophic risks
- Known for the time horizon chart widely shared on Twitter showing exponential growth in AI autonomous capabilities
- Conducts both benchmark-style evaluations (HCAST, SWAR, RE-Bench) and economic-style field experiments (RCTs with developers)
- Puts significant effort into making agents as performant as possible given underlying models, "churning through a load of AI tokens"
- Hiring research engineers, research scientists (both academic and scrappy startup backgrounds), and director of operations
- Website: metr.org/careers
- Ran an unpublished greenfield hackathon randomizing AI access; found only ~4 percentile point improvement with AI allowed, with enormous overlap between groups
- Exploring monitored vs. unmonitored capabilities as a way to extend the useful range of time horizon measurements
- Investigating in-the-wild transcripts and fuzzy-goal environments as alternative evidence sources for capability measurement
