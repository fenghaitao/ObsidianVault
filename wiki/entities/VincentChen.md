## Definition
Vincent Chen is a research fellow and co-founder at [[SnorkelAI]], a frontier AI data lab. He works at the intersection of academic research and enterprise deployment, with a focus on building benchmarks that define and advance AI capabilities.

## Key Information
- Co-founder and research fellow at Snorkel AI
- Works with both Snorkel's internal research teams and academic collaborators at Stanford, UW (University of Washington), and Wisconsin
- Helps run the [[OpenBenchmarksGrant]], Snorkel's $3M commitment to fund open benchmarks
- Has reviewed over 120 benchmark applications from academia and industry labs
- Presented at AIE (aiDotEngineer) conference on the framework for building effective agent benchmarks
