## Definition
Chris Lovejoy is a medical doctor turned AI builder and the founder of Notius Labs. Trained at the [[University of Cambridge]] and worked in the [[NHS]] before moving into AI in 2018. He was the first technical employee at [[Anterior]], worked with [[Tandem]] (the UK's largest clinical AI product provider), and developed the Oracle-Evaluator-Architect framework for incorporating domain expertise into AI organizations.

## Key Information
- **Background**: Medical doctor, trained at University of Cambridge, worked in NHS for several years
- **AI career**: Moved into AI in 2018, trading and building models
- **Key roles**: First employee at Anterior (prior authorization startup); worked with Tandem (clinical AI)
- **Framework**: Created the Oracle-Evaluator-Architect framework for domain expertise in AI organizations
- **Speaking**: Presented at AI Engineer conference in San Francisco; talk reached ~100,000 viewers
- **Thesis**: The system for incorporating domain insights is more important than model/pipeline sophistication
- **Company**: Founder of Notius Labs
