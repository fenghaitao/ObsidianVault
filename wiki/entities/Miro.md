## Definition

Miro is an online collaborative whiteboard platform used for brainstorming, diagramming, and design collaboration. It is listed as an export connector destination for [[ClaudeDesign]].

## Key Information

- Listed as one of the connector destinations for Claude Design, allowing users to send their designs directly to Miro boards.
