## Definition

StrongDM is cited by [[ColeMedin]] as a company reportedly running a real, production [[DarkFactory]] — a fully autonomous spec-in/shipped-code-out pipeline with no human review step — even in the banking industry, where shipped code has to be correct.

## Key Information

- One of the few companies Cole names as documenting a working dark factory in production; he notes most other reports at this autonomy level are rumor-level rather than documented.
