## Definition

Clayton Christensen was a Harvard Business School professor and author of "The Innovator's Dilemma" and "The Innovator's Solution," which Jeetu Patel calls "the Bible in tech."

## Key Information

- Author of "The Innovator's Dilemma" and "The Innovator's Solution"
- Jeetu Patel calls these books "the Bible in tech" and recommends reading them every few years
- The books explore why successful companies fail when faced with disruptive innovation and how to avoid that trap
