## Definition

GarageBand is a music creation software application developed by Apple. It was used as an example in the panel of a low-visibility product that makes promotion difficult.

## Key Information

- Ryan L. Peterman used GarageBand as an example of a product at Apple where getting promoted to senior+ levels is unlikely because "no one cares about GarageBand"
- The counterpoint was that even on GarageBand, you could find cross-team impact by improving real-time audio in the OS
- Used to illustrate the heuristic that team and product choice significantly affects promotion potential
