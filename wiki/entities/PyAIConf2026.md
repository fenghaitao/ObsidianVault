## Definition

The PyAI Conference 2026 was the first conference organized by Pydantic, focused on the intersection of Python and AI. Featured talks from Guido van Rossum, Samuel Colvin, Sebastián Ramírez, Armin Ronacher, Pamela Fox, Hamza Tahir, and many others.

## Key Information

- Organized by Pydantic
- Featured an "Open Source in the age of AI" panel with Guido van Rossum, Samuel Colvin, Sebastián Ramírez, and Jeremiah Lowin
- Talks covered: Monty, MCP tool schemas, developer tools for agents, multi-agent orchestration, FastAPI updates, agent infrastructure, AI research failures, and more
- Talks were recorded and published on the Pydantic YouTube channel
