## Definition

ElevenLabs (rendered "11 Labs" in the source transcript's ASR) is an AI voice-technology company cited by [[Jason Lemkin]] as an example of a hyper-growth AI-native company running an old-school GTM play (an in-person roadshow, podcast appearances) precisely because those plays still generate high ROI at current demand levels.

## Key Information

- Lemkin cites ElevenLabs' active in-person roadshow and podcast circuit ("why are they on Lenny's podcast? It works") as proof that traditional GTM "plays" (events, roadshows, podcasts) still work in 2026 — it's the old "playbooks" (blanket, high-volume application of these plays) that are broken, not the plays themselves. See [[Plays Vs Playbooks]].
- Cited statistic: roughly 50% of ElevenLabs' sales now go through enterprise channels, used by Lemkin as evidence that even the newest AI-native companies inevitably build out traditional enterprise sales motions once they scale — "everyone eventually goes enterprise."
