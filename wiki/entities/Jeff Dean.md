## Definition

Jeff Dean is a Google engineer whom Mike Stonebraker credits, through the Spanner project, with Google finally abandoning eventual consistency and MapReduce.

## Key Information

- Stonebraker: "Jeff Dean finally figured that out, and when they did Spanner, Spanner had a conventional transactional system," after which Google "completely abandoned eventual consistency and completely abandoned map reduce."
