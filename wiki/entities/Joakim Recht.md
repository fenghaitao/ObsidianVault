## Definition

Joakim Recht is a Distinguished Engineer who was one of the few at Uber (among thousands of engineers). He built Odin, Uber's platform for operating stateful workloads, and later left for an AI enterprise-automation startup.

## Key Information

- Grew to Distinguished Engineer at Uber, one of two Distinguished Engineers grown in his Denmark office (out of ~70 people, among ~4,500 engineers company-wide)
- Built Odin, which went from containerizing Schemaless MySQL to operating all of Uber's stateful workloads (~120,000 servers, ~half a million databases) with ~20 people
- Promotions followed Odin's scope expansion: first version → promotion, expansion to standard MySQL/Cassandra → principal, hand-off of the mature platform → distinguished
- Strongly believes "a software engineer needs to write code" at every level; wrote code daily through Distinguished
- Advocates "running code beats perfect code" and "leading by example" (taking the shovel work, delegating the hard-but-growth work down)
- Preferred mentoring people outside his own org to plant seeds and get two-way benefit; kept mentoring informal and unscheduled
- Left Uber after a management change and a top-down cloud-migration decision (based on price "no engineer believed") triggered a drain of senior engineers
- Joined an AI enterprise-automation startup; considered Databricks but didn't want to rebuild the same infrastructure again
