## Definition
Jacob Steinhardt is a professor whose post framing research as a Markov decision process (MDP) is Vlad Feinberg's preferred lens for research projects.
## Key Information
- Vlad calls the post "really excellent" and says he loves to frame his research work this way.
- The framing models a research project as a stochastic dependency graph of milestones; in research, transitions may be unreliable and some nodes hidden (a hidden MDP), unlike the deterministic DAG of software engineering.
- Traversing an MDP well requires estimating, a priori, each approach's success rate and time cost — which is what people colloquially call "research taste."
