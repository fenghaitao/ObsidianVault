## Definition

Sherwin Wu is the head of engineering for OpenAI's API and developer platform. He has a unique broad view into the AI startup ecosystem, as essentially every AI startup integrates with OpenAI's APIs.

## Key Information

- Head of engineering for OpenAI's API and developer platform
- Previously worked at OpenDoor, where he led the home pricing model team (building algorithms to determine how much to pay for houses)
- First job out of college was at Quora, where he owned the newsfeed code and was the primary reviewer for newsfeed PRs
- Reports that 95% of OpenAI engineers use Codex daily, and 100% of PRs are reviewed by Codex
- Engineers who use Codex more open 70% more PRs, and the gap is widening
- Describes the current era as the most exciting in his career, and predicts the next 2-3 years will continue this
- Personally uses Codex for all his coding as an engineering manager
- Uses ChatGPT with internal knowledge (GitHub, Notion, Google Docs) for management tasks like performance reviews
- Believes managers will be able to manage much larger teams with AI tools, similar to how engineers manage 20-30 Codex agents
- Management philosophy: spend more than 50% of time with top performers, treat them like surgeons, unblock them before they ask
- Is "chronically online" on X and company Slack to stay on top of AI developments
- Advice for builders: "Build for where the models are going and not where they are today"
- Advice for companies deploying AI: need both top-down buy-in AND bottom-up adoption with an internal AI tiger team
- Believes business process automation is a massively underrated AI opportunity outside of Silicon Valley
- Views OpenAI fundamentally as an ecosystem platform company — the API was the first product
- Predicts multi-hour coherent AI tasks and multimodal audio as the next 12-18 month frontiers
- Life motto: "Never feel sorry for yourself" — always maintain a sense of agency
- Twitter/X handle: @SherwinWu
- Loves hearing about what people are building; encourages founders to reach out on X
- Favorite book: "There Is No Antimemetics Division" by qntm (fiction), "Breakneck" by Dan Wang (non-fiction)
- Big anime fan, recommends Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3
- Favorite product: Ubiquiti home networking and security cameras
- Previously worked at OpenDoor building home pricing models; learned that power lines, floor plans, and curb appeal are surprisingly important real estate variables
