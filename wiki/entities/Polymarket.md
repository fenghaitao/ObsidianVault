## Definition

Polymarket is a decentralized prediction market platform built on blockchain technology that allows users to trade on the outcomes of real-world events. It serves as a benchmark for evaluating the forecasting accuracy of AI-driven simulation models.

## Key Information

- Tracked by [[SimFrancisco]]'s synthetic population model as a forecasting accuracy benchmark during the [[ClaudeOpus4.8]] Build Day hackathon (June 2026).
- [[SimFrancisco]]'s 10,000-resident synthetic electorate tracks prediction markets like Polymarket within a couple of percentage points.
- Operates as a decentralized information markets platform, using blockchain-based smart contracts for trade settlement.
- Allows users to trade on event outcomes including elections, sports, crypto events, and cultural phenomena.
- Used alongside [[Kalshi]] as a real-world validation signal for [[SyntheticPopulation|synthetic population]] forecasting models.
