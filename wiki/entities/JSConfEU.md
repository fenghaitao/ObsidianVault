## Definition

JSConfEU was a web development conference in Berlin, run by Malte Ubl for 10 years until its final event in the summer of 2019. It ended just before COVID-19 would have forced its cancellation.

## Key Information

- Location: Berlin, Germany
- Organizer: Malte Ubl (now CTO of Vercel)
- Duration: 10 years
- Final event: Summer 2019
- Ended with "impeccable timing" — would not have been possible one year later due to COVID-19
- Malte hoped someone else would take the reins, but COVID-19 prevented that
- Malte sees AI engineering conferences as the legitimate successor to web development conferences for the tech community
