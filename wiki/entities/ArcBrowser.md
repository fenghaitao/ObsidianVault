## Definition

Arc is a web browser shipped in 2022 by The Browser Company of New York, focused on making the internet more personal, organized, and delightful. It was later succeeded by Dia, the company's AI-native browser.

## Key Information

- Shipped in 2022 by The Browser Company of New York
- Designed to be more personal, organized, and crafted than existing browsers
- Loved by millions of users
- Described by the company as an "incremental improvement" over browsers of its time
- Succeeded by Dia in 2025, which takes an AI-native approach
