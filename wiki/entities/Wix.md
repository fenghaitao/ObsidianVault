## Definition

Wix is a cloud-based web development platform that allows users to create websites through drag-and-drop tools. It is listed as an export connector destination for [[ClaudeDesign]].

## Key Information

- Listed as one of the connector destinations for Claude Design, allowing users to send their designs directly to Wix for website building.
