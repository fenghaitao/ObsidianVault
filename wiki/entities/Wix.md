## Definition

Website-building company where [[Zevi Arnovitz]] worked as a PM (via its student program, then on the core "editor" product team) before joining [[Meta]].

## Key Information

- Site of Zevi's [[10x Learner Vs 10x Doer|Failure Corner story]]: he bombed his first product review on Wix's editor team, then learned the expectation on a junior PM was to be a fast learner, not an immediately polished operator.
- Zevi describes seeing an increasing number of Wix job candidates, toward the end of his time there, arriving having already built their own products with AI tools — a data point he cites for how quickly non-technical building has spread.
