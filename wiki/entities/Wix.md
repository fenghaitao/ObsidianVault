## Definition

Wix is a website building platform where Zevi Arnovitz previously worked as a Product Manager in the editor team (the core product).

## Key Information

- Zevi started at Wix through their student program and was placed in the editor team
- The editor team had four other PMs who were among the best at Wix, with much more experience than Zevi
- Zevi's first product review at Wix was a failure — he tried to impress rather than learn
- Key mentors at Wix: Ner (best product sense), Oya (methodology/frameworks expert), Yara (head of product, system thinking)
- Zevi's Wix experience taught him to be a "10x learner" rather than trying to be a "10x PM"
- By the end of his time at Wix, Zevi was interviewing candidates and noticed more people building their own stuff with AI
