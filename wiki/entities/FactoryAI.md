## Definition
Factory AI is a company building autonomous software engineering tools, including a coding agent called Droid. Their mission is to bring autonomy to software engineering. Mario Zechner called FactoryDroid the "Lamborghini" of coding agent harnesses — at the frontier, really good, with a fantastic team.

## Key Information
- Founded approximately 2.5 years before December 2025 (mid-2023)
- Mission: bring autonomy to software engineering
- Product: Droid, a coding agent with specification mode and plan mode
- Helps organizations assess and improve their codebase readiness for AI agents
- Provides ROI analytics for organizations adopting AI coding tools
- Believes the path to 5-7x productivity gains is through organizational validation investment, not just better models
- Mario Zechner: "Amp and FactoryDroid, the Porsche and Lamborghini of coding agent harnesses. So, if you can afford them, please use them. They're at the frontier. They're really good and the teams are fantastic."
