## Definition
Asaf Bord is a professional at Northwestern Mutual who led the research and development of GenBI (Gen AI + Business Intelligence) at the company. He presented at the aiDotEngineer conference about building GenBI in a risk-averse Fortune 100 enterprise.

## Key Information
- Works at Northwestern Mutual, a 160-year-old financial services company
- Led the GenBI research initiative, starting from a "pie in the sky" concept
- Advocated for using real messy data rather than synthesized data to ensure production readiness
- Designed an incremental, phased approach with six-week sprints and tangible deliverables at each stage
- Emphasized building trust with both users and leadership through transparent progress and early value delivery
- Described the multi-agent architecture: metadata agent, RAG agent, SQL agent, and BI agent
- Noted that the RAG agent alone automated ~80% of report-finding work for a 10-person BI team
- Presented thoughts on the future of SaaS pricing in the Gen AI era, shifting from per-seat to usage-based models
