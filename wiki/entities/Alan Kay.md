## Definition

Alan Kay is the computer scientist who developed Smalltalk on the West Coast at the same time Barbara Liskov worked on CLU — a parallel, largely unconnected stream of object-oriented research.

## Key Information

- Developed Smalltalk while Liskov was working on Clu; two independent research streams existed with no internet (only ARPANET), so they weren't talking to each other.
- Smalltalk has inheritance (classes and subclasses), while Clu has independent "clusters" without inheritance.
- Liskov read the Smalltalk-based literature for her 1986 OOPSLA keynote, which produced the Liskov substitution principle.
