## Definition
Microsoft Teams is Microsoft's messaging and collaboration platform, mentioned as an alternative to Slack for integrating AI agents with human-in-the-loop review workflows.

## Key Information

- Mentioned alongside Slack and Google Chat as messaging platforms for agent integration
- Can serve as the chat interface for human-in-the-loop review in n8n workflows
- Less commonly used than Slack in the AI engineering community ("Hopefully, no one uses Teams")
