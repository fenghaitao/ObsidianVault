## Definition

Jeff Weinstein is a product manager at Stripe known for thinking about the entire business, not just his own product area. He previously worked with Jessica Fain at Box. Lenny describes him as the "epitome" of thinking on behalf of the entire business.

## Key Information

- PM at Stripe
- Previously worked with Jessica Fain at Box
- Known for constantly tweeting and sharing about Stripe as a business — not just his own product area
- Described by Lenny as the "epitome" of thinking like a CPO: thinking about the company broadly and then connecting his specific work to company goals
- Described as "charismatic and able to really put himself in other people's shoes"
