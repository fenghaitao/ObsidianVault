Acquired is a business history podcast hosted by Ben and David. Boris Cherny recommends it, especially the Nintendo episode, as a favorite podcast besides Lenny's Podcast.

## Key Information

- Business history podcast hosted by Ben and David
- Boris: "The way that they get into business history and bring it alive is really, really good"
- Boris recommends starting with the Nintendo episode
- One of Boris's favorite podcasts
