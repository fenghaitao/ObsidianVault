## Definition
Tiger Beetle is a company building a high-performance transactional database, which James Cowling names as one of the "few really great companies" influenced by his Viewstamped Replication Revisited paper.
## Key Information
- Cowling cites Tiger Beetle as an example of the paper's lasting influence beyond academia.
- The company's deterministic transaction-processing focus aligns with the consensus/replication lineage (Paxos, Raft, Viewstamped Replication, Virtual Synchrony) that Cowling describes as "all basically the same thing."
