## Definition

Health insurance company founded by [[Todd Park]], cited by [[Eric Ries]] in *Incorruptible* as an example of a simple, clear, purpose statement ("treat every customer the way you would your own parents").

## Key Information

- Its internal instruction to employees — treat every customer the way you would your own parents — is cited by Ries as a model of how a purpose statement should read: simple, easy to understand, and immediately actionable, in contrast to vague consulting-style mission language.
- Founder Todd Park is separately cited as the source of the "[[Culture Bank]]" concept (the "Todd Park rule": only make deposits, never intentionally make withdrawals from an organization's trust), which he in turn learned from Howard Schultz at Starbucks.
