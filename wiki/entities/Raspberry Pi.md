## Definition

Raspberry Pi is a series of small single-board computers. Jia Chen used a Raspberry Pi Pico in her first winning hackathon project at Ohio Hackathon.

## Key Information

- Jia used a Raspberry Pi Pico with various modules to build a distance-measuring device for her first hackathon win at Ohio Hackathon (Hacks)
- The device functioned as an extension of a walking cane for people with visual disabilities, measuring distance between the Pi and objects in front of it
- The project extended the effective range from 10 feet to 20 feet, detecting distance wherever the user pointed their arm
