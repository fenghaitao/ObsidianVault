## Definition
Google Assistant is Google's voice assistant. Kitze used it with Google Home and IFTTT to voice-log todos around the house — saying "tell my assistant to [task]" would split the command and store it via IFTTT.

## Key Information
- Used by Kitze with Google Home for voice-based todo logging
- Combined with IFTTT to split commands: "tell my assistant to" → IFTTT takes second half
- "It wasn't smart. It wasn't AI, but I was building towards something where I can offload my thoughts"
- Part of Kitze's early productivity system evolution
