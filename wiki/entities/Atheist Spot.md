## Definition

Atheist Spot (atheistspot.com) was an early website and side project by Lenny Rachitsky, described as "Reddit for atheist news." It was a community site aggregating news and discussion about atheism.

## Key Information

- Run by Lenny during college when he was a "very big atheist" (he still identifies as a Jewish atheist)
- Monetized through Google AdWords, but all the ads were for religious dating sites (Christian Mingle, etc.) because most articles were about religion
- Lenny attended atheist conventions
- He has since mellowed: "I don't care. Whatever. Believe it or don't believe."
