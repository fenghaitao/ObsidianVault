## Definition

Mark Zuckerberg (Zuck) is the founder and CEO of Meta (formerly Facebook). Philip Su observed his deliberate personal growth and self-improvement during his tenure at the company.

## Key Information

- Philip Su was impressed by Zuckerberg's ability and willingness to personally grow over time — those who worked with him for many years could trace exactly how he improved
- Zuckerberg was open to coaching and made deliberate efforts to improve — a small concrete example: someone coached him on how to hold his head when speaking publicly (he initially tilted his head slightly upward, and over years deliberately corrected to face the crowd straight on)
- His communication at AMAs greatly improved over Philip's first two years at Facebook
- Philip noted that Zuckerberg felt genuine in how he led teams
- When Philip joined Facebook at age 33, the median age was 27, making him the oldest person in the Seattle office for seven months; most people he worked for (including Zuck) were younger than him
- In 2025, Zuckerberg predicted AI would be able to produce mid-level engineer code later that year
- In 2009, Zuckerberg paired with Michael Novati on a hackathon to build emoji reactions (any emoji on any post) — the idea later launched properly as Reactions
- Michael once made a public bet with Zuckerberg during the 2010 lockdown that he wouldn't commit code; Zuckerberg actually merged a PR, so Michael had to stop ripsticking
- When Michael messaged Zuckerberg directly about a controversial internal post, Zuckerberg was "totally fine with it" and encouraged him to keep posting
