## Definition

Mark Zuckerberg is the founder and CEO of Meta (formerly Facebook). Sam Lessin uses him as an example of a famous person where the right etiquette is to not be sycophantic but also not pretend you don't know who they are.

- Sherwin Wu references the iPhone as an example of democratization: "you're using the same iPhone that Mark Zuckerberg's probably using" — the same applies to AI
