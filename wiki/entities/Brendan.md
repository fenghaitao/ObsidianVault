## Definition

Data scientist at [[Anthropic]] who independently figured out how to install and use a terminal, Node.js, and [[Claude Code]] to do SQL analysis — an early, surprising signal of [[Latent Demand]] for non-coding uses of Claude Code that [[Boris Cherny]] cites as a precursor to [[Claude Cowork]].

## Key Information

- Cherny recalls being shocked to see Brendan (a non-engineer) using a terminal at all, since it's normally considered an intimidatingly low-level engineering tool; within a week, the rest of the data science team followed Brendan's example.
