## Definition

Cory Doctorow (transcribed in this source, garbled, as "Cy Corey Doctr") is a writer credited by [[Lenny Rachitsky]] as the originator of the "[[Centaur And Reverse Centaur|centaur and reverse-centaur]]" framing for human/AI control dynamics, discussed in the [[Adam Mosseri]] interview.

## Key Information

- Coined (per Lenny Rachitsky's citation in this episode) the centaur/reverse-centaur metaphor: a "centaur" is a human torso on a horse's body (human in charge of the machine/tool); a "reverse-centaur" is a horse's head on a human body (the machine/AI in charge, with the human just "doing its bidding") — used as a cautionary framework for AI-augmented work.
