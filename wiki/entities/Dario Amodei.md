## Definition

Referenced in the episode as having stated that roughly 90-100% of Anthropic's code is written by Claude, with humans monitoring/watching it.

## Key Information

- Fadell recalls Dario Amodei saying that 90-100% of all of Anthropic's code is written by their own Claude models, with engineers monitoring the output.
- This claim is contrasted with the episode's account of Anthropic's/Claude's leaked source code, which experienced software architects reportedly found brittle and poorly layered — used by Fadell as a cautionary example about "cognitive surrender" to AI-generated code.
- Per [[Eric Ries]] (Incorruptible): Dario was a first-time founder when he and [[Daniela Amodei]] left [[OpenAI]] to start [[Anthropic]], before the generative-AI/ChatGPT boom — not a hot company by the standards of the time, and none of the top venture funds joined the round; the investors who did back Anthropic were "effective altruism"-style true believers in its AI-safety mission.
- Ries was brought in (via an early investor introduction) to advise Dario and Daniela on governance before they incorporated; they wrote [[Public Benefit Corporation]] status and the right to later establish a mission-oversight trust into Anthropic's charter from day one, later standing up the **[[Long-Term Benefit Trust]]** at the Series C.
- Does not hold founder-control dual-class shares the way Mark Zuckerberg or Larry Page/Sergey Brin do; Ries frames Anthropic's trust-based structure as more institutional, and in his view more durable, than founder control.
- Cited as the leader under whom Anthropic has refused to release models it judges too dangerous and turned down a reported $200 million Pentagon contract — actions Ries attributes to the protection the Long-Term Benefit Trust provides against investor pressure to prioritize growth over safety.
- Per [[Benedict Evans]] (episode 07): used as Evans's example of "argument from authority" reasoning he distrusts — Evans says he's interested in Dario's opinions on where AI models are headed technically over the next 6-12 months, but not particularly interested in his opinions on labor economics/comparative advantage just because he runs an AI lab; Evans also states he doesn't believe the cynical view that Dario's public job-apocalypse commentary is merely "pumping" Anthropic's stock.
