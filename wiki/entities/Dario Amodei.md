## Definition

Dario Amodei is the CEO and co-founder of Anthropic. He was one of the first authors on the seminal scaling laws paper and has been a driving force behind Anthropic's focus on AI safety, coding, and exponential thinking.

## Key Information

- CEO and co-founder of Anthropic
- One of the first four authors on the scaling laws paper
- Has a "notebook channel" on Slack where employees are encouraged to publicly disagree with him
- Pushed the aggressive case scenario during 2025 revenue planning — people were "freaking out" about hitting the targets, but Dario thought they could go "much higher" — and they did
- Has been "laser focused" on the importance of coding to accelerate the research loop for years
- Dario and Daniela "really believe in" the culture heavily — Amol says they've invested a lot in it
- At an all-hands, someone disagreed with something Dario said, then went into his notebook channel to challenge him publicly, sparking a big debate — this is encouraged
