## Definition

Nobel laureate physiologist; co-founder (with wife [[Marie Krogh]]) of the Nordisk Insulin Laboratorium, predecessor to [[Novo Nordisk]].

## Key Information

- Learned of a new Canadian technology to isolate insulin during a 1920s North American lecture tour with Marie; the couple negotiated to commercialize it in Denmark.
- Incorporated the resulting company as a for-profit owned and governed by a nonprofit foundation — an "industrial foundation" structure, decades ahead of when this kind of concern became a mainstream business topic. See [[Spiritual Holding Company]].
