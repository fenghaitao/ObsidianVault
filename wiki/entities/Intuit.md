## Definition

Intuit is the company behind TurboTax, which built an AI financial assistant powered by Claude that generates clear, accurate tax explanations for millions of customers.

## Key Information

- The TurboTax AI assistant achieved higher customer ratings than non-Claude experiences in the previous tax season.
- Intuit Credit Karma and Intuit TurboTax were added as Claude connectors on April 23, 2026, part of a broader expansion bringing everyday-life apps (alongside work tools) into Claude conversations. See [[Integrations]].
