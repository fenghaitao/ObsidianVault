## Definition

Shivam Verma is the Tech Lead of the User Representations team in Spotify's AI Foundation org. He presented at aiDotEngineer on how Spotify approaches personalization in the era of LLMs.

## Key Information

- Tech Lead, User Representations team, Spotify AI Foundation org
- His team builds user embeddings that serve as the foundation for all downstream recommendation models at Spotify
- Previously worked as a machine learning engineer at Twitter
- Based in London
- Presented Spotify's three-pillar approach to LLM-era personalization: user modeling, catalog understanding via semantic IDs, and steerable generative recommender systems with soft tokenization
