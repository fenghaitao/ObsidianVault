## Definition

Roald Dahl was a beloved children's author known for his irreverent style and wild imagination. Rachel Lockett reads his books (Witches, Matilda) to her children (ages 5 and 7).

## Key Information

- British children's author known for his irreverent, imaginative style
- Rachel Lockett's children (ages 5 and 7) love his books
- Books mentioned: Witches, Matilda
- Wes Anderson has adapted some of his stories into films
- Rachel notes: "He's a character from what I hear about his life"
