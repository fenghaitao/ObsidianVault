## Definition

Block is a financial services company cited for internal AI agent adoption, reaching 4,000 of 10,000 employees across 15 job profiles.

## Key Information

- Internal AI agent reached 4,000 active users out of 10,000 employees across 15 different job profiles (sales, design, product, customer success, operations).
- Adoption doubled in one month, with weekly user engagement increasing 40–50%.
- Framed as an example of enhancement rather than replacement — the same emphasis Anthropic recommends for internal change management.
