## Definition

RCA Laboratories (the David Sarnoff Research Laboratory, transcribed "David Sorov") was RCA's research arm in Princeton, New Jersey, where Judea Pearl worked in the computer research group on emerging memory technologies.

## Key Information

- Early computer research investigated replacement phenomena for magnetic core memories, including photochromic, semiconductor, and superconducting approaches.
- Pearl was in a group designing superconducting memories (16-by-16-bit plates) and investigated permanent currents in thin superconducting films, discovering what became the "Pearl vortex."
- The semiconductor memory direction, which his group doubted, ultimately won out over superconducting memories.
