## Summary

Founder and former CEO of Amazon. Known for instilling Amazon's Leadership Principles, particularly Customer Obsession. Emphasized that long-term profits and customer satisfaction are the same thing. His philosophy of writing culture (six-page documents, reading at the start of meetings) shaped Amazon's internal communication practices.

## Key Details

- Advocated that "long-term profits and customers are the same thing"
- Under his leadership, Customer Obsession was the highest priority from interns to VPs
- Established the writing culture at Amazon, including the six-page document format and reading meetings
- During his tenure, nobody gave lip service to the customer experience—it was the highest priority
