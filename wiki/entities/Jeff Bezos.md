## Definition

Amazon founder, cited by [[Mark Pincus]] as the source of two management principles: staying "[[Close To The Metal]]" and "the number one job of a CEO is to be right." Also cited by [[Tony Fadell]] as sharing his belief that repeated failure and iteration, not one-shot success, is how you find the right product.

## Key Information

- Pincus says he "stole" the line "the number one job of a CEO is to be right" from Bezos — prioritizing picking the right product/strategy over being a great operator, communicator, or manager.
- Cited (with Steve Jobs and Zuck) as an example of a founder who would go work deeply, hands-on, with a team for stretches of time on the thing that mattered most.
- [[Andy Jassy]] and the rest of Amazon's C-suite reportedly all passed through the "tech assistant" role under Bezos — cited as evidence for the "[[Teaching Hospital Model]]" of mentorship.
- Per Tony Fadell: shares his view that iteration through failure is how products succeed — "You got to fail a few times till you get to find your way," and it's only failure if you stop, otherwise it's learning.
- Associated with Amazon's "Working Backwards" / press-release-first product methodology, which Fadell compares to his own "write the press release before you start the project" advice in *Build*.
