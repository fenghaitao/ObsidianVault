## Definition

Jeff Bezos is the founder of Amazon, cited by Stewart Butterfield for two key concepts: "divine discontent" and "your margin is my opportunity."

## Key Information

- "Divine discontent" — the idea that customer standards continually rise, so the bar for quality is always moving up
- "Your margin is my opportunity" — Butterfield reinterpreted this as "tilting your umbrella is our opportunity," meaning small acts of courtesy and empathy create competitive advantage
