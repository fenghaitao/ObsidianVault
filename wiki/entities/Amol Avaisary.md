## Definition

Amol Avaisary is the Head of Growth at Anthropic, leading the growth team during the company's unprecedented expansion from 1B to 19B+ ARR in 14 months. He previously led growth at Mercury and MasterClass, and was a founder and investment banker before moving into product.

## Key Information

- Head of Growth at Anthropic, leading a team of roughly 40 people across engineers, designers, PMs, and data
- Got the job by cold-emailing Mike Krieger (CPO) — the only PM Mike hired from a cold email
- Previously led growth at Mercury and MasterClass
- Was a founder and investment banker before moving into product; almost became an account executive instead of going into product
- At Mercury, his team spent a full quarter fixing onboarding quality ("forget metrics") — became the single most impactful quarter of his career at that point
- Describes the Anthropic growth role as: 70% "success disasters" (firefighting things that break because growth is too fast), 30% proactive bread-and-butter growth work
- Leading the CACHE initiative (Claude Accelerates Sustainable Hypergrowth) — using Claude to automate growth experimentation
- Avowedly averse to PRDs — 60-80% of what his team ships has no PRD, just Slack messages
- Uses Claude/Co-work extensively for daily automations: data monitoring, life admin (reimbursements via Brex, inbox clearing), manager feedback, self-coaching, and misalignment detection
- Core growth philosophy: "Be comfortable leaving money on the table" — forgo metric impact to prioritize safety, brand, quality, and user experience

### Personal Background
- Suffered a traumatic brain injury in early 2022 from a kick to the head during Muay Thai sparring
- Spent 9 months off work; couldn't walk, look at screens, or listen to music for more than 20 seconds for the first 2-3 months
- Wife did everything for him including texting his friends
- Returned to work after 8 months, then got reinjured a month into joining Mercury (off another 2-3 months)
- Still not 100% healed — has occasional dizziness and headaches
- "One of the best things that could have happened to me" — forced healthy habits: no alcohol, no caffeine, regular breaks, meditation
- Does a meditation retreat at Spirit Rock at least once a year
- Wrote about his brain injury in a guest post for Lenny's Newsletter in 2023
- Formerly did Muay Thai for many years
- Big Wolverine (Michigan football) and 49ers fan
- Australian; says "she'll be right" as a life motto
- Shut down his mental health startup after 3 years — raised a couple million, had 7-10 employees, and had to tell investors they lost their money
- Life motto: "Sometimes in life you just have to go for it" (from Eros Resmini, former CMO of Discord)
