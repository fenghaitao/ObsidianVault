## Definition

PostHog is a product analytics platform for understanding how users actually use an application — used for debugging, testing, and rolling out changes. It sponsored [[ColeMedin]]'s video on [[Google]]'s Open Knowledge Format, and Cole reports using it himself in [[Archon]].

## Key Information

- **Install-with-AI onboarding**: a single command/wizard sets up analytics for an application in minutes.
- **Custom dashboards**: Cole uses PostHog dashboards to monitor daily usage of [[Archon]], down to individual run-level detail.
- Positioned by Cole as essential once something is in production — "you can't be flying blind."
