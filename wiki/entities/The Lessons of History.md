## Definition

Book by [[Will Durant]] (referred to as "Lessons from History" by "Alan Durant" in the transcript), named by [[Sam Lesson]] as "probably the most approachable non-obvious book I love" and his single strongest book recommendation in the episode's lightning round.

## Key Information

- Lesson: "I would really really recommend it. It's a short read." He describes it as offering an exceptional ratio of "hours of investment to intellectual return."
- [[Lenny Rachitsky]] mentions he listened to it on Audible and that it's short enough to finish "in a couple hours."
