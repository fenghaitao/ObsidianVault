## Definition

Otter is a software product of Atoms that processes restaurant orders. Brian Attwell cites it as part of Atoms' food-cost thesis.

## Key Information

- Processes 18% of the world's orders and makes them "a bit more efficient"
- Has hundreds of thousands of restaurants using it
- One of the individual products Atoms markets while keeping the core company stealthy
