## Definition
Adrian Bertagnoli (also transcribed as Adrian Berczynski) is a founding engineer at Callosum, previously at Colossyan. He researches and builds infrastructure for heterogeneous intelligence -- the paradigm where different model architectures, sizes, and hardware work together in multi-agent systems.

## Key Information
- Founding engineer at [[Callosum]], building heterogeneous intelligence infrastructure
- Previously worked at [[Colossyan]]
- Presented at aiDotEngineer on scaling heterogeneous intelligence as the next paradigm beyond homogeneous scaling
- Co-developed **heterogeneous recursion**, extending recursive language models with multi-model/multi-chip mapping for long-context tasks
- Led work on heterogeneous multi-modal web navigation, beating GPT-5.2 and Gemini 2.5 on Video Web Arena
- Working with [[Aria UK Institute]] on a £3M grant for the UK's first heterogeneous collocated cluster
