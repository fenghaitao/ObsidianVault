## Definition

Tenex is a company co-founded by [[AlexLieberman]] (also co-founder of Morning Brew). Alex Lieberman cited Claude Design as a core part of his tech stack for designing Tenex's site, brand assets, and presentations.

## Key Information

- Co-founded by Alex Lieberman, who also co-founded Morning Brew.
- Alex Lieberman uses [[ClaudeDesign]] for design directions for Tenex's site, new brand assets, and presentations.
- Lieberman praised Claude Design's "approachable UX with strong taste & design instinct" and the seamless handoff between Claude Design and [[ClaudeCode]].
