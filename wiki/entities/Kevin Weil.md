## Definition

Kevin Weil is the product leader who was James Everingham's product partner at Instagram and joined him on Meta's Novi/Libra crypto project.

## Key Information

- James's product partner at Instagram ("I remember telling my product partner Kevin Weil at the time, 'Hey, I think I'm going to go do this.'").
- When James explained the crypto mission to him, Kevin came back the next day with "I think I got to go do this, too."
- He was part of the team that went over to start the Libra project at Meta.
