## Definition

Kevin Weil (transcribed in this source as "Kevin Whale") is the former Chief Product Officer (CPO) at [[OpenAI]] and a prior guest on [[Lenny's Podcast|Lenny's Podcast]]. (Note: episode 39 refers to him as OpenAI's CPO in the present tense; the two sources' framing of his tenure status wasn't reconciled at time of ingest.)

## Key Information

- Referenced by [[Lenny Rachitsky]] for a widely quoted line from his own podcast appearance: "This is the worst the model will ever be" — used in the [[Adam Mosseri]] episode as a touchstone for the idea that AI model capability only improves from any given point forward.
- Per episode 39: praised [[Alexander Imbiricos]] (Codex product lead) as "simply the best."
- Per episode 40 (Edwin Chen): [[Lenny Rachitsky]] recalls asking Weil, alongside [[Mike Krieger]] (Anthropic's CPO), how much longer dedicated product teams will be needed given AI models' growing capability.
