## Definition

Kevin Weil is the Chief Product Officer (CPO) at OpenAI.

## Key Information

- CPO at OpenAI
- Described Alexander Imbiricos as "simply the best"

- Coined the phrase "this is the worst the models will ever be" — a reminder that today's models are the least capable they'll ever be, especially for software engineering
- Sherwin Wu frequently references this quote as a north star: the models will only get better, and trust in them will grow over time
- Kevin Weil was a previous guest on Lenny's Podcast where he said this exact line
- Connected Lenny Rachitsky with Jeetu Patel — described the work Jeetu has done to turn Cisco from "an older, slower, more traditional enterprise to a very AI forward company"
- Was a guest at Cisco's 12-hour AI summit alongside Jensen Huang, Sam Altman, Marc Andreessen, and other tech leaders
- Had a great point on the podcast that talking is a beautiful way to handle every level of intelligence — it scales well across the spectrum and works for 200+ IQ as well as lower IQ
