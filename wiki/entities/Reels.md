## Definition

Reels is [[Instagram]]'s short-video feature/product, Instagram's response to the short-form video format popularized by [[TikTok]].

## Key Information

- The first version of Reels (built ~2019) was built on top of [[Stories]] rather than as its own standalone surface — a decision [[Adam Mosseri]] calls one of his biggest mistakes during his Instagram tenure.
- Because Stories had a relatively low read-through rate and more Stories content than people had time to consume, most Reels posted into Stories were never seen and then disappeared.
- Mosseri believes that if Instagram had launched the version of Reels it eventually shipped (around summer 2020) a year earlier (summer 2019), [[TikTok]] would likely be smaller today than it became — TikTok's explosive growth coincided with the 2020 pandemic, when people had more free time and were more willing to use sound-on video.
- Since then, Instagram has continued to invest heavily in video, creative tools, ranking, and recommendations, which Mosseri says has driven most of Instagram's growth in the years since.
