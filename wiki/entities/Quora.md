## Definition

Quora is a question-and-answer platform. Around 2017, it was considered a highly desirable company to work for, particularly at UCLA.

## Key Information

- Ryan Peterman recalled that Quora was "one company that was absolutely S-tier" around 2017 at UCLA
- Ryan noted that Quora is no longer considered as prestigious as it was then
- Evan King, who attended Cornell, had not heard much about Quora and suggested it might have been more of a "California thing"
