## Definition

Hello Interview is a mock interview and interview preparation platform co-founded by Evan King and Stephan. It provides in-person mock interviews with current senior engineers and managers from target companies, AI-guided learning resources, and free educational content (including a YouTube channel focused on system design interviews).

## Key Information

- Founded by Evan King and Stephan (Evan's former manager at Meta) after two previous startup acquisitions
- Initially attempted to build AI mock interviews but the quality wasn't sufficient; pivoted to in-person mock interviews after customers repeatedly asked to pay for them
- Coaches are carefully vetted — if any coach drops below the quality bar, they part ways and find a replacement
- Offers free resources that are considered "some of the best free resources that exist on the internet" for interview preparation
- Has a YouTube channel where Evan breaks down common system design problems
- The company is still run by only 2 full-time employees (Evan and Stephan), leveraging AI tools like Claude to accelerate development
- Ryan Peterman's roommate independently used and highly recommended Hello Interview's free resources and mock interviews, crediting them as critical for his interview success
- Competes with services like Interviewing.io in the mock interview space
- Ryan's buddy used Hello Interview's free system-design resources as the single most impactful prep tool when applying to AI labs (and got an Anthropic offer)
- Hello Interview partnered with Austin McDonald to provide behavioral-interview resources on its site, and sponsored this episode ([[summary-20260316 - Meta Hiring Lead： Avoid Downleveling, Liars, Passing OpenAI And Anthropic Interviews]])
