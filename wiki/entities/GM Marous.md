## Definition

GM Marous introduced the concept of "exposure time" or "exposure hours" — the idea that being exposed to great stuff (great user experiences, onboarding flows, websites) helps develop taste and judgment.

## Key Information

- Introduced the concept of "exposure time" on a previous Lenny's Podcast episode
- The idea: deliberately expose yourself to great work to develop your taste and judgment
- Referenced by Lazar as a key practice for developing quality standards
