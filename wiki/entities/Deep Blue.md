## Definition
Deep Blue was an IBM chess computer that in 1997 became the first chess engine to defeat a reigning world champion, Garry Kasparov. It was a Type A (brute force search) engine in Claude Shannon's classification.

## Key Information
- 1997 match against Garry Kasparov marked the first time a computer beat the best human chess player
- Represented the triumph of Type A engines (brute force search through all possible moves), which became the dominant approach as computers grew more powerful
- After Deep Blue's victory, Type B (selective/intuitive) engines were largely ignored until DeepMind's AlphaGo and AlphaZero revived interest in neural network approaches
- The Play Magnus team referenced Deep Blue as a historical milestone in the intertwined history of chess and AI
