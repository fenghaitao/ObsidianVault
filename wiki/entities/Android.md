## Definition

Android is Google's mobile operating system. Rahul Pandey deliberately pivoted his career from recommendation systems to Android development at Pinterest, and later worked as an Android engineer at Meta on the Portal device.

## Key Information

- Rahul chose Android at Pinterest because he wanted to be closer to the product and build things end-to-end
- One of his criteria was wanting to build side projects on his own — his whole family was on Android, so he could show them apps he built
- Android was strategically important to Pinterest in 2015 because international growth was a big initiative, and Android is dominant outside the US
- Both Pinterest and Meta used Java for Android development (no Kotlin at the time)
- At Meta, Rahul worked on a forked version of Android for the Portal hardware device, with 30 custom Android apps sitting on top
- The Portal Android build system was very different from standard Android, using Buck and custom tooling
- Rahul published Android tutorials on YouTube starting in 2019, which helped give him signal to leave Meta and start a company
