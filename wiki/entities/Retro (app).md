## Definition

Retro is a small community photo sharing app where users can only share photos from a given week (as opposed to all time). It has no ads, no like counts, and none of the typical social media engagement mechanics. It is known for its beautifully crafted design.

## Key Information

- Users can only share photos from the current week, not from their entire photo library
- Has no ads, no visible like counts — stripped of standard social media features
- Jenny Wen has been using it for 2 years since it launched
- After 2 years of use, users can look back at each week from previous years, creating a "really special way to live through each week"
- Known as a beautifully crafted app; Jenny recommends it for building taste in design
- "Designers love Retro" — Jenny Wen
