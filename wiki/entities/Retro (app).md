## Definition

Small community photo-sharing app [[Jenny Wen]] cites as her favorite recently-appreciated product (used it for two years) — shares photos only from the current week (not all-time), with no like/comment counts or ads.

## Key Information

- Wen values it for creating a retrospective, weekly diary-like way to look back on her life over time, and for its craft as a beautifully designed app.
- Distinct from the Palmer-Luckey-founded startup's Game-Boy-style retro handheld device mentioned in episode 12 — this is an unrelated photo-sharing app.
