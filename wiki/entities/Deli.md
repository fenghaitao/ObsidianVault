## Definition

Deli (also known as Delphi) is a Sequoia-backed AI platform that creates digital clones — AI agents trained on a person's entire body of content (interviews, writing, podcasts) that can converse in their voice and style. It was SaaStr's first AI agent in production and the catalyst for their broader AI GTM transformation.

## Key Information

- Sequoia-backed; Brian Halligan (HubSpot founder/chairman) worked with them early while at Sequoia
- Used by Lenny Rachitsky for LennyBot (lennybot.com), which ingested every interview and piece of content he's ever produced
- Used by Jason Lemkin for "Digital Jason," which ingested 10,000+ pieces of content and 12 years of material
- Lemkin broke Deli initially because he had too much data to ingest — took about a week to get going
- Digital Jason started doing support autonomously (answering event questions, refunds, logistics) and then closed a $70K sponsorship deal on its own — the catalyst for SaaStr's AI transformation
- People spend hours a day interacting with Digital Jason, asking about sales woes, hiring decisions, and uploading LinkedIn profiles for advice
- Key learning: a general-purpose horizontal agent (not trained for sales) could close deals, proving the concept for specialized sales agents
- Required about an hour of daily training/review in the early days to fix mistakes (wrong dates, hallucinations); now well-trained and autonomous
