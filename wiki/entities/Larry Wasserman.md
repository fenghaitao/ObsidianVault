## Definition

Larry Wasserman is a statistician and author whom John Myles White calls "one of the four people I love, love, love more than anybody," recommending everything he has written for learning statistics.

## Key Information

- White has never met him but considers his books "the embodiment of hyper intense honesty."
- "If you want to learn statistics, I don't think any book has ever been better than the books he's written."
- Wasserman tells you exactly what he assumes and what is implied, and is clear about what he does not claim.
