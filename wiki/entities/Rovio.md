## Definition

Game studio that made 45 different, largely unrelated games before landing its hit, [[Angry Birds]], cited as the "pure innovation" (non-Proven-Better-New) path to product success.

## Key Information

- Described by [[Mark Pincus]] as "wildcat drilling" — high-variance, low-odds innovation, as opposed to the lower-variance [[Proven Better New]] approach taken by companies like [[OMG Pop]].
