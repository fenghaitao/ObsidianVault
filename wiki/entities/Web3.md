## Definition

Web3 refers to a vision of a decentralized internet built on blockchain technology. Evan King and Stephan founded their first startup in the Web3 space.

## Key Information

- At the time Evan and Stephan started their company, Web3 was being promoted as "the next iteration of the internet — a decentralized better internet"
- Evan was "the crypto guy" at Meta due to his exposure to crypto through Emin Gun Sirer at Cornell
- Evan's first startup was a "social intelligence layer" combining Web2 activities (mainly Twitter) with on-chain (Web3) data to do rankings and predictions
- The company grew to ~100,000 users (a significant portion of the active Web3 space at the time) and raised money
- Interest in acquisition came, but the market took a downturn (Bitcoin and crypto prices dropped), which affected the outcome
- Evan noted the irony that at the time of the interview (January 2025), Bitcoin and crypto were "back up to the moon"
- The Web3 startup was eventually sold/acquired
