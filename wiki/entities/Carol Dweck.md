## Definition

Carol Dweck is a psychologist and author of the book "Mindset," in which she coined the term "growth mindset." Zevi Arnovitz cites her book as life-changing and foundational to his philosophy of being a "10x learner."

## Key Information

- Author of "Mindset: The New Psychology of Success"
- Coined the term "growth mindset" — the belief that abilities can be developed through dedication and hard work
- Zevi: "That book completely changed my life. I was always with a fixed mindset and then after reading that I kind of understood that it was something holding me back."
- Zevi describes the book as appearing self-help but being "completely psychological and based on research"
