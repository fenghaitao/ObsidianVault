## Definition

Marc Andreessen is a co-founder of Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) and former co-founder of Netscape. He was quoted by Lenny Rachitsky as saying: "AI makes good people better and it makes great people exceptional."

## Key Information

- Co-founder of Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)
- Co-founder of Netscape
- Quoted: "AI makes good people better and it makes great people exceptional"
- This quote was referenced in the context of Sherwin Wu's management philosophy of spending more time with top performers
- Investor in [[Applied Intuition]] and mentor to [[Qasar Younis]]
- Quote-tweeted Qasar Younis's first tweet: "This is the best AI CEO nobody knows. Follow for the free alpha."
- Urged Qasar to go online and join Twitter/X to propagate ideas about physical AI and societal change
- Philosophy: barbell strategy — consume today's news (X) and 10-year-old books
- Qasar describes him as a heavy influence and mentor figure
- Was a guest at Cisco's 12-hour AI summit, interviewed by Jeetu Patel — discussed how declining birth rates and AI arriving "just in time" to save humanity
- Jeetu Patel started his interview with Marc by referencing Marc's recent Lenny's Podcast appearance
