## Definition

Surge AI (surgehq.ai) is the leading AI data company that trains frontier AI models for every major AI lab. Founded by Edwin Chen in 2020, it teaches AI models what is good and bad using human data, and measures how well they are progressing.

## Key Information

- Hit over $1B in revenue in under 4 years with fewer than 100 employees
- Completely bootstrapped — never raised any VC money; profitable from day one
- Built with a deliberately small, elite team — Edwin believed 90% of people at big tech companies could be fired and the company would move faster
- Products include: SFT, RLHF, rubrics, verifiers, RL environments, and more
- Customers include every frontier AI lab (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, etc.)
- Quality is the core differentiator: Surge goes far beyond checkbox data labeling to assess deep, nuanced quality in each domain
- Gathers thousands of signals on every worker and project — keyboard strokes, review scores, code standards, and even trains models on worker outputs to measure quality
- Operates like a research lab as much as a startup, with its own internal research team and forward-deployed researchers
- Has a research team building better benchmarks and leaderboards to counter the flawed ones driving the industry
- Website: surgehq.ai; blog: surgehq.ai/blog
- Always hiring people who love data and the intersection of math, language, and computer science
