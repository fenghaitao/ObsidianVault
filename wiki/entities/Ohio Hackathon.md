## Definition

The Ohio Hackathon (called "Hacks") was a hackathon event in Ohio. Jia Chen secretly rented a car and drove there without telling her parents, winning first place in the hardware category as a college freshman.

## Key Information

- Jia attended this hackathon as a freshman in the spring semester of college
- She secretly rented a car and drove to Ohio without telling her parents
- She found a friend on Discord servers to drive with her, but competed solo
- Won first place in the hardware category with a Raspberry Pi Pico distance-measuring device for people with visual disabilities
- This was her second hackathon overall and first win
