## Definition
Patrick Debois is a pioneer of the DevOps movement (coining the term "DevOpsDays" in 2009) and is now at Tessl, working on context development lifecycle tooling. He curates content for the AI DevCon conference in London.

## Key Information
- Pioneer of the DevOps movement: in 2009, he asked "what if ops looked more like dev?" which sparked DevOpsDays and the broader DevOps movement
- Now at Tessl, a company building tooling for the Context Development Life Cycle (CDLC)
- Curates content for AI DevCon, an AI developer conference in London (June 1-2)
- Presented at the AI Engineer conference on the "Context Development Life Cycle" — applying software engineering rigor to context management
- Core thesis: context is the new code; LLMs are just the engine, context is the fuel
- Proposes the CDLC infinity loop: Generate → Test → Distribute → Observe → Adapt
- Advocates for treating context with the same engineering discipline as code: testing, packaging, versioning, security scanning, and CI/CD
