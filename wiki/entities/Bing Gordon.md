## Definition

Colleague/friend of [[Mark Pincus]] credited with the idea of adding "more dimensionality" (the "[[Bold Beat]]") to games at [[Zynga]] — taking a boring, rote mechanic (like poker) and giving it a new dimension via social features.

## Key Information

- Co-developed the idea that Zynga's most successful features were co-op play, gifting, and other socially-valuable mechanics that let players feel creative, express themselves, and connect with friends (the "invest, express, connect" loop).
- Predicted that the products/games made during this era would eventually be exhibited "in the Smithsonian" as historically significant internet treasures.
