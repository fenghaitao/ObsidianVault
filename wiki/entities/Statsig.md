## Definition

Statsig is an experimentation and feature-flagging product that John Myles White says is essentially Meta's Deltoid re-built externally, later acquired by OpenAI.

## Key Information

- White: "as far as I can tell every time I've looked at it, the Statsig product... is just Deltoid."
- In his perception almost the entire UI and functionality are the same as Deltoid.
- He does not know Statsig's current state after it was bought by OpenAI.
