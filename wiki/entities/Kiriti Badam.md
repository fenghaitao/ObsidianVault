## Definition

Kiriti Badam ("Kiti") works on [[Codex]] at [[OpenAI]] and has spent the last decade building AI/ML infrastructure, previously at [[Google]] and [[Kumo]]. Together with [[Aishwarya Naresh Reganti]] (his wife of four years), he has led or supported 50+ AI product deployments across companies including [[Amazon]], [[Databricks]], [[OpenAI]], and [[Google]], and co-teaches an AI product-building course on [[Maven]].

## Key Information

- Co-created the "[[Continuous Calibration Continuous Development]]" (CCCD) framework with Reganti, and co-articulates the episode's two foundational concepts — [[Non-Determinism In AI Products]] and the [[Agency Control Trade-off]].
- Recounts personally experiencing the "one-click agent is marketing" lesson: worked with customers who built end-to-end autonomous agents from day one, hit unmanageable hot-fixing on a multi-step customer-support workflow, and ultimately had to shut the product down — motivating CCCD's staged-agency design.
- Describes OpenAI's [[Codex]] eval philosophy at length: because Codex is built for open-ended developer customizability rather than a handful of fixed workflows, the team pairs targeted regression evals (protecting core behavior) with heavy direct customer/social-media feedback monitoring and per-engineer "vibes" testing against a shared list of hard problems for every new model release — illustrated via Codex's recently launched code-review product, where AB-testing catch rates and watching for user annoyance/opt-outs mattered more than a pre-built eval suite.
- Rejects the "eval vs. production monitoring" framing as a false dichotomy — see [[Evals Vs Production Monitoring]] — arguing each catches different classes of problems and neither alone suffices.
- Predicts 2026 will bring more proactive/background agents that plug directly into where work already happens (citing [[ChatGPT]] Pulse as an early version of this pattern) and can prompt the user back with unsolicited progress updates, rather than waiting to be asked.
- Names emails as his (joking) "overrated" pick in the lightning round; recommends the *Three-Body Problem* series by [[Cixin Liu]] as his favorite sci-fi; recently discovered the video game *[[Expedition 33]]*; names [[Raycast]] and "caffeinate" as his favorite recent productivity discoveries; life motto is Steve Jobs's "you can only connect the dots looking backwards."
- Describes Reganti as having "this really amazing knack of teaching and explaining something in a very understandable and easy to comprehend way," which he says empowers people navigating an overwhelming, fast-moving AI landscape.
