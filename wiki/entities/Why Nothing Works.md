## Definition

*Why Nothing Works* is a recent book referenced by [[Stewart Butterfield]], explaining why an increasing number of institutional "vetoes" makes it progressively harder to get anything done — in government permitting/construction as well as inside large organizations.

## Key Information

- Butterfield calls the book somewhat repetitive as writing but says its underlying content is important — connects it to his broader point that most political rhetoric falsely implies things would work fine if not for a specific villain (billionaires, immigrants, "freeloaders"), when the real explanation is usually structural complexity and accumulated veto points, not sabotage.
- Related anecdote he cites: a viral tweet thread (source untraceable, cited approximately) detailing how many years and how many separate agencies were involved just to get a single residential stop sign installed.
