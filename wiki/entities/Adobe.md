## Definition

Adobe is a multinational software company known for creative and design tools including Photoshop, Illustrator, and Creative Cloud. It is listed as an export connector destination for [[ClaudeDesign]].

## Key Information

- Listed as one of the connector destinations for Claude Design, allowing users to send their designs directly to Adobe applications.
