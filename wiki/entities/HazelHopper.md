## Definition
Hazel Hopper is a researcher who, along with Arjun Ramani, is working on a paper cataloging the full set of capabilities needed for AGI and assessing where current AI stands on each.

## Key Information
- Co-authoring a paper with Arjun Ramani on the "often small number of problems" that constitute the full capability set for AGI
- The paper aims to enumerate all capabilities expected for AGI and evaluate progress on each
- Referenced by Joel Becker in the context of whether all AI capabilities follow the same exponential trend
