## Definition
Jacob Lauritzen is the CTO of Legora, a vertical AI company building a collaborative AI workspace for law firms. He advocates for moving beyond chat interfaces for complex agent work toward high-bandwidth, domain-specific artifacts.

## Key Information
- CTO of Legora, a vertical AI company serving over 1,000 customers across more than 50 markets
- Legora has raised significant funding and is growing extremely fast — described as "maybe the fastest in history"
- Legora is hiring engineers in London
- Argues that chat is a one-dimensional, low-bandwidth interface that collapses complex agent work trees into linear conversations
- Proposes high-bandwidth artifacts (documents, tabular reviews) as the right interface for agent-human collaboration
- Introduces the Verifier's Rule (coined by Jason Warner) as a framework for understanding which tasks AI can solve
- Distinguishes between Trust (how much review is needed) and Control (how effectively humans can steer agents)
- Advocates for skills over planning: skills encode human judgment into work nodes and handle contingencies, while planning requires doing all the work upfront
- Promotes non-blocking elicitation: agents make decisions and log them, humans review and reverse later
