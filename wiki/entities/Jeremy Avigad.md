## Definition

Jeremy Avigad (transcribed "Jeremat"/"Jerem") is a mathematician and logician (Carnegie Mellon University) who was Lean's first user and influenced its design.

## Key Information

- Was Lean's first user; with a mathematics background, he would request a new feature and get a fix "the same day," which de Moura credits with attracting and growing the math community.
- Convinced de Moura not to use higher-order logic (which was much easier to implement) because it would never attract serious, Fields-Medal-level mathematicians; dependent type theory is more powerful and "beautiful" for abstract objects.
- De Moura cites him (with Terence Tao, Kevin Buzzard, and Patrick Massot) as a mathematician who insists on dependent types.
