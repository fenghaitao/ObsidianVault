## Definition

The Power Broker is a 1,100-page biography of Robert Moses by Robert Caro. It spans Moses' entire life and career, showing how someone changes over decades. Jenny Wen recommends it as an important reminder that careers are long and for understanding how someone gets things done.

## Key Information

- Written by Robert Caro; approximately 1,100 pages
- Biography of Robert Moses, a controversial urban planner who shaped New York
- Jenny Wen calls it "an incredibly aggressive recommendation" due to its length
- Jenny recommends it because: few memoirs span someone's entire life, it shows how people change over decades, it's a nuanced view of a controversial figure, and it's a reminder that careers are long
- Julie Zhuo was also seen reading it
- Despite the short attention span era, Jenny believes it's worth reading end to end
