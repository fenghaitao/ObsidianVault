## Definition
Unix is the operating system created at Bell Labs (by Ken Thompson, Dennis Ritchie, and others), where Bjarne Stroustrup built C++.

## Key Information
- Created and still being built at Bell Labs when Bjarne joined: "I was in the home where Unix was invented."
- Bjarne's original ambition was a "distributed Unix" (had he succeeded, Unix clusters might have arrived ~10 years earlier), but it was not a one-person job.
- The Unix team was small enough (five to seven people) that Bell Labs' management worried it was getting "too big" for its model of individual researchers.
- The early Unix/C programmers numbered "a few dozen" — mostly PhDs or geniuses — a very different crowd from today's ~47 million developers.
