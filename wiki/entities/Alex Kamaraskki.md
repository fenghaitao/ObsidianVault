## Definition

Alex Kamaraskki is an AI security expert and researcher known for speaking publicly about the risks of AI prompt injection and jailbreaking. He was a previous guest on Lenny's Podcast.

## Key Information

- Frequently cited by Sander Schulhoff for his assessments of AI security risks.
- Key quote: "The only reason there hasn't been a massive attack yet is how early the adoption is, not because it's secured."
- Argues that none of the current AI security problems have any meaningful mitigation.
- Believes that hoping the model "just does a good enough job" at not being tricked is fundamentally insufficient.
