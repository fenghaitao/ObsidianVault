## Definition
Lyria Real-Time is Google DeepMind's predictive music generation model that creates music continuously in real time, responding to prompt changes like a live DJ mixing tracks. Unlike diffusion-based models, it is a predictive model that streams music indefinitely until stopped.

## Key Information
- Predictive model (not diffusion-based) — generates music continuously in real time
- Accepts prompts on-the-fly and transitions to new music in approximately 2 seconds
- Sessions limited to 10 minutes
- Demonstrated via "Space DJ" interactive demo: navigate through planets where each planet represents a musical prompt, music blends as you move between them
- Potential applications: adaptive game soundtracks that change based on player location, activity (fighting, cooking, exploring), HP level, etc.
- Guillaume Vernade's personal favorite model due to its underutilized creative potential
- Can produce vocalizations (not full voices) alongside instrumental music
- Demonstrated mixing genres on the fly: post-punk, K-pop, neo-soul, chill, Australian dub
