## Definition
Finn is Intercom's AI agent for customer support, launched on the day GPT-4 was released. It resolves customer support tickets autonomously and has grown to over 8,000 customers with revenue approaching $100 million.

## Key Information
- Launched the day GPT-4 came out — claimed to be the first product released on GPT-4
- Over 8,000 customers
- Revenue approaching $100 million
- Industry-leading average resolution rates
- Used by Anthropic, Snowflake, Linear, Glean, and LaunchDarkly for their customer support
- Works for businesses of all sizes
- Now served 100% by Intercom's own model for English text-based conversations, outperforming frontier models on cost, speed, and quality
- Processes approximately 2 million resolutions per week
- Intercom also sells direct access to its suite of models
- Intercom has been building AI features since approximately 2018
- Can be interacted with via the messenger, CLI, or at ideas.finn.ai
