## Definition

Eric Antinau (also rendered "Eric Anton" in the transcript, likely the same person) is a former [[Facebook]] colleague of [[Molly Graham]], now at [[OpenAI]], described by [[Lenny Rachitsky]] as "epic" and "the least well-known but most brilliant person" in Graham's life. He suggested topics/questions for this episode, including its closing question.

## Key Information

- Prompted the episode's closing question about Graham's career pivot: after a major growth spurt at Facebook, Graham had ambitions to become a COO/CEO-level "big deal boss," then significantly pivoted toward community-building and helping leaders with their careers, turning down big C-suite offers. Antinau's own metaphor for this shift: Graham "was a dog... and became a cat," or switched from "AC current to DC current."
- Graham says Antinau is "actually better at metaphors than I am," and that she regularly reuses his metaphors.
- Credited (along with [[Sarah Caldwell]] and Ashley Murphy) with suggesting topics/questions for this episode.
