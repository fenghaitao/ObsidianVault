## Definition

Fiona was the founding manager for the Facebook Marketplace team. Boris cites her as the source of the latent demand case study: Marketplace was built because 40% of posts in Facebook groups were buying and selling.

## Key Information

- Founding manager for the Facebook Marketplace team
- Observed that 40% of posts in Facebook groups were buying and selling, leading to the creation of Marketplace
- First product was buy-and-sell groups, then full Marketplace
- Boris cites her as a key example of latent demand in product development
