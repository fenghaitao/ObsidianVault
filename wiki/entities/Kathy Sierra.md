## Definition

Kathy Sierra is cited by [[Lenny Rachitsky]] and [[Max Schoening]] as the originator of the product philosophy that a great product should make its user feel like a superhero, rather than a product simply boasting about its own features.

## Key Information

- Schoening's paraphrase of her pitch: "instead of making talking about your product and how amazing it is, it's about we will make you a superhero. Like Mario getting the little flower and having superpowers now versus look at our incredible product."
- Applied by Schoening to explain why automated code-review tools historically underperformed compared to AI coding agents: publicly "roasting" a developer's code (making them feel bad) fails, whereas tools like [[Claude Code]] and [[Codex]] let a developer publish work co-created with the agent and get "bragging rights" for how good a developer they appear to be — a superhero framing rather than a critique framing.
- Cited as informing how Notion thinks about its own AI agent's reception: users describing it as making them feel unexpectedly capable.
