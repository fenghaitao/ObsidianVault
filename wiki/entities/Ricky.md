## Definition

Ricky (online handle "finding Ricky") is a Staff Engineer at Google who reached the L6 level by age 28, having started as a new grad in 2017. He creates non-tech social media content and appeared as a guest on Ryan L. Peterman's podcast.

## Key Information

### Career Timeline at Google
- Started as a new grad (L3) in 2017 at $180K compensation
- Promoted to L4 (mid-level) in 1.5 years at ~$250K
- Promoted to L5 (senior) in another 1.5 years (L3→L5 in 3 years total) at ~$350K
- Promoted to L6 (staff) in 3 more years at ~$520K
- All promotions succeeded on the first attempt
- Worked in the ads organization the entire time (~7 years on the same team)

### Management Experience
- Became a Tech Lead Manager roughly 2 years before the interview (around 2022)
- Motivated by enjoying helping people grow and finding projects for others
- Found it awkward being a younger manager among older managers
- Wished he had waited longer before becoming a manager, preferring the lower stress of senior IC

### Personal Reflections
- Struggled with Impostor Syndrome early in his career, wondering if he was a "personality hire"
- His advice to his younger self: trust your judgment more, push boundaries earlier
- Attributes roughly 50% of success to luck (stable team, good product area match) and 50% to preparation and effort
- Emphasizes that the grass was always greener on his side of the lawn, which is why he stayed at Google
- Gave a talk at UCLA in May 2025 with Ryan about industry secrets for students, covering the engineering ladder, internship success, promotion strategies, and the importance of asking questions
- Had a college recruiting experience with Yelp where he failed a HackerRank coding challenge due to a segfault, but successfully contested it and got a second chance, moving on to the interview round
- Attended Monta Vista High School in the Bay Area, a famously competitive high school that Ryan jokes contributed to his drive
- His mother doubted he would succeed without an MBA, but later validated his staff engineer level after looking it up on Xiaohongshu
- He and Ryan are roommates; Ryan's ambition and competitive nudging ("what if we got promoted, bro?") helped motivate Ricky's accelerated career path
- At the UCLA talk, described his job in Google Ads as "making ads pretty" and noted that his work is visible even with ad blockers
- His core advice to students: ask tons of questions, even if it feels uncomfortable — it's better to ask and learn than to stay silent and fail
