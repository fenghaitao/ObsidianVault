## Definition

Conviction is a venture capital firm founded by Mike Vernal and Sarah Guo, hyperfocused on AI investments. Matt McGinness recommends them as smart investors for AI entrepreneurs.

## Key Information

- Founded by Mike Vernal and Sarah Guo
- Hyperfocused on AI investing
- McGinness initially thought this focus was narrow but now views it as the right move
- Sarah Guo co-hosts the podcast "No Priors"
- Recommended by McGinness as having deep hypotheses about AI investing formed from seeing nearly every AI deal in Silicon Valley
