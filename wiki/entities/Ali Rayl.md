## Definition

Ali Rayl is an early Slack employee who implemented the "Hey Stuart, what do you think?" initiative — a practice of sitting with CEO Stuart Butterfield for half an hour to download his expertise and understand his belief system before bringing formal designs.

## Key Information

- Early Slack employee
- Implemented "Hey Stuart, what do you think?" — a practice of informal half-hour sessions with Stuart Butterfield to understand his beliefs and past experience before formal design reviews
- This initiative was created because the team realized they were coming to Stuart with finished designs that were completely different from what he had in mind
- The approach treated the conversation as a user interview, downloading the executive's expertise early
