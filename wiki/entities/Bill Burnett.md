## Definition

Bill Burnett is the co-author of "Designing Your Life," a book that applies design thinking principles to career and life planning. Rachel Lockett recommends this book highly.

## Key Information

- Co-author of "Designing Your Life"
- Rachel Lockett lists it as one of the books she recommends most to others
- Lenny also loves the book, noting "people don't talk about that book enough"
