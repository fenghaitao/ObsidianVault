## Definition

David Singleton is the former Site Director of Google London who later became Stripe's CTO. He gave Philip Su critical advice about landing teams when Philip was building Meta's London office.

## Key Information

- David Singleton was the former Site Director of Google London before becoming Stripe's CTO
- He gave Philip Su what Philip called "the best advice" for building remote offices: Google had experimented with landing teams and concluded that the landing team should commit to a minimum of two years
- Philip contrasted this with other companies that send a landing team for only 3-6 months before leaving the site on its own, which he considered a mistake
- Philip used this advice when building Meta London, asking his 12-person landing team to commit to two years
