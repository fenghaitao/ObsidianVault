## Definition

David Singleton is the former Site Director of Google London who later became Stripe's CTO. He gave Philip Su critical advice about landing teams when Philip was building Meta's London office.

## Key Information

- David Singleton was the former Site Director of Google London before becoming Stripe's CTO
- He gave Philip Su what Philip called "the best advice" for building remote offices: Google had experimented with landing teams and concluded that the landing team should commit to a minimum of two years
- Philip contrasted this with other companies that send a landing team for only 3-6 months before leaving the site on its own, which he considered a mistake
- Philip used this advice when building Meta London, asking his 12-person landing team to commit to two years

### Career Story (Ex-Stripe CTO, 2025)
- Grew from junior engineer at Google to VP (four levels above staff), then CTO of Stripe, then co-founder of Dev Agents
- Stayed nearly 12 years at Google by re-learning every 18-24 months and doing "six different jobs," including mobile, ads, and Android
- Asked to become a product manager but was pitched the Tech Lead Manager role instead; kept one foot in code by building the first Google Translate iPhone app on 20% time
- As VP he ran Android Wear as a general manager across product, engineering, partnerships, UX, and marketing
- At Stripe he became CTO, helped push reliability past five nines, and replaced whiteboard interviews with realistic pair-programming
- Co-founded Dev Agents with early-Android-era colleagues to build a consumer platform for AI agents
