## Definition
Kyutai is a non-profit AI research lab founded approximately 2 years ago (circa 2024) with funding from philanthropists including Eric Schmidt and Xavier Niel. It focuses on open research in speech AI, producing models like Moshi (full-duplex speech-to-speech), speech-to-speech translation, and Pocket TTS. Gradium AI was spun off as the for-profit structure to commercialize the technology.

## Key Information
- Non-profit AI research lab
- Founded ~2 years ago (circa 2024)
- Funded by philanthropists including Eric Schmidt and Xavier Niel
- Focus: open research in speech AI
- Key outputs: Moshi (first full-duplex speech-to-speech model), speech-to-speech translation, Pocket TTS (CPU model)
- Gradium AI is the for-profit spin-off for production use beyond open source
- Neil Zeghidour was a co-founder
