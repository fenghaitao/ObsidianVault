## Definition
Vlad Feinberg is a Google DeepMind Distinguished Engineer (L9) and the pre-training area lead for Gemini, author of the post "How to get a job at a frontier lab."
## Key Information
- Leads pre-training for Gemini; his team delivers the Flash and Flash-Lite models used for AI Overviews, AI mode in Search, ads, YouTube, and other first-party models, and is the technical POC for the Google-Apple partnership.
- Previously did pure research at Google Brain, focused early on maximizing first-author papers at NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR before leaning into LLM work.
- His team's research breaks into three verticals: distillation, inference co-design, and new quantization methods.
- Wrote the post to counter AI "FUD" (a fear-mongering tweet he attributes to a poster named Didi) and to give constructive direction after being asked constantly how to work at DeepMind, including at a Princeton lecture.
- Open hiring invitation: candidates who complete the hand-written Scaling Book exercises and the transformer exercise, and send a video, are invited to interview (New York office); he received submissions within a week of posting.
- Garnered a "voracious demand" framing for kernel/low-level engineering across labs.
- Lists the traits he values as "intent, mathematical maturity, and grit."
- Career advice to his younger self: chase the problems people actually face in the world (even smaller or "menial" parts), and be the kind of coworker people want to see succeed — a lesson from mentor Todd Lipkin.
- Credits a very small SFT contribution to the first Bard for a Jeff Dean spot bonus, encouraged by his then-manager Rohan Aneja.
