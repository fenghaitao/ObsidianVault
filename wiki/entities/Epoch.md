## Definition
Epoch is an AI forecasting and research organization that tracks and reports on compute trends, including physical and economic constraints on AI scaling.

## Key Information
- Reports on potential compute growth constraints including physical limits (power constraints) and economic limits (dollars as a constraint)
- Their analysis suggests compute constraints may not bite through 2030 but could become significant sometime after 2030
- Referenced by Joel Becker as a source for understanding potential slowdowns in compute growth that would causally affect AI capability growth
