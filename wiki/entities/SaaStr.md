## Definition

SaaStr is the world's largest community for B2B founders, founded by Jason Lemkin. It runs major annual events (10,000+ attendees), produces content, and operates as an eight-figure revenue business with ~100 sponsors. The company has become a pioneering case study in replacing human sales teams with AI agents.

## Key Information

- Started as a blog in 2012, then meetups, then the first major event in 2015
- Annual event draws 10,000+ attendees; has run ~20 major events (12 in US, 7 in Europe) plus ~300 smaller events over the years
- Eight figures in annual revenue with ~100 sponsors
- Revenue comes from two main streams: sponsorships (~$70-80K average) and tickets ($200-$2,000)
- Replaced ~10 human GTM staff with 20 AI agents + 1.2 humans (one AE + part-time chief AI officer Amelia)
- The 10 former GTM desks are now labeled with agent names: Reply (Replit), Quali (Qualified), Arty (Artisan), and Agentforce
- Tools built for the community at saster.ai: valuation calculator, pitch deck reviewer, AI VC, and more
- Operates both saster.com and saster.ai domains
- Famous SaaStr talks include: "Everything That Breaks on the Way to One Billion" (Ben Chestnut/Mailchimp) and "Rippling Secrets to Hypergrowth" (Matt Plank/Sam Blonde)
