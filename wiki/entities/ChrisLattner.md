## Definition
Chris Lattner is co-founder of Modular and a renowned compiler engineer who created LLVM, Clang, Swift, and MLIR. He previously held engineering leadership roles at Apple, Tesla, Google, and SiFive.

## Key Information
- Co-founded [[Modular]] with other world-class experts who built TensorFlow, PyTorch, LLVM, MLIR, and XLA
- Previously spent significant time at Apple where he developed the philosophy that infrastructure "should just work"
- Key figure behind LLVM compiler infrastructure, Swift programming language, and MLIR compiler framework
- Advocates for rebuilding the AI stack from the bottom up for the GenAI era, rather than building on 15-year-old foundations like CUDA
- Believes Python developers and PyTorch users should be empowered with new tools that give them "superpowers" rather than being forced to learn entirely new systems
