## Definition
Adaptive ML is a company that builds an RL Ops (reinforcement learning operations) platform called the Adaptive Engine. The platform enables large enterprises to build, evaluate, and serve in production their own specialized large language models using reinforcement learning.

## Key Information
- **Product**: Adaptive Engine — an holistic RL Ops platform to evaluate, tune, and serve the best LLMs for business
- **Core thesis**: RL is not just another algorithm — it is the one algorithm that industrializes bringing models to production
- **Platform approach**: Abstracts the complexity of RL (e.g., PPO requires orchestrating 4 LLMs simultaneously) by exposing pre-built recipes
- **Model foundation**: Built on top of open-source models (Gemma, Mistral, Qwen, etc.)
- **Key clients**: AT&T (summarization), Manulife (agent workflows), CCS (medical supply customer support)
- **Co-founders**: Alessandro Cappelli (chief customer officer) and the core founding team that previously trained Falcon
- **Origin story**: The founding team trained Falcon (~3 years ago, one of the most widely adopted open-source models) and realized RL was the gap between open-source and proprietary frontier models
