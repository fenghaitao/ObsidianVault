## Definition

Digital Equipment Corporation (DEC) was a major early computer manufacturer whose employees included Casey Muratori's father.

## Key Information

- Casey grew up with computers at home because his father worked at DEC, which is how he learned to program as a child.
- DEC was a central company in computing history, best known for its minicomputers.
