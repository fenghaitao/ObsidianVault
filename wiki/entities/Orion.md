## Definition

Meta's most advanced AR (augmented reality) glasses product/prototype; [[Caitlin Kalinowski]] led the AR glasses hardware team behind it.

## Key Information

- Uses waveguides and micro-LEDs that Kalinowski says are "a bit ahead of their time" — not yet ready for mass production due to low yields and high cost.
- Features a 70-degree field of view, binocular — Kalinowski describes the experience as immersive in a way that's hard to convey until you try it, and considers it a genuine proof point for where AR is headed.
- Part of Kalinowski's broader view that AR glasses (with a mostly-off display you activate on demand) are a likely long-term direction, once input methods for communicating with glasses discreetly in public are solved.
- Framed as one link in a technology lineage running VR to AR to robotics/autonomy, sharing underlying spatial-computing technology (SLAM, depth sensing) with self-driving cars, drones, and robots.
