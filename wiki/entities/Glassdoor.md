## Definition

Glassdoor is a website where employees and former employees anonymously review companies and their management. Annie Pearl was formerly CPO at Glassdoor.

## Key Information

- Annie Pearl was formerly CPO at Glassdoor (as well as Calendly)
- Annie Pearl is now at Microsoft
