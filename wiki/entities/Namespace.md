## Definition
Namespace is a company building high-performance compute infrastructure designed to eclipse the traditional CI/CD paradigm. Founded by Hugo Santos, it focuses on enabling Continuous Compute for agentic software development at scale.

## Key Information
- Building high-performance compute infrastructure positioned as the successor to CI/CD
- CEO is [[HugoSantos]], former microservices lead at Google
- Works with companies at the forefront of development: [[Fall]], [[Zed]], [[Ramp]]
- Core thesis: agentic software development requires fundamentally new infrastructure, not incremental CI/CD improvements
- Focus areas include: high-speed build and test in the inner loop, stateful development environments, pre-merge queue serialization
- Emphasizes hardware-software co-design for caching as the orchestration layer
- Addresses ingress shaping, rate limiting, agentic identity, and retries at scale as infrastructure concerns
- Sees the "opportunity to merge" as a critical bottleneck that must be minimized as code generation accelerates
