## Definition

Mike Christensen is a Staff Engineer at Ably, a real-time messaging platform. He presented at the aiDotEngineer conference on why direct HTTP streaming (SSE-based) limits AI product UX quality and how durable sessions built on pub/sub provide a better architecture.

## Key Information

- **Role**: Staff Engineer at Ably
- **Presentation**: "Why Your AI UX Is Broken (and It's Not the Model's Fault)" at aiDotEngineer
- **Core argument**: Direct HTTP streaming fundamentally limits AI product experiences; decoupling agents from clients via durable sessions is the solution
- **Research basis**: Spoke with engineering teams at 40+ companies across 10 industries shipping AI agents, co-pilots, and assistants to millions of users
- **Three foundational capabilities identified**: Resilient delivery, continuity across surfaces, live control
