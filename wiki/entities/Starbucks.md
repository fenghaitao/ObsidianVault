## Definition

Coffee chain built by [[Howard Schultz]], cited by [[Eric Ries]] as the origin of the "[[Culture Bank]]" concept later passed on to and popularized by [[Todd Park]].

## Key Information

- Referenced only in passing, as the company Howard Schultz built and from which Todd Park learned the "culture bank" principle (deposits vs. withdrawals of organizational trust).
