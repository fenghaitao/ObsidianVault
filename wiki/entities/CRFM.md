## Definition
CRFM (Center for Research on Foundation Models) is a Stanford University research center that studies foundation models. It is known for building [[Helm]], a standardized modular harness for reproducible model evaluation.

## Key Information
- Based at Stanford University
- Focus on foundation model research
- Built [[Helm]] (Holistic Evaluation of Language Models)
- Pioneered standardized modular evaluation harnesses
