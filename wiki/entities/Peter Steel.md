## Definition

Peter Steel is a thought leader referenced by Lazar for his observation that AI will replace deterministic work — tasks where X input equals Y output with a clear line — but not human-dynamic work.

## Key Information

- Referenced by Lazar: "People that just do math stuff, AI is gonna come for you"
- Distinction: anything deterministic (clear input → output) gets eaten by AI; human dynamics and relationships remain
- Helps explain which skills are future-proof vs. automatable
