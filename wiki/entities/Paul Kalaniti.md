## Definition

Paul Kalaniti was an Indian-origin neurosurgeon and author who wrote the memoir When Breath Becomes Air after being diagnosed with terminal lung cancer at age 31-32. He died in 2015.

## Key Information

- Neurosurgeon diagnosed with stage IV lung cancer at 31 or 32
- Wrote [[When Breath Becomes Air]] as a memoir during his illness
- Argued against Socrates' "the unexamined life is not worth living" with his own question: "If the unexamined life is not worth living, was the unlived life worth examining?"
- His book is [[Aishwaria Raanti]]'s favorite, read during COVID
