## Definition

Demis Hassabis is the CEO and co-founder of Google DeepMind. He was mentioned on the podcast for his vision of combining image models, LLMs, and world models (Genie) for multimodal AI experiences.

## Key Information

- Leads Google DeepMind
- Has publicly stated that multimodal AI — combining image models, LLMs, and world models — will be a major direction for their work
- Genie is their world model project
- His vision aligns with predictions for 2026: multimodal experiences becoming mainstream
