## Definition

Hanson Wong is an engineer who worked with Michael Bolin on the Miles file-search index at Meta and is now on the Codex team with him at OpenAI.

## Key Information

- Contributed the clever index-maintenance ideas for Miles, including representing files seen at each commit with bits indicating presence/absence.
- Enabled fuzzy (camelCase) file matching by designing how the index was maintained and queried.
- Is described as "on the Codex team with me now" by Bolin at OpenAI.
