## Definition

Laurent is a staff engineer and engineering manager (first name only given in the episode) whose career spans Apple, Meta/Facebook, Airbnb, Instagram, and Stripe, with a specialization in developer productivity. He was also Ryan L. Peterman's mentor and helped him reach Staff.

## Key Information

- Core specialization is developer productivity — making software engineers as effective and fast as possible.
- Worked at Apple and Meta (Facebook) before joining Airbnb in 2016 as one of two test-infrastructure engineers.
- Transitioned from IC to manager at Airbnb by pitching his manager rather than waiting to be asked, then returned to being an IC after getting bored.
- Ran intern calibrations at Airbnb and led org-wide transparency sessions explaining what really happens in calibration.
- Left Airbnb for Instagram/Meta in 2020, motivated partly by the four-year stock cliff; led work to adopt Facebook reliability tooling for Instagram after a July 4th on-call experience.
- Was tech lead of build/test/tooling at Stripe, scaling the organization from ~35 to ~140 engineers.
- Introduced to situational leadership by Scott Renfro during Facebook's bootcamp program.
- Credits Radical Candor by Kim Scott as the most influential book on management in his career.
