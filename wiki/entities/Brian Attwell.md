## Definition

Brian Attwell is the CTO of Atoms (Travis Kalanick's company, referenced in the transcript as Cloud Kitchens). He reached Senior Staff at Uber in under four years after graduating, started his career at Google, and interned at Apple.

## Key Information

- Reached Senior Staff at Uber in under 4 years by outworking everyone: 9am to midnight plus weekends (~90–100 hours/week)
- On an early Uber team (mobile maintainability), he and Yi Wang built after-hours competing prototypes (RxJava vs. other concurrency options on Android/iOS) until teammates trusted their judgment, becoming de facto tech leads and top-three committers in the mobile org
- Collapsed on the street at Uber from overwork/anxiety; a separate near-death medication scare made him regret not having accomplished more, and he "worked harder than ever" after
- On management: leaders at Google, Uber, and his current company all told him to do it; he procrastinated so much coding that a direct report didn't realize Attwell was his manager
- Redesigned the company's leveling/incentive structure with the head of Picnic (and Travis Kalanick's feedback), deriving twelve skill gradients from ~1,000 examples of high performance via principal component analysis
- Believes a company's standards decay by entropy: it takes energy to raise a standard and little to lower it
- Developed a "deep-seated skepticism" for how tech companies are run from his Google experience (a non-working "nice guy" was kept rather than fired)
- Interning at Apple, an awkward first-year undergrad encounter with Steve Jobs failed to impress (and likely killed the prototype); his real regret is not being honest when he knew mid-project that a direction was wrong
- Career advice to his younger self: "be more courageous and less embarrassed"
