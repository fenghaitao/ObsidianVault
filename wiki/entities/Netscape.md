## Definition

Netscape was the pioneering web-browser company James Everingham joined pre-IPO; it lost the first browser war to Microsoft's bundled Internet Explorer.

## Key Information

- Grew from ~150 people to over 3,000 within a year; IPO'd ~16 months after founding (September 9, 1995).
- Original model sold the browser for ~$35; Microsoft bundling Internet Explorer for free "instantly killed all the revenue going to Netscape."
- Open-sourced its browser (a move suggested by Jamie Zawinski) to rally thousands of developers against Microsoft's resources; that code fed Mozilla, Firefox, and Chrome. Obstacles included stripping SSL (treated as a munition) and censoring swear words/variable names in the code.
- The IE team placed a giant "E" logo on Netscape's San Francisco lawn with a "best wishes" card — "friendly competition" between colleagues.
