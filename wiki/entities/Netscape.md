## Definition

Early web browser company displaced by [[Microsoft]]'s Internet Explorer in the 1990s browser wars, cited by [[Benedict Evans]] as a historical analogy for thinking about AI platform leverage.

## Key Information

- Microsoft used its Windows distribution advantage to force Internet Explorer adoption over Netscape — but Evans notes that winning that specific browser war ultimately "didn't matter" in the long run, because value in the industry moved further up the stack anyway.
- Used to frame Evans's open question about AI foundation models: does winning today's "browser war" (chatbot UX) actually confer durable value, or does it end up mattering as little as the 1990s browser war did for Microsoft? See [[Model Commoditization]].
