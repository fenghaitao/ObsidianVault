## Definition

Anti-gravity is Google's competitor to Cursor — an AI-powered IDE/coding environment. Zevi Arnovitz uses it with Gemini for frontend/UI work.

## Key Information

- Google's competitor to Cursor in the AI-powered IDE space
- Zevi uses it with Gemini for UI and frontend work
- Zevi describes Gemini's behavior within Anti-gravity as "terrifying" — it will delete things, make mistakes, then produce beautiful results
- Shows the thought process of the AI as it works, which can be anxiety-inducing to watch
