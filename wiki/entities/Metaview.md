## Definition

Metaview is an AI recruiting platform that provides AI agents to find candidates, take interview notes, gather insights, and identify the best candidates. Used by 11 Labs, Brex, Replit, Deel, and 5,000+ other organizations. Sponsor of Lenny's Podcast.

## Key Information

- AI recruiting platform with a suite of AI agents that behave like recruiting co-workers
- Finds candidates based on exact criteria
- Takes interview notes automatically
- Gathers insights across the hiring process
- Customers close roles 30% faster
- Used by 11 Labs, Brex, Replit, Deel, and 5,000+ other organizations
- Described as giving teams "a real unfair advantage in hiring"
- Try free at metaview.ai/lenny
