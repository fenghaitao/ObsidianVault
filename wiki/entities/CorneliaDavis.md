## Definition
Cornelia Davis is a Developer Advocate at Temporal with a career spanning over 30 years in distributed systems. She worked at Pivotal on Cloud Foundry from the early 2010s during the microservices movement and authored a book on microservices.

## Key Information
- Developer Advocate at Temporal
- Spent the bulk of her career in the distributed systems space
- Worked at Pivotal on Cloud Foundry from the early 2010s, during the movement toward microservices architectures
- Cloud Foundry was the early container technology on the market, incubated at VMware, using Linux containers, container orchestration, and eventual consistency before Docker and Kubernetes existed
- Authored a book on microservices
- Has been writing software for over 30 years
- Presents workshops and talks on building durable agents with Temporal and the OpenAI Agents SDK
