## Definition
Zapier is an automation company cited as an exemplar of AI-assisted engineering adoption, particularly for using bots and agents to accelerate developer onboarding.

## Key Information
- Built a series of bots and agents that assist with developer onboarding
- Reduced time-to-effectiveness for new engineers to 2 weeks, compared to industry benchmarks of 1-3 months
- Used AI-driven productivity gains to justify hiring more engineers rather than cutting headcount
- Cited as having the right attitude: using AI to increase competitive edge through growth, not reduction
