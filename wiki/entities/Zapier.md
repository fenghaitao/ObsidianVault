## Definition

Zapier is a no-code automation platform that connects different apps and services. Zevi Arnovitz's brother, a non-technical entrepreneur, replaced Zapier (along with AirTable) by building his own CRM and automation system entirely with AI.

## Key Information

- Zevi's brother was paying for Zapier and AirTable before replacing them
- He built a full-fledged CRM and automation system completely on his own using AI tools
- Represents the trend of non-technical people using AI to replace paid SaaS tools with custom-built solutions
