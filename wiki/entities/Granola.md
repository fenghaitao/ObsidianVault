## Definition

Granola is an AI-powered note-taking tool that Rachel Lockett uses in her coaching sessions to take notes so she can be fully present with clients. It provides synthesis of sessions and tracks insights across client engagements.

## Key Information

- Used by Rachel Lockett to take notes during coaching sessions
- Allows her to be fully present with clients instead of taking manual notes
- Provides a synthesis of what happened in sessions and next steps
- Organizes notes into folders per client, enabling cross-session pattern analysis
- Helps her look at insights across her work with each client over time
- Available as a free one-year subscription for annual subscribers of Lenny's Newsletter
- Elena Verna uses Granola for AI summaries of meetings, calling it "super helpful" and "very powerful"
