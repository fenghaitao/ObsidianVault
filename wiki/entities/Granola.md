## Definition

Granola is an AI-powered meeting notes and productivity tool. It is an MCP provider that supports [[EnterpriseManagedAuthorization|Enterprise-Managed Authorization (EMA)]] for its [[MCPConnector|MCP connector]].

## Key Information

- **EMA support at launch:** Granola was among the MCP providers supporting Enterprise-Managed Authorization when [[Anthropic]] launched the feature in June 2026.
- **MCP connector:** Granola provides an MCP connector in Claude's directory, enabling Claude to access meeting notes and productivity data.
