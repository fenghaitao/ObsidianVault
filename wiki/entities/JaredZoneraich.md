## Definition
Jared Zoneraich is the founder of PromptLayer, an AI engineering workbench company based in New York. He is a coding agent enthusiast who rebuilt his engineering organization around Claude Code and presented an analysis of how Claude Code and other coding agents work internally.

## Key Information
- Founder of PromptLayer, launched 3 years before December 2025
- Based in New York City
- Rebuilt his engineering organization around Claude Code with a rule: if you can complete something in less than an hour using Claude Code, just do it
- Spends half his time kicking off agents and half using his own product to build agents
- Big enthusiast of coding agents; dogfoods PromptLayer extensively
- Presented a talk analyzing Claude Code internals based on system prompt leaks and personal research
- Advocates for simple architecture in agent design: "give it tools and get out of the way"
- Believes in rigorous prompt engineering and involving both product and engineering teams in AI development
- Argues that domain experts (lawyers for legal AI, etc.) should be involved alongside engineers
- His company processes millions of LLM requests per day
