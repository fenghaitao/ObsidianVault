## Definition

Twitch is the live-streaming platform Amazon acquired for ~$970M, whose integration Ethan Evans led as an advisor to CEO Emmett Shear.

## Key Information

- Emmett Shear remained CEO after the acquisition, so the integration VP had a goal but no authority
- Several hundred employees at acquisition, growing to ~1,000 within a couple of years
- Was bleeding cash like a classic venture-funded company and needed to move toward profitability — a gap its leaders initially did not see as a problem
- Emmett Shear learned that not all early employees scale into their roles, and some friends had to be replaced or leveled
