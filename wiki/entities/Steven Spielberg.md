## Definition

Steven Spielberg is a legendary film director and producer. In the podcast, Jacob Warwick shares a story where a talent agent closed a deal by pre-scheduling a lunch between a hot director and Spielberg, demonstrating the "Selling the Vacation" technique.

## Key Information

- Legendary film director and producer
- Used in a negotiation story: an agent trying to sign a director who wanted to move from documentaries into film production closed the deal by scheduling a lunch with Spielberg before the meeting even happened
- The agent's pitch: "What are you doing on Friday? I cleared a lunch with your assistant. Great, I'll have you meet Steven." → "Steven who?" → "Steven Spielberg." → "Where do I sign?"
- This exemplifies the [[Selling the Vacation]] technique — making the desired future a tangible reality
