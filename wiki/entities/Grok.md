## Definition

Grok is an AI model developed by xAI, known for having a very different personality and approach to answering questions compared to other frontier models.

## Key Information

- Edwin cites Grok as an early example of model differentiation — it already has a distinctly different personality from other AI chatbots
- This is an example of how AI models will increasingly diverge based on the values and objective functions chosen by their creators
- Marc Andreessen uses Grok's "Bad Rudy" voice mode as his favorite dinner party trick — a foul-mouthed raccoon avatar in the Grok app
- "Bad Rudy" is a voice mode feature within the Grok app that Marc finds "absolutely amazing, hysterical"
