## Definition

Grok is xAI's AI assistant, now built into [[Tesla]] vehicles (accessible by holding the right steering-wheel scroll wheel), named by [[Tomer Cohen]] and [[Lenny Rachitsky]] as a recent discovery in the episode's lightning round.

## Key Information

- Lenny Rachitsky discovered it by accident in his Tesla; supports a logged-out mode and a logged-in mode that connects to the user's account.
- Tomer Cohen separately uses ChatGPT's voice mode extensively during commutes and wishes for a dedicated steering-wheel button to invoke an "AI friend" in the passenger seat; notes his wife dislikes Grok's specific personality/brand and doesn't want it used around her.
- Both note the value of a distinct assistant "personality" (versus a purely neutral information-only response style), and that voice assistants interrupting too eagerly is a common annoyance, though configurable.
- Cohen jokes that Rivian should add an equivalent in-car assistant.
