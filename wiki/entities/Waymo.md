## Definition

Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company (owned by Alphabet/Google). Edwin Chen recently took his first Waymo ride and described it as "magical" and "living in the future."

## Key Information

- Edwin took his first Waymo ride in San Francisco and found it exceeded expectations
- In San Francisco, driverless Waymo cars are extremely common — "just like all over the place"
- Edwin says Waymo "deserves the hype"
- **Waymo approach (L4):** Many sensors, lots of compute, HD maps. Functions better in geographically constrained areas
- Contrast with [[Tesla]]'s L2++ approach: fewer sensors, no HD maps, cheaper compute, available everywhere
- In 5-7 years, both L4 and L2++ technologies will be much more ubiquitous globally
