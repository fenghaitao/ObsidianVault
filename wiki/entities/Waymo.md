## Definition
Waymo (transcribed "Whimo" in this transcript) is the Alphabet-owned autonomous driving / robotaxi company.
## Key Information
- Sergey Levine calls autonomous driving's takeoff "one of the most inspiring things" in the industry.
- In San Francisco you can hail a Waymo, be driven to your destination, "and there's no driver sitting there."
- Used as a case-in-point against the "technology of the future, always in the future" (nuclear-fusion-style) criticism leveled at robotics.
- Levine argues it is "not an accident" that it landed in the mid-2020s: the puzzle pieces for large-scale ML became ready to combine with real physical systems.
- Contrasted with robotics: robotic foundation models run a much thinner software stack than traditional autonomous-vehicle stacks — partly because AV work started earlier with older technologies, and partly because driving is more safety-critical.
