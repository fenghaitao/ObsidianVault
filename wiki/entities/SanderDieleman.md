## Definition
Sander Dieleman is a research scientist at Google DeepMind with over a decade of tenure, working on the generative media team. He contributes to models like Veo and NanoBanana, and writes about the intuition behind generative models on his blog at sander.ai.

## Key Information
- Research scientist at Google DeepMind for over 10 years
- Member of the generative media team, working on Veo and NanoBanana
- PhD background; noted that academic research culture does not incentivize data curation, which is critical for large-scale generative models
- Maintains a blog at sander.ai focused on diffusion models and generative AI intuition
- Has a longer version of this talk available on YouTube
- Coined the phrase "diffusion is basically spectral auto-regression" to describe how diffusion generates images coarse-to-fine in frequency space
- Uses JAX as primary framework (having been at Google before PyTorch existed)
