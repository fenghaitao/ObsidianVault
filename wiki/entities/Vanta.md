## Definition

Vanta is a compliance and risk-management automation company, presented in this episode as a supporting sponsor of [[Lenny's Podcast]].

## Key Information

- Automates compliance across 35+ security/privacy frameworks (including SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA) and is used by 15,000+ companies, per the sponsor read, including [[Cursor]], [[Ramp]], Duolingo, Snowflake, and Atlassian.
- Positioned in the ad as helping security leaders keep up with the risk introduced by teams shipping products faster than ever thanks to AI.
- Also read as a supporting sponsor in episode 07 (Benedict Evans), with the same "15,000+ companies... Cursor, Ramp, Duolingo, Snowflake, and Atlassian" customer list and "35+ security/privacy frameworks (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA)" positioning, plus a listener discount offer ("$1,000 off Vanta").
