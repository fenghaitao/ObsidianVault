## Definition

Vanta is a trust management platform that helps companies get compliant (SOC 2, ISO 27001) and stay that way with AI automation and continuous monitoring. It also helps complete security questionnaires significantly faster.

## Key Information

- Trust management platform for compliance automation
- Helps with SOC 2, ISO 27001, and vendor risk management
- Uses AI automation and continuous monitoring
- According to an IDC study, Vanta customers slashed over $500,000 a year and are three times more productive
- Helps complete security questionnaires up to five times faster
- Sponsor of Lenny's Podcast
- Website: vanta.com/lenny
