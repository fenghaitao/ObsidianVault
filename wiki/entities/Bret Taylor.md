## Definition

Bret Taylor is a technology executive and entrepreneur, known for co-creating Google Maps, serving as CTO of Facebook, founding Quip (sold to Salesforce), and serving as CEO of Salesforce. Molly Graham worked with him on scaling Quip.

## Key Information

- Molly Graham worked with him on scaling Quip, which was later sold to Salesforce
- Graham describes his personality as deeply shaping Quip's culture, consistent with the "80% of culture is founder personality" principle
- One of the key leaders Graham lists as having learned from: "Zuck, Cheryl, Larry and Sergey, Brett Taylor"
