## Definition

Ramp is a company that uses [[ClaudeCode]] to accelerate software delivery across hundreds of services.

## Key Information

- Uses Claude Code for performance optimization work spanning a large number of services.
- Also cited as an example of using Claude Code to accelerate debugging across hundreds of services — see [[Debugging]].
- Quote: *"When we discovered Claude Code, our teams immediately recognized its potential and integrated it into our workflows."* — Austin Ray, Senior Software Engineer at Ramp.
- **Enterprise-Managed Authorization:** Ramp was cited in June 2026 as one of the organizations rolling out [[EnterpriseManagedAuthorization|Enterprise-Managed Authorization (EMA)]] for [[MCPConnector|MCP connectors]] across its teams.
