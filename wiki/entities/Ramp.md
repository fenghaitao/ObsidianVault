## Definition

Ramp is cited by [[Dan Shipper]] (alongside [[Shopify]]) as a company that now runs its own single company-wide "super agent," supporting his prediction that companies converge on one shared agent rather than many personal agents.

## Key Information

- Dan Shipper couldn't recall Ramp's agent's name during the conversation but noted the company has adopted the same "one agent for the entire company" pattern he sees emerging broadly.
- Also named as a customer of sponsor [[Vanta]] (compliance/security automation), alongside Cursor, Duolingo, Snowflake, and Atlassian.
- Per [[Keith Rabois]] (episode 15): he led its seed round (~May 2019) and preempted its Series A by September of the same year, based partly on the speed at which Ramp went from nothing to shipping physical corporate cards (a process that usually takes 9-12 months in financial services, which Ramp did in ~3). Cited as one of his key "tempo as success signal" examples, alongside [[Square]] and Fair. Ramp's board meetings reportedly open with a slide showing the exact day-count since founding (via days.ramp.com), reflecting an internal obsession with velocity.
- Cited by Rabois as one of the companies that has "basically skipped hiring senior outside people," instead growing leadership internally (e.g., promoting a chief of staff into a CMO or head-of-product role) — his "value creation vs. value preservation" hiring distinction.
