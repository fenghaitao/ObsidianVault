## Definition

Strella (referred to phonetically as "Strea"/"Stella" in the transcript) is an AI-powered customer-research platform, presented as a sponsor of this [[Lenny's Podcast]] episode.

## Key Information

- Positioned as the first platform to use AI to run and analyze in-depth user interviews automatically; its AI moderator asks real follow-up questions and surfaces patterns across hundreds of conversations within hours instead of weeks.
- Cited customers: product/design/research teams at [[Amazon]] and Duolingo, used for Figma prototype testing, concept validation, and customer-journey research.
