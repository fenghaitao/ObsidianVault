## Definition

Strella is an AI-powered customer research platform that automates in-depth user interviews. It uses AI to run and analyze interviews, enabling fast and continuous user research.

## Key Information

- First platform to use AI to run and analyze in-depth interviews automatically
- AI moderator asks real follow-up questions, probes deeper on vague answers
- Surfaces patterns across hundreds of conversations in hours, not weeks
- Used by product, design, and research teams at companies like Amazon and Duolingo
- Use cases: Figma prototype testing, concept validation, customer journey research
- Gets insights overnight instead of waiting for the next sprint
- Website: strella.io/lenny
- Sponsor of this podcast episode
