## Definition

KERNEL is a company that provides browser capabilities for [[ClaudeManagedAgents]] through its CLI, authenticated via [[AgenticVaults|vaults]], and connects agents to databases for usage tracking and customer conversation monitoring.

## Key Information

- One of the first integrations to give Managed Agents web browser capabilities alongside [[Browserbase]].
- Uses environment variables in vaults to securely connect agents to databases that track usage and customer conversations.
- Agents flag usage surges as they happen, allowing the team to confirm with customers whether the activity is intended.
- Published [integration documentation](https://www.kernel.sh/docs/integrations/claude-managed-agents) for Managed Agents.
