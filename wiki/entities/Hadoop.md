## Definition

Hadoop is the open-source reimplementation of Google's MapReduce white paper, which Google had no involvement in and got no credit for.

## Key Information

- Hadoop reimplemented the MapReduce white paper: similar, but not the same ("anytime you re-implement something, it's not the same").
- It anchored the "big data revolution" at the time Kubernetes was being conceived.
- Brendan Burns used it as the key argument for open-sourcing Kubernetes: if Google only writes papers, other people reimplement them and Google loses the driver's seat.

- Mike Stonebraker: Hadoop is "ridiculously inefficient"; a distributed database system beats "the heck out of" it (the 2011 paper), and Google later abandoned MapReduce entirely.
