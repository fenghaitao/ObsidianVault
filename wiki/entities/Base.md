## Definition

Base is the largest Ethereum Layer 2 (L2) blockchain, created as a global community by Coinbase. It is a community of creators and developers.

## Key Information

- The largest Ethereum L2 in the world
- Created by Coinbase as a global community for creators and developers
- Run by a former frontline engineering manager at Coinbase who was a client of Rachel Lockett for 10 years
- The leader had a tattoo of a sun with a redwood grove reminding him of his core strengths and purpose
- Represents the fulfillment of a long-term vision of creating a global movement
