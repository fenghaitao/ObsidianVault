## Definition

Evan Spiegel is the co-founder and CEO of Snapchat.

## Key Information

- Holds "a really high bar for performance" down to the pixel level, which the guest compares to Steve Jobs — in product reviews he points out pixel-level issues and asks for fixes
- The guest learned to build "a ready demo, not a normal demo" for Spiegel's reviews
- Cares deeply about personal connections, especially with engineers — he keeps his promises and appreciates the value of engineering
- Reportedly told a leader that "no matter what happens, you should never fire the first 15 engineers," because they are the founding members
- Bought back the original "blue house" in Venice Beach and began hosting board meetings there, signaling his care for personal relationships, which the guest links to Snapchat's mission of enabling intimate connection
