## Definition
SWAR is a suite of atomic problems used by METR for evaluating AI capabilities, ranging from trivial tasks (e.g., "which file contains the passwords") to more complex challenges.

## Key Information
- One of three task distributions used in METR's time horizon measurements
- Contains problems that "maybe GPT-2 can do, maybe it can't" — spanning a wide difficulty range
- Used alongside HCAST (software tasks requiring autonomy) and RE-Bench (challenging ML research tasks)
- Part of the benchmark-style evidence that shows rapid exponential progress in AI capabilities
