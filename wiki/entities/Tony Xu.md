## Definition

Tony Xu is the co-founder and CEO of DoorDash. Keith Rabois cites him as an example of excellence in ruthless referencing — doing 20 references on every single senior hire.

## Key Information

- Co-founder and CEO of [[DoorDash]]
- Does 20 references on every single senior hire — cited by [[Keith Rabois]] as an example of ruthless referencing
- Pitched DoorDash to Keith with the stat: "93% of restaurants in the United States don't deliver" — which convinced Keith it was a big opportunity
- With Evan, walked into Keith's office and described what they wanted to create — Keith connected it to [[Andrew Mason]]'s quote about phones having two buttons: "I'm bored" and "I'm hungry"
- Keith was an early investor in DoorDash
