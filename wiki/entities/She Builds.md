## Definition

She Builds (shebuilds.lovable.app) is a recurring women-only hackathon run by [[Lovable]], offering participants unlimited free Lovable access for 48 hours.

## Key Information

- Created in response to [[Elena Verna]]'s concern that women are significantly underrepresented among Lovable's users (she cites third-party data suggesting only ~20% of users are women, despite the brand's deliberately warm, approachable design).
- Verna reports participants building hyper-local, non-venture-scale solutions for problems they personally face — e.g., tools to help with elderly parents, kids, household organization, or church/community groups — projects she says would never have justified traditional software investment but are now feasible via vibe coding.
- Runs on a recurring cohort basis (next cohort starting December 15 at time of episode), with a larger planned event around International Women's Day.
- The site itself was vibe-coded by [[Lazar]], including its application/submission process.
