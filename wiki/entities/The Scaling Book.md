## Definition
The Scaling Book is a resource on large-language-model scaling laws whose exercises Vlad Feinberg uses as a hands-on hiring screen for pre-training roles; its algebra expresses the pipeline-prefill optimization behind Flash 2.0.
## Key Information
- Vlad's open offer: candidates who complete the detailed, hand-written Scaling Book exercises (plus the transformer exercise) and send a video are invited to interview for his team — if they can work from the New York office.
- The exercises demonstrate initial knowledge of scaling laws, engineering willingness to implement a real transformer, and the "bread-and-butter math" used to size LLMs.
- Co-authors Dwarak Rajagopal and Reiner Pope published a write-up of the Flash 2.0 pipeline-prefill optimization expressible in the book's algebra.
- Completing the exercises is recommended for its own sake, independent of any DeepMind job, and helps prepare for interviews elsewhere.
