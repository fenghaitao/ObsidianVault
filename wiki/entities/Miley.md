## Definition

Miley is a friend of Dr. Becky Kennedy who coined the term "factory settings" to describe the childhood brain wiring that forms the blueprint for the rest of a person's life.

## Key Information

- Friend of Dr. Becky Kennedy
- Coined the term "factory settings" for childhood brain wiring: "that's when our brain is wiring, it's kind of when we form the blueprint or my friend Miley calls it the factory settings for the rest of our kids' lives"
