## Definition

Claire Vo is a longtime engineer, three-time chief product officer, founder of ChatPRD, and host of the "How I AI" podcast (a sister podcast to Lenny's Podcast). She is a power user of OpenClaw, running 9 agents across 3 Mac Minis.

## Key Information

- Three-time Chief Product Officer with deep engineering background
- Founder of ChatPRD, an AI product management company
- Host of "How I AI" podcast on YouTube, Spotify, and Apple Podcasts
- Launched a Maven course for executives on transforming engineering, product, and design organizations
- Went from OpenClaw skeptic to running 9 agents (Polly, Finn, Sam, Howie, Max, Kelly, Holly, Sage, Q) across 3 Mac Minis
- First OpenClaw install took 8 hours and resulted in her personal family calendar being deleted
- Describes OpenClaw as "the most mind-blowing and important AI experience I have had since ChatGPT"
- Uses Claude Code as a "god mode administrator" to manage and fix her OpenClaw agents
- Married with three boys (ages 9, 6, and newborn), lives in San Francisco
- Takes ballet as her single non-coding hobby
- Life mottos: "Fast beats right" (work) and "Most people you work with won't be at your funeral" (personal)
- Uses the full Silicon Valley sleep stack: Aura (Oura Ring), Apple Watch, Eight Sleep
- Co-invests with her husband
- Previously built her own computers in high school (Fry's Electronics era)
- Manages her agents like employees: role scoping, progressive trust, clear onboarding
- Communicates with agents primarily through Telegram, using voice notes for "ramble mode"
- Uses Linear as a tasking substrate for her agents to assign her tasks
