## Definition

Pfizer is a pharmaceutical company that uses Claude to accelerate research workflows, saving 16,000 hours of annual research time.

## Key Information

- Used Claude to handle literature review, data synthesis, and documentation.
- Freed researchers to focus on scientific problem-solving rather than tedious workflow management.
- Cited alongside [[NovoNordisk]] as a production example of measurable ROI in life sciences.
