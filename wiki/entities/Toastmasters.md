## Definition

Toastmasters International is a public-speaking and leadership club where Ryan L. Peterman practiced speaking before starting his podcast.

## Key Information

- Ryan used Toastmasters to give speeches that got graded as public-speaking practice
- It was effective but infrequent, and he didn't reflect as hard because the speech wasn't tied to a deliverable
- He started the podcast to get a higher-frequency, higher-commitment speaking practice loop
