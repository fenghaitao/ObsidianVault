## Definition

Company behind an AI wearable device (the "AI Pin," which projected a screen onto the user's hand as a display substitute); cited by Tony Fadell as an example of a screen-replacement approach he's skeptical of.

## Key Information

- Fadell is skeptical of Humane's hand-projection approach to replacing a traditional screen, saying it is "different, not better" — you still need something to project onto, so it doesn't truly eliminate the need for a display.
- Used as a counterpoint in Fadell's argument that even in a long-term, voice-primary future, devices will still need some form of display for visual information (maps, glanceable content, etc.).
