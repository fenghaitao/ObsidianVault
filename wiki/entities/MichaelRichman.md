## Definition
Michael Richman is an engineering leader at Bitly who co-leads the company's AI coding tools strategy, co-wrote the Bitly MCP server, and created Cmd+Ctrl (Command and Control), an open-source system for remote monitoring and interaction with AI coding agents.

## Key Information
- Leads several engineering teams at Bitly, the link shortener company.
- Co-leads Bitly's AI coding tools strategy and trains engineers on AI skills and best practices.
- Co-wrote the Bitly MCP server.
- Created Cmd+Ctrl (Command and Control) to solve the problem of interacting with coding agents outside the terminal or IDE — nothing existed to solve it at the time.
- Runs multiple coding agents simultaneously across Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini, and GitHub Copilot in various states of completion.
- Introduced the concept of FOMAT (Fear of Missing Agent Time) — the anxiety of being away from your dev machine when an agent needs input or completes.
- Argues that the traditional concept of "flow" is transforming into agent choreography: orchestrating multiple parallel agents, moving between them to unblock and redirect.
- Starts his day issuing prompts from bed via Cmd+Ctrl, then resumes sessions at his dev machine.
- Emphasizes that time away from agents is when the best ideas emerge, and systems must support reaching agents during those breaks.
- Believes we need systems that are a "single pane of glass" into all agent sessions, regardless of platform or machine.
