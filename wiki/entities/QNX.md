## Definition

QNX is the Canadian real-time operating-systems company where Bryan Cantrill did kernel development for two summers before joining Sun Microsystems.

## Key Information

- Cantrill worked at QNX in Canada on OS/kernel development across two summers, work he still looks back on fondly ("I love working for QNX. I love being in Canada.").
- The experience was part of why Sun hired him two levels deep as a new grad (he entered as an MTS3 rather than the more common MTS2).
- He decided not to return to QNX professionally because he wanted to move to Silicon Valley.
