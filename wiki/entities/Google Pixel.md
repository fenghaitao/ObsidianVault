## Definition
Google Pixel is Google's Android smartphone line. Kitze cites the latest Pixel as evidence that local AI agents on phones are already viable — it can launch apps in the background, order coffee, and perform automated tasks.

## Key Information
- Latest Google Pixel can launch apps in the background and order coffee autonomously
- Kitze uses this as evidence that local phone agents are already here
- Represents the direction where Apple may compete with local Siri agents
- Contrasts with iOS limitations: "it can maybe send you a push notification and if Tim Cook allows"
