## Definition

Author of *[[Crossing the Chasm]]* and prior [[Lenny's Podcast]] guest; [[Jason Cohen]] recommends reading the actual book rather than secondhand summaries, since most people only know the diagram, not the deeper argument.

## Key Information

- Cohen highlights Moore's definition of a market as a group of people who respect each other's opinions — meaning early adopters spread word of mouth only to other early adopters, and jumping to a new market can't be done by reusing case studies aimed at the old one.
