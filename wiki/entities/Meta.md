## Definition

Meta is the parent company of [[Instagram]] and [[Facebook]], led by Mark Zuckerberg ("Zuck," "Mark" in the transcript). [[Adam Mosseri]] is Head of Instagram at Meta.

## Key Information

- In 2026, Meta/Instagram product teams are restructuring from large specialist teams to small generalist "[[Pods|pods]]" (see [[Pods]]) built around a new "[[Product Staff|product staff]]" role (see [[Product Staff]]).
- Does not use token-spend leaderboards for engineers; treats AI token/compute budget as a constrained resource alongside GPUs, CPUs, storage, RAM, labeling opex, and headcount payroll. See [[Token Budgeting]].
- Mark Zuckerberg reportedly encouraged [[Adam Mosseri]] to become a public-facing spokesperson for the company's social-media platforms rather than rely solely on conventional press-release communication, contributing to Mosseri's high public visibility on Twitter/Threads.
- Associated historically with major public controversies, including the Cambridge Analytica lawsuit era, that shaped how the company (and Mosseri specifically) communicates about product changes.
- Has advocated at a policy level for parents to be able to approve which apps their kids download onto devices.
- [[Zevi Arnovitz]], a current Meta PM with zero technical background (previously at [[Wix]]), ships production-quality side projects entirely via AI coding agents; his engineers at Meta reportedly ask him to teach them his workflow. See [[Slash Command Development Workflow]].

### Fiona Fung's tenure (pre-Anthropic)

- [[Fiona Fung]] joined Meta as a manager but spent roughly her first quarter as an individual contributor, to learn what it's like to ship as a Meta engineer before formally taking on reports.
- She started and led the [[Facebook Marketplace]] team, taking it from idea to launch; it now generates over $100 billion in GMV (gross merchandise value) annually.
- She also oversaw work on Meta's first smart glasses product and later helped build Orion, Meta's first AR (augmented reality) glasses product.
- She then moved to Instagram, where she led infrastructure, growth, integrity, and safety teams, overseeing an org of 500+ people across her time at Instagram and Meta.
- Example of dogfooding/customer-visit value from her Meta era: a small research trip to Chile to test a Facebook Marketplace regional launch revealed the feed performed poorly on the region's slower LTE connections — a growth blocker dashboards alone hadn't surfaced.
- The last production software Fung personally shipped at Meta was around 2017; she describes regaining the confidence to ship code again only after using [[Claude Code]] at [[Anthropic]].

### Per Benedict Evans (episode 07)

- Cited as an example of using existing distribution to push an AI product (Meta AI / the Llama-based assistant) despite no clear product superiority over ChatGPT or Gemini — Evans notes Meta AI ranked between ChatGPT and Gemini in usage survey data purely because Meta "sprayed it on every service surface," not because tech insiders rated it well.
- Used as a mobile-platform-shift case study: unlike Yahoo (which missed mobile) or Google (unaffected either way), the shift to mobile was "great" for Meta/Facebook because a phone's camera and always-on notifications suited social media better than PC ever did — cited as a reason not to assume the AI platform shift affects every incumbent the same way.
- Referenced regarding pricing/margin structure debates: Evans's electricity-industry analogy (TV makers don't pay a cut to the electricity company) is framed as a warning against assuming AI labs will capture value the way platform owners like Meta/Facebook did on mobile.
