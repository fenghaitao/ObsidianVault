## Definition

Meta (formerly Facebook) is the parent company of Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and other platforms. Zevi Arnovitz works there as a Product Manager.

## Key Information

- Zevi Arnovitz is a PM at Meta
- Zevi's engineers at Meta ask him to teach them his AI-powered development workflow
- The Meta PM interview process is described as "super competitive and difficult"
- Zevi used AI extensively to prepare for his Meta PM interview
- Marc Andreessen believes Meta glasses (Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses) are part of a coming wearables revolution driven by AI voice technology
- Marc sees Meta glasses as a key form factor for the AI voice and wearables future
- Boris Cherny worked at Meta, responsible for code quality across Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp
- Boris: "I was like one of the top few most productive engineers" at Instagram, which was part of Meta
- Boris helped build Threads at Meta
- Nikhyl Singhal was a long-time executive at Meta before founding the Skip community
- Nikhyl notes that working at a big brand like Meta can sometimes hurt in interviews because "it's hard to even talk about what you've done" — achievements like "I managed to make this piece of the algorithm go a little faster" fall flat when the product world has totally changed
