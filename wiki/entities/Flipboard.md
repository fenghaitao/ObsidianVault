## Definition

Flipboard is a social-magazine/news app company where Ryan Olson interned (~50 people, ~4 interns) and where he built and open-sourced FLEX.

## Key Information

- Around 50 people and 4 interns when Ryan joined for the summer; an "extremely high density of talent" (people from Apple's iOS frameworks).
- Ryan built FLEX, an in-app iOS debugging tool, during his internship and open-sourced it with company approval; it was later used at Facebook and Instagram.
- The intern class included Dylan Field (later Figma) and Devin Finzer (later OpenSea).
- Co-founder Evan Doll (formerly Apple) personally recruited Ryan over the phone; Ryan almost declined the call but was glad he answered.
- The iOS team was roughly 3–5 people through most of his time there.
