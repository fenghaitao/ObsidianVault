## Definition

Vulcan, co-founded by Aleksander Mekhanik and Tanner Jones (April 2025) with no traditional engineering background, uses Claude and [[ClaudeCode]] for AI-powered regulatory analysis, tackling centuries of accumulated regulatory code complexity.

## Key Information

- Built a prototype for Virginia's governor's office by May 1, 2025 (pre-Claude Code, via copy-pasting Claude outputs) and won the contract over established consulting firms.
- Hired CTO Christopher Minge (prior experience at Google on Gemini and Waymo); velocity multiplied again once Claude Code launched in June 2025.
- **Impact**: reduced the average price of a new home in Virginia by $24,000, saving Virginians over a billion dollars annually by identifying redundant/duplicative regulatory requirements.
- Virginia's governor signed **Executive Order 51**, mandating that all state agencies use agentic AI regulatory review.
- Raised an $11M seed round; secured state and federal government contracts with three founders, only one properly technical.
- Tanner Jones: suggests founders with strong command of language and structured critical thinking (e.g., humanities backgrounds) may see marginal prompting benefits, since the medium of AI collaboration is language.
