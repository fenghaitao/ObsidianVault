## Definition

Milana (getmilana.ai) is a company that builds an AI product engineer powered by [[ClaudeManagedAgents]], using [[AgenticVaults|vaults]] to securely connect its agent to customer codebases for automated bug finding and fixing.

## Key Information

- Uses environment variables in vaults to securely connect its AI product engineer to a customer's codebase.
- The agent finds and fixes bugs automatically.
- Large-scale data analysis runs faster than before with the Managed Agents infrastructure.
- Cited by Anthropic (June 2026) as an example of vaults enabling secure codebase access without exposing credentials.
