## Definition
AT&T is a major telecommunications company and Fortune 500 enterprise that is a customer of Adaptive ML, using RL-trained models for transcript summarization at scale.

## Key Information
- **Use case**: Summarizing every transcript between a customer and an agent
- **Scale**: Summarization alone costs millions of dollars at scale when using large proprietary models
- **RL benefit**: Training a smaller, RL-optimized model dramatically reduces serving costs while maintaining quality
- **Enterprise context**: Any feature or use case that becomes a commodity at AT&T's scale (internal employees or customer-facing) costs millions of dollars
- **Platform**: Uses Adaptive ML's Adaptive Engine for RL-based model training and deployment
