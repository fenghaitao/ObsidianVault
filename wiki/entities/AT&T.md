## Definition
AT&T was the corporate owner of Bell Labs and Bjarne Stroustrup's employer.

## Key Information
- Bell Labs was AT&T's research arm; Bjarne worked there "for the next couple of decades."
- AT&T, "primarily a user of software," was in favor of C++ standardization.
- The push to standardize C++ under ISO was partly because competitors would not adopt a language owned by a corporation (AT&T) they sometimes competed with, and a boss could "run over" its steward.
