## Definition

David Hewitt is a developer at Pydantic working on Logfire, and a maintainer of PyO3 (the Rust-Python bridge). Presented at PyAI London 2026 with Pablo Galindo Salgado on the open source maintainer crisis.

## Key Information

- Works on Pydantic Logfire and PyO3
- Presented a Blue Sky firehose agent demo at Google London meetup, showing Python filtering bottleneck solved by Rust rewrite
- Co-presented "Maintaining OSS in the age of AI" at PyAI London 2026
- Notes the irony of producing drive-by AI PRs himself while complaining about AI slop
- Maintains projects at three different levels of AI exposure (PyO3, Pydantic, Malt)
