## Definition

Princeton University rejected Barbara Liskov's graduate application on the grounds that she was a woman.

## Key Information

- After her bachelor's degree, Liskov applied to several graduate math programs and received a postcard from Princeton reading "We do not admit women."
- She knew Princeton had no women in its undergraduate program but hadn't realized the exclusion extended to graduate study; the rejection contributed to her taking a programming job and discovering computer science.
- Vlad Feinberg gave a lecture at Princeton; a common question afterward was "how do I work at DeepMind," which partly motivated his "how to get a job at a frontier lab" post.
