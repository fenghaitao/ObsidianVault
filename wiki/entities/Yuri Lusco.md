## Definition

Yuri Lusco was a rising star professor at Stanford University whose research lab Rahul Pandey joined as a research assistant. He spun his lab's work out into the startup Cos, which Rahul joined as employee #2.

## Key Information

- Professor at Stanford, described as a "rising star"
- Rahul was a research assistant in his lab during his fifth year at Stanford
- His research was in recommendation systems and big data (effectively machine learning)
- Rahul described him as "undoubtedly one of the best people in the field in the world"
- Decided to spin the lab's work out into a company (Cos)
- Stanford is known for allowing faculty to have a revolving door with academia and industry
- One of Rahul's reasons for joining Cos was the opportunity to work closely with and learn from Yuri
