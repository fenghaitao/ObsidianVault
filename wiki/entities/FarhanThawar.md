## Definition
Farhan Thawar is the head of engineering at Shopify, known for securing early access to GitHub Copilot for Shopify a full year before its public release and pioneering the strategy of trading churn for competitive advantage in AI adoption.

## Key Information
- Head of engineering at Shopify
- Heard about GitHub Copilot being developed internally at GitHub and directly pinged Thomas Dohmke (GitHub CEO)
- Told Dohmke: "I didn't ask if it's for sale. We would like to roll out to all of Shopify, and in return, we will give you feedback for 3,000 people"
- Shopify got Copilot access in 2021, a year before public release
- Gives unlimited AI tool budget to Shopify engineers
- Strategy: trading off churn and expense to be months ahead of competition
- Concerned about costs now but believes it's still worth it: "it would look silly if I said you cannot have these tools — how would I hire the best?"
