## Definition

James Clear is the author of "Atomic Habits." Molly Graham cites his quote "winners and losers have the same goal" to emphasize that setting goals alone is insufficient without a process for follow-up and accountability.

## Key Information

- Author of "Atomic Habits"
- Quoted by Molly Graham: "Winners and losers have the same goal" — meaning goals by themselves are not enough; you need a process to follow up and learn from them
- This quote is used as part of Graham's sixth rule of goal setting: goals alone are not enough
