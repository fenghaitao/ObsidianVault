## Definition

James Clear is the author of *Atomic Habits*. [[Molly Graham]] cites his line "winners and losers have the same goal" as the last of her "[[Six Rules For Goals And Alignment]]" — the point that setting a goal alone accomplishes nothing without a follow-up process to hold people accountable and learn from the attempt.

## Key Information

- Quoted by Graham to make the point that founders who set goals and then don't build any process to track, learn from, or hold people accountable to them are missing the actual work of goal-setting.
