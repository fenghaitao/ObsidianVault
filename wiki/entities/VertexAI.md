## Definition
Vertex AI is Google Cloud's enterprise AI platform offering cloud-hosted access to Gemma 4's larger models (31B and 26B) for production deployment and development.

## Key Information
- Cloud-hosted option for Gemma 4's 31B and 26B models
- Enterprise-grade AI platform on Google Cloud
- Complements AI Studio as a production-oriented deployment option
