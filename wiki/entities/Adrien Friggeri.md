## Definition

Adrien Friggeri is a software engineer who rose from a new-grad PhD hire to Principal Engineer (IC8) at Meta, with a career spanning Facebook's early data-science team, Instagram, Clubhouse, and Meta's smart-glasses/wearables hardware group.

## Key Information

- Holds a PhD in social-network analysis; was pre-allocated to Facebook's "data science" team (~20 people) in 2011-12, which built A/B testing and data-measurement infrastructure.
- First project (with Lada Adamic and others) was studying how rumors spread on social networks; found false rumors spread faster than true ones and that debunking a rumor did not meaningfully stop its spread.
- Adopted a software-engineer identity after his first manager Cameron Marlow told him "software engineers have power at this company... you are a software engineer."
- As an IC4, built A/B testing for Instagram by hacking a Facebook API endpoint to log exposures and replicating the bucket-hashing logic; was promoted to IC5 without even knowing promotions existed.
- Built Bento, Meta's Jupyter notebook platform, and later led data developer tools as a Tech Lead Manager (TLM2).
- Left Meta to attempt a startup, found he was not an entrepreneur, and effectively took a sabbatical; boomeranged back to Meta twice (for managers James Pierce and Vincent Hardy) and joined Clubhouse briefly.
- Pivoted from "the data guy" to product/hardware, leading all user "experiences" on an unreleased smart-glasses line, and earned IC8 (Principal Engineer) after two promotion cycles.
- Cites Robert Heinlein's "specialization is for insects" as a personal philosophy; active mainly on LinkedIn.
