## Definition

Vinod Khosla is the founder of Khosla Ventures and a prominent venture capitalist. He was on the board of Square and is credited with the foundational insight: "The team you build is the company you build."

## Key Information

- Founder of [[Khosla Ventures]]
- Co-founder of Sun Microsystems
- Was on the board of [[Square]] where [[Keith Rabois]] worked
- Told Keith: "The team you build is the company you build" — described as the most important lesson for creating a startup
- Described as a mix of founder-driven and technology-driven investor (unlike purely founder-driven investors like Keith Rabois, or purely technology-driven investors like [[Marc Andreessen]])
- Keith Rabois considers him a mentor and key influence
