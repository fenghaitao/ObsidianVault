## Definition
Bryan Bischof (referred to as "Brian" in the transcript) is a practitioner and co-author of the O'Reilly white paper "What We Learned from a Year of Building with LLMs." He presented the strategic considerations section of the keynote at the AI Engineer Summit, focusing on the model-as-moat fallacy, continuous improvement, and long-term planning with LLM cost trends.

## Key Information
- Co-authored the O'Reilly white paper on LLM application building lessons alongside five other practitioners
- Presented the strategic track of the keynote, arguing that the model is not the moat for almost any company
- Advocated building in your "zone of genius" — leveraging product expertise rather than competing with model providers
- Framed LLM cost reduction as faster than Moore's Law: roughly an order of magnitude every 12-18 months across three capability tiers (DaVinci, text-davinci-002, GPT-4/Sonnet)
- Demonstrated that a chatbot-powered video game NPC experience would become economically viable at consumer price points within approximately 30 months from mid-2023
- Emphasized starting with evals and getting products in front of users even in beta, citing Apple Intelligence, Photoshop, and Hex as examples
- Traced the continuous improvement cycle through MLOps, DevOps, Lean Startup, and Toyota's Kaizen
