## Definition

FastAPI is a modern Python web framework for building APIs. Jia Chen referenced FastAPI as an example of a tech stack detail that hackathon judges do not care about during pitches.

## Key Information

- Jia cited FastAPI as an example of a technical detail that hackathon competitors mistakenly emphasize during their pitches
- Her advice: judges are often non-technical and want to see a working demo, not hear about the tech stack (MongoDB, Next.js, FastAPI, etc.)
- Sebastian Ramirez, FastAPI's creator, was an early, influential supporter of Charlie Marsh's Ruff; when he said "I'd like to use it," Charlie asked what it would take for him to adopt it and then built those things.
