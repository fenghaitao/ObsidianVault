## Definition

Asian Dad Energy is the pseudonymous YouTube handle of an anonymous big-tech veteran who vlogs about being laid off after 25 years in tech, spanning engineering consulting and a big-tech chief-architect role.

## Key Information

- Anonymous; he uses the pseudonym "Asian Dad Energy" for a vlog that went viral unexpectedly.
- 25-year software engineering career: engineering consulting (junior → senior → tech lead → architect → technical director) then big-tech senior enterprise / chief architect for his final several years.
- On a known layoff round, he voluntarily swapped himself to the top of the cut list to spare a couple of junior engineers, "buying them a matter of months."
- Started vlogging because (per his therapist's suggestion) it was easier to turn on a camera than to write his anxious thoughts into a book; found it therapeutic when others resonated.
- The episode introduction describes him as "the anonymous big techie who got laid off after 25 years in tech."
