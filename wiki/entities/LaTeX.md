## Definition

LaTeX is the document preparation and typesetting system created by Leslie Lamport as a set of macros on top of TeX ("tech" in the transcript audio), separate the content of writing from its formatting.

## Key Information

- Lamport built LaTeX because he needed macros to make TeX do what he wanted for a book, and realized a little extra effort would make them usable by others.
- Was inspired by Scribe's idea of describing the logical structure of a document and letting the system handle formatting.
- A typographic designer at Addison-Wesley was responsible for the typographic design in the standard LaTeX styles.
- Took him six to nine months of "spare time" — during which he sometimes billed other projects.
- He discovered that when something was hard to explain, it needed to change: writing about LaTeX forced improvements to LaTeX itself.
