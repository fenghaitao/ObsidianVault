## Definition

Kevin Systrom is the co-founder and CEO of Instagram (transcribed in the episode as "Kevin Cyester"), which Facebook acquired in September 2012.

## Key Information

- Co-founded Instagram with Mike Krieger; Facebook/Meta acquired Instagram in September 2012.
- Alongside Mike Krieger, was among the early Instagram leaders Adrien Friggeri pitched A/B testing to; the team deprioritized it during the AWS-to-Facebook infrastructure integration.
