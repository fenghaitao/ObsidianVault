## Definition

Amie Vora (also spelled Army Arora) is a product leader at Anthropic and Amol Avaisary's manager. She was a guest on Lenny's Podcast and has written extensively about product management.

## Key Information

- Product leader at Anthropic; Amol Avaisary's manager
- Was a guest on Lenny's Podcast
- Has written extensively about product management publicly
- Amol uses Claude to model her feedback style: based on her public writing, internal communications, and their discussions, he asks Claude weekly "what feedback would Amie have for me this week?"
- Amol describes her as "phenomenal"
