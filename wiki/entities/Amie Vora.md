## Definition

Head of product at [[Anthropic]] (name transcribed inconsistently across episodes as "Ami"/"Army Arora"/"Ammy Vora"/"Amivora") and [[Amol]]'s manager; a former [[Lenny's Podcast]] guest, cited by Amol as an example of the exceptional talent density at Anthropic ("I feel like I'm playing for Real Madrid").

## Key Information

- Amol uses her public writing about product plus internal Slack history as training material for a Claude workflow that generates simulated weekly feedback "from her" on his own performance — an example of the "soft coaching" AI use case he describes.

### Per episode 34 (Molly Graham)
- Referred to as "Ammy Vora"/"Amivora" in this episode's transcript. [[Molly Graham]] credits her with the framing "all advice is just someone telling you what they did," which Graham repeats throughout the episode as her own stance on giving advice.
- Cited by [[Lenny Rachitsky]] as a former podcast guest and speaker at the Lenny Friends Summit.
