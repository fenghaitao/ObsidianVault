## Definition

Amelia is [[SaaStr]]'s "Chief AI Officer," the sole full-time-equivalent-adjacent human (she spends ~20% of her time on this) who orchestrates SaaStr's fleet of 20 AI go-to-market agents alongside one full-time human AE.

## Key Information

- Role: manages/orchestrates all 20 agents, spending 10-15 hours a week reviewing agent outputs — described by [[Jason Lemkin]] as exhausting because "agents work all night and they work weekends and they work on Christmas," so the job never fully stops.
- Segments SaaStr's contact base across agents (inbound, outbound, lapsed high-end, lapsed low-end) so agents don't conflict with each other — Lemkin frames this as a core, underappreciated skill: knowing how to route/segment a lead base is not new (marketers already do this for A/B testing and segmentation) but doing it with zero human follow-through is a new failure mode.
- Directly involved in a specific deployment: alongside a colleague named Mia, ran a consulting-style call with a public B2B company worth "well over $10 billion" that wanted AI GTM help but, on questioning, had never actually deployed or trained an agent themselves — illustrating Lemkin's broader point that most people asking for help haven't done the reps.
- Lemkin's job-role framing: he explicitly calls Amelia's title "Chief AI Officer," not "GTM engineer" (a title he's skeptical of as a distinct, already-formed profession) — see [[Chief Orchestration Officer]].
- Lemkin's hiring advice for this role going forward: promote from within — ideally someone "nerdy," already quant, who loves sitting with data, likely coming out of product, marketing, or RevOps rather than traditional sales.
