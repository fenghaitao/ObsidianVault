## Definition

Jake Bolam is a Principal Engineer (IC8) at Instagram (Meta), who grew from IC6 to IC8 across two promotions by leading large infrastructure migrations and adopting a "leading through others" archetype.

## Key Information

- Joined Meta as an IC6 on Facebook Groups, which grew from 10-15 to ~700 people; had a slow remote onboarding during COVID but a supportive manager
- Was repeatedly pulled from front-end product into front-end infrastructure and then backend infrastructure at every company he joined
- Moved to Instagram web as IC6, was immediately productive, and was asked to be "Uber TL" across multiple teams
- IC7 promotion: a 2-year front-end/backend infra migration delivered mostly in ~6 months, freeing resources to ship ads on Instagram web in 3 months
- IC8 promotion: leading the multi-year migration of Instagram's backend to Meta's main stack — a project most of the org deemed impossible/stupid; year one was framed as "fix the devx between stacks"
- Uses "server champions" (~40 cross-org product-team leads) as his most important feedback channel for infra changes
- Maintains work-life balance via time shielding: ~50% focus blocks, no meetings Wednesdays/Fridays, 1:1s batched Monday afternoons
- Keeps a flat-folder VS Code note-taking system (tens of thousands of files, links between them, optional graph view) as an extension of his memory
- Career advice: "go where you're valued" — match your strengths to a team that appreciates them
