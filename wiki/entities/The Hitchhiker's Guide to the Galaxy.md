## Definition

The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy is a science fiction comedy series by Douglas Adams. Sam Lessin references it while reading the third book to his son, specifically mentioning "Bistromathics" — the idea that the most complicated math in the universe is the math of your bill at a restaurant, used to power spaceship travel.
