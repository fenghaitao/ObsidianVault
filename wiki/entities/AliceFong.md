## Definition

Alice Fong is a member of [[Anthropic]]'s corporate finance and strategy team. She joined in March 2025 and is responsible for running the analysis and pulling the metrics that go into the quarterly board deck prepared for the CFO.

## Key Information

- Role sits at the center of the finance organization: other finance teams partner directly with the business (e.g., go-to-market finance with sales), and everything they learn flows back to corporate finance.
- Responsible for preparing the narrative the CFO and board need: how revenue performed, what's happening to margins, how cash is being deployed, and what it means for the rest of the year.
- Uses [[ClaudeCowork]] and [[ClaudeForExcelPowerPoint|Claude for Excel]] in parallel: Claude Cowork for writing and synthesizing information in documents and decks, Claude for Excel for editing directly in the financial model.
- Uses Claude Cowork [[Projects|projects]] with separate contexts for separate audiences (monthly review vs. board deck), since tone and conventions differ.
- Reported freeing 10 to 20 hours a week for higher-impact judgment work using Claude.
- Advises finance teams getting started to begin simple (ask Claude to read a doc and summarize), then push boundaries on recurring workflows where consistency compounds and project memory gets richer each pass.
- Runs almost entirely on Claude Cowork projects, Claude for Excel, and [[GoogleSuiteConnector|Google Suite Connector]].
