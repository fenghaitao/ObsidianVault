## Definition

Jessica Fain is a product leader who has worked at Box, Slack, Brightwheel, and Webflow. She is uniquely skilled at the art and science of influence, particularly influencing executives. She served as Chief of Staff to two CPOs at Slack (April Underwood and Tamar Yehoshua), giving her deep insight into how executives make decisions.

## Key Information

- Product leader at Box, Slack, Brightwheel, and currently at Webflow
- Served as Chief of Staff to April Underwood and Tamar Yehoshua at Slack
- Pitched April Underwood for the Chief of Staff role while eight months pregnant, during a two-day overlap before April's maternity leave
- After her Chief of Staff stint, took over Slack's core product team (channels, emoji, search, messaging)
- Implemented the "Customer Love Sprint" at Slack — a two-week sprint where engineers picked what to work on and shipped 65 improvements
- Led the PM career ladder redesign at Webflow
- Describes herself as an Enneagram type 2-wing-3 (people person + achiever)
- Early in her career received feedback to "show up more like a man" — more aggressive, more decisive — but learned authenticity is her superpower
- Not very online; primarily on LinkedIn
- Her first PM manager was Annie Pearl at Box
