## Definition
Lyra 3 is Google DeepMind's music generation model, available via AI Studio API. It supports configurable genres, moods, and lyrics in multiple languages, with Pro and Clip preview variants.

## Key Information
- Music generation model from Google DeepMind
- Available via AI Studio with Lyra 3 Pro Preview and Lyra 3 Clip Preview variants
- Supports configurable sound configurations (acoustic folk, '90s alt rock, electronic, danceable, etc.)
- Generates lyrics in multiple languages (demonstrated with Spanish lyrics)
- Can be integrated into apps via AI Studio's gallery with remix capability
- Demonstrated generating a theme song for a vegan basketball food truck concept
- Presented at AIE conference Gen Media session by Guillaume
