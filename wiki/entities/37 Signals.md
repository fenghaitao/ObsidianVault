## Definition

37 Signals (the company behind Basecamp) is cited by Jason Cohen as an example of a bootstrap company that hit a natural size limit and chose to maximize profit instead of revenue — demonstrating that not growing can be a valid and intentional choice.

## Key Information

- Example of a company that chose to maximize profit over revenue growth
- Founders are getting paid millions in dividends without needing to grow further
- Illustrates that "do you need to grow?" is a legitimate question
- Represents the bootstrap philosophy where growth at all costs is not the goal
- Growing bigger might mean becoming an organization the founders don't like or serving a market segment they don't want to serve
