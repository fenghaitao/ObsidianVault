## Definition
Greg Brockman is a co-founder of OpenAI who advocates for modularity in AI system design, keeping clear boundaries on human-designed components while letting AI code everything in between. He has also emphasized the importance of evals for AI startups.

## Key Information
- Co-founder of OpenAI
- Preaches the concept of modularity: keep clear boundaries on what is human-designed and let AI code everything in between
- Was a highlight for swyx and the AI Engineer community to chat with personally
- His modularity philosophy aligns with fighting slop by maintaining human oversight on critical design decisions
- Quoted by Aman Khan as saying evals are emerging as a real moat for AI startups
