## Definition

Deloitte is a professional services firm making a significant investment in Anthropic's AI platform as part of its own AI transformation.

## Key Information

- Ranjit Bawa, Global Technology and Ecosystems and Alliances Leader: "Deloitte is making this significant investment in Anthropic's AI platform because our approach to responsible AI is very aligned, and together we can reshape how enterprises operate over the next decade. Claude continues to be a leading choice for many clients and our own AI transformation."
- **Claude Security services partner (April 2026)**: named among the services partners (with Accenture, BCG, Infosys, PwC) helping enterprises deploy Claude-integrated security solutions for vulnerability management, secure code review, and incident response, as part of [[ClaudeSecurity|Claude Security]]'s public beta launch.
- **CTEM built on Deloitte Ascend (May 2026)**: Deloitte's [[CTEM|Continuous Threat Exposure Management]] service runs discovery, validation, prioritization, and remediation as one workflow, including countermeasure design when no patch exists. [[Claude4.7Opus|Claude Opus]]'s code reasoning and automated stability testing gives teams the confidence to remediate in hours rather than days or weeks. Quote: *"CTEM built on Ascend exists to help reduce decision latency in vulnerability remediation — the gap helps determine whether attackers or defenders win the window."* — Adnan Amjad, partner and US Cyber leader, Deloitte.
