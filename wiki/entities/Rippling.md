## Definition

Rippling is a unified workforce-management (payroll, HR/HCM, IT, spend) software platform, last valued at over $16 billion, founded and led by CEO [[Parker Conrad]]. [[Matt MacInnis]] is its Chief Product Officer and former longtime COO.

## Key Information

- Over 5,200 employees; over 1,300 people in the R&D organization; tens of thousands of customer companies, which the company estimates as under 1% of its addressable market.
- Mission (of the product org, nested under the company mission "to free smart people to work on hard problems"): to build the most successful business-software platform in history, on top of the "people primitive" — the record of who's doing what and who's accountable — rather than the customer-record primitive that companies like Salesforce built on.
- Explicitly contrasted with [[Workday]], which MacInnis calls widely-regarded-as-bad software that, despite Workday's commercial success, never became a broad general-purpose business platform built on the people primitive.
- Product lines: payroll, HCM, IT, spend, and (announced as forthcoming at time of episode) a business-intelligence/data-management product, plus an AI layer built on Rippling's unified business data graph — pitched as a major differentiator versus point-solution competitors who can only integrate to each other via thin channels (e.g., flat files over SFTP).
- Internal practices described in the episode: [[Deliberate Understaffing]] of every project; [[The Pickle (Product Quality List)]] as its product-quality checklist; a dedicated escalations team trained to find true root causes ([[Escalations Are A Gift]]); Parker Conrad personally runs payroll for all 5,200+ employees and personally installs/dogfoods every new internal app.
- Cultural artifact: a company-wide "Conscious Business reading club," with copies of *[[Conscious Business]]* handed out "like candy."
- Fellow (the coffee-maker company) is cited as a Rippling customer.
