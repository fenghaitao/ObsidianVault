## Definition

Rippling is a high-growth HR and IT platform company. It is referenced in the context of a SaaStr talk ("Rippling Secrets to Hypergrowth") and as a company that had to deliberately develop cold calling capabilities later in its growth journey.

## Key Information

- Matt Plank (Rippling) and Sam Blonde (former CRO of Brex, now on Rippling's board) gave a SaaStr talk called "Rippling Secrets to Hypergrowth" — praised as a "10/10 for GTM"
- Rippling was late to develop cold calling as a capability because the original CRO (who came from Jason Lemkin's team) never did cold calling
- Had to bring in someone with Brex/Sam Blonde cold calling experience to build that muscle
- Matt McInness, former COO and now CPO at Rippling, was also a guest on Lenny's Podcast
- Represents the "hypergrowth AI-era company" end of the bifurcated B2B market
- Matt McGinness, former COO and now CPO at Rippling, was also a guest on Lenny's Podcast sharing advice on when to quit a startup: if it's four or five years in and just not clicking, it's probably time to move on
