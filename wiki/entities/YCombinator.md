## Definition

Y Combinator (YC) is a startup accelerator that has launched over 5,000 companies with a combined valuation of over $800B since 2005, including Airbnb, Stripe, and DoorDash.

## Key Information

- Anthropic profiled three YC-backed startups — [[HumanLayer]], [[Ambral]], and [[Vulcan]] — building substantially with [[ClaudeCode]], compressing development cycles from weeks to hours and enabling non-technical founders to compete with established players.
