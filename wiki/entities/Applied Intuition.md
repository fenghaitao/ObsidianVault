# Applied Intuition

Physical AI company co-founded by [[Qasar Younis]] and Peter. Valued at $15B. Adds AI to vehicles: cars, tractors, planes, submarines, mining rigs, and military systems.

## Business
- ~10 years old, 1,000+ engineers
- 18 of the top 20 automakers are customers
- Also serves the largest global construction, mining, and trucking companies
- Department of Defense customer
- Described as "Waymo or Tesla but without the hardware"
- Has never spent any money it raised — profitable and sustainable business
- Investors include [[Marc Andreessen]], [[Naval Ravikant]], [[Joe Montana]], [[Elad Gil]]

## Company Values
- Meta values: **Radical Pragmatism**
- Move fast, move safe
- Never disappoint the customer
- Technical mastery
- High output matters
- Laugh a lot
- Half the work is follow-up
- "Our best work is done alone and quietly"
- Values were derived from observing what made them successful, then codified
- Managers are assessed and compensated on adherence to values

## Culture
- Cleaning zen: every week, everyone cleans their own area (inspired by Japanese schools)
- No-shoe policy (also influenced by Japan)
- Culture of listening to naysayers: the best idea wins, regardless of who proposes it
- Encourages everyone to speak up, even the most junior person
- Truth-seeking / best-idea-seeking culture

## Philosophy
- Intentionally stayed under the radar for ~10 years
- Focus on craft, product, and customers over public promotion
- "The score takes care of itself" / "The valuation takes care of itself"
- Influenced by Berkshire Hathaway more than typical Silicon Valley darlings
