## Definition

Dr. Satya Nitta is the founder of Emergence, an R&D AI company focused on advancing the science and development of AI agents. He previously worked at IBM Research and brings experience from building large-scale AI deployments.

## Key Information

- Background includes work at IBM Research, where he contributed to the IBM Watson platform.
- Leads Emergence, which he describes as a "distributed systems meets AI R&D team."
- Focuses on enterprise AI agents, particularly for workflows like claims processing, RPA, and document processing.
- Presented at the aiDotEngineer conference launching Emergence's orchestrator agent and Agent E web agent.
