## Definition
DJI is a technology company known primarily for drones, but also produces audio equipment including Bluetooth lapel microphones. Joe Reeve used a DJI Bluetooth lapel mic (costing approximately £200) for the statue app viral video.

## Key Information
- Produces a Bluetooth lapel microphone used by Joe Reeve for the statue app video
- The mic costs approximately £200 and "makes the audio much better"
- Used in combination with CapCut mobile editing
- Represents the minimal but impactful hardware investment for creating high-quality viral content
- Joe Reeve borrowed his wife's DJI lapel mic for the recording
