## Definition

Co-founder and CEO of [[Box]]; former boss and close friend of [[Jeetu Patel]], credited with the "[[Permission To Play]]" ("right to win") framework and with teaching Patel that persistence/stamina beats intellect.

## Key Information

- Patel and Levie developed the "right to win"/permission-to-play concept together while working on Box product strategy.
- Levie's defining trait per Patel: extraordinary persistence and staying power, more than raw intellect, even though he's also very smart.
- Patel and Levie (plus co-founder Jeff Kiser) have had dinner together every 6 weeks in Palo Alto for 6 years since Patel left Box.
