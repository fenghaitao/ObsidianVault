# Carlos Galan

## Definition

Carlos Galan is an engineer at Auth0 (Okta), co-presenting the "Identity for AI Agents" workshop. He is based in Spain (Mallorca) and focuses on async authorization and CIBA-based approval flows for AI agents.

- **Organization**: Auth0 (Okta)
- **Location**: Mallorca, Spain
- **Role**: Co-presenter of the "Identity for AI Agents" workshop at aiDotEngineer (2026-01-14)
- **Expertise**: Async authorization (CIBA), Rich Authorization Requests, OAuth scopes, agent identity
- **First time**: in New York and the US at the time of the talk
- **Personal**: Father of two, joined Auth0/Okta over two years ago
