## Definition

Bridgewater Associates is a premier global asset management firm that seeks to understand the economy in a fundamental way, uncovering cause-and-effect linkages that drive global markets. Analysts spend significant time stress-testing hypotheses, often in code.

## Key Information

- Built an AI-enabled **Investment Analyst Assistant** using [[Claude2|Claude]] on [[AmazonBedrock]] to support junior team members.
- The assistant takes basic instructions, generates Python code, works through errors, and outputs charts and tables — described by CTO Aaron Linsky as working "much like a first or second-year analyst would."
- Asks clarifying questions before executing when uncertain about the right path.
- Supports in-place code editing, letting analysts choose when to lean on Claude versus make simple changes themselves — enabling quicker iteration loops.
- Use case example: investigating the Federal Reserve's tightening of commodity prices by creating data visualizations (charts and tables) to test hypotheses and synthesize findings.
- Quote: *"Claude is the highly capable model behind our upcoming Investment Analyst Assistant on Amazon Bedrock."* — Aaron Linsky, CTO - AI/ML at Bridgewater Associates.
