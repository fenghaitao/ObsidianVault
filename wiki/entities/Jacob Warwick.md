## Definition

Jacob Warwick is a professional negotiator who works behind the scenes with senior tech executives, professional athletes, and Hollywood celebrities to navigate complex career negotiations. He operates "fingerprintless" — all correspondence goes through him before reaching the recipient, similar to an attorney.

## Key Information

- Has helped clients secure over $1 billion in additional compensation
- Works with C-suite executives, Fortune 500 companies, Hollywood talent, and professional athletes
- Operates behind the scenes — clients do the talking, Jacob advises
- Clients' average comp increase: ~40%; some see 100-400% increases
- Has negotiated against guests on Lenny's Podcast (unnamed)
- Formerly homeless ~12 years ago; self-taught in negotiation and psychology
- Previously a marketing manager making $12/hour, then jumped to $120K/year in one negotiation
- Is on the board of the [[Cody Dieruf Foundation]] for cystic fibrosis
- Has a son with cystic fibrosis
- Is writing a book called "Predetermined" (with a question mark)
- Not on social media, rarely does interviews; maintains a low profile
- Has a free negotiation course on YouTube and written scripts/guides on Substack
- Not accepting new clients — booked up
- Favorite products: Claude Co-work, MacroFactor, Whoop, Gemini
- Life motto: "Always give more than you expect to receive"

## Key Philosophy

- Core philosophy: make it clear to the company what pain you will solve and why it's worth paying you more
- Negotiation is collaboration, not confrontation — whiteboarding a solution together
- Information and timing create power in negotiation
- The path to wealth is through ownership, not W-2 employment
- Haste equals risk — slow down the process
- "If you have the ability to say no, you have infinite power"
- "What got you here won't get you there" — tactics change as you mature in your career
- 10x rule: create 10 times more content than you consume
