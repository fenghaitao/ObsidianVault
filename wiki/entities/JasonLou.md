## Definition
Jason Lou (also referenced as Jason Liu) is an AI consultant and co-author of the O'Reilly white paper "What We Learned from a Year of Building with LLMs." He presented the operational considerations section of the keynote at the AI Engineer Summit, focusing on team composition, hiring pitfalls, and the dangers of tool-churning without developing expertise.

## Key Information
- Has consulted with approximately 20 companies ranging from pre-seed to public over the past year
- Co-authored the widely-cited O'Reilly white paper on LLM application building lessons
- Presented the operational track of the keynote alongside Hamel Hussein
- Argued against prematurely hiring ML engineers before having data-generating products
- Criticized the tendency to chase tools (vector databases, embedding models, new papers) instead of developing processes and expertise
- Emphasized that success does not come from buying more tools but from developing evaluation and data literacy
