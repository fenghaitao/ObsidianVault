## Definition

Dippu Singh leads the emerging data technologies and AI architecture initiatives at [[Fujitsu North America]]. He presented at the [[aiDotEngineer]] 2026 conference on applying generative AI to contact center voice streams.

## Key Information

- **Role**: AI architecture lead at Fujitsu North America
- **Domain**: Contact center Voice AI, low-latency intelligence extraction, generative AI pipelines
- **Notable Work**: Designed a four-stage pipeline (voice capture → STT → generative AI core → CRM sync) that reduced after-call work time by ~50% in contact center deployments
- **Key Technical Contributions**: Stereo channel mapping for speaker separation, early-stage PII masking in audio streams, few-shot prompt orchestration for structured call summaries, automated hallucination checks on LLM outputs
- **Roadmap Vision**: Three-phase plan encompassing explainable AI for operator coaching, predictive staffing from intent data, and agent harassment protection via acoustic analysis
