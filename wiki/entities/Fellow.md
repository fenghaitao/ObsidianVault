## Definition

Fellow is a coffee-maker company whose product [[Matt MacInnis]] names as his favorite recent product discovery in the episode's lightning round.

## Key Information

- MacInnis praises the interface (roast-level selection, temperature control, blooming, gram guidance) and owns three units — one each at his office, home, and garage.
- Fellow is also a customer of [[Rippling]]; MacInnis notes, only half-jokingly, that it has never escalated a complaint to him directly and invites Fellow employees to do so.
