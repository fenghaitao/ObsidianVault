## Definition

Fellow is a premium coffee equipment company, maker of the Fellow coffee maker that Matt McGinness describes as his favorite product. The company is also a Rippling customer.

## Key Information

- Makes a premium coffee maker with an interface for setting light/medium/dark roast, adjusting temperature, blooming coffee, and measuring grams
- Matt McGinness owns three Fellow coffee makers (office, home, garage)
- Fellow is a Rippling customer
- McGinness's favorite recently discovered product
