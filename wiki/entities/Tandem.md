## Definition
Tandem is the largest clinical AI product provider in the UK in terms of adoption. Their medical AI scribe product listens to doctor consultations and generates medical notes. They serve many different specialties, countries, types of notes, and use cases.

## Key Information
- **Product**: Medical AI scribe — listens to doctor consultations and generates medical notes
- **Scale**: Largest clinical AI product provider in the UK by adoption
- **Domain expert model**: Started with a single Oracle (Roy, a medical doctor), then evolved to a decentralized Oracle model
- **Decentralized Oracle**: Hired doctors across specialties, countries, and use cases to handle long-tail prompt customizations
- **First domain expert**: Roy — medical doctor who also worked at [[McKinsey]]
- **Platform feature**: Built platform support for long-tail prompt customizations with thousands of prompt variations
- **Challenge**: Serving many different specialties, countries, note types, and use cases simultaneously
- **Why Oracle works for them**: Medical expertise required, subjectivity in notes (no perfect medical note), many customizations needed
