## Definition

Jessica Lessin is the founder and CEO of The Information, a technology news publication. She is married to Sam Lessin and co-hosts the "More or Less" podcast.

## Key Information

- Runs The Information, a technology news publication
- Co-hosts "More or Less" podcast with Sam Lessin, Dave Morin, and Brett Morin
- Sam describes her as "good at prompting us with what's actually going on in the world"
- Married to Sam Lessin since college; they have three children
