## Definition

Founder of tech-news publication [[The Information]] and wife of [[Sam Lesson]]. Referenced in this episode both as a source of etiquette feedback on Lesson's material and as co-host of his podcast, *More or Less*.

## Key Information

- Lesson describes her as good at "prompting us with what's actually going on in the world" on their podcast, after which he and his co-hosts react/"complain about it."
- He notes she and other women flagged that a forthcoming, separate "fem etiquette course" is needed to cover etiquette topics specific to women that the main book doesn't address.
- Lesson tells a story about the first time he met her (before they started dating): he used his own "great to see you" line on her despite clearly not remembering whether they'd met before — they had, and she remembered, making it a formative, slightly embarrassing anecdote he still cites.
- His children have picked up small etiquette habits around her specifically — e.g., their six-year-old now stands whenever she comes to the dinner table, an old-fashioned etiquette custom Lesson finds funny given its narrow, gendered origin.
