## Definition

Kickstarter is a crowdfunding platform on which Ryan L. Peterman launched his ergonomic-keyboard passion project.

## Key Information

- Ryan's ergonomic keyboard ("that I wish existed") raised the funds he needed within a day of launching on Kickstarter.
- He expects the keyboard project to lose him money in aggregate, framing the goal as passion rather than profit.
- He has been using the first sample off the assembly line for two weeks and plans to bring it to production at the highest possible quality bar, on track to ship early next year.
