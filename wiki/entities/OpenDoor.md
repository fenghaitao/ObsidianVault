## Definition

OpenDoor is an online real estate company (iBuyer) where Sherwin Wu previously worked, leading the team that built the home pricing model to determine how much to offer for houses.

## Key Information

- iBuyer (instant buyer) real estate company
- Sherwin Wu led the team building the pricing model to determine home offers
- Surprising variables in home pricing: high-voltage power lines significantly lower prices; floor plans are critically important but very hard to quantify; curb appeal and front door quality have high ROI
- Floor plans were not digitized — a handful of people had paper floor plans of homes in Phoenix and Dallas
- Zillow research found that front door replacement has the highest ROI among home improvements
