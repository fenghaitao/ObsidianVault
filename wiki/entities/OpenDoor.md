## Definition

Company [[Keith Rabois]] cites (alongside one other unnamed company on whose board he sits) as an example where the CMO is the organization's number-one consumer of AI tokens — evidence of intellectual curiosity being the trait that future-proofs executives in the AI era. [[Sherwin Wu]] previously worked here building home-pricing models.

## Key Information

- Per Sherwin Wu: he built the models estimating how much to pay for houses. Surprising price-influencing variables he found: proximity to high-voltage power lines; overall floor-plan quality (hard to quantify algorithmically — often only identifiable by an ops person physically walking the home); and curb appeal/front-door quality (citing a claim that front-door replacement has the highest ROI of any home improvement).
- Floor plans for many homes were not digitized; a small number of people held paper floor plans for large numbers of homes in markets like Phoenix and Dallas.
