## Definition

The Millennium Prize Problems are seven famously unsolved mathematical problems for which the Clay Mathematics Institute offers a one-million-dollar prize each for a correct proof.

## Key Information

- P vs NP is one of the Millennium Prize Problems: there is a million-dollar prize for a proof in either direction (equivalence or separation).
- Wigderson treats the prize as a minor motivator: "you don't work for the million dollar... a million dollar is nothing... for some of these problems."
- The Poincaré conjecture (another Clay Millennium problem) is cited as an example of a proof so complex it took years to verify and fix small bugs.
