## Definition

Friend of [[Sam Lesson]] and co-host, with Brett Morin and Lesson's wife [[Jessica Lessin]], of Lesson's low-key podcast *More or Less*.

## Key Information

- Lesson describes the podcast (named as a pun on "Lesson"/"the Morins") as essentially "talking to your friends for an hour a week" that they would do "even if no one was listening," recorded despite the group not living close together.
- Lesson and his wife are self-described contrarians who "hate everything by default," while the Morins "think everything is amazing" — a dynamic he says makes the show work.
- Lesson says the show is "weirdly useful" despite no growth strategy or monetization — friends and industry contacts pick up specific ideas from it and follow up, occasionally leading to business conversations.
