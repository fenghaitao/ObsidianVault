## Definition

Oded Goldreich is a cryptographer and co-author, with Silvio Micali and Avi Wigderson, of the GMW result that every provable statement has a zero-knowledge interactive proof.

## Key Information

- Wigderson describes the paper as "joined with Od [Oded] and Silvio Micali," his favorite paper, which showed zero-knowledge proofs are universal, not ridiculous.
- The result assumes one-way functions and uses three-colorability plus NP-completeness to extend zero-knowledge to everything provable.
