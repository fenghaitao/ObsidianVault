## Definition

Sam Lessin is a partner at Slow Ventures, former VP of Product at Facebook, a two-time founder, and author of a book on proper etiquette for Silicon Valley. He co-hosts the "More or Less" podcast.

## Key Information

- Partner at Slow Ventures (slow.co), a venture capital firm
- Previously VP of Product at Facebook
- Two-time founder (sold a company to Airbnb)
- Published a book on proper etiquette, teaching classes for founders (initially for YC founders)
- Co-hosts the "More or Less" podcast with Dave Morin, Brett Morin, and his wife Jessica Lessin (who runs The Information)
- Runs a newsletter with no public signup page — access via emailing lesson@gmail.com or using his bot at wlesson.com
- Built Letter Meme (lettermeme.com), an AI-powered personal news aggregator that turns newsletters into daily cartoons
- Philosophy: "funny but useful" — content should be at the intersection of hilarious and genuinely useful
- Contrarian view: seed venture capitalists investing in companies branded as "AI companies" will lose an impossibly large amount of money; AI is a powerful tool for existing businesses, not a great startup opportunity
- Famous for a viral rant against Calendly, which he considers disrespectful
- Has a poster in his gym reading "Carthage must burn" from the Facebook hackathon after Google Plus launch
- Recommended books: "Lessons from History" by Will Durant, "Man's Search for Meaning" by Viktor Frankl, "The Ancient City," "Area 51"
- Also recommended: "The Fish That Ate the Whale," "The Last Kings of Shanghai"
- Favorite TV show: Land Man
- Married to Jessica Lessin; has three children (ages 8, 6, 4)
- Email: lesson@gmail.com
