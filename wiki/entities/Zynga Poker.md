## Definition

[[Zynga]]'s poker game, the company's first product — a deliberately "embarrassingly small" starting point for [[Mark Pincus]] after the more ambitious failure of [[Tribe]].

## Key Information

- Pincus describes choosing to build "a poker game" (not even a broader Facebook app) as his humble restart after Tribe's failure — illustrating the "being less ambitious" principle that preceded Zynga's later scale.
- Along with [[Words With Friends]], proved more enduring than [[FarmVille]]/[[CityVille]] because it successfully transitioned to mobile.
