## Definition
Number 10 Data Science (10DS) is a team at 10 Downing Street, originally set up during the pandemic to ensure the most important decisions in the country are informed by the best possible evidence. It is now radically scaling up its AI engineering and development capability to drive AI adoption within Number 10 and across strategically important parts of the UK state.

## Key Information
- Set up during the pandemic, partly in response to it
- Core business: making sure the most important UK decisions are informed by the best evidence
- Scaling up AI engineering and development capability
- Operates as a small insurgent unit at the center of government
- Recruits exclusively outsiders with a ~0.7-0.8% acceptance rate technical selection process
- Pays market rates within reason, operates with unusually high autonomy and political backing
- Forward-deploys engineers into policy teams, operational teams, and other departments
- Has a fellowship program that places external talent into government
- Takes on both low-hanging fruit (internal builds in days/weeks) and complex system-wide challenges (partnership model)
