## Definition

The Cody Dieruf Foundation is a nonprofit organization based in Bozeman, Montana, that helps rural communities access healthcare. Jacob Warwick serves on the board and is personally connected through his son's cystic fibrosis diagnosis.

## Key Information

- Based in Bozeman, Montana
- Focus: helping rural parts of Montana get access to healthcare
- Jacob Warwick serves on the board
- Jacob's son has cystic fibrosis, which is a genetic condition
- Mission includes funding life-saving procedures like lung transplants
- Example: funded a life flight to Denver Children's Hospital for a 27-year-old with cystic fibrosis who needed a lung transplant
- The foundation's work exposed a tragic gap: a mother lost one son because she didn't know the foundation existed, then saved her second son through the foundation's support
