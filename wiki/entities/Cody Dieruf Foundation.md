## Definition

Nonprofit based in Bozeman, Montana, where [[Jacob Warwick]] serves on the board, helping rural Montana families access healthcare for conditions like cystic fibrosis (Warwick's son has the condition).

## Key Information

- Funded an emergency life-flight to Denver Children's Hospital that saved the life of a young man needing a lung transplant, after his mother (who had already lost one son to the same genetic condition) discovered the foundation existed — a story Warwick describes as core to his motivation for the work.
