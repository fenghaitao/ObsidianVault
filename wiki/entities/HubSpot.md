## Definition

Marketing software company cited by [[Jason Cohen]] as an example of a successful new-channel pivot: tested selling through agencies instead of only direct, which grew to roughly 50% of revenue within four to five years and became a major driver of continued growth.

### Per episode 35 (Jason Lemkin)

- Founder/chairman [[Brian Halligan]] built his own AI "digital clone" using [[Delphi]] before [[Jason Lemkin]] did for [[SaaStr]] — cited by Lemkin as a direct inspiration, particularly Halligan's choice to synthesize the collective voices of people he'd worked with rather than just his own.
- Cited by Lemkin alongside Salesforce as an example of an "old school B2B SaaS" company at billions in revenue that still has plenty of good inbound leads its human sales team fails to call back promptly.
