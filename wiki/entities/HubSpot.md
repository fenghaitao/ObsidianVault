## Definition

HubSpot is a leading CRM and marketing/sales platform for businesses. It is mentioned as an example of a large B2B SaaS company that, despite its scale, doesn't have so many great leads that it can call them all back — illustrating the "lead-rich vs lead-poor" dynamic even at large companies.

## Key Information

- Even at billions in revenue, HubSpot doesn't have so many great leads that they can call them all back
- Brian Halligan is the founder and chairman; he created a Deli digital clone while at Sequoia
- HubSpot represents the "old school" B2B SaaS world where growth has decelerated and demand has evaporated relative to the 2021 era
- Jason Lemkin's point: even HubSpot-level companies can benefit from AI agents because no human team can service all their leads
- The HubSpot CRM is one of the systems where companies store leads that AI agents can then work through
- Jason Cohen cites HubSpot as an example of creative channel expansion: they famously tested selling through agencies instead of direct, and it ended up being 50% of their revenue after 4-5 years
