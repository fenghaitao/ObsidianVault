## Definition

AI-driven dating app pitched by [[Sam Lesson]] in the lightning round (rendered as "June date" / "June and Dane" in the transcript; exact product name uncertain due to transcription ambiguity). Builds match profiles by analyzing a user's ChatGPT chat history rather than a traditional dating-app questionnaire.

## Key Information

- Premise, per Lesson: instead of another "virtual girlfriend"/loneliness chatbot play, the founders realized that ChatGPT chat histories are "one of the best sources of information" for matching real people — users respond to a structured prompt the app designed, which distills an "unbelievably good profile of who you are," and matches are made based on the implications of what people actually ask ChatGPT about.
- Lesson (happily married, not a user for dating purposes) says he pulled his own profile out of curiosity and found it "shockingly good" as a description of who he actually is.
- Described as functioning somewhat like "Tinder 2.0," with the app receiving one high-confidence match at a time rather than an endless swipe feed.
- Lesson connects the idea to an unrelated, unsuccessful matchmaking experiment he ran with his own newsletter subscribers (mismatched gender ratio killed the liquidity), suggesting this app might have enough scale/liquidity to make the model work where his own attempt didn't.
