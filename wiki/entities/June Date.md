## Definition

June Date is an AI-powered dating app that Sam Lessin recommends. It uses users' ChatGPT histories to build profiles and match people based on what they're actually asking AI about.

## Key Information

- Described as "Tinder 2.0" — uses ChatGPT histories as a source of information for matching
- Users ask ChatGPT a structured prompt designed by June Date; it pulls out a detailed profile of who they are
- Matching is based on the implications of what users actually ask ChatGPT about
- Sam Lessin tested his own profile and found it "shockingly good" at describing who he actually is
- Receives one high match each day
- Sam thinks this is a great example of a business that uses AI implications but is not an "AI company"
