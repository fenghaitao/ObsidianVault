## Definition

Khan Academy is a free online-learning platform that David J. Malan discusses as a stylistically different educational resource (with its own "cast of characters") from CS50.

## Key Information

- Malan sees healthy competition (e.g., Khan Academy) as good, driving different courses and teachers to try new things.
- He contrasts Khan Academy's early style — Sal Khan's handwritten style for his niece/nephew — with CS50's shared aesthetic and lecturing style.
- Charlie Marsh took a job at Khan Academy right after school; despite big-tech pay being better, the experience was what he wanted and later paid off, feeding the cross-ecosystem tooling insight behind Astral.
