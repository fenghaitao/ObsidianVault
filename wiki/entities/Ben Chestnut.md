## Definition

Ben Chestnut is the founder of Mailchimp. He gave a SaaStr talk titled "Everything That Breaks on the Way to One Billion" shortly after Mailchimp was acquired, which Jason Lemkin considers one of the best talks across all SaaStr events.

## Key Information

- Founder of Mailchimp
- Gave a SaaStr talk called "Everything That Breaks on the Way to One Billion" shortly after Mailchimp's acquisition
- The talk is praised by Jason Lemkin as one that "will change the way you think"
- Discussed how the Mailchimp acquisition deal almost fell apart
- Challenged almost every B2B metric: ran a profitable business at scale without really caring about CLTV, CAC, or standard metrics
- Focused on what really matters for customers
- The talk represents a rare moment of genuine challenge to conventional B2B thinking — "everyone's talking their book"
