## Definition
Guillaume Vernade is a Developer Advocate at Google DeepMind, focused on GenMedia models (image, video, music, and speech generation). He bridges the gap between developers and internal product teams, ensuring released AI products meet real-world developer needs.

## Key Information
- Developer Advocate at Google DeepMind for 2 years (as of May 2026)
- Previously worked at Google on Stadia, the game streaming platform (now defunct)
- Former video game producer before joining Google 6 years ago
- Primary focus: GenMedia models including Nano Banana, VEO, Lyria, and TTS
- Role includes creating documentation, code samples, demos, skills, and prompt guides
- Advocates internally for developers — fighting for API consistency across models (e.g., Imagen brand consolidation)
- Key concern: European data sovereignty and model availability on EU endpoints
- Presented at aiDotEngineer conference demonstrating book illustration using GenMedia suite
