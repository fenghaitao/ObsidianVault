## Definition

Michal Cichra is a software engineer at Safe Intelligence who previously worked at Microsoft and Red Hat, with 10 years of experience working on a single product. He presented at aiDotEngineer on capturing decisions for humans and AI alike.

## Key Information

- Currently at Safe Intelligence, where the team recently released Spec 27, a product to test agents
- Previously worked at Microsoft and Red Hat
- Spent 10 years working on a single product, giving him deep insight into long-term consistency problems in software
- Advocates for structured decision capture: ADRs for architecture decisions, PRDs for product requirements, BDD with Cucumber for executable specifications, and Design Systems for consistent UI
- Emphasizes that both humans and LLMs suffer from limited context — people forget and leave, LLMs have no memory and suffer context compacts
- Describes a reinforcement loop (git hooks, skills, CI, linters) to enforce rules automatically and give agents feedback at commit time
- Believes context compacts are not to be feared: sessions with 20-50 compacts are fine because important things survive and agents look them up again
