## Definition

EPFL (École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne) is a Swiss technical university in Lausanne where Scala creator Martin Odersky is a professor.

## Key Information

- Martin Odersky teaches at EPFL (transcribed in the podcast as "DPFL").
- The programming courses he taught, on Coursera and at EPFL, are partly based on Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs (the former MIT introductory text).
