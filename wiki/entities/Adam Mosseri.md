## Definition

Adam Mosseri is the head of Instagram whose TED talk on the future of decentralized creators seeded the project Sash helped explore on Instagram's blockchain team.

## Key Information

- Gave a TED talk arguing that a creator's followership should exist outside any single platform, with platforms merely choosing whether to recognize it
- The talk prompted Instagram to explore what decentralized following meant as a product, with Sash prototyping the technical side
- That initiative involved partnering with Google, Spotify, Shopify, and Nike on cross-company incentives before crypto winter shut the project down
