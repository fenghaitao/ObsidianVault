## Definition

"Punch" is a Broadway show Dr. Becky references, based on a true story about a man who accidentally killed someone in a bar fight and later connected with the victim's parents.

## Key Information

- True story about a man who punched someone at a bar, the person fell and died
- He later got to know the victim's parents after incarceration
- Parents asked him: "What are you going to do with your life now?"
- He said nobody had ever asked him that question or believed in him before
- Dr. Becky uses this story to illustrate MGI (Most Generous Interpretation): people aren't taking advantage — they're asking "Is there an adult here who will help me?"
