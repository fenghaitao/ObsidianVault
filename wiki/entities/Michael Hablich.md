## Definition
Michael Hablich is the Product Manager for Chrome DevTools at Google, a guest lecturer at a university near where he lives, and has 20 years of experience in tech spanning developer, tester, QA engineer, project manager, product manager, and program manager roles.

## Key Information
- Product Manager for Chrome DevTools at Google
- Guest lecturer at a university near his home
- 20 years of experience in tech across multiple roles: developer, tester, QA engineer, project manager, product manager, program manager
- Led the Chrome DevTools for Agents (MCP) project, which builds a purpose-built DevTools experience for AI coding agents
- Key insight: agents are a fundamentally different user class than humans — they share goals but have different cognitive bottlenecks
- Advocated for measuring agent interface efficiency via "tokens per successful outcome" rather than raw token counts
- Emphasized that friction can be intentional in agent UX, especially around security (autoconnect feature requires human consent every time)
- Available for hallway track conversations and LinkedIn connections at conferences
- Argues that "agents are our next users — let's help them help us"
