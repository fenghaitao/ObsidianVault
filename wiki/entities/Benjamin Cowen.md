## Definition
Benjamin Cowen is a Forward Deployed Machine Learning Engineer at Modal, where he works with a wide range of AI applications across physics simulations, quantum chemistry, voice processing, LLMs, and agents. He presented at AI Engineer on the emerging pattern of fine-tuning as a middle ground between frontier APIs and full model training.

## Key Information
- Role: Forward Deployed ML Engineer at Modal
- Works across diverse AI domains: physics simulations, quantum chemistry, voice processing, LLMs, agents
- Observed large-scale reinforcement learning as a rapidly growing area among Modal customers
- Core thesis: as companies mature and products specialize, fine-tuning becomes the natural next step for better performance and cost efficiency
- Advocates for preparing for fine-tuning now: collect data, develop evals, plan the transition
