## Definition

StaffEng.com is a website and resource about the Staff Engineer role, providing articles, guides, and frameworks for understanding engineering career ladders and the staff+ levels.

## Key Information

- Ryan L. Peterman referenced and displayed a chart from StaffEng.com to level-set what a Staff Engineer is
- The chart showed the engineering career ladder: entry level, mid-level, senior, then branching into IC track (staff, principal, distinguished) or management track
- Ryan noted he has no affiliation with the site but recommended it as a great resource
