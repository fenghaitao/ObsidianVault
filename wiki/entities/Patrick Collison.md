## Definition

Patrick Collison is co-founder/CEO of [[Stripe]], cited by [[Stewart Butterfield]] for an anecdote where, given rare access to [[Sundar Pichai]] at an industry event, he chose to complain about Gmail's poor UX for dragging email recipients between To/CC fields rather than discuss any higher-stakes business topic.

## Key Information

- Butterfield cites this as evidence that genuinely great product people are perpetually, specifically bothered by small software details most people would never think to complain about — a recurring theme he connects to his own obsessive product dissatisfaction.
