## Definition

Patrick Collison is the co-founder and CEO of Stripe, mentioned by Stewart Butterfield as an example of a product leader who is deeply irritated by poor UX — specifically, the difficulty of dragging recipients between To/CC fields in the Gmail app.

## Key Information

- At a conference, approached Sundar Pichai (CEO of Google) specifically to complain about the difficulty of dragging recipients between To and CC fields in Gmail
- This story illustrates how even the most successful product leaders are constantly unsatisfied with how products work
- Butterfield uses this as an example of how poor UX can create lasting irritation and emotional negative associations
