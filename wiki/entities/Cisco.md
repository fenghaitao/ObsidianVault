## Definition

Cisco is a critical infrastructure company for the AI era, with approximately 90,000 employees. It is transforming from a traditional networking company (known for routers and WebEx) into an AI-first platform company that plays a key role in the AI infrastructure buildout — connecting GPUs, providing network bandwidth, and building the backbone for AI at scale.

## Key Information

- ~90,000 employees; ~43,000 watched the internal AI summit stream
- CEO: Chuck Robbins
- CPO and President: Jeetu Patel (~30,000 people under him)
- Key products: networking hardware (routers, switches), WebEx (collaboration), AI infrastructure (networking for GPU clusters)
- Three areas where Cisco impacts AI's constraints: (1) Infrastructure (power, compute, network bandwidth), (2) Trust (safety and security for AI systems), (3) Data gap (AI training data)
- Underwent transformation from a holding company of 251+ acquisitions to a platform company ("loosely coupled but tightly integrated")
- Shifted from a walled garden to an open ecosystem approach
- Made a top-down commitment to being AI-first, accelerated after ChatGPT's launch in November 2022
- Hosted a 12-hour AI summit with Jensen Huang, Sam Altman, Marc Andreessen, Fei-Fei Li, and many other tech leaders
- Chuck Robbins' vision: "Not just an iconic company, but an iconic and innovative company"
