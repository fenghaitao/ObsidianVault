## Definition

Networking/infrastructure company (90,000 employees, ~$40-45B in product revenue), led by CEO [[Chuck Robbins]] with [[Jeetu Patel]] as Chief Product Officer and President. Recast over the past ~5.5 years as a critical AI infrastructure provider and "AI first" platform company.

## Key Information

- Provides the networking, optics, safety/security, observability, and data-platform technology that lets many GPUs (potentially across data centers hundreds of km apart) operate as one coherent, synchronized training cluster.
- Identifies as addressing (alongside compute/power itself) two other constraints on AI's growth: the "trust deficit" of non-deterministic AI systems, and the coming shortage of human-generated training data (driving growth in proprietary/synthetic/machine data, an area Patel says Cisco can play a major role in).
- Transformed from a "holding company" of 251 acquisitions and thousands of discrete products into a tightly-integrated platform company: customers don't have to buy everything, but products that are bought together are designed to "work like magic."
- Deliberately operates in an open ecosystem rather than a walled garden, willing to partner with direct competitors when that's what serves a shared customer.
- Hosted a 12-hour AI summit (guests including Jensen Huang, Sam Altman, Marc Andreessen, Fei-Fei Li, Kevin Weil, and the CEOs of Intel and AWS) shortly before this episode.
