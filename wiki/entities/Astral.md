## Definition
Astral is a Python developer-tools startup founded by Charlie Marsh, creator of Ruff and uv, that was acquired by OpenAI.

## Key Information
- Charlie left Spring Discovery to start a company with a friend (who left Meta); they explored an "idea maze" of ideas, but Charlie kept gravitating to developer tools and eventually focused on Ruff full-time.
- Raised seed, series A, and series B — all preemptive and investor-initiated; the A and B were never announced (only surfaced in the acquisition blog post).
- Business model: keep the open-source tooling free and non-commercial, then sell managed software that is the "natural next thing" — starting with a hosted private package registry with first-class uv support.
- Also built a curated Python/GPU distribution (like a Linux distribution) to solve PyTorch/CUDA install ergonomics; post-acquisition, parts of that platform can be released freely to grow the ecosystem.
- Ran as a remote-first company with a small, senior-heavy team (e.g., Andrew Gallant); Charlie favored spending minimal time fundraising and prioritizing a few trusted investors.
- Maintains an AI policy for contributions: retain useful human insight while filtering out agent-authored comments that add no value.
