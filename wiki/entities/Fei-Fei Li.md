## Definition

Fei-Fei Li is a prominent AI researcher and professor at Stanford University, known for her work in computer vision and as co-director of Stanford's Human-Centered AI Institute (HAI).

## Key Information

- Stanford professor and AI researcher
- Was a guest at Cisco's 12-hour AI summit, alongside Jensen Huang, Sam Altman, Marc Andreessen, and other tech leaders
- Co-director of Stanford's Human-Centered AI Institute (HAI)
