## Definition
Fei-Fei Li is an AI researcher whose company World Labs builds 3D world environments using a different approach than Google DeepMind's Genie 3, focusing on Unity/Unreal Engine-style asset generation.

## Key Information
- AI researcher and founder of World Labs
- World Labs takes a 3D environment generation approach to world model building
- Approach differs from Genie 3's pixel-by-pixel 2D frame generation
- Mentioned by Paige Bailey as an example of alternative approaches to world model building
