## Definition

AI researcher and founder of [[World Labs]], working on world models; a previous guest on [[Lenny's Podcast]], raised in episode 09 as a candidate direction for physically-grounded AI models.

## Key Information

- [[Lenny Rachitsky]] connects her work at World Labs to [[Caitlin Kalinowski]]'s stated need for AI models that understand physical properties like friction, weight, and contact for real [[CAD]] work.
- Kalinowski says she isn't familiar with the specifics of World Labs' work but is interested to learn more, viewing world models generally as likely part of, not the entire, solution for AI-assisted physical engineering.
