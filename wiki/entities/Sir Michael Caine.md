## Definition
Sir Michael Caine is a renowned British actor whose voice was used for an AI agent experience built by Joe Reeve: a red British phone booth (K6) where visitors could pick up the phone and talk to an agent voiced as Sir Michael Caine.

## Key Information
- Iconic British actor
- His voice was used for an ElevenLabs-powered AI agent inside a red K6 phone booth at the AIE event
- The phone booth experience was designed to put voice AI into the physical world rather than through a screen
- Part of Joe Reeve's experimentation with embedding voice AI into real-world objects
- Demonstrates the cultural and nostalgic appeal of using recognizable voices for AI agents
- Boris Starkov (Eleven Labs) built the phone booth experience by reverse engineering a Viking VOIP phone protocol with Claude Code, connecting the legacy hardware to Eleven Labs' conversational AI agent via Twilio
