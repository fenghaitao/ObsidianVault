## Definition

Butler Lampson (transcribed in the episode as "Butler Lamson") is a computer scientist and Turing Award winner who was one of the original researchers at Xerox PARC and the person who recognized the importance of Paxos.

## Key Information

- Was one of the two senior figures (with Chuck Thacker) at Xerox PARC building distributed personal computing.
- Recognized the importance of the Paxos algorithm, together with the idea that "you can implement anything because it's implementing a state machine," and helped evangelize building systems with Paxos and state machines.
- Leslie Lamport says Lampson "really deserved his Touring award" for his understanding of building systems.
- His recognition was one reason Lamport felt no urgency to publish Paxos for eight years.
