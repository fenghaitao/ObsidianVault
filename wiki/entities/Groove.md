## Definition

Groove is a SaaS company known for a widely-cited case study on cancellation surveys. Jason Cohen references their finding that changing the cancellation question from "Why did you cancel?" to "What made you cancel?" doubled the rate of usable responses (from 10% to 20%).

## Key Information

- Published a case study online about cancellation survey effectiveness
- Same email, same audience — only changed the question phrasing
- "Why did you cancel?" → 10% usable responses
- "What made you cancel?" → 20% usable responses
- The phrasing shift moves people from giving generic answers (budget, too expensive) to specific, actionable reasons (integration issues, missing features)
- This finding is a key part of Jason Cohen's cancellation diagnosis advice
