## Definition

[[Tesla]]'s humanoid robot, cited by [[Caitlin Kalinowski]] as one of a small number of companies/products "very ahead" in humanoid robotics, alongside [[Figure]] and [[1X Neo]].

## Key Information

- Grouped with Figure and 1X Neo as a leading humanoid robot program.
- Kalinowski's broader assessment: current humanoids, including Optimus, are still advanced prototypes rather than mass-deployed products; "at scale" for her means hundreds of thousands to millions of units, gated primarily by supply chain (actuators, magnets, silicon) and safety validation.
