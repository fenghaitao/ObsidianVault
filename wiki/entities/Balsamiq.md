## Definition
Balsamiq is a wireframing tool that Brendan O'Leary used early in his career to design UI mockups. He references it in an anecdote about providing proper context to junior engineers (and by analogy, AI agents).

## Key Information
- A wireframing tool that uses Comic Sans font and silly smiley face icons as placeholders
- Brendan used it to design a patient history form wireframe for an iPad app at a healthcare software company
- The anecdote: interns faithfully implemented the wireframe including Comic Sans and emoji placeholders because the spec didn't clarify what was placeholder vs. real — illustrating that the fault was Brendan's for not providing the right context
- This story serves as the foundation for Brendan's argument that AI agents, like junior engineers, need carefully curated context to produce correct output
