## Definition
Andrew Kelley is the creator of the Zig programming language.

## Key Information
- Charlie cites Kelley's data-oriented design talk as highly influential, reshaping how he looks at software — "these people really care about what they're doing."
- The talk covers design decisions in the Zig compiler and thinking about memory and allocation.
