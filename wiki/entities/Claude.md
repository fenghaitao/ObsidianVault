## Definition

Anthropic's AI assistant/coding agent (including Claude Code); central example in the episode of both AI's coding capability and its risk of producing brittle, under-architected software when used without human oversight.

## Key Information

- Reportedly writes 90-100% of Anthropic's own code, per Dario Amodei, as recounted by Fadell.
- Claude's/Anthropic's leaked source code was reportedly judged brittle and poorly layered by outside software architects, despite functioning — Fadell's key evidence for "cognitive surrender" risk.
- Fadell describes a constructive approach: use Claude Code for well-scoped, well-architected sub-segments (rather than trusting it to architect an entire system end-to-end), with a human still designing the overall architecture and reviewing security/maintainability.
- Referenced generically as "cloud code" / coding-agent tooling that can produce impressive prototypes but shouldn't replace human product, architecture, and marketing judgment.

### Episode 28 — Dr. Becky Kennedy

- [[Dr. Becky Kennedy]] (CEO of [[Good Inside]]) mentions using Claude (alongside [[ChatGPT]]) as part of her rapid-prototyping workflow, and says the way she gets the most out of prompting is a "vomiting" approach — dumping all her thoughts unfiltered rather than pre-organizing them into a neat package, which she notes especially helps women who've been conditioned to over-organize before speaking up.

### Episode 31 — Zevi Arnovitz

- [[Zevi Arnovitz]] used a Claude project as an interview coach when preparing for his Meta PM interview, explicitly instructing it not to be encouraging but to give unvarnished, effective feedback — sometimes asking it to play the candidate and give a model answer he could learn from.

### Episode 33 — Aishwarya Naresh Reganti and Kiriti Badam

- [[Lenny Rachitsky]] cites [[Dan Shipper]]'s claim that the top predictor of a company's AI success is whether its CEO personally chats with Claude or [[ChatGPT]] many times a day, illustrated by the [[Rackspace]] CEO's daily AI-catch-up habit (see [[AI Product Success Triangle]]).
