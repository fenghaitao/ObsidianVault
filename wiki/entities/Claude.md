## Definition

Claude is Anthropic's frontier AI model, known for excelling at coding and writing. Edwin Chen attributes its strengths largely to higher-quality training data and a more tasteful approach to post-training.

## Key Information

- Was significantly better at coding and writing than competitors for a long period, to the point where almost every AI coding product sat on top of Claude
- Demonstrates a perfectionist model behavior: it will iterate through 30+ versions of an email to craft the "perfect" version, even when the task doesn't warrant that level of effort
- Claude's "Artifacts" feature — generating mini-apps and UI within the chat — is cited by Edwin as an underhyped AI capability
- The quality gap between Claude and competitors was driven by better data, better post-training choices, and a more principled approach to objective functions
- Anthropic's constitutional classifiers have made Claude models significantly harder to jailbreak for SEAB burn information, but humans can still do it in under an hour
- Claude Code was recently hijacked by attackers who split malicious requests across separate instances to bypass the model's defenses
- Claude is considered among the best-defended models, but Sander Schulhoff argues no meaningful progress has been made on solving adversarial robustness overall
- Zevi Arnovitz describes Claude's personality as the "perfect CTO": very communicative, very smart, opinionated but collaborative
- Zevi prefers Claude because it doesn't just go with the flow — it challenges ideas and is opinionated rather than sycophantic
- Zevi uses Claude as his "dev lead" — the primary agent with the most project context
- Claude's behavior contrasts with ChatGPT's sycophantic tendency to agree with users even when they're wrong
- Zevi: "If regular ChatGPT was a CTO, that would be the CTO who goes along with your dumbest ideas"
- Jenny Wen says Claude is not yet hirable as a designer: "it is pretty good at a first pass and at presenting a bunch of different ideas to you, but nothing there quite feels like special and hirable yet"
- Jenny believes Claude's design sense will get better; designers may be "holding on to that a little bit too much"
- Nikhyl Singhal has been "pretty all in on Claude these last 3 months" for building — uses it for web properties, matching agents, recruiting automation, and content tools
- Nikhyl standardizes on one tool at a time rather than switching; finds Codex's highest reasoning level "quite advanced" but prefers to standardize
- Nikhyl's approach: "What can I do to obsolete anything and everything I do on a daily basis?" using Claude
