## Definition

Oscar Wilde is a famous writer and wit, traditionally credited with the quote "Be yourself. Everyone else is taken." Jason Cohen notes that there is no evidence Wilde actually said it, but no evidence of who did either.

## Key Information

- Famous writer and wit
- Traditionally attributed quote: "Be yourself. Everyone else is taken."
- Jason Cohen tried to verify the attribution for his book annotations and found no evidence Wilde said it — but also no evidence of who actually said it
- Jason uses this as his life motto
