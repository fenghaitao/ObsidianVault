## Definition
Laurie Voss is Head of Developer Experience at Arize AI. He previously co-founded npm Inc., where he was known for advocating JavaScript. At Arize, he focuses on how to test AI systems and build AI systems that actually work, delivering hands-on workshops on agent evaluation.

## Key Information
- Head of Developer Experience at Arize AI
- Co-founded npm Inc. in a former life — "some of you may remember me from when I used to talk incessantly about JavaScript. Now, I talk incessantly about AI."
- Delivered a comprehensive workshop at AIE Code Summit on evaluating AI agents using Phoenix
- Emphasizes practical, hands-on approach: reading traces, writing evals based on observed failures, iterating systematically
- Advocates for evals as tests for a "brave new world of applications that are very hard to test"
- Demystifies ML jargon: traces are logs, evals are tests, rubrics are just prompts
- Philosophy: don't just hand-wave "look at your data" — actually read traces on a regular basis
- Uses Claude Haiku as agent model because it's "reliably dumb" (makes mistakes to test against) and Claude Sonnet as judge
- Active on Bluesky as @seldo.com
