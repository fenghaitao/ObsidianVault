## Definition

[[Anthropic]] team member who shapes [[Claude]]'s "character" — cited by [[Cat Wu]] as one of the people she trusts most for high-quality model/harness evaluation feedback.

## Key Information

- Wu describes character design as a harder job than coding a feature, since success in coding is verifiable while crafting a character requires strong personal conviction about who Claude should be, plus the ability to clearly articulate what's working and what isn't.
- Credited (alongside the broader Claude team) with Claude's positive, low-ego, confident-but-earnest personality, which Wu and Cat Wu's colleagues consider core to why people enjoy working with Claude Code and Cowork, not a cosmetic detail.
