## Definition

Attention is an AI-powered RevOps tool (alongside competitor Momentum) that automatically tracks every action a salesperson takes and records it in the CRM in real time, creating full transparency into sales activity.

## Key Information

- One of two similar RevOps AI tools SaaStr uses (the other being Momentum)
- Automatically tracks every single thing a human does in real time — every communication, every action
- Both tools are described as "great" and "very similar"
- Creates complete transparency into sales rep activity, ending the era of reps who can "phone it in"
- Part of the broader trend of AI bringing radical transparency to sales organizations
