## Definition

Law firm [[Eric Ries]] says he helped start, in part out of frustration with traditional lawyers discouraging founders from adopting mission-protective corporate structures.

## Key Information

- Does not charge founders by the hour (unlike traditional law firms, whose hourly billing Ries suggests can create a disincentive to help founders quickly implement mission-protective provisions).
- Ries notes the firm's main line of business is actually AI-assisted back-office acceleration, with governance/mission-protective advisory as a secondary, personally-motivated offering.
- Recommended by Ries as a resource for founders whose own lawyers are reluctant to help implement mission-protective provisions or public benefit corporation filings.
