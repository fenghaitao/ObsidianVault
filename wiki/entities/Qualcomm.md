## Definition
Qualcomm is a semiconductor company and hardware partner for Google AI Edge, providing NPU-accelerated platforms for on-device AI inference including IoT/robotics development platforms and mobile chipsets like the Snapdragon used in Samsung Galaxy S25.

## Key Information
- Semiconductor company and Google AI Edge hardware partner
- Provides NPU-accelerated platforms for on-device AI inference
- IoT/robotics development platform used for Gemma 4 benchmarking with NPU acceleration
- Snapdragon chipsets (e.g., in Samsung Galaxy S25) used for on-device model demos
- FastVLM (500M params, Apple) demonstrated running real-time video scene description on Qualcomm hardware
- Google AI Edge continues to optimize LiteRT-LM performance with Qualcomm as a partner
