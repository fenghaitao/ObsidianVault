## Definition

Merge is a unified-API integrations platform, presented as a sponsor of this [[Lenny's Podcast]] episode; positioned as "Plaid, but for everything B2B SaaS."

## Key Information

- Lets B2B SaaS companies embed a single API to ship 220+ customer-facing integrations across accounting, HR, ticketing, CRM, and file-storage systems.
- Cited customers: Merall AI and [[Ramp]].
- Ad mentions a new product supporting secure deployment of connectors to AI agents, so companies can power AI workflows with real customer data (automatic onboarding, "AI ready" data pipelines).
