## Definition

Evan Tana is a partner at SPC who originated the Legibility Framework — a 2x2 matrix of illegible vs. legible founders and ideas. Jenny Wen uses this framework to think about how designers can spot and transform illegible ideas.

## Key Information

- Partner at SPC (a VC firm)
- Originated the Legibility Framework shared on Twitter
- Framework: founders can be illegible or legible, ideas can be illegible or legible
- If both founder and idea are legible, the idea is probably not novel and someone is already doing it
- The most interesting opportunities are where the idea is illegible (on the frontier, people might not get it yet)
