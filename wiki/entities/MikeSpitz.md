## Definition
Mike Spitz is an engineering leader at PFF who presented a case study on building a "post-engineer" engineering organization at the aiDotEngineer conference. He led a two-engineer team that achieved 25x deployment frequency and 10x output using AI coding agents.

## Key Information
- **Role**: Engineering leader at PFF
- **Company**: [[PFF]] — sports data company serving NFL/NCAA teams and consumers
- **Presentation**: "Agents Don't Do Standups: Building the Post-Engineer Engineering Org" at [[aiDotEngineer]]
- **Case Study Duration**: January to March 2026
- **Key Achievement**: Two engineers using [[ClaudeCode]] delivered features in under 2 months that were previously estimated at 4 months
- **Approach**: Started experimenting with AI coding agents in November 2025, then scaled to a formal case study with the two strongest engineers
- **Philosophy**: Eliminated Scrum and traditional Agile ceremonies; replaced with bi-daily huddles, spec-driven development, and autonomous QA
- **Engineering Culture**: Emphasizes encoding engineering patterns as composable skills, slow phased AI adoption, and using agents for work engineers hate (style reviews, ticket management)
