## Definition

Workplace is Meta's internal version of Facebook, used for company-wide communication, sharing work updates, and building visibility within the organization. It was referenced by Rahul as a key channel for communicating impact at Meta.

## Key Information

- Rahul described Workplace as "basically like an internal version of Facebook"
- Used at Meta for posting updates about work and keeping people informed
- Rahul's technique: before posting anything, check the membership of the Workplace group to understand the audience (engineers, leadership, cross-functional mix)
- Tailoring communication depth based on the Workplace group's composition is a key skill for building visibility
- Workplace is a concrete example of how visibility is built at large tech companies through internal communication platforms
