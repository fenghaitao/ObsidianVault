## Definition

Steve Jobs was the co-founder and former CEO of Apple. Brian Attwell recounts a run-in with Jobs during his first-year undergraduate internship at Apple.

## Key Information

- Brian, interning at Apple as a first-year undergrad, built a tweak/fork of the OS that made phones easier to use in sunlight
- His mentor (Ken) loudly praised the prototype in a cafeteria, drawing Jobs over to look
- Brian downplayed it ("it's 50–100 lines of code, it wasn't even that hard"), and Jobs sighed, rolled his eyes, and walked away without saying anything
- Ken explained Brian had lost a chance to impress the icon and likely killed the project's prospects (the team had no funding for it)
- Brian's takeaway: he didn't know how to pitch and was an awkward 18–19 year old, so "there was very little chance that was ever going to go right"
