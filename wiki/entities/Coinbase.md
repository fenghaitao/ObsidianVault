## Definition
Coinbase is a cryptocurrency exchange company where CEO Brian Armstrong aggressively mandated AI tool usage, firing an engineer who didn't comply within a week.

## Key Information
- CEO Brian Armstrong sent an email requiring all engineers to use AI tools
- Fired an engineer a week later who didn't comply
- Engineers have base salaries of $300-400K plus equity
- The firing sent a clear message and everyone started using AI tools
- Example of top-down AI adoption pressure in Big Tech
