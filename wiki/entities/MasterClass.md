## Definition

MasterClass is an online education platform where celebrities and experts teach courses. Amol Avaisary previously worked in growth at MasterClass.

## Key Information

- Online education platform featuring celebrity and expert instructors
- Amol Avaisary previously worked in growth at MasterClass
- MasterClass's purchase flow includes a quiz (questions about interests, what you're here for) — adds friction that was thoroughly tested and proved to be a significant revenue driver
- The quiz helps users feel the product is for them by understanding their interests and recommending relevant content
- Amol cites this as an example of "good friction" in onboarding
