## Definition

Online education/subscription company where [[Amol]] worked on growth before joining [[Anthropic]]; case study for his "[[Good Friction]]" philosophy.

## Key Information

- Its purchase flow includes a multi-question quiz ("what are you here for?") before checkout, which looks like unnecessary friction but was thoroughly tested and found to be a significant revenue driver — it helps users feel the product/content selection is tailored specifically to them.
- Cited by Amol as directly inspiring a similar quiz pattern later adopted by a former colleague at [[Calm]] (the meditation app).
