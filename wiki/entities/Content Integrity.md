## Definition

Content Integrity is a Meta organization whose mission was to build machine learning models to identify and take action on violating content, covering everything from hate speech to graphic violence to suicide to terrorism to child exploitation imagery to pornography.

## Key Information

- The org started with pornography as the main focus — making sure the site didn't have any pornography
- Expanded to cover many violation types: hate speech, graphic violence, suicide, terrorism, child exploitation imagery (CEI)
- The organization grew rapidly during Evan King's tenure, splitting into multiple teams
- Had a culture of young, relatable engineers
- The Seattle office team was hidden on the second floor in the corner so that nobody would walk past desks and see disturbing content on monitors
- Evan King joined the terrorism team within Content Integrity, which was a brand new team being moved from the London office
- The terrorism team eventually split into three teams: terrorism, child exploitation imagery, and graphic violence
- Content Integrity was an "integrity" org within the larger Integrity organization at Meta (several thousand people)
- The org was deeply fulfilling for Evan because he felt he was having a real-world impact fighting harmful content
