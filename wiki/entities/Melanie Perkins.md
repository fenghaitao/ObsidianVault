## Definition

Melanie Perkins is CEO and co-founder of [[Canva]], a prior [[Lenny's Podcast]] guest cited by [[Lenny Rachitsky]] for having been rejected by roughly 100 investors before finally securing funding.

## Key Information

- [[Stewart Butterfield]] distinguishes her perseverance from generic "never give up" advice: she never wavered on her belief in the underlying product/vision, and was only refining her pitch/articulation to investors — a different situation from persisting with a fundamentally unproven idea.
