## Definition

Melanie Perkins is the CEO and co-founder of Canva, mentioned as an example of perseverance in fundraising.

## Key Information

- Went through a hundred investor rejections before securing investment for Canva
- Butterfield distinguishes this perseverance from pivoting: she always believed in the product concept and vision
