## Core Summary

The Code with Claude London 2026 opening keynote, delivered by Boris (Anthropic), framed the exponential growth of AI capabilities against linear business adoption. Key themes: the collapsing distance between idea and execution, Anthropic as a platform company, and three product layers (models, Managed Agents, Claude Code). Lisa from research covered model evolution from Claude 3 through Opus 4.7, the advisor strategy for cost-efficient frontier intelligence, and advice to design for the next model version. Angela and Caitlyn introduced Claude Managed Agents features including self-hosted sandboxes and MCP tunnels.

## Key Points

- Boris's personal story: learning to code on a TI-83 calculator; the "calculator feeling" of bringing ideas to life is returning at scale.
- **Model evolution:** 17 Claude versions shipped; Opus 4.7 can own outcomes end-to-end with judgment on ambiguous tasks. Eight frontier models in 12 months.
- **Advisor strategy:** split execution (smaller model) from advising (larger model); Sonnet + Opus advisor performed better and cheaper than Sonnet alone. Eve Legal got frontier quality at 5x lower cost.
- **Task horizon:** models went from minutes (last year) to hours (today) to expected continuous operation (future).
- **Scaffolding warning:** as models get smarter, complex harnesses can hold them back; simpler primitives (filesystem, sandbox) often work better.
- **Customer stories:** Spotify (1000+ PRs/month, 90% faster migrations), Binti (20 days off foster family licensing process).
- **Managed Agents updates:** self-hosted sandboxes (Cloudflare, Modal, Vercel), MCP tunnels for private server access, multi-agent orchestration, outcomes with rubric-based iteration.
