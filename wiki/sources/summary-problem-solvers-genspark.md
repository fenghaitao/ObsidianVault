## Core Summary

Kay Zhu, CEO of Genspark, discusses building an all-in-one AI workspace (spreadsheets, slides, documents) for white-collar workers. The partnership with Anthropic is characterized by curiosity and openness — every new model is an "invitation to the party." Key philosophy: there are no moats in the traditional sense because everything moves so fast; the only moat is team culture and execution speed. The sum of secrets kept today will be worthless tomorrow. Deep mutual trust and tight feedback loops define successful partnerships.

## Key Points

- **Genspark:** All-in-one AI workspace — spreadsheets, slides, documents for white-collar workers.
- **Partnership style:** Every new model is an invitation to the party; small startups can't do everything alone.
- **No traditional moats:** Everything moves so fast that only team culture and execution speed matter.
- **Openness philosophy:** "The sum of the secret you kept today will be worthless tomorrow."
- **Trust and feedback:** Deep mutual trust and tight feedback loops define successful partnerships.
- **Future:** "I don't know what Genspark will be in 3 years. A lot of new things will happen and it will even seem like magic."
