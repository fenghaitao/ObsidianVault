## Core Summary

Scott Wu, CEO of Cognition (creator of Devin, the first AI software engineer), discusses building tools that help institutions build software 10x faster. A former world champion competitive programmer, Scott describes the collaborative partnership with Anthropic — from early conversations where the concept was immediately understood to ongoing transparency as both companies build overlapping products. Key philosophy: there won't be a single product that does all of software engineering; there are many experiences to serve, and more to do together than separately.

## Key Points

- **Cognition/Devin:** First AI software engineer; serves banks, health insurers, governments, private equity firms.
- **Mission:** Help build software around the world 10x faster.
- **Partnership origin:** Early conversations with Dario were immediately understood — no pitching needed.
- **Co-opetition philosophy:** No single product will do all of software engineering; more to do together than separately.
- **Transparency:** Anthropic has been transparent about product plans even as both build overlapping tools.
- **Vision:** A world where building software is as easy as telling your computer what to do.
