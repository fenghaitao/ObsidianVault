## Core Summary

HubSpot describes using Claude across customer success and internal development. Claude's "taste" in marketing content was the early differentiator — outputs felt uniquely on-brand. A CSM used Claude to analyze a hospitality business's revenue patterns, identifying best/worst performing weekends — analysis far beyond the typical CSM role. Internally, Claude Code reduced codebase maintenance time, accelerated idea-to-prototype cycles, and increased productivity by 40%. The shared mission: giving businesses access to tools and information previously impossible.
