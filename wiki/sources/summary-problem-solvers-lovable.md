## Core Summary

Anton Osika, CEO of Lovable, discusses building a platform that lets anyone create software through conversation. The partnership with Anthropic evolved from customer-vendor to collaborative building, with dedicated Anthropic team members working alongside Lovable. Key themes: the generational shift in software creation, the underrated importance of trust in AI, and unlocking human agency by removing friction from starting companies and building products. Lovable's value of "care deeply" drives obsession with customer needs and product craft.

## Key Points

- **Lovable:** Platform for anyone to build software through conversation — unlocking new economies.
- **Partnership evolution:** From customer-vendor to collaborative building with dedicated Anthropic team members.
- **Trust as underrated moat:** A trusted brand that people love and return to is very rare in AI and not magically achieved.
- **Generational shift:** Frictions for starting companies and building products are disappearing — fundamentally more human agency unlocked.
- **Values-driven:** "Care deeply" — constant obsession with customer needs and product craft.
