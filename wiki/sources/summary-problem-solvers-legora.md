## Core Summary

Max Junestrand, CEO of Legora, discusses building the workspace where legal work happens — whether by humans, agents, or in collaboration. Claude models, especially Opus, excel at fine legal drafting because they match the style of existing documents and individual lawyers. Each new model release unlocks new capabilities, and Legora designs its system to improve with every release. The legal industry is profoundly changing because no prior technology could work with language and reasoning like LLMs. Differentiation comes from having the highest velocity in the industry.

## Key Points

- **Legora:** Unified workspace for legal work — reviews, drafting, research — by humans and agents collaboratively.
- **Claude for legal drafting:** Opus extraordinarily good at fine drafting, matching document style and individual lawyer style.
- **Rising tide:** Each new model unlocks new capabilities; Legora builds boats on the rising tide so the system improves with every release.
- **Industry transformation:** No technology before has worked with language and reasoning like LLMs — profoundly changing one of the oldest professions.
- **Differentiator:** Highest velocity in the legal tech industry — if a firm goes all-in on Legora, Legora goes all-in on them.
