## Core Summary

Lidia from Anthropic's Claude Code team announces Claude Tag, a feature that lets Claude collaborate alongside teams in Slack-style channels. Claude has its own account with scoped permissions per channel — it can see contract information in legal channels, edit codebases in engineering channels, but cannot cross boundaries. Claude Tag opens 65% of Anthropic's product pull requests. Memory respects channel boundaries, so what Claude learns in a private channel stays there. The feature enables multiplayer collaboration where Claude reacts to product decisions in real time, opens PRs, and keeps teams moving without cross-team communication lag.

## Key Points

- **Claude Tag:** Claude collaborates in team channels with its own account and scoped permissions.
- **65% of PRs:** Claude Tag opens 65% of Anthropic's product pull requests.
- **Scoped access:** Per-team, per-channel permissions — legal channel sees contracts, engineering channel edits codebase. Claude literally cannot see cross-channel content.
- **Memory boundaries:** What Claude learns in private channels or DMs stays there.
- **Multiplayer workflow:** Team members tag Claude, Claude reacts to product decisions in real time, opens PRs, lands changes, and keeps teams moving.
- **Autonomous actions:** Claude makes edits directly (e.g., Google Drive) with its own accounts and permissions.
