## Core Summary

A channel-update video where [[ColeMedin]] explains a content shift toward **live streaming** (Mon/Thu/Sat, 9am Central) and depth over chasing Claude-release hype. Low durable-knowledge value, but it states his content philosophy and introduces the **Dark Factory** experiment.

## Key Points

- **The "Claude hype" critique**: most AI YouTube now rushes the same video within hours of every new model/feature/plugin. Cole respects those creators but wants to stay deeper — engineering principles and real systems, not news churn — even though the algorithm favors hype.
- **Why news videos matter less**: you can often just **ask Claude Code how a new feature works** — it has an internal skill to search its own up-to-date documentation (e.g. for newly-released `/by-the-way`, the "dreaming" memory system, "routines"). So many release-reaction videos are skippable.
- **Content cadence**: keep ~one polished video/week *plus* multiple weekly live streams (1.5–2.5h) of live building; streams auto-post to the channel's live tab; may later cut polished clips from streams. He still covers major releases (e.g. Karpathy's LLM-Wiki, Claude Agent Teams) — but after tinkering and integrating them, not within 4 hours.
- **What he builds live**: **[[Archon]]** (his open-source harness builder) and the **Dark Factory** — an experiment where a codebase is handed *entirely* to AI coding assistants, with **no human allowed to review or write any code** (a maximal test of agent autonomy).
