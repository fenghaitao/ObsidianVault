## Core Summary

Brian Casel traces the AI coding timeline (2022-2025) and identifies five skills that give experienced builders an unfair advantage: product management, spec creation, systems architecture, context engineering, and workflow orchestration. The core message: experience matters more than ever, just applied differently — as architects directing agents rather than coders writing lines.

## Key Points

- Timeline: 2022-23 (tab completion/skepticism) → 2024 (agent mode, vibe coding split) → early 2025 (Claude Code, spec-driven development).
- Five skills: (1) Product Management — knowing what to build and why; (2) Spec Creation — shaping specs through AI Q&A; (3) Systems Architecture — choosing right patterns; (4) Context Engineering — managing what agents have in context; (5) Workflow Orchestration — knowing which tool/model for which situation.
- "Our experience matters now more than ever because our bottleneck is no longer writing code."
- AI can implement any pattern but can't choose which is right — that's the human craft.
