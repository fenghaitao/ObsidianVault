## Core Summary

Brian Casel builds the same invoicing app twice — once with Claude Opus 4.5, once with Google Gemini 3 — to compare frontier models. Both produced functional apps with minor bugs. His conclusion: frontier models are converging; the gap between them matters less than the builder's skill at directing them. Product instincts, architectural decisions, and taste are the real unfair advantage.

## Key Points

- Same spec, same requirements, head-to-head build of an invoicing app.
- Opus 4.5: mostly functional, one bug (line items not saving on draft).
- Gemini 3: more bugs (search not wired, client creation error, UI overlap), but fixable.
- Both used the front-end design skill from Anthropic for UI.
- Key takeaway: models are converging — the builder's skill matters more than model choice.
- A proper spec-driven approach would have prevented most issues; this was an abbreviated speedrun.
