## Core Summary

Nikhyl Singhal, former Meta/Google exec and founder of the Skip community, delivers a stark assessment of the product management profession: the "information mover" PM is going extinct, while builders are entering a renaissance. In the next 12-24 months, companies will shed massive staff and rehire smaller, AI-first teams. The key skill is "judgment" -- deciding what to build and whether changes are good. Builders are having the time of their lives with comps at all-time highs, while non-builders who don't love sitting down and making things are in serious trouble. He estimates roughly half of PMs fall into each camp.

## Key Points

- The "information mover" PM -- whose day was moving information between stakeholders -- is becoming a dinosaur
- Builders are in a renaissance: direct connection between ideas and impact, compensation at all-time highs, more offers than ever
- Non-builders are in trouble: if you don't love building stuff, you're in the wrong industry
- In 12-24 months: massive shedding of staff, then massive rehiring of AI-first people (e.g., shed 30K, hire 8K)
- "Judgment" is the paramount skill: evaluating whether changes are good, what to build, what's differentiated
- PMs are now getting CHRO, CEO, and founder roles -- product judgment is becoming a cross-functional superpower
- Everyone will have access to the best software engineers (Claude Code, Codex) -- all bad software will get fixed
- The industry is exhausted: nothing is constant, everyone is in a state of alert
- Despite the narrative, PM job openings are at a 3+ year high
- Founding CEO is now open to PMs: 14 founders in Skip community vs. 1 just a few years ago
