## Core Summary

Michele Catasta, President and Head of AI at Replit, discusses making coding accessible to everyone — users express ideas in natural language and see them turn into applications. Replit chose Claude (starting with Sonnet 3.5 in early 2024) because Anthropic consistently leads in coding model quality. The partnership includes early research preview access, enabling same-day launches (Sonnet 3.7 + Replit Agent V2). Replit has crossed 40 million registered users. The core mission: removing the complexity barrier so people who never felt permission to create software can now go from zero to one.

## Key Points

- **Replit:** Natural language to application — users don't feel they're writing code, just creating ideas.
- **Model choice:** Sonnet 3.5 (early 2024) was the inflection point; Anthropic has kept the lead in coding models since.
- **Partnership:** Early research preview access enables same-day launches and marketing pops.
- **40 million users:** Crossed 40 million registered users.
- **Life-changing impact:** People who never felt permission to create software are now building businesses.
- **Engine analogy:** Models are the car engine; Replit builds the entire car around it.
