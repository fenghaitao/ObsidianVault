## Core Summary

Michael Truell, CEO of Cursor, discusses the company's mission to be the best place to work with coding agents for both professional engineers and non-engineers. Cursor grew from 15 to 700 people in two years, now serving over 60% of the Fortune 500. The partnership with Anthropic is described as deep and productive, with tight alignment between Cursor's product development and Anthropic's model capability increases. Key differentiator: deep product focus from the start, giving users more autonomy and empowerment.

## Key Points

- **Cursor mission:** Best place to work with coding agents for professionals and non-engineers alike — approachable yet powerful.
- **Growth:** 15 to 700 people in 2 years; serves 60%+ of Fortune 500; rivals biggest public software companies.
- **Anthropic partnership:** Deep, committed, principled. Anthropic's platform commitment enables lasting businesses to be built on top.
- **Product-model alignment:** Tight productive alignment between Cursor's product work and Anthropic's model capability increases.
- **Differentiator:** Deep product focus — giving users autonomy and empowerment.
