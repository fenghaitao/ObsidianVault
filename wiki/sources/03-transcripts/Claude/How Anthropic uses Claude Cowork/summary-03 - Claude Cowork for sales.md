## Core Summary

Brittany, a growth account executive at Anthropic, demonstrates how Claude Cowork transforms sales preparation and follow-up. The account strategy builder skill pulls data from Salesforce, data warehouse, call recordings, Slack, email, and the web in parallel, synthesizing everything into a full account brief (spend, stakeholder mapping, models used, open deals, risk signals) in minutes instead of hours. Post-meeting, the call transcript processor generates personal action items, an internal Slack summary, and a customer follow-up — each requiring approval before sending. The 30-minute post-meeting flow is reduced to minutes, with higher quality than memory-based notes.

## Key Points

- **Account strategy builder skill:** Parallel data pulls from Salesforce, warehouse, call recordings (90 days), Slack, email, web. Synthesizes into full account brief with spend, stakeholders, models, deals, and risk signals.
- **Skill creation:** Describe what you'd want to know walking into a meeting; Claude drafts a skill file (plain text, verifiable) defining data sources, usage signals, and presentation format.
- **Pre-meeting impact:** Full account landscape before first conversation enables strategic first impression instead of orientation meeting.
- **Post-meeting workflow:** Call transcript processor generates three outputs: personal action items, internal Slack message (key takeaways, next steps, action owners), customer follow-up. Each requires approval before sending.
- **Time savings:** Post-meeting flow reduced from 30 minutes to minutes; more thorough than memory-based notes.
- **Quality improvement:** Every customer conversation is better because the AE shows up with real context.
