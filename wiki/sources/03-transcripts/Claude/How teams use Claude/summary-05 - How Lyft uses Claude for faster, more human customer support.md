## Core Summary

Lyft adopted Claude for customer support during a period of rapid rider/driver growth that overwhelmed existing support systems. Claude's personality was the differentiator — support interactions felt more organic, with customers opening up more about their issues. Resolution time decreased by 87% (30+ minutes to seconds in some cases). Millions saved were reinvested into upskilling support agents, avoiding burnout, and focusing human care on issues requiring empathy.

## Key Points

- **Problem:** Rapid rider/driver growth overwhelmed support queues; long wait times for issue resolution.
- **Claude differentiator:** Personality — more organic support interactions, customers conversed more and opened up about issues.
- **87% resolution time decrease:** 30+ minute issues resolved in seconds in some cases.
- **Cost savings:** Millions saved, reinvested into agent upskilling, burnout prevention, and human-care issues.
- **Transformational shift:** AI handles routine issues; humans focus on empathy and care.
