## Core Summary

Mark (Anthropic product lawyer) and Anna (Freshfields global co-head of AI) discuss how Claude Cowork transforms legal work. Beyond basic chat, Cowork enables lawyers to upload large bodies of documents (memos, product roadmaps, legislation) and have Claude synthesize across them to create new artifacts like Word documents, Excel spreadsheets, and PowerPoints. The conversation highlights the "context gap" challenge — engineering teams move at the speed of light while legal specs constantly change — and how AI helps bridge it. Core theme: AI handles information synthesis while human judgment keeps the rudder straight.

## Key Points

- **Beyond chat:** Cowork enables creating Word docs, Excel spreadsheets, and PowerPoints — the file types knowledge workers actually use.
- **Context gap challenge:** Engineering teams move fast; legal specs change daily. Claude synthesizes across large bodies of documents to bridge the gap.
- **Freshfields scale:** 5,700 staff and lawyers worldwide using AI. The question is how to bring all separate AI efforts to the center.
- **Human judgment:** "Our judgment is the thing that keeps the rudder straight" — AI handles information processing, humans handle risk navigation.
- **Anthropic's "light and shade" value:** Acknowledging both the risk and promise in AI technology.
