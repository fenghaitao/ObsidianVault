## Core Summary

Swyx (co-founder of AI Engineering) presents 6 things to know about AIE World's Fair 2026: biggest event yet, expo-focused hallway track, 50% new topics (auto research, inference, post-training, memory, verticals), extra day on July 2, sponsor perks worth $38K, and the vision of a "buffet" of curated content.

## Key Points

- Largest AIE ever — roughly 10x the size of World's Fair 2024. 15-person team organizing 600+ attendees.
- Expo-focused: talks are optional/recorded; hallway track is the real value. Four Expo stages.
- 50% evergreen topics, 50% completely new: auto research, inference track, post-training/pre-training (data quality), memory/continual learning, verticals.
- Extra day July 2 — "some of the best stuff." Don't fly out early.
- Sponsor offers worth $38K in attendee perks.
- YouTube growth: World's Fair 2024 content driving 10x audience expansion.
