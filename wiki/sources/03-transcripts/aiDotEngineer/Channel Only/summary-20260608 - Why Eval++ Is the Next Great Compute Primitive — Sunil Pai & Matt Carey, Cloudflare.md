## Core Summary

Sunil Pai and Matt Carey from Cloudflare present their journey building AI agents on Cloudflare's infrastructure, centered on Durable Objects as the stateful compute primitive. They discuss eval++ as the next compute primitive — running evaluations at the edge as a fundamental infrastructure service.

## Key Points

- Cloudflare's agent infrastructure built on Durable Objects: stateful compute at the edge with built-in database capabilities.
- Durable Objects provide strong consistency, low latency, and automatic state management — ideal for agent harnesses.
- Eval++ as a compute primitive: evaluations should be as fundamental as compute, storage, and networking.
- Cloudflare's edge network (300+ cities) enables globally distributed agent execution.
- Team based in London and Lisbon, building agents for over a year.
