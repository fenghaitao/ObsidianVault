## Core Thesis

The most important problem in technology today is AI, and for applied AI practitioners, the most important problem is code. The AI Engineering Code Summit is a cross-industry, single-track event designed to bring the best people together to push the entire AI coding industry forward.

## Summary

Jed Borovik, Gemini assistant at Google and lead of the Jules coding agent product engineering team, opens the 2025 AI Engineering Code Summit in New York. He frames the event through Richard Hamming's famous "You and Your Research" question: "What's the most important problem in your field, and why aren't you working on it?" For technology professionals, the answer is AI; for applied AI practitioners, the answer is code.

Borovik distinguishes the AI Engineering Conference's two formats: the larger World's Fair and the smaller, single-track Summit. This Summit is intentionally intimate, designed to convene the world's best minds around a single theme: AI coding. The previous day featured a leadership track on how AI is transforming software organizations; today focuses on the patterns, systems, and products that make that transformation possible.

He acknowledges sponsors, highlighting Google DeepMind as the presenting sponsor (noting the recent launch of Gemini 3 and Nano Banana Pro), Anthropic as platinum sponsor, and multiple gold and silver sponsors with expo booths.
