## Core Summary

Eoin Mulgrew from the Number 10 Data Science team (10DS) presents how a small insurgent unit at the center of UK government is driving AI adoption across the state. Operating with unusual political backing, autonomy, and a 0.7% acceptance rate recruitment process focused exclusively on outsiders, 10DS embeds forward-deployed engineers with policy teams, lawyers, and prison wardens to ship AI tools in weeks rather than years. Key results include: a policy simulation tool for universal credit decisions, saving £1.5M on statute book analysis, a delivery red-teaming tool used daily, public-facing delivery dashboards, a new public service built in 2 months, and partnerships with the AI Safety Institute, Incubator for AI, and Just AI.

## Key Points

- **Insurgency model**: small elite team at the center with political backing, market-rate pay, high autonomy, and custom recruitment targeting outsiders exclusively
- **0.7-0.8% acceptance rate** on a "grueling" technical selection process — recruiting missionaries, not mercenaries
- **Forward-deployed engineers** embedded with policy advisers, lawyers, comms teams, pollsters — co-designing solutions from idea to implementation in weeks
- **Low-hanging fruit** done internally; complex backlogs tackled via partnership model embedding engineers into other departments
- **Policy simulation**: AI tool allowing policy teams to test the impact of decisions (e.g., universal credit changes on household finances) before making them
- **Statute book analysis**: saved £1.5M by embedding an engineer with in-house lawyers for 2 weeks instead of outsourcing
- **Delivery red-teaming tool**: a PMO-in-your-pocket that interrogates delivery reports and flags optimism bias in reporting teams
- **Public dashboards**: two published in two months, including the AI Opportunities Action Plan tracker
- **New public service**: built in 2 months, launching in 2.5 weeks — normally a year+ in discovery
- **Partnerships**: AI Safety Institute (AISI), Incubator for AI (spin-out of the fellowship), Just AI (founded by former fellow Dan James, deploying engineers into prisons)
- **Extract tool**: collaboration with DeepMind, built on Gemini, digitizes planning application processes including hand-drawn maps — rolling out to every local authority in England
- **AI tutors in education**: working on safeguards and benchmarks for frontier models in classroom environments, measuring cognitive load
- **Scaling ambition**: want the insurgency model to become BAU; next 12-24 months focused on horizontal use cases across call centers, police transcription, DWP/HMRC
