## Core Summary

A rapid-fire, ~50-item chronological recap of the AI industry in 2025, framed by [[ColeMedin]] as a map of the trends and big players heading into 2026. The throughline: capital flooded in (AI startups captured ~50% of global funding), [[Anthropic]] cemented itself as the enterprise/coding-LLM leader, agentic coding went mainstream, and the capability frontier moved from raw models toward context-optimized primitives like [[ClaudeSkills]]. It is a news survey, not a how-to — its value to this wiki is the timeline of milestones that contextualize concepts ingested elsewhere.

## Key Points

- **Money & adoption**: $37B invested in enterprise AI in 2025 (vs $1.7B in 2023); 84% of developers using or planning to use AI tools; ~half of all developers use AI coding assistants daily; Microsoft 365 Copilot adopted by 90%+ of Fortune 500.
- **DeepSeek shock**: DeepSeek R1 (open-source) widely credited with the ~$600B single-day Nvidia market-cap drop — the largest one-day loss for a single US company in history. DeepSeek 3.2 later competes with the frontier but is too large for most to self-host.
- **[[VibeCoding]] goes official**: [[AndrejKarpathy]] coined "vibe coding" in Feb 2025; it now has its own Wikipedia page.
- **Anthropic's year**: Claude 4 family ("coding king"), $13B raise at $183B valuation, $1.5B authors'-copyright settlement (~$3K/book × ~500K books), IBM partnership, Claude Code for web, [[ClaudeCode]] reaching $1B revenue, acquisition of the Bun JS runtime, Claude Opus 4.5 ("the AI coding king"), and donating [[ModelContextProtocol]] to the new Agentic AI Foundation (a Linux Foundation fund co-founded with Block and OpenAI).
- **[[ClaudeSkills]]** released Oct 2025, gaining major traction by December as a more context-efficient ([[ProgressiveDisclosure]]) alternative/complement to MCP — framed as a defining 2026 paradigm beyond the Claude ecosystem.
- **Frontier models**: Google's Gemini 2.5 then Gemini 3 (widely considered the most powerful general LLM at year-end); OpenAI GPT-5 → 5.1 → 5.2 and Sora 2 (with a $1B Disney investment); open-source pressure from Qwen 3, Kimi K2, GLM 4.7, Llama 4.
- **Coding-agent landscape**: OpenAI [[Codex]] (cloud + CLI), Amazon's "Kiro"/Hero agent gaining enterprise interest, Cursor hitting $500M ARR (fastest-growing SaaS), Google effectively absorbing Windsurf and shipping the [[Antigravity]] agentic IDE alongside Gemini 3.
- **Consolidation & infra**: OpenAI's $40B raise ($300B valuation), the $500B Stargate infrastructure project, circular Microsoft/Nvidia/Anthropic investments (hinting at bubble dynamics), Databricks' $134B valuation + Neon acquisition, and Nvidia buying Groq's assets (~$20B).
