## Core Summary

Anthropic introduced a dedicated artifacts space in the Claude app and the ability to embed AI capabilities directly into artifacts, turning static [[ClaudeArtifacts|artifacts]] into interactive, AI-powered apps — for example, a flashcard app that lets end users pick their own topic and generate their own cards, rather than a single-use flashcard set. Since the original artifacts launch, users had created over half a billion artifacts. The new artifacts space is available to Free, Pro, and Max users from the sidebar. Later updates (tracked in the article) extended availability to Team and Enterprise plans (July 31, 2025), iOS and Android apps (July 21, 2025), and added MCP and persistent storage support (October 21, 2025).

## Key Points

- New dedicated **artifacts space** in the Claude app sidebar for browsing, creating, and customizing artifacts.
- Artifacts can now embed AI capabilities directly, turning them into **interactive apps** rather than single-use outputs — e.g., a customizable flashcard app instead of a fixed flashcard set.
- Over half a billion artifacts created by users since the feature's original launch.
- Initial availability: Free, Pro, and Max users (June 25, 2025).
- Musician Rick Rubin's "The Way of Code" project (81 meditations paired with reshapeable interactive artifacts) is cited as an example of the shift toward conversation-as-creative-medium.
- Embedding AI capabilities directly into artifacts is in beta.
- Tracked updates: Team/Enterprise availability (July 31, 2025); iOS/Android app availability (July 21, 2025); MCP and persistent storage support (October 21, 2025).
