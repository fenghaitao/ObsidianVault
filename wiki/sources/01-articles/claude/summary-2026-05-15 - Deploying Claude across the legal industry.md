## Core Summary

Anthropic published a practical deployment guide for legal-industry leaders: which Claude product to use for which workflow, the connectors and practice-area plugins purpose-built for legal work, and a three-phase adoption roadmap teams can run today. The article frames the case with a stat from the 2026 [[FTIConsulting|FTI Consulting]] / [[Relativity]] General Counsel Report: 87% of general counsel now report generative-AI use within their teams, up from 44% the prior year and just 20% in 2023, driven by more complex matters and faster client turnaround expectations. It positions the firms and legal departments "pulling ahead" as the ones embedding agentic AI into how lawyers actually practice, citing legal teams running Claude across contract review and redlining, M&A diligence, privacy impact assessments, regulatory monitoring, litigation prep, and outside counsel oversight — most running more than one Claude product to cover this range of work. This closely parallels and reinforces the messaging of the [[LegalAI|May 12, 2026 legal-industry connector/plugin expansion]] announced three days earlier.
