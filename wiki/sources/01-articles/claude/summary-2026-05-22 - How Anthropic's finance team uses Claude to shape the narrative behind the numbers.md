## Core Summary

Alice Fong, on Anthropic's corporate finance and strategy team, describes how she uses [[ClaudeCowork]] and [[ClaudeForExcelPowerPoint|Claude for Excel]] to maintain a single coherent financial narrative across quarterly board decks, monthly reviews, and model audits. Claude holds the integrity layer underneath the work -- validating that every number and claim reconciles to a single source of truth, reading the narrative as a board member would, and catching contradictions -- freeing 10 to 20 hours a week for higher-impact judgment work. The article also surveys how other functions across the CFO organization (accounting, corporate development, tax and treasury) use Claude Cowork, and offers practical advice for finance teams getting started.

## Key Points

- Claude Cowork validates that every number and claim in board decks reconciles to a single source of truth, and re-checks after each refresh up to the morning the deck goes out.
- Claude reads narratives the way a board member would, flagging contradictions or places where the reader lacks context.
- For monthly financial reviews (variance analysis against forecast), Claude writes first-pass commentary in a consistent voice by referencing the prior month's document.
- Claude for Excel evolved from being unable to follow cross-tab references to tracing balance sheets that won't balance through multiple tabs to find root causes.
- Project memory in Claude Cowork preserves context across board cycles and monthly reviews, growing richer each pass.
- Separate projects are maintained for separate audiences (monthly review vs. board deck) since tone and conventions differ.
- Across the CFO org: accounting uses Claude for GL-to-subledger and bank reconciliations; corporate development runs screening reports for acquisition targets; tax and treasury handles transfer pricing, R&D credit, and nexus questions with primary-source citations.
- Alice Fong's advice: start simple (read a doc and summarize), then push boundaries on recurring workflows where consistency compounds and project memory gets richer.
