## Core Summary

This short Anthropic blog post is the official recap of **Code w/ Claude SF 2026**, the San Francisco leg of Anthropic's annual developer conference, published the week of the event and pointing readers to the full keynote/breakout-session recordings on YouTube. It frames the event's throughline as "the AI exponential" — the shrinking distance between an idea and production software — as demonstrated across live coding sessions, customer deep-dives, and hands-on tutorials. Two concrete product changes are announced alongside the conference: Claude Code rate limits were doubled and Claude Opus API limits were raised, both live as of publication; and Claude Managed Agents gained four new features for building/deploying cloud-hosted agents at scale (detailed in a separate, not-yet-ingested blog post). The article highlights customer talks from Asana, Cursor, GitHub, Replit, and Vercel on designing production-ready agents, and closes by announcing the conference's expansion to London (May 20–21) and Tokyo (June 5–6), with Day 1 keynotes and breakout sessions streamed live at both. Most of the substantive technical content (specific session takeaways) lives in the individual conference transcripts already ingested under `wiki/sources/03-transcripts/Claude/Code with Claude 2026 - San Francisco/`; this article mainly corroborates that material and adds a handful of new facts (rate-limit changes, exact expansion dates, the Managed Agents feature-count teaser).
