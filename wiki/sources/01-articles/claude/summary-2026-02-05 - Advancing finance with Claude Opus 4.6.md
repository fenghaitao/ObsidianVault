## Core Summary

Anthropic launched [[Claude4.6Opus|Claude Opus 4.6]] with a finance-specific push: improved Cowork first-pass deliverables, a more capable Claude in Excel, and a new Claude in PowerPoint (research preview beta). Opus 4.6 improves 23+ percentage points over Sonnet 4.5 on Anthropic's internal ~50-task Real-World Finance evaluation, and posts state-of-the-art scores on external benchmarks Finance Agent (60.7%) and TaxEval (76.0%) from Vals AI.

## Key Points

- **Research**: improves on BrowseComp and DeepSearchQA (extracting specific answers from dense unstructured documents rather than generic summaries).
- **Analysis**: state-of-the-art 60.7% on Finance Agent (SEC filings research, +5.47% over Opus 4.5) and 76.0% on TaxEval, both from Vals AI.
- **Creation**: measured via GDPval-AA plus the Real-World Finance eval; first-pass spreadsheets/presentations come out right more often — demonstrated on a commercial due-diligence task that would typically take a senior analyst 2-3 weeks.
- **Claude in Excel**: better at planning/clarifying assumptions on complex models; adds pivot table editing, chart modification, conditional formatting, sorting/filtering, data validation, finance-grade formatting, auto-compaction for long conversations, and drag-and-drop multi-file support.
- **Claude in PowerPoint** (new, research preview beta, Max/Team/Enterprise): reads existing layouts/fonts/masters before building or editing decks in-line; builds from client templates or generates first-pass presentations from scratch.
- **Cowork + corporate finance plugin**: gives Claude a desktop folder to read/edit/create files in directly, with a finance plugin pre-teaching workflows like journal entries, variance analyses, and reconciliation.
- Customer quotes: Hebbia's Aabhas Sharma (PowerPoint hours → minutes); Shortcut AI's Nico Christie ("watershed moment for spreadsheet agents"); Hg Catalyst's Lloyd Hilton (Excel due-diligence automation); BCI's Ben Letalik (multi-tab Excel analysis).
- Emphasizes human review remains essential for high-stakes financial work despite the capability jump.
