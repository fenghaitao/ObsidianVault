# Claude takes research to new places

**Source:** Anthropic Blog | Published: April 15, 2025  
**Author:** Anthropic

## Summary

Anthropic announces two major capabilities expanding Claude's contextual awareness and research potential: **[[Research|Research]]** capability and **[[GoogleWorkspaceIntegration|Google Workspace integration]]**.

### Research Capability

Claude's new [[Research]] feature enables in-depth research by:

- Conducting agentic searches that build on each other iteratively
- Searching across both the public web and organizational context
- Exploring different angles of questions automatically
- Working through open questions systematically
- Delivering comprehensive answers with easy-to-check citations

The research operates at the speed needed for practical knowledge work — delivering thorough answers in minutes rather than hours.

### Google Workspace Integration

[[GoogleWorkspaceIntegration|Google Workspace integration]] connects Claude to:

- **Gmail** — Email threads and correspondence
- **Google Calendar** — Calendar commitments and scheduling
- **Google Docs** — Document repositories and organizational knowledge

This eliminates manual file uploads and repeated context provision, enabling Claude to:

- Pull together meeting notes from email threads
- Identify action items from follow-up communications
- Search for relevant supporting documents
- Provide inline citations for verification

### Enterprise Cataloging

For [[ClaudeEnterprise|Claude Enterprise]] organizations, a new **cataloging** feature enables [[RetrievalAugmentedGeneration|Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG)]] over organizational document ecosystems. This specialized indexing allows Claude to find information:

- Buried in lengthy documents
- Scattered across multiple files
- Without requiring explicit file path specification

Enterprise-grade security keeps data within organizational control.

## Availability

- **Research:** Early beta for Max, Team, and Enterprise plans in US, Japan, Brazil
- **Google Workspace Integration:** Beta for all paid users; Team and Enterprise admins must enable domain-wide access
- **Enterprise Cataloging:** Available to Claude Enterprise organizations

Users enable Research via the chat settings toggle.
