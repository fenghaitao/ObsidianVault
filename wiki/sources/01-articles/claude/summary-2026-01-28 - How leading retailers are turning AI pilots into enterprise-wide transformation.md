## Core Summary

Anthropic's Enterprise AI Transformation Guide for Retail identifies three steps separating retail AI leaders from organizations stuck in pilot purgatory: lay a governance/stakeholder foundation, launch carefully-selected lower-risk pilots that prove value within weeks, and scale what works while building organizational capability. [[Shopify]], [[LOreal|L'Oréal]], and [[LotteHomeshopping|Lotte Homeshopping]] are cited production examples.

## Key Points

- Retail-specific bottlenecks: fragmented tech stacks (e-commerce, POS, inventory, CRM systems not designed to interoperate), seasonal demand cycles that make ROI hard to model, and scarcity of talent combining retail-operations and AI expertise. PwC: 88% of executives plan to increase AI investment this year.
- **[[Shopify]]**: Claude powers Sidekick, converting natural-language merchant questions into ShopifyQL queries that previously required technical expertise.
- **[[LOreal|L'Oréal]]**: built a multi-agent system with Claude at the core, orchestrating 15+ specialized agents that turn user questions into insights and visualizations for 44,000 employees across 150 countries.
- **[[LotteHomeshopping|Lotte Homeshopping]]**: deployed a Claude-powered AI assistant providing 24/7 support for partner suppliers — QA inquiries, documentation validation, and regulatory-requirement guidance.
- Winning organizations started with one specific operational problem, proved value within weeks, then expanded — rather than attempting broad transformation upfront.
