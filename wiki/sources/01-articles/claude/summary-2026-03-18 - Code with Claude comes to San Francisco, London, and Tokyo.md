## Core Summary

Anthropic announced that its developer conference, [[CodeWithClaude|Code with Claude]], is expanding beyond San Francisco (where it launched the prior year) to also run in London and Tokyo this spring. The event is a full day of live workshops, demos of new Claude capabilities, technical sessions from Anthropic engineers and developers at startups/enterprises, and 1:1 office hours with the teams behind Claude. In-person space is limited and allocated by random drawing (applicants notified in early April), while livestream access and session recordings are open to everyone.
