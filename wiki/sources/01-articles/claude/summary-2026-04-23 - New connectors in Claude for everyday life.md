## Core Summary

Anthropic expanded Claude's connector ecosystem beyond work tools to apps people use in everyday life: AllTrails, Audible, Booking.com, Instacart, Intuit Credit Karma, Intuit TurboTax, Resy, Spotify, StubHub, Taskrabbit, Thumbtack, Tripadvisor, Uber, Uber Eats, and Viator, with more planned. Since launching in July 2025, the Claude directory has grown to over 200 connectors used by millions of people, and users often chain multiple connectors in a single conversation (e.g., pulling data from Amplitude, building a Canva deck, and posting to Asana). Connectors now surface dynamically and contextually within a conversation — Claude suggests the right app for the task at hand (e.g., AllTrails for a hike recommendation) and lets the user refine results conversationally. Claude remains ad-free with no paid placements or sponsored answers; when multiple connectors could help, all options are shown, ranked by usefulness. Users stay in control: connected data isn't used for model training, apps can't see other conversations, connections can be revoked anytime, and Claude checks with the user before booking or purchasing anything on their behalf.

## Key Points

- New everyday-life connectors (April 23, 2026): AllTrails, Audible, Booking.com, Instacart, Intuit Credit Karma, Intuit TurboTax, Resy, Spotify, StubHub, Taskrabbit, Thumbtack, Tripadvisor, Uber, Uber Eats, Viator — more coming.
- The Claude connector directory (launched July 2025) has grown to 200+ connectors spanning design, finance, productivity, and health apps.
- Users commonly chain several connectors within one conversation (example given: Amplitude query to Canva deck to Asana task).
- Connectors now appear dynamically/contextually mid-conversation based on what the user is doing, rather than requiring manual selection up front; results can be refined conversationally (e.g., "shorter," "more scenic," "dog-friendly" hikes via AllTrails).
- When more than one connected app could answer a request, Claude surfaces all relevant options ranked by usefulness — no paid placement or sponsored answers; Claude stays ad-free.
- Data/privacy controls: a connected app's data is not used to train Anthropic's models, the app cannot see the user's other Claude conversations, and users can disconnect at any time.
- Claude is designed to check with the user before taking an irreversible action (e.g., booking or purchasing) on their behalf.
- Connectors are available on all Claude plans; mobile support is in beta. Full list at claude.ai/directory/connectors. Third-party developers can submit their product to the directory.
