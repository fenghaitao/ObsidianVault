# Claude 2 on Amazon Bedrock

**Core thesis**: Claude 2 became available on Amazon Bedrock in August 2023, enabling enterprises to build secure, scalable generative AI applications through a fully managed AWS API service. Early adopters including LexisNexis, Lonely Planet, and Ricoh USA used it for legal AI, travel content, and enterprise data workflows.

## Summary

[[Anthropic]]'s [[Claude2]] model was made available on [[AmazonBedrock]] — Amazon Web Services' fully managed foundation model service — in August 2023. This followed an earlier launch in April 2023 when Claude 1.3 and Claude Instant were among the first models available on Bedrock.

Claude 2 was described as Anthropic's state-of-the-art model at the time, excelling at:
- Thoughtful dialogue
- Content creation
- Complex reasoning
- Creativity
- Coding

It was built using [[ConstitutionalAI]], Anthropic's training methodology for safer AI.

## Enterprise Adopters

### LexisNexis Legal & Professional
A global provider of information and analytics serving 150+ countries across law, corporate, government, and academic markets. Selected Claude 2 on Amazon Bedrock for its:
- Long context windows for processing detailed legal documents
- High-quality technical analysis
- Data security standards
- Fast outputs

Quote from Jeff Reihl, EVP & CTO: *"Legal use cases also require high-quality technical analysis, long context windows for processing detailed documents, and fast outputs. That's why we've chosen Claude 2 on Amazon Bedrock as an important part of our AI strategy."*

### Lonely Planet
One of the world's most trusted travel information sources. Used Claude 2 on Amazon Bedrock to unlock decades of travel content and help customers plan trips.

Quote from Chris Whyde, SVP of Engineering and Data Science: *"We've turned to Claude 2 on Amazon Bedrock to integrate generative AI in a scalable, reliable, and secure way."*

### Ricoh USA
Workplace solutions and digital transformation services company. Used Claude 2 on Amazon Bedrock to generate high-quality training datasets while preserving data integrity. Selected for:
- Adherence to HIPAA and SOC II standards
- Proficiency in generating training data
- Customer security

Quote from Ashok Shenoy, VP of Portfolio Development: *"We selected Claude 2 on Amazon Bedrock for its proficiency in generating this data, as well as its adherence to stringent standards including HIPAA and SOC II."*

## Key Themes

- **Enterprise trust**: Security, compliance (HIPAA, SOC II), and responsible AI with human oversight were top selection criteria.
- **Long context windows**: Particularly valued for document-intensive use cases (legal).
- **Managed infrastructure**: Bedrock's fully managed API model lowered the barrier for enterprises to adopt foundation models.
- **Partnership milestone**: Marked the deepening of the Anthropic-AWS collaboration beyond the initial Bedrock launch.
