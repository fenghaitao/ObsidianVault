# Claude can now connect to your world

**Source**: Anthropic blog (May 1, 2025)  
**URL**: https://claude.com/blog/integrations

## Summary

Anthropic announced two major feature expansions for Claude: **Integrations** and advanced **Research** capabilities. [[Integrations]] allow users to connect third-party apps and tools to [[Claude.ai]] using [[ModelContextProtocol|remote MCP servers]]. Simultaneously, [[Research|advanced Research mode]] was expanded to conduct deeper investigations across web sources, [[GoogleWorkspaceIntegration|Google Workspace]], and any connected integration—taking up to 45 minutes for complex investigations.

## Key Announcements

### Integrations
- Users can now discover and connect remote MCP servers directly to Claude
- Initial launch includes integrations with 10 major platforms: Atlassian (Jira, Confluence), Zapier, Cloudflare, Intercom, Asana, Square, Sentry, PayPal, Linear, and Plaid
- More integrations coming from Stripe, GitLab, and Box
- Developers can build custom integrations in as little as 30 minutes
- Users can connect any number of integrations to gain deep context about their work and take actions across platforms

### Advanced Research
- Research mode can now conduct comprehensive investigations lasting 5-45 minutes (most complete in 5-15 minutes)
- Can search across web, Google Workspace, and any connected integration
- Claude breaks down complex requests and investigates each part systematically
- All research includes transparent citations linking to original sources

### Web Search Expansion
- Web search is now globally available on all [[Claude.ai]] paid plans
- Previously limited to United States, Japan, and Brazil

## Integration Examples

### Zapier
Through pre-built workflows, Claude can access thousands of apps—e.g., automatically pulling sales data from HubSpot and preparing meeting briefs from calendar information.

### Atlassian (Jira + Confluence)
Claude can collaborate on product building, manage tasks, and scale work by creating multiple documentation pages and work items simultaneously.

### Intercom
Users can respond faster to customer feedback. Intercom's AI agent Fin can file bugs in Linear based on user reports, with Claude managing the entire workflow from feedback to resolution.

## Availability

As of May 2025 (with June 3, 2025 update):
- **Integrations**: Beta on Max, Team, Enterprise plans; coming to Pro
- **Advanced Research**: Same plan availability as Integrations
- **Web Search**: Available globally on all paid Claude.ai plans
