## Core Summary

Anthropic published a downloadable, comprehensive guide to building, testing, and distributing [[ClaudeCodeSkills|Skills]] — covering standalone workflows and MCP-enhanced integrations, technical requirements, skill structure, testing approaches, and common troubleshooting patterns, aimed at developers, power users, teams standardizing Claude usage, and MCP connector builders.

## Key Points

- Target audiences: developers wanting consistent workflow-following, power users automating repeatable tasks (document creation, research), teams standardizing how Claude operates org-wide, and MCP connector builders pairing integrations with reliable workflows.
- If the top 2-3 workflows to automate are already known, building and testing a first working skill with skill-creator takes about 15-30 minutes.
- Includes a dedicated section on combining MCP and Skills for connector builders.
