## Core Summary

Anthropic's cybersecurity partners are now live with Claude Opus-powered defensive offerings, reporting significant improvements in defense capabilities. The work falls into three areas: offensive testing at scale (Wiz, Palo Alto Networks, CrowdStrike), closing the gap between finding and fixing vulnerabilities (Accenture, Trend Micro, Deloitte), and deploying governed AI into production (PwC). BCG, Infosys, and SentinelOne are also building offerings on Opus.

## Key Points

- Wiz Red Agent uses Opus to reason like a human pentester across 150,000+ production assets weekly, surfacing thousands of high- and critical-severity findings validated with proof of exploitability.
- Palo Alto Networks' Unit 42 Frontier AI Defense uses Opus to find hidden vulnerabilities, map attack paths, and build hardening roadmaps against AI-enabled attacks.
- CrowdStrike's Frontier AI Readiness and Resilience Service pairs Opus with AI Red Team Services to continuously hunt for zero-days and accelerate remediation before code reaches production.
- Accenture's Cyber.AI platform took internal security testing coverage from ~10% to over 80% across 1,600 applications and 500,000+ APIs, cutting scan turnaround from 3-5 days to under an hour.
- Trend Micro's TrendAI Vision One uses Opus-assisted vulnerability research with virtual patching, protecting at-risk systems up to 96 days before a vendor patch is available via the Zero Day Initiative.
- Deloitte's CTEM built on Ascend runs discovery, validation, prioritization, and remediation as one workflow, with Opus enabling remediation in hours rather than days or weeks.
- PwC's Claude Native Cybersecurity offering addresses both getting AI safely into production (Secure AI Adoption) and modernizing the cyber function itself (Scaled Frontier Defense).
