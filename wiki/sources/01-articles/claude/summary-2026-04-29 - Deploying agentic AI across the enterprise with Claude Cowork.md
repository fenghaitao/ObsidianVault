## Core Summary

This short Anthropic blog post (published 2026-04-29, last modified 2026-06-21) announces a guide for deploying [[ClaudeCowork|Claude Cowork]] across a business function. It frames Cowork as bringing Claude Code-style agentic delegation to non-engineering roles (analysts, lawyers, account executives, marketers) by meeting work where it already happens: local files/folders, connected apps like Slack and Google Drive, and the browser. It notes Cowork pairs with [[ClaudeForExcelPowerPoint|Claude for Excel and Claude for PowerPoint]] to carry context across spreadsheets and slide decks in one workflow, and that plugins, skills, and commands make Claude more customizable to a given work environment. The guide itself (linked externally, not included in the scraped page) reportedly covers where to start, how to structure a pilot, common use cases, and best practices for driving adoption at scale, drawing on examples from Anthropic's own teams and customers including [[ThomsonReuters|Thomson Reuters]], [[Zapier]], and [[Jamf]]. The article is a thin marketing teaser: it does not reproduce the guide's actual content, use cases, or customer specifics — those live behind the external link.
