## Core Summary

Anthropic made [[ClaudeEnterprise|Claude Enterprise]] purchasable directly on its website with no sales conversation required — set up a workspace, configure SSO, and invite team members in minutes — bundling Claude, [[ClaudeCode]], and [[ClaudeCowork|Cowork]] (with plugins for sales, finance, legal, marketing, and more) under enterprise security and administrative controls.

## Key Points

- **Security/admin controls**: SSO and domain capture for access management, SCIM-automated provisioning, audit logs and a Compliance API, custom data-retention policies, usage analytics (activity/feature adoption/spend), and no training on customer content by default.
- **Pricing**: seat-plus-usage model; usage billed at API rates so organizations pay only for what they use, with spend caps configurable at org and individual-user levels.
- **Connects to existing tools**: Microsoft 365, Slack, and other platforms via connectors; Excel and PowerPoint via built-in chat sidebars.
- Customer quotes: Canva's Samantha Garrett (fastest, most meaningful tech adoption she's seen); Quantium's Adam Driussi (deep analytical expertise + AI unlocks more ambitious challenges); Zapier's Anna Marie Clifton (Skills "spread like lightning," turning individual productivity into company-wide leverage); Deloitte's Ranjit Bawa (significant platform investment given aligned responsible-AI approach); NBIM's Stian Kirkeberg (best performance on analysis/reasoning and long multi-document context retention).
- Tailored terms, tiered usage incentives, HIPAA-readiness, or dedicated support still route through the sales team.
