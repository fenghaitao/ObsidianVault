# Introducing the Claude Team plan and iOS app

## Overview

[[Anthropic]] announced two major product updates in May 2024: the [[ClaudeTeamPlan]] subscription offering for team collaboration and the [[ClaudeIOSApp]], enabling mobile access to Claude.

## The Claude Team Plan

### Pricing & Availability
- Cost: $30 per user per month
- Minimum commitment: 5 seats
- Sign-up: available at https://claude.ai/

### Key Features
- **Workspace management:** Enable teams to create shared workspaces with increased usage limits
- **Team management tools:** Manage users and billing across the team
- **Security & privacy:** Built with security and data privacy in mind; protects sensitive business information
- **Future collaboration features (coming weeks):**
  - Citations from reliable sources to verify AI-generated claims
  - Integrations with data repositories (codebases, CRMs)
  - Iterative collaboration on AI-generated documents and projects
  - Highest standards of security and safety maintained throughout

### Value Proposition
The Team Plan enables businesses to customize workflows based on unique team needs rather than being constrained by existing tool limitations. It positions Claude as an AI partner for deep work, expert knowledge, creative collaboration, and productivity enhancement across the organization.

## The Claude iOS App

### Features
- Free to download
- Available on [[AppleAppStore]]
- Enables access to Claude from anywhere, at any time
- Exceptional for:
  - Brainstorming ideas on the go
  - Getting quick answers to questions
  - Analyzing scenes and images from the real world

### Positioning
Described as putting "frontier intelligence in your back pocket" — making Claude's full capabilities available to users in mobile contexts.

## Strategic Significance

These announcements represent [[Anthropic]]'s expansion strategy:
1. **Workspace collaboration** — addressing enterprise team use cases
2. **Platform accessibility** — extending reach to mobile users
3. **Integration ecosystem** — laying groundwork for repository and CRM integrations
