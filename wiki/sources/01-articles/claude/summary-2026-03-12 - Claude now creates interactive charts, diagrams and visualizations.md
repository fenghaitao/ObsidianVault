## Core Summary

Anthropic brought a beta version of "Imagine with Claude" (previewed the previous fall) into regular chat conversations: Claude can now build custom, interactive charts, diagrams, and visualizations in-line in its responses — distinct from [[Artifacts]], which are permanent, shareable/downloadable creations. These in-line visuals are temporary aids to understanding that change or disappear as the conversation evolves.

## Key Points

- Examples: asking how compound interest works produces an interactive curve to play with; asking about the periodic table produces a clickable interactive visualization.
- On by default — Claude decides when a visual would help, or a user can request one directly ("draw this as a diagram," "visualize how this might change over time"); users can ask for adjustments or to dig deeper afterward.
- Distinguished from Artifacts by purpose and permanence: Artifacts are polished, shareable/downloadable side-panel outputs; these visuals appear in-line to aid live discussion and are temporary.
- Part of a broader wave of response-format improvements: purpose-designed formats for recipes (ingredients/steps) and weather queries (visual), plus direct interaction with connected apps like Figma, Canva, and Slack (MCP Apps).
- Available on all plan types at launch. *Update (April 22, 2026): also available in Claude Cowork on all paid plans.*
