## Summary

Anthropic announced that Claude models are now authorized for FedRAMP High and IL-2 workloads through Google Cloud's Vertex AI platform. This enables federal civilian agencies and defense organizations to use Claude while meeting strict regulatory compliance requirements.

## Key Points

- **FedRAMP High Authorization**: Federal agencies can use Claude in healthcare, law enforcement, finance, and emergency services while maintaining compliance for sensitive unclassified data.
- **DoD IL2 Support**: Defense contractors and agencies can use Claude with non-controlled unclassified information.
- **Model Family Access**: Agencies have access to the complete Claude model family, including Claude 3.7 Sonnet.
- **Managed Infrastructure**: Vertex AI offers fully managed serverless models via APIs, eliminating infrastructure provisioning needs.
- **Future IL5 Compatibility**: The announcement indicates progress toward future IL5 compatibility for more sensitive DoD workloads.

## Features Enabled

- Building AI applications and workflows with compliance to stringent security standards
- Access to complete Claude model family
- Support for digital transformation initiatives with frontier AI capabilities

## Getting Started

Federal agency employees and contractors access Claude through:
1. Google Cloud environment with Assured Workloads for FedRAMP High or IL2
2. Claude models via Vertex AI Model Garden
3. Vertex AI API endpoints
