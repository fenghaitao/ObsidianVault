# Claude Android App

Claude is now available on Android with full vision, multilingual, and reasoning capabilities across web, iOS, and Android platforms. The new Claude Android app brings the power of Claude—including the most powerful model, [[Claude3.5Sonnet]]—to Android users.

## Key Information

- **Availability**: Free and accessible with all plans (free, Pro, Team)
- **Capabilities**: Vision, multilingual support, reasoning
- **Distribution**: Google Play Store
- **Feature parity**: Works identically to Claude on iOS and the web
- **Model access**: Includes [[Claude3.5Sonnet]], the most capable Claude model

## Use Cases

The app enables Claude to assist across diverse scenarios:
- Business work (drafting proposals between meetings)
- Travel assistance (translating menus, research)
- Creative tasks (brainstorming, gift ideas)
- Communication (composing speeches, writing)
- Code review and programming assistance
- Grant writing and content generation
- Learning and explanation (complex topics, exam prep, programming concepts)
