## Core Summary

Anthropic lowered [[ClaudeTeamPlan|Claude Team]] pricing and introduced annual discounts for both seat tiers: standard seats to $20/month (annual) or $25/month (monthly), premium seats to $100/month (annual) or $125/month (monthly), with premium seats including 5x the usage of standard seats.

## Key Points

- Every Team seat includes more usage than the Pro plan; admins can purchase additional capacity for power users beyond seat allowances.
- [[ClaudeCode]] is included with every Team seat (developers and non-technical roles like product managers alike).
- Shared workspace with connector-based access to internal knowledge; centralized admin controls and billing; no training on customer content by default.
