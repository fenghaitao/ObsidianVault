## Core Summary

Anthropic announces "The Founder's Playbook," a practical guide for building an AI-native startup with Claude, distributed as a downloadable PDF (not reproduced in the source article itself). The playbook remaps the four core stages of the startup lifecycle — Idea, MVP, Launch, and Scale — for what's possible in 2026, giving each stage explicit goals, exit criteria, common failure modes, and AI-powered exercises. Its central framing: AI is shifting the founder's role from individual contributor to orchestrator, letting founders without prior coding experience ship production applications and reach revenue before scaling headcount.

## Key Points

- **Audience**: founders deciding how to architect their company around AI from day one, and early operators helping them get there.
- **Role shift**: the founder's role moves from individual contributor (doing the work directly) to orchestrator (directing AI-driven work), freeing founders to focus on the work only they can do.
- **Framework**: remaps the four startup lifecycle stages — Idea, MVP, Launch, Scale — each with its own goals, exit criteria, common failure modes, and AI-powered exercises for 2026-era AI-native building.
- **Observed pattern**: founders with no prior coding background are shipping production applications and automating their most tedious workflows, and reaching revenue before scaling headcount.
- **Format**: practical exercises, frameworks, and prompts for using [[Claude]] at each stage, published as a full PDF linked from the article (body content of the PDF itself was not available to this ingestion — only the announcement/teaser text was ingested).
