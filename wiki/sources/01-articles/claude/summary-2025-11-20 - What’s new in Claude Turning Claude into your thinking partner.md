## Core Summary

A roundup piece framing several recently-shipped Claude features — memory, voice/dictation, mobile actions, Claude for Chrome, connectors, and file creation — as together turning Claude into a persistent "thinking partner" that carries context across a full idea-to-finished-work cycle, rather than answering isolated questions.

## Key Points

- [[ClaudeMemory|Memory]] carries preferences, projects, and working style across conversations (e.g., "prefer prose over bullet points," an upcoming Q1 launch).
- **Voice mode**: natural spoken conversation with Claude on mobile for brainstorming and quick questions; dictation available on both mobile and desktop.
- **Mobile actions**: drafting messages, creating calendar invites, and making plans using location.
- **[[ClaudeInChrome|Claude for Chrome]]**: handles forms, extracts data, and automates repetitive browser steps.
- **Connectors**: Notion, Canva, Slack, Linear, Figma, and others let Claude pull in context and take action directly.
- Ties together previously-announced capabilities (file creation, artifacts/prototyping) as part of one continuous "thinking partner" narrative rather than introducing new features itself.
