## Core Summary

Brian Casel interviews Colleen Schnettler, a Ruby on Rails developer who built her own voice-to-LinkedIn tool in two days after hating every paid AI LinkedIn tool. She demonstrates her workflow using Super Whisper for voice notes, a custom app that auto-generates LinkedIn posts, and the Compound Engineering plugin from Every for structured plan/execute/review cycles with sub-agents. Key takeaways: build your own tools, keep specs light, and trust the feel — she still manually clicks through every feature before merging.

## Key Points

- Colleen tried every paid AI LinkedIn tool and hated them all — too generic, too robotic. Built her own in 2 days.
- Workflow: voice notes via Super Whisper → custom app watches folder → auto-generates LinkedIn posts.
- Uses Compound Engineering plugin (from Every) for structured development: plan → execute → review via specialized sub-agents (security sentinel, architect, strategist).
- Creates lightweight plans before big features but skims at high level rather than reading every line.
- Runs 2 Claude Code instances in parallel for multitasking.
- Switches to GPT-5 in Windsurf when Claude gets stuck — cross-model comparison for troubleshooting.
- Still manually clicks through every feature in the browser before merging: "There is still a feel to an app that AI can easily miss."
- Colleen's LinkedIn framework: teach me, help me, inspire me, show me — each post categorized into one bucket.
