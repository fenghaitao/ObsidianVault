## Core Summary

Mark, an in-house product lawyer at Anthropic, demonstrates how Claude Cowork transforms legal workflow. A scheduled morning task acts as a personalized chief of staff, sorting Gmail items by urgency. The /brief skill pulls relevant review files, reads Slack and Gmail threads, and returns a targeted memo pointing to the exact paragraphs needed — replacing hours of re-reading 40-page memos. Claude drafts replies for approval, and closes Jira tickets to build a corpus of institutional knowledge. The core theme: lawyers can do more with less by offloading busy work to focus on strategic thinking.

## Key Points

- **Scheduled morning brief:** Cowork task runs daily, sorting Gmail items by due date and urgency — like a personalized chief of staff.
- **/brief skill:** Names a product and change; Claude pulls the review file, reads Slack/Gmail threads, returns targeted memo pointing to relevant paragraphs. Built as an open protocol plugin customizable to any company's playbooks.
- **Trust but verify:** Lawyer checks the source paragraphs verbatim before responding. Human-in-the-loop is the whole job.
- **End-to-end workflow:** Draft reply → approve → close Jira ticket → build institutional knowledge corpus accessible to entire legal department.
- **Strategic shift:** Instead of busy work (pulling documents, searching files), lawyers use their brains for strategic thinking — "why I went to law school in the first place."
