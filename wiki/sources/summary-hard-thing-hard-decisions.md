## Core Summary

Brian Casel discusses the difficulty of product decisions in the AI era. When everything is buildable, the hard part is deciding what to build and what to say no to.

## Key Points

- The hard thing isn't building — it's deciding what to build.
- AI makes everything buildable; restraint becomes the key skill.
