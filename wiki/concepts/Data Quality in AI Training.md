## Definition

Data quality in AI training refers to the depth, nuance, and sophistication of the human-generated data used to teach AI models what is good and bad. It goes far beyond simple checkbox verification to assess subtle, subjective qualities like creativity, emotional resonance, and taste.

## Key Information

- Most people "don't understand what quality even means in the space" — they think you can just throw bodies at a problem and get good data, which is "completely wrong"
- Quality assessment requires distinguishing between simply checking boxes (e.g., "Is this a poem? Does it contain eight lines? Does it contain the word moon?") and evaluating Nobel Prize-level qualities (uniqueness, subtle imagery, emotional impact, surprise)
- Surge AI gathers thousands of signals on every worker and project: keyboard strokes, response speed, review scores, code standards, and even trains models on worker outputs to measure quality
- The approach mirrors Google Search: first remove the worst content (spam, low quality), then discover the best of the best
- Quality is inherently subjective and complex — it's "hard to measure" and requires technology to capture the richness of human judgment
- The quality of training data is what made Claude significantly better at coding and writing than competitors for a long period
- Each vertical (poetry, coding, writing, technical documentation) requires different quality signals and different expertise
