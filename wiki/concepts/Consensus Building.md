## Definition

Consensus Building is securing buy-in from managers, peers, and leadership ahead of a decision (especially a promotion) so the decision is ratified rather than debated in the room.

## Key Information

- Adrien Friggeri: you need strong underlying impact plus consensus built ahead of time, so there is no debate in the calibration room.
- For his IC8 promotion, he socialized the case over two cycles — the first cycle his manager only "felt the water" with leaders, and people had context by the following half.
- He enlisted a heavyweight D2 leader by asking for monthly check-ins, so that leader was involved and supportive throughout the process.
- The same principle applies to risky bets: align with your manager and skip ahead of time ("overcommunicate") and be ready to hear "no."
- Contrast: locking yourself in a room for three months and surfacing later is far riskier than keeping everyone on the same page.
