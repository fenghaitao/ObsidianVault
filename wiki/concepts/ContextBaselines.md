## Definition
Context Baselines refer to the distinction between low-context human baselines (experts on their first week with unfamiliar tasks) and high-context human baselines (experts deeply familiar with specific tasks and codebases) used when evaluating AI capabilities.

## Key Information
- METR's benchmark baselines use low-context humans: experienced experts but on their "first day or first week on the job," without prior exposure to the specific tasks
- The RCT study used high-context humans: top contributors with ~5 years of experience on specific repositories who often already knew the solution and were "limited by how fast they can type"
- AI performance relative to low-context baselines may overstate practical usefulness compared to high-context baselines
- This distinction is one of the key hypotheses for reconciling the gap between benchmark evidence and real-world productivity measurements
- Low-context baselines make AI look more capable; high-context baselines reveal that expert humans with deep familiarity are much faster
