## Definition
Cognitive debt is the state of not being up to speed with everything your codebase can do or all of the code in your codebase. It arises when AI agents produce code faster than humans can understand and review it, creating a knowledge gap between what exists and what the human knows.

## Key Information
- Term coined recently (as of early 2026) and referenced by Chris Parsons
- The concern: if AI writes code continuously and humans don't review it, humans lose track of what the codebase contains and how it works
- Parsons addresses this by never letting AI close a project without his review — he always wants to be the "final step of verification"
- He also gets Claude to summarize all sessions from the day into a paragraph so he doesn't forget what happened
- Related to the broader question of what work humans uniquely need to stay involved in
