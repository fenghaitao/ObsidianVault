## Definition

Span of Control is the ratio of direct reports to managers in an organization. Companies sometimes reduce it (raise the ratio) during layoffs, which puts managers on the chopping block rather than only ICs.

## Key Information

- Moving managers around is inherently painful because their blast radius is larger — part of why leadership roles earn more
- During layoffs, companies often target a desired manager-to-IC ratio, so managers aren't automatically safer than ICs
- A "span of control reduction" (e.g., requiring 12 reports per manager) can send managers first
- Whether removing a manager is hard depends on how load-bearing they are to their team and cross-functional partners
