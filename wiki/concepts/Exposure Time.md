## Definition

Exposure Time (also called "exposure hours") is the practice of deliberately exposing yourself to great design, great products, and great people to develop your taste and judgment. The concept was introduced by GM Marous on Lenny's Podcast.

## Key Information

- Originated by GM Marous on a previous Lenny's Podcast episode
- Core idea: being exposed to great stuff (great UX, onboarding flows, websites, designs) informs your taste and judgment
- Lazar treats this as his primary skill development: "I'm deliberately exposing myself to people and resources that I know I need to consume to level up"
- Practical application: follow good designers on X, watch world-class designers work (e.g., 40-50 minute design videos), study design styles
- "Set aside more time on learning than building" — exposure time compounds
- Contrast with just building: building without exposure leads to mediocre output
- In the AI era, where everyone can produce "good enough," exposure time is what separates good enough from world-class
