## Definition

"Playing the game" is coasting at a job — doing the minimum to stay near average while mentally disengaging from the promotion chase.

## Key Information

- After a 5/5 rating came with no promotion or compensation change, Jay became "super jaded" and switched to "going through the motions" of "playing the game"
- He still got promoted while doing the minimum: in a 10x-growth company, a competent engineer becomes load-bearing and a domain expert by default
- He felt many big-tech people were just playing the game and wanted something bigger to work toward
