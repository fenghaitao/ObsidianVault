## Definition

Jeetu Patel's principle of being generous with praise, encouragement, and expressions of care — especially in private settings. It's the counterbalance to his "debate in public" approach: be direct in public, but be lavish with support in private.

## Key Information

- Articulated by Jeetu Patel
- "Don't be stingy with words" — be generous with praise and encouragement in private
- This is the complement to his approach of debating and critiquing in public
- "Some people are very stingy with words with people in private. You can't be stingy with words."
- Jeetu shared a story of someone who saw his LinkedIn post about this and started telling their parents "I love you" every time they visited them in India
- "What could be more rewarding to me than that" — the ripple effect of generosity with words
- Jeetu's final advice to listeners: "Pay it forward. Help the next person out a little bit more."
