## Definition
Developer marketing is the art of communicating a technical product's value honestly and quickly — something Charlie Marsh treats as underrated and even a "dirty word" among engineers.

## Key Information
- Hypothesis: thousands of amazing GitHub projects never get discovered because they don't know how to market themselves; the "best technical solution" does not automatically win.
- Practical guidance: include a visual hook (e.g., a benchmark graph), a strong tagline ("compatible with your existing stuff and much faster"), and assume a reader gives you ~10 seconds.
- Honesty over clickbait: communicate the most interesting pieces in a way you believe is true and authentic, and avoid "chart crimes" like non-zeroed benchmark axes.
- Modeled partly on early OpenAI blog posts where reading none of the text still conveys what was done and why it matters.
