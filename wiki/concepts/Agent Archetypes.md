## Definition

Agent Archetypes is a framework presented by Malte Ubl (CTO of Vercel) categorizing four practical types of AI agents that companies can build today without major process changes. These represent low-hanging fruit for automation that can save companies millions or billions of dollars.

## Key Information

The four archetypes are:

1. **Agent as a Service**: Automating human agent roles (support, sales) to run 24/7 instead of 9-to-5. Agents don't need to sleep, enabling round-the-clock operation.

2. **Compressed Research**: An agent performs the research phase of a business process automatically, then hands off to a human for the decision. The process stays the same but the research phase goes from 30 minutes to 5 minutes, saving massive time without increasing risk.

3. **Surface Existing Information**: Making scattered company knowledge practically usable. Information exists somewhere (Slack, recordings, issue trackers) but isn't practically accessible. Agents can surface and organize it.

4. **Eliminating Boring Work**: Asking employees "what do you hate most about your job?" and building agents to handle the boring parts. Vercel's own support agent has a 90% deflection rate and dramatically improved support team job satisfaction.
