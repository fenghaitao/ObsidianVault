## Definition

Structured programming is a programming paradigm emphasizing clear control flow (sequences, loops, and conditionals) over unstructured jumps.

## Key Information

- Knuth's "Structured Programming with go to Statements" was his proposed response to the software crisis.
- Dijkstra's "Go To Statement Considered Harmful" letter helped launch the movement.
