## Definition
Local Models for Agents is the trend of running AI models directly on user devices (phones, laptops) for agent tasks, avoiding cloud API calls. Kitze predicts this will be the winning approach for mainstream users — local models getting "insanely good" combined with tool capabilities from installed apps.

## Key Information
- Kitze's prediction: "local models are getting insanely good and they're going to get even better this year and next year"
- "I think for most normies and most people, they'll be completely fine with a local agent like Siri getting tool capabilities from all of their local installed apps"
- Advantages: no wasting credits, data doesn't go anywhere, phone magically does things
- Google Pixel already demonstrates: launches apps in background, orders coffee
- Apple may win: "it would be the funniest thing if Apple wins all of this"
- Kitze went "full hipster mode" — moved everything to local infrastructure
- Switched to Android for full local agent control
- Contrasts with cloud agents that are "too nerfed" and custom agents that require too much tinkering
