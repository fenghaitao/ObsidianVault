## Definition
Open Source Models are AI models with publicly available weights that can be downloaded, self-hosted, and customized, as exemplified by the Gemma 4 family available on Hugging Face, Kaggle, and Ollama.

## Key Information
- Gemma 4 models are available for self-hosting via Hugging Face, Kaggle, and Ollama
- Released under Apache 2.0 license for maximum developer accessibility
- Enables integration from initial testing through to production deployment
- Cloud-hosted options (AI Studio, Vertex AI) also available for larger models
- Gemma 4 sets a new precedent for what's possible with small open-source models
