## Definition

DoD Impact Level 2 (IL2) is a Department of Defense compliance standard that authorizes cloud services for defense contractors and agencies working with non-controlled unclassified information.

## Key Information

- **Scope**: Applies to defense contractors and Department of Defense organizations handling non-controlled unclassified information (NFCI).
- **Data Classification**: Lower than IL4 and IL5, suitable for information that is not classified but requires DoD-level security controls.
- **Use Cases**:
  - Defense research and development
  - Acquisition and planning activities
  - Logistics and supply chain management
  - Non-sensitive operational information

## IL2 Authorization for Cloud Services

[[Anthropic]]'s [[Claude]] models are now authorized for DoD IL2 workloads through [[GoogleCloud]]'s [[VertexAI]] platform, enabling defense organizations to use frontier AI capabilities while maintaining compliance with DoD security requirements.

## Relationship to FedRAMP

While DoD IL2 applies to defense workloads, [[FedRAMP]] High is the corresponding compliance standard for federal civilian agencies.

## Higher Impact Levels

- **IL4**: More stringent controls for sensitive unclassified information
- **IL5**: Highest standard for classified or highly sensitive defense information; future compatibility being developed

## Future IL5 Compatibility

[[GoogleCloud]] and [[Anthropic]] have indicated progress toward future IL5 compatibility, which would enable use with more sensitive Department of Defense workloads, expanding the scope of defense AI applications.
