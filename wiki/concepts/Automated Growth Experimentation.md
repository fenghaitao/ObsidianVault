## Definition

Automated Growth Experimentation is the use of AI to autonomously run the growth experimentation loop: identifying opportunities, building features, testing them, and analyzing results. It is an emerging practice pioneered at Anthropic through the CACHE initiative, and represents a future where AI handles the smaller optimization work that traditionally occupies 60-70% of a growth team's time.

## Key Information

- Four-part lifecycle that can be automated:
  1. Identifying opportunities (trends, past patterns)
  2. Building the feature and getting it ready to ship
  3. Testing and ensuring quality/brand standards
  4. Analyzing data and gathering learnings post-ship
- Currently at "junior PM, 2-3 years in" win rate — not yet at senior PM level
- Started with small-scale copy changes and minor UI tweaks
- The missing piece: cross-functional stakeholder management (still needs humans)
- "The amount of time — are people spending less time on each area? Are your results getting better? As long as week on week that's getting better, the whole initiative is scaling"
- Human review can be automated away over time: brand guidelines as a skill, models getting better at understanding do's and don'ts
- Growth teams are "more inherently suited to tackle this earlier" because of the data-driven, measurable nature of growth work
- This is a sign of things to come across product development generally — AI moving from "taking our orders and building" to "telling us what to do"
