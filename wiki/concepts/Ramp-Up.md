## Definition

Ramp-Up is the process of onboarding into a new company or level and reaching full productivity, which becomes harder for senior ICs who must rebuild context, infrastructure knowledge, and relationships.

## Key Information

- Igor framed Meta ramp-up as climbing the ladder from "zero" (knowing less than a two-week-old intern) back up to your level as fast as possible; the more senior you are, the more levels you must climb.
- He spent too much time reading docs rather than getting hands dirty; building and learning in parallel is better.
- Meta's management lacked a "recipe" for ramping senior external hires, because most senior staff grew internally.
- Cruise was easier to ramp up in because it was smaller and less crowded; Meta's senior space felt crowded with too many senior folks competing for scope.
- Senior-IC job mobility is riskier than it looks, and failure stories are rarely shared.
- Adrien Friggeri's ramp-up advice for a new domain: do "social-network exploration" first (ask who to talk to), don't be afraid to ask dumb questions, get your hands dirty checking out the code, and pick a hard problem to solve so you learn by doing.
