## Definition

Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) is a post-training technique where AI models learn by having humans compare multiple outputs and indicate which one they prefer. Edwin Chen analogizes it to "learning by writing 55 different essays and someone telling you which one they like the most."

## Key Information

- RLHF became dominant after SFT as the next evolution in post-training
- It relies on human preference judgments rather than direct demonstration
- The human analogy: learning through comparative feedback rather than copying
- RLHF is one of the products Surge AI provides to frontier AI labs
- Like SFT, RLHF is not rendered obsolete by newer techniques — it complements them
