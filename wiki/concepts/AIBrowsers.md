## Definition

AI browsers are a category of web browsers built with AI as a core design principle from the ground up, featuring integrated AI assistants that work alongside users for personalization, automation, and enhanced productivity. They represent a fundamental shift from traditional browsers that treat AI as an add-on feature.

## Key Information

- Built with AI speed and security in mind from the ground up, not retrofitted
- Key capabilities: AI assistants with personal context, memory, computer-use, form autofill, scheduling, email composition
- Security challenge: browsers sit at the intersection of private data, untrusted content, and external communication (the "lethal trifecta")
- Dia by The Browser Company is a leading example, shipped in early 2025
- Represents an evolution beyond traditional browsers like Chrome, which were designed before LLMs existed
