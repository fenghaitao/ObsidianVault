## Definition

Freedom and Responsibility is the core principle of Netflix's culture: hire exceptional people, give them the context of what needs doing and why it matters, and trust them to execute without micromanagement.

## Key Information

- From the culture memo (originally ~15 slides, first published around 2007-08): "we don't hire brilliant jerks," "we pay top of market," a "keepers test" (aggressive but fair about letting people go), and freedom and responsibility.
- The ethos: "we want to inspire people to sail the seas — we don't want to tell them how to build a ship."
- In practice: trust, freedom, responsibility, and giving people context rather than micromanaging them.
- Patty McCord embodied it by telling employees to be impressive in an 8-hour day rather than working 24/7, and telling a burnt-out team lead "figure out how to set your team up so that you don't have to work all the time."
- David Ronca's caveat: the culture memo is "aspirational — it's what we aspire to be" — and the culture did not scale cleanly as the company grew.
