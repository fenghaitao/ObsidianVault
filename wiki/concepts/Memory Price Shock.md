## Definition

Anticipated spike in memory (RAM/DRAM) prices, driven by AI datacenter demand, that [[Caitlin Kalinowski]] warns will hit consumer hardware and robotics companies — prompted by a question from [[Matic]]'s founder.

## Key Information

- Kalinowski has advised startups to pre-buy memory to hedge against this shock before it hits.
- Illustrated via [[Matic]]'s supply chain: a robot vacuum has 50-150+ major parts; a missing diecast part is recoverable in a few months, but unavailable RAM or SoC/silicon can require a catastrophic full board redesign — "all it takes is one piece to not be available and your whole thing is screwed."
