## Definition

Network Effects in the career context refers to how personal connections and relationships multiply opportunities and can change career trajectories. Jia Chen emphasized that network is powerful even for people on the same skill level.

## Key Information

- Jia wished she knew before college how much network really mattered — knowing the right opportunities and having the right people advise on applications
- Despite good test scores and GPA, people with research internships or good referral letters would still beat out others
- Network can propel a person at any stage of their career — the people someone knows can change the trajectory of where they go
- Jia demonstrated this firsthand: Sprint.dev's angel investment came from meeting someone at a Luma hot pot, and a second investor overheard and joined
- Ryan supported this: "most things are decided by people" — knowing someone can help with internships (Disney), getting into YC, or starting a company
- Jia's network also included the "community college kid building rockets in his backyard" who became her hackathon teammate
