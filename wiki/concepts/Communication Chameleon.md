## Definition

Communication Chameleon is the idea that product leaders must adapt their communication style to match the preferences of each executive they work with — like "love languages but appropriate for work." Some execs spark with a design, others with a customer story, a dashboard, or an experiment. Understanding and adapting to these preferences is crucial for influence.

## Key Information

- "We as product leaders have to be communication chameleons. We have to be speaking their language — like love languages, but appropriate for work"
- Different execs respond to different stimuli: design, customer stories, dashboards, experiments
- "Do they really turn a spark with a design, with a customer story, with a dashboard, with an experiment?"
- The goal: "How do you understand how their best brain, how their best expertise gets turned on to give you the best of themselves"
- Some execs hate PowerPoint; some only want data; some want a doc with 10 minutes of quiet reading time
- Some execs are willing to do pre-reads, some aren't
- "You just have to be willing to accept that they are also human and you're trying to give them the best shot at inculcating the information in the way that is meaningful to them"
- Advice for execs: "Just tell people, I prefer a doc. I want no upfront explanation. I want 10 minutes of quiet reading time"
