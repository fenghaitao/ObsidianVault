## Definition

CTO vs VP of Engineering is the role split James Everingham used with Mike Krieger at Instagram: the CTO owns technology, roadmap, and external brand, while the VP/Head of Engineering owns people, process, staffing, and execution.

## Key Information

- Mike Krieger ("Mikey") brought James in as engineering hit ~100 people — "a common place where teams start to break."
- CTO side: works with customers, evaluates new technologies, owns the product/technology roadmap, runs experiments, and builds the external engineering brand.
- VP/Head of Engineering side: hiring, execution, org structure, and performance reviews — "people management thing."
- They wrote and published their job descriptions to the org (plus flowcharts for "who to ask for what") to remove initial confusion about roles.
- "People confuse the difference" — companies often say "we need a new CTO" while actually describing VP-of-Engineering problems.
