## Definition

A strategic approach where a company operates with interoperability and openness rather than in a walled garden — being comfortable with having competitors' products coexist with your own. Articulated by Jeetu Patel as the third major change in Cisco's AI transformation.

## Key Information

- Articulated by Jeetu Patel
- "We cannot operate in a walled garden. We have to make sure that we operate in an open ecosystem."
- This means being completely comfortable with having competitors' products alongside Cisco's
- The decision was made about 5-5.5 years ago when Jeetu first joined Cisco
- One of three key changes that drove Cisco's AI-first transformation
- The mental model shift: from protecting the walled garden to embracing openness
