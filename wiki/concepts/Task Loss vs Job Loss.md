## Definition

A framework for understanding AI's impact on employment, articulated by Marc Andreessen: rather than focusing on "job loss" (the elimination of entire roles), the more accurate lens is "task loss" — individual tasks within a job get automated while the job itself persists longer than its constituent tasks.

## Key Information

- Articulated by Marc Andreessen on Lenny's Podcast
- The core insight: "Everybody wants to talk about job loss but really what you want to look at is task loss. The job persists longer than the individual tasks."
- Jobs are bundles of tasks, and AI typically automates specific tasks rather than entire job functions
- This reframing shifts the conversation from fear of obsolescence to understanding how roles evolve
- Implies that workers should focus on the tasks that remain human-intensive rather than worrying about entire job categories disappearing
