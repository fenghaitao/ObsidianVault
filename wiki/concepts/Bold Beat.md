## Definition

[[Mark Pincus]]'s term (developed with [[Bing Gordon]] at [[Zynga]]) for taking something that has become boring or rote (like poker) and adding "a whole new dimension" to it — most often a social dimension — to reinvigorate it.

## Key Information

- Applied at Zynga by adding social features — real identities, gifting, co-op play — to classic game mechanics (poker, word games), turning them into venues for meeting new people or strengthening relationships.
- Connected to the "invest, express, connect" design loop: players want to feel creative (invest/express) and then connect with others around what they made or did.
- Illustrates that "better" and "new" in [[Proven Better New]] don't have to be complex — a single well-chosen new dimension (like social features layered onto poker) can transform a rote mechanic into a hit.
