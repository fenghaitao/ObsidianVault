# Model Scaling

## Definition

The practice of increasing model capability by scaling up compute resources, data, and model parameters. Poolside is scaling their models with over 40,000 GB300 GPUs coming online at their West Texas data center campus.

## Key Information

- **Poolside's Scale**: Over 40,000 GB300 GPUs, multi-gigawatt data center campus
- **Impact**: "Missing ingredient" that was unlocked, enabling Poolside to scale models to higher levels of capability
- **Infrastructure**: Requires building physical data centers ("putting shovels in ground again like we did in the '90s and early 2000s")
- **Progression**: Each generation of models benefits from increased compute availability
- **Goal**: Continue pushing state-of-the-art through scaling
