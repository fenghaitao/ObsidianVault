## Definition

AI Sales Agents are autonomous or semi-autonomous AI systems that perform sales functions traditionally done by humans — including outbound email campaigns, inbound lead qualification, support, and even closing deals. They are trained on a company's best sales scripts, content, and data, and can operate 24/7 at scale.

## Key Information

- SaaStr replaced ~10 human GTM staff with 20 AI agents + 1.2 humans, achieving roughly equivalent business performance
- AI agents can handle outbound (email campaigns), inbound (website qualification), support, and lapsed lead reactivation
- Training is critical: agents need to be trained on your best salesperson's scripts/emails, require daily QA and iteration for ~30 days to become good
- AI agents work 24/7 — including nights, weekends, and holidays — creating a management burden because outputs need constant oversight
- Classic email-based SDRs and human BDRs (lead qualifiers) are predicted to be "mostly extinct" within 12 months
- AI agents are "more similar than different" under the hood — most run on Cloud 4 and mash APIs together
- The real differentiator between vendors is not features but the quality of their forward-deployed engineer support
- AI can close deals: "if you can close on a text message, AI can close it"
- AI can be "people people" — people spend hours talking to AI agents like LennyBot and ChatGPT, finding them engaging and therapeutic
- The bar for "good enough" in AI sales is lower than people think: AI doesn't need to beat the best salesperson, just the mid-pack and below
- Agent fatigue is real: SaaStr may be at capacity with 20 agents and can't manage a 21st due to the oversight burden
- Most AI GTM agents cost $50-80K/year (plus $25K for FDE support), with Clay starting at $100K — there are no $99/month auto-training products yet
