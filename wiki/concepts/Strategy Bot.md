## Definition

A Strategy Bot is an AI agent that constantly watches metrics, the market, the roadmap, and what's working and what's not — then proactively suggests strategic pivots and opportunities. Lenny Rachitsky raised this concept during the conversation with Amol Avaisary, who believes we'll get there later in 2026.

## Key Information

- Lenny's framing: "Teams will have a strategy bot — imagine an agent that's constantly watching metrics, the market, the roadmap, what's working and what's not, and just like 'Hey Amol, here's what I think we should do now. Here's the pivot we should take. Here's where we're going to win.'"
- Amol's response: "I think we're close. I think we'll get there later this year to the point where that's very very effective. That's my gut."
- The misalignment detection work is a version of this — Claude proactively looking across context and distilling insights
- The next evolution: looking across more data sources, more proactivity
- Connects to the CACHE initiative: automating the "identify opportunities" part of the growth experimentation loop
- "This level of proactivity and getting looking across a bunch of context and distilling insights"
