## Definition

P(doom) stands for "probability of doom" — a concept in AI safety that asks: given a malicious or uncontrolled AI, what is the probability that it causes catastrophic harm?

## Key Information

- The concept is discussed in the context of the AI safety subfield called "control," which studies how to keep a potentially malicious AI contained and useful.
- An analogy used: "not only do you have a god in the box, but that god is angry. That god's malicious. That god wants to hurt you. Can we control that malicious AI and make it useful to us and make sure nothing bad happens?"
- Sander Schulhoff's MATS (ML Alignment and Theory Scholars) program works on topics including AI safety, security, sabotage, eval awareness, sandbagging, and control.
- The concept connects to the broader alignment problem of AI systems — how to keep them from doing things we don't want.
- The parallel to prompt injection: every security team deploying AI agents must think about containment, similar to the control problem.
