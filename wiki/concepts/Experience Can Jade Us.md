## Definition

Jeetu Patel's observation that experience, while valuable, can create harmful biases. The combination of experience with complete inexperience creates the magic — fresh perspectives ask questions that experienced people might never think to ask, while pattern recognition guides the way.

## Key Information

- Articulated by Jeetu Patel
- "One of the worst things I think companies do sometimes is they put too much emphasis only on solely on experience."
- "Experience is good but experience can actually be meaningfully bad in some areas where you get too biased."
- "You almost have to say that I have to have the ability to unlearn."
- "The combination of experience with complete inexperience is what creates the magic because the inexperience allows you to ask questions that you might have never had with experience."
- "The combo of those two gives you the best of the pattern recognition plus the charting new territory that's never been kind of walked on before."
- Relates to the debate about young people and AI: "People worry about young people and people graduating out of college right now and jobs and AI, but they're the people that are most open-minded about what AI can do for them."
- "Entry level people will never be hired again — that's the stupidest thing a company can do because now what you've done is you have completely shut the door to new fresh ideas."
