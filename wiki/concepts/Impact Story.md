## Definition

An Impact Story is a compelling narrative of accomplishments and delivered value that an engineer uses to sell themselves during interviews and negotiations. It is the primary vehicle for job hopping to higher levels, since interviewers only see the output and impact, not the hours worked or negative internal data.

## Key Information

- Zach emphasizes that "how you interview is the highest-leverage, most important part of your career — more important than the impact you even have on the role"
- In interviews, the new company sees a different vantage point: they know what you accomplished and the impact you had, but not how many hours you worked, any negative internal feedback, or the sustainability concerns your current company may have
- Zach stayed at Airbnb an extra six months specifically because he didn't yet have a good enough impact story to leave
- An impact story should "make it sound like a movie" — make it compelling, impressive, and demonstrate a trajectory of solving increasingly hard problems
- Zach's impact story from Facebook that sold Netflix included: developing the Reachability metric to prevent notification spam, building the first cross-app growth dashboard, and making the notifications ML pipeline 90% cheaper
- A strong impact story also serves to counteract the "disloyal job hopper" stigma that comes with short tenures — it reframes you as "extremely ambitious and on a trajectory to solve hard problems"
- At Netflix, Zach's impact story of interfacing with 11 teams and solving complex security problems was what Airbnb recognized as staff-level work
- The concept applies regardless of tenure: always think about what story you'll tell about your impact at your current company
