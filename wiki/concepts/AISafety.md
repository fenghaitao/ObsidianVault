## Definition

AI safety is the practice of ensuring AI systems behave as intended and don't cause harm. Niamh Gavin's PyAI Conf talk highlighted real-world safety failures: an agent naming containers "MS-13" (triggering security escalation) and a training run consuming all compute debating theological vs. biological claims.
