## Definition
Brandolini's Law (also known as the Bullshit Asymmetry Principle) states that the amount of energy needed to refute bullshit is an order of magnitude bigger than that needed to produce it. swyx references this as the closest existing law to the slop problem.

## Key Information
- Original formulation: the energy needed to refute BS is orders of magnitude larger than needed to produce it
- swyx uses this as an analogy for the slop problem in AI
- Inspired swyx's own "Law of Anti-Slop": the amount of taste needed to fight slop is orders of magnitude larger than that needed to produce it
- The asymmetry is exacerbated by AI: token generation costs dropping 100-1000x per year makes slop production increasingly cheap while fighting it remains expensive
- Represents the fundamental challenge in maintaining quality in an era of abundant AI-generated content
- In the 2024 AI Engineer Summit keynote, Eugene Yan adapted Brandolini's Law to LLM applications: the amount of energy needed to catch and fix defects is an order of magnitude larger than needed to produce them. This was used to motivate the importance of automated guardrails.
