# The Score Takes Care of Itself

A philosophy embraced by [[Qasar Younis]] and [[Applied Intuition]]. Focus on the work, the craft, and the process — the results (score, valuation, metrics) will follow naturally.

## Core Idea

"The score takes care of itself." — attributed to Bill Walsh, legendary NFL coach. Applied Intuition echoed this with "The valuation takes care of itself" in their Series D announcement.

## Key Points
- [[Joe Montana]] (former 49ers quarterback under Bill Walsh) is an investor in Applied Intuition
- The philosophy connects to the broader ethos of staying quiet, focusing on craft, and not getting wrapped up in your own narrative
- Applied Intuition has never spent any money it raised — the business fundamentals took care of themselves
- "Sometimes people come to our office and say 'you must have this giant cleaning staff.' It's like, actually we clean our office."
- Qasar: "Like the Japanese schools... students clean their own schools. We have a cleaning zen every week."
- There's a direct line between "be quiet and alone," "clean your desk," and "well-written software" — all part of the same arc
- Half the work is follow-up — maintenance is the business
