## Definition
Life OS is the vision of a unified operating system for personal productivity — a single tool that integrates todos, habits, calendar, events, routines, and all other life-management functions. Kitze has pursued this vision since childhood, realizing he never wanted a to-do app but rather "sort of like a life OS."

## Key Information
- Kitze's realization: "I never wanted a to-do app, I wanted like sort of like a life OS"
- Requires integration of todos, habits, planner, events — "if these three are not together, I can never make like a mini OS"
- The friction problem: forms to input data are the bottleneck; oscillating between intense usage and complete abandonment
- Kitze's Benji was an attempt at a Life OS with 60+ features — still unshipped after 3-4 years
- Vision extends to "Benji phone and a Benji OS"
- Discord and Telegram "were not meant for Life OS" — makeshift UIs for something that needs purpose-built interfaces
- The ultimate Life OS: computer ingests all life information (notifications, emails, todos) and greets user with the next task, then the next, with breaks
