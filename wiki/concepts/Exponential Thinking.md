## Definition

[[Anthropic]]'s core internal mental model, described by [[Amol]]: because underlying model capability grows exponentially, the product value an AI company can deliver compounds exponentially too — very unlike a typical non-AI product, where added features raise user value only incrementally (Amol estimates 30-50% more value over two years for a normal product, versus 100-1000x for an AI-native one).

## Key Information

- Practical consequence for growth strategy: since the differential between today's product value and future product value is so large for AI-native products, teams should weight more heavily toward large strategic bets rather than small/medium optimizations — the opposite allocation from a typical growth team (Amol's team runs roughly 50/50 or more toward large bets, versus a traditional 60-70% small-to-medium / 20-30% large split).
- Illustrated by agentic coding: a market that didn't exist ~18 months before this episode, whose value quickly dwarfed the prior, more limited AI-coding-assistance market — evidence that new markets unlocked by capability jumps can eclipse previous ones.
- Culturally reinforced inside Anthropic: Amol notes that internally, "linear charts are just not cool" — teams default to showing metrics on a log-linear scale, reflecting the exponential framing baked into how the company talks about its own progress.
- Applies at the organizational-culture level too: Amol argues teams that think linearly underestimate how soon big changes will actually arrive, while those thinking in exponential terms correctly expect major shifts sooner than intuition suggests.
