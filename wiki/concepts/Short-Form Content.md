## Definition

Short-Form Content is a content strategy focused on creating brief, high-impact videos (3-5 seconds) that require minimal effort but generate high leverage through broad reach. Jia Chen used this strategy to grow to 50K followers while balancing other commitments.

## Key Information

- Jia's approach: make 3-5 second reels that get a lot of views with minimal effort, or casual reels filmed wherever she was during the day
- She recognized that short-form content provides high leverage — a small amount of effort can reach a large audience
- Her philosophy: "there's 24 hours in the day and enough time for a one minute reel"
- A lot of people overthink content and think it's hard to balance, but short-form content is relatively manageable
- Her first hackathon video blew up after her 16th win because people love interesting content and seeing students win cash prizes
- A video about receiving angel checks for Sprint.dev got 500K views on Instagram, leading to more investment
- Jia's advice for getting over fear of posting: consult close friends first, build a support network of people who will support you no matter what
