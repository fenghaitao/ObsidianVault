## Definition

The software crisis was the late-1960s/early-1970s concern that software projects were chronically late, over budget, and unreliable.

## Key Information

- It was one of the two threads that converged in Knuth's "Structured Programming with go to Statements."
- It motivated the structured-programming movement.
