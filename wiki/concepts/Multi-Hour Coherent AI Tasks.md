## Definition

Multi-Hour Coherent AI Tasks refers to the next frontier in AI agent capability: models that can work coherently on tasks lasting hours (rather than minutes), maintaining context and making good decisions over extended periods. Sherwin Wu predicts this will be achievable in the next 12-18 months.

## Key Information

- Currently, frontier models can do multi-hour software engineering tasks ~50% of the time; ~80% reliability for tasks under an hour
- The trend line (tracked on benchmarks like Meter) shows rapid improvement in task duration capability
- Sherwin's prediction: in 12-18 months, models could do multi-hour tasks "very, very coherently"
- Eventually: 6+ hour tasks where you dispatch the agent and have it work on its own for a while
- Current products optimize for minute-long tasks (Codex, Cursor, etc. are interactive and optimized for ~10-minute tasks)
- The types of products you build around multi-hour agents will look very different from current tools
- You'll want to give the model feedback, and you probably don't want it to "completely run wild for a day"
- The universe of things the model can do expands dramatically with longer task duration
