## Definition

"We're on the same team" is a powerful opening phrase for difficult conversations. Dr. Becky describes it as "the most amazing way to have a more productive conversation with anyone." It establishes that the conversation is about the behavior, not the person's identity, and that you're working together toward a solution.

## Key Information

- "If that's the only thing people take from this, that is the most amazing way to have a more productive conversation with anyone. I want to say we're on the same team."
- Full example: "I want to say we're on the same team. I know you're a good person. You probably don't need me to tell you that we need to start meetings on time. We both know that. It's also been happening consistently, which lets me know something is going on that I want to get to the bottom of with you."
- The phrase creates psychological safety: the person doesn't need to defend their identity, only discuss their behavior
- "We all want the same outcome" — even kids who hit don't want to feel out of control
- "I think actually we all want the same outcome."
- Using MGI leads to the realization: "We're on the same team."
