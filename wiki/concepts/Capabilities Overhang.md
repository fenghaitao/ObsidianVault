## Definition

The gap between the capabilities that AI technology already possesses and what enterprises are actually able to adopt and deploy in practice. There is a "paradox of progress" — on one end, AI is solving amazing scientific problems; on the other, enterprises are struggling with adoption.

## Key Information

- Articulated by Jeetu Patel at Cisco's AI summit as a key takeaway
- "Capabilities overhang is real" — more functionality exists than organizations can currently absorb
- Organizations need help bridging the gap between available AI capabilities and practical adoption
- Beyond obvious use cases like coding, applying AI to other business functions requires nuance and domain-specific understanding
