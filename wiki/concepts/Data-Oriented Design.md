## Definition
Data-oriented design is an approach to performance that centers on how data is laid out and accessed in memory, popularized in part by Zig creator Andrew Kelley.

## Key Information
- Charlie cites Andrew Kelley's data-oriented design talk as highly influential, reshaping how he looks at software.
- The talk covers design decisions in the Zig compiler and thinking deeply about memory and allocation.
- Echos his broader point that performance is about more than the language — even within Rust there is "a ton of room" to think about design (e.g., Andrew Gallant's u64 version representation in uv).
