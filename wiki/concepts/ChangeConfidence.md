## Definition
Change confidence is a qualitative metric measuring the percentage of engineers who feel confident in the changes they are putting into production. It is a key indicator in DX's measurement framework and shows extreme volatility under AI adoption.

## Key Information
- Defined as the percentage of people who answered positively that they feel confident in changes going to production
- DX data shows an average 2.6% increase in change confidence with AI adoption
- Per-company breakdown reveals extreme volatility: some organizations see +20% while others see -20%
- This volatility is masked by innocuous-looking industry averages
- Part of the quality dimension in DX's measurement approach
