## Definition

Taste Profile is a Spotify feature (launched May 2026, initially in limited markets) that exposes what Spotify's models know about a user and allows the user to edit that understanding via natural language. User edits feed back into Spotify's generative recommender system, improving personalization.

## Key Information

- **What It Does**: Surfaces a textual representation of what Spotify understands about the user's taste, then lets the user add, remove, or modify that understanding
- **User Control**: Users can tell Spotify what to keep, what to forget, and what to emphasize — e.g., "start listening to Justin Bieber more" or "stop recommending this podcast"
- **Feedback Loop**: Edits are sent back into the generative model, upgrading its ability to understand and recommend for that specific user
- **Steerability**: Part of Spotify's broader move toward steerable, user-controlled personalization via natural language
- **Expansion**: Initially launched in limited markets with plans to expand later in 2026
