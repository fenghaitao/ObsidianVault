## Definition
Chat Agents are AI agents accessed through a text-based chat interface. They became the dominant app interaction paradigm in 2025, with companies like Linear, PostHog, and gov.uk moving their home screens to chat interfaces. Chat agents support tool calling, RAG, and declarative interaction, but are limited to text — they represent the baseline that voice agents upgrade.

## Key Information
- Became the default app home screen in 2025 for many SaaS products
- Declarative interaction model: users describe what they want, agents execute via tool calling and RAG
- Provide a great quick-start experience for users
- Luke Harries's prediction: chat agents will either die or add voice
- Chat agents already handle tool calling, RAG, evals, and integrations — voice wraps around this infrastructure
- The government (gov.uk) also adopted chat agent interfaces
- Limitation: doesn't feel like "building the future" — voice is the natural next step
