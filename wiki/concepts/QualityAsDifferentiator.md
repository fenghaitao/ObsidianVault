## Definition
Quality as differentiator is the strategic insight that in a world where AI makes software fast and cheap to produce, quality and craftsmanship become the key competitive differentiators. The bar is being set much higher, and craftsmanship is what separates thoughtful software from "vibe-coded slop."

## Key Information
- Articulated by Maggie Appleton of GitHub Next
- In a world of fast, cheap software, quality becomes the new differentiator
- The bar is being set much higher — craftsmanship sets you apart from "vibe-coded slop"
- Craft still costs time and energy — it's not free
- To buy the time and energy needed for craft, teams must do fewer things better, which requires strong alignment
- Agents have gifted back the time that implementation used to consume
- The opportunity is not to go faster and build more of the same crappy software, but to make much better software through rigorous critical thinking and better alignment
- By doing more exploration, more research, and thinking through problems more deeply than before
- Agents should help teams do higher quality work, get aligned faster, and build a few exceptional things rather than a thousand crappy ones
- There are more distractions than ever — it's easy to prompt your way to the wrong thing or add unnecessary features
- Before coding agents, no one had enough time to make products the way they wanted — implementation consumed all the time and effort
