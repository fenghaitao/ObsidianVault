## Definition
On-Device Robotics is the practice of powering robots entirely with on-device AI (vision, audio, speech) running locally on embedded hardware, without cloud dependency. Demonstrated by Prince Canuma using MLX Audio and MLX VLM to give a Richie Mini robot perception and voice capabilities.

## Key Information
- Robot perception powered by on-device MLX: MLX VLM for vision, MLX Audio for speech/audio
- Voice interaction via Marvis TTS with real-time voice cloning (e.g., Jarvis/Iron Man voice)
- All processing happens locally on the robot's hardware, no cloud dependency
- Enables agents that can "hear, see, and sound just like you or your loved ones"
- Runs on Apple Silicon hardware (iPhone, iPad, Mac) or embedded in the robot
- Demonstrated with Richie Mini robot by Prince Canuma
- Represents convergence of on-device vision, audio, and robotics capabilities
- Future direction for on-device AI: moving beyond screens to physical interaction
