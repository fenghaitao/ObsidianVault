## Definition

Simultaneous Localization and Mapping — the mapping/spatial-tracking technology used in devices like [[Matic]]'s robot vacuum (kept on-device rather than sent to the cloud, for privacy) and originally developed at scale for VR headsets like [[Oculus]]'s [[Quest 2]].

## Key Information

- [[Caitlin Kalinowski]] notes VR development at Oculus/Meta drove foundational SLAM, depth-sensing, and spatial-perception technology that is now directly reused across robotics and autonomy.
