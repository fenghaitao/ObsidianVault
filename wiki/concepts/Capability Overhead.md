## Definition

Capability Overhead is the challenge where AI model capabilities improve faster than products can adapt to diffuse those benefits to users. It is described by Amol Avaisary as "one of the biggest problems in the industry" — by the time you test a new model's capabilities, build the right onboarding flows, and ship them, the next model has already unlocked newer capabilities that make those learnings irrelevant.

## Key Information

- "The models are just getting better so quickly. The real challenge is on the product side of how do we start to diffuse those benefits to people"
- Even internally at Anthropic, when a new model is available, you need to "really carve out time" to understand what it can do and update your priors
- For most users: they may have a model capable of "all sorts of crazy things" but they come in asking "what's the weather in SF?" — they don't get the most out of the product
- The cycle: new model → run tests → figure out capabilities → build right on-ramps → ship new flow → next model is already out, making learnings irrelevant
- "It's actually just like a really difficult problem to stay on top of"
- This is why activation is such a critical challenge in AI products
- The right approach: identify user characteristics, recommend the right feature/product, add friction to help users understand what's possible
