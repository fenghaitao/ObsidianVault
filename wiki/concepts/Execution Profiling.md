## Definition

Execution profiling is the measurement of where a program spends its time, used to target optimization.

## Key Information

- The newly available execution profiler was one of the two threads behind Knuth's "premature optimization" essay.
- Casey argues profiling is the correct basis for optimization: only optimize what you've measured as mattering.
