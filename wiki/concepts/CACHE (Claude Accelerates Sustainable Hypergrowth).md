## Definition

CACHE (Claude Accelerates Sustainable Hypergrowth) is an Anthropic growth platform initiative to use Claude to automate the growth experimentation loop. It was kicked off in early 2026 and led by Alexey Komissarouk. The name was not coined by Amol Avaisary, who finds it "a little cringey."

## Key Information

- Acronym: Claude Accelerates Sustainable Hypergrowth
- Led by Alexey Komissarouk (growth engineer who teaches at Reforge)
- Kicked off "a couple of months ago" (from mid-2026) — only became possible with Opus 4.5/4.6
- Automates four parts of the growth experimentation lifecycle:
  1. **Identifying opportunities**: Claude identifies opportunities based on trends and past patterns
  2. **Building the feature**: Claude builds and prepares the feature for shipping
  3. **Testing**: Ensuring quality and brand standards are met
  4. **Analyzing results**: Gathering data and learnings post-ship
- Currently delivering results at a "junior PM, 2-3 years in" win rate — not yet at senior PM level
- Started with small-scale copy changes and minor UI tweaks
- "It ultimately prints money" — you can press play and it works
- Human-in-the-loop for brand approval currently, but expected to decrease as models improve
- The one missing piece: cross-functional stakeholder management still needs human brains
- "This wasn't available at all a couple of months ago" — the exponential is kicking in
