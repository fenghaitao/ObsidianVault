## Definition

An AI Native Employee is someone who starts with the question "what can AI do here?" before adding their own value, rather than starting with their own value and augmenting with AI. This mindset shift is a key differentiator for working at AI companies.

## Key Information

- Elena Verna: "The mindset is completely shifted. You start with what can AI do here versus where do I add value."
- At AI companies, AI use is an expectation, not a nice-to-have or something someone asks you to do
- AI is described as "average intelligence" that gets the platform up, then human thinking and creativity take it to the next level
- The leap to becoming AI-native happens much faster when surrounded by people who expect it, vs. reading about it
- AI-native new graduates are entering the workforce with this mindset already built-in
- Elena recommends joining an AI company to "leapfrog on what it means to be an AI-native employee"
- AI-native employees use AI for everything: brainstorming (ChatGPT deep thinking), prototyping (Lovable/vibe coding), meeting summaries (Granola), voice-to-text (Whisper Flow), customer support automation
