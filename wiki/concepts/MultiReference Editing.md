## Definition
Multi-Reference Editing is a capability where a generative model takes multiple input images simultaneously and combines or edits them intelligently. Black Forest Labs' Flux 2 supports up to 10 reference images at once, enabling use cases like outfit creation from multiple clothing images and virtual furniture placement.

## Key Information
- Flux 2 supports up to 10 images simultaneously as references
- Model intelligently combines references: e.g., given six clothing images, creates a coherent outfit with jacket worn properly and tie correctly placed
- E-commerce applications: virtual furniture placement (imagine a sofa in your living room), product visualization
- Character, product, and style consistency maintained across edits
- Flux 2 Klein supports multi-reference editing in under 1 second (vs. Kwen ~20s)
- Enables storyboarding: starting from an input image, create narrative sequences with consistent characters
