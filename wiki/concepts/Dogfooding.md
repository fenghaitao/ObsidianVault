## Definition

Dogfooding (eating your own dog food) is the practice of using your own product internally to test it and discover improvements. At OpenAI, dogfooding is done extensively but can sometimes produce different signals from the general market.

## Key Information

- OpenAI "dogfoods product a ton" — all technical staff use Codex, and usage intensity has grown significantly over the year
- Codex has been accelerating OpenAI across the entire year, from research to design to marketing
- The cloud version of Codex was a massive internal accelerator, but the signal from dogfooding was different from the general market
- OpenAI employees are "very used to this kind of prompting — think up front, run things massively in parallel, and come back to it later asynchronously"
- This is because they "train reasoning models all day," so they have different usage patterns than typical developers
- Lesson: internal dogfooding is valuable but must be complemented with external market signals
- Alexander Imbiricos maintains multiple ChatGPT Pro accounts (at $200/month each) to correctly dogfood the signup and onboarding experience
