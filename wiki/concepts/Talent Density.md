## Definition

Talent Density refers to the concentration of exceptional engineers within a team or company. Philip Su observed that OpenAI had the highest talent density of any group he'd worked with, even higher than early Facebook.

## Key Information

- Philip described OpenAI (~120 engineers) as having the highest talent density of any group of engineers he'd worked with
- He ranked the talent density progression: Microsoft < Facebook (early) < OpenAI
- At Microsoft, his Tablet PC team had three distinguished engineers on a team of ~20 people — rare talent density at that company
- At Facebook, the quality jump from Microsoft was significant — "full of amazing people, especially in early Facebook"
- OpenAI was "even a notch above" early Facebook in talent density
- High talent density means people have exceptional technical capabilities and you learn a lot from peers
