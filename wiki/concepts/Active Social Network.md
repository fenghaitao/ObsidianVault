## Definition

Proprietary metric ("ASN") built at [[Zynga]] measuring how many reciprocal social interactions ("round trips") a player had with a friend or another player — e.g., taking a turn and having it returned, gifting and being gifted back.

## Key Information

- Going from an ASN of 0 to 1 correlated with an 80% chance the player would return the following month.
- Reaching an ASN of 4 correlated with an 80% chance the player would be active on 22 of the next 30 days.
- Framed by [[Mark Pincus]] as connected to basic positive-feedback psychology ("someone likes your photos, comments... that's probably the biggest dopamine hit we get") and as a metric the team could deliberately design/innovate against, unlike raw virality.
- A leading indicator for [[Day 365 Retention]], Zynga's primary north-star success metric.
