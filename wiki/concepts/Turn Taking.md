## Definition
Turn Taking in voice agents is the ability to detect when a user has finished speaking and when the agent should respond, using emotion-context-aware detection of pauses and semantic boundaries. It is a critical component of natural voice interaction, preventing the agent from interrupting or waiting too long.

## Key Information
- Must be emotion-context-aware to handle natural pauses, hesitations, and semantic completions
- Combines pause detection with semantic understanding of when a thought is complete
- A core component of ElevenLabs Voice Engine alongside speech-to-text and text-to-speech
- Poor turn taking leads to robotic-feeling conversations (interruptions or awkward silences)
- Enables natural back-and-forth conversation flow in voice agents
