## Definition

The traditional design process (research → discovery → diverge → converge → prototype → mock) that designers have been taught and treated as gospel is dying. It was already fading before the age of AI, but AI has accelerated its demise dramatically. Engineers can now spin off features rapidly with AI tools, and designers can't keep up with the old process.

## Key Information

- Jenny Wen gave a talk in Berlin (September 2025) called "Don't Trust the Design Process" arguing this
- The process was treated as gospel: "trust the process" was the mantra
- It was already dying before AI, but AI has accelerated the change
- Even 3-4 months after her talk, Jenny felt it was already becoming outdated
- The talk was Jenny's most resonant ever, but also received backlash from designers invested in the traditional process
- The result of engineering changing a bunch is that design is "sort of forced to change"
- Designers are now spending less time mocking (down from 60-70% to 30-40%) and more time pairing with engineers and implementing
