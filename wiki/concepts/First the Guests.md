## Definition

"First the guests" is a family motto used by Jessica Fain and her husband with their three kids: when hosting guests, guests always go first. It represents a philosophy of being of service to others, being respectful, welcoming, and kind — and putting others ahead of yourself.

## Key Information

- Jessica Fain's family motto: "First the guests" — guests always go first in their household
- Represents values of service, respect, welcoming, and kindness
- "When you are of service to others, when you are inclusive, when you can put them first ahead of yourself, it actually brings a lot of joy to you"
- Aligns with Airbnb's core value "Be a host"
- Also aligns with the philosophy: "If you want a village, be a villager" — building community by reaching out and being there for others
