## Definition

Business Process Automation (BPA) in AI refers to the application of AI to automate repeatable business operations and standard operating procedures (SOPs), as distinct from open-ended knowledge work like software engineering. Sherwin Wu identifies this as a massively underrated opportunity because Silicon Valley focuses on engineering while most of the economy runs on repeatable processes.

## Key Information

- Sherwin Wu is "extremely bullish" on this category and considers it underrated
- The distinction: software engineering is open-ended, non-repeatable knowledge work (you don't build the same feature twice). Most jobs are repeatable business processes with SOPs that don't deviate much.
- Examples: support lines, utility companies, insurance claims processing — these run on established procedures
- Key characteristics of BPA opportunities: high determinism, repeatable operations, fully integrated with business data and systems
- Silicon Valley's bubble: we talk about coding and engineering, but most of the world's work is business processes
- "It's bigger than you would think it is based off of how people talk about it or don't talk about it on X or Twitter"
- The opportunity is not just in automating tasks but in transforming how entire companies operate
- This is distinct from the agent/AI coding trends that dominate AI discourse
