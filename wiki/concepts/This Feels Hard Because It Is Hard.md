## Definition

"This feels hard because it is hard, not because I'm doing something wrong" is Dr. Becky Kennedy's life motto. It reframes difficulty as inherent to the situation rather than evidence of personal failure. It is equally true in parenting and at work.

## Key Information

- Dr. Becky's favorite life motto: "This feels hard because it is hard, not because I'm doing something wrong."
- "It's so interesting how often something is hard and we tell ourselves the story that we're stupid or we're not good at it."
- "There's something so simple about reminding myself, no, like this feels hard because it is hard. Like I'm feeling this moment, right? It's just hard."
- "I find that I always feel my feet on the ground again when I tell myself that."
- "This is so true in parenting at work."
