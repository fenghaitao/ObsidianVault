## Definition

AI DJ is a Spotify product that provides a conversational recommendation experience — users can talk to it and it plays and recommends content. It is part of Spotify's shift toward steerable, natural language-driven personalization powered by generative recommender systems.

## Key Information

- **Conversational Interface**: Users interact with AI DJ through natural language conversation
- **Generative Backend**: Powered by Spotify's unified generative recommender system that combines user embeddings, semantic IDs, and soft tokenization
- **Product Category**: One of several steerable personalization products alongside Prompted Playlist and Taste Profile
- **Part of LLM Era Shift**: Represents Spotify's move from passive recommendation feeds to interactive, user-steered experiences
