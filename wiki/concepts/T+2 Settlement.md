## Definition

T+2 settlement means a trade settles two business days after execution; during that window, intermediaries must post collateral to counterparties.

## Key Information

- During the GameStop saga, Robinhood needed to post billions of dollars in collateral on Jan 28, 2021 for open positions because of T+2 settlement
- This collateral requirement — not price manipulation — is why Robinhood paused buys on meme stocks
- Jay cited T+2 (and the roughly 40-year-old financial rails behind it) as evidence the financial system is archaic, which inspired building Sei
