## Definition

Giving Product Away for Free is an AI-native growth strategy where companies treat LLM inference costs as marketing spend rather than margin drain. By removing the barrier of entry — including free credits for hackathons, events, and ambassadors — companies drive word-of-mouth and let users do the distribution for them.

## Key Information

- Elena Verna calls this "part of our growth secret sauce" at Lovable
- Strategy: "If somebody wants to do all of the marketing and activating for us, why would we prevent them? Take it. How much do you need?"
- LLM costs on free/premium giveaways are tracked as marketing cost, not as something to reduce for margin improvement
- This is uncomfortable for traditional tech companies with high margin profiles, but AI companies have lower margins (40% range) and must flip the script
- Giving product away works because: (1) AI is so new people need to explore capabilities for free, (2) the "wow moment" drives word-of-mouth, (3) once hooked, users are more likely to stay
- The more mind-blowing the product, the more you should give it away for free
- Lovable sponsors every hackathon and event with free credits; treats them "like candy"
- This is more cost-efficient than paid marketing: cost per eyeball through product giveaways is lower than Google AdWords
- It's product-led growth supercharged: the product itself drives awareness through agents in the ecosystem
- Essential in competitive markets where competitors will give it away if you don't
