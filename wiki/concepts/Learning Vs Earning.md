## Definition

Learning Vs Earning is a career decision framework stating that at any point in your career, you should be getting at least one of learning or earning from your job. If you're getting neither, it's time to leave. This framework helps engineers evaluate whether to stay or move on.

## Key Information

- Zach references the adage: "In your career, you should be learning or earning, or both"
- Zach prioritized learning over earning throughout his career, even at the staff level — this is why he left Airbnb to pursue entrepreneurship
- At Netflix, Zach was earning a lot ($550K+) but stopped learning when the org was restructured into Data Science, which no longer aligned with his career goals — he quit with nothing lined up
- At Airbnb, Zach was making great money but "got bored" because he stopped learning, which motivated his eventual departure
- Zach contrasts this with most Netflix engineers, who he believes are more motivated by earning and retiring early, while Meta engineers are more learning-motivated
- The framework explains why Zach was willing to leave Facebook for Netflix even though Facebook offered a clearer path to principal engineer — he would learn more at Netflix
- The framework also explains why Zach took a risk on entrepreneurship: the learning opportunity of building a business outweighed the earning security of a staff engineer salary
