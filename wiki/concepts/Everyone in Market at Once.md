## Definition

A market phenomenon unique to the AI era where 50%+ of prospects are actively in-market for AI productivity tools simultaneously, compared to the traditional 3-5% for B2B software. This is driven by top-down mandates ("we need to adopt AI") and creates unprecedented demand for AI-native companies while further starving legacy B2B of buyers.

## Key Information

- Traditional B2B dynamic: 3-5% of prospects in-market per year — you'd send "a trillion emails" hoping 2026 was the year they'd switch
- AI-era dynamic: "In many categories, we're north of 50% in market" — not one law firm looking at Harvey, "it's everyone"
- Driven by top-down pressure: "We need to adopt AI. We need to be more productive."
- Example: "I need to bring a Vibe Code tool into my company. Go out and do the work. Compare Replit, Lovable, and whoever else and buy one."
- This is a version of the "AI bubble" that will eventually end — "not everybody can be in market every year. It exhausts an enterprise."
- The phenomenon will revert to old-school dynamics eventually, but for now it totally changes how you run GTM
- It creates a bifurcated market: AI-native companies drowning in demand, legacy B2B struggling to find any buyers
- "We've never had so many people in market at once" — except for artificial windows like 2020
