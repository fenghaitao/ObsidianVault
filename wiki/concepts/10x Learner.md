## Definition

"10x Learner" is Zevi Arnovitz's philosophy, learned from a failed product review at Wix, that the key to success as a junior PM is not trying to be a 10x performer but being a 10x learner. No one expects you to know all the answers — they expect you to learn fast.

## Key Information

- Originated from Zevi's first product review failure at Wix, where he tried to impress senior PMs instead of learning from them
- After failing, Zevi realized: "They had zero expectation of me being a 10x PM, but the expectation of me was being a 10x learner"
- Practical application: Zevi identified each senior PM's unique strength and used them as mentors for that specific area
  - Ner: best product sense
  - Oya: methodology and frameworks expert
  - Yara (head of product): system thinking, third and fourth order effects
- This approach had two benefits: (1) Zevi learned a ton, (2) his success felt like their success — they were proud of their mentee
- Connects to Carol Dweck's growth mindset: "becoming is better than being"
- Zevi: "This is probably the best tip that I give now to junior PMs — be the best learner you can be at the beginning"
- The AI workflow embodies this philosophy: Zevi uses `/learning-opportunity` to constantly learn from AI about technical concepts
