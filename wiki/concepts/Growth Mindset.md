## Definition

Growth mindset is the belief that abilities can be developed through dedication and hard work. Tomer Cohen describes it as a "second religion" at home and frames it as "becoming is better than being."

## Key Information

- Tomer Cohen describes growth mindset as a "second religion" in his household.
- His favorite phrase from the concept: "becoming is better than being" — emphasizing progress, iteration, and the journey over reaching a fixed state.
- He applies this to the full-stack builder model: there is no end state to reach, only continuous progress and improvement.
- He measures himself by the delta between versions of himself (e.g., "Tomer version 2026 versus 2025").
- This philosophy ties directly to his approach to organizational change: continuous progress, not a one-time transformation.
- It also connects to the observation that top talent has an innate need to stay at the cutting edge of how they build.
- Zevi Arnovitz cites Carol Dweck's book "Mindset" as life-changing: "I was always with a fixed mindset and then after reading that I kind of understood that it was something holding me back"
- Zevi's "10x learner" philosophy is a direct application of growth mindset to product management
- Zevi's failure at his first Wix product review taught him: "They had zero expectation of me being a 10x PM, but the expectation of me was being a 10x learner"
- Zevi: "Since then I've been really really trying to cultivate a growth mindset"
