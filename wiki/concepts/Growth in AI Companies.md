## Definition

Growth in AI Companies is a fundamentally different discipline from traditional growth. According to Amol Avaisary, if your core product value is AI-backed, growth teams should shift from 60-70% small/medium optimizations to 70/30 or 50/50 large bets vs. small bets, because exponential product value improvement makes micro-optimizations relatively insignificant.

## Key Information

- **Key shift**: Index more toward larger swings as opposed to smaller optimizations
- Traditional growth: 60-70% small/medium bets, 20-30% larger swings
- AI growth at Anthropic: 70/30 or 50/50 flipped the other way
- Reason: "The product value that we will deliver in 2 years' time is probably like a thousand X what it is today"
- Only applies to AI-first products — if AI is a side feature, traditional growth allocation may still be appropriate
- "If the primary value that your product delivers is underpinned by AI as a central element of it, then you should operate this way"
- Examples of AI-first companies that should operate this way: Lovable, Cursor, and other AI-native businesses
- Still do small optimizations — "the compounding value is not immaterial" — but take on "much larger core product type of swings"
- The growth team built the Chrome extension (a "very AI-filled, research-heavy product") — something Amol wouldn't have done at a traditional company

### How AI Is Reshaping Growth Roles
- **Automation**: The CACHE initiative is automating the growth experimentation loop
- **PM/Eng ratios**: Engineering is getting 2-3x productivity from Claude Code, but PMs and designers haven't gotten the same leverage — creating a bottleneck
- **Two-Week PM Rule**: Engineers act as PMs for projects under 2 weeks
- **Product-Minded Engineers**: Value goes up "an order of magnitude"
- **Counterintuitive**: May need MORE PMs, not fewer, because engineers are so accelerated
- **Capability Overhead**: AI models improve faster than products can adapt — activation is a critical challenge
- **Success Disasters**: 70% of growth time is firefighting problems caused by success
