## Definition

Garden leave is a paid non-compete period after leaving a finance firm, during which an employee is compensated to not work so they cannot leak strategies or alpha to a competitor.

## Key Information

- Many quant firms include a non-compete clause in the contract; upon leaving, the company decides the period — from zero up to ~2 years (occasionally 3, though rare), with the norm roughly 6 months to 2 years.
- During garden leave you are "paid to not work" and typically compensated well, so it is not necessarily a downside ("you just sit at home and garden").
- Purpose: prevent leaking ideas/alpha; by the time leave ends (e.g., 2 years later), special alphas and trading strategies are often no longer relevant.
