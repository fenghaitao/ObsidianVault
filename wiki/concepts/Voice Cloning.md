## Definition
Voice cloning is the ability to replicate a person's voice from a short audio sample, capturing tone, pitch, accent, and vocal quirks. Once cloned, the system can synthesize any text in that voice. Gradium AI demonstrated cloning from as little as 10 seconds of audio.

## Key Information
- Requires as little as 10 seconds of audio to clone a voice
- System analyzes: tone, pitch, accent, and "little quirks that make your voice yours"
- Once cloned, typing text produces speech in the cloned voice
- Demonstrated by cloning podcaster Joe Rogan's voice
- Included in Gradium Phonon's on-device TTS
- Kokoro, a competing on-device TTS model, notably lacks voice cloning
- Part of Gradium's streaming TTS offering for cascaded systems
- Demonstrated on-device via MLX Audio and Marvis TTS: Prince Canuma cloned the Jarvis (Iron Man) voice for his Richie Mini robot, running entirely on-device
