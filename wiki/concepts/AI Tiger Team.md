## Definition

An AI Tiger Team is a dedicated internal team within an organization tasked with exploring AI capabilities, applying them to specific workflows, evangelizing best practices, and creating bottom-up excitement for AI adoption. Sherwin Wu recommends this as a critical component of successful enterprise AI deployments.

## Key Information

- Recommended by Sherwin Wu as the solution to the top-down-only AI adoption anti-pattern
- The team explores the full extent of AI capabilities, applies them to specific workflows, does knowledge sharing, and creates excitement
- The most effective members are often "technical adjacent" — people who are technical but not software engineers (e.g., support/operations leads who are Excel wizards)
- These are the people who "light up and get excited" around AI tools
- Not every company has software engineers — the tiger team fills the gap for non-tech companies
- The team should hold hackathons, seminars, knowledge sharing sessions, and create "seeds of excitement" internally
- Analogous to the internal Codex power users at OpenAI who share best practices across the org
- Key principle: find the high performers in AI adoption and empower them to evangelize
