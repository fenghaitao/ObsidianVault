## Definition
Developer Experience (DX) is the practice of improving the tools, processes, and environments that software developers use to build software, with the goal of increasing productivity, satisfaction, and code quality.

## Key Information
- In the age of AI coding agents, DX must account for both human developers and AI agents as users of development tools and processes
- Core principle: "what's good for humans is good for AI" — DX improvements benefit both audiences
- Key DX investment areas for the AI era include: standardized development environments, deterministic validation, testable codebases, documented intent and context, and improved code review processes
- The field has historically dealt with developers wanting to adopt unvetted new tools; AI agents amplify this tension but also create new opportunities
- Fast iteration loops (e.g., 30-second CI vs 20-minute CI) are a critical DX concern for agent-assisted development
- DX professionals face the challenge of predicting which investments will remain valuable as AI technology rapidly evolves
