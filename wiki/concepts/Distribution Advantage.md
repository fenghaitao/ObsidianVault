## Definition

Distribution Advantage is the marketplace insight that with roughly equal product quality, the product with superior distribution (e.g., bundled as the default) wins. Netscape's technically superior browser lost to Microsoft's free, bundled Internet Explorer for exactly this reason.

## Key Information

- "It's not a technology war, it's a distribution war" — James's core lesson from the browser wars.
- When two products have similar quality, "people are going to just use the one that's the default. There's no reason to switch."
- Microsoft bundled IE into Windows and gave it away free, instantly killing Netscape's ~$35-per-copy browser revenue.
- Career application: think like an investor about the companies you join — understand "what the war is" (technology, data, or distribution) and where you're over-weighted.
