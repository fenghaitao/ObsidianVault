## Definition

Visual ideation is the practice of using visual mockups and design exploration during the earliest shaping phase of a product, before locking decisions into a spec or PRD. It helps builders (especially non-designers) visualize ideas, explore alternatives, and communicate intent before writing detailed requirements.

## Key Information

- Happens before plan mode, before PRD creation — it's the messy, exploratory phase where you're still figuring out what you want.
- Claude Design is useful here: mock up rough ideas, generate A/B/C options, screenshot the best one, paste into Claude for shaping conversations.
- The visual helps Claude ask better clarifying questions during the strategic planning conversation.
- Brian's workflow: Claude Design mockup → screenshot → paste into claude.ai → "Help me shape up a detailed scope and product plan for this."
- Contrasts with jumping straight into plan mode without strategic thinking first.
- NimbleList offers a similar capability with built-in visual editors (mockups, diagrams) alongside agent sessions.
