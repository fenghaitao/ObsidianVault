## Definition

AI's impact on influence is the observation that as AI commoditizes execution, the leverage in product management shifts from doing the work to deciding what work survives and getting others to buy in. Influence becomes the 10x skill because the core human value-add is no longer execution but alignment, strategy, and stakeholder buy-in.

## Key Information

- "We are entering a golden age of product management — not of product managers, but of product management"
- As execution plummets in complexity, leverage shifts from doing the work to deciding what work survives and getting buy-in
- PMs who made careers on being the most type-A, Gantt-chart-master, best note-taker will be displaced by AI
- Core skills that remain: having interesting ideas grounded in user empathy, curiosity, testing, iteration, and influencing people to fund not just V1 but V2, V3, V4
- "The act of influence, the act of stakeholder management, the act of learning is the 10x skill"
- AI is not good at being an "anthropologist" yet — it's based on existing knowledge, not novel insights from user empathy
- "We're going to have so much software we won't even know what to do with it. But what software actually works? What software actually matters?"
- We are all becoming "directors of work" with agents as colleagues — we need to codify what's important, set guardrails, and train agents on product philosophy
- Strategy clarity becomes 10x more important because mistakes compound faster when building happens faster
