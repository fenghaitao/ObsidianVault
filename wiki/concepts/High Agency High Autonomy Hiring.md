## Definition

High Agency High Autonomy Hiring is a talent philosophy that prioritizes candidates who have "fire in their belly" (passion for the work itself, not just a paycheck), high agency (can figure things out independently), and high autonomy (can own work from start to finish without supervision). This is Lovable's core hiring approach.

## Key Information

- Key traits: passion (work is their hobby), high agency, high autonomy, ability to create clarity from chaos
- "This is not a paycheck for them. They want to do this for some ulterior reason. This is the biggest opportunity of their life."
- "This is global maxima against any other opportunities that are in front of them at the moment."
- People who look for clarity are not a fit; people who can "create clarity out of chaos" are
- The approach values "best talent" differently: not big company logos or success stories, but passion, fire, and ability
- New graduates and ex-founders (failed startup founders) are becoming the hottest talent because they have high agency and autonomy
- Ex-founders are now in "hot demand" — personas that traditionally wouldn't be prioritized in corporate hiring
- "I can figure out things that are tangential to me that I don't need other specialty. I can go do it myself."
