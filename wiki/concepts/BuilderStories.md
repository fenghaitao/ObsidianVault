## Definition

Builder Stories is a video series on Brian Casel's YouTube channel where fellow builders share their screens and demonstrate exactly how they work in the AI-first era. Guests include Arvid Kahl, Brennan Dunn, and Colleen Schnettler.

## Key Information

- Format: screen-share interviews where builders show their actual workflows, tools, and setups.
- Guests demonstrate real projects, not theoretical approaches.
- Full unedited conversations available to Builder Methods Pro members.
- Featured guests and their topics:
  - [[ArvidKahl]]: Podscan, 98% AI-coded, comprehension debt, video-to-docs workflow.
  - [[BrennanDunn]]: RightMessage, sub-agent team, Linear integration, Fern Desk.
  - [[ColleenSchnettler]]: voice-to-LinkedIn tool, Compound Engineering, cross-model troubleshooting.
