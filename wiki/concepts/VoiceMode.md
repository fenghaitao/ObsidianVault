## Definition

Voice Mode is Claude Code's built-in voice dictation feature. Brian Casel notes that speaking prompts (vs typing) produces higher detail, accuracy, and precision because typing fatigue leads to self-editing and reduced ambition in prompts.

## Key Information

- Built into Claude Code (not a third-party tool).
- Brian already uses WhisperFlow for voice dictation but notes the built-in option is significant.
- Speaking produces better prompts: more detail, more accuracy, less self-editing.
- Typing fatigue unconsciously causes builders to cut ideas shorter and be less ambitious.
- Part of a trio of 2026 features (with Auto Plan and Auto Memory) reshaping Claude Code workflow.
