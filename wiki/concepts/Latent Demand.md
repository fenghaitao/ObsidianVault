## Definition

Latent demand is the product principle that you cannot get people to do what they do not already do; instead, find the intent they already have and steer it so they can do it more easily. Boris calls it "the single most important principle in product."

## Key Information

- Facebook Marketplace: Facebook Groups were 40% buying/selling posts — a commerce use case the product was never designed for, later formalized as Marketplace
- Facebook Dating: roughly 60% of profile views were opposite-gender non-friends, evidence of latent dating intent
- The method: design products that can be "hacked," watch how users abuse them, then build the formal product around revealed intent
