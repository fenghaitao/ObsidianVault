## Definition

Build vs Buy in the AI Era is the recalibrated decision framework where the speed and low cost of AI-assisted building often makes "build" the faster option compared to setting up and configuring enterprise SaaS tools. When you can build something in 1-2 hours, buying becomes the slower choice.

## Key Information

- "At this point, I'm at a stage where if it takes me an hour or two hours to set up a big enterprise account somewhere, I'm just going to build it myself faster."
- Example: Custom feature adoption matrix — nothing off-the-shelf could be adopted faster than building it
- The calculus has flipped: traditional "buy" was faster because building took weeks/months; AI-assisted building takes hours
- Applies to internal tools especially: expense management, employee onboarding, custom tracking
- But also applies to external products: Lovable's Shopify integration, merch store
- "I'm in that position of build versus buy. I'm in the build boat so to speak."
- Contrast with traditional wisdom: "Nobody ever got fired for buying IBM" → now "Nobody ever got slowed down for building it themselves"
