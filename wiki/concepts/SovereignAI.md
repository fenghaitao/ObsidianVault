## Definition
Sovereign AI refers to national or regional initiatives to develop AI models tailored to local languages, cultures, and needs, often leveraging open models like Gemma as a foundation and backed by government investment.

## Key Information
- Driven by the need for AI models that serve local languages and cultural contexts
- Open models like Gemma enable sovereign AI by allowing fine-tuning on local data without dependency on foreign API providers
- Examples: Sarvam (India, government-backed, training models for India's many official languages), AI Singapore (Southeast Asian languages)
- Multilingual tokenizers (like Gemma's Gemini-based tokenizer) are critical enablers — they work well for low-resource languages independently of model capabilities
- Represents a growing trend where nations invest in AI infrastructure for linguistic and cultural sovereignty
- Open models with permissive licenses (Apache 2.0) facilitate sovereign AI by removing licensing barriers
