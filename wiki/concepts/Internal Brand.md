## Definition

Internal Brand is an engineer's reputation within their company — being known as someone who gets things done, is approachable, and can be trusted with high-impact problems. It is critical for getting the scope needed for Staff promotion and can matter more than seniority.

## Key Information

- Seniority doesn't matter as much as internal brand for getting high-scope projects
- If your manager, director, or senior manager has a big problem, they think about who to give it to — you want to be the first person that comes to mind
- Internal brand is built through visibility, being approachable, unblocking teammates, and consistently delivering. Zach emphasizes that internal brand is distinct from personal branding (LinkedIn, content creation) — it is about how you are known within the company by the people you work with. Zach's internal brand with Jender at Facebook enabled him to get hired at Netflix as a Senior Engineer despite limited software engineering experience. At Airbnb, Zach built internal brand quickly by completing a pipeline migration in one quarter that was planned for four quarters, demonstrating he could handle risky, complex infrastructure work.
- Peer feedback is essential for promotion — you need at least 3-4 coworkers who can vouch for you
- Cross-discipline vouching is even more powerful: product managers, data scientists, and engineers in other orgs
- If "engineers love him but product managers hate them, you're still not moving up"
- Internal brand can be built through methods that fit your personality — tech talks, answering questions on internal Stack Overflow, running a tech talk series, or other meta-contributions
- Visibility is non-negotiable for moving up, but there are many ways to achieve it without self-promotion that feels uncomfortable

- Ricky built his internal brand by establishing a track record of finding impactful projects — "people see your name and they're like okay, this project's probably a good idea"
- Long tenure at the same company and org (7 years at Google) compounds internal brand as everyone knows your track record
- Steve Huynh's 18 years at Amazon meant his internal brand was deeply established — many principal engineers knew his work and could champion his promotion packet
- Steve's principal promotion was aided by the fact that other principal engineers had context on his work and could speak to his packet
