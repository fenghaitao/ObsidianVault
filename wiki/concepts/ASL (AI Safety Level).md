## Definition

ASL (AI Safety Level) is Anthropic's classification system for model safety. Opus 4 was Anthropic's first ASL-3 class model, released in May 2025.

## Key Information

- ASL-3 is a significant safety classification at Anthropic
- Opus 4 was the first ASL-3 class model released by Anthropic
- Released in May 2025
- Opus 4 was also the inflection point where Claude Code adoption went exponential
- Higher ASL levels indicate more capable models requiring more sophisticated safety measures
- The safety classification is part of Anthropic's broader safety framework
