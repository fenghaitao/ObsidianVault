## Definition
Autonomy Horizon is one of the three "next wave" benchmark dimensions identified by [[SnorkelAI]]. It refers to how long an agent can operate autonomously before reliability breaks down — and whether benchmarks capture different points on the autonomy slider, from co-pilot to fully autonomous agent.

## Key Information
- Benchmarks should represent realistic and frontier horizon lengths
- Should be intentional about different points on the autonomy slider: co-pilot vs. fully autonomous
- Real-world long-horizon challenges: agents lose track of context from weeks ago, product specs change, organizational reorgs shift priorities midstream
- Real-world settings represent much more complexity with continual learning and state/environment changes
- One of three axes Snorkel is most excited about for the next generation of benchmarks
