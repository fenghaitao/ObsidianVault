## Definition

IQ and Job Performance is the idea (advocated by Brian Attwell) that general mental acuity is one of the most well-researched predictors of on-the-job performance, and that interviews should test it rather than recall or gameable drills.

## Key Information

- Attwell: the IQ-performance correlation is "one of the most well-researched results in applied psychology"
- It's less visible at top companies because everyone admitted is already 1–2 standard deviations up, so within-team differentiation by IQ shrinks
- Good interviews test on-the-spot tradeoffs, reasoning, and holding many (sometimes conflicting) requirements in your head — e.g., reading a 5-page postmortem and debating its structural problems
- Bad signals: tests of recall, experience, or mindsets that "anyone can pass if they practice enough" (e.g., gameable LeetCode)
- Companies don't build better processes because it's a lot of work; leadership must be dedicated (his company runs ~20 interview experiments at a time)
- He is considering adding an IQ screen, perhaps first for PMs (whose interviews are harder and less well-defined); some high-bar companies already use cognitive screens in the screen stage to filter early
