## Definition

Leaving Money on the Table is a growth principle articulated by Amol Avaisary: being comfortable forgoing short-term metric impact to prioritize safety, brand, quality bar, and user experience. It is a core operating principle for the Anthropic growth team and reflects a long-term view that the best products don't squeeze every last dollar.

## Key Information

- Core principle: "Be comfortable forgoing metric impact in order to prioritize safety, our brand, to hold a high-quality bar, and to maintain a great user experience"
- "One of the biggest mistakes growth teams make is just trying to squeeze every last dollar"
- "Look beyond the short-term and zoom out — the very best products all operate this way"
- "That's actually the thing that's going to drive more growth long-term as well"
- Two types of controversial tests:
  1. So controversial you wouldn't ship regardless of results — don't run it
  2. Cringey but not a red line — run it, but require a high level of return for the result
- For Anthropic, AI safety is firmly in category 1 (non-negotiable)
- The safety stance will become a "significant competitive advantage" as risks and stakes get higher
- Analogous to fundraising: don't squeeze out the last dollar because you want people to come back next time
- Amol posts about this principle in his notebook channel to scale the belief across the growing team
