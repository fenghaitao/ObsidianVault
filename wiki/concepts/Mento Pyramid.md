## Definition

The Mento Pyramid is a method for presenting information to executives, developed by Barbara Mento (reportedly the first female consultant at McKinsey in the 1950s). It flips the traditional presentation order: start with the recommendation, then the options explored, then the supporting evidence — rather than the bottom-up approach of process → conclusions → recommendation.

## Key Information

- Developed by Barbara Mento, reportedly the first female consultant at McKinsey
- Reverses the traditional presentation order: Recommendation first → Options explored → Supporting evidence last
- Instead of: "Here's our process, here's what we did, here's our conclusions, here's our recommendation"
- Executives are busy and want the conclusion first
- Jessica Fain's gloss: people are different, so also understand the individual exec's communication preferences (some hate PowerPoint, some want data only, some want a doc with quiet reading time)
- "If you're listening to this, it's really helpful if you tell people because you actually know what you like and don't like"
