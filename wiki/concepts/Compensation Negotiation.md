## Definition

Compensation negotiation is the process of advocating for higher total compensation — salary, bonus, equity, severance, and other benefits — when receiving a job offer or during performance reviews. Jacob Warwick's framework reframes it as a collaborative process of understanding the value exchange between you and the company, rather than an adversarial fight over money.

## Key Information

- A simple pushback ("What's the chance there's a little more?") typically yields 20%+ improvement
- Jacob's average client sees 40% movement; some see 100-400% increases
- The process starts far earlier than the offer stage — it begins with your LinkedIn profile, what you tell recruiters, and every interview answer
- Companies have significant information asymmetry: they negotiate thousands of times a day; you negotiate 4-5 times in your career
- The path to wealth is through ownership, not W-2 employment
- Key principles: never share your number first, never negotiate over email, slow down the process, negotiate with the decision maker not the recruiter
- Core philosophy: make it clear what pain you will solve and why it's worth paying you more
