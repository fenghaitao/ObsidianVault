## Definition

Baseline Happiness is the psychological concept that each person has a default level of happiness they return to after positive or negative events. While life events cause temporary spikes or dips, people tend to revert to their baseline. The key insight is that this baseline can be raised through intentional practice.

## Key Information

- Learned by Lenny Rachitsky through a University of Pennsylvania online course on the psychology of happiness
- Key finding: amazing events temporarily raise happiness; terrible events temporarily lower it; both revert to baseline
- The main thing you can work on is improving the baseline so you return to a higher place
- Methods to raise baseline: thinking more optimistically, practicing positive outlook, avoiding catastrophic spiraling
- Exercise brings you out of negative (from -1 to 0) rather than making you actively happier
- Lenny estimates his happiness is ~70% genetic but he actively works on the remaining 30%
