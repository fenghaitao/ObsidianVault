## Definition

Intensity preservation is the leadership principle that the founder CEO's intensity is the most powerful source of energy in a business, and every layer of management must mirror that intensity rather than buffer against it. Each concentric circle of management risks an order-of-magnitude drop-off in intensity.

## Key Information

- The purest form of ambition and most intense source of energy in a business is the founder CEO.
- Every next concentric circle of management beyond the founder CEO has the potential to be an order of magnitude drop-off in intensity.
- Two layers of management with two orders of magnitude drop-off = a very dysfunctional organization.
- Leaders must NOT buffer their teams from the CEO's intensity; they must mirror it.
- There will always be plenty of people who advocate for relaxing; there is an infinite supply of buffers. Leaders should not be among them.
- "Don't be a chill boss. Chill doesn't accomplish anything. Be intense. Be good. Be respectful. Be intense."
- The reaction from teams to intensity is not exhaustion but invigoration: "What a relief that I get to go be intense."
- Practical modeling: Parker Conrad calls people into Slack huddles immediately when problems arise; McGinness posts feedback publicly in product channels.
