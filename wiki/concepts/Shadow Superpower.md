## Definition

[[Nikhyl Singhal]]'s term for the paradox that people who most thoroughly mastered the old way of working are often the *most* resistant to recognizing a new way is needed — their very success reinforces the instincts that no longer apply.

## Key Information

- Mechanism: mastery of a system makes that system feel like "how things work," so the better someone is at the old game, the less incentive (and less visible signal) they have to change — especially if their employer still rewards them for old-mode performance in the near term.
- Contrasted with people weaker at the old system, who have less to lose and are comparatively more open to trying something new.
- Framed by Singhal as one of several forces (alongside plain exhaustion, lack of time, and the moving-target nature of AI skills) that make "[[Crossing The Threshold]]" hard even for otherwise capable, successful people.
