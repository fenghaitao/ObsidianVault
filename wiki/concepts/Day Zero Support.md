## Definition
Day Zero Support is the commitment by the MLX ecosystem to have frontier open-source models available on MLX the same day they are released. This is achieved through partnerships with frontier labs (like Google DeepMind) and rapid community implementation.

## Key Information
- MLX ecosystem provides day-zero support for all major open source model releases
- Example: Gemma 4 was available on MLX the day it was released
- Achieved through partnerships with frontier labs and rapid community implementation
- Prince Canuma (Neywa Labs) works directly with frontier labs for coordinated releases
- 4,000+ models have been ported to MLX overall
- Ensures Apple Silicon users never have to wait to run the latest models on-device
- Key competitive advantage of the MLX ecosystem vs other on-device frameworks
