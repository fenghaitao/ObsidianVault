## Definition

The Convergence of PM, Engineer, and Designer is the observation that AI tools are collapsing the traditional boundaries between product management, engineering, and design roles. The Venn diagrams that used to be separate are now overlapping, and everyone is becoming a "forward deployed engineer" or "AI assistant engineer."

## Key Information

- "These Venn diagrams of engineer, designer, PM used to be very separate. Now they're converging."
- PMs win today because they bring clarity — the core skill for AI tools
- Designers are the next winners because design is emotional decision-making, which AI struggles with
- Engineers remain essential for infrastructure, scaling, and maintenance
- Everyone is becoming a builder: "Everybody becomes an engineer in the world of the future, a designer, a PM"
- The term is irrelevant: "forward deployed engineer," "AI assistant engineer," "LLM engineer," "vibe coder" — all describe the same convergence
- "We're all using LLMs for raw output based on good judgment or bad judgment"
- Implication: text stack doesn't matter anymore (HTML, React, etc.) — the end user just wants a stellar experience
- The skill that matters: "Quality, taste, design. That's all you need to optimize for in the future."
