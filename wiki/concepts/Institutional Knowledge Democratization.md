## Definition
Institutional Knowledge Democratization is the use of AI to make organizational knowledge — how things are done, why decisions were made, where code lives — accessible to everyone in a company, reducing the anxiety of asking questions and the barrier to productivity for new employees.

## Key Information
- Articulated by Thomas Dohmke: the biggest challenge when joining a new company is "what's all the institutional knowledge, how are things being done"
- Humans naturally dislike asking a thousand questions, especially as new employees who fear appearing incompetent
- AI copilots can democratize access to all the information in companies, not just code but processes, decisions, and tribal knowledge
- This changes "how we work — and not only for developers in the workforce, but for really every human"
- Complements natural language programming: new employees can ask questions in natural language rather than needing to know where information is stored
