## Definition

The GROW model is a simple four-category framework for asking powerful coaching questions that unlock insight and help someone solve their own problem. The four categories are: Goal (what does success look like?), Reality (where are you stuck?), Options (what paths could you take?), and Way forward (what will you do next?).

## Key Information

- **G — Goal**: What does success look like? What's the outcome you want? Any question defining the best case scenario.
- **R — Reality**: Where are you stuck? What are your current challenges? What have you tried?
- **O — Options**: What are the various paths you could take? What could you do next? Expand the opportunities.
- **W — Way Forward**: What are you going to do next? Concrete next step.
- These are four kinds of questions, not a rigid sequence. You don't have to follow the order — adapt based on where the person is clearest.
- Sometimes just talking it out loud and creating space for someone helps them realize they already know the answer.
- The model is transferable across domains — it works for technical infrastructure decisions, influencing executives, or go-to-market strategy, not just emotional/personal topics.
- Rachel Lockett demonstrated the model live with Lenny on the podcast, coaching him on his struggle with endless work and the "Indiana Jones boulder" of constant content demands.
- People are more bought in when they arrive at their own solutions through coaching questions rather than being told what to do.
