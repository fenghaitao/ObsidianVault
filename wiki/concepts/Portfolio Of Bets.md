## Definition

Portfolio Of Bets is a strategy for building a promotion case to Staff Engineer by maintaining 2-3 concurrent projects rather than betting everything on a single project. This approach hedges against the many factors in big tech that are outside individual control.

## Key Information

- Introduced by Rahul as a core strategy for getting promoted to staff at Meta
- Rahul had two projects: a main project (re-architecting a calling system) and a side project (internal debug-ability tool) — both contributed to his promotion
- When blocked on one project, you can work on the other, maintaining momentum
- The key is balance: 2-3 projects, not 10 — you need focus, not fragmentation
- Ryan Peterman noted that any promo packet is a track record of multiple halves of work, not just one great project
- This approach also helps build the "track record" needed for the promo committee to recognize your name
- The side project should ideally be something you have more control over (like an internal tool), while the main project might have more external dependencies
- Having multiple projects also creates more opportunities for visibility across different parts of the organization
