## Definition

Team Match is the current onboarding process at Meta that replaced Boot Camp. New hires are matched with a team before starting, rather than exploring teams during an initial undeclared period.

## Key Information

- Meta replaced Boot Camp with Team Match, which Evan King considers "a shame" because Boot Camp gave new engineers more control
- Under Team Match, new hires are matched with a team before starting, reducing the exploratory aspect
- Evan speculated the change was made for logistical reasons — "there's a lot of overhead" in Boot Camp
- Boot Camp was described as a two-way process where the engineer felt "in control" — they could try different teams and choose the one they preferred
- The loss of Boot Camp means new engineers have less agency in team selection and less ability to try different types of work before committing
