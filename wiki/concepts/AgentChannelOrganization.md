## Definition
Agent Channel Organization is the practice of organizing personal AI agent interactions into dedicated messaging channels (e.g., Discord channels) mapped to specific job types. Each channel serves a distinct purpose, and new channels are created when a certain type of conversation recurs frequently enough to warrant its own space.

## Key Information
- Radek Sienkiewicz uses dedicated Discord channels organized by agent job type
- Channel mapping: general (all conversations), inbox (link drops for knowledge base), consulting (client projects with full context), video research (YouTube research), briefing (morning briefings), Instagram (social posting), YouTube (video creation), open-claw (maintainer work), playground (testing new models/workspaces/setups)
- Channels emerge organically: "if enough times I have a certain type of conversation, I added a specific channel for it"
- The playground channel is unique — it changes depending on day, month, or need, used for testing different models, workspaces, or memory setups
- Successful playground experiments get promoted to production channels; failures get discarded
- This organization pattern addresses the limitation of single-channel agent interaction — different job types need different contexts and configurations
- Builds on the incremental adoption approach: channels are added only when a recurring need is identified
