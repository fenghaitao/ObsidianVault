## Definition

Amazon Refreshers are additional equity grants given to employees, typically at the discretion of managers. They are not a formal, structured program like at some other big tech companies.

## Key Information

- Refreshers at Amazon are at the discretion of managers, not a formal program
- One of Steve's best managers gave him a significant refresher before leaving: "He was like YOLO, he was like I'm out, and so he hooked me up"
- The refresher was approximately $350K in additional equity, which then appreciated significantly the following year
- This was not a typical occurrence — it was described as an "outsized performance bonus" combined with stock price appreciation
- Steve noted that Amazon does not have a formal discretionary equity program for high performers that he knew of
- The lack of structured refreshers is one reason why Amazon's compensation can be less competitive than Meta or Google at higher levels
