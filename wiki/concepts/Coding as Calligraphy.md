## Definition

Coding as Calligraphy is Lazar's analogy for the future of hand-coding: just as fine penmanship became a rare art form after printing was commoditized, writing code manually will become a rare art form admired for its craft rather than a practical necessity.

## Key Information

- "Coding is going to be like calligraphy. You writing code is going to be the equivalent of fine printing on a canvas and people like, 'Oh my god, you wrote that code. That's so amazing.'"
- Hand-coding will become commoditized; most elite engineers already rely on AI for code generation
- "It's going to be so rare that it's going to become an art"
- This doesn't mean engineers disappear — elite engineers are still needed for infrastructure, scaling, and maintenance
- The application layer is being commoditized; infrastructure layer still needs deep engineering
- "Most elite vibe coders rely on AI. Again, it's an amplifier."
- Everyone becomes an engineer/designer/PM in the future — the terms converge
