## Definition
Outcome-based pricing is a charge metric where customers pay based on business results generated, such as tickets resolved, candidates hired, or qualified leads generated. It aligns pricing directly to customer ROI but is harder to attribute value than consumption-based metrics.

## Key Information
- One of three charge metric types in Stripe's AI pricing framework (alongside consumption-based and workflow-based)
- Aligns to customer ROI — customers pay for business results, not technical consumption
- Easier to sell than consumption-based pricing (customers understand "candidates hired" better than "1,000 API calls")
- Harder to attribute value — requires data to prove the connection between product usage and business outcomes
- Example: Intercom prices based on number of tickets resolved without human intervention
- Example: recruiting AI could price based on candidates hired
- Example: lead generation AI could price based on qualified leads generated
- Only 5% adoption as of the presentation, but growing
- Moving from consumption-based to outcome-based: harder to implement but easier to align to value
- Moving from outcome-based to consumption-based: easier to implement but harder to sell
