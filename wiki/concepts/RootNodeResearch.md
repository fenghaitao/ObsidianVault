## Definition
Root Node Research is Google DeepMind's research philosophy of identifying and solving the deepest, most fundamental unsolved problems (root nodes) rather than optimizing surface-level improvements (leaf nodes). Solving root nodes enables massive downstream impact across many leaf-node applications.

## Key Information
- **Metaphor**: Research problems as a tree — root nodes are deep foundational problems, leaf nodes are surface applications
- **Strategy**: Don't waste time on leaves; find the deepest unsolved problems and solve those first
- **Partnership**: Partner broadly with the world to find root nodes and bring solutions to leaf nodes
- **Mission alignment**: Aligned with DeepMind's mission to build AI responsibly for the benefit of humanity — solve problems worth solving
- **Examples**: Weather prediction (root: modeling chaotic atmospheric dynamics → leaves: hurricane warnings, agriculture planning), world models (root: interactive environment generation → leaves: gaming, education, simulation), omnimodal embeddings (root: unified cross-modal representation → leaves: search, retrieval, agentic logic)
