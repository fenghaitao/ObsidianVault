## Definition

Opportunity vs Preparedness is Steve Huynh's framework for getting a job: it takes both opportunity (unevenly distributed, unpredictable — a referral, a chance connection) and preparedness (within your control — skills, knowledge, interview readiness). Both must align for success.

## Key Information

- Getting a job requires two things: opportunity and study/preparedness
- Opportunity is unevenly distributed and unpredictable — you don't know when it will pop up
- Preparedness is within your control — upleveling yourself, gaining knowledge and skills
- Both must align: you can't just have the opportunity without the skills, and you can't just have the skills without the opportunity
- Steve got into Amazon through networking (a high school friend from AP Computer Science referred him), but he also had the coding skills from years of self-taught programming
- Today, interview preparation resources are abundant (LeetCode, etc.), which didn't exist when Steve was entering the industry
- Steve believes it was actually harder to get into tech from a non-traditional background back then, despite the easier access today
- To be an outlier for opportunity: genuine, personalized networking — not scripted DMs, but real conversations about the team and the work
