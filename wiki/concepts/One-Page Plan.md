## Definition

The one-page plan is a simplifying framework from Alpine Investors that aligns vision, values, strategy, KPIs, annual goals, and quarterly goals on a single page. It's part of the "People First Operating Rhythm" and is designed to give everyone in the company clarity on how their work ladders up to the big-picture vision. Companies that implement it show higher returns.

## Key Information

- **Column 1**: Vision and Values
- **Column 2**: Strategic Intentions and KPIs
- **Column 3**: Annual Goals
- **Column 4**: Quarterly Goals
- Created by Alpine Investors as part of their "People First Operating Rhythm"
- Portfolio companies that institute the rhythm result in higher returns
- The plan is only part of the system — equally important is the operating rhythm: when you come together to kick off the year, reflect quarterly, and celebrate wins.
- Key reflection question: "What's an inconvenient truth?" — air the things that need to be talked about that no one is discussing because everyone is too busy.
- Executive teams need time away from "being on the dance floor" to get "on the balcony" — time to work on the business, not just in it.
- Before the one-page plan, companies typically have vision, strategy, goals, and values living in different places, talked about to different degrees, and employees can't easily answer "what are our top three priorities and how do they relate to the vision?"
