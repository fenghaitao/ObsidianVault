## Definition
Thrive Coding is a data-driven AI development approach coined by Aman Khan as the antithesis of vibe coding. Instead of evaluating AI outputs by gut feel, developers use systematic evaluation (evals) and data to build confidence in their outputs.

## Key Information
- Contrasted with vibe coding, where developers ship based on "looks good to me" without systematic verification.
- Core principle: use data to be more confident in the output while still building rapidly.
- Involves creating datasets, running experiments (A/B tests on prompts), applying LLM-as-judge evals, and validating those evals against human labels.
- The goal is to move from prototyping to production with confidence, not just shipping fast.
