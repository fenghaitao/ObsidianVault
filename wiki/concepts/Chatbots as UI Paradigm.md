## Definition

Chatbots and terminals may persist as lasting UI paradigms for AI because they provide infinite flexibility — a way to interact with models that scales across all intelligence levels. Specific UIs will still exist for tactile, direct interactions, but they will increasingly be generated by models rather than hand-coded. The chatbot paradigm opened up "infinite ways to work with the model."

## Key Information

- Jenny believes chatbots aren't going away: "this opened up this new way of infinite ways to work with the model and to sort of talk to the computer that we just didn't have before"
- Specific UIs will still exist for very specific, tactile interactions — and will be increasingly generated by models
- Kevin Weil's insight: "talking is such a beautiful way to handle every level of intelligence" — it scales from low IQ to 200+ IQ
- Claude's widgets (interactive elements within chat) have had good reception because people still like to see, touch, and click UIs
- Widgets are more efficient than typing for certain interactions
- The combination of chat flexibility + specific UI widgets is the likely future
- Even innovations like WhatsApp/Telegram/SMS integration are "just another form of chatbot" — but the unlock is real
