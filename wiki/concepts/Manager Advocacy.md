## Definition

Manager Advocacy refers to the proactive support a manager provides for an engineer's career growth, including creating opportunities, advocating behind the scenes during promotion discussions, and pushing for accelerated timelines. It is one of the most critical factors in rapid career growth.

## Key Information

- Evan King's manager was the primary driver of his rapid growth: he proactively created the tech lead opportunity, advocated for the Realtime Integrity team, and pushed behind the scenes for Evan's promotions
- Evan learned that his manager "was doing a ton of advocating for this new team" and that "behind the scenes, my manager was doing far more than I appreciated at the time"
- The manager's approach: identify people with capabilities, "give them more than they can chew," make them feel empowered, and support them while empowering them
- Evan described his manager as "an incredible leader" whose "path up the chain was fast" and whose "influence was fantastic"
- Many of Evan's boot camp classmates didn't have the same promotion growth because "they didn't have a manager that was advocating for them to the same degree"
- The combination needed: you need to be a high performer (so your manager wants to invest in you) AND your manager needs to be fantastic (to push that rate of acceleration)
- Managers are incentivized to grow their team — they're "rewarded for growth within their team and how many higher levels are within their team"
- The right manager is also a form of luck — "you could have had a manager that actively disliked you and even though you're killing it, you might have been delayed on the promos"
