## Definition
Semantic VAD (Voice Activity Detection) is an enhanced form of voice activity detection that goes beyond simple audio level detection to understand semantically when a speaker has finished their turn. It is a component of Gradium AI's streaming cascaded voice systems, helping determine conversation turn boundaries intelligently.

## Key Information
- More sophisticated than basic audio level-based VAD
- Understands semantic turn-taking cues, not just silence detection
- Part of Gradium AI's streaming voice pipeline alongside streaming STT and streaming TTS
- Helps manage the conversation flow in cascaded voice systems
- Important for natural turn-taking in voice conversations
- Mentioned as part of Gradium's "classic stuff" in their cascaded system implementation
