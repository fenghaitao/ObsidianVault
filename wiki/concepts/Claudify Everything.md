## Definition

"Claudify Everything" (or "Claudify it") is the principle that what's better than doing something yourself is having Claude do it. It's a mindset shift where the default approach to any task is to first try delegating it to AI.

## Key Information

- Boris: "What's better than doing something? Having Claude do it"
- This is a core principle Boris teaches his team at Anthropic
- When underfunding projects, people are "kind of forced to Claudify" — they use AI more because they have to ship quickly
- The memory leak example: a newer engineer just had Claude debug it, while Boris (with older habits) was manually doing heap snapshots
- Boris: "For those of us that have been using the model for a long time, you still have to kind of transport yourself to the current moment and not get stuck back in old model"
- The principle of speed reinforces this: "If you want to go faster, a really good way to do that is to just have Claude do more stuff"
