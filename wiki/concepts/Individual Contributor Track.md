## Definition

The Individual Contributor (IC) track is the career path for engineers who advance without becoming managers, progressing through Senior, Staff, Principal, and Distinguished Engineer levels. It is unique to engineering among most professions.

## Key Information

- The IC track diverges from the management track at the Senior Engineer level
- Staff Engineer is the first level on the IC track where you must be a leader without being a manager
- Engineering is unique in having a viable IC path — most professions (sales, product management) require moving into management to increase compensation and scope
- Engineers have "leverage" through tools and technology that allows them to multiply their own and others' productivity
- The IC track progression follows scope: entry level (task) → mid-level (feature) → senior (project) → staff (multiple projects/product) → principal/distinguished (industry-wide impact)
- The panel focused on the decision to pursue the IC ladder rather than the management track
