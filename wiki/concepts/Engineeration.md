## Definition

Engineeration (engineer + vacation) is David Singleton's practice of a senior leader deliberately taking a few days to join a team and complete a small real project, then writing a detailed friction log. It keeps leaders connected to what he calls the "one foot level" — the actual work of the team.

## Key Information

- Singleton did this about once a quarter at Stripe (he skipped the busy final quarter), declaring himself "on vacation" so urgent-but-not-critical requests would wait
- Each engineereration produced a friction log of everything frustrating or weird, which became input for where to invest in developer productivity and for smarter operational reviews
- He encouraged other managers to do the same; those who did found it "incredibly valuable"
- The practice extended to a "sales occasion" where he shadowed salespeople to understand how internal tools were actually used
- It generalizes a manufacturing practice of leaders periodically working on the factory floor, applied to software
