## Definition
GenAI Ops (also called LLM Ops or MFM Ops) refers to the operational practices and infrastructure needed to keep AI applications up to date, manage models in production, and maintain the full lifecycle of generative AI systems.

## Key Information
- An emerging operational discipline for maintaining AI applications in production
- There are many evolving terms for this practice: GenAI Ops, LLM Ops, MFM Ops
- Part of the broader AI integration workflow alongside model evaluation, customization, and agent development
- Addresses the challenge of keeping AI systems current as models and best practices rapidly evolve
