## Definition
Quality Quarter was Sentry's three-month initiative dedicated exclusively to improving codebase quality — removing technical debt, simplifying code, and making the codebase more legible. Activities included removing all `any` types from TypeScript, eliminating TODO comments, simplifying complex code, and removing unused feature flags.

## Key Information
- Three-month dedicated period at [[Sentry]] focused on codebase quality improvement
- Activities included:
  - Removing all `any` types from TypeScript
  - Eliminating accumulated TODO comments
  - Simplifying complex code
  - Removing unused feature flags
- Purpose: make the codebase cleaner and more maintainable
- Important context for AI-assisted development: cleaner codebases are more legible for AI agents
- Described by [[Priscila Andre de Oliveira]] as critical because Sentry's codebase has 15+ years of accumulated code and ~100 PRs merged daily
- Demonstrates that even with "going all in AI," traditional software engineering quality practices remain essential
