## Definition

Co-creation with AI agents is the experience of building and customizing an AI agent yourself, rather than using a pre-built, one-size-fits-all solution. This sense of crafting and ownership fundamentally changes the user-agent relationship and produces better engagement than commercialized, growth-hacked AI products.

## Key Information

- **The feeling:** "I don't feel like I'm using Claude. I feel like I'm using Polly. I feel like I'm using Finn. I feel like I'm using these things that I built."
- **The analogy:** Building your own PC in high school — "it didn't make the computer better than what I could have bought off the shelf. It probably didn't make it cheaper. But I learned so much and it really felt like it was mine."
- **Contrast with commercial AI products:** ChatGPT and Claude use growth hacks — "If you want me to, I can tell you..." or "If I were you, the next steps would be..." — to keep you engaged. OpenClaw's closers are just nice: "This sounds like a fun podcast for you. Enjoy it."
- The open-source nature enables this: you can read the docs, understand the code, customize everything
- Claire Vo: "This co-creation experience just engenders a very positive feeling when interacting with this product that doesn't feel commercialized yet."
- "It just makes me feel like I can go commercialize a bunch of stuff. Very empowering experience."

## Why It Matters

- Co-creation produces deeper engagement and loyalty than off-the-shelf solutions
- It changes the user from a consumer to a builder
- The sense of ownership and craftsmanship makes the inevitable frustrations more tolerable
- It's a fundamentally different product philosophy from commercial AI assistants
