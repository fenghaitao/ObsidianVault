## Definition

Acquisitions in Silicon Valley is an "overloaded term" that can range from blockbuster deals (WhatsApp acquired by Facebook for $19 billion) to acqui-hires where a failed startup is acquired primarily for its team. Rahul Pandey experienced the latter when his startup Cos was acquired by Pinterest after 8 months.

## Key Information

### The Spectrum of Acquisitions
- Blockbuster: WhatsApp acquired by Facebook for $19 billion — "everyone became filthy rich"
- Acqui-hire: A company that effectively failed, acquired primarily for the team, with the acquisition label used to "save the dignity of the founders"
- Middle ground: Cos was somewhere in between — closer to the failure end, but an "opportunistic bet" on interesting IP and academic ideas

### Rahul's Acquisition Experience
- Cos was acquired by Pinterest after 8 months of operation
- The pitch: pairing Cos's technology and know-how with Pinterest's existing product-market fit for a "1+1=3" outcome
- The company never had a chance to really fail or succeed — it ended within 8 months
- Financially, it wasn't life-changing money — Rahul entered Pinterest with a slightly higher package than a normal new grad
- The acquisition process was chaotic and stressful: Rahul was told on a Friday that he'd interview on Monday, with 72 hours to prepare after nearly a year out of interview practice
- He almost didn't pass the interviews — he was borderline and had to do two additional follow-up interviews
- If he hadn't passed, he would have gotten nothing (no equity vested after only 8 months) and would have effectively been fired

### The Career Value of an Acquisition Story
- Despite the financial modesty, the story of being part of an acquisition has immense career value
- Rahul gets "a lot of mileage" out of telling the story of being an enterprising 22-year-old who took a bet on a startup that got acquired
- The narrative you can create about what you did, why you did it, and why it's interesting is "incredibly powerful"
