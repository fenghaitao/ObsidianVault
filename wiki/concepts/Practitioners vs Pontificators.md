## Definition

The distinction between advice from people actively doing the work (practitioners) and those who theorize from a distance (pontificators). The best advice comes from practitioners sharing real, lived experience rather than abstract theorizing.

## Key Information

- Lenny: "The source of the best advice is from practitioners doing the thing for real. You know, not just pontificating."
- This principle is central to Lenny's newsletter: most posts are guest posts from people sharing the best thing they learned in their career
- Michelle Rial demonstrated this with her children's book: her first attempt before having kids wasn't good, but after having Jude, the experience made the work great
- "People always say if you want to write, read." Michelle read many children's books with Jude, which informed her work
- The best stuff comes from actual experience, not just thinking you know what you're doing
- Lenny: "It's based on like real life doing the thing"
