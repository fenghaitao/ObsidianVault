## Definition

A governance structure — used by [[Patagonia]] — where a trust with no economic dimension of its own owns/oversees a company, including trustees plus a distinct "purpose protector" role empowered to sue the trustees themselves if they ever deviate from the mission.

## Key Information

- [[Eric Ries]] compares this checks-and-balances structure to a government, and cites it as one of the two "simplest" [[Spiritual Holding Company]] templates alongside nonprofit-foundation ownership ([[Novo Nordisk]], [[Zeiss]]).
- Academic research cited by Ries: companies protected by structures like this are six times more likely to survive to year 50 and show superior return on invested capital versus conventional [[Shareholder Primacy]] companies.
