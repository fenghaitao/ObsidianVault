## Definition

Career Decision Making is the practice of making explicit, conscious choices about career moves rather than drifting by default. Steve Huynh identified the deferral of career decisions as his biggest regret from 18 years at Amazon.

## Key Information

- Steve's biggest regret: deferring decisions — staying put by default rather than making an explicit choice about whether to leave
- The default mode is to stay — inertia keeps you in place
- The regret isn't about staying, it's about not making it an explicit decision: "Did you take in all of the considerations and then say 'I am going to continue to stay'?"
- Reading the writing on the wall and making a sober decision about whether to stay or leave is critical
- On certain teams, Steve should have recognized the situation earlier and taken the initiative to change
- The advice: "Did you make it a decision or not?" — turn the default into an explicit choice
- This connects to the broader theme of people-pleasing: are you on this path because you chose it, or because it was put in front of you?
