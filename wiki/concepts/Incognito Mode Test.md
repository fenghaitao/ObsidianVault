## Definition

The Incognito Mode Test is a customer experience audit practice recommended by Jason Lemkin: open your browser in incognito mode with a fresh Gmail address and experience your own product as a new user — try support, contact sales, sign up for the newsletter, and use the product. The thing that makes you "cry the most" (the worst experience) is what you should fix first with an AI agent.

## Key Information

- Steps: open incognito browser → use a fresh Gmail address → try support → try to contact sales → sign up for newsletter → try the product → do everything a new user would do
- "You're going to cry about some of the things you've seen. You're going to cry how bad your support is. You're going to cry how long it takes sales to get back to you."
- The specific instruction: "pick the thing that makes you cry the most over your mulled wine and go buy that agent and fix it"
- Recommended frequency: ideally once a quarter, at minimum once a year
- Purpose: founders get lost in strategic thinking and forget about the actual customer experience — onboarding, support, contact forms
- The incognito/fresh email approach ensures you experience what a real new user experiences, not what a logged-in, known user sees
- "So many founders just get lost — you forget about what these things you forget about the onboarding workflow and you forget about support and you forget how bad contact me"
- The actionable twist vs. traditional advice: instead of emailing your PM about bugs, deploy an AI agent to fix the experience permanently
