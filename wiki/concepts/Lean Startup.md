## Definition

Startup methodology popularized by [[Eric Ries]]'s book *[[The Lean Startup (book)|The Lean Startup]]* (2011): build-measure-learn, minimum viable product (MVP), and treating business assumptions as testable hypotheses rather than fixed plans.

## Key Information

- Ries argues today's top AI labs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Claude Code) build in a textbook lean-startup way — shipping small, admittedly-unready "research preview" experiments and iterating based on real usage — even though they don't use the branded terminology and didn't anticipate how popular those experiments would become.
- Recalls the "backlash cycle" every methodology goes through: e.g., people once claimed Quibi's failure "proved" lean startup doesn't work; Ries's response is to judge methodologies by whether companies using them succeed, not by whether the branded terms are used publicly.
- Positioned as the predecessor to *[[Incorruptible (book)|Incorruptible]]*: Lean Startup is about how to build a successful company; Incorruptible is about how to protect one once it succeeds.
