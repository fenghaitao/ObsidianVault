## Definition
Agent Fatigue is the community burnout phenomenon Kitze documents in the personal AI agent space — initial explosive enthusiasm followed by decline as users encounter persistent unreliability in cron jobs, multi-agent coordination, memory, and model personality degradation.

## Key Information
- Tinker Club trajectory: initial explosion of sign-ups → now ~5 people per meetup
- "Slowly turning into Open Claw Anonymous" — support-group atmosphere
- Community sentiment: "Yeah, mine didn't do it like the fucking cron job just managed to drive me fucking nuts"
- "It went a bit depressing, but I think we'll bounce back"
- Root causes: cron jobs unreliable, multi-agents breaking, agents forgetting context, model personality degradation
- Kitze's own life: "My life is far from solved. It's never been more chaotic." — "It's a mess. But it's a performative mess."
- Even hardcore tinkerers (people who build pinball machines) are "freaking tired of trying to make this thing work"
- "Let alone people who have lives, let alone people who have like busy lives and jobs" — mainstream adoption blocked
- Kitze still optimistic: "We'll figure out like, you know, we'll figure it out"
