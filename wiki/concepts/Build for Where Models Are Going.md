## Definition

"Build for where the models are going and not where they are today" is Sherwin Wu's core strategic advice for AI product builders. Rather than optimizing for current model limitations, design products for future model capabilities — even if the product only works 80% today, model improvements will soon close the gap and unlock a significantly better experience.

## Key Information

- Sherwin Wu's primary advice for AI builders and startups
- The most successful AI startups build products for an "ideal" capability that is maybe 80% of the way there today
- When new models ship (e.g., o3 → 5.1 → 5.2), the product suddenly "clicks" and becomes incredible
- Counter-example: if you build only for current model limitations, your product is a local maximum that gets disrupted
- This is the strategic response to "models will eat your scaffolding for breakfast"
- The models are getting better so quickly that you often don't need to wait long for capabilities to catch up
- This advice applies especially to the tooling and scaffolding space around AI
- Related to the concept that listening to customers is not always the right strategy in AI, because customers are often anchored to current model limitations
