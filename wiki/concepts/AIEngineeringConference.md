## Definition
The AI Engineering Conference is a conference brand with two formats: the World's Fair (a larger, multi-track event) and the Summit (a smaller, single-track event designed to bring the best people together around a single important theme).

## Key Information
- Two-brand format: World's Fair and Summit
- World's Fair: larger, multi-track format for broader community engagement
- Summit: intentionally smaller, single-track, designed for depth on a single theme
- The Summit format is designed to convene the world's best minds around one focused topic
- The 2025 Summit theme was AI coding
- Not affiliated with a single company — cross-industry event pushing the whole AI coding industry forward
- Summit features a leadership track (organizational transformation) and a technical track (patterns, systems, products)
