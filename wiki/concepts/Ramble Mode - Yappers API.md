## Definition

Ramble mode (also called the "Yappers API") is the concept, coined by Hillary Gridley, that the highest bandwidth interface for an LLM is simply chatting to it — rambling about what you need in natural language, particularly through voice notes. Rather than trying to structure everything perfectly upfront, you just talk.

## Key Information

- Origin: Hillary Gridley — "The highest bandwidth API for an LLM is just chatting to it"
- The "Yappers API" is a humorous name for this approach: just yap at the AI
- Claire Vo recommends using voice notes in Telegram when onboarding an OpenClaw agent:
  - When the agent asks "Who are you? What do you need?", click the voice button
  - Ramble: "I'm Claire, I run ChatPRD, I also have this podcast called How I AI. I've got this complicated..."
  - The agent makes sense of the ramble and builds its identity from it
- Voice notes are transcribed via Whisper, then processed by the LLM
- This is contrasted with the "old mental model" of structured onboarding (forms, fields, bios)
- The agent can also talk back in a robotic voice if desired

## Why It Matters

- Voice notes bypass the bottleneck of typing and allow for much higher bandwidth of information transfer
- It's especially useful for parents (like Jessie Janes) who "literally don't have hands" — they can capture ideas while with their kids
- It makes onboarding feel natural and conversational rather than bureaucratic
- "It's not 'is there a Google API for this?' It's literally rambling"
