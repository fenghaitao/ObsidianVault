## Definition

Fast Feedback Loops is the practice of responding to user feedback extremely quickly — ideally within minutes. Boris Cherny applied this to Claude Code: fixing every bug within minutes, which made users feel heard and encouraged more feedback.

## Key Information

- Boris: "In the early days, what I would do is anytime that someone sends feedback, I would just go in and I would fix every single thing as fast as I possibly could. So like within a minute, within 5 minutes"
- The mechanism: "This just really fast feedback cycle, it encourages people to give more and more feedback. It's just so important cuz it makes them feel heard"
- The contrast: "Usually when you use a product, you get feedback, it just goes into a black hole somewhere and then you don't get feedback again"
- "If you make people feel heard, then they want to contribute and they want to help make the thing better"
- Now Claude does a lot of this work: Boris points it at a Slack channel and it finds issues, puts up PRs
- Boris's Twitter engagement: users were "kind of surprised by the pace at which we're able to address feedback nowadays"
- "If someone has a bug, I can probably fix it within a few minutes. Cuz I just wrote a Claude and as long as the description is good, it would just go and do it"
