## Definition

Shipping Velocity is a growth strategy and cultural value where companies release features, updates, and improvements at extremely high frequency (daily or near-daily) to maintain constant market noise, drive re-engagement, and make the product feel alive and evolving.

## Key Information

- Lovable's #1 core value in the development team: "We do anything and everything to keep it going up."
- Serves as a resurrection strategy (people come back to check what's new) and re-engagement strategy (users check socials instead of relying on newsletters)
- The short cycle from user feedback to delivery makes users feel heard
- Requires product engineers who can also announce their own features (not just funnel through marketing)
- Requires high autonomy and agency: people own features from start to finish
- Garav (CEO of Mirage/Captions) has a similar policy: ship a marketable feature every week
- Creates a feeling that "the product is alive, it's changing every single week"
- Big launches (tier 1) still happen as step-function PMF changes, but the daily noise maintains momentum between them
- Quality is maintained through hiring: people with passion, fire, high agency, and high autonomy
- If something fails, just pivot — the velocity makes failures low-cost
