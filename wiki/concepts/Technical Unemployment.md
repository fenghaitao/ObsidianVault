## Definition

Technical Unemployment is the concern that AI and automation will replace human workers at scale. Philip Su has been concerned about this since 2018, before the term was widely used, and it was a driving reason for his career decisions.

## Key Information

- In 2018, Philip was already "complaining to co-workers that I think we're going to experience technical unemployment" — back then the term hadn't really come around yet
- Most of his co-workers disagreed with his assessment at the time
- This concern was a primary reason he left Facebook — he wanted to start a BC Corp to bridge the rising income gap in America
- His podcast "Peak Salvation" explores these concerns through his experience working at Amazon's flagship warehouse over peak season
- Philip believes "as Americans, we need to get together and figure out how we help the average person through this change"
- He warns that "the change is going to be massive, it's going to come sooner than people expect, and then very bad things can happen if we don't help the whole society adapt to it"
- From a universe/evolutionary perspective, AI is unique: "we might be the first species to create something smarter than us"
- This concern also applies to specialization: a specialty might become irrelevant in the age of AI, like being a COBOL expert hoping Y2K happens again
