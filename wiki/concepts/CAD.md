## Definition

Computer-aided design — the dense, solid engineering geometry work (understanding friction, weight, contact) that [[Caitlin Kalinowski]] says current AI (LLMs, video models) cannot yet do well.

## Key Information

- Kalinowski wants a "Codex for hardware engineering" — an AI coding-agent equivalent for CAD work — but believes the biggest bottleneck to building one is that proprietary CAD data is a major moat manufacturers guard closely, starving any general model of training data.
- [[World Labs]] (world models, founded by [[Fei-Fei Li]]) is raised as a candidate direction for AI that understands physical properties well enough for real CAD work, though Kalinowski says she isn't familiar with its specifics and views world models as likely part of, not the whole of, the solution.
