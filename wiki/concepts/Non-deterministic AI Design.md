## Definition

Non-deterministic AI models cannot be fully designed through traditional mockups and prototypes because their behavior is inherently unpredictable. You can't mock up all the states or create a clickable prototype that captures the model's actual behavior. Designers must use the actual models underneath and observe how real users interact with them to discover use cases.

## Key Information

- "You can't mock up all the states" — non-deterministic models have too many possible outputs
- "You can't theorize and you can't even make a clickable prototype with it" — traditional design tools are insufficient
- Designers must use the actual models underneath and see people try them with real use cases
- Use cases are discovered as people use the models, not designed upfront
- This is a key reason why rapid shipping and iteration is replacing traditional upfront design
- Example: Claude Co-work emerged from observing how people actually used Claude, not from a designed vision
