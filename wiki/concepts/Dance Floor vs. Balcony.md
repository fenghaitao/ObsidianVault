## Definition

The dance floor vs. balcony is a metaphor for stepping back from the day-to-day hustle (the dance floor) to gain perspective and reflect on the bigger picture (the balcony). It applies to co-founder relationships, executive team strategy, and personal career management.

## Key Information

- **Dance floor**: Being in the hustle and bustle of running and scaling the business — the urgent, day-to-day work.
- **Balcony**: Stepping back to look down at what's happening — reflecting on how things are going, whether the relationship/strategy is still working, and what needs to change.
- Co-founders need regular balcony time: "How are we doing? Is this still working for both of us?"
- Executive teams need balcony time away from being in the business to work on the business — quarterly reflections, annual kickoffs.
- The metaphor connects to the one-page plan operating rhythm: quarterly gatherings to reflect on what worked, what didn't, and to air "inconvenient truths."
