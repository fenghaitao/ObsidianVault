## Definition

Following the Pull is a career and creative philosophy of pursuing what intuitively draws you in, rather than rigidly sticking to a predetermined plan. It involves noticing what feels compelling and letting that guide your next steps.

## Key Information

- Demonstrated by Lenny Rachitsky: his newsletter was not in any of his career plans (A through D), but he followed the pull of writing
- Demonstrated by Michelle Rial: she started a children's book in the back of her notebook while working on a different project, simply because the idea kept pulling at her
- Key signals: intrinsic enjoyment, positive external feedback, and a sense of compulsion
- Friend Lee Jacobs' framework: "How often do you enjoy something and people value it? That Venn diagram is rare"
- Contrasts with purely rational career planning; incorporates intuition and emotional resonance
