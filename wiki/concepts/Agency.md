## Definition

Agency is the proactive taking of initiative to seek out and create opportunities rather than waiting for them to come to you. It is a critical factor in capitalizing on luck and turning "nos" into "yeses."

## Key Information

- Ryan Peterman demonstrated agency when his desired manager at Meta had no headcount — he begged and convinced the manager to find headcount, saying "I promise you I'm going to really work as hard as I can please please please"
- Ryan noted: "the best opportunities are not the ones that seek you out but the ones that you seek out — the ones that are seeking you out are in a position of less strength"
- Evan reflected: "so much of this stuff is luck and opportunity, and if you have that extra agency to go and take initiative and seek things out, you can turn things that were nos into yeses"
- Agency was described as "a huge part of actually capitalizing on opportunity"
- Both Evan and Ryan demonstrated agency in seeking out the best teams and opportunities rather than accepting what was offered
- Evan's agency was also shown in his early career: building side projects at Zillow beyond his role, starting the hacking club, and being inquisitive rather than bounded by what he was told to do
- Jia Chen demonstrated extreme agency: secretly renting a car and driving to Ohio for a hackathon without telling her parents, winning first place; she described hackathons as helping develop agency — being able to think on your feet, do something new, and not be afraid to do it in an unfamiliar environment
- Jia emphasized that agency is "not the audacity, but the initiative to kind of just go, not take permission, and just go and send it"
- Jia's agency was further demonstrated by founding Sprint.dev from a bored Thursday night, shipping an MVP overnight, and raising angel investment at a Luma event
