## Definition

Distribution Systems are the channels and methods through which a product reaches its users. Jia Chen identified distribution as a key win condition for starting a company, using her social media following as her primary distribution channel.

## Key Information

- Jia listed "distribution systems for people" as one of her win conditions before dropping out of college to start a company
- Her primary distribution system was her Instagram following, where she posted the Sprint.dev MVP at 9:00 AM and it immediately crashed from too much traffic
- After receiving angel checks, she posted a video about it that got 500K views on Instagram, leading to more angel investors reaching out — demonstrating how distribution compounds
- The concept ties into her broader philosophy: having the ability to market products and reach users is as important as technical ability to build
- Distribution was listed alongside technical ability and network confidence as the three key win conditions
