## Definition

The Master Plan is the first PRD in Lazar's AI development documentation framework. It's a 10,000-foot overview that explains the intent, audience, and emotional goals of the project. It references the other PRDs for detailed parameters.

## Key Information

- First document in the PRD framework
- Contents: why you're building this, who you're building it for, how you want them to feel
- References other PRDs: "The design needs to feel modern and slick but for exact parameters consult design guidelines.md"
- Serves as the "compass" — the high-level direction for the AI agent
- "It's really just like, hey, this is why I'm doing this. This is who I'm doing it for. This is how I want them to feel."
- Analogous to telling a technical co-founder your startup idea
