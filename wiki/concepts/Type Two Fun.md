## Definition

Type Two Fun is fun that is hard — even unpleasant — in the moment, but is remembered later as one of the best times of your life. David Singleton learned the term at Stripe and uses it as a management aspiration.

## Key Information

- Singleton wants to create a lot of type two fun for teams: challenging, high-accomplishment periods that feel rewarding in retrospect
- It reframes the "happy team" question: teams that report the greatest happiness are rarely performing at their very best; teams with feedback and a desire to change often report the best time a year later
- He explicitly does not want anyone unhappy, and wants to build an organization where he'd be happy doing any role
- The idea complements his observation that "teams that are perfectly happy are rarely performing at their very best potential"
