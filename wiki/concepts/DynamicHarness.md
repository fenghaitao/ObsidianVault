## Definition
Dynamic Harness is a future vision proposed by Tejas Kumar where agents generate their own harnesses on the fly before executing a task. The agent would be self-aware of potential failure modes, create a custom harness (similar to plan mode but on steroids), execute the task within those guardrails, and return the result — all without human harness engineering.

## Key Information
- Proposed by Tejas Kumar as the next logical step toward AGI
- Timeline: 2025 was the year of agents, 2026 is the year of harnesses, 2027 could be the year of dynamic on-the-fly generated harnesses
- The agent would know where it might hallucinate or fail and proactively build guardrails
- Analogous to plan mode, but the agent creates an actual runtime harness rather than just a plan
- Would eliminate the need for manual harness engineering for each new task
- Represents a meta-level capability: agents that can reason about their own limitations and build scaffolding to compensate
- Self-aware agents creating their own deterministic constraints
