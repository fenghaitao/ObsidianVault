## Definition

Jenny Wen's management philosophy: tasks typically classified as "low leverage" for managers (testing the product, reproducing bugs, fixing small issues, making personal cards) actually become high leverage when done by a senior leader. These actions demonstrate deep care, product familiarity, and create a culture where nothing is "below" anyone. The leaders Jenny respects most intentionally choose these tasks.

## Key Information

- Standard management advice: categorize work into what only you can do vs. what anyone can do; "low leverage" tasks should be delegated
- Jenny's counterpoint: the leaders she respects most choose "low leverage" tasks themselves, and it becomes high leverage because it's them doing it
- Examples: senior leaders deeply testing the product, dogfooding, reproducing bugs, sharing logs with engineers, nitpicking
- Mike Krieger has put in PRs himself — it signals "we're all on this team together and nothing is below this person"
- Cultural examples: making someone's anniversary card personally, vibe coding something nice — shows deep care
- Creates product familiarity, signals that the leader cares deeply, and builds team cohesion
- Must be balanced with high standards — the combination of psychological safety and high expectations
