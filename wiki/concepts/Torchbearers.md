## Definition

One of the named governance frameworks [[Eric Ries]] formalizes in *[[Incorruptible (book)|Incorruptible]]* — individuals within an organization who personally carry and enforce the founding mission forward, particularly at succession points.

## Key Information

- Listed by Ries alongside [[Mission Guardian]], [[Director's Oath]], and [[Mission Protective Provisions]] as one of the book's structural mechanisms for keeping a company's ethos intact across leadership transitions.
