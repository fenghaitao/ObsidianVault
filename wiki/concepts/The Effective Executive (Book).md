## Definition

The Effective Executive is a classic 1960s management book by Peter Drucker. Matt McGinness recommends it as full of simple, enduring advice on leading teams effectively, noting that "the good stuff is the stuff that's been in print for 70 years."

## Key Information

- Author: Peter Drucker
- Originally published in the 1960s
- Uses dated pronouns (secretary as "she," executive as "he") but the content is enduring
- "Chock full of simple, enduring advice on how to be effective at leading teams"
- McGinness's principle: "The good stuff is the stuff that's been in print for 70 years"
- One of three books McGinness recommends most often
