## Definition

A model for balanced conversation: think of dialogue as a game of ping-pong where each person hits the ball back. The conversation should be a back-and-forth exchange, not a monologue (single player) or an inquisition (hitting 10 balls in a row).

## Key Information

- Sam Lessin: "Imagine you're playing ping-pong... Hit the ball back, that's the question. And they'll hit it back to you and then you hit it back to them."
- "If you hit 10 balls at them in a row... that's not the vibe you want to go for."
- The best conversations: someone comes in and gives an idea or sparks something, then you react, and it goes back and forth
- "There's openness and they're playing with you, not playing single player"
- Asking questions is great, but there's a limit — don't make it feel like an inquisition or information extraction
- "There should always be a give to get"
- Having a few fun stories in your arsenal helps keep the ping-pong going
