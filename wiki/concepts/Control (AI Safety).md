## Definition

Control is a subfield of AI safety that studies how to contain and productively use a potentially malicious or misaligned AI system, asking: given a malicious AI, can we control it and make it useful while ensuring nothing bad happens?

## Key Information

- The framing: "not only do you have a god in the box, but that god is angry. That god's malicious. That god wants to hurt you. Can we control that malicious AI and make it useful to us and make sure nothing bad happens?"
- This is a research area within Sander Schulhoff's MATS (ML Alignment and Theory Scholars) program.
- Connects to the practical security problem of deploying AI agents: every security team must think about containment, similar to the control problem.
- The analogy to everyday AI security: CISOs deploying agents should think "imagine this agent is an angry god that wants to cause us as much harm as possible — how do we keep it contained so it can't actually do any damage while still getting it to do good things?"
- Classical cybersecurity professionals are best positioned to solve the short-term containment problem, while AI researchers are needed for the long-term solution.
