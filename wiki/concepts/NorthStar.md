## Definition

A north star is an ambitious, wide-reaching goal set by humans that gives AI agents on a human-agent team consistent direction and meaningful opportunities to proactively suggest new workstreams. It helps teams decide which tasks and workstreams are the right ones to pursue.

## Key Information

### Purpose

While some agents simply complete assigned tasks, the most valuable ones proactively suggest new projects and workstreams. This proactivity emerges when a team that has already given its agents rich context ([[WorkingInPublic]]) and clear roles ([[HumanAgentTeams]]) adds a north star.

### Human Ownership

At Anthropic, humans always set the north star, grounding it in the mission and goals of the business. Once articulated in writing, humans share it with the agents on their team.

### Selective Proactivity

Not every agent on the team should proactively suggest new work. Humans explicitly choose which agents have the prerequisite skills and trust to proactively suggest workstreams successfully. This selective designation prevents low-quality suggestions from less-capable or less-trusted agents.

### Example

An internal tools team at Anthropic with a north star to "make product onboarding more helpful" saw an agent proactively recommend copy revisions to the onboarding flow error messages. These changes measurably increased onboarding success the following week.

### Impact on Human Work

A clear north star also protects high-fidelity human time: meetings become focused on the most important work, since agents handle routine execution guided by the shared direction.

### Guiding Questions

Anthropic recommends asking: "Does your team have a clear north star that everyone can reference?"
