## Definition

Connect Before Correcting is the principle that you must build a bridge of connection with someone before attempting to correct their behavior. Connection is what forms a bridge between two people so they can act together in the same interest. It is not a "soft" topic — it's the foundation for both cooperation and productivity.

## Key Information

- The "taxes" example: If your partner says "We have to do our taxes" vs. "Whoa, I realize I didn't tell you this. We have to get our taxes done tonight. You look like you're just settling in to that book. Can we get on top of this together? I know we're on the same team."
- Connection is seeing someone "in my reality as a full human being, not just as an object in his world to get something done"
- "Connection first of all just feels good anyway, but it's not such a soft topic. Connection is what forms a bridge between two people so they can act together in the same interest."
- The first step is a mindset, not a script: "We feel people's intention, not just their intervention."
- The mindset: being present without an agenda — even for 30 seconds. "No one wants something of me. They just kind of see me."
- If you approach connection with a hidden agenda ("I'm going to connect to Lenny first so he'll water my plants"), it feels dirty and doesn't work
- Concrete examples with kids: sitting next to them and putting your hand on their back for longer than feels natural; saying "I'm happy to see you"; watching them play and describing what they're doing instead of peppering with questions
- At work: "Hey, tell me a little about your long weekend" — and not counting down the seconds for them to finish so you can get to the agenda
- Related to [[Radical Candor]]'s "challenge directly but care deeply"
- "Efficiency and relationship building are often in opposition. We're doing one or the other."
