## Definition

Internal [[Anthropic]] growth-platform initiative (acknowledged by [[Amol]] as an awkward acronym) to automate growth experimentation itself using [[Claude]] — not just building growth features faster, but having Claude identify, build, test, and analyze growth experiments with progressively less human involvement.

## Key Information

- Led by [[Alexey Komissarouk]] (who teaches growth engineering at Reforge) on Anthropic's growth platform team; kicked off only a couple of months before this episode, and only became viable with Opus 4.5/4.6 — earlier models "just weren't seeing the results."
- Evaluated across four stages of the growth-shipping lifecycle: (1) identifying opportunities from trends/past patterns, (2) building the feature, (3) testing it against quality/brand bars, (4) analyzing shipped results for learnings — Claude's competence is scored separately on each stage.
- Current performance, per Amol: roughly the win rate he'd expect from a junior PM (2-3 years' experience), not yet senior-PM level — but improving rapidly given how recently it became possible at all.
- Currently limited to small-scale changes (copy tweaks, minor UI adjustments) with a human approving before shipping; Amol expects the human-review requirement to shrink over time as brand/quality guidelines are codified into skills Claude can reliably apply on its own.
- Explicitly does NOT (yet) cover the one part of the shipping lifecycle Amol considers still fundamentally human: cross-functional stakeholder alignment — see [[Notebook Channels]] and the broader "alignment" theme also raised by [[Cat Wu]] and [[Nikhyl Singhal]].
