## Definition

Decision Maker is a strategy for students at non-target schools to stand out by becoming a decision maker in their area — whether through club leadership, large side projects, or personal brand. Jia Chen proposed this as the answer to succeeding without a prestigious school name.

## Key Information

- Jia's advice for non-target school students: "Become a decision maker in the area" — whether it's leadership in clubs, a big project many people use, a large side project, or a personal brand
- This is essentially about working on your agency so you can stand out when your school is not a differentiator
- Core principle: "If your school's not an outlier, you should be an outlier in the things that you do"
- Jia herself exemplified this at Michigan State University by winning 21 hackathons, building a 50K social media following, and founding a startup — all of which made her a decision maker rather than a passive student
- The concept applies beyond school: being a decision maker in any context signals capability and initiative more than credentials
