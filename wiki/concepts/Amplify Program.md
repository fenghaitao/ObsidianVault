## Definition
The Amplify Program is Amplifon's global, cross-functional AI adoption program launched in January 2025. It sets the rules for AI adoption across the organization through an operating model (Control Tower + Committee) and three pillars: governance, platform, and factory.

## Key Information
- **Launch**: January 2025 at [[Amplifon]], the world leader in hearing care solutions
- **Operating Model**: Control Tower (limited set of people including chief roles deciding security, legal, technology guidelines and strategy focus) plus Committee (responsible for running strategy in countries and corporate, prioritizing use cases for value delivery)
- **Three Pillars**:
  1. **Governance**: Ensure alignment with AI regulatory, strategy, and centrally defined guidelines; make people aware of the program and rules of play; keep everyone informed of how value is delivered and rolled out
  2. **Platform**: Set up certified infrastructure for AI development; establish ways of working to deliver processes and services for scaling AI applications
  3. **Factory**: Development teams focused on rolling out solutions to market; emphasis on scalability and reusability across different domains and countries
- **Key Problems Addressed**: Maintenance and operations (short LLM lifecycle), governance and compliance (knowing where AI is used), enterprise scaling (centralized guidelines across multiple teams and infrastructures)
- **Technical Output**: Enterprise-grade registry system with AI Gateway, MCP Registry, A2A Registry, and Use Case Registry; blueprint repositories for MCP and A2A development; CICD-integrated deployment and metadata publishing
- **Goal**: Make developers' lives easier by letting them focus on business logic while infrastructure, security, deployment, and maintenance are handled centrally
