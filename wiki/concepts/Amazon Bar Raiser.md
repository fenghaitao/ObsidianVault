## Definition

The Amazon Bar Raiser program is an interviewer training and assessment program at Amazon. Bar Raisers are trained to conduct interviews, assess talent, and level candidates, with the authority to veto hiring decisions to maintain a high bar.

## Key Information

- Steve Huynh was a Bar Raiser trainer who trained thousands of people at Amazon on how to conduct interviews, assess talent, and level people
- The training was originally live sessions but has since moved to a set of videos
- Bar Raisers focus on behavioral interviewing as the key differentiator — coding and system design are the "anti" (necessary but not sufficient), while behavioral is the "poker" part
- Amazon will reject candidates with strong coding and system design if behavioral answers are poor
- The fundamental question the Bar Raiser is trying to answer: "Do I want to work with this person?"
