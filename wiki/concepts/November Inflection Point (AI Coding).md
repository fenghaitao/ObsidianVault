## Definition

The November Inflection Point refers to November 2025, when GPT-5.1 and Claude Opus 4.5 crossed a qualitative threshold in AI coding: previously, coding agents would "mostly work" but required close attention; suddenly, they "almost always" did what they were told. Simon Wilson coined this framing, describing it as the moment when AI coding went from promising to practically transformative.

## Key Information

- Simon Wilson's framing of the November 2025 AI coding breakthrough
- Two models triggered it: GPT-5.1 (OpenAI) and Claude Opus 4.5 (Anthropic), both released in November 2025
- The improvement was "incremental but crossed a threshold" — a qualitative leap, not just quantitative
- Software engineers who tinkered over the holidays had a "moment of realization": "Oh, wow, this stuff actually works now"
- "A lot of people woke up in January and February and started realizing, 'Oh, wow, this technology which I'd been kind of paying attention to, suddenly it's got really, really good'"
- The reverberations were still being felt months later, as engineers grappled with what it means for their careers
- Both Anthropic and OpenAI spent the entirety of 2025 focusing their training efforts on coding, using reinforcement learning and reasoning models
- Simon Wilson: "The reverberations of that are still shaking us... A lot of people woke up in January and February and started realizing... I can turn out 10,000 lines of code in a day and most of it works"
