## Definition

SaaS Copocalypse is a term used in Silicon Valley to describe the trend of companies increasingly choosing to build their own software with AI agents rather than buying SaaS products. Malte Ubl argues SaaS companies will be fine, but the trend means more software being made and more work for engineers.

## Key Information

- Term used in Silicon Valley discourse
- Trend: companies answer the "buy vs. make" question with "make" more often, enabled by AI agents
- Malte Ubl's take: SaaS companies will be all right, but more companies making more software leads to more engineering work
- Part of the broader thesis that cheaper software creation leads to more software demand (software market elasticity)
- Connects to the idea that agents fill the gap between "all software that should exist" and what was economically viable
