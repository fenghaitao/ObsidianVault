## Definition

One of the named governance frameworks [[Eric Ries]] formalizes in *[[Incorruptible (book)|Incorruptible]]* — a formal commitment board directors make to uphold a company's mission, distinct from and in addition to standard fiduciary duty under [[Shareholder Primacy]].

## Key Information

- Listed by Ries alongside [[Torchbearers]], [[Mission Guardian]], and [[Mission Protective Provisions]] as one of the book's structural mechanisms for embedding mission protection directly into governance roles rather than relying on informal goodwill.
