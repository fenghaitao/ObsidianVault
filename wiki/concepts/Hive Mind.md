## Definition

[[Jason Lemkin]]'s term, inspired by the TV show *Pluribus*, for a future state of AI go-to-market agents that share data and learnings across all of a company's customers/agents simultaneously — as opposed to today's siloed agents that each only know their own segment.

## Key Information

- Lemkin's *Pluribus* analogy: in the show, a hive mind connects nearly everyone except a small handful of holdouts; he maps this to AI GTM agents eventually sharing "all of the data across all of your agents" and knowing everything that happens across every customer interaction, rather than operating as isolated, segment-specific instances (see [[Chief Orchestration Officer]] on today's need to manually segment agents to avoid conflict).
- Lemkin's claim about today vs. the hive-mind future: "right now, get your AI agents to be almost as good as humans, but working 24/7 and at scale — that's pretty damn good... [but] when AI really is the hive mind... then humans are at risk in GTM. They're going to be so much better when they're the hive mind."
- Ties this to [[Salesforce]]'s emerging role: despite being "founded in the 90s," Salesforce has become the shared database/hub that most competing AI GTM agent vendors plug into — Lemkin frames this as an early, partial instance of the hive-mind pattern (a common data substrate across otherwise-separate agents), predicting a "renaissance" for Salesforce as CRM specifically because of this integration role.
- Explicitly speculative/future-facing: Lemkin frames today's "master agents that can manage agents that can manage agents" discourse (which he says people already talk about on social media) as not yet real — "we're not there yet... but I'm excited for it."
