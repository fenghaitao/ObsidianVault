## Definition

Relationship Investment is the conscious allocation of time and effort to friendships, romantic relationships, and life outside of work. Evan King identified this as his biggest area of neglect during his rapid career growth.

## Key Information

- Evan's biggest advice to his younger self: "really invest in relationships and the aspects outside of work"
- He was "underinvested in friendships and romantic relationships" because he was "putting so much effort into work"
- Evan realized: "you spend all this time road mapping, goaling, checking in with yourself about your career progression, but in terms of general life progression I didn't apply nearly that same vigor"
- Moving to LA and having friends outside the tech bubble was transformative: "now a lot of my friends don't work in tech and we invest a lot more in each other"
- The result: "my life is so much more fulfilled because of this"
- Small wins matter: "I'm moving apartments right now and I have any number of people that I can call to help me move — these small things are really what matter in life, more so than your quick career progression"
- Evan emphasized that you can do both: "I think you can absolutely do both" — career growth and relationship investment
- If forced to choose: "your relationships matter more than anything else in the world, so I would optimize for those as opposed to career"
