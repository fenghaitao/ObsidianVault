## Definition

Agent platform portability is the strategy of betting on portable patterns (skills, recurring schedules, human-in-the-loop) rather than committing to a single agent platform. Since agent platforms change every few weeks, the patterns you build on top of them should be transferable.

## Key Information

- Core thesis: "Don't marry a platform." Platforms will change; patterns shouldn't have to.
- Portable assets: agent skills (markdown files with step-by-step instructions), the Night Shift pattern (interface + schedule + human review), API-driven custom apps.
- Brian runs two platforms simultaneously: [[HermesAgent]] for routine jobs (GPT), [[ClaudeCowork]] for creative jobs (Opus).
- He previously used [[OpenClaw]] and ported all his skills to Hermes easily since they're just markdown files.
- Job categorization helps decide which platform: routine/recurring vs. high-stakes creative.
- Pricing changes (like Anthropic's June 2026 policy limiting Max plan to Claude's own tools) reinforce the need for portability.
