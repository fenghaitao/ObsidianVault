## Definition

A growth agent is an AI agent built at LinkedIn that is trained on the company's unique growth loops, funnels, and historical tests, and can critique product ideas for their growth potential and impact.

## Key Information

- Trained on LinkedIn's unique internal growth processes, loops, funnels, and past test results.
- Can critique product specs and ideas, evaluating how good they are from a growth perspective.
- The LinkedIn user research (UXR) team adopted it to evaluate which surfaced member features have the biggest growth opportunity — a use case not originally envisioned.
- Like the trust agent, the growth agent cannot be purchased off-the-shelf because it requires LinkedIn-specific knowledge.
- Designed to work with the trust agent and other agents via the orchestrator layer.
