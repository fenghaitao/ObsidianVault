## Definition
AI for Engineers is the human-centric design philosophy behind GitHub Copilot, articulated by CEO Thomas Dohmke: "we're not trying to build an AI engineer, we're trying to build AI for engineers." It positions AI as a tool that augments and empowers developers rather than replacing them, keeping the human at the center of the development process.

## Key Information
- Coined by Thomas Dohmke, CEO of GitHub, as the guiding philosophy for GitHub Copilot
- Contrasts with building an "AI engineer" (autonomous agent that replaces the developer)
- Core principle: meet developers where they are — integrate AI into existing workflows (IDE, pull requests) rather than requiring developers to adapt to AI
- Practical manifestation: ghost-text auto-completions that work like smarter auto-complete, sidestepping hallucination concerns since developers naturally adjust auto-completions
- The Copilot name itself visualizes this philosophy: a copilot, not an autopilot
- Extends to Copilot Workspace: human can interact, change bullet points, and modify code at every step of the issue-to-PR workflow
