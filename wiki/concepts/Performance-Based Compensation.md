## Definition

A creative approach to compensation negotiation that ties additional pay to measurable performance milestones, rather than fighting for a higher base salary alone. When base salary and equity are capped, performance-based triggers can unlock significant additional compensation while aligning incentives between the employee and the company.

## Key Information

- Jacob: "Often times we're putting performance incentives, milestone triggers. If the company grows to 100 million ARR and I contributed this to it, what would that outcome look like?"
- Example: Tom Brady's Tampa Bay contract:
  - Win 8 games → $500K
  - Win 12 games → $1.5M
  - Go to playoffs → extra
  - Win the division → extra
  - Super Bowl → extra
  - Win Super Bowl MVP → $2.5M more
- Benefits for the company: incentives are aligned; the employee has skin in the game and wants to see the success happen
- Benefits for the candidate: unlocks compensation beyond capped salary bands
- Jacob: "Those that I found are more confident to push towards performance-based tend to have more skin in the game. Tend to want to stick around and see that success happen."
- Performance-based structures can include: additional stock tranches, cash bonuses, title upgrades, retention bonuses
- Also applies to creative deal-making: when a Hollywood writer's base fee was capped, performance milestones (if the movie makes more than $50M) could unlock additional compensation
