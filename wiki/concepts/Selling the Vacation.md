## Definition

"Selling the Vacation" is Jacob Warwick's technique for psychologically walking a hiring manager into a utopian future where you've solved their biggest problems. Instead of competing on credentials and past experience, you make them visualize a future where working with you has eliminated their pain — making you the only person they can imagine hiring.

## Key Information

- Jacob's framework for discovery in interviews:
  1. **Why am I here?** — "What's exciting to you about me? What did I miss?"
  2. **Label the problems** — "It sounds like you have a challenge with this product line. It sounds like AI's been a problem."
  3. **SWOT analysis** — "What have you done about it? How big of a problem is this? What have you done well? What have you failed at? What should you be doing that you're not? What should you absolutely not touch?"
  4. **Sell the vacation** — "Fast forward 6 months. We've solved the churn. We've got two product launches. When you head into that board meeting, how can we ensure your head is held high?"
- The technique forces the hiring manager to visualize a utopian future with you right next to them
- "When it comes to your competition, there is no competition. I'm the only person who walked you into a vacation that you want to be in."
- Example: a talent agent closed a director by scheduling a lunch with Steven Spielberg before the meeting — making the dream future tangible
- This technique creates an emotional connection that logic and credibility alone cannot achieve
