## Definition

Compressed Research is one of Malte Ubl's four agent archetypes. It describes an agent that automates the research phase of a business process while leaving the final decision to a human, compressing what took 30 minutes into 5 minutes without changing the process or increasing risk.

## Key Information

- Part of Malte Ubl's Agent Archetypes framework
- The business process shape: business event → research → human decision
- The agent only does the research phase; the process and human decision remain unchanged
- Key advantage: does not increase risk profile and does not require process change
- If run 100,000 times per year, compressing 30 minutes to 5 minutes saves enormous amounts of money
- Vercel examples:
  - Contact Sales form: agent checks LinkedIn, Googles the company, determines company size, routes to the right person (~75% of time it identifies the inquiry as actually needing support)
  - Abuse reports: agent investigates whether a website is abusive before a professional makes the final decision
