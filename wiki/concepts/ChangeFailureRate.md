## Definition
Change failure rate is a DORA metric measuring the percentage of deployments that result in failures requiring remediation. Under AI adoption, some organizations have seen up to a 2% increase, which against an industry benchmark of 4% represents up to 50% more defects.

## Key Information
- Industry benchmark: approximately 4% change failure rate
- DX data shows a 1% average reduction in change failure rate with AI adoption
- Per-company data shows some organizations experiencing up to a 2% increase
- A 2% increase against a 4% benchmark means shipping up to 50% more defects than before AI adoption
- This is a critical quality metric to track alongside AI adoption to ensure velocity gains don't come at the cost of quality
- Part of the quality/speed balance: increasing PR throughput without creating tech debt
