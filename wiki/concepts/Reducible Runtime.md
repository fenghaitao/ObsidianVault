## Definition
Reducible runtime is a metric proposed by Michele Catasta for measuring agent autonomy: the span of time during which the user does not have to make any technical decisions and the agent can accomplish the task in full autonomy. The goal is to maximize this metric.

## Key Information
- Tasks have a natural level of complexity with a minimum irreducible amount of work.
- Agents go through a loop of planning, implementing, and testing.
- The goal is to maximize the reducible runtime — the portion of work where the agent operates independently.
- Contrasts with using raw runtime as a "vanity metric" or "badge of honor."
- Important for non-technical users who cannot be expected to make technical decisions during the process.
