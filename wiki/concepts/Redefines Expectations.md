## Definition

Redefines Expectations (RE) is Meta's rarest performance rating, above "greatly exceeds expectations" — the performer doesn't just exceed the bar but redefines it.

## Key Information

- Meta's ascending rating ladder: meets all, exceeds, greatly exceeds, then redefines expectations (RE)
- RE is extremely rare; most engineers never earn it once in their careers
- Simon earned RE twice — once as an IC4 and again during his IC4→IC5 half (the latter possibly boosted by forward-crediting the COVID half)
- An RE is a *rating*, not a promotion: you can get the highest rating without a promo
- The distinction: ratings measure *what* you achieved; promotions measure *how* you achieved it and whether the behaviors match the next level
- The COVID half produced no official ratings/promos, only an annotation like "significantly above," which roughly equated to a GE rating
