## Definition

A short squeeze occurs when a rising stock forces short sellers to buy back shares to close their positions, adding buy pressure that drives the price even higher.

## Key Information

- Shorting requires borrowing and selling a stock, then buying it back to return it; the buyback adds upward price pressure
- During the GameStop saga (Jan 2021), meme stocks like GameStop and AMC experienced short squeezes as hedge funds and institutions were forced to close shorts
- Robinhood paused buys on these stocks on Jan 28, 2021 due to collateral requirements, which coincided with price declines and public anger
