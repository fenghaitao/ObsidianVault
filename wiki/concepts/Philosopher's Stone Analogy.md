## Definition

[[Marc Andreessen]]'s central metaphor for AI's significance: alchemists (including [[Isaac Newton]]) spent decades trying and failing to find the "philosopher's stone" — a process to transmute common lead into rare gold — and AI has now literally achieved the equivalent, transmuting the most common material in the world (sand/silicon) into the rarest (thought).

## Key Information

- Newton, despite inventing calculus and Newtonian physics, was privately obsessed with alchemy and never solved the transmutation problem; Andreessen frames AI as succeeding where the greatest scientific mind in history failed.
- Used to argue this is "a very, very historic time" — not an incremental technology, but a category-level breakthrough comparable to converting a worthless raw material directly into humanity's most valuable resource (cognition/thought).
