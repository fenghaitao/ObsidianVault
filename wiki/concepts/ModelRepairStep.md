## Definition
The model repair step is a stage in the edit prediction training pipeline where bad teacher model predictions are identified through heuristic checks and then sent to another frontier model with a corrective prompt to fix them. It improves the quality of training data generated by the teacher-student distillation process.

## Key Information
- **Heuristic Checks**: Offline static evaluations detect common failure modes — undoing what the user just typed, ignoring the editable region boundary, and other quality issues
- **Repair Prompt**: Failed predictions are sent to another frontier model with a prompt like "it failed in this way, can you fix it?"
- **Position in Pipeline**: Sits between initial teacher prediction generation and the prompt formatting stage
- **Reusability**: Everything up to and including the repair step is cached and reusable across experiments
- **Motivation**: Frontier model predictions are inherently noisy — even with good prompts, 100K requests yield inconsistent results
- **Related Concept**: Similar to the verifier-fixer pipeline pattern used in code refactoring, but applied to model training data generation
