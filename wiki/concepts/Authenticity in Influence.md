## Definition

Authenticity in influence is the idea that influence is not tied to one personality type. You can be shy, introverted, data-oriented, or technical and still communicate authentically to build trust and influence. The key is to double down on who you are rather than trying to imitate someone else's style.

## Key Information

- Influence is not one personality type — "you could be shy and introverted or super data-oriented and technical"
- "All types of personalities can in their own authentic way communicate with the people around them to build trust to build influence"
- Jessica Fain's early career feedback: "Show up more like a man would. Show up more aggressively, show up more decisively"
- She learned: "Who I am and how I show up is actually my superpower that I can double down on"
- "I don't need to be like someone else. What I need to do is think about what I want to accomplish and how I want to grow and do that in a way that is true to me"
- Lenny agrees: "You can accomplish the same things other people can through your own strengths. You don't have to do it the way they're doing it"
- Jessica Fain describes herself as an Enneagram type 2-wing-3 (people person + achiever)
