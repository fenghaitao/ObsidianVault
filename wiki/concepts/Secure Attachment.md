## Definition

Secure attachment is the nature of a relationship you really want to have with your child (or anyone). Dr. Becky learned in clinical psych grad school that secure attachment is defined by the presence of repair, not the absence of mistakes. This was a hopeful revelation: secure attachment is something you can realistically get good at.

## Key Information

- "The thing that really differentiated secure attachment, which is the nature of a relationship you really want to have with your kid, is the presence of repair."
- "Secure attachment isn't defined by getting it right all the time. Secure attachment is just defined by we're all going to mess up, but secure attachment has an adult who's willing to repair."
- "That felt very hopeful to me because it felt like something I could realistically get good at."
- Repair reestablishes trust and connection, which leads to better cooperation
- Without repair, conversations become "run-on conversations that are really an act of defensiveness"
