## Definition

Parallel Prototyping is Lazar's methodology of starting 4-5 AI projects simultaneously with different approaches to rapidly gain clarity on what to build. It's unintuitive to traditional engineers but highly effective for vibe coders.

## Key Information

- Developed by Lazar as a natural workflow (attributed to his non-technical background)
- Four parallel approaches:
  1. **Brain dump via voice** — use Lovable's voice function or Whisper Flow to dump ideas, just press send
  2. **Typed thoughtful prompt** — write out a more deliberate version with more clarity
  3. **Mock design reference** — find a design on Mobin or Dribbble, attach screenshot as reference
  4. **Code template reference** — export code snippets from 21st Dev or Build UI for pixel-perfect results
- Two wins: (1) clarifies the idea as you see the tool build it, (2) lets you pick the right direction instead of being locked into the first design
- "By the time you do all of these three/four, you're already at a level of clarity that you wouldn't have if you just sat with an empty piece of paper"
- Saves tokens in the long run: upfront cost is higher, but avoids spinning wheels on a bad design
- Contrast with traditional engineering: "This is not how someone that has been a software engineer or a PM or designer would approach stuff"
- Also a productivity hack: Lazar builds 5-6 projects simultaneously, switching between Lovable tabs
