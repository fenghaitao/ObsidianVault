## Definition

Thought leadership is the strategy of positioning yourself (or your company) as the authority in a space so the market listens to you — the benefit Brendan Burns says Google explicitly pursued by creating and open-sourcing Kubernetes.

## Key Information

- Being "front and center" in thought leadership is valuable and was articulated to Google leadership as part of the Kubernetes case.
- Creating a brand-new playing field changes the narrative: instead of "tail light chasing" AWS's VMs, GCP could lead the container-orchestration conversation and gain a "lot more voice in the market."
- Control of the story forces people to listen even when they aren't yet on your platform; the perception benefit is hard to quantify but real.
- At the time it was cheap to attempt: the whole bet was roughly eight or nine engineers.
