## Definition

Founder Socials is a growth strategy where a company's founder(s) actively post on social media about the company's journey, metrics, lessons learned, and product updates. It is a core component of the building-in-public strategy and a key organic growth lever for AI companies.

## Key Information

- One of Lovable's biggest growth strategies, alongside employee socials and building in public
- Anton Osika (Lovable CEO) regularly posts about growth numbers, new features, and lessons learned
- Grant (Gamma CEO) is cited as another example of a founder who builds in public effectively
- Works on X (Twitter) and LinkedIn for B2B companies
- Content must show personality and authenticity — "corporate scrubbing has to completely fall off"
- "People want to rally behind a team they want to win, and for that you need to be vulnerable, you need to be authentic"
- Becomes harder to maintain as the company scales, but is a critical differentiator in early stages
- Part of the broader shift from SEO to social as organic marketing strategy
