## Definition
Cognitive exhaust fumes are the digital activity that is a byproduct of your cognition — emails, journal entries, browser history, task management, and other digital traces. Individually, each piece is just waste, but collectively analyzed, they can diagnose how you think and operate, much like analyzing car exhaust can diagnose an engine.

## Key Information
- Term coined by Šimon Podhajský
- Analogy: like exhaust fumes from a car engine — individually waste, but diagnostic when analyzed
- Includes email, journal entries, browser history, task manager data, CRM interactions, and other digital traces
- The cross-source combination is what makes the exhaust valuable — no single source tells the full story
- Three key insights the exhaust reveals: intention-action gaps, attention drift, and relationship decay
- Described as "the most underused data set you own"
- The exhaust becomes contaminated (cognitive pollution) if AI writes back to the source systems
