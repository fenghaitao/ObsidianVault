## Definition

SPOTTAK is a hiring evaluation framework used by Matt McGinness to decode his intuition about candidates. It stands for: Smart, Passionate, Optimistic, Tenacious, Adaptable, and Kind. When a candidate doesn't click, the framework helps identify which dimension is missing.

## Key Information

- Acronym: Smart, Passionate, Optimistic, Tenacious, Adaptable, Kind (six attributes)
- Origin: McGinness adopted it from someone else (hat tip to unknown originator)
- Not a top-down scoring rubric; used as a diagnostic tool when intuition about a candidate is unclear
- When a candidate doesn't feel right, go down the list to identify the specific gap: "They weren't passionate about the idea," "They talked negatively about their previous manager — not optimistic," etc.
- McGinness has interviewed thousands of candidates and relies heavily on intuition, arguing that HR's advice to avoid intuition is "complete BS" if you have good intuition
- After forming an intuitive reaction, the leader must decode it so it can be expressed productively to others
- Used in combination with the Alpha/Beta framework: is this person high-alpha or low-beta, and does that fit the role?
