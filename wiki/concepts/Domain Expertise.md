## Definition
Domain expertise is the deep, specialized knowledge of the workflows, nuances, use cases, and judgment criteria within a specific field or industry. In the context of AI product development, domain expertise is what enables organizations to assess AI quality, make informed decisions between approaches, and build differentiated products that truly understand customer needs.

## Key Information
- **Two types**: Formal domain expertise (doctors, lawyers — licensed professionals) and informal domain expertise (writers, journalists — practitioners with deep domain understanding without formal credentials)
- **Granularity matters**: Being "a doctor" may not be sufficient — the domain expert should have direct experience of the specific use case (e.g., medical coding, not just medicine)
- **Why it matters for AI**: Front-end models are good enough; the gap is how organizations operationalize expert judgment around them
- **Three common mistakes**: Not hiring domain experts (or hiring too late), hiring the wrong kind (not granular enough), and not fitting them into the organization correctly
- **Quality requires judgment**: Appraising AI quality requires domain expertise to determine what "good" looks like
- **Organizational imperative**: Winning in vertical AI is fundamentally an organizational problem — the system for incorporating domain insights matters more than model sophistication
