## Definition

[[Mark Pincus]]'s speculative concept for an AI-agent-mediated layer of trust and shared context that could broker social relationships — acting like "a great chief of staff" that knows both parties' context and dynamically adjusts how much attention/information to share between them.

## Key Information

- Example given: an agent negotiating a hangout between two people ("Lenny, let's hang out" / "Mark, I'd love that") without either person's full calendar/availability being exposed, avoiding the social awkwardness of looking too available or being scheduled into an unwanted commitment.
- Framed as a possible mechanism for rebuilding "lead generation" style value in dating, jobs, and general listings — extending the historic value of platforms like [[LinkedIn]] and [[Napster]] into an agentic-AI context.
- Connected to Pincus's broader claim that current AI chat products (Claude, GPT) have no social/"cocktail party" layer yet — the social membrane is one candidate for what that layer could look like.
