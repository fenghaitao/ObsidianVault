## Definition
Policy simulation is the use of AI to allow policy teams to test the impact of different policy decisions before they are made. It enables more decisions to be informed by high-quality modeling at a far faster rate, without replacing human analysis. Example: modeling how universal credit changes would impact household finances.

## Key Information
- Built by [[Number10DataScience]] for policy teams in 10 Downing Street
- Allows testing the impact of different policy decisions before they're made
- Applied to universal credit decisions and their impact on household finances
- Does not replace human analysis — augments it
- Result: far more decisions informed by high-quality modeling, at far faster rate
- Risk: sycophancy — AI telling users what they want to hear. Mitigated by red-teaming models and upskilling users
