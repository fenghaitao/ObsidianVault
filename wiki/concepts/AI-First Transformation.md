## Definition

The process of fundamentally reorienting a company to be "AI-first" — where AI adoption is non-negotiable and success is aligned with personal and organizational outcomes. This requires top-down commitment, clarity on what is and isn't up for debate, and reassurance that AI won't take jobs but that not using AI will.

## Key Information

- Articulated by Jeetu Patel based on Cisco's transformation
- Three key drivers: (1) Top-down clarity — AI-first is non-negotiable, go all in without hedging, (2) Redefine success — move from siloed GM ownership to platform thinking, (3) Shift from walled garden to open ecosystem
- "Innovation is a choice" — large companies can innovate; it's a binary choice made every hour
- Large companies experiment a lot but fail at going all in when experiments work — they keep hedging
- Cisco's transformation accelerated after ChatGPT's launch in November 2022 ("the seminal moment")
- Employees must understand that personal success and company success are aligned with AI dexterity
- The reverse is guaranteed: if you don't use AI, you won't be successful
