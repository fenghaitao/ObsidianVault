## Definition
Apache 2.0 License is a permissive open-source license adopted by Gemma 4 to make models more accessible for everyday developers, enabling integration from initial testing through to production deployment.

## Key Information
- Deliberately chosen by Google DeepMind to increase developer accessibility
- Allows easy integration throughout the development lifecycle (testing to deployment)
- Represents a shift toward more permissive licensing for the Gemma model family
- Enables commercial use, modification, and distribution
- Represents a direct response to community feedback — previous Gemma versions had restrictive licensing that users complained about
- Enables sovereign AI initiatives by removing licensing barriers for national/regional model development
