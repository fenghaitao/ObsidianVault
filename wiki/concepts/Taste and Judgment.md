## Definition

Taste and Judgment is the accumulated sense — built through repetition and feedback — of what changes to make, where, and how aggressively, in both code and product.

## Key Information

- Michael Novati's thesis: his raw output didn't increase on the path from prolific coder to IC7; what crossed the line was accumulating taste and judgment
- Analogy: like the patina on a cast-iron pan, taste builds in layers through history and experience; some people start with stronger instincts, but it always requires reps
- In coding, it means knowing which parts of the system are sensitive to change, and how big a change is appropriate
- In product, a PM's taste is built by shipping lots of things and seeing what fails
- The loop is: do practice, get feedback, iterate, keep going — rushing ahead (entering the "Olympics" before building the base) is the common mistake
- It governs the push/release process too: judgment and earned credibility determine whether the "pushing team" trusts your changes
