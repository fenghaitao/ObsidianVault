## Definition

Unshipping is the practice of deleting low-usage features so the majority of users get a cleaner product, even though a small subset loses access.

## Key Information

- Adding features helps a small percentage of users but clutters the app for everyone else; screen real estate is a limited resource competing features fight over
- Features must clear a usage bar or be deleted — a few users are upset, but the result is better for the majority and net-better on average
- Boris absorbed this in Instagram's culture, which emphasized craft, human experience, and shipping things people actually use over pure data
