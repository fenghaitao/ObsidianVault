## Definition

Community as Growth Lever is the strategy of using a user community (e.g., Discord, ambassador programs) to amplify word-of-mouth, social posting, and retention. It brings people together to explore capabilities, help each other, and create a sense of belonging that drives engagement.

## Key Information

- Elena Verna: "Community amplifies that word-of-mouth. It amplifies all of the social posting. It amplifies retention mechanisms."
- Lovable's community runs on Discord (nothing custom-built) with hundreds of thousands of members
- Community managers ensure questions get answered and the right groups are created
- An ambassador program has been created for power users
- Community was started very early on at Lovable and is cited as a "huge part of Lovable's success"
- Not everyone can build a community, but "at least plugging into somebody's community is quite important"
- Community makes software more human by enabling peer-to-peer connection
