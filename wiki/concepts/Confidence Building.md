## Definition

Confidence Building refers to the moments and experiences that establish an engineer's belief in their own capabilities. These moments are crucial for enabling future growth, as they free the engineer from anxiety about whether they are good enough.

## Key Information

- Evan's first confidence-building moment: at Zillow, his manager (a former Googler) told him he was the best intern he had ever worked with — "to this day probably the biggest compliment I ever got, I was just beaming"
- His second confidence-building moment: at Meta, his manager pulled him into a room and told him he was promoted (IC3-IC4) without Evan ever asking — "I was shaking with giddiness"
- From that moment on, Evan had "the assurance that I was good and I was capable" and was "freed of that anxiety questioning whether or not I was good enough"
- This freedom allowed him to "just kind of continue to grow at that pace"
- Both confidence moments came from external validation — a manager's recognition — rather than internal self-assessment
- Evan noted that these moments were "super necessary for me to go on and achieve the promotions that were ultimately achieved"
- The confidence from Zillow carried into Meta, and the Meta promotion further solidified it
