## Definition

Brand Equity in the context of a software engineering career refers to the prestige and signaling value of having a recognizable company name on your resume. It sets a floor for future opportunities and makes it easier to get hired and recruited.

## Key Information

- If you go to a name that is known, anyone who sees your resume knows "you're at least that good" — you're at least that bar
- Brand equity makes it "a lot easier to get hired" — a known name on your resume is a strong signal
- Ryan noted that after joining Amazon (which he considered the "lowest tier of the FAANG ones"), his LinkedIn was "blowing up" with recruiter messages even without active posting
- The speakers recommended starting in big tech for the first few years to "lock in" the prestige, then do whatever you want
- Well-known startups like Perplexity can also carry some brand equity, though less than FAANG companies
- Brand equity is part of the "high expected value" of the big tech path, as opposed to the high variance of startups
