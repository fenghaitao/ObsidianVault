## Definition

"Think Like a CPO" is a mindset for product leaders: expand your viewpoint beyond your own feature or team to consider the entire business, ecosystem, and industry. It means putting yourself in the shoes of the executive and thinking about what's best for the company, not just your own product area.

## Key Information

- To be more senior, broaden your perspective to the whole ecosystem, whole organization, whole industry
- Connect your work back to business outcomes — don't just say "our KR is to ship this," explain why it matters for the business or user
- This is how you get seen as a strategic thinker, not just a tactical executor
- "If you want to grow into a product leadership role, expanding your viewpoint is a way to show up as that strategic leader you say you want to be"
- Jeff Weinstein at Stripe is cited as the epitome: constantly thinking about Stripe as a business, not just his product area
- At Webflow, "product citizenship" was added to the PM career ladder as a trait of seniority — embedding back in the organization, mentoring others, broadening horizons on tooling, technology, storytelling, and strategy
- If you're working on something that doesn't shift a business outcome or isn't urgent, consider changing course
- "Think like a CPO. Put yourself in their shoes"
- Stuart Butterfield noted that the CEO is often the only one who can kill things across the organization — but if everyone thinks like an owner, they'll bring those opportunities up too
