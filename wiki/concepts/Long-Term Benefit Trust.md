## Definition

[[Anthropic]]'s mission-oversight trust, stood up at its Series C — a modern implementation of [[Eric Ries]]'s [[Mission Guardian]] concept, functioning as a [[Spiritual Holding Company]]-style outside body protecting the company's mission against investor pressure.

## Key Information

- Written into Anthropic's charter as a right from day one (alongside [[Public Benefit Corporation]] status), then formally established at the Series C round.
- Ries credits this trust with protecting Anthropic's ability to refuse to release models it judges too dangerous and to turn down a reported $200 million Pentagon contract — decisions he attributes to the trust shielding leadership from investor pressure to prioritize growth over safety.
- Contrasted by Ries with founder-control dual-class share structures (e.g. Mark Zuckerberg, Larry Page/Sergey Brin) — he frames Anthropic's trust-based structure as more institutional and, in his view, more durable than personal founder control.
- Anthropic was not a "hot" company by 2026-era generative-AI-boom standards when [[Dario Amodei]] and [[Daniela Amodei]] founded it (pre-ChatGPT boom); no top venture funds joined the initial round, and early investors were largely "effective altruism"-style believers in its AI-safety mission.
