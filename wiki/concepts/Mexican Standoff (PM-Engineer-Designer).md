## Definition

Marc Andreessen's description of the current dynamic between product managers, engineers, and designers in the AI era: each role now believes they can do the other two because AI tools have lowered the barriers. Every coder thinks they can also be a PM and designer; every PM thinks they can code and design; every designer thinks they can be a PM and coder. And they're all kind of correct.

## Key Information

- Articulated by Marc Andreessen on Lenny's Podcast
- AI tools have collapsed the skill barriers between the three core product development roles
- The result is a "Mexican standoff" — each role is empowered to encroach on the others' territory
- This isn't a negative dynamic; Andreessen argues all three are "kind of correct" — AI makes it possible
- The resolution is the emergence of the "super relevant specialist" who combines multiple domains
- This represents a fundamental restructuring of how product teams operate
