## Definition

PRD-less Development is the practice of shipping products without formal Product Requirements Documents, relying instead on Slack messages, quick conversations, and prototyping. Amol Avaisary is "averse to PRDs" and estimates that 60-80% of what the Anthropic growth team ships has no PRD.

## Key Information

- Amol: "I'm averse to PRDs. I just hate documentation. I'm just like go go go. Just cut the blockers."
- 60-80% of what the growth team ships has no PRD
- 20-30% of stuff where it's really important to get right — documentation should be really good and people should spend a lot of time on it
- For small changes: "This is just on Slack — purely on Slack. Messages back and forth."
- Requires good caliber of product-minded engineers who can understand context and audience
- For larger things: still believes in a proper cross-functional kickoff — get legal, safeguards, everyone in the room for 30 minutes
- Even when creating a PRD, it's often: "5 minutes before the meeting, I'll put it into Claude — here are some thoughts, here are the things I need to think about"
- If he does create a PRD: uses a Claude skill with format built in, fills out why, problem, key considerations, cross-functional stakeholders
- "My default is if I can avoid the doc and if we can just jump to action, then that's what we should do"
- Increasingly, Amol just jumps to prototyping the thing instead of writing about it
