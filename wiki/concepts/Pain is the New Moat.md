## Definition

"Pain is the new moat" is a concept coined by Kiti Bottom describing how successful AI companies build competitive advantage not by being first to market, but by going through the pain of understanding what's non-negotiable, iterating through failures, and building institutional knowledge. This hard-won knowledge becomes the company's moat.

## Key Information

- **Coined by**: [[Kiti Bottom]]
- **Core idea**: Successful companies building in any new area succeed not because they're first to market or have a fancy feature, but because they "went through the pain of understanding what are the set of non-negotiable things and traded them off with model capabilities."
- **No shortcuts**: There's no textbook, no straightforward way, no known threaded path. The process is: "let's try this, if this doesn't work, let's try this."
- **The moat**: The knowledge built across the organization — the lived experience, the product evals, the institutional understanding — that's the competitive advantage. "That pain is what translates into the moat of the company."
- **For individuals**: Persistence is extremely valuable. "Going through the pain of learning this, implementing this, and understanding what works and what doesn't work" — that's the real differentiator.
- **Analogy**: Like turning coal into diamond — the pressure/iteration creates value.
- **Contrast**: People who try to skip the pain by buying "one-click agents" or expecting instant results are setting themselves up for failure.
