## Definition
The End of Apps is Kitze's prediction that most consumer applications will disappear, replaced by generative UI and AI agents. Users will perform tasks directly through a futuristic OS rather than installing and using individual apps. Only specialist software (color grading, movie making, music making) will survive.

## Key Information
- "We're actually not going to need most consumer apps"
- Normies won't vibe code but will do any task in a futuristic OS — UI generates on the fly
- "They'll be doing tasks and they'll forget about 'I need an app to do a task.' They'll just do it."
- A small set of apps will survive: software for specialists (color grading, movie making, music making)
- The current computer experience is "absolutely insane" — greeted by 17 updates for unused apps, tabs from yesterday
- Kitze's ChatGPT plugins moment: called his wife saying "it's over for all the apps, for all SaaS"
- Apple may be the one to deliver this: local Siri agent with tool capabilities from installed apps
- Google Pixel already demonstrates the direction: launches apps in background, orders coffee
