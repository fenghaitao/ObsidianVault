## Definition

Miso Making is Boris Cherny's post-AGI plan and a philosophical practice in long-term thinking. White miso takes at least 3 months; red miso takes 2-4 years. The practice teaches patience and thinking on time scales very different from engineering.

## Key Information

- Boris learned miso making while living in rural Japan
- In the town where he lived, everyone made miso and pickles; it was a way to build friendships through trading
- White miso: at least 3 months to make
- Red miso: 2, 3, or 4 years to make
- Boris: "You just have to be very patient. You kind of mix it up and then you just like let it sit"
- Boris: "The thing that I love about it is just thinking in these long time scales"
- This long-term thinking, combined with reading sci-fi, led Boris to join Anthropic: "I just felt like I had to contribute to it going a little bit better"
- Boris still makes miso
