## Definition

Agent harassment protection is a proposed system for contact centers that uses low-latency sentiment and acoustic analysis to detect when a customer becomes verbally abusive, triggering supervisor alerts or seamlessly transferring the call to an AI voice agent to protect the human operator's mental health. It is Phase 3 of Dippu Singh's contact center AI roadmap.

## Key Information

- **Problem**: Contact center agents face an increasing amount of verbal abuse from customers, with no systematic protection
- **Detection Method**: Low-latency sentiment analysis combined with acoustic analysis to identify abusive speech patterns in real time
- **Response Options**:
  - Trigger alerts to supervisors or upper management
  - Seamlessly transfer the call to an AI voice agent, removing the human operator from the abusive interaction
- **Philosophy**: The goal is to transform contact centers from "cost centers of high stress into highly efficient intelligence-gathering engines that protect their workforces"
- **Roadmap Position**: Phase 3 — the most important phase for human well-being, building on Phase 1 (explainable AI for coaching) and Phase 2 (predictive staffing)
- **Relationship to Cognitive Load**: Extends the concept of [[Operator Cognitive Load]] beyond administrative burden to include emotional and psychological safety
