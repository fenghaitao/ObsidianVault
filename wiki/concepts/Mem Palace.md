## Definition
Mem Palace is a knowledge management approach created by Mina Jovovich for AI-assisted personal knowledge bases. It was referenced by Chris Parsons as an alternative to the Zettelkasten method.

## Key Information
- Created by Mina Jovovich
- Knowledge management system designed for AI-assisted workflows
- Referenced alongside Andrej Karpathy's "LLMs as wiki" article and Zettelkasten as different approaches to personal knowledge management with AI
- Chris Parsons mentioned it as one of "lots of different systems for that out there"
