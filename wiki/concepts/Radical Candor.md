## Definition

Management philosophy from [[Kim Scott]]'s book of the same name, summarized on [[Lenny's Podcast]] as "challenge directly but care deeply" — giving direct, honest feedback while genuinely caring about the person receiving it.

## Key Information

- One of the most frequently mentioned books on [[Lenny's Podcast]], second only to *High Output Management*; [[Jacob Warwick]] credits it (alongside his wife's influence) with giving him the confidence to be more assertive in his career.
- [[Dr. Becky Kennedy]] connects her own [[Connecting Before Correcting]] framework to Radical Candor's "challenge directly but care deeply" formulation when discussing how to give hard feedback without severing trust.
- [[Lenny Rachitsky]] recounts a Kim Scott anecdote (in the Dr. Becky Kennedy episode) about someone she managed who was underperforming; she avoided giving direct feedback to spare his feelings, and nine months later had to fire him — he then asked why she never told him along the way. Kennedy uses this as the connecting example for her [[Resilience Over Happiness]] framework: withholding hard feedback to avoid short-term discomfort causes greater harm long-term.
