## Definition

Radical Candor is a feedback culture, particularly associated with Netflix, where colleagues give immediate, direct feedback with the intention of helping each other grow. It can feel harsh to newcomers from more gentle company cultures but is rooted in genuine care for professional development.

## Key Information

- Zach described Netflix's culture as having "this feedback culture — the radical candor of just give feedback immediately"
- Engineers coming from "kinder" or "more chill" companies often initially perceive Netflix colleagues as mean or rude
- The deeper truth Zach recognized: "Everyone here really wants me to grow — that's what it's actually about"
- This direct feedback culture was something Zach was able to learn from and later impressed people at Airbnb
- Radical Candor at Netflix is connected to the broader "freedom and responsibility" culture, where managers have significant power to hire and fire
- Zach notes that this culture "has minuses as well — it's not all pluses"
- The ability to receive and benefit from radical candor is a skill that transfers across companies and impressed Airbnb during Zach's interviews
