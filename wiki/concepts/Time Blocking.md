## Definition

Time Blocking is a time management technique where specific time slots are allocated to specific tasks. Jia Chen used this to balance a 3.5+ engineering GPA, 15 hackathons in one semester, and growing to 50K social media followers.

## Key Information

- Jia used time blocking to schedule specific times and locations for homework tasks
- She ensured all homework was done before Friday at 5:00 PM so she could fully focus on winning hackathons on weekends
- She believed in full focus on the current environment rather than multitasking (e.g., doing homework at hackathons)
- For content creation, she used short-form content (3-5 second reels) that required minimal effort but had high leverage
- Her approach to content: recognize that there are 24 hours in a day and enough time for a one-minute reel, so film wherever you are
- She also attended office hours for hard classes passively, acquiring information from others' questions and professor's offhand exam hints

### Jake Bolam's IC8 System
- Jake Bolam's time-shielding system: no meetings before lunch, none on Wednesdays/Fridays, ~50% of the week as focus blocks, 1:1s batched Monday afternoons, and project meetings Tuesday/Thursday afternoons

### David Singleton's Weekly "Rocket Doc"
- Every Sunday night he writes what a great week would look like: the outcomes he'd get done, kept in a Google Doc titled "Rocket Doc"
- During the week he returns to that doc instead of his inbox or calendar; he later shared it with his executive assistant as a collective goal to chase

### David Fowler's Eight-Hour IC Block
- After falling into the "meeting trap" at partner level, his mentor told him to block at least eight hours a week for thinking and building
- Protecting that block required learning to decline meetings aggressively
