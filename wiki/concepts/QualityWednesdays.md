## Definition
Quality Wednesdays is a weekly team practice at Linear where the entire engineering team (~25 engineers, fully remote) gathers for 30 minutes and each engineer presents one quality fix they found themselves in the product. The practice was created by CTO Tuomas Artman to institutionalize attention to detail and build a culture where every engineer is constantly on the lookout for quality issues.

## Key Information
- Created by Tuomas Artman, CTO of Linear, about 3-4 years ago
- Started when Tuomas found 35 problems in a single small UI menu during an offsite and realized the entire application must have thousands of small quality issues
- To date, Linear has fixed 2,500-3,000 small quality details through this practice
- Each engineer must find their own problem — they are not handed to them
- Fixes range from one-pixel changes to backend efficiency improvements
- The side effect: engineers are always on the lookout for quality issues while building unrelated features, because they know they need a fix for Wednesday
- This constant awareness means fewer quality regressions are introduced in the first place
- Quality Wednesday fixes do not count as bug fixes — bugs are handled separately through the Zero Bug Policy
- Gergely Orosz observed a session and described it as "awesome" and "aspirational"
- Recommended for both small and large startups, especially now that AI agents make it easier to find and fix quality issues
