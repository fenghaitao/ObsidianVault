## Definition

The Associate Full-Stack Builder (APB) program is LinkedIn's replacement for its traditional Associate Product Manager (APM) program, designed to train new hires in coding, design, and product management so they can operate as full-stack builders.

## Key Information

- LinkedIn is ending its APM program and launching the APB program starting January following the podcast.
- New hires will be trained in coding, design, and product management at LinkedIn through a rigorous training process.
- After training, APBs join pods (small, cross-functional teams) as full-stack builders.
- The program is designed to grow to become a material part of LinkedIn's workforce over time.
- The training materials developed for APBs will also be used to upskill the broader organization.
- The program targets people with strong technical skills who haven't been engineers at a company yet, or people with great design taste who haven't designed at scale.
- This program is a key cultural change lever — it signals to the organization what the future of building looks like.
