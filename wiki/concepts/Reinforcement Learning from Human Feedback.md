## Definition
Reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) is the technique of aligning LLMs using human preferences; Vlad Feinberg names the broader reinforcement-learning literature it belongs to as a rich domain for frontier-lab candidates.
## Key Information
- Since RLHF, deep-RL algorithms such as PPO have moved from "in question" to having an accepted place in production systems.
- Vlad recommends starting from RL basics and working up to value-based and policy-gradient methods as a rich literature tree to crawl.
- It is one of several in-demand domains for frontier-AI research, alongside kernels, programming-language abstractions, scaling-law literature, and distributed-systems × optimization.
