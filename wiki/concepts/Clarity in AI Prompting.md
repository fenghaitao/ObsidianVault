## Definition

Clarity in AI Prompting is the emerging core skill of being specific, precise, and context-rich when communicating with AI tools. It's the difference between getting exactly what you want and getting something that looks right but is fundamentally wrong.

## Key Information

- "Feels like an emerging core skill is learning clarity in the ask of the AI"
- AI doesn't understand "you know what I mean" — it lacks human context and shared experience
- The Genie/Aladdin metaphor: "I want to be taller" → genie makes you 13 feet tall. Vague requests = wrong results.
- Lazar's approach: "I'm optimizing 100% of my time today on good judgment, clarity, quality, taste"
- Practical techniques:
  - Use voice brain-dumping to get initial ideas out
  - Provide references (screenshots, code snippets) instead of just describing
  - Build multiple versions to compare and gain clarity
  - Create PRDs to document requirements precisely
- "Coding is not the problem we're solving for here. The problem we're solving for is clarity."
- AI tools are "super eager to write code" — they'll start coding immediately instead of seeking clarity
- Clarity compounds: spending a day planning saves weeks of fixing
