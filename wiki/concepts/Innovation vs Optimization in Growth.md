## Definition

Innovation vs Optimization in Growth describes the fundamental shift in how growth teams operate at AI-native companies. Instead of spending 80-95% of time optimizing existing funnels and user journeys (traditional growth), AI growth teams spend 95% on innovation — launching new features, standing up new growth loops, and reinventing solutions — and only 5% on optimization.

## Key Information

- Elena Verna: "I usually spend maybe 5% innovating on growth in my previous roles. Right now I'm spending 95% innovating on growth and only 5% on optimization."
- Traditional growth work (optimizing drop-offs, activation funnels, conversion rates) is largely not worth the time in fast-moving AI categories
- Optimization is "not going to win against the competition" — the market moves too fast and competitors emerge weekly
- "Everybody and their mother is starting a vibe coding business nowadays. To be ahead of them is not optimization of the problem. It's reinvention of the solution."
- Growth teams at AI companies end up working on core product features (Shopify integration, voice mode), not just surface-level growth surfaces
- Most growth frameworks are built for optimization, not innovation — it's "really hard to come up with frameworks for innovation because by definition they're innovative"
- The shift is driven by: (1) rapidly changing market, (2) perishable demand that must be captured quickly, (3) intense competition requiring constant reinvention
- This is uncomfortable for experienced growth leaders who built careers on optimization frameworks
