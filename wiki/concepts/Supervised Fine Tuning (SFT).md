## Definition

Supervised Fine Tuning (SFT) is a post-training technique for AI models where the model learns by mimicking expert demonstrations. Edwin Chen analogizes it to "mimicking a master and copying what they do."

## Key Information

- SFT was the original method by which models started getting post-trained
- It is one of several post-training techniques, alongside RLHF, rubrics/verifiers, and RL environments
- SFT is not obsolete — each new technique complements the previous ones rather than replacing them
- The human analogy: learning by watching and copying an expert
