## Definition

The Women in AI Gap refers to the significant disparity between men and women in AI adoption and participation. Despite AI lowering the barrier to building software, women are adopting AI tools at much lower rates, threatening to reverse diversity gains made in tech over the last decade.

## Key Information

- Multiple reports show a massive gap between women and men adopting AI technology
- Lovable users are ~20% women at most, despite the pink/purple/heart branding that might appeal to women
- Those adopting AI now are getting the highest pay and most opportunities (e.g., $1M aqua-hires)
- Elena Verna: "I can't think of one $1 million aqua-hire that has been in the news that is a woman"
- Most AI company CEOs are men; AI company composition is mostly men
- Elena worries the gap will affect hirable talent and set back workplace diversity
- Software needs to be built for everyone, which requires a representative sample of builders
- The gap persists even though a computer science degree is no longer required to build
- The "She Builds" initiative at Lovable is one effort to bridge this gap
- Elena: "I think we're early on enough that we can bridge the gap"
