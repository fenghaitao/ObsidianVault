## Definition

Conscious Business is a book by Fred Kaufman that serves as a framework for thinking about human interaction in business — described by Matt McGinness as "effectively a user manual for human beings." It was recommended to him by Brian Shrier at Sequoia.

## Key Information

- Author: Fred Kaufman
- Described as "a user manual for human beings"
- Particularly recommended for younger leaders (20s and 30s) getting the hang of being a CEO or product leader
- Used at LinkedIn most notably as a leadership framework
- McGinness has a Conscious Business reading club at Rippling; every member of his product leadership team goes through it
- McGinness gives out copies "like candy" and keeps multiple copies on hand
- Recommended by Brian Shrier of Sequoia when he was on the board of Inkling
- McGinness regrets not reading it sooner: "Changes your life"
