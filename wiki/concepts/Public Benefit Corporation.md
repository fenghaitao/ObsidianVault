## Definition

A corporate legal structure that lets a company's charter formally weigh mission/public benefit alongside shareholder returns; [[Anthropic]] adopted this status from its founding, at [[Eric Ries]]'s advice, as one of its [[Mission Protective Provisions]].

## Key Information

- [[Dario Amodei]] and [[Daniela Amodei]] wrote Public Benefit Corporation status — and the right to later establish a mission-oversight trust — into Anthropic's charter from day one, before later standing up the [[Long-Term Benefit Trust]] at the company's Series C.
- Law firm [[Virgil]] (co-founded/advised by Ries) helps founders whose own lawyers are reluctant to implement this kind of filing.
- Cited by Ries as one of the governance modes used by several major AI labs (alongside Anthropic) at odds with standard [[Shareholder Primacy]].
