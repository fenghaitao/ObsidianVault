## Definition

Hero Quest is Michael Bolin's term for the ego-driven belief that you can single-handedly swoop in and solve a Gordian-knot engineering problem ("if only someone would come in and solve this"). It is a trap he fell into and had to relearn more than once.

## Key Information

- After his E8 promotion, Bolin tried to help fix Facebook web speed — a large, stale, data-heavy problem outside his mobile/dev-tools strength — and admitted none of his wacky experiments (compiling V8 from source, tweaking JavaScript generation) panned out.
- The lesson: "I can do a lot of things but there's a smaller subset of things that I really genuinely enjoy doing and I'm going to be a lot more successful if I stay in the things I genuinely enjoy."
- The antidote is being honest about the subset of work that fits your strengths, and expanding it slowly rather than assuming you must be the best at everything.
- This mirrors Bolin's broader three-step career plan: find what you love, find what your employer values, and lean into the intersection.
