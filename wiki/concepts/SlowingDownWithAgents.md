## Definition
"Slowing down with agents" is Mario Zechner's call for discipline and intentionality when using AI coding agents. Rather than maximizing agent output, developers should think about what they're building and why, learn to say no, focus on fewer features that matter, and use agents to polish rather than generate critical code.

## Key Information
- Mario Zechner: "Slow the f*** down. Think about what you're building and why, and don't just build because your agent can do it now. That's stupid."
- "Learn to say no. This is your most valuable capability at the moment."
- "Fewer features, but the ones that matter, and then use your agents to polish the [heck] out of that."
- "Enlighten your users, not your token maxing desires."
- Cap the amount of generated code you need to review
- Non-critical code: "five slop ahead" (relaxed review)
- Critical code: "read every line"
- "If you do anything important, write it by hand. You can use a clanker to help you with that, but don't let it make the decisions for you."
- "Friction is the thing that builds the understanding of the system in your head, which is important. And it's also where you learn new things."
- "All of this requires discipline and agency. And all of this still requires humans."
