## Definition

AI Interview Prep is the practice of using AI tools to prepare for job interviews — including mock interviews with AI coaches, AI-generated quiz games for practice, AI analysis of question banks, and AI feedback on recorded interviews.

## Key Information

- Zevi Arnovitz used a comprehensive AI-powered approach to prepare for his Meta PM interview:
  - Created a Claude project as his interview coach, fed with the best online resources and frameworks (including Ben Arez's work)
  - Mock interviewed with the AI coach, asking it to "make me as ready as possible, not make me feel good"
  - Built a quiz game in Base44 to practice segmentation questions (played on the bus to work)
  - Used Comet (Perplexity browser) to analyze Lewis Lynn's question bank for most-asked questions
  - Asked Claude to "play the candidate" and give perfect answers for questions he didn't have time to mock
- Zevi emphasizes that human mocks (cold outreach on LinkedIn) are still essential — AI alone isn't enough for top-tier interviews
- Lenny and Nome Seagal researched how people use AI to record interviews and get feedback on performance
- The feedback loop in interviewing is traditionally missing — no one tells you what you did wrong, but AI can
- Ben Arez wrote a guest post for Lenny's Newsletter with prompts for AI-powered interview preparation
- Zevi's approach: "Every time I'm faced with a new challenge or problem, I think AI first how to solve it"
