## Definition

Compressing the talent stack is the idea (attributed to Scott Belsky) that AI tools blur the boundaries between traditional roles (PM, designer, engineer) because individuals can now do much more across functions, reducing communication overhead and making teams more efficient.

## Key Information

- Attributed to Scott Belsky; the concept is that "the boundaries between these roles are a little bit less needed than before because people can just do much more"
- Every time someone can do more, you can skip one communication boundary and make the team more efficient
- At OpenAI, this manifests as designers doing substantial PM work, vibe coding prototypes, and landing PRs directly
- Product marketers at OpenAI are making string changes and updating docs directly from Slack using Codex
- Alexander Imbiricos notes that as a PM, he can answer questions, understand changes, and prototype faster than writing specs
- The trend doesn't eliminate roles but shifts what "details" matter — like how Swift developers don't need to know assembly, but a few specialists still do
- The most important remaining skill is deep understanding of customer problems, not the ability to build
