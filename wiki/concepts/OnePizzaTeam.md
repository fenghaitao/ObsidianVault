## Definition
A one-pizza team is what a two-pizza team becomes when AI tools reduce the number of engineers needed. The term was used by a VP of engineering at John Deere to describe how their teams are shrinking, partially due to AI tools. swyx jokes that he is a one-pizza team "because I eat a lot of pizza."

## Key Information
- Originated from Amazon's "two-pizza team" concept (teams small enough to be fed by two pizzas)
- John Deere (200-year-old company): VP of engineering reported two-pizza teams becoming one-pizza teams
- Partially attributed to AI tools reducing the number of engineers needed
- swyx's joke: "I am a one-pizza team because I eat a lot of pizza"
- Reflects broader trend of AI enabling smaller teams to accomplish more
