## Definition
An open model ecosystem is the community-driven network of tools, fine-tunes, quantizations, and integrations built around open-weight AI models, exemplified by the Gemma ecosystem where Google DeepMind collaborates with open-source projects (Unsloth, MLX, llama.cpp, Hugging Face, vLLM, C Lang) to ensure compatibility at launch.

## Key Information
- Google DeepMind's philosophy: "be where the community is" — support existing tools rather than forcing framework adoption
- Collaborations ensure that when new models launch, community tools work out of the box
- Users should not need to switch frameworks (e.g., to Keras) to fine-tune — HuggingFace Transformers, Unsloth, and other tools should all work
- Ecosystem includes: fine-tuning tools (Unsloth), inference engines (llama.cpp, MLX, vLLM), hosting platforms (Hugging Face, Kaggle, Ollama), and programming languages (C Lang)
- Community contributions: quantizations, fine-tunes, device ports (Nintendo Switch running llama.cpp with Gemma)
- Success measured by community adoption: 500M+ downloads, 100K+ community models, 10M Gemma 4 downloads in first week
- Contrasts with walled-garden approaches where models are tied to specific platforms or APIs
