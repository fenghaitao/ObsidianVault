## Definition

Amazon Leadership Principles are a set of 11-12 core values that guide decision-making, hiring, and performance evaluation at Amazon. They serve as the cultural framework that every employee is expected to operate within, from interns to VPs.

## Key Information

- Under Jeff Bezos, Customer Obsession was the highest priority across all levels
- Frugality is one of the Leadership Principles, sometimes taken to extremes (difficulty getting adequate hardware, no free food, vulturing monitors from departing interns)
- Steve Huynh's best managers were "principles-based" — they reasoned from the principles as axioms and made decisions from that foundation
- The Leadership Principles are used in interviewing (behavioral questions map to principles), performance reviews, and promotion assessments
- Steve described having a degree of "Stockholm syndrome" after 18 years, still saying "we" when referring to Amazon and believing in the principles
