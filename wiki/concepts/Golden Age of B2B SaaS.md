## Definition

The "Golden Age of B2B SaaS" is Sherwin Wu's prediction that the collapsing cost of building software will lead to an explosion of small, specialized B2B SaaS companies. As AI makes it dramatically easier to build software, there will be hundreds of small startups building bespoke software for specific niches, enabling a rich ecosystem of interoperating small businesses.

## Key Information

- A second/third-order effect of the one-person billion dollar startup trend
- The logic: if one person can build a billion-dollar company, many more people can build $10M or $100M companies
- These smaller startups will build bespoke, tailored software for specific verticals and use cases
- Example: a podcast host might buy support software from a one-person startup that built a specialized tool just for podcasters
- The opportunity space is so large that even building something that "some people really love" can result in a massively valuable business
- Sherwin: "We might actually enter into a golden age of B2B SaaS and just like software and startups in general"
- The implication for individuals: a $10M business is life-changing for a founder, even if it's not venture-scale
- This trend may reshape the VC ecosystem — fewer venture-scale returns but more viable small businesses
