# Move Fast, Move Safe

A core value of [[Applied Intuition]] that holds two seemingly conflicting ideas in tension: be open to input and debate, then make decisions quickly and execute decisively.

## Core Idea

"We assess our managers on adherence to those values. Literally, we compensate and promote against those values. The behavior we're actually looking at under speed is decisiveness."

## Key Points
- The specific wording is "Move fast, move safe" — not just "move fast"
- This is Applied Intuition's first company value, derived from observing what made them successful
- Two conflicting ideas held in tension:
  1. Be open, listen to naysayers, seek the best idea regardless of source
  2. Make decisions quickly, execute decisively once the decision is made
- "You have to hold those in tension. This is why you as the co-founder or founder get paid the big bucks."
- At some point, no more information will come — you have to decide
- "The middle way is typically the right way. It's hard to find the middle way because these are conflicting ideas."
- Managers are literally assessed, compensated, and promoted on adherence to these values
- The values are not abstract — they are operationalized in performance reviews
