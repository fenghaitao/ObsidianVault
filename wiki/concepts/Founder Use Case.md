## Definition

The Founder Use Case is one of Lovable's two primary user segments: non-technical people who use Lovable to start their own companies by building apps completely from scratch. They range from those already monetizing to those still building toward product-market fit.

## Key Information

- One of Lovable's two main use cases (alongside the Employee Use Case)
- Non-technical founders building apps completely from scratch using AI
- Some are already monetizing their apps; others use them for lead generation for services or physical goods
- Some are still building — Lovable monetizes on the act of building, so the progression to PMF generates ongoing revenue
- Much more efficient and effective compared to hiring an engineer in terms of price
- Covers B2C, B2B, e-commerce, and other categories
- The other use case is the Employee Use Case: employees within companies building internal tools, prototypes, and landing pages
