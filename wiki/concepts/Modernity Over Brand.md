## Definition

[[Nikhyl Singhal]]'s observation that career-signal value has shifted from company pedigree/brand name toward demonstrated fluency with current AI tools and working methods.

## Key Information

- For roughly the prior decade, "have you seen the movie before" (i.e., worked at a recognizable, at-scale company) was the dominant hiring signal for product leaders.
- Because how product is built has changed so fundamentally, multi-year experience at even a top-tier brand can now read as *dated* rather than impressive, if that company's working methods lag current practice — spending years at a company not currently AI-forward can be a liability, not an asset.
- Interview signals have shifted accordingly: candidates are increasingly evaluated on which tools they use and how they think through a live scenario, rather than narrative accounts of what they shipped years ago.
