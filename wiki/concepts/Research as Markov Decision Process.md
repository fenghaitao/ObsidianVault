## Definition
"Research as an MDP" is Jacob Steinhardt's framing — adopted by Vlad Feinberg — of a research project as a stochastic dependency graph (Markov decision process) between milestones, some nodes hidden and transitions unreliable.
## Key Information
- In the software-engineering case, the DAG to the goal is essentially deterministic; in research, some nodes (ideas) may not work, and unknown nodes may exist (a hidden MDP).
- Expanding a node is a stochastic endeavor: whether an approach works opens up a different set of possibilities.
- Optimal traversal therefore requires estimating each approach's success rate and time investment in advance, unlike writing out a software design.
- The skill of building that intuition is the essence of research taste.
