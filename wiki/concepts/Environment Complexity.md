## Definition
Environment Complexity is one of the three "next wave" benchmark dimensions identified by [[SnorkelAI]]. It refers to how complex, realistic, and dynamic the operating environment is in a benchmark — and whether it is representative of real-world settings that professionals actually use.

## Key Information
- Real codebases have org-specific policies, Slack context, screenshots, flaky toolchains, distributed CI, human reviewers with preferences, and many parallel contributors
- Benchmarks today capture only a fraction of this complexity
- The gap between benchmark environments and real-world environments is often where agents fail
- Applies beyond coding to any domain where agents operate in complex organizational contexts
- One of three axes Snorkel is most excited about for the next generation of benchmarks
