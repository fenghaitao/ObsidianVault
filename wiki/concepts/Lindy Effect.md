## Definition

The idea that something's expected remaining lifespan is proportional to how long it has already persisted — cited by [[Lenny Rachitsky]] as part of his reasoning for committing further to his newsletter after 9 months of consistent weekly writing (if he'd sustained it for 9 months, he could likely sustain it at least 9 more).
