## Definition

A humor strategy: make fun of yourself, not other people. Making fun of yourself is always safe and signals confidence. Making fun of others requires an "incredible level of familiarity" and is a high-risk maneuver if people aren't on the same wavelength.

## Key Information

- Sam Lessin: "Make fun of yourself, not other people."
- "You can make fun of yourself as much as you want."
- Making fun of others: "The second it feels disparaging or people aren't on the same wavelength, it's a very high-risk maneuver."
- Self-deprecating humor demonstrates confidence — it shows you're comfortable enough to laugh at yourself
- Sam applies this to his own work: acknowledging his hypocrisy (e.g., "I'm frequently not early") and laughing at himself being "the etiquette guy"
