## Definition
Voice AI is the domain of artificial intelligence focused on speech-based interaction, encompassing speech-to-text (STT), text-to-speech (TTS), and speech-to-speech models. The field aims to achieve natural, human-like voice conversations that match or exceed the interaction quality depicted in the 2013 film "Her."

## Key Information
- Encompasses three main architectures: cascaded systems (STT → LLM → TTS) and speech-to-speech models (single model)
- Key challenges: latency, full-duplex conversation, tool calling, paralinguistic understanding, cost, and privacy
- The "Her moment" is the industry's shorthand for achieving truly natural voice interaction
- Currently dominated by cascaded systems due to their practicality and reliability
- Voice is expensive — TTS costs dominate over LLM costs in consumer applications
- Most hyperscaler voice modes run at a loss as marketing
- On-device processing (TTS on smartphone CPU) is emerging as a solution for cost and privacy
- Full duplex (true bidirectional conversation) remains unsolved outside of Moshi
- Neil Zeghidour argues voice is not a commodity — the last mile is the hardest to solve
