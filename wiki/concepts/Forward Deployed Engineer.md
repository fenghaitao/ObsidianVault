## Definition

A Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) is a technical role responsible for ensuring that an AI agent works correctly at go-live for a customer. The term originated at Palantir (for nine-figure enterprise deals) but has been adapted for AI GTM — where the FDE's primary job is making the customer successful with the AI agent, not supporting a pod of sales reps.

## Key Information

- Originated at Palantir for nine-figure deals: embedding teams on-site for ~6 months to get software working
- In the AI GTM context, the FDE's #1 job is to make the customer a success — not to support a pod of 8 sales reps like a traditional SE
- Jason Lemkin's example: an AI leader's FDE closed a $3M deal entirely on their own — sales was only involved in procurement
- The FDE is a combination of customer success and sales engineer, but inverted: their priority is customer success, not sales support
- The ideal FDE profile: a "mediocre engineer who loves the product" — someone who coded before but doesn't want to code much anymore
- The vendor's willingness to provide FDE support is more important than raw feature comparisons when choosing an AI GTM tool
- "Forward deploy engineer" is described as "a scary term" for "someone that's going to help you do this"
- AI agents will fail without proper training and onboarding — the FDE makes the difference between a 5% success rate (2024) and a 100% go-live success rate
- Marc Benioff has deployed ~2,000 people at Salesforce doing FDE-style work for Agentforce
- For startups: the FDE model is an opportunity to compete with bigger players by being hands-on with deployment
