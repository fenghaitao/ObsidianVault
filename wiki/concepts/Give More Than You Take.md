## Definition

A core principle of relationship-building etiquette: approach interactions with the goal of giving more than you take, rather than extracting value. This signals an abundance mindset and builds trust.

## Key Information

- Sam Lessin: "Understand how to give more than you take. Understand how to build a relationship, not collect business cards."
- This is the opposite of transactional networking — not treating people as "just a number" or "just a potential check"
- Manifested in conversations: don't deliver a four-minute startup spiel; instead engage in back-and-forth exchange
- In dining: within reason, always offer to pay (even if you expect to be turned down)
- In humor: make fun of yourself, not others
