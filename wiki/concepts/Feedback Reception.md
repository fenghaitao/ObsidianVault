## Definition

Feedback Reception is the ability to hear, process, and act on constructive criticism about one's performance or readiness for promotion. It is considered by managers as a key indicator of readiness for Staff Engineer and higher levels.

## Key Information

- How you handle feedback is "as much a part of whether you're ready for promotion as anything else"
- A Staff Engineer needs to be able to hear feedback from leaders, hear feedback from their team, implement it maturely, and be self-reflective
- If you take feedback and get mad, argue, or get combative, you're "further proving the point that you're not necessarily ready"
- Lee's example: first promotion attempt to L6 at Amazon was rejected; Lee was very tactical about it — asked what was missing, identified the biggest objections, and created artifacts to address each gap
- Lee was promoted a month later without doing anything substantially different except "ask how to fix it and come up with the ideas of what we going to fill the gap"
- The willingness to hear feedback and take action on it was what got the promotion, not doing one more project
- Karly emphasized that asking early for promotion has no downside as long as you can take feedback and handle hearing "no"
- However, if you genuinely disagree with the feedback and believe it's wrong, interviewing externally to test the market is also a valid option
