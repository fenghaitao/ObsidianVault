## Definition

Non-Technical Judging is the recognition that most hackathon judges are not technical, and that pitches should focus on the demo and user experience rather than technical complexity or tech stack details.

## Key Information

- A big misconception is that the most theoretically correct or technically complex ideas will win — most judges are actually non-technical
- Jia's pitching technique: look into the judge's eyes to see if they are actually following, keep the pitch concise, state the idea and what it does, then focus on the demo
- The key is having judges interact with the demo so they can see for themselves that the product works
- A common mistake: explaining the entire tech stack (MongoDB, Next.js, FastAPI) thinking judges will be impressed, when they just want to know if the project is "hype" and whether they would use it
- However, aiming high for technical complexity combined with flawless execution can still win — the complexity should be invisible to the judge because the implementation is seamless
- The winning formula: a unique idea with a fully connected, working frontend and backend

- Asian Dad Energy (consulting): non-technical C-suite executives can't judge whether engineering work is actually good, creating information asymmetry — only engineering integrity and a firm's reputation prevent consultants from shipping junk and declaring victory
