## Definition

Exponential Thinking in AI is the mindset of evaluating progress and making decisions on a log-linear scale rather than a linear scale. At Anthropic, this is a core cultural principle: "linear charts are just not cool. Everything is log-linear." The implication is that product value in 2 years will be 100-1000x what it is today, which fundamentally changes how growth teams should allocate their efforts.

## Key Information

- At Anthropic, "everyone is talking about the exponential" — it's a core fixation
- "Linear charts are just not cool. Everything is log-linear. Show me it log-linear scale."
- As model capabilities grow exponentially, they keep unlocking new markets where the value "significantly dwarfs" previous markets
- Example: agentic coding didn't exist 1.5 years ago, now its value is bigger than the previous AI coding market
- Product value in 2 years: 100-1000x what it is today (vs. 30-50% for a non-AI company like a grocery delivery app)
- For growth teams: this means shifting from 60-70% small/medium bets to 70/30 or 50/50 large bets vs. small bets
- Reason: if future product value is 1000x, capturing small optimizations misses the forest for the trees
- "If you're looking at it from a linear lens, you will see the world very differently" — this applies to understanding AI risks too
- If you think linearly, you underestimate how soon things will happen
- Only applies to AI-first products; if AI is a side feature, traditional growth allocation may still be appropriate
