## Definition

Empire building is prioritizing raw headcount over impact because headcount is the easiest, most defensible proxy for a leader's seniority and readiness for promotion.

## Key Information

- Exists because it is rewarded: "counting people is the easiest thing to do" — "no one can debate, well, Ethan has 42 people and Ryan has 17."
- Some companies write down promotion thresholds (a whisper number at Amazon that later got written as ~90 people for director), contradicting the leadership principle "there's no bonus for additional head count."
- Ethan calls the 90-person threshold "quite literally" a several-hundred-thousand-dollar-a-year bonus for accumulating people.
- Shell games: leaders lend reports across an org for ~6 months to check a promotion box and then may not take them back; companies rarely down-level people who later drop below scope.
- Replacing headcount with an impact-based standard is possible but harder and harder to defend ("why I have 42 people and you have 17? I must be a bigger manager").
