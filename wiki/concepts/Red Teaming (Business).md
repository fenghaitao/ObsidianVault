## Definition

Red teaming is originally a military tactic that has been applied to business: taking an outsider's perspective to stress-test your own ideas. It counters the tendency to become "obsessed with your own thinking" and helps identify failure modes before committing resources.

## Key Information

- Originally a military tactic, now applied to business strategy
- The practice: take an outsider's perspective to your idea and stress-test it
- Counters the tendency to get "wrapped around the axle" with your own thinking
- Particularly important with prototyping: "You build a prototype and you're like, 'Oh, looks so beautiful...' but it's actually not a very good idea"
- Should be done proactively: ask "What are the outcomes you're most afraid of? What would a failure state be for you in this experiment?"
- AI can be used as a red teaming tool: "Poke holes in my ideas. Tell me what you would do differently"
