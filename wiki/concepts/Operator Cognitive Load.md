## Definition

Operator cognitive load is the mental burden placed on contact center operators who must simultaneously handle complex customer emotions, navigate multiple CRM systems, and document everything perfectly during and after calls. It is the primary driver of operator stress and turnover in the contact center industry.

## Key Information

- **Causes**:
  - Managing complex customer emotions during calls
  - Navigating multiple customer data platforms and CRM systems
  - Documenting and summarizing calls accurately and completely
  - [[AfterCall Work (ACW)]] — the administrative burden of typing notes, summarizing, and selecting disposition codes
- **Impact**: High stress is the #1 reason operators leave the profession, creating a negative spiral: understaffing → higher stress for remaining operators → turnover → more understaffing
- **AI Mitigation**: Automating ACW removes the repetitive administrative burden of typing notes, reducing cognitive load and directly combating the stress linked to staff turnover
- **Engineering Philosophy**: The solution is not to hire more people but to "fundamentally engineer the stress out of the workflow"
