## Definition

Marc Andreessen's metaphor for AI: the "philosopher's stone" is a technology that transforms the most common thing in the world (sand, i.e., silicon) into the most rare thing in the world (thought, i.e., intelligence). The analogy draws on the alchemical pursuit of turning base metals into gold, positioning AI as a similarly transformative breakthrough.

## Key Information

- Coined by Marc Andreessen on Lenny's Podcast
- Sand (silicon, the raw material for computer chips) is essentially free and abundant
- Thought (intelligence, reasoning, creativity) has historically been the scarcest and most valuable resource
- AI bridges this gap by converting raw compute into intelligence at scale
- The metaphor frames AI not as a threat but as a miraculous technological advance that democratizes intelligence
