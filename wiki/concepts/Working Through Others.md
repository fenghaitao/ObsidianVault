## Definition

Working Through Others is the ability to achieve impact by empowering and directing other engineers rather than doing all the work yourself. It is the core mechanism for scaling yourself at higher IC levels.

## Key Information

- Evan's manager emphasized: "you don't have to do everything yourself, in fact at the higher levels you can't do everything yourself"
- The key to going from senior to staff is "scaling yourself" through others
- Ryan Peterman experienced this through an intern: "this intern basically showed me the power of leverage or working through others — I was always a workhorse, and then I realized I can entrust him with some of this and I can go take on more stuff"
- Working through others allows you to "take on more scope than you can physically deliver"
- It's described as the "name of the game to be able to take on more scope"
- Evan explicitly enjoyed "working through others and leverage" as an IC6, finding it more satisfying than taking direction
- The ability to work through others requires trust, delegation skills, and the ability to support and empower people
