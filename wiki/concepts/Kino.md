## Definition
Kino is internet slang for high-quality, tasteful, creative, and authentic content — the opposite of slop. The term was popularized in the AI engineering context by Paul Rambles and adopted by swyx.

## Key Information
- The opposite of "slop" in internet slang
- Idea originated from Paul Rambles
- Examples: creative Sora videos like "cats playing digs" (vs. boring talking-head Sora videos), Netflix's "K-pop Demon Hunters" (vs. "Electric State"), fresh and early trend adoption
- Something that is kino can degenerate into slop if overused or if a creator is late to a trend
- Represents the quality standard that AI engineers should aspire to
- Can be produced by the same tools/studios that produce slop — the difference is in taste and execution
