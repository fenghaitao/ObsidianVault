## Definition

[[Eric Ries]]'s ombnibus term in *[[Incorruptible (book)|Incorruptible]]* for governance structures where a for-profit company is owned or overseen by a separate entity (a nonprofit foundation, a perpetual purpose trust, or a benefit trust) whose sole job is protecting the mission — rather than the for-profit entity governing itself.

## Key Information

- Two "simplest" templates, per Ries: nonprofit-foundation ownership (industrial foundation), as used by [[Novo Nordisk]] and [[Zeiss]]; and the perpetual purpose trust, as used by [[Patagonia]].
- [[Anthropic]]'s [[Long-Term Benefit Trust]] is presented as a modern variant of the same underlying pattern — an outside body empowered to act as [[Mission Guardian]] against [[Financial Gravity]].
