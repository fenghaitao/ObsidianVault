## Definition

Portfolio Approach is a promotion strategy of having multiple projects contributing to your case rather than betting everything on a single large project. Rahul Pandey used this approach at Meta, combining a cross-functional infrastructure migration project with a bottoms-up developer tool to build a clear IC6 promotion case.

## Key Information

### Rahul's Two-Project Portfolio
- Project 1 (cross-functional, top-down): Migrating Portal's video calling infrastructure back to Messenger's shared RTC infrastructure — cross-functional, managing people from multiple teams, PMs, and leadership
- Project 2 (bottom-up, engineering-driven): A debug tool for Portal engineers — purely engineering-driven, built for himself and other engineers, saving hundreds of engineering hours per month
- The combination of both led to a clear promo case

### Why the Portfolio Approach Works
- Some people succeed with one big project, but Rahul found the portfolio approach worked better for him
- The cross-functional project demonstrated leadership and influence across teams
- The bottom-up tool demonstrated technical initiative and direct impact
- Together they covered both the "influence through others" and "direct technical contribution" dimensions of staff-level work

### Related Concepts
- This is similar to the Portfolio Of Bets concept discussed in other transcripts
- The diversification reduces risk — if one project doesn't land as expected, the other can still carry the case
- Having a portfolio means you're not dependent on a single project's success or framing
