## Definition

The Customer Love Sprint was a two-week initiative at Slack, implemented by Jessica Fain after taking over the core product team. The entire engineering team stopped what they were doing and spent two weeks "painting the inside of the cabinets" — polishing product craft and fixing rough edges. Engineers picked what they worked on; the only rule was to ship something good for users.

## Key Information

- Implemented by Jessica Fain after taking over Slack's core product team (channels, emoji, search, messaging)
- Context: there was a feeling from the executive team that Slack had "lost its mojo around product craft"
- Engineers picked what they worked on (supported by PM, design, and customer support)
- Only rule: ship something that was good for users
- Result: 65 improvements shipped in one sprint
- Had a big, fun judging competition that executives took part in
- Spoke directly to what executives believed was Slack's differentiation — exceptional user experience
- Brought back a cultural element that had been lost
- "You could call this a bug bash" but they made it exciting and fun instead
