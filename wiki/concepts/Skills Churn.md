## Definition

Skills churn refers to the rapid rate at which the skills required for a given job are changing, driven by AI and technological acceleration. LinkedIn data shows that by 2030, 70% of skills required for current jobs will have changed.

## Key Information

- LinkedIn data shows that by 2030 (4 years from the podcast date), the skills required to do a given job will have changed by 70%.
- The implication: whether or not someone is looking to change jobs, their job is changing — the only question is whether they keep it.
- The fastest-growing jobs in the market are growing by over 70% from the previous year's fastest-growing jobs.
- 70% of today's fastest-growing jobs were not on the list of jobs a year ago; many didn't exist a decade or two ago.
- Some jobs (e.g., nurses) will see less impact, while others will see 90-95% impact.
- Engineering is seeing the most AI investment in terms of agents, leading to dramatic changes in what it means to be an engineer.
- This data is the foundational justification for LinkedIn's full-stack builder model: the time constant of change now exceeds the time constant of response.
