## Definition

Deeply Feeling Kids (DFK) are children who are labeled as dramatic, oppositional, or defiant, but whose core fear is being "too much." They don't fear loss of control — they fear being too much — and they act it out, unfortunately getting it confirmed by the world. They are Dr. Becky's "passion project within the passion project."

## Key Information

- "The most misunderstood kids in the world"
- Labeled dramatic, oppositional — professionals often tell parents the issue is they don't punish enough or aren't consistent
- Their core fear is being "too much" — they can act it out and get it confirmed by the world, creating a vicious cycle
- "They tend to have big explosive feelings and try to push the people away they care about the most in the exact moments they actually need them, thereby confirming their fears: 'See, I really am too much.'"
- "Leave me alone" is what a deeply feeling kid screams when out of control — and it's actually their deepest fear, not their wish
- Dr. Becky's second children's book "Leave Me Alone" (Feb 2026) is focused on these kids
- She describes one of her own three children as a deeply feeling kid: "0% people pleasing, this third child of mine"
- The book serves as the "connection moment" for a kid to see themselves represented
