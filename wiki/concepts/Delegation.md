## Definition

Delegation is the practice of entrusting work to others rather than doing everything yourself. At higher IC levels, delegation becomes essential — you cannot do everything yourself and must scale your impact through others.

## Key Information

- Evan King's manager had to repeatedly tell him to delegate: "you need to be able to delegate — I could just do it and I could do it quickly, so why do I need anyone else around to help?"
- This was the "first point in Evan's career where things stopped coming naturally"
- The key feedback Evan received when going from senior to staff was "scaling yourself" — the buzzword was delegating and working through others
- Ryan Peterman experienced delegation through an intern: "this intern basically showed me the power of leverage or working through others — I realized I can entrust him with some of this and I can go take on more stuff"
- With the intern, Ryan felt like "two people at once" and was "shipping twice as much impact"
- At higher levels, "you can't do everything yourself, and you need to figure out how to empower people, you need to figure out how to delegate, and you need to figure out how to increase your scope through others"
