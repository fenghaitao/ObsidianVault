## Definition

"End it or mend it" is a decision framework used by a co-founder client of Rachel Lockett when their relationship reached a breaking point. It frames the choice as binary: either commit to fixing the relationship (mend it) or consciously part ways (end it). Either outcome is considered success because it provides clarity.

## Key Information

- A co-founder came to Rachel Lockett saying "End it or send it" (later paraphrased as "end it or mend it")
- The framework forces a decision point rather than muddling in frustration and ambiguity
- "Mend it" path: Name the current state, use 360 feedback, be open and vulnerable, recognize what each person is missing, create new rhythms of connection, make changes to the leadership team to fill gaps
- "End it" path: Sometimes coming together and really grappling with the dynamic reveals that one co-founder is unhappy and unlikely to change. A loving exit is still success because it provides clarity.
- The key is not remaining in the dark, frustrated, and unconscious about the interpersonal dynamics
- You're making a choice based on your strengths, what the business needs, and the relationship dynamic
