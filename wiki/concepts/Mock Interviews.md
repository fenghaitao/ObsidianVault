## Definition

Mock Interviews are practice interviews conducted with experienced interviewers to prepare candidates for real technical interviews. They are a core service offering of Hello Interview and are considered highly valuable for interview preparation.

## Key Information

- Hello Interview provides in-person mock interviews with current senior engineers and managers from the candidate's target company
- The company initially tried to build AI mock interviews but the quality wasn't sufficient — customers repeatedly asked to pay for in-person mocks
- Coaches are carefully vetted: "if anybody drops below our bar of expectations from a coach, we part ways and move on to another coach"
- After every free mock interview (done to gather training data), customers would say "please let me pay you to do more of that"
- Ryan Peterman's roommate independently used Hello Interview's mock interviews and credited them as critical to his interview success
- The interview process is described as "this contrived thing that you need to study specifically" — even senior engineers can be overconfident about system design interviews
- Mock interviews are valuable because they provide realistic practice with feedback from someone who understands the specific company's interview process
