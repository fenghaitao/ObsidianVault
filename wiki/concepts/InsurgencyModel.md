## Definition
The insurgency model is an organizational approach where a small elite team operates at the center of a large bureaucracy with unusual levels of political backing, autonomy, market-rate pay, and custom recruitment — effectively operating outside normal constraints to prove what's possible before scaling. Eoin Mulgrew describes it as "let us take the shackles off" and use the team as a proof point for wider reform.

## Key Information
- Small elite team at the center of government operating with different rules
- Key enablers: political backing/mandate, autonomy, market-rate pay, custom recruitment
- Custom recruitment targets exclusively outsiders with a ~0.7% acceptance rate technical selection process
- Designed as a hack to get around systemic constraints — a pilot to prove what's possible
- Goal: make the model become business-as-usual (BAU) across government
- Focus on recruiting "missionaries, not mercenaries" — people motivated by mission impact
- Has produced spin-off teams: [[IncubatorForAI]] in DST, [[JustAI]] in MOJ
- Can tackle both low-hanging fruit (quick internal builds) and complex system-wide problems (partnership model)
