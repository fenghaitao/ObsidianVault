## Definition

AI as Coach is the practice of using Claude to provide management coaching and feedback by modeling a manager's perspective based on their public writing, internal communications, and past discussions. Amol Avaisary uses this weekly to get feedback from the perspective of his manager, Amie Vora.

## Key Information

- Amol asks Claude: "Based on what you know of Amie — publicly, she's written extensively about product, and internally, and our discussions — what feedback do you have for me as Amie?"
- He gets this every week
- "You can think about it as soft coaching — I think that is unlocked in my opinion already"
- Quality is "hit or miss right now" — decent on some things, occasionally catches something crucial
- "It's like working with a coach who's like kind of drunk at times — sometimes says something you're like why would you bring that up, but other times you're like wow"
- Can also be done for direct reports: Claude looks at their work, OKRs, discussion transcripts, and suggests key takeaways and feedback
- Multiple ways to set up: public profile (if they've written extensively), project/skill with their context, or Slack MCP to look at what they've said recently
- "Based on how they're spending their time — what are the priorities my manager has that I don't know?"
- As models improve, the accuracy and signal will continue to improve rapidly
