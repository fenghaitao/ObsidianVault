## Definition
Cloud Agents vs Custom Agents describes Kitze's framework of two diverging futures for AI agents: cloud agents (Cohere, OpenAI, Perplexity) that are polished but limited, serving the masses; and custom agents (OpenClaw, Hermes) that are powerful but require tinkering, serving enthusiasts. Neither wins outright.

## Key Information
- **Custom agents** (OpenClaw, Hermes): for tinkerers, but "never going to work" for mainstream
- Tinker Club members who build pinball machines are "freaking tired of trying to make this thing work"
- "No one will have time to tweak this. They would just like a served solution for them so everything works out of the box."
- **Cloud agents** (Cohere, OpenAI, Perplexity): "too nerfed" — Cohere "cannot do like even 5% of the things that OpenClaw can do"
- Cloud agents "will be for the masses but it won't satisfy the tinkerers, the people who want to self-host, own the models"
- Kitze's conclusion: neither wins — "I don't think that either of them is going to win in the long run"
- Kitze himself is "not going to be happy until I, you know" — will keep tinkering
- Third path: Apple may win with local Siri agents for normies
