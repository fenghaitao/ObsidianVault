## Definition
Ad-sponsored inference is an experimental economic model where a coding agent displays non-intrusive ads for developer tools in its terminal and editor interfaces, using the ad revenue to subsidize the inference costs of its free-tier agent.

## Key Information
- Implemented by Amp Code as a "mini ad network" delivering ads for other developer tools
- Ads are described as "very subtle" and "non-intrusive"
- Sponsors the Rush Agent's inference costs, making it free for users
- Motivation: cost is identified as one of the biggest barriers to coding agent adoption, especially among students and hobbyists
- Beyang Liu notes that adoption among college students is lower than expected given young people's propensity for new technology, attributing this to cost
- The idea was initially dismissed as unworkable but became viable as inference costs declined
