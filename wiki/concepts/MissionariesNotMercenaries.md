## Definition
"Missionaries, not mercenaries" is a recruiting philosophy articulated by Eoin Mulgrew for Number 10 Data Science. It means that while competitive pay matters to make government roles economically viable, the primary motivation must be mission impact — because "a paycheck is not going to get you out of bed in the morning when stuff gets hard."

## Key Information
- Pay matters but is not sufficient alone — mission provides intrinsic motivation
- People will take pay cuts if the work is economically viable and the challenges are compelling
- The decisions crossing a minister's desk are "some of the most important things you could possibly work on"
- Applied in practice: recruiting from big tech, labs, Y Combinator, research institutes
- Targets people who "probably did not think they would be working in the civil service this time last year"
- Contrasts with traditional government recruiting optimized for process, not exceptional technical talent
- Successfully attracted: Harvard dropouts, YC founders, serial entrepreneurs, top researchers
