## Definition

Jeetu Patel's strategic directive for navigating rapid AI progress: don't prepare for the world of today — anticipate what AI will be capable of in 6 months and prepare for that world instead. Today's limitations will not be the same limitations in 6 months.

## Key Information

- Articulated by Jeetu Patel
- "AI is moving so fast right now. One of the things I tell my team is fast forward 6 months from now and anticipate what that's going to do and get prepared for that world."
- "Don't get prepared for the world of today thinking that you're not going to be able to get there because in 6 months your assumption sets are going to be different."
- "Please don't actually then bias yourself with the assumption sets you have right now to not move forward."
- Part of the broader point about timing: "You have to also be good enough to know that when something is going to be ready in 6 months, you can't think about what it's doing today"
