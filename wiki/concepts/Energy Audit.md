## Definition

The energy audit is a two-week exercise for identifying your natural gifts and energy drains. Every night, reflect on the day and write down the five things that gave you the most energy and the five things that depleted your energy the most. After two weeks, patterns emerge that reveal what you should be doing more of and less of.

## Key Information

- **Duration**: Two weeks, every night
- **Method**: Write down 5 things that gave you the most energy and 5 things that depleted your energy the most each day
- **Outcome**: Pattern recognition — what are the natural gifts you're living in vs. the things that are slowly draining you (a "slow leak in your gas tank")
- Alternative methods to discover your gifts:
  - Ask 5-10 people in your life: "When I walk in the room, what shows up? What are my strengths? What are my gifts?"
  - Review your calendar over the last month: What activities are you excited to do? What do you dread? Why?
- The goal is to design your life so you spend ~80% of your time in your gifts
- Lenny used this approach when leaving his job — he paid attention to what gave him energy and followed that pole, discovering writing was energizing while advising was depleting
