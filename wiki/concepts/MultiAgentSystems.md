## Definition

Multi-agent systems coordinate multiple AI agents to accomplish complex tasks. Key patterns: async delegation with timeouts, parent agents controlling sub-agent lifecycles, middle management layers, and agents querying their own observability. Abhishyant Khare presented principles for resilient multi-agent systems at PyAI Conf 2026.
