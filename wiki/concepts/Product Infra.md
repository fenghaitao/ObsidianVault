## Definition

Product infra (product infrastructure, or "client infra") is a leveraged engineering area that sits between product and infrastructure — building the architecture, safeguards, and tooling that let product teams iterate quickly and safely.

## Key Information

- Marius spent most of his Instagram web time in product infra rather than shipping product features
- Focus areas: site architecture, type checking, tests (manual and automated), and safeguards so engineers can iterate quickly with confidence
- It is leveraged because improvements (e.g., shared error-handling notes, lint rules) scale to the whole company even outside your org
- Senior engineers should seek leveraged areas because delivering strong IC5/IC6/IC7 product impact alone becomes hard as scope expectations rise
