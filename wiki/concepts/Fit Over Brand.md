## Definition

A dress philosophy: the fit of clothing matters far more than the brand or price. A well-fitting $20 shirt is better than a misfitting $500 shirt. Most people can intuitively tell if something fits but have no idea what it costs.

## Key Information

- Sam Lessin: "A well-fitting $20 shirt is way better than a misfitting $500 shirt."
- Most people have no idea what things cost — they can just tell if it fits
- Wearing a misfitting expensive item signals the wrong things: lack of cultural awareness, not matching the room, not showing sensitivity
- "Are you trying to impress me because you have a fancy outfit? Like what are we talking about?"
- For startup founders: don't show up with a Rolex — it's "very classless" and signals trying too hard
