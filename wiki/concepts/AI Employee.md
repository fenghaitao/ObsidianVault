## Definition
An AI Employee (or AI Coworker) is an AI agent designed to function as a member of a company's workforce, living where humans work (e.g., Slack), having access to company tools and integrations, and participating in discussions, threads, and channels like a human teammate. Unlike personal AI agents or tools, an AI employee is treated as "a hire, not a tool" — with appropriate access scoping, shared context, and organizational awareness.

## Key Information
- Fryderyk Wiatrowski of Viktor defines an AI employee as "just like a human employee" — lives where you live, has access to your tools, participates in discussions
- An AI employee has no web app; it lives in the communication platform (Slack) just like teammates
- It inherits the context of all company tools — a horizontal, broad understanding that no single human employee has
- Fryderyk frames it as "universal PhD level understanding to all areas of the company"
- Unlike personal agents where each user connects their own integrations, an AI employee's integrations are shared: one person connects, the whole team benefits
- Key distinction from tools: "Viktor is not a tool, it's a hire" — users should not give it access to personal accounts as shared integrations, just as you wouldn't give a new hire access to your personal email
- AI employees can be proactive — joining conversations, suggesting workflows, verifying claims against data — but proactivity must be earned and rolled out gradually to avoid security backlash
- The vision for AI employees traces back to Gottfried Leibniz in the 17th century, who wanted to automate "the labor of calculation"
- Three pillars for a great AI coworker: (1) helps get work done, (2) knows the company, (3) is friendly
