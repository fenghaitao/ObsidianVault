## Definition
Stripe Projects is a Stripe product built on [[Shared Payment Tokens]] that enables SaaS businesses to express their products and handle recurring payments in an agent-friendly way.

## Key Information
- **Built on Shared Payment Tokens**: Underlying credential primitive is shared payment tokens with spend limits and seller scoping
- **Product expression**: SaaS businesses can express their product catalog for agents to discover and purchase
- **Recurring payments**: Supports monthly/subscription billing patterns through token refresh flows (similar to OAuth access/refresh)
- **Agent-friendly**: Part of Stripe's broader push to make products agent-friendly rather than human-only
- **Relationship to ACP**: Complements the [[Agent to Commerce Protocol]] for SaaS-specific commerce patterns
