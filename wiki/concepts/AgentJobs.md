## Definition

Agent jobs is the concept of defining recurring roles (not one-off tasks) for AI agents, modeled after human hiring practices. A job is a recurring need on a predictable cadence that an agent owns and executes without being prompted each time.

## Key Information

- Key distinction: delegate jobs (recurring needs), not tasks (one-off requests).
- A job has a predictable cadence: daily, weekly, monthly.
- Three systems needed: scheduling/dispatching, processes (skills), and output artifacts (markdown files).
- Agent hiring advantage over humans: no minimum workload, no salary to justify, pennies per task.
- Two types of recurring tasks: things you're currently doing (get off your plate) and missed opportunities (things not getting done).
- Brian's BMHQ dashboard: Kanban board with task templates, agent-specific views, execution logs.
- Tasks reference skills by name rather than containing detailed instructions.
