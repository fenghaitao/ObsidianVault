# Our Best Work is Done Alone and Quietly

A core value of [[Applied Intuition]], articulated by co-founder [[Qasar Younis]]. The philosophy of staying under the radar to focus on product and customers rather than public promotion.

## Core Idea

"Every minute you're writing something for public consumption, you're not focusing your very limited time that you have on your customers and your product. And ultimately, that's the only thing that's going to produce and yield results."

## Key Points
- [[Applied Intuition]] stayed intentionally under the radar for ~10 years while building a $15B company
- Qasar: "If it was up to me, we would do that forever"
- Practical reasons (not just contrarianism):
  - Focus on craft over promotion
  - Pivoting is easier before you're publicly committed — once you have employees and investors, changing direction loses credibility
  - "The score takes care of itself" / "The valuation takes care of itself"
- Qasar's personal psychology: growing up as an immigrant outsider made him skeptical of the mainstream
- He was able to do this because he was already known in the ecosystem (YC COO, personal network)
- Counterpoint: if you don't have a network, building a public following can be a powerful tool for recruiting, investors, and customers
- Naval: "Fame itself is a tool and it's powerful"
- Qasar only joined Twitter/X in 2026 at the urging of [[Marc Andreessen]] and [[Elad Gil]]
