## Definition

Layoffs are structural headcount reductions. Asian Dad Energy argues recent big-tech layoffs are driven by companies cutting headcount to keep revenue flat while lowering cost, then marketing the move as AI-driven efficiency to lift stock valuation.

## Key Information

- His conjecture: in the AI investment race, companies cut staff so product lines keep selling while cost drops, then use "AI efficiency" PR to boost the stock — a performative, sector-wide trend rather than one employer's issue.
- He expects continued layoffs because of years of AI overinvestment without clear returns (his feeling, which he admits could be wrong; stock/productivity upside is also possible).
- His own experience: he knew the round was coming, found he was on the list, and swapped himself to the top to spare two junior engineers.
- Emotional toll: a destabilizing "cocktail" of anxiety, panic, excitement, fear, and hope that lasted days, leaving him too unsettled to negotiate despite a generous package.
- Big-tech severance was structured and generous: garden leave, months of severance, PTO payout, and an education/equipment stipend.
- John Myles White: the possibility of being laid off is "the number one emotional thing that causes people stress," beating out perks; before layoffs people freaked out when perks were cut, but once layoffs were on the table they tolerated the cuts.
- MSL (Meta Superintelligence Labs) is more stressful than other frontier labs because of the sense it might have layoffs, though it has had fewer rounds than OpenAI and Anthropic.
