## Definition

Frugality is one of Amazon's Leadership Principles that emphasizes accomplishing more with less. It became a recurring inside joke among Amazon employees, invoked whenever something was crappy, cheap, or resource-constrained.

## Key Information

- Became an inside joke: anytime something was subpar, employees would say "oh, frugality"
- Examples: no free food, inadequate hardware, interns leaving and vultures coming to steal their monitors
- Steve had to "beg, borrow, and plead for hardware" — trying to get a MacBook Pro with enough RAM so the fan wouldn't overheat when building code
- The term "being Frid" (frugal + stupid) describes frugality taken to counterproductive extremes
- Steve noted that free food is a perk — its absence doesn't make a company bad, but frugality at Amazon sometimes went too far
- If you're hiring tens of thousands of software developers, give them computers that work
