## Definition

"How strongly do you feel about this?" is a calibration question to ask executives when they suggest something. It helps distinguish between a passing thought, a random idea, and a mandate — preventing teams from deprioritizing everything to chase every executive comment.

## Key Information

- Execs are moving so quickly they won't always give context of urgency — you have to ask for it
- When an exec says something, ask: "How strongly do you feel about this?" or "How urgent do you think that is?"
- Follow up with: "Here's what the team is working on now. Do you think that trumps these priorities?"
- If they say yes, listen and do the thing they say trumps it
- More often than not, they'll say: "Oh no, no. That's just a random idea. Put it on the backlog"
- Jessica Fain has been working on getting better at signaling this herself as a leader: "This is just an idea. This is a mandate. This is something I want to see"
- "It's hard sometimes to remember to do that because your brain is spinning with ideas"
