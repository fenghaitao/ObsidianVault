## Definition

Exposure Therapy for Code is Zevi Arnovitz's recommended gradual approach for non-technical people to become comfortable with AI coding tools. Instead of jumping straight into advanced tools like Cursor, users should progressively expose themselves to more code-facing environments.

## Key Information

- Zevi, a non-technical PM who was "afraid of looking at code," developed this approach through his own journey
- The progression: (1) Start with GPT Projects — beautiful UI, super simple, chatbot interface, (2) Graduate to Bolt or Lovable — AI app builders with more direct code interaction, (3) Move to Cursor in light mode — still approachable, (4) Go full terminal/dark mode — full developer environment
- Zevi: "If you see this where I'm working... you might be excited to start using those, but I would really recommend starting slow"
- "Code is terrifying. It's the scariest thing in the world to look at. And I look at it as kind of like exposure therapy."
- Starting in a chatbot (GPT Projects) forces you to "take the time to converse and to learn" rather than just generating code
- Zevi recommends this even though the technology has progressed — the gradual approach builds real understanding
- The goal is to "slowly, slowly, gradually ease in until you like open a terminal, go full dark mode, go full dev"
