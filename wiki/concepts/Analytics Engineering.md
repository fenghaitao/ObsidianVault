## Definition

Analytics Engineering is a discipline focused on SQL, experimentation, and product analytics — more analytical and less infrastructure-focused than traditional Data Engineering. Zach notes that what was called "Data Engineering" at Facebook in 2016 would be titled "Analytics Engineering" today.

## Key Information

- Zach describes Analytics Engineering as involving "more SQL, experimentation, product analytics focused" work
- This is distinct from Data Engineering, which is more about "big data pipelines and Spark" and is more technical/infrastructure-focused
- Zach's role at Facebook (titled Data Engineer at the time) was actually Analytics Engineering by today's standards
- Zach wanted to leave this type of work because he was drawn to more technical data work that wasn't "just SQL"
- At Facebook, he was already doing full-stack work (React, PHP, Hack) beyond the typical Analytics Engineering scope, which motivated his desire to switch to Software Engineering
- When Netflix collapsed Data Engineering into Data Science, Zach resisted because he had already done "the analytics org" at Facebook and didn't want to go back
- The evolution of the title reflects the maturation of the data field: what was once broadly called "Data Engineering" has split into more specialized roles
