## Definition

Digital transformation is moving a business from analog, spreadsheet-driven workflows to fully digital customer experiences (mobile apps, web); as sold by consultants it was a smooth vision, but the reality underneath was often messy.

## Key Information

- The 2010s saw every company talk about digital transformation — going from spreadsheets and printouts to mobile apps and digital customer journeys.
- Going "zero to one" made early projects hard to get wrong: even a mediocre implementation looked transformative because it digitized an analog process.
- The sold vision is a smooth diagram (customer insights → decision → orders/revenue), but the actuality is dirty — duct-tape implementations, swivel-chair workarounds, broken APIs, and unmaintained codebases.
- The gap between the ideal sold and the messy reality — and being the one who has to reconcile them — is what burned Asian Dad Energy out of consulting.
