## Definition

[[Amol]]'s term for the ~70% of his time as [[Anthropic]]'s head of growth spent not on proactive optimization, but on firefighting problems caused by things going *too* well — acquisition, activation, and monetization systems all breaking under extreme, rapid scale.

## Key Information

- Compares this to the experience of early hypergrowth at companies like Facebook, Uber, or DoorDash: metrics charts all trend up and to the right, but day-to-day the work is largely urgent, painful firefighting rather than calm strategic optimization.
- Emotionally counterintuitive: teams can feel embattled and stressed even while every dashboard looks great, since the felt experience is one crisis after another rather than steady, satisfying progress.
- The remaining ~30% of time goes to more standard, proactive "bread and butter" growth work — deciding resource allocation across products, long-term pricing/packaging, and readying new products (e.g., Cowork) for growth-team optimization.
