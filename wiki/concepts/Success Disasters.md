## Definition

Success Disasters are situations where things have gone so well that other things are now breaking — a phenomenon common at hypergrowth companies. The term was coined internally at Anthropic to describe the 70% of the growth team's time spent firefighting problems caused by success rather than doing proactive work.

## Key Information

- Internally coined term at Anthropic by the growth team
- 70% of Amol Avaisary's time as Head of Growth is spent on success disasters
- Categories affected: acquisition, activation, monetization — all have firefighting from urgent issues
- "All the charts are green, fully up and to the right, and everyone's just like it can be quite tough emotionally still"
- Common at companies that have gone through rapid growth: Facebook, Uber, DoorDash early on
- "Scaling this much just brings a lot of challenges"
- The remaining 30% is proactive work: which products to put resources behind, long-term pricing/packaging, when to lean into new products
- Amol: "You need to sort of step back and just realize that we're very lucky to have these problems"
