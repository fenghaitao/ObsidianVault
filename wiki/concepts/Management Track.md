## Definition

The Management Track is the career path for engineers who transition into people management, diverging from the IC track at the Senior Engineer level. It involves managing teams rather than building software directly.

## Key Information

- The management track diverges from the IC track at the Senior Engineer level
- In most professions outside engineering, the only way to increase compensation and scope is to move into management
- Engineering is unique in having a viable alternative through the IC track
- The panel was focused on the decision to pursue the IC track rather than management
- Lee was promoted to L6 as a manager at Amazon, providing perspective from both sides
