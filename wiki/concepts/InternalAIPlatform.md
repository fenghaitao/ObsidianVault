## Definition
Internal AI platforms are the custom AI infrastructure systems that large tech companies are building in-house rather than buying off-the-shelf. These include custom background coding agents integrated into monorepos, MCP gateways with service discovery, retooled on-call tooling, and AI-categorized code review systems.

## Key Information
- Observed at Uber, Airbnb, Intercom, Meta, Microsoft, and mid-size companies
- Three reasons companies build their own: (1) low-risk way to get hands-on with AI, (2) custom solutions work better for massive codebases that don't fit in context windows, (3) anything with "AI" in it gets funded — "agent experience" gets headcount, "developer platform" doesn't
- Gergely Orosz predicts: by next year, every large company will have built the same things
- "If you're at a large company and you're not already building an MCP gateway, what are you even doing?"
- From outside, these companies don't seem to be shipping more features — but internally there's massive AI infra investment
- It feels wasteful but is rational: innovation, recruitment, and competitive advantage
