## Definition
Contributor poker is Zig's term (as relayed in the episode) for placing bets on open-source contributors — an investment that breaks down once pull requests are agent-generated.

## Key Information
- Historically, a new contributor could show promise, take feedback, fix their PR, and steadily grow into a maintainer; maintainers were "making investments in people."
- Agent-written PRs break the loop: the author pastes your comments back into the agent, updates the PR, and merges — there is no compounding feedback and nobody learns.
- This relates to the falling cost of submissions: putting up a plausible PR costs nearly nothing, while reviewing it still takes real effort.
- Charlie sympathizes with Zig's strict "no LLM-written or LLM-assisted code" policy, even though Astral's own AI policy is far more permissive.
