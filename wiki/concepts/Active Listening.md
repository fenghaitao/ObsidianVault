## Definition

Active Listening (as Stefan Mai advises new managers) is showing interest, reflecting back what someone said, and asking curious follow-ups so people feel heard and open up — the opposite of treating a one-on-one like a transactional API exchange.

## Key Information

- Engineers used to "API-like" interactions often treat one-on-ones as an efficient laundry list, which blocks the deep information a manager needs
- Show interest, use subverbals ("mm," "I heard you"), reflect back what they said, and ask questions that show curiosity
- The power dynamic of becoming someone's manager corrodes former peer bonds, so the same information won't flow automatically
- The "jewel" of understanding a person's goals and frustrations is what makes you an effective manager for them — you have to earn it
