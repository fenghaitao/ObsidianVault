## Definition
AI Product Manager (AIPM) is an emerging product management role with elevated expectations for technical literacy, AI system understanding, and the ability to specify and evaluate AI agent behavior beyond traditional PRDs.

## Key Information
- The expectations of the PM role are changing as AI becomes central to products — stakeholders, executives, and customers all expect more.
- AI engineers expect PMs to come with higher-resolution requirements than a Google Doc, including understanding of agent system architecture.
- AIPMs should be literate in code and able to use AI coding tools (like Cursor) to prototype and iterate.
- Aman Khan argues PMs have more leverage than ever because they can now build prototypes themselves rather than being reliant on engineering teams.
- The role involves managing eval datasets, human labeling workflows, and ensuring the eval system is in good shape for the development team.
- Suggested mental model: replace roles with "skill stacks" (e.g., "I like talking to customers and coding on the side") rather than rigid PM/engineer boundaries.
