# AGI

## Definition

Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) refers to AI systems with human-level or superior intelligence across a broad range of tasks. Poolside describes the current era as the "awkward teenage years ahead of AGI," where companies are building applications that bridge the gap between raw model intelligence and economically valuable work.

## Key Information

- **Poolside's Mission**: To close the gap between models and human intelligence
- **Current Phase**: "Awkward teenage years" — models are increasingly capable but still require significant bridging to deliver real-world value
- **Evolution Path**: Completions → Chat → Agentic → Increasingly Autonomous
- **Infrastructure Requirements**: Requires massive compute (multi-gigawatt data centers, 40,000+ GPUs) to scale toward AGI
- **Interface Evolution**: Form factors will continue to change as models become more capable and autonomous
