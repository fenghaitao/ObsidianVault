## Definition

Misalignment Detection with AI is the practice of using Claude (via the Slack MCP) to proactively scan organizational communications and identify areas of potential misalignment across projects, teams, and stakeholders. It is one of the most effective ways Amol Avaisary uses Claude.

## Key Information

- Uses Claude with Slack MCP to scan Slack for misalignment
- Amol has this running on a weekly schedule: Claude looks at his projects, what's top of mind, and finds areas of potential misalignment
- "It does a really, really good job"
- Example: Scott (enterprise lead at Anthropic) found major misalignment areas that would have caused teams to "spin their wheels significantly or do overlapping work"
- This is a version of the "strategy bot" concept — AI proactively looking across context and distilling insights
- "This level of proactivity and getting looking across a bunch of context and distilling insights — I think we'll get there later this year"
- The cross-functional coordination layer is where AI is starting to reduce significant toil
- "6 months ago that wasn't possible and 6 months from now what is going to be possible there?"
- How to set up: download Co-work desktop app, connect Slack MCP (may need team/enterprise admin permissions), ask Claude
