## Definition

Model Personality Types is Zevi Arnovitz's framework for understanding different AI models by anthropomorphizing them as distinct human personalities. This mental model helps him know which model to use for which task and how to interpret their behavior.

## Key Information

- Zevi's characterizations of major AI models:
  - **Claude**: The perfect CTO — very communicative, very smart, opinionated but collaborative. Doesn't just go with the flow. "It's your dream, a very communicative but very opinionated dev lead."
  - **GPT/Codex (GPT-5.1 Max)**: The best coder in the company who comes to the office in a hoodie and sandals, sits in a dark room. Solves the hardest bugs but is "really not communicative" — just fixes things without explaining. "You only bother him when you have the worst bugs."
  - **Gemini**: A crazy scientist who's super artsy, super talented at designing. Watching it work is terrifying — it will delete things, make mistakes, then produce beautiful results. "If you sit next to it and watch it work, it's terrifying. Like, you would fire that person instantly."
- Zevi: "The easiest way for me to think about AI is to imagine it as people"
- This mental model helps Zevi play to each model's strengths and mitigate weaknesses using other models
- The personality differences are why multi-model peer review works: each model catches different things
- ChatGPT's sycophantic tendency is the anti-pattern: "If regular ChatGPT was a CTO, that would be the CTO who goes along with your dumbest ideas"
- Zevi uses this framework to decide task allocation: Claude for architecture/planning, GPT for hardest bugs, Gemini for UI/frontend
