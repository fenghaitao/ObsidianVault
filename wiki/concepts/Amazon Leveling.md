## Definition

Amazon's Engineering Leveling is the SDE career ladder from SDE1 (entry level) through SDE2 (mid-level), SDE3 (Senior), and L7 (Principal Engineer). Amazon notably skips the "Staff" level that exists at Meta and Google, making the SDE3 to Principal gap effectively a two-level jump.

## Key Information

- SDE1: Junior/entry level — focus on independence, understanding the software development lifecycle, taking tasks through to completion
- SDE2: Mid-level — focus on ownership, being a steward of the team's codebase, active participant in SDLC. A wide band; upper SDE2s are equivalent to senior engineers at other companies. Debated as a terminal level
- SDE3: Senior — higher scope, team-level influence
- L7: Principal Engineer — cross-team influence, acting as sponsor/backstop/catalyst/evangelist across multiple organizations
- Amazon skips a staff level — Steve believes inserting a staff level between SDE3 and Principal would solve many problems
- SDE1 is "up or out" within ~2 years; SDE2 is debated as a terminal level
- The granularity of Amazon's levels is "terrible" — SDE2 covers a very wide range of capability
- Many engineers who couldn't make the SDE3 to Principal jump at Amazon went to Meta and Google and thrived as Staff/Senior Staff/Principal
