## Definition

Running several [[Claude Code]]/[[Claude Cowork]] agent sessions simultaneously on different tasks — a behavior [[Cat Wu]] says grew steadily through late 2025 as task success rates rose, and part of Anthropic's [[Building Blocks Progression]] roadmap.

## Key Information

- [[Boris Cherny]] personally always has around five agents running at once, across the terminal, desktop app, and iOS app (each accounting for roughly a third of his usage) — the first thing he does most mornings is check on agents he kicked off earlier.
- Anthropic's roadmap anticipates this scaling much further, toward tens or hundreds of agents running in parallel and remotely (since that volume won't fit on a local machine), requiring a management interface to triage which tasks need human attention.
- Reduces the anxiety some users report feeling when a single agent is blocked or waiting on a question, since a multi-clauding workflow means there's always another task to check on rather than idling.
