## Definition

A psychological technique based on Robert Cialdini's principle of reciprocity: create a sense of obligation by giving value before asking for anything. Jacob Warwick uses this by making introductions, offering help, and providing value during the very first phone call — before any contract is signed.

## Key Information

- Based on Robert Cialdini's reciprocity principle from "Influence"
- Jacob: "I can plant seeds with my clients really easily because I'm on the phone with somebody and I'm understanding who they need to connect with."
- Jacob's technique: make 2-3 introductions during the first phone call — "Just hold on a second. Hold on. I'm triggering a reciprocity imbalance."
- Benefits of the technique:
  - Creates a reciprocity imbalance — they feel they owe you
  - Gives 3 different touch points to follow up
  - Provides a 360° view of the person through the introductions
  - Creates social proof through the introducers
- "What I want to do is show that I have the resources to support them, whether they sign a contract or not."
- The principle also applies to the interview process: provide value during the interview (insights, connections, ideas) so the hiring manager feels they've already received value from you
