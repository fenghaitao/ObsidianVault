## Definition

Cold Email is the practice of reaching out to someone you don't know via email. Amol Avaisary perfected this skill as a founder and used it to land his Head of Growth role at Anthropic by cold-emailing Mike Krieger. He is the only PM Mike has hired from a cold email.

## Key Information

- Amol's cold email tactics:
  1. **Subject line**: tested copy with very high open rate (secret formula)
  2. **Channel**: use personal email, not LinkedIn or work email — everyone is getting outreach there, so response rates are lower
  3. **Content**: keep it very short — here's who I am, here's why I'd be a good fit, we should chat
  4. **Follow up**: "If I really care about it, I should just keep reaching out until they tell me please stop"
- Amol's email to Mike Krieger: "Hey, love what you guys do, love the product. I think you guys badly need a growth team. Want to chat?"
- Mike responded and they started talking; Anthropic was just starting to think about hiring a growth team
- Amol learned cold emailing as a founder when he had to get really good at it
- "These things typically don't work. And then you should always follow up a few times."
- Lenny: "It makes sense that a talented growth person would be very good at cold email. That's almost like an interview step."
