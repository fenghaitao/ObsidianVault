## Definition

She Builds is a women-only hackathon initiative created by Elena Verna at Lovable to address the gender gap in AI adoption. It provides women with unlimited access to Lovable for 48 hours in a community setting to build together.

## Key Information

- Created by Elena Verna at Lovable to address the gender gap in AI adoption
- Women get unlimited access to Lovable for 48 hours
- Participants build solutions for hyper-local, hyper-relevant needs: elderly parents, kids, households, church groups, kids' basketball teams
- These are solutions that would never have been built before because software was too expensive and they wouldn't become $100M companies
- Runs in cohorts; a massive edition is planned for International Women's Day
- URL: shebuilds.lovable.app (built on Lovable as a minimum lovable product)
- Elena initially vibe-coded the first version of the site and submission process herself
- Goal: bring more diversity to software creation by empowering women to build for their unique needs
