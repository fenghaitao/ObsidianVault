## Definition

"Be yourself. Everyone else is taken." is a life motto traditionally attributed to Oscar Wilde, though Jason Cohen notes there is no evidence Wilde actually said it — and no evidence of who did. It is Jason Cohen's personal life motto.

## Attribution

- Commonly attributed to Oscar Wilde
- Jason Cohen tried to verify the attribution for his book annotations and found no evidence Wilde said it
- Also no evidence of who actually originated the quote
- "Let's say it's Oscar Wilde because he said lots of things like that"

## Deeper Meaning

- Easy to hear and dismiss, but increasingly true with experience
- When you see others online doing their thing, it's tempting to want to be like them — but you have to be yourself
- The people who love you or like what you do want you to be yourself because that's what they love
- If you change to be like someone else, you lose what made people connect with you in the first place
