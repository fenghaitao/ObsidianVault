## Definition
A Domain Native AI Organization is one that structurally bakes domain expertise into its AI product development process, treating the system for incorporating domain insights as more important than model or pipeline sophistication. The organization is designed around the domain expert's role in assessing and improving AI quality.

## Key Information
- **Core thesis**: Winning in vertical AI is an organizational problem, not a model problem
- **Three models**: Oracle (direct embedding), Evaluator (quality measurement), Architect (self-improving systems)
- **Common failure mode**: Hiring domain experts but treating them as consultants/advisors rather than giving them ownership and decision-making authority
- **Principal Domain Expert**: A single individual ultimately accountable for AI quality, avoiding consensus-by-committee
- **Evolution**: Organizations should start with Oracle at small scale and progress to Evaluator or Architect as needed
- **Hiring philosophy**: Hire for domain expertise as the base requirement plus as many adjacent skills as possible (prompting, data science, engineering, product management)
- **Ownership principle**: Domain experts must be in the room for decisions — not just advisors
- **Adjacent skills**: The right domain expert has breadth beyond pure domain knowledge, enabling them to evolve from Oracle to Evaluator to Architect as the organization scales
