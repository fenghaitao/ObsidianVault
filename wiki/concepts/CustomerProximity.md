## Definition
Customer Proximity is the practice of exposing all engineers directly to customer feedback, pain points, and requests — rather than filtering it through product managers or support teams. At Linear, this creates a "fire hose" of customer insight that engineers cannot escape, ensuring they build with deep understanding of real user needs.

## Key Information
- Linear has Slack channels with all big customers, open to any engineer to browse and participate in
- Every customer meeting is recorded, and interesting points are tagged so anyone can search and review them
- Engineers are exposed to a "fire hose" of customer feedback — they "cannot really escape seeing and feeling the customer pain or joy"
- This is critical for building great products, especially as Linear moved from building for themselves (engineers) to building for larger corporations and enterprises
- Linear's PMs constantly talk with customers to figure out what to build next
- Customer proximity is what enables the product engineer role: engineers need direct customer exposure to develop product judgment
- Tuomas Artman recommends this as the primary way for engineers to grow product sense: "get closer to your customers"
