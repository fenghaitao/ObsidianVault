## Definition

Research finding (cited by [[Marc Andreessen]]) that one-on-one tutoring is the single most reliable method known for improving educational outcomes, routinely raising a student's performance by two standard deviations — from the 50th percentile to roughly the 99th.

## Key Information

- Historically available only to the wealthy/aristocratic (Andreessen's examples: Alexander the Great tutored by Aristotle; European royal families) since one-on-one tutoring has never been economically feasible to provide at scale.
- Andreessen argues AI removes this cost barrier for the first time: a child can ask an LLM unlimited questions, get instant feedback, ask it to "dumb it down," and be quizzed to check understanding — for free or near-free, at any hour.
- Basis for Andreessen's own approach to homeschooling his 10-year-old: a hybrid of traditional schooling plus AI tutoring, citing the private school system Alpha as an example already built around this philosophy, alongside Khan Academy's nonprofit push in the same direction.
