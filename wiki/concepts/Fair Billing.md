## Definition

Fair billing is a Slack policy where the company stopped charging customers for seats they weren't actively using, even though the signed contracts entitled them to charge for those seats. It was considered innovative at the time.

## Key Information

- Slack stopped charging for inactive seats despite contractual rights to do so
- This was considered very innovative at the time
- Part of a broader pattern of customer-friendly policies at Slack, including:
  - Free credits for businesses struggling during COVID
  - SLA with 100x money back for downtime, applied automatically and proactively
  - Customer value mantra: "the measure of our success will be the amount of value that we create for customers"
