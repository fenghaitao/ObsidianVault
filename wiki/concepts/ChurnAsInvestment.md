## Definition
Churn as investment is Shopify's strategy of deliberately accepting higher costs, friction, and instability from early AI tool adoption in exchange for being months ahead of competitors. It treats the pain of bleeding-edge adoption as a strategic investment rather than a cost to minimize.

## Key Information
- Pioneered by Shopify under Farhan Thawar (head of engineering)
- Shopify was the first company to get GitHub Copilot access in 2021 — a full year before public release
- Strategy: give unlimited AI tool budget, spend significant time ironing out bugs
- Trade-off: higher churn and expense now for being 6 months ahead of competition
- Farhan Thawar: "it would look silly if I said you cannot have these tools — how would I hire the best?"
- Three benefits: innovation, recruitment, competitive advantage
- Makes sense for tech companies where being ahead matters; less relevant for non-tech companies
- Gergely Orosz: "it looks silly, but it's rational"
