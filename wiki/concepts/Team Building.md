## Definition

Team Building is the process of selecting and assembling teammates for collaborative projects. Jia Chen advocates for selecting teammates based on skills and demonstrated passion rather than credentials.

## Key Information

- Jia's approach: find teammates based on skills, not credentials (PhD, research, internships, titles)
- She looks at people's GitHub projects to see how active their commits are and what experience they have with the relevant use case
- This method selects for teammates who are more passionate, put in more effort, are scrappier, and have a growth mindset rather than a limiting mindset
- For grand prizes, a team is an advantage because you can divide tasks and specialize
- Beyond skills, motivation for the full 24 hours is critical — when someone gives up, only three other people can keep the energy up
- Best results come when every single member is "absolutely full sending the hackathon"
- One person's intense effort can be motivating to the rest of the team
