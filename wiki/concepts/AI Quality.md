## Definition
AI Quality refers to the standard of outputs produced by AI systems, assessed against domain-specific criteria. Appraising AI quality requires judgment, and the best judgment involves domain expertise. In Chris Lovejoy's framework, how an organization defines, measures, and improves AI quality determines its ability to build differentiated AI products.

## Key Information
- **Judgment-based**: AI quality assessment fundamentally requires judgment, not just metrics — though metrics help
- **Domain expertise requirement**: The best judgment comes from domain expertise baked into the organization and product
- **Subjective vs objective**: Some domains have clearly measurable quality (prior authorization: correct/incorrect), others are subjective (meeting notes: no objectively perfect note)
- **The quality gap**: Front-end models are good enough; the gap is operationalizing expert judgment around them
- **Three approaches to quality**: Oracle (direct human judgment), Evaluator (defined metrics and measurement systems), Architect (automated quality improvement)
- **Quality as differentiator**: Building a system that can accurately measure and improve AI quality is key to building a differentiated product
- **Gartner statistic**: ~50% of generative AI projects abandoned — poor AI quality assessment is a contributing factor
