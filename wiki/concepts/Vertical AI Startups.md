## Definition

Vertical AI startups are companies that apply AI to solve a specific, deep problem for a particular customer segment, rather than building general-purpose AI tools. Alexander Imbiricos is bullish on this approach given the commoditization of general AI capabilities.

## Key Information

- Alexander Imbiricos is "bullish on vertical AI startups"
- The reasoning: as AI makes building product easier, the competitive advantage shifts from "being good at building" to "having a really meaningful understanding of the problems that a certain customer has"
- If you could only choose one core competency for a new company, it should be deep customer understanding, not technical building ability
- "If you're starting a new company today and you have a really good understanding and network of customers that are currently underserved by AI tools, you're set"
- Conversely, "if you're good at building websites but you don't have any specific customer to build for, you're in for a much harder time"
- The pattern: a general AI tool can solve many problems, but a vertical solution that understands a specific problem (like presentations) and plugs into specific workflows will win
- This is a shift from the era when "it was so hard to build product that you mostly just had to be really good at building product"
- "Ideas are still not worth as much as some people think" — execution and distribution still matter enormously
