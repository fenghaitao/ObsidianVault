## Definition

Conway's law states that a system's structure mirrors the communication structure of the organization that built it.

## Key Information

- "Products look like the org chart": boundaries between teams become boundaries in the product.
- Casey Muratori calls it the one software-engineering "law" he would bet on, and notes it extends to AI agents, not just humans.
