## Definition

Conway's Law (Melvin Conway, 1967): any organization that designs a system will produce a design whose structure mirrors the organization's own communication structure. Referenced by [[Matt MacInnis]] to describe a product org he inherited at [[Rippling]] as "locally optimized but globally incoherent" — individual teams/systems each made sense on their own terms but didn't cohere as a whole, reflecting fragmented communication paths rather than a deliberate global architecture.
