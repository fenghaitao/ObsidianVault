## Definition
Interactive Editing is the capability to edit images in real time with sub-second latency, enabling users to guide and modify image generation as they think rather than waiting for generation to complete. Black Forest Labs' Flux 2 Klein achieves this at 500ms for editing and 300ms for generation.

## Key Information
- Flux 2 Klein achieves 500ms for image editing, 300ms for generation
- At least on par with other open-source models in quality while being ~30x faster (Kwen ~15-20s)
- Enables: real-time mock-up rendering, interactive visual engines for gaming/films, guided generation
- Represents a step toward BFL's vision of visual intelligence
- Combines speed with quality: not just fast, but state-of-the-art or better in benchmarks
- Multi-reference interactive editing still under 1 second
