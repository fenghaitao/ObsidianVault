## Definition
Exocortex is the concept that AI will evolve beyond being a tool or chat interface into becoming an extension of human cognition — an external brain. The name EXO Labs is derived from this concept.

## Key Information
- Posits that AI is not just a tool but an extension of the self, analogous to a part of one's brain
- Raises the question: "Do I actually want to rent my brain?" — arguing against cloud-dependent AI
- Andrej Karpathy's quote "not your weights, not your brain" encapsulates the privacy and autonomy argument
- Becomes increasingly relevant as AI moves beyond chat interfaces into agentic systems (OpenClaw, Claude Code) that have deeper access to personal data and workflows
- Implies local-first AI where the user owns and controls both the model weights and the inference hardware
- Drives EXO Labs' mission to make frontier AI runnable on local consumer hardware
