## Definition
"Agent Manager" is a self-given title by Priscila Andre de Oliveira describing a software engineer who no longer writes code directly but instead orchestrates AI agents through prompting. Since December 2025, she has not coded at all — she only prompts agents to produce code, while focusing her own effort on comprehension and steering. She describes it as "orchestrating a bunch of agents" and notes that "the industry is changing."

## Key Information
- Coined by [[Priscila Andre de Oliveira]] as a self-given "promotion" (with no salary raise)
- Since December 2025, she has not written code — only prompts
- Her "reports" (the AI agents) "don't complain"
- She uses three monitors to manage multiple agents simultaneously
- The role requires strong comprehension skills to steer agents correctly and verify their output
- Key principle: "You need to understand the research your agent did" before proceeding to planning and implementation
- This role reflects the industry shift toward AI-assisted development where engineers spend more time on comprehension, planning, and review
- Contrasts with pure vibe coding: agent managers maintain understanding and quality standards
