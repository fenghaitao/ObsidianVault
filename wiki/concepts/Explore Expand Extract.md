## Definition

Explore, Expand, Extract (the "three X's") is Kent Beck's framework for the lifecycle of companies and teams — from risk-tolerant search for breakout opportunities, to scaling what works, to disciplined extraction of incremental value.

## Key Information

- Explore: asymmetric upside — you pay a constant cost to search for a 100x outcome, so move fast and don't over-constrain
- Expand: scale what you've found to work
- Extract: the ceiling is ~1-2% improvements with catastrophic downside, so risk-averse discipline dominates
- Amazon culturally skews toward extract (lean, process-heavy, "mechanisms"); early Facebook sat closer to explore
- Leaders should ask which phase their org is in and match their leadership style to it
