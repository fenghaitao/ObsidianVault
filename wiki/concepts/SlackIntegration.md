## Definition

Slack integration for Claude Code extends codebase intelligence beyond engineering terminals into the broader organization. Anyone in product, support, sales, or marketing can ask Claude technical questions, request feature prototypes, or query business data — all directly in Slack.

## Key Information

- Three use patterns from Anthropic's internal usage:
  1. Feature ideas start as working PRs against production codebase (not mockups).
  2. Anyone can ask technical questions about the codebase directly in Slack.
  3. Claude combines codebase knowledge with analytics/event data for business questions.
- Benefits: support resolves issues faster, marketing ships on deadline, sales follows up while conversations are warm, engineers stay focused.
- The codebase becomes a shared organizational intelligence resource.
- Claude Skills can extend this further: connect to event stores or analytics dashboards.
