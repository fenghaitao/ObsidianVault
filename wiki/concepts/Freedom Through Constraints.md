## Definition

[[Amol]]'s life and business principle: constraints, rather than purely limiting you, can create freedom by eliminating excess choice — once a path is clearly the only viable one, decision-making becomes simpler and more focused.

## Key Information

- Business application: Amol attributes much of [[Anthropic]]'s success to this dynamic — lacking Meta/Google's distribution, OpenAI's first-mover advantage, or comparable funding forced the company to pick a very narrow focus (coding, B2B) rather than spreading resources thin, maximizing its odds of reaching escape velocity with a generalizable technology.
- Personal application: Amol credits the same principle with helping him recover from a severe traumatic brain injury — being physically unable to do most things forced him to focus entirely on the few things that mattered (basic function, careful pacing) rather than being overwhelmed by unlimited possible responses.
- Distilled as a choice: when a constraint removes your ability to get what you want, you can either resist/fight reality (and suffer) or accept the situation and decouple your happiness from the outcome — described by Amol as the harder but more effective response.
