## Definition

Personal Brand is the public image and reputation an individual builds through content, projects, and visibility. Jia Chen advocates for students to build a personal brand as a form of leverage and luck surface area.

## Key Information

- Jia believes personal brand is good for students regardless of their environment — it gives leverage instead of sharing work with only five people
- A personal brand amplifies subsequent actions: instead of reaching five recruiters, a project can reach 500, increasing the chances of good opportunities
- Jia's content strategy: short-form, high-ROI reels (3-5 seconds) that get high views with minimal effort; casual reels filmed wherever she was during the day
- Her first hackathon video (after 16th win) blew up because people love interesting videos and seeing students win big cash prizes
- She got over the fear of posting by consulting close friends first, building a support network of people who would support her no matter what
- Jia's video about receiving angel checks for Sprint.dev got 500K views on Instagram, leading to more angel investors reaching out
- Ryan's core advice aligned: get projects out of localhost, post them on the cloud, and put yourself out there
