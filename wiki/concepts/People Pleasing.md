## Definition

People Pleasing in a career context is the tendency to seek external validation, follow expected paths, and make decisions based on others' expectations rather than personal desire. Steve Huynh identified this as the single biggest lesson he wishes he could have taught his younger self.

## Key Information

- Steve's advice to his younger self: "The person you really need to please is yourself"
- Are you doing what you want to do, or are you doing it because that's the expectation of somebody else?
- Are you climbing the ladder because you want to get to the top, or because the ladder was put in front of you?
- The culture Steve was raised in emphasized external validation: do well in school, get a job, get married — with a constant feeling of being "ahead or behind"
- After achieving something, the tendency is to look to someone else for validation rather than internal satisfaction
- Steve acknowledged that his younger self probably wouldn't have listened to this advice
