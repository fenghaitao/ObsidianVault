## Definition

"Entropy of standards" is Brian Attwell's observation that organizational standards naturally decay over time: it takes a lot of energy to raise a standard and very little to lower it, so without active intervention the bar falls until the company dies or a trauma resets it.

## Key Information

- "It takes a lot of energy to raise a standard. And it takes very little energy to lower it. Few people will complain if you suddenly ask everyone to do less work"
- With entropy in a system, standards fall over time "until the company dies, or until some kind of trauma kicks it back into place"
- Example: Google's DeepMind team works hard now because of an existential threat to Alphabet
- Connected to his Google story: large companies' performance is decoupled from individual teams, making it easy for managers to do convenient-but-company-harming things (e.g., keeping a likable non-worker)
