## Definition

Focus on Strengths is advice (from a consulting framework, relayed by Sash's manager) that instead of fixing all your weaknesses, you should position yourself so your strengths are showcased and your weaknesses simply don't get in the way.

## Key Information

- The natural instinct is to fix shortcomings; the better move is to make sure they don't block you while you double down on strengths
- Position yourself where you can lead with your strengths — that's where you'll be most successful long-term
- Echoes an early-Facebook VP's lesson: real impact comes from taking something great and making it excellent, not lifting something poor to passable

### David Fowler's Amplify-Strengths Advice
- His mentor told him not to fix all weaknesses (he'd be average at best) but to make his strengths "superhuman," since no one gets promoted for being average
- He now consciously builds teams so someone covers his weak spots (scheduling, checklists) while he focuses on what he's best at
- He initially reacted to 360 feedback as "I need to fix everything"; his mentor reframed it toward doubling down on strengths
