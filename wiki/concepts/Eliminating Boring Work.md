## Definition

Eliminating Boring Work is one of Malte Ubl's four agent archetypes. It involves asking employees what they hate most about their job and building agents to handle those boring, repetitive tasks, thereby improving both efficiency and job satisfaction.

## Key Information

- Part of Malte Ubl's Agent Archetypes framework
- Method: ask people "what do you hate most about your job?" and build agents for those tasks
- Case study: Vercel's in-house support agent with 90% deflection rate
- Result at Vercel: support team job satisfaction "exploded" because they no longer handle boring cases (credit card rejections, etc.) and instead focus on interesting, complex cases where they can really help people
- Described as "a very noble mission that we should all kind of strive to do"
- Contrasts with the fear narrative around AI replacing jobs — instead, AI removes the boring parts and lets humans focus on meaningful work
