## Definition

A 2x2 framework credited to VC [[Evan Tana]] (partner at South Park Commons): both founders and ideas can be "legible" (clearly articulable, easy to understand) or "illegible" (frontier, not yet well-articulated) — the most interesting investment opportunities are illegible ideas, since a legible idea is usually already obvious enough that someone else is already building it.

## Key Information

- If both founder and idea are legible, the idea is probably not novel — it's easy to understand precisely because it isn't new.
- The interesting quadrant is an illegible idea: one that's genuinely on the frontier, where people (including the people building it) may not yet be able to explain why it matters or how to describe it clearly.
- [[Jenny Wen]] adapts this VC framework to her own role as a design leader at [[Anthropic]]: spotting internal prototypes/ideas that have real energy and excitement behind them internally but aren't yet legible, then using storytelling, UX, and form-factor design to make them legible and shippable.
- Concrete example: an internal "Claude Studio" prototype that Wen initially found confusing ("illegible" to her), but which she recognized had real energy behind it among the people building and using it — it ultimately seeded both Anthropic's "skills" framework and key interface ideas that became part of [[Claude Cowork]].
- Wen's practical heuristic: pay attention to what people internally are getting excited about even if you don't immediately understand why — the energy itself is the signal worth investigating.
