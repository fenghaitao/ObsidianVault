## Definition

Underfunding as a Principle is deliberately under-resourcing projects to force people to use AI more. When teams are under-resourced, intrinsic motivation to ship quickly drives them to leverage AI tools, leading to better AI utilization and faster shipping.

## Key Information

- Boris: "When you underfund everything a little bit, people are kind of forced to Claudify"
- The mechanism: put one engineer on a project instead of a team; their intrinsic motivation to ship quickly drives them to use Claude to automate work
- "If you have a good idea, you just really want to get it out there. No one has to force you to do that"
- Connected to the principle of speed: "if you can do something today, you should just do it today"
- Early on, this was critical because Claude Code's only advantage was speed
- Boris: "If you hire great engineers, they'll figure out how to do it. And especially if you empower them to do it"
- Not just about cost savings — it's a strategy for maximizing AI utilization
