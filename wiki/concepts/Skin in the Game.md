## Definition

Skin in the game (from Nassim Taleb) is the idea that decision-makers should personally share the consequences of their decisions; Ryan Olson applies it to argue senior engineers and managers should keep hands-on in code.

## Key Information

- Ryan argues a senior IC who has to code won't hand off an architecture that becomes "someone else's problem" — they stay involved and see the issues firsthand.
- Similarly, a manager embedded in the day-to-day work operates better than one abstracted from it.
- This runs against Facebook's school of thought that strictly separates individual contributors and management; Ryan prefers the Tesla-style flexibility where ICs have reports and managers stay technical.
