## Definition
The Evaluation Gap is the growing asymmetry between AI agent capabilities (which are advancing rapidly) and our ability to measure those capabilities in practice (which is falling behind). Closing this gap is described by [[VincentChen]] of [[SnorkelAI]] as one of the most important research challenges in the field.

## Key Information
- Agent capabilities are marked by real progress: hill climbing on model cards, improving "vibes" especially in coding
- But enterprises hesitate to deploy agents in high-stakes environments (finance, insurance, healthcare)
- The hesitation is not about capabilities — it's about the inability to measure them reliably
- Closing the gap requires a toolkit: field deployments, red teaming, private human evals, crowdsource labeling, and open benchmarks
- Open benchmarks are a critical piece because they define progress, not just measure it
