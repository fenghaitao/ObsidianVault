## Definition

Jeetu Patel's life motto: staying power, persistence, curiosity, and hunger matter more than raw intelligence. You can become smart through learning, but you can't teach hunger.

## Key Information

- Articulated by Jeetu Patel as his answer to "what's something you wish you'd known before taking on this role"
- "It's very important to have smart people, but you can become smart if you have curiosity and hunger and staying power and persistence."
- "You can't teach hunger" — it's an innate trait
- The trait of "learning to learn" and constantly being hungry with stamina and persistence is "far more important than the absolute measure of intellect"
- Intellect is "very very trainable and learnable over time and improvable over time"
- Jeetu also observes this in AI: "When you watch AI work, it's just like partly the reason it's so good is it just keeps trying. 'Okay, this didn't work. Let me keep going.'"
