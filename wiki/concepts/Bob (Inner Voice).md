## Definition

"Bob" is Molly Graham's personification of the emotional, unhelpful inner voice that emerges during periods of change. Bob's job is to make you the worst version of yourself. The key discipline is to let Bob do his thing but not act on the emotions Bob generates.

## Key Principles

- Bob is the voice that says "you're pissed off," "you're tired," "you're scared"
- Bob's job is to make you the worst version of yourself — all these emotions are normal but they are not useful
- They are "not the compass that should be telling you what to do"
- The two-week rule: give it two weeks before acting on any emotional impulse. These emotions are like waves that roll through, and typically go away in a couple of days
- "You want to send the rage email at 9 p.m., you still want to send it at 8 a.m." — these emotions don't go away in 24 hours, but they do fade
- If something lasts longer than two weeks, it's actually something you should pay attention to and talk to someone about (manager, friend, coach)
- "Everything else is just Bob"
