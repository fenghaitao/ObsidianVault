## Definition
Agent Marketing Automation uses a crew of specialized AI agents to handle content creation, social media analysis, and marketing outreach. Rather than a single agent writing posts, a crew of agents with distinct roles collaborates to research, draft, review, and refine marketing content.

## Key Information
- Demonstrated by Joao Moura at CrewAI with a marketing crew of four specialized agents:
  - **Content Creator Specialist**: Generates initial drafts from rough ideas
  - **Social Media Analyst**: Checks facts, analyzes LinkedIn, and researches what others are discussing on the topic
  - **Senior Content Writer**: Refines and polishes the content into a publishable draft
  - **Chief Content Officer**: Final review and approval, drawing on company history and experience
- The crew searches the internet to learn about topics, looks at the author's previous content/style, and produces polished LinkedIn posts
- Result: 10x more views in 60 days for CrewAI's LinkedIn presence
- Human provides only "rough ideas that kind of suck" as input; the crew transforms them into high-quality output
- Represents a shift from human-as-creator to human-as-editor/director
