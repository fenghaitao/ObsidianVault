## Definition

Temporal Modeling is a machine learning approach that incorporates time-based patterns and sequences into model predictions. It was one of the sophisticated techniques used by the Realtime Integrity team at Meta for live video detection.

## Key Information

- The Realtime Integrity team used "sophisticated sequencing, temporal modeling" as part of their ML approach
- Temporal modeling was used alongside core embeddings from Meta's AI org and applied ML on top
- Despite the sophistication of temporal modeling, the breakthrough came from a much simpler approach: incorporating comment signals
- The comment insight was temporal in nature — comments indicating an atrocity appeared before the models could detect it visually
- The temporal modeling approach was part of the team's focus on "pinching pixels and audio waves" before the comment signal breakthrough
