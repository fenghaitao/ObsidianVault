## Definition
Eval as Requirements is a framework where AI product managers replace traditional PRDs with evaluation datasets and eval criteria as the acceptance criteria for AI features. The eval dataset defines what "good" looks like, and the eval criteria serve as the test suite.

## Key Information
- Proposed by Aman Khan as the evolution of product requirements for AI-powered products.
- Instead of handing engineers a PRD, the PM provides: an eval dataset (curated examples) and eval criteria (what to test for).
- This shifts the PM's role from writing documents to curating data and defining measurable quality standards.
- Works in conjunction with the development-to-production eval loop: start small, iterate, ship, and repeat with production data.
- The eval becomes the shared source of truth between PM and engineering for what constitutes acceptable quality.
