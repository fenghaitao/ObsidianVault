## Definition

[[Nikhyl Singhal]]'s 12-24-month prediction: companies will lay off large numbers of legacy staff (e.g., 30,000) while simultaneously rehiring a much smaller AI-first cohort (e.g., 8,000) — a distinct pattern from simple headcount reduction.

## Key Information

- Rooted in Singhal's observation that many companies over-hired during the prior growth era without getting proportional output for that headcount growth (his Google anecdote: a team of 20,000-40,000 could likely run on roughly 500-2,000-3,600 people if optimized purely for "keeping the lights on").
- The rehired cohort is expected to be qualitatively different — "AI first" — not simply cheaper or smaller versions of the old roles.
- Framed as a source of near-term "chaos" and confusion in the job market, but not necessarily bad news for the industry overall: Singhal notes PM job openings are simultaneously at their highest level in 3+ years, since the *type* of person companies want has changed, not the overall demand.
- Complements [[Builders Vs Information Movers]]: the "shed" population skews toward information movers; the "rehire" population skews toward builders with strong judgment.
