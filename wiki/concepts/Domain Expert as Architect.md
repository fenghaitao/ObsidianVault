## Definition
The Domain Expert as Architect is the third and most advanced model in Chris Lovejoy's framework for incorporating domain expertise into AI organizations. In this model, the domain expert designs a system that automatically improves itself — there should be minimal human-in-the-loop in the actual improvement process. The system learns from usage and user interactions to self-improve.

## Key Information
- **System design role**: The domain expert creates the ability for automated improvement rather than performing improvements directly
- **Minimal human-in-the-loop**: The goal is that the actual improvement process doesn't require constant human intervention
- **Learning from usage**: The system adapts dynamically and learns from how it's being used at the edge
- **Best when**: Manual iteration is too slow, there's large variation in how rules/policies are interpreted across organizations, and you can identify methods to automate improvements
- **Prerequisites**: Must have progressed through Oracle (understanding failure modes) and potentially Evaluator stages first
- **Required skills**: Domain expertise + experience working on LLM-powered products, engineering skills, plus all skills from Oracle and Evaluator roles
- **Progression path**: The Oracle's deep understanding of failure modes is critical for designing effective Architect systems; someone who evolves through all three roles has uniquely valuable insight

### Case Study
- **[[Anterior]]**: Evolved from Evaluator to Architect when manual iteration by engineers couldn't keep up with variation in how different organizations interpret prior authorization policies. [[ChrisLovejoy]] designed methods for automated improvement that learn from usage at the edge.
