## Definition

The Code Machine Archetype is one of several staff engineering archetypes. It describes engineers who achieve staff-level impact through extraordinary individual productivity — executing at 4-5x the output of a typical engineer — while still being expected to share knowledge and scale themselves.

## Key Information

- Rahul referenced this archetype from Meta's internal leveling framework: "the code machine archetype"
- Some engineers are "ridiculously productive" — their individual execution is worth 4-5 engineers
- These engineers can have staff-level impact by doing almost all the execution themselves
- They are still expected to scale themselves by sharing their knowledge and expertise
- Unlike the Tech Lead archetype, Code Machines may spend almost 100% of their time on execution rather than leadership
- However, they still need to communicate their work and ensure others can benefit from what they build
- The existence of this archetype shows that there is no single path to staff — it depends on your strengths
- Other archetypes: Tech Lead, Domain Specialist
