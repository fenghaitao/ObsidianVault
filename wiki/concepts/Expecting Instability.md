## Definition

Expecting Instability is a mindset advocated by Molly Graham for navigating fast-growing, constantly changing companies. Instead of seeking stability, leaders should expect and embrace instability as the only constant, finding guiding lights and compasses to navigate through the "tornado."

## Key Principles

- "We as humans seek stability — our brains would like things to stop changing. That is just not a reality inside of companies that are growing and changing as fast as OpenAI or a lot of the companies today."
- "You need to start to expect instability. Assume things are going to change. Assume you're going to have a new boss in six months."
- "You need to stop expecting that anything's going to be the same in 6 months or a year. You will have a different job. You will have a different boss."
- "How do you almost see the instability as stability? Because it's the only thing that is definitely going to be true."
- Find guiding lights and compasses: "some things will always be true" — get to know the customer (they have the answer), be patient (everything is going to change), just keep trying
- "It's sort of the same thing as 'serve the business, not the people' — what are the things that will always be true?"
- These guiding lights are "immovable objects, compasses in the face of a storm"
- All you ultimately take away from a company: people who want to work with you again and what you learned
- "Careers are long and nobody tells you that. This moment feels so dire and so hard and so scary and it's going to be okay."
- "The most interesting careers are winding and they have starts and stops and failures and successes"
