## Definition
Public sector AI transformation is the application of AI technologies to improve government service delivery, operational efficiency, and decision-making. The UK context includes a £40 billion annual productivity opportunity (per Tony Blair Institute), 7.25 million NHS waiting list patients, 350,000 backlogged court cases, and only 1 in 5 planning applications decided on time.

## Key Information
- UK public sector employs ~400,000 people across diverse roles: policy advisers, call center operators, prison wardens, nurses
- Key challenges: legacy systems, hierarchical bureaucracy, slow processes, pay constraints, regulations
- Most civil servants are not policy people in Westminster — they are operational staff in call centers, prisons, hospitals
- Government is described as "an industry of 400,000 people" — the biggest industry ripe for AI disruption
- Low-hanging fruit: simple AI use cases that can be done in days (workflow automation, document analysis)
- Complex challenges: huge backlogs requiring partnership models and horizontal approaches
- Key horizontal use cases: transcription for police, call center operations in DWP/HMRC
- Political will exists to seize new technology and make a dent in these problems
