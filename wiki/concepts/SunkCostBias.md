## Definition
Sunk Cost Bias is the cognitive tendency to continue investing in a project because of resources already spent, rather than based on its future prospects. In enterprise AI research, it can lead organizations to pour money into failing initiatives simply because they have already invested heavily.

## Key Information
- Identified by Asaf Bord as a key risk that Northwestern Mutual's incremental delivery approach was designed to eliminate
- Traditional research funding model: "We already paid a million dollars, let's just get through the project and see what we get"
- Northwestern Mutual's approach: six-week sprints with tangible deliverables, allowing leadership to stop funding at any phase boundary
- This structure reframes each phase as a fresh investment decision rather than a continuation of past commitments
- Also protects against competitor FOMO: if a third-party solution like Databricks Genie becomes superior, the company can switch without feeling they wasted prior investment
- The benchmarks and learnings produced at each phase retain value even if the project is discontinued
