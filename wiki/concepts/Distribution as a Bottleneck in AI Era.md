## Definition

Distribution as a bottleneck in the AI era is the idea that as AI makes building software dramatically easier and faster, the limiting factor becomes getting anyone to pay attention to what you've built. With a flood of new tools, those with marketing dollars, brand reputation, and existing customer bases will dominate.

## Key Information

- "If everyone thinks they can build the next Salesforce on their own... you're going to have just a flood of available tools"
- "Who gets the attention? It is still going to be who has the marketing dollars, who has the brand reputation, who has the existing customer base"
- "Distribution is everything"
- The quintessential example: Gemini and Google's tools — their continued dominance is driven by distribution tools, not just AI capability
- Lenny notes this is an "underappreciated new bottleneck" — every day there's a new life-changing product, making attention the scarce resource
- Also underrated: trust — building trust with users and internal teams is a function of influence and empathy
