## Definition

The Directly Responsible Individual (DRI) is the single person ultimately accountable for a project's outcome, even when other owners handle individual pieces of it.

## Key Information

- Marius was the DRI for Threads web: ultimately accountable even though other senior engineers owned meaty sub-pieces
- "Recursive accountability": sub-owners are accountable for their parts, but the overall DRI remains on the hook for the whole outcome
- Ownership means anticipating failure modes (DNS, infrastructure, QA, feedback channels) and working backward to prevent them
- Tech-leading style: a driving instructor with a second steering wheel — intervene at the moment of slippage, then back off as trust grows
