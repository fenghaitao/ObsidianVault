## Definition

"Routines" is a [[Claude Cowork]] feature, described by [[Fiona Fung]], that automates recurring manual work by running async agents on a schedule (e.g. overnight) against a set of inputs like feedback channels.

## Key Information

- Fung's concrete example: Routines now automate her old daily ritual of manually scanning feedback channels each morning — an agent instead summarizes themes and drafts PRs overnight for her to review upon waking.
- Positioned as an example of Anthropic dogfooding its own product (Cowork) to remove manual toil from its own team's workflow.
