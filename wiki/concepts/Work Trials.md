## Definition

Work Trials are a hiring practice where candidates do paid trial work for a couple of days to be evaluated in action before being hired full-time. This is used extensively at Lovable because the company's pace and culture are not for everyone.

## Key Information

- Lovable does "a lot of trials for people — trial work to see them in action for a couple of days"
- Candidates are paid as part of the work trial
- Probation periods are also used at the start of employment
- Purpose: the pace at Lovable is "insane" and not for everyone; work trials filter for fit
- After 10 days of vacation, Elena felt like she needed to re-onboard because everything had changed
- The company is upfront about how chaotic things are and prioritizes people who can "create clarity out of chaos"
- Work trials are especially important when asking people to relocate to Stockholm
