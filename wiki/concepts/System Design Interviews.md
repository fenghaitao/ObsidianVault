## Definition

System Design Interviews are a type of technical interview that assesses a candidate's ability to design large-scale distributed systems. They are a key focus area for Hello Interview's content and mock interview services.

## Key Information

- System design interviews are "this contrived thing that you need to study specifically" — even experienced senior engineers can be overconfident
- Ryan's roommate found that "he was overconfident when it came to system design because he felt like 'I'm a senior engineer and I've been designing systems at work'"
- Hello Interview's free resources and YouTube channel focus heavily on system design problems
- The YouTube channel features Evan "breaking down common system design problems" and showing "how I think through these problems"
- Evan reads engineering blogs from other companies to learn system design concepts, both for teaching and for his own learning
- The system design content is completely free on YouTube
- Other resources like "some book on system design" were found to be "not super helpful" compared to Hello Interview's approach
