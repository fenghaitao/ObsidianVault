## Definition

One of [[Eric Ries]]'s named frameworks in *[[Incorruptible (book)|Incorruptible]]* describing a company whose non-negotiable mission-aligned principles are treated as 100% fixed, not subject to case-by-case ROI debate.

## Key Information

- Supported by [[Clay Christensen]]'s line, quoted by Ries: "it's easier to do the right thing 100% of the time than 98% of the time" — once a principle is fully non-negotiable, there's no need to relitigate it whenever a tempting exception arises, because the answer is already known.
- Framed as the mechanism by which a company avoids [[Groupon]]-style incremental erosion (creeping from "one email a day" to eight), where each individual exception seemed locally justified.
