## Definition
Overoptimism About AI is the tendency for developers and experts to overestimate how much AI tools will improve their productivity, leading to overuse and disappointment when real-world results fall short of expectations.

## Key Information
- In METR's RCT, economics and ML experts predicted ~40% time savings from AI; developers predicted 24-25% before the study and ~20% after; actual result was a 19% slowdown
- Even after completing the study and experiencing the slowdown, developers still believed AI had been helpful
- This overoptimism may cause developers to overuse AI tools, spending more time instructing and correcting AI than they would have spent doing the work themselves
- Identified as one of the key contributing factors to the productivity paradox in the study
