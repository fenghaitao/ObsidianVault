## Definition
Slop is low-quality, inauthentic, or inaccurate content that can be produced by both humans and AI. It was a candidate for the Oxford English Dictionary 2024 Word of the Year (lost to "brain rot"). The opposite of slop is "kino."

## Key Information
- Defined by Oxford as content "generated using artificial intelligence" that is low-quality, inauthentic, or inaccurate
- swyx argues the Oxford definition is incomplete: slop does not require AI — any human or AI can be an "agent of slop"
- Examples of slop: Netflix's "Electric State" (vs. "K-pop Demon Hunters" which is kino), engagement bait on social media, insufficiently tested software releases, code that creates tech debt
- The cost of generating tokens (and thus slop) is dropping 100-1000x every year, making the problem worse
- swyx's Law of Anti-Slop: the amount of taste needed to fight slop is orders of magnitude larger than that needed to produce it
- Something that starts as kino can degenerate into slop if overused or if a trend becomes stale
- Fighting slop requires curation, accountability, modularity, and using AI itself as a tool for quality
