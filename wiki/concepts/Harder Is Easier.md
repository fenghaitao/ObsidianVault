## Definition

[[Eric Ries]]'s principle in *[[Incorruptible (book)|Incorruptible]]* that the harder, more expensive-looking short-term choice is often the one that compounds into durable long-term value — resisting [[Financial Gravity]] pays off, even though it looks costlier in the moment.

## Key Information

- Illustrated by [[Cloudflare]]'s free universal-SSL story (the [[Figure It Out Principle]]): the harder path (building custom cryptography, sacrificing short-term premium-tier conversion) produced an order-of-magnitude top-of-funnel growth increase and, per Ries, was central to the company becoming worth $70 billion.
- Illustrated by [[Yvon Chouinard]]'s uncompromising, non-ROI notion of product quality at [[Patagonia]] — a stance Ries says most people think is "insane," which he argues is exactly why it worked.
- Contrasted with [[Groupon]], which took the easier short-term path (sending more emails for more revenue) and eroded its founding principle one incremental step at a time.
