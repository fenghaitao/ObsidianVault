## Definition

Friction Logging is the practice of using a product as its actual user and writing down everything that feels frustrating or weird, then feeding those notes into team priorities and quality bars.

## Key Information

- Singleton produced "very detailed friction logs" from his engineererations at Stripe; they fed both developer-productivity investment and operational-review questions
- He recommends any senior leader (or anyone) periodically put on their user's hat, use the product, and log friction — even for infrastructure where the user is another team
- At Stripe, "walk the store" companywide meetings dogfooded the product in public, and bugs flowed into a high-visibility mailing list
- At Dev Agents the team runs a weekly process of using everything and writing meticulous friction logs that decide what to work on next
- The artifact makes explicit what "good enough" and "the bar" mean, beyond raw metrics
