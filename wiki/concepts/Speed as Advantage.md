## Definition

Speed as Advantage is the principle that when you're competing in a crowded market, speed can be your only competitive advantage. Boris Cherny applied this to Claude Code: "If you can do something today, you should just do it today."

## Key Information

- Boris: "Early on, it was really important because it was just me. And so, our only advantage was speed"
- "That's the only way that we could ship a product that would compete in this very crowded coding market"
- The principle: "If you can do something today, you should just do it today"
- This is still a principle on the Claude Code team today
- Combined with AI: "if you want to go faster, a really good way to do that is to just have Claude do more stuff"
- Creates a virtuous cycle: speed → using AI more → even more speed
- User feedback loop: fixing bugs within minutes makes users feel heard and encourages more feedback
