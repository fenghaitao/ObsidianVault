## Definition
The Last Mile Problem in AI refers to the challenge of getting an AI product to truly understand the specific nuances of the workflows, use cases, and customer contexts it serves. It's the gap between having a capable model and having a product that genuinely works for real users in their specific domain.

## Key Information
- **Core challenge**: Models may be technically capable, but products fail because they don't understand domain-specific workflow nuances
- **Domain expertise as solution**: The system for incorporating domain insights is more important than model sophistication for solving the last mile
- **Manifestation**: Products that work in demos but fail in real usage because they miss edge cases, workflow variations, and domain-specific judgment criteria
- **Organizational root cause**: Often caused by building AI products without deep understanding of exactly what workflows are being automated and how domain experts actually perform these processes
- **Related statistic**: ~50% of generative AI projects were abandoned last year (per Gartner) — the last mile problem is a key contributor
- **Framework connection**: The Oracle-Evaluator-Architect framework is Chris Lovejoy's proposed solution to systematically address the last mile problem
