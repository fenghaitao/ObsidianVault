## Definition
Forward-deployed engineering (FDE) is a methodology where engineers are embedded directly with domain teams — policy advisers, lawyers, prison wardens, parole officers — to observe their workflows, understand their pain points, co-design solutions, and take things from idea to implementation in days or weeks. It is the core operating model of Number 10 Data Science and its spin-off teams.

## Key Information
- Engineers embed with domain teams rather than working from a central office
- Process: observe workflows and pain points → co-design solutions → implement in weeks
- Applied across diverse contexts: policy teams at Number 10, lawyers at Cabinet Office, prison wardens at HMP Wandsworth
- Contrasts with traditional government IT procurement which can take years in discovery alone
- Enables building tools that domain experts actually need and will use
- First forward-deployed engineers in the history of 10 Downing Street embedded with policy and operational teams
- The model has been replicated by spin-off teams: [[JustAI]] deploys FDEs into prisons, [[IncubatorForAI]] into departments
