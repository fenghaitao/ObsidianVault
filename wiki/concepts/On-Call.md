## Definition

On-call is the practice of engineers being responsible for operating and responding to a live production system; Marc Brooker argues it is one of the best ways to learn how systems actually behave and to discover which problems matter.

## Key Information

- Brooker stayed on-call for ~15 years because the majority of his in-practice distributed-systems knowledge came from on-call and analyzing postmortems/COEs.
- Colleges teach CS fundamentals, programming, and math, but not the grounded knowledge of how systems run and how customers actually use them — on-call fills that gap.
- Rote ticket-closing should be automated; deep experts' time belongs on the unexpected, deeply understood and communicated broadly.
- Being close to operating your system is how you identify what to fix — otherwise your theories "are probably not going to be right."
