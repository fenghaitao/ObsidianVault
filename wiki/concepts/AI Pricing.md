## Definition
AI pricing is the discipline of designing billing models for AI-powered products, which face fundamentally different economics than traditional SaaS: low and variable margins, unpredictable compute costs, and the need to translate technical consumption (tokens, API calls) into customer-understandable value.

## Key Information
- AI companies grow 3x faster than traditional SaaS but face unique pricing challenges
- AI margins are low and impacted by user behavior — 5-10% of power users can consume 80% of compute
- Neither pure subscription nor pure usage-based pricing offers a complete answer
- External infrastructure costs are unpredictable, creating margin risk
- Technical pricing (tokens, API calls) overwhelms customers who think in terms of outcomes
- Pricing cannot keep up with product velocity — features rapidly move from premium to standard
- 33% of AI-powered businesses cite unpredictable compute costs as a top concern
- 41% cite difficulty defining the value being delivered
- 84% say pricing isn't keeping up with product rollout speed
- Iteration is a competitive advantage — the first price is a hypothesis, not a commitment
- Hypergrowth companies (100%+ YoY growth) changed pricing 3+ times in 2 years
- Hybrid pricing grew from 6% in 2024 to 41% — a 7x increase
- 56% of AI company leaders now use hybrid pricing
- 78% of AI companies build on Stripe for billing infrastructure
