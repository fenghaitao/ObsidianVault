## Definition

Agent orchestration is the practice of coordinating multiple AI agents to work together on complex tasks. Includes patterns for delegation, lifecycle management, async coordination, and self-observability. Abhishyant Khare's Co-Founder CTO product demonstrates orchestration at scale with a CTO agent managing engineering, support, finance, and marketing departments.
