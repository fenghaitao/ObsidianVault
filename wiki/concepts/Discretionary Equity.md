## Definition

Discretionary Equity (also called Additional Equity / AE) is an extra grant of restricted stock units (RSUs) that a director chooses to award a top performer outside the normal compensation cycle.

## Key Information

- A director decides to grant extra RSUs to high performers, usually for exceptional ratings or delivering company-priority work
- Simon received it twice: once around his IC5→IC6 promo, and once about a year later after his team shipped a priority product faster than anticipated
- Grants are often communicated via an unexpected short (15-minute) no-agenda calendar invite from a director
- The internal comp group skews toward top performers posting, so this grant appears more common than it is
- Combined with a low starting stock package, discretionary equity can meaningfully lift total compensation toward or above market
