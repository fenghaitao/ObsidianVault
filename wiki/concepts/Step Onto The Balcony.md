## Definition

Step Onto The Balcony is a leadership metaphor (from Stripe management coach Jeff Lawrence) for the habit of stepping back from the "dance floor" of day-to-day work to observe how the whole system is functioning.

## Key Information

- A leader is on the dance floor doing work, but must repeatedly prompt themselves to step onto the balcony and look down at how everything is working
- Singleton says it is especially important for Tech Lead Managers who are both IC and manager: the risk is getting sucked into their own code and losing track of what each team member needs to be unblocked
- As a manager-of-managers, he stepped onto the balcony more to reflect on how the entire group configures itself and what systems (reviews, hiring, goals) it runs
- The habit pairs with engineereration: balcony thinking, then deliberate immersion in real work
