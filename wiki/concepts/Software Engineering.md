## Definition

Software Engineering is the practice of designing, building, and maintaining software systems. At large tech companies, it is typically the primary engineering track with the highest compensation and status, distinct from specialized tracks like Data Engineering or Analytics Engineering.

## Key Information

- At Facebook, Software Engineers were on the E track (IC track) and received 30-40% more equity than Data Engineers at the same level
- Zach described a "Software Engineer Supremacy" culture at Facebook where SWEs were regarded as higher status than DEs
- Zach was denied a transition from Data Engineer to Software Engineer at Facebook without a down-level, despite writing 90% of the JavaScript code on his team of 15 data engineers
- Facebook's standard practice at the time: going from DE to SWE triggered a down-level, but going from SWE to DE did not
- Zach successfully transitioned from Data Engineer to Software Engineer at Netflix through Jender's support: hired as Senior Data Engineer, then transitioned to Senior Software Engineer within 6-12 months
- At Netflix, transitioning to Software Engineer while also being at a staff-level scope proved to be too much change at once — "you only want to change one variable at a time"
- Zach's advice: don't focus exclusively on technical skills; after senior engineer, soft skills become more important than pure technical ability
- Zach's side projects and weekend work on full-stack development (JavaScript, React) were critical for his transition to Software Engineering
