## Definition

No-code development is a paradigm for building applications without writing traditional programming code. Users describe what they want in natural language or manipulate visual interfaces, and an AI or platform generates the working application. This dissolves the boundary between describing an idea and building it, making software creation accessible to non-technical users.

The rise of AI-powered tools like [[ClaudeArtifacts|Claude Artifacts]] and [[AgenticCoding|agentic coding]] has accelerated this paradigm: users can now describe an app in conversation and receive a shareable, interactive product, with no coding knowledge required.
