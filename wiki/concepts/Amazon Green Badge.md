## Definition

Amazon uses a color-coded badge system to distinguish employment types: Blue Badge (full-time employee), Green Badge (contractor/temp), and Yellow Badge (vendor). Steve Huynh started as a Green Badge contractor before transitioning to a Blue Badge full-time SDE.

## Key Information

- Blue Badge: full-time employee
- Green Badge: contractor / temporary worker
- Yellow Badge: vendor
- Steve Huynh spent ~9 months as a Green Badge Support Engineer contractor before transitioning to full-time Blue Badge
- Support Engineers at Amazon write scripts and maintain systems, with less emphasis on coding interviews compared to SDE roles
- The Green Badge route can be an entry point into Amazon for those without traditional CS backgrounds
