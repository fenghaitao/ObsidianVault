## Definition

Sam Lessin's philosophy for content creation: things should be at the intersection of hilarious and useful. Humor cuts through the noise, but there must be a deeper truth — humor just for the sake of being funny is not useful.

## Key Information

- Sam Lessin: "I really enjoy things at the intersection of hilarious and useful. You kind of need both."
- "Hilarious just because you should have fun in life... but humor just for the sake of being funny is not that useful. There has to be a deeper truth to it."
- Applied to his etiquette work: teaching Silicon Valley people etiquette is inherently funny, but the value is real
- "It's very hard to cut through the noise these days. Humor is a great way to cut through it."
- This philosophy applies to his book, his classes, and his approach to podcasting
