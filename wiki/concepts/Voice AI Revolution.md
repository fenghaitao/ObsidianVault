## Definition

Marc Andreessen's prediction of a coming revolution in AI voice technology and wearables, encompassing AI voice assistants, emotional voice experiences, voice input devices, and smart glasses. He believes the voice modality is going to be "really great" and transformative.

## Key Information

- Articulated by Marc Andreessen on Lenny's Podcast
- Marc is "completely in love with all the AI voice stuff" and thinks it's "absolutely amazing, hysterical"
- Key examples cited: Grok's "Bad Rudy" voice mode, Sesame's emotional voice experiences, voice pendants and wearables, Meta glasses
- Voice input is the next frontier: "all the voice input stuff" and wearables are "going to be big"
- Marc predicts "a whole wearables revolution" driven by AI voice
- The technology enables natural, conversational interaction with AI that goes beyond text
