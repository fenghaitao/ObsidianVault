## Definition

Runway is the finite number of months a person can fund themselves (typically from savings) before needing income. Ryan L. Peterman gave himself a six-month runway of savings to test whether his podcast could become self-sustaining after leaving Meta.

## Key Information

- Ryan earmarked six months of savings to work on the podcast full-time after leaving Meta.
- Four months in he still hadn't made a dollar, and only began taking sponsors seriously as his bank account approached zero.
- Cutting his burn rate (by recording every episode himself) extended the runway: he earmarked six months but is now nine months in and the podcast "sustains itself."
