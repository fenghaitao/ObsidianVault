## Definition

"Teranova" (terra nova, Latin for "new land") is Sam Lessin's metaphor for the recurring pattern of new frontiers that create enormous opportunity. The American West was the original Teranova; the internet was "digital Teranova"; and every generation since has been desperate for their own Teranova — whether mobile, crypto, or AI.

## Key Information

- Sam Lessin: "The US was amazing for so many years because we had the West. If you were going to work hard, you could go west and you'd do great."
- "We were blessed because we had the internet. The internet was digital Teranova... we got to build fortunes and do amazing stuff."
- "Ever since then, whether it's mobile... or crypto... to now the AI god narratives, every generation is desperate for their Teranova."
- His skepticism about AI as Teranova: "The story is it has to be real. AI is a powerful tool. It's incredibly powerful for existing businesses and existing structures. It's not a great startup opportunity."
- Crypto is "the closest thing to Teranova" in his view, but AI is overhyped as a frontier
