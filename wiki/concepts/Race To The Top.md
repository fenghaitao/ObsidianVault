## Definition

[[Anthropic]]'s internal term (per [[Boris Cherny]]) for deliberately open-sourcing and publishing safety-relevant research and tooling, so that the whole industry — not just Anthropic — is pushed toward safer practices.

## Key Information

- Concrete example: Anthropic open-sourced a sandbox for running [[Claude Code]] that enforces boundaries on what an agent can access on a user's system — built to work with any coding agent, not just Claude Code, specifically to make it easy for competitors to adopt the same safeguard.
- Framed as Anthropic's practical lever for influencing how the AI industry evolves overall, given that Anthropic can't unilaterally control other labs' safety practices.
