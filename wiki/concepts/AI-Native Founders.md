## Definition

A new class of founders who leverage AI to build and run entire companies with minimal or no team. Marc Andreessen describes the most leading-edge AI-native founders as thinking about whether you can have "entire companies where the founder does everything."

## Key Information

- Described by Marc Andreessen on Lenny's Podcast
- The most cutting-edge AI-forward founders are exploring the possibility of one-person companies where AI handles all functions
- This represents a radical departure from the traditional startup model of building a team
- AI tools now enable a single founder to handle product management, engineering, design, marketing, sales, and operations
- Closely related to the concept of vibe coding and the collapse of role boundaries between PM, engineer, and designer
