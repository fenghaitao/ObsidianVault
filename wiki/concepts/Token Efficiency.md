## Definition
Token efficiency is Vincent Koc's term for the 2026 shift in AI-assisted development: moving from brute force token consumption (token maxing) to structured, intentional agent usage. It emphasizes not wasting tokens, being smarter about loop design, and keeping the human in the loop as an active manager rather than a passive observer. The core thesis: "2025 was about token maxing. 2026 is about not wasting them. It's about token efficiency. It's about agent in the loop."

## Key Information

- **Origin**: Vincent Koc's framing of the 2026 evolution in AI-assisted engineering
- **Core shift**: From "burn tokens and hope" to structured, intentional agent usage
- **Key components**:
  - **Agent in the loop**: Human stays actively involved — not just launching agents and waiting
  - **Smarter loops**: Bot looping over Ralph looping — structured reward mechanisms, opinionated design
  - **Swim lanes**: Organizing work into parallel streams with appropriate oversight levels per lane
  - **Intuition-driven management**: Using developed intuition to know when agents are on-track vs. bullshitting
  - **Skills as leverage**: Agent Development Environment as a force multiplier
- **Relationship to dark factory**: Token efficiency is what makes the dark factory sustainable — without it, the factory is just token maxing at scale
- **Bottleneck shift**: "Tokens are no longer the problem" — the bottleneck becomes human attention, taste, and judgment
- **Contrast**: Token maxing was the necessary phase to build intuition; token efficiency is the engineering discipline applied on top
