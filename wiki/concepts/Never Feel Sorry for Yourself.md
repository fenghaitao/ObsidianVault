## Definition

"Never feel sorry for yourself" is Sherwin Wu's personal life motto. It reminds him that no matter what happens at work or in life, you always have a sense of agency to pull yourself up. He repeats it to himself and to many others.

## Key Information

- Sherwin Wu's personal life motto
- "There's a lot of things that are going to happen at work, in life. And reminding yourself to never feel sorry and that you always have a sense of agency to kind of pull yourself up is something that I've had to tell myself a lot"
- He also repeats this to many other people
- Aligns with his management philosophy of empowering people and treating them like surgeons
- Connects to the broader theme of agency and leverage that runs through the entire conversation
