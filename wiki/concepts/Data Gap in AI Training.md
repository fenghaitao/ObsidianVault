## Definition

One of the three key constraints holding back AI progress: the world is running out of human-generated data publicly available on the internet for training AI models. New sources of training data are needed to continue advancing AI capabilities.

## Key Information

- Articulated by Jeetu Patel as one of three AI constraints Cisco addresses
- "So far we've trained these models with human-generated data publicly available on the internet, but we are running out of human-generated data"
- This represents a fundamental bottleneck for continued AI advancement
- Cisco sees this as an area where they can contribute — enabling new data pipelines and sources
