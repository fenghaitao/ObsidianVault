## Definition

Design-review norm at [[Slack]], described by [[Jessica Fain]]: after [[Stewart Butterfield]] gave explicit design direction in a review ("I want the button here, the interaction to be this"), the team would build exactly what he asked for *plus* two additional alternative versions they felt good about, creating a structured forum to debate the merits of each approach.

## Key Information

- Balances two competing needs Fain identifies in executive influence: showing you have your own point of view/domain expertise, while remaining genuinely open to the executive's direction rather than treating it as a rubber stamp.
- Complements the "office hours"/"Hey Stuart, what do you think?" practice Slack also used — bringing Butterfield in early (before a fully-baked design) via a half-hour conversation to extract his instincts and experience before the team committed to a direction.
