## Definition

A rule from Slack's design review process with Stuart Butterfield: after a design review where Stuart gave specific direction, the team would come back with exactly what he asked for plus two more versions they felt good about. This created a forum for conversation and debate about the merits of each approach, rather than just executing orders.

## Key Information

- **Origin:** Slack design review process with Stuart Butterfield.
- After Stuart gave design direction, the team would deliver: (1) exactly what he asked for, plus (2) two other versions they felt good about.
- "It gave us a forum for conversation to debate the merits of each type of approach."
- Jessica notes: "Frankly, he always had better ideas than we did" — but the process allowed for co-creation.
- This is related to, but distinct from, the general three-options (Goldilocks) approach. Stuart Plus Two More is specifically about delivering what was asked while still offering alternatives.
