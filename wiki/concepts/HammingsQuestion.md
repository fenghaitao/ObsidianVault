## Definition
Hamming's Question is a framing device from Richard Hamming's "You and Your Research" talk: "What's the most important problem in your field, and why aren't you working on it?" It challenges practitioners to focus on the highest-impact problems rather than incremental or comfortable work.

## Key Information
- Originates from Richard Hamming's famous talk "You and Your Research"
- Used by Jed Borovik to frame the 2025 AI Engineering Code Summit
- Applied to technology: the most important problem is AI
- Applied to applied AI: the most important problem is code
- Serves as a prioritization heuristic for directing effort toward maximum impact
- Frequently referenced in hiring contexts, according to Borovik
