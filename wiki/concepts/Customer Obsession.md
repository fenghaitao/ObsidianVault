## Definition

Customer Obsession is the primary Amazon Leadership Principle, holding that the customer experience is the highest priority above all other considerations. Jeff Bezos articulated that long-term profits and customer satisfaction are ultimately the same thing.

## Key Information

- Under Jeff Bezos, nobody gave lip service to the customer experience — it was the highest priority from interns to VPs
- You were always doing the right thing if you had the customer in mind, 100% of the time
- Bezos's framing: "long-term profits and customers are the same thing — there's no difference there"
- Chasing short-term profits or focusing on competitors can lead to optimizing for the wrong thing
- Working backward from the customer is the North Star for decision-making
