## Definition

A platform architecture principle where customers don't have to buy everything at once (loosely coupled), but when they do use multiple products together, the integration is seamless and magical (tightly integrated). Articulated by Jeetu Patel as a core characteristic of Cisco's platform transformation.

## Key Information

- Articulated by Jeetu Patel
- "You don't have to buy everything all at once, but boy, when you do buy two things together, they work like magic"
- Balances the reality that not every customer uses only Cisco top to bottom (there's an ecosystem) with the desire for a unified experience
- The customer should feel the same emotion — reliability, trust, elegance, and design — no matter what Cisco product they use
- Part of Cisco's shift from a holding company to a platform company
