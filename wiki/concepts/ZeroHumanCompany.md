## Definition
A zero-human company is the vision of a business that can nearly run itself through AI agents — where employees are AI agents, goals are set by humans, and jobs are automated through an orchestrator. The human acts as CEO, providing high-level direction, taste, and accountability while agents handle execution across engineering, marketing, sales, finance, and operations.

## Key Information
- Paperclip's tagline: "open-source orchestration for zero-human companies"
- The human remains in control as CEO, setting goals and providing taste/preferences
- AI agents fill roles across the org chart: CEO (delegation), CTO, coders, CMO, content strategist, video writer, etc.
- Key principle: "These tools can do anything except know what you value" — the human must communicate expectations accurately
- The human is accountable for the work; agents execute under human direction
- Practical approach: start small, build agent by agent, ensure quality before fanning out
- Not every agent needs frontier model pricing — cheaper models (e.g., Qwen 3.6+ via OpenRouter) can handle simpler tasks
- Dotta Bippa's framing: "Do not worry about AI taking your job. When you use something like Paperclip, you will be in charge of thousands of agents helping you build your business"
