## Definition

The System Design Interview is the technical portion of big tech interviews where candidates design large-scale distributed systems. Steve Huynh describes it as "necessary but not sufficient" — the "big blind" of the interview process, alongside the coding interview.

## Key Information

- Steve uses a poker analogy: system design is the "big blind" of getting into a company — necessary but not sufficient
- The interview prep industry has recently developed a hyperfocus on system design preparation
- The behavioral interview is what separates candidates after clearing the coding and system design bars
- If a senior candidate can't design a system, they would be "that 5% that leave" within the first year
