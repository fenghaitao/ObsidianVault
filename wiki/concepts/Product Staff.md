## Definition

"Product staff," per [[Adam Mosseri]] (Head of [[Instagram]]), is a new generalist role at Instagram/[[Meta]] that is an evolution of the traditional PM — able to do some of what a designer, a data scientist, and a researcher each used to do, leveraging modern AI tools — that anchors each "[[Pods|pod]]."

## Key Information

- On a 50%-sized pod, the team might be four engineers plus a single product-staff generalist, with no dedicated data scientist, designer, researcher, or content designer — the product-staff role absorbs those functions.
- Enabled by functional "bleeding" across roles: a lot of what a data scientist does at a large company is described as "relatively mechanical" (e.g., basic waterfall/funnel analysis), and internal tools now let a generalist pull that kind of analysis automatically instead of requiring bespoke specialist work.
- Mosseri compares product staff's rise to how, historically, strong PMs of any background could become general managers (GMs) — but says this "different-functions-can-become-X" dynamic is now "just so much more true" for product staff, drawing converts from design and data science who want to expand their cross-functional reach.
- Mosseri expects the strongest designers, data scientists, and researchers won't uniformly move into product staff roles — the majority will stay in their craft — but some of the strongest will convert, since the blurred functional boundaries let people who always had broad opinions (on strategy, go-to-market, etc.) act on them directly.
- Mosseri frames this as possibly "the age of the generalist," while still expecting a durable, important role for the rare, deeply specialized expert (the "phenomenal machine learning engineer" or "genius data scientist") who is brought in when work specifically requires that depth.
- Selection criteria for product staff overlap with Mosseri's broader hiring philosophy: grit, being a quick learner, self-awareness, plus (increasingly) curiosity and a willingness to try things and be wrong in public. See [[Taste]].
