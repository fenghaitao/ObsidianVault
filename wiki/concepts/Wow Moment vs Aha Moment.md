## Definition

Wow Moment vs Aha Moment describes the evolution of the user activation milestone in AI products. The traditional "aha moment" (user realizes the product's value) is being replaced by the "wow moment" (user is amazed by what's possible, even if the output isn't complete). The wow moment is about demonstrating capability and sparking curiosity, not about delivering immediate practical value.

## Key Information

- Elena Verna: "It doesn't need to be an aha moment anymore. It just needs to be a wow moment."
- For Lovable, the wow moment is the first preview generation after the first prompt — even though it's not a complete product, the user thinks "this is possible, I had no idea, I want to keep building"
- The wow moment is about demonstrating what's possible (capability) rather than delivering immediate practical value
- This makes the experience "addictive" — users keep coming back to explore more
- The wow moment is critical for removing the barrier of entry in new AI categories where people don't yet understand what the product does
- Contrasts with the traditional aha moment, which is about the user understanding the specific value proposition for their use case
- In AI products, the wow moment can happen in seconds (a 10-second video of the product in action)
