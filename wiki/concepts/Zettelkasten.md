## Definition
Zettelkasten (German for "slip box") is a knowledge management method where each note captures a single thought and lives in a flat folder structure. Chris Parsons uses this approach for his personal knowledge vault of thousands of markdown files.

## Key Information
- Knowledge management methodology: one note per thought, flat folder structure
- Used by Chris Parsons for his vault of thousands of markdown files
- Combined with Obsidian for viewing/editing and Claude Code for AI-assisted work
- Parsons uses Lianne (CLI embeddings tool) to search across all notes semantically
- Contrasted with hierarchical folder structures and other approaches like Mem Palace (Mina Jovovich)
- Parsons recommends starting today because "it takes years to put together" a useful knowledge base
- Also referenced Andrej Karpathy's "LLMs as wiki" article as a related approach
