## Definition

A big part of the modern design role is helping engineers and teams execute — not just telling them "here's the design." Designers act as consultants: jamming on ideas, whiteboarding, giving feedback on what engineers built, and explaining principles rather than just making decisions. This is the new execution-support mode of design.

## Key Information

- Designers no longer have time to make beautiful mocks before engineers ship
- Instead of gatekeeping, designers consult: they explain the "why" behind decisions to help engineers extract principles
- Example: "No, I think we should have a button here because not everybody realizes you can prompt this" — teaching, not blocking
- Designers point engineers to design systems and reusable components in code
- The goal is to equip engineers with tools they can use in the future without the designer
- Jenny estimates the "jamming and pairing directly with engineers" slice of designer time has grown from ~20% to 30-40%
- Designers also spend time implementing polish and last-mile details themselves using coding tools
