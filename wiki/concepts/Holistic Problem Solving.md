## Definition

Holistic Problem Solving is the approach of looking at the entire problem space rather than narrowly focusing on a single technical dimension. It is what allows engineers to find simple, high-impact solutions that specialists might miss.

## Key Information

- Evan's approach: "while most people were focused narrowly on the model improvements, I was focused on the holistic problem"
- This allowed him to see the comment signal solution that ML PhDs missed — they were "focused on pinching pixels and audio waves"
- "Those who are able to take a step back, view the problem more holistically, and then propose the solutions that regardless of their technical sophistication are going to make the largest progress — they excel"
- Evan's non-ML background was actually an advantage: "I by the nature of having less expertise there was less focused on those things"
- The holistic approach considers all possible signals, not just the ones within your domain of expertise
- Ryan's compute efficiency project also demonstrated holistic thinking: looking at the entire video encoding pipeline rather than individual components
- The pattern: being a generalist who can see the big picture is often more valuable than being a specialist optimizing one dimension
