## Definition

Data Engineering is the practice of building and maintaining data pipelines, infrastructure, and platforms that process, transform, and serve data at scale. It involves technologies like Hadoop, Spark, and Scala, and differs from Analytics Engineering which focuses more on SQL and product analytics.

## Key Information

- Zach was hired at Facebook as a Data Engineer (IC3), though he notes that the role today would be titled "Analytics Engineering" — more SQL, experimentation, and product analytics focused
- Data Engineering today is more about big data pipelines, Spark, and more technical infrastructure work — less analytical than what Zach did at Facebook
- At Airbnb, Data Engineering was done very differently: Zach coded in Scala 95% of the time (not SQL), building high-quality pipelines integrated with online production systems like Pricing and Availability
- At Facebook, Data Engineers were on a separate track from Software Engineers (DE track vs SWE track) and received 30-40% less equity at the same level
- Zach described this as a "Software Engineer Supremacy" culture at Facebook where SWEs were regarded as higher status than DEs
- At Netflix, the Data Engineering org was eventually collapsed into Data Science in 2019, with the VP, directors, and managers all fired — a decision that drove Zach to leave
- Zach's career goal was to transition from Data Engineering to Software Engineering because he wanted to work on "data stuff that wasn't just SQL" and because SWEs got better compensation
- At Airbnb, Data Engineering was the perfect blend of creating pipelines while still having technical integration and optimization concerns with online systems
