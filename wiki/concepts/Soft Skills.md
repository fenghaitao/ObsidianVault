## Definition

Soft Skills are the non-technical abilities — communication, persuasion, influence, and collaboration — that are equally important as coding ability for success in software engineering. They are what enable engineers to work effectively in the "giant group project" that is a software organization.

## Key Information

- Ryan noted that what makes a really good software engineer is having "a decent level of technical skills" but also "a decent level of soft skills" — learning how to persuade and influence others
- Work in software engineering is essentially a "giant group project" with thousands of engineers; soft skills are what resolve disagreements and ship products
- The effective approach to disagreement: acknowledge the other person's frustration, validate their point of view, and propose a compromise — "they are much more willing to buy into your idea"
- Soft skills are just as important as being a great coder; the job is far more than just coding
- Writing is a core soft skill: every code change has writing around it — design docs, code descriptions, results summaries — and writing is how you influence others at scale
