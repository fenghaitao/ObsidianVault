## Definition

A framework for distinguishing fundamental technological shifts (mega trends) from temporary excitement without lasting impact (hype cycles). The core heuristic: if you need a PhD to understand what something does, it's probably not a mega trend.

## Key Information

- Articulated by Jeetu Patel
- "When there's a mega trend, don't fight it. AI is a mega trend."
- Heuristic: "Is it easy to understand what this could do in its ultimate form for most people, or do you need to have a PhD to understand what someone's saying? When you feel like you need a PhD to understand what someone's saying, chances are it ain't going to be a mega trend."
- A mega trend impacts a large population of the world — if the thing is too complicated, it won't have that outsized effect
- Web3 is Jeetu's classic example of a hype cycle: "I couldn't make heads or tails out of a use case"
- AI is easy to understand: "You go to ChatGPT, you ask it a question, you get an answer"
- Having the judgment to distinguish between the two improves with age and pattern recognition
