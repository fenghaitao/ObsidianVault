## Definition

Barrels and Ammunition is a team-building framework by Keith Rabois. "Barrels" are the rare individuals who can independently drive an initiative from inception to success. "Ammunition" is everyone else who supports barrels. The number of barrels defines the number of important initiatives a company can pursue simultaneously.

## Key Information

- PayPal had 12-17 barrels out of 254 people -- considered an exceptionally high ratio
- A large successful company might have only 2 barrels
- Hiring more ammunition without expanding barrels just adds collaboration/coordination tax ("drag coefficient")
- A barrel is someone who can "take an idea and make it happen" -- fire and forget
- If a barrel encounters problems, they proactively come back with diagnosis, root causes, and what they've already tried
- The smoothie test: an intern who solved a logistics problem everyone else failed at became a barrel
- The ratio of barrels to ammunition dictates how many important initiatives can be pursued simultaneously
