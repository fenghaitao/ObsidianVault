## Definition

A common pitfall in hiring negotiations: the role expands significantly during the interview process (more teams, more responsibilities, higher expectations), but the compensation offer remains anchored to the original scope you discussed early on. This happens because you shared a number too early, before understanding the full scope of the role.

## Key Information

- Jacob: "They scope creep the role. They talk about how they want a senior hire. They're excited about you. They get you excited about it. And then they give you the offer and it's actually 20% under the number that you originally shared."
- The trap: "You said you only wanted 140." Now they're using your own number against you, and it's awkward to come back from
- This is why you shouldn't share your number first: you don't know the full scope yet
- Example: A senior PM role becomes a director role during interviews — leading five people, an engineering pod, a product-led growth motion — but the offer stays at the original PM number
- "Nobody's job description is what they actually do" — the JD is always incomplete
- Protection: slow down, understand the full scope before discussing numbers, and be ready to say "After our conversations, it sounds like the scope of the role has increased. Are you open to revisiting the comp structure?"
