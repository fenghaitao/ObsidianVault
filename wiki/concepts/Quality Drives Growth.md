## Definition

Quality Drives Growth is the principle that investing in product quality — even at the expense of growth metrics — can be the single most impactful growth lever. Amol Avaisary discovered this at Mercury when his team spent a full quarter fixing onboarding quality ("forget metrics, forget growth, forget everything else") and it became the most impactful quarter of his career up to that point.

## Key Information

- At Mercury, Amol's growth team spent a full quarter fixing onboarding quality with the explicit instruction: "Forget metrics, forget growth, forget everything else"
- Mercury's onboarding for regulated banking was extremely complex: registered agent vs. legal address vs. physical address, beneficial ownership details
- The team had invested heavily in quality throughout the rest of the product but hadn't done it for onboarding — the first experience users have
- Result: significant uplift in onboarding start-to-completion
- "Probably until I joined Anthropic, the single most impactful quarter that I've ever had as a growth team"
- "Quality drives growth" — Amol has tried to bring this principle to Anthropic
- This is a broader principle: the best products invest in quality, and that drives long-term growth
- Counterintuitive to traditional growth thinking which focuses on metrics and conversion optimization
