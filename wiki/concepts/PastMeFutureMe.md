## Definition
Past Me / Future Me is a mental model used by Radek Sienkiewicz to frame the purpose of a personal AI agent. The past self is lazy and does nothing; the present self has to do everything; the future self is an all-powerful being who can handle anything. The agent's job is to help the future self as much as possible, so that when you wake up tomorrow, as much as possible has been done by someone other than you.

## Key Information
- Radek wrote an article years ago about "the past me, the present me, and the future me"
- Past me: "completely stupid guy. He does nothing. He's lazy."
- Present me: has to do everything for the past self
- Future me: "some kind of god creature. It can do anything. That creature is all powerful."
- The problem: "if I don't do something today, it's fine, that other creature will do it for me"
- The solution: become friends with the future self — treat the future self as a person you want to help
- The agent's purpose: help the future self as much as possible
- Practical manifestation: nightly automation (3-6 AM) so that when Radek wakes up, everything is fresh and ready
- Radek closes his talk with this framework as the "whole purpose of this setup"
