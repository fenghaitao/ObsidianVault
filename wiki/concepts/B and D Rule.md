## Definition

The B and D rule is a dining etiquette mnemonic: make a circle with your thumb and forefinger on each hand. The left hand forms a "b" (bread plate is on your left), and the right hand forms a "d" (drinks are on your right). This helps you identify which bread plate and which drink are yours at a formal table setting.

## Key Information

- Sam Lessin: "People get this wrong all the time. You sit down at a big table and you're like, 'Which is mine?'"
- Simply look at your hands: left hand makes a "b" shape, right hand makes a "d" shape
- Bread plate is on your left, drinks are on your right
