## Definition

Blast Radius describes how many engineers, teams, and systems a given engineering effort touches. At staff-plus levels, an effort's size is measured less by code written and more by the blast radius of the problem solved.

## Key Information

- Jake Bolam's framing: at IC6 you lead one team (~10-12 people); IC7 spans multiple teams; IC8 is "another step" of blast radius, scaling with the importance of the problem
- Working on backend infrastructure that 3,000 engineers consume gives a very large blast radius versus a front-end surface with <100 engineers
- Top-level archetypes can have large blast radius via leadership ("leading through others") or deep specialization (a compiler fixer whose 10-line change saves hundreds of millions)
- Jake's IC8 project touched ~150 teams and made migrated infrastructure 20% faster, illustrating blast radius beyond a single org
