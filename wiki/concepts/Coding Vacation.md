## Definition

Coding Vacation is Boris Cherny's preferred type of vacation: traveling to a new city and coding all day. He did this in December in Copenhagen and other European cities. It was during one such vacation that he started engaging on Twitter.

## Key Information

- Boris: "My favorite kind of vacation... it's just like code code all day. It's the best"
- During a December trip to Europe (Copenhagen, several countries), Boris was "nomading around" while coding every day
- It was during this trip that he got bored, opened Twitter, and started engaging with Claude Code users
- "I ran out of ideas for like a few hours. I was like, 'Okay, what do I want to do next?'"
- This led to his Twitter engagement strategy: finding bugs and fixing them in minutes
