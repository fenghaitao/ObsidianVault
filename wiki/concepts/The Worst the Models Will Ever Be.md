## Definition

"This is the worst the models will ever be" is a quote from Kevin Weil (VP of Science at OpenAI) that serves as a north star for AI builders. It reminds us that today's AI models are the least capable they will ever be, and that capabilities will only improve over time. This applies especially to software engineering — the models will get better at coding, and trust in them will grow.

## Key Information

- Coined by Kevin Weil, VP of Science at OpenAI
- Kevin Weil was a previous guest on Lenny's Podcast where he said this exact line
- Sherwin Wu frequently references this quote as a guiding principle
- Applied to software engineering: "this is the worst that the models will ever be for software engineering as well"
- The implication: over time, people will trust models more and more, and the models will get better and better
- This is why you should "build for where the models are going and not where they are today"
- The quote encapsulates the rapid pace of AI progress and the moving-target nature of building on AI
