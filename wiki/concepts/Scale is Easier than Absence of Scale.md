## Definition

Jeetu Patel's counterintuitive observation that operating at scale is easier than operating without it. With 30,000 people, if you need five people for a new idea, you can reallocate internally. A startup with three people would need to raise money or pivot entirely.

## Key Information

- Articulated by Jeetu Patel
- "Absence of scale is way harder than the presence of scale."
- Example: "If I have a startup with three people and we need to prosecute another idea that requires five people working on it, I have to go raise money or pivot my entire business. If you have 30,000 people and you have an idea that requires five people, you just figure out a way that you allocate the dollars internally."
- The challenge with scale is communication: "the probability of miscommunication, the telephone game, has a profoundly negative effect if you're not intentional about it"
- This is why being the "custodian of the message" and not delegating storytelling is so critical at scale
