## Definition

Engineering integrity is the professional obligation — and the personal pride in craft — to tell the truth about the quality of what you build, especially when the buyer or client cannot judge it for themselves.

## Key Information

- Asian Dad Energy: "if it is a steaming pile of turds, it's your obligation to tell your client... the $20 million that you've invested is a steaming pile of turds."
- He recalled a literal university course on engineering integrity — having respect for the truth, quality, and integrity of what you build.
- Two forces keep consultants honest: personal pride (treating end-product quality as a reflection of oneself) and a consulting firm's reputation (deliver junk to client one and you lose clients two and three).
- Quality-caretaking is not only in big tech; he saw many consultants and senior leaders who cared deeply about the quality of what they shipped.
