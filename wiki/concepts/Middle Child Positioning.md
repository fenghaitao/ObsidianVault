## Definition

[[Evan Spiegel]]'s framing of [[Snapchat]]'s market position: much larger in scale than companies like Pinterest or Reddit, but much smaller than [[Meta]] or [[Google]] — occupying an in-between position with both the benefits and the challenges of a "middle child."

## Key Information

- The benefit: enough scale to do genuinely interesting/ambitious things (near-Fortune-500 scale, ~1B monthly active users).
- The challenge: risk of being overshadowed by larger incumbents ("older siblings") while also facing attention competition from smaller, newer entrants ("younger siblings").
- Spiegel frames the year of this episode as Snap's moment to define itself distinctly in the market, tied to the launch of [[Spectacles]]/Specs and proving the core ad business's durable profitability.
