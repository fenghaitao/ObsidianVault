## Definition

"Member of technical staff" is a generic job title (used in place of role-specific titles like "PM," "designer," or "engineer") that [[Andrew Ambrosino]] discusses as an early precedent for the kind of role fluidity now spreading more broadly under [[Role Collapse]].

## Key Information

- Per Ambrosino, the practice did not originate at [[OpenAI]] — he traces it back to Xerox, and recalls it being used at a company called "Up There," where he interned early in his career.
- Historically a convention in research-focused organizations/companies, predating the current wave of AI-driven role fluidity.
- Discussed in this source as one visible signal that a person's function or specialty isn't fixed by title — foreshadowing (per the interviewer) a broader trend where job titles/roles may become less meaningful as AI changes how work is organized.
- Ambrosino stops short of predicting all companies will converge on titleless/roleless structures — he explicitly believes distinct disciplines/skill sets (PM, design, engineering) still matter, even as the boundaries between them blur (see [[Role Collapse]]).
