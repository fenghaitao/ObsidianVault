## Definition

In the AI era, quality and trust are maintained not through perfect upfront design but through shipping early, iterating rapidly, and responding to user feedback. Trust degrades when you ship early and never improve. The promise is continuous iteration: "we're going to put it out there, take your feedback, and make it better."

## Key Information

- Jenny Wen's framework: "building trust through speed"
- The key is making people feel heard and that their feedback is actually appreciated and used
- Trust is lost when you release early and nothing ever happens; shipping early is fine if you commit to continuous improvement
- "This is the worst it's ever going to be" — products should only get better from launch
- Anthropic's approach: launch as "research preview," caveat the flaws, then iterate publicly
- The team tweets and responds to feedback, shipping fixes rapidly
- Claude Code enables very fast fixes, creating a virtuous cycle of feedback and improvement
- Users feel heard and are more willing to provide feedback when they see rapid iteration
