# L2 Plus Plus (L2++) Autonomy

The autonomous driving approach exemplified by [[Tesla]]'s Full Self-Driving (FSD): fewer sensors, no high-definition maps, and cheaper compute. Contrasts with [[Waymo]]'s L4 approach.

## Core Idea

Two competing approaches to autonomous driving will coexist and converge, making autonomy ubiquitous within 5-7 years.

## Key Points
- **L2++ (Tesla approach):** Fewer sensors, no HD maps, cheaper compute. Available everywhere. "L2 plus plus."
- **L4 (Waymo approach):** Many sensors, lots of compute, HD maps. Better in geographically constrained areas.
- Every car company on the planet is now working on an L2++ product like Tesla FSD
- In 5-7 years, both technologies will be much more ubiquitous globally
- Downward pricing pressure will make L2++ near-free, like nav systems became free with CarPlay/Android Auto
- "We're at that moment for L2++ systems where people are willing to pay thousands of dollars for a semi-automated vehicle"
- Once every car has some level of autonomy, full autonomy becomes an expectation
- This same paradigm applies to construction, mining, defense, and all physical machines
