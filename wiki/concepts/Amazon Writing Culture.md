## Definition

Amazon's Writing Culture is the practice of expressing ideas through written documents (typically six-page narratives) rather than PowerPoint presentations. Meetings start with 30 minutes of silent reading before discussion begins, ensuring everyone is on the same page.

## Key Information

- Amazon meetings start with 30 minutes of silent reading of the document before any discussion
- This forces everyone to be "fast forwarded to the same place" before discussion begins
- In contrast to the typical pattern where documents are sent ahead and nobody reads them
- Jeff Bezos established this culture, which Steve described as a "superpower" for Amazon
- Writing culture implies a reading culture — both are essential
- Steve's English/Creative Writing degree became an unexpected advantage at Amazon because of this culture
- The ability to write clearly and communicate effectively is a high-leverage skill at Amazon
