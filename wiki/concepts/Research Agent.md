## Definition

A research agent is an AI agent built at LinkedIn that is trained on member personas (e.g., small business owners, job seekers) using past research, support tickets, and internal knowledge to critique product specs from a user-needs perspective.

## Key Information

- Trained on LinkedIn member personas — not just world knowledge but all past research, support tickets, and internal knowledge.
- Effectively critiques product specs from the perspective of specific personas (e.g., what a small business owner would think about a marketing spec).
- In one example, the research agent critiqued a marketing spec so well that it shifted the team's direction to focus on different integration tools.
- Provides visibility into the corpus of knowledge inside the company that individual team members cannot easily access.
- Cannot be purchased off-the-shelf; requires LinkedIn-specific persona data and research history.
