## Definition
Paralinguistic understanding is the ability of a voice AI system to interpret non-verbal cues in speech — such as tone, pitch, emotion, hesitation, and discomfort — and use that information to inform its responses. It is what allows an AI to understand not just what was said, but how it was said and what that implies about the speaker's state.

## Key Information
- Paralinguistic cues include: tone, pitch, emotion, hesitation, discomfort, confidence, sarcasm
- In the "Her" movie clip, the AI understands the character is uncomfortable purely from his tone
- Speech-to-speech models technically preserve paralinguistic information (it's in the audio), but models trained on audio versions of instruct datasets never learn to exploit it
- If a model is trained only on factual question-answering from audio, it has no reason to capture emotional cues
- Moshi had the technical capability for paralinguistic understanding but was never trained to use it
- This is part of the "intelligence gap" between natural-sounding models and useful models
- Requires deliberate training on conversational data that rewards paralinguistic awareness
- One of the key challenges for reaching the "Her moment"
