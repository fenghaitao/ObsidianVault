## Definition

Adjacent User Theory, created by Bangaly Kaba, states that the biggest opportunity for product-market fit expansion during no-growth stages is to capture adjacent users — those just outside the core user base with somewhat similar needs but different geography, use case, or requirements.

## Key Information

- Created by Bangaly Kaba in a well-known article "many years ago"
- Adjacent users are just outside the core user base: similar needs but different geo, slightly different use case, or slightly different requirements
- The theory describes how to continue growing PMF without going to the next product horizon
- Elena Verna applies this to AI companies: the problem is that AI companies are so focused on recapturing pioneers (core users) that they don't have time to reach adjacent users
- Elena worries this creates a gap: pioneers get pulled too far into capabilities, and the latent majority (adjacent users) may never catch up
- Traditional companies would spend years reaching adjacent users; AI companies can't because they're constantly recapturing the core
