## Definition
Agent Quests are agent-suggested tasks in AgentCraft where agents proactively identify work that needs doing — refactoring, testing, cleanup — and present it to the human as quests that can be approved with a single click, reducing the cognitive burden of deciding what to work on.

## Key Information
- **Agent-initiated**: Agents suggest missions rather than waiting for human direction — "find missions for me to do"
- **One-click approval**: Humans review suggested quests and approve them with a button click
- **Task types**: Refactoring, testing, documentation, and other maintenance work the human doesn't want to think about
- **Addresses cognitive limits**: Humans can only hold so many ideas in their head at once; quests offload the "what should I work on" decision
- **Gaming metaphor**: Named after RPG quest systems where NPCs give the player missions
- Represents the second step in AgentCraft's autonomy ladder: direct prompting → quests → campaigns → channels
- Part of the broader strategy to remove the human from the bottleneck: first by reducing the need to think of tasks, then by reducing the need to monitor execution
