## Definition

"Themes for Discussion" is a meeting tactic: when an executive leaves many comments on your document, bubble up the biggest and most controversial themes at the top for live discussion. This prevents getting bogged down in minor questions and ensures the limited meeting time is spent on the "spiciest" topics that can only be resolved through conversation.

## Key Information

- When a leader comments extensively on a doc, add a section at the top: "Themes for Discussion"
- Bubble up the biggest and most controversial pieces that can only be discussed live
- Anything merely curious or logistical (timeframe, staffing needs) can be answered offline
- "What I can't get again is the time for discussion with that group"
- The safer execs feel and the more they think you've understood the most spicy/controversial topics, the better the discussion
- "That's where you get to the good stuff and you really get their insight and input"
- Verify your understanding: "Rachel, was that right? Did I sort of get your thoughts right? Anything you would add to this?"
