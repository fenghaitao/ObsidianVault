## Definition

"Building Trust by Killing Things" is the idea that one of the most powerful ways to build trust with executives is to deprioritize or kill your own projects. This demonstrates that you have the same aligned incentives as the executive — thinking about the good of the company and user outcomes, not just your own empire.

## Key Information

- "One of the biggest things you can do to build trust is kill things. Deprioritize things."
- This is a "very, very senior way of thinking" — it shows aligned incentives with the executive
- Everyone is always trying to acquire more resources, more investment, more features. Showing you can kill things that aren't working is a trust-building move
- "If you're thinking about how do you be more senior, how do you show up in a way that is in a leadership mindset" — killing things is a key signal
- Be specific about how you'll know something isn't working and when you'll come back with a decision
- The ultimate trust-building move: tell leaders "here's our goal, here's all these ideas, but we don't think this is the right goal, and here's why we should kill this product"
