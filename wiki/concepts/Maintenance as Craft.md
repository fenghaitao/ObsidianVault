# Maintenance as Craft

The philosophy, articulated by [[Qasar Younis]], that maintenance, hygiene, and follow-through are the real substance of business — not just the exciting creative work.

## Core Idea

"Half the work is follow-up. That is the business. It's not more complex than that."

## Key Points
- Qasar's automotive engineering roots: automotive engineering is fundamentally an exercise in quality and maintenance
- A factory is about safety and maintenance — "there's not a lot of complex things. Operationally strong is keeping an eye on a handful of things and making sure you're doing them really good"
- "A man who cannot command himself is not fit to command others" — maintenance starts with self-discipline
- If you maintain yourself, your team, and your company, the products come out of that system
- Qasar's analogy: car enthusiasts obsess about detailing underneath the driver seat where nobody will look — same ethos applies to company building
- Applied Intuition has a weekly "cleaning zen" where everyone cleans their own area (inspired by Japanese schools)
- No-shoe policy in the office (also Japan-influenced)
- "There's a direct line between 'be quiet and alone,' 'clean your desk,' and 'well-written software'"
- "I think it's important that you're not getting so wrapped up in your own narrative... it's like, clean up your desk"
- Stripe Press published a book about maintenance being underappreciated — Qasar agrees
