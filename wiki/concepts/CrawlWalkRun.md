## Definition
Crawl-Walk-Run is a phased rollout methodology that gradually introduces a new tool or system to increasingly broader and less expert audiences, starting with the most capable users who can validate outputs and provide informed feedback.

## Key Information
- Applied by Northwestern Mutual for their GenBI rollout in three phases:
  - **Crawl**: Release to BI experts who can independently verify results and know what "good" looks like — analogous to GitHub Copilot for data analysts
  - **Walk**: Expand to business managers who are closer to BI teams, can spot errors from experience, and are more tolerant of imperfections
  - **Run**: Eventually target executives who demand clear, concise, and fully trustworthy answers — acknowledged as a distant vision that may never be achievable
- Each phase builds trust incrementally while gathering feedback from users with appropriate expertise levels
- Reduces the risk of users abandoning the tool after a single bad experience by matching user sophistication to system maturity
- Particularly suited to risk-averse enterprises where trust must be earned gradually
