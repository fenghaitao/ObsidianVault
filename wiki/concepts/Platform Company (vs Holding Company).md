## Definition

A platform company is one whose products are tightly integrated so the customer feels the same emotion regardless of which product they use — the opposite of a holding company that runs a collection of siloed, independent business units. Cisco transformed from a holding company of 251+ acquisitions into a platform company.

## Key Information

- Articulated by Jeetu Patel describing Cisco's transformation
- A holding company model: everyone wants to be a GM with their own sales, marketing, product, and engineering teams — running siloed $40M businesses
- A platform company: tightly integrated products where the customer experiences the same reliability, trust, elegance, and design across all products
- "Loosely coupled but tightly integrated" — customers don't have to buy everything at once, but when they buy two things together, they "work like magic"
- This was the second major change at Cisco for AI transformation
- The goal: "not a holding company of 251 acquisitions and thousands of different products" but a platform company
