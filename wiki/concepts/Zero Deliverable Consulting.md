## Definition

Zero deliverable consulting is Simon Wilson's consulting model: one-hour paid calls where the client gets his full attention and expertise, but he produces no written report, no code, and no deliverables. He describes it as fitting his lifestyle perfectly — he doesn't want to find clients, invoice, negotiate, or do full-day engagements.

## Key Information

- Simon Wilson's consulting model: "I don't write a report, I don't write any code. You just get my time for an hour"
- "I'm very lazy when it comes to actually making money. I don't want to go out and find clients and I don't want to invoice them and chase them and negotiate"
- Uses intermediaries to channel these opportunities to him
- "It fits into my lifestyle perfectly because I don't want to be doing full day-long engagements"
- When asked how people can reach him, he was "almost hesitant to answer because I might get people talking to me and not going through an intermediary"
- This model is enabled by his reputation as a leading voice in AI and software engineering
