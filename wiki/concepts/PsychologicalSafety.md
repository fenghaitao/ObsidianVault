## Definition
Psychological safety is the shared belief that a team is safe for interpersonal risk-taking. Identified by Google's Project Aristotle (2012) as the single biggest indicator of team productivity, it is also critical for successful AI adoption in engineering organizations.

## Key Information
- Google's Project Aristotle (2012) studied characteristics of highly performant teams, expecting the recipe to be high performers + experienced managers + unlimited resources
- The study found overwhelmingly that psychological safety was the biggest indicator of productivity, not any of the expected factors
- Applies directly to AI adoption: engineers need to feel safe experimenting with AI tools without fear of being replaced
- Leaders must proactively communicate that AI is for augmentation, not replacement, to maintain psychological safety
- Fear of AI reduces adoption effectiveness; reducing that fear is a leadership responsibility
