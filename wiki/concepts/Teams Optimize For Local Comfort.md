## Definition

An observation by [[Matt MacInnis]] (CPO, [[Rippling]]): teams will always drift toward optimizing for their own local comfort over company-level outcomes, not out of deliberate malice but as an emergent, near-universal property of human groups.

## Key Information

- MacInnis is explicit that this isn't primarily about people consciously colluding to slack off (though he notes unions do this "unequivocally deliberately") — it's what happens by default, over time, in any collection of people, including highly capable product managers or engineers, absent active counter-pressure.
- Positioned as the reason leaders must treat fighting organizational [[Compounding Plus Power Law Plus Entropy|entropy]] as a daily, unrelenting task rather than a one-time fix: entropy "creeps into the system insidiously and slowly," and comfort is the leading indicator that it has.
- Directly connects to [[Extraordinary Results Require Extraordinary Effort]]'s claim that comfort at work is itself a sign something has gone wrong.
