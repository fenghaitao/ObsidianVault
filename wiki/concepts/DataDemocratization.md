## Definition
Data Democratization is the goal of making data and insights accessible to everyone in an organization at their fingertips, without requiring them to rely on a specialized BI team to find reports, interpret data, or answer business questions.

## Key Information
- Core motivation behind Northwestern Mutual's GenBI initiative
- Traditional bottleneck: BI teams spend significant time (~20% of capacity) just finding and sharing the right reports with stakeholders
- GenBI aims to remove this dependency by letting users ask business questions directly and get data-driven answers
- At Northwestern Mutual, 80% of the BI team's report-finding work was automatable through the RAG agent component
- True democratization requires trust: users must be confident the answers they receive are accurate
- The crawl-walk-run approach acknowledges that different user groups have different abilities to verify results, affecting how democratization should be rolled out
- Good metadata is a prerequisite: without well-documented data, even AI systems cannot democratize access effectively
