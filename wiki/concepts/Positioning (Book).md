## Definition

Positioning is a classic marketing book by Al Ries and Jack Trout, recommended by Stewart Butterfield as essential reading. Its core insight is that it's almost impossible to create a net new idea in someone's head — it's much easier to combine existing ideas.

## Key Information

- Recommended by Butterfield as "an absolute classic" and "very short"
- "I would recommend everyone read it"
- **Core insight**: it's almost impossible to create a net new idea in someone's head. It's much easier to take existing ideas and combine them
- **Examples**: "Jaws meets Star Wars," "Uber for pets"
- **Connection to "We Don't Sell Saddles Here"**: you're not just creating the product, you're creating the market — they're one and the same
