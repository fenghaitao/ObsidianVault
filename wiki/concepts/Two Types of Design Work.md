## Definition

Design work is becoming stratified into two distinct types: (1) supporting implementation and execution — helping engineers and teams execute as they rapidly ship features, and (2) creating vision and direction — pointing teams toward a cohesive goal. The second type is the hardest to make time for but is especially important when everyone can spin off features in any direction.

## Key Information

- **Type 1: Execution support** — Engineers use AI tools to create features rapidly; designers don't have time to make beautiful mocks anymore; they act as consultants, giving feedback, whiteboarding, and helping with polish
- **Type 2: Vision/direction** — Creating a vision that points people in the right direction; critical in a world where anyone can build anything in any direction
- Vision work has changed shape: used to be 2-5-10 year visions, now it's 3-6 months out
- Vision work is no longer about creating beautiful decks; it's often just a prototype that points people in the right direction
- Without vision work, efforts become inefficient because people build random things without a common cause
- Designers are "better off not blocking" engineers and should "let them cook" rather than gatekeeping
