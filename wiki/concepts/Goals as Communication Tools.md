## Definition

Goals as Communication Tools is a philosophy articulated by Molly Graham that the primary purpose of setting goals is communication — to create clarity and help people know what to work on when they show up at their desk each day. A 100-line spreadsheet of OKRs fails this purpose entirely.

## Key Principles

- "At the end of the day, goals are a communication tool. That's what they are."
- Goals are designed to create clarity to help people answer: "I'm going to show up at my desk. What should I work on? What's the most important thing?"
- A 100-line spreadsheet that "feels like it's written in Greek" doesn't create clarity for anyone
- Graham has "a bone to pick with OKRs" — while helpful for Google and others, they are often misapplied as bureaucratic exercises rather than communication tools
- The test: can an intern who started on Monday understand your goals? If not, they're failing as communication tools
- Goals must explain acronyms and use numbers that make sense to average people
- "Either you are very clear with [people] about what the priorities are or they're going to prioritize for you"
- The right goal-setting approach depends on company stage — what's right for a seed-stage company is not right for an established business
