## Definition

A Word-of-Mouth Loop is a growth mechanism where the product experience is so remarkable that users naturally share it with others, creating a self-reinforcing cycle of acquisition. The only way to create it is to "blow their socks off" when they experience the product.

## Key Information

- Elena Verna: "The only way to create a word-of-mouth loop is just to blow their socks off when they actually experience your product."
- The loop requires the product to create something for customers that is "worth talking about" — stories they want to share, experiences that feel empowering
- Users should feel like "I have superpowers now, and I can't wait to tell others."
- Lovable's advantage: the product name itself ("lovable") drives the internal culture of creating lovable experiences
- Word-of-mouth is the primary growth driver for Lovable — they're "not in control of a lot of our growth" because of it
- The loop is amplified by community, social posting, and influencer marketing
- Customer word-of-mouth is the hardest to stand up because it requires genuine product excellence
- **Jason Cohen's perspective:** Existing customers bringing in new customers (referrals, word-of-mouth) is one way to offset the fact that cancellations grow exponentially with company size. Unlike marketing, referrals scale with the customer base — triple the customers, and referrals triple too. This is especially relevant in consumer products where NRR expansion is harder to achieve.
