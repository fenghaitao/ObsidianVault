## Definition
The "Gemmaverse" is the community ecosystem of fine-tuned, quantized, and customized models built on the Gemma family, encompassing over 100,000 community-created models and 500 million total downloads across the entire Gemma model family.

## Key Information
- 500M+ total downloads across all Gemma models
- 100K+ community-created models (quantizations, fine-tunes, variants)
- 10M Gemma 4 downloads in the first week after release
- Over 1,000 models based on Gemma 4 within the first week
- Community contributions span: fine-tunes for specific languages (Southeast Asian, Indian), medical applications (cancer therapy research), safety models, and device-specific ports
- Official variants from Google: Shield Gemma (safety), Med-Gemini (medical)
- Community research: AI Singapore (multilingual), Sarvam (sovereign AI), cancer therapy pathway discovery
- Represents the vision of open models as an enabler for ecosystem innovation rather than just a product to consume
