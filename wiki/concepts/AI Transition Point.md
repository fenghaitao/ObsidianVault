## Definition

AI Transition Point refers to the current era of artificial intelligence as a major technological shift comparable to the internet and mobile revolutions. Philip Su felt he had missed two previous transition points (internet and mobile at Microsoft) and was determined not to miss AI.

## Key Information

- Philip Su missed two major transition points while at Microsoft: the internet revolution (Microsoft as a whole missed it) and the mobile revolution (Microsoft largely missed it)
- He caught the social network revolution at Facebook, which he considers a "huge transition point in humanity"
- AI, he believes, could be "as big or maybe bigger than the internet's impact on humanity"
- From a universe perspective: "we might be the first species to create something smarter than us. From an evolutionary perspective, that's just really weird."
- Once AI surpasses human intelligence, second-order questions emerge: "what is actually happening in the universe? We might answer the Fermi question."
- This was the primary reason he returned to industry to join OpenAI
- He evangelized GitHub Copilot to friends early on when AI was still nascent, but had a hard time convincing people to pay for it
