## Definition

An Irish goodbye is leaving a social event without making a production of saying goodbye to everyone. Sam Lessin recommends it as the best exit strategy for large group settings — you just quietly disappear, perhaps thanking the host on the way out.

## Key Information

- Sam Lessin: "There are lots of scenarios where I think an Irish goodbye is the best goodbye — you just kind of disappear."
- In large group settings, it's the preferred approach
- You might thank one person (the host) on the way out
- The opposite: "The 'I am leaving now' — let me kind of say goodbye to everyone and hug everyone. It's too much. It's too much."
- When you do say goodbye: stand when people leave the table, stand to shake hands, don't take calls during goodbyes
