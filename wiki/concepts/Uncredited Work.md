## Definition

Uncredited Work is deliberately spending a meaningful portion of your time on high-value activities that will never show up in a performance review — helping individuals, org contributions, or low-visibility code.

## Key Information

- Jake Bolam spends 20-30% of his time on work he'll never get PSC credit for (helping a person, wider-org contributions, some coding)
- Rationale: spending 100% of your time only on credited work means dropping a lot of high-value but invisible help
- A Meta IC10 essay openly describes the same 20% practice; Jake was already operating this way before reading it
- Performance reviews (PSC at Meta) reward large, measurable items, so many small acts that make others 5% better never get recorded
- Early-career people who over-deliver on uncredited help (responding late, unblocking others) can bank credibility that compounds later
