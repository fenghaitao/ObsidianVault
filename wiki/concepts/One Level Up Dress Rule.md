## Definition

A simple dress etiquette rule: dress one level up from the expected dress code of the room — not two, not three, just one. This is "an easy way to win" without looking like you're trying too hard.

## Key Information

- Sam Lessin: "Dress one level up. It's an easy way to win. Not two, not three, but one."
- You can reduce formality (e.g., take off a jacket to go from suit to business casual), but you can't easily increase it
- If you're unsure about the dress code, just ask — "it shows a level of confidence and calm and humility"
- Different cities have different expectations (New York vs. San Francisco)
- General principle: "Look put together, look like you cared, look like you made some effort, but don't overdo it"
- Unless you're intentionally breaking a rule, which should be done "with a lot of cultural understanding"
