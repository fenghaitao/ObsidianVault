## Definition

[[Applied Intuition]]'s stated meta-value, per [[Qasar Younis]]: all of the company's other values reduce to these two words. Practically, it means checking whether any piece of founder advice actually applies to your specific situation before adopting it, rather than following advice (or philosophy) for its own sake.

## Key Information

- Younis gives his own "stay quiet, don't build in public" advice as an example of advice that only works because he's already personally known and trusted in the venture ecosystem — a founder without that network would need the opposite strategy (build a public following to recruit talent, investors, and customers), per [[Naval Ravikant]].
- Extends to how Applied Intuition derived its company values: not by philosophizing about what values *should* be, but by empirically identifying the actual reasons the company was already succeeding once it had traction.
- Connects to the company's emotion-minimized decision culture — see [[Best Idea Wins Culture]] — where the goal is finding the actually-correct path rather than a philosophically appealing one.
