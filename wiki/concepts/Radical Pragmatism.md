# Radical Pragmatism

The meta-value of [[Applied Intuition]], as articulated by co-founder [[Qasar Younis]]. All other company values can be reduced to these two words.

## Core Idea

Make decisions based on what works, not ideology, ego, or what's popular. Be humble enough to listen to all inputs, then decisive enough to execute.

## Key Points
- All of Applied Intuition's values reduce to "radical pragmatism"
- The company's values were derived from observing what made them successful, then codified
- Qasar intentionally stayed quiet for ~10 years not because being "under the radar is cool," but because it was pragmatically better for focus
- Qasar joined Twitter/X not because he wanted to be public, but because it was pragmatically the right move to propagate ideas
- "Leave your baggage and trauma in the background, and really let's think more pragmatically"
- Pragmatism can mean being contrarian (building quietly) or conventional (joining social media) — the principle is the same
