## Definition

After-Call Work (ACW) is the administrative post-processing phase in contact center operations where operators type up call notes, summarize conversations, and select disposition codes after a customer call ends. It represents a major operational bottleneck, often consuming nearly as much time as the call itself.

## Key Information

- **Time Impact**: Average ACW time of 6.3 minutes for an average 6.5-minute call — a nearly 1:1 ratio of administrative work to customer interaction
- **Quality Issues**: Summarization relies on individual operator memory and writing skills, leading to highly inconsistent data quality
- **Stress Factor**: The repetitive administrative burden contributes significantly to [[Operator Cognitive Load]] and is a driver of staff turnover
- **AI Solution**: Dippu Singh's VoiceOps pipeline targets ACW specifically, using generative AI to automate summarization and data extraction, reducing ACW time to 3.1 minutes (~50% reduction)
- **Enterprise Impact**: Across 500 seats handling thousands of calls per day, the reduction reclaims dozens of full-time headcount equivalents purely from efficiency gains
- **Data Quality Shift**: Moves categorization from subjective/operator-dependent to standardized/uniform output, creating a consistent [[Voice of the Customer (VoC)]] dataset
