## Definition
A "token billionaire" is someone who spends over a billion output tokens per day on AI models (~$1,000+), fully saturating their day with token consumption to maximize agent productivity. Coined by Ryan Lopopolo as a self-description and aspirational goal.

## Key Information
- Coined by Ryan Lopopolo (OpenAI), who spends over a billion output tokens per day
- Represents the philosophy of fully leaning into agents doing the full job of software engineering
- Token usage split roughly in thirds: planning/ticket curation/documentation, implementation, and CI
- Aspirational goal: every engineer should become a token billionaire
- The dream state is having 50 agents running 24/7 without human interaction
- Every "continue" typed to an agent is a failure — the harness should provide enough context for autonomous completion
