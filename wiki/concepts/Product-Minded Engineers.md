## Definition

Product-Minded Engineers are engineers who can think like PMs — they understand user needs, business context, and can manage cross-functional stakeholder relationships. In the AI era, their value has gone up "an order of magnitude" because they can act as mini PMs, reducing the bottleneck created by PMs and designers not getting the same productivity boost from AI as engineers.

## Key Information

- Engineers who can "really step in as mini PMs if the PM is absent"
- Their value goes up "an order of magnitude" in the AI era
- Under the Two-Week PM Rule at Anthropic, they handle security, legal, and cross-functional stakeholder conversations for smaller projects
- Amol's growth team has always hired product-minded engineers, but now they're "more formally leveraging" them
- The need arises because engineers are getting 2-3x productivity from Claude Code, but PMs and designers haven't gotten the same leverage
- A team of 5 engineers + 1 PM + 1 designer now effectively has 15-20 engineers' worth of output
- "Not everyone can do it" — this is a distinct skill set that not all engineers have
- Becoming interdisciplinary is key career advice: "The engineer who is highly product-minded is a unicorn, an absolute unicorn"
