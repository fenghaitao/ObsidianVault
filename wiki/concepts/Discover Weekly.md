## Definition

Discover Weekly is Spotify's flagship personalized playlist product, launched in 2015, that delivers a fresh set of song recommendations to each user every week. It was one of the earliest mainstream applications of machine learning for personalization in consumer products.

## Key Information

- **Launched**: 2015, making it one of Spotify's longest-running ML-powered products
- **Personalization**: Generates a unique playlist for each user every week based on their listening history and preferences
- **ML Foundation**: Built on recommender system technology — part of the "traditional recs" pipeline (candidate generation + ranking) that Spotify is now evolving toward generative approaches
- **Historical Significance**: Cited as one of the coolest ML products of its era, representing the early wave of personalized AI in consumer applications
- **Aman Khan**: Previously worked on Spotify's ML platform including Discover Weekly and search embeddings
