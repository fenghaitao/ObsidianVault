## Definition
Accessibility AI refers to using on-device AI to restore or augment sensory capabilities for people with disabilities — such as helping blind users navigate the world through vision models and voice interfaces, or enabling speech-based computer control for those who cannot type. On-device execution is critical for reliability in areas with poor internet connectivity.

## Key Information
- Prince Canuma's personal motivation: his father went blind in 2020
- On-device AI enables accessibility without requiring internet (critical in regions like Africa)
- Vision: phone camera + MLX VLM describes surroundings to blind users in real-time
- Audio: speech-to-text enables computer control for users who cannot type
- Omnimodels (vision + audio + text) enable multimodal accessibility interactions
- On-device execution ensures reliability regardless of connectivity
- No subscription costs: only the energy bill for running models locally
- Example: MLX VLM goggles prototype (2020/2021) evolved into phone-based accessibility
- Richie Mini robot demonstrates physical-world accessibility agent
