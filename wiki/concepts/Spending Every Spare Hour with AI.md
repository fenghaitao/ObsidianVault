## Definition

Marc Andreessen's career advice for the AI era: people who want to improve themselves and develop their careers should be spending every spare hour talking to AI, becoming fluent in AI tools and capabilities as a form of continuous learning and skill development.

## Key Information

- Articulated by Marc Andreessen on Lenny's Podcast
- "People aren't fully grasping how much this [is] changing. And people who really want to improve themselves and develop their careers should be spending every spare hour in my view at this point talking to AI"
- The advice is about building fluency and comfort with AI as a tool, similar to how previous generations needed to learn computers
- This is not just about technical skills — it's about understanding AI's capabilities, limitations, and how to leverage it
- The implication is that AI literacy will be as fundamental as computer literacy
