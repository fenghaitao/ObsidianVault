## Definition

Ikigai is a Japanese concept meaning "reason for being." It is often represented as the intersection of four elements: what you love, what you are good at, what the world needs, and what you can be paid for.

## Key Information

- Michelle Rial referenced the Ikigai concept when Lenny described the Venn diagram overlap of his newsletter: he enjoyed it and people valued it
- Lee Jacobs pointed out this rare overlap: "How often do you enjoy something and people value it?"
- Lenny noted that the key third element — actually enjoying it — was missing from his initial framing but was crucial
- Lenny's newsletter represents the intersection: he is good at writing, people value it, he enjoys it, and it can be monetized
