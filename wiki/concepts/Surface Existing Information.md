## Definition

Surface Existing Information is one of Malte Ubl's four agent archetypes. It describes agents that make scattered company knowledge practically usable by finding and organizing information that technically exists but is not practically accessible.

## Key Information

- Part of Malte Ubl's Agent Archetypes framework
- Described as "probably the most magical thing you can do in any company today"
- Common scenario: information exists somewhere in the company (Slack messages, meeting recordings, issue trackers) but for all practical purposes cannot be used
- Example: issue trackers are often out of date, but the information to update them exists in Slack conversations or meeting recordings
- An agent can automatically keep issue trackers updated by mining existing information sources
- Key question: "Why doesn't a manager already have the list of updates they're asking for? Why hasn't an agent already done that research?"
