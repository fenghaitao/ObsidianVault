## Definition

Disagree and Commit is a leadership principle where you voice disagreement with a decision but then fully commit to executing it once the decision is made. Philip Su described this as a form of wisdom that comes with experience — recognizing that a team pulling together in a slightly suboptimal direction is better than a team split between the perfect direction and debate.

## Key Information

- Philip described this as "what Amazon means by disagree and commit"
- When young, Philip had idealism: "there was a right direction to go and two degrees off was wrong"
- With experience, he learned: "a whole team pulling 98% correct versus half the team pulling 100% correct and the other half debating which is correct — the team pulling together is going to get further"
- This is a form of wisdom he continues to develop over time
- It connects to the broader theme of valuing alignment and execution over perfect direction
