## Definition

The abundance mindset is the opposite of the scarcity mindset in social and business interactions. It's the belief that "this isn't your one shot — you'll have other opportunities," which enables you to show up calm, confident, and focused on building relationships rather than extracting value.

## Key Information

- Sam Lessin frames it as the antidote to the "one shot, one opportunity" mentality (from Eminem's "Lose Yourself")
- "This is part of the story. This is not the entire story."
- "Even if in your heart of hearts, you're like, 'This really is my one shot,' you kind of want to show up with the self-confidence and the calm of abundance"
- The scarcity mindset leads to transactional behavior: trying to convince everyone, collecting business cards, projecting desperation
- The abundance mindset leads to: giving more than you take, building relationships, being inclusive in conversations, leaving people wanting more
- Practical manifestation: at a high-stakes event like the Kleiner Perkins holiday party, don't treat it as your only chance — approach it with calm confidence
