## Definition

The Positively Delusional Mindset is the attitude that absolutely everything is possible with AI tools until proven otherwise. It's a key advantage non-technical vibe coders have over technical people who know "what shouldn't be possible."

## Key Information

- Core idea: "You have to come with this delusion that absolutely everything is possible until proven wrong."
- Non-technical people succeed because they don't know they "shouldn't" be able to build certain things
- Examples: building Chrome extensions, desktop apps, and video generation in Lovable before those were official features
- "People like me don't know that they are not supposed to be building XYZ and that's how we actually are able to build it."
- Must be balanced with self-awareness: "I didn't come into this... yes, I am delusional... but I'm also well aware that I need to be better"
- Failure example: Lazar spent a week trying to build an image generation app before OpenAI released the API — "I tried to do it when it was impossible"
- The balance: be delusional enough to try, self-aware enough to learn from the agent when something is truly impossible
