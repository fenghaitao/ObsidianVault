## Definition

Being on Distribution is the AI research term for observing what the model naturally tries to do and designing products around that behavior. It is the research equivalent of latent demand, applied to the model itself rather than to users.

## Key Information

- Boris: "In research, we call this being on distribution. You want to see what the model is trying to do"
- In product terms, latent demand is "the same exact concept, but applied to the model"
- The principle: instead of putting the model in a box with rigid workflows, "the product is the model. We want to expose it. We want to put the minimal scaffolding around it"
- Give the model the minimal set of tools, let it decide which tools to run and in what order
- This is how Claude Code was designed: "We inverted that. We said the product is the model"
- The traditional approach: design an application, then make the model do one component of it with specific APIs
- The Claude Code approach: give the model tools and a goal, let it figure out the rest
