## Definition

Product as the high order bit is the realization that product is the most important variable in a business's success. Getting product right makes everything else (finance, sales, marketing, recruiting) easier. This insight came to Matt McGinness after transitioning from COO to CPO.

## Key Information

- As COO, McGinness's job was to accept the product as-is and optimize everything around it (sales, marketing, recruiting, operations).
- After moving to CPO, he realized: "Product is the high order bit. If you get the product right, it fits in the market. Everything else gets easier."
- Setting the "high order bit to one" (product) cascades benefits through the entire organization.
- This is a contrast to the common view from outside product (especially sales, GTM) that product is just one function among many.
- "The joy of leading the product function is that I get to set the highest order bit in the business's success to one."
