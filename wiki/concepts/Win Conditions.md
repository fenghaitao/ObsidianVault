## Definition

Win Conditions are predetermined criteria that must be met before making a major career decision, such as dropping out of college to start a company. Jia Chen developed this framework for her own decision to leave Michigan State University.

## Key Information

- Jia did not recommend dropping out of college immediately — she recommended setting win conditions first
- Her win conditions for dropping out: having the background and network to raise investment, having the skills to land clients and survive in SF on her own money, having technical skills with the right people to build the product, and having distribution systems for marketing
- Her specific win conditions checklist: distribution system, technical ability to execute on the project, confidence in her network for both clients and investments
- Jia had already demonstrated all these conditions before dropping out: she had won 21 hackathons, built a social media following, and had a network that led to angel investment
- The framework is personalized: "depending on what kind of person you are or how much you know yourself"
