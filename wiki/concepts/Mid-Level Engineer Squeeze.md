## Definition

The mid-level engineer squeeze is the observation, identified by ThoughtWorks, that AI coding tools most benefit senior engineers (who have expertise to amplify) and junior engineers (who get accelerated onboarding), while mid-career engineers face the most disruption. They lack the deep expertise to leverage AI effectively and don't benefit from the onboarding acceleration that helps beginners.

## Key Information

- Identified by ThoughtWorks at an offsite with engineering VPs from various companies (mid-2026)
- **Senior engineers**: AI amplifies their existing expertise. "I can talk to the agent at a very high level. I can use sophisticated engineering language that I've mastered over the years, which they appear to know as well"
- **Junior engineers**: AI solves onboarding problems. Cloudflare and Shopify both hired ~1,000 interns in 2025 because AI reduced onboarding from a month to a week
- **Mid-level engineers**: "If you're mid-career, if you haven't made it to sort of super senior engineer yet, but you're not sort of new either, that's the group which ThoughtWorks resolved were probably in the most trouble"
- "They don't have that expertise to amplify and use with these tools. And it's not as beneficial — they've got all the boosts that the beginners were getting, they've got already"
- Simon's advice: "Lean into this stuff and figure out how do I help this make me better... The only universal skill is being able to roll with the changes"
- A parallel observation: AI is "coming at the middle" of many things — the middle of the product development process, the middle of seniority
