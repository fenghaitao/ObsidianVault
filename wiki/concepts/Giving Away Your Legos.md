## Definition

Giving Away Your Legos is a career growth framework created by Molly Graham. It describes the process by which, as a company scales, you must give away the responsibilities and skills you've mastered (your "Legos") and move on to the next shiny pile of Legos — new challenges and areas of growth.

## Key Principles

- As companies scale, roles change and responsibilities get redistributed; holding on to what you're good at limits your growth
- You have to grow as fast as your company is growing to truly take advantage of the scaling opportunity
- Embracing change is far better than fighting it — you almost cannot see what is around the corner
- When people get "layered" (someone brought in above them), Graham has seen many stories where it ended up being a great thing even though they couldn't see it at the time
- Holding on to things almost always leads to the worst version of yourself
- The Legos aren't even your Legos — they belong to the CEO and shareholders
- A key lesson: "step into the future and let the past go. See what you're going to learn."
- Sometimes you'll learn it's time to leave or that this isn't the right pile of Legos for you, but it'll take you somewhere worth exploring
