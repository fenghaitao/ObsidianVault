## Definition

Multimodal Audio AI refers to the next frontier of native speech-to-speech AI models that can understand and generate audio directly. Sherwin Wu identifies this as a hugely underrated domain, especially for enterprise and business settings, predicting significant improvements in the next 6-12 months.

## Key Information

- Sherwin Wu is particularly excited about audio improvements in multimodal models over the next 6-12 months
- "Native multimodal models" — speech-to-speech models that process audio directly, not via text transcription
- Audio is "hugely underrated" in enterprise and business settings
- "A lot of the world's business is done via audio" — meetings, calls, support lines, services, operations
- Everyone talks about coding (text), but we communicate in audio
- There is interesting work on new types of models and architectures specifically for multimodal audio
- The unlock: AI can now participate in the audio-native workflows that dominate business operations
