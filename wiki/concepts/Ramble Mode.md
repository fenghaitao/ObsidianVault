## Definition

Onboarding technique for AI agents, credited by [[Claire Vo]] to [[Hillary Gridley]] (the "Yappers API"): instead of filling out structured forms or fields, simply talk/ramble at length — by voice note if possible — to give the agent the highest-bandwidth possible context dump, which it then organizes itself.

## Key Information

- Contrasted with old-fashioned structured onboarding UX (Vo compares it to being forced to fill out rigid fields when signing up for ChatGPT-style products) — an [[Open Claw]] agent instead just asks open questions like "who am I? who are you?" and lets the user answer in free-form, unstructured language.
- Vo's specific recommendation: do first-time agent onboarding on your phone, using a voice note (not typed text) — ramble naturally about who you are, what you need, your constraints — and let the agent parse and structure it into its own memory/identity files.
- Named for the idea that free-associative talking ("yapping") is actually a more information-dense input channel for an LLM than structured data entry, since it captures nuance, tone, and implicit context a form can't.
