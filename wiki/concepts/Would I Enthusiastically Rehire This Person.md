## Definition

"Would I enthusiastically rehire this person for the same role?" is a binary talent evaluation question used at Stripe (and similarly at Netflix). It provides immediate clarity on whether someone is right for their current role. A "no" answer doesn't necessarily mean firing — it means action is needed: a conversation, a performance plan, a different role, or a search for a replacement.

## Key Information

- Used at Stripe as a standard talent evaluation question
- Netflix (under CTO Elizabeth Stone) operates similarly
- The question is clarifying because it's binary — you have an immediate, honest physical reaction
- If the answer is "no," it doesn't mean fire them immediately. It means you need to do something: talk to them, put them on a performance plan, move them to a different role, or begin a search.
- In quickly scaling businesses, it's natural that the leadership team's job will change over time. A CFO who was fine at an earlier stage may become the wrong fit; they might be better as a controller.
- If you're trying to build a truly successful company, the bar should be: only hire people who hit this bar enthusiastically.
- Talent and the environment you put talent in is everything — besides product-market fit and timing, everything else is human.
