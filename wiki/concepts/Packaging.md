## Definition

Packaging is Steve Huynh's term for the skill of communicating your experience, who you are, and what you're about in a coherent, efficient story during interviews. It is the key to performing well in behavioral interviews and avoiding being down-leveled.

## Key Information

- Packaging is about communicating your experience to the interviewer in an efficient, coherent way
- It doesn't have to be entertaining, but it should be efficient and tell a good story
- Effective packaging is about empathy — helping the interviewer answer the question "do I want to work with this person?"
- The stories you tell and the way you communicate betray your level — "SD1 stories" from someone applying for senior creates incongruity
- Even simple questions like "Tell me about yourself" should reflect your level and passions
- Steve's example: "My name is Steve Huynh, I have a passion for backend distributed systems, I love customer-facing products, I'm a sports fan, I love building teams and upleveling engineering culture"
- This communicates both level (team/org-level thinking) and personality (sports, passion)
