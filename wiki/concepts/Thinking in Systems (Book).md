## Definition

Thinking in Systems is a book by Donella Meadows that provides a framework for understanding how systems work. Matt McGinness recommends it as the best generic framework for systems thinking, noting that readers will extrapolate from it to every aspect of their lives.

## Key Information

- Author: Donella Meadows (died partway through writing; her fellow professors finished the manuscript)
- Described as "the best generic framework for thinking about how systems work"
- McGinness: "You will extrapolate from this book to every aspect of your life after you read it"
- One of three books McGinness recommends most often
