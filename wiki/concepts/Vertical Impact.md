## Definition

Vertical Impact is the direct impact on the business through the products and features you build — as opposed to how you build them. Coined by Zach Wilson as one of two directions of impact for staff engineers.

## Key Information

- Zach described creating impact in two directions: Vertical Impact (direct business impact) and Horizontal Impact (enabling team velocity)
- Vertical impact is the "what" — the direct contributions to business outcomes, revenue, user experience, or product metrics
- For Zach at Airbnb, this was delivering the pricing and availability data sets that powered Smart Pricing and had significant business impact
- For Ryan Peterman, this was making video processing at Instagram 94% cheaper
- The key insight: you need both vertical and horizontal impact to truly stand out as a staff engineer
- If you only have vertical impact, you're missing the multiplicative leverage that horizontal impact provides
- Combining both is what makes people say "wow, this person is really changing things"
