## Definition

Founder Market Fit is the alignment between a founder's background, skills, and experience with the market or product they are building. Jia Chen's angel investor was attracted to Sprint.dev because of her extensive hackathon background building a hackathon platform.

## Key Information

- When Jia pitched Sprint.dev to angel investors, she told them about her 21 hackathon wins and her hackathon app
- The angel investor liked the "product market fit or the founder market fit" — Jia's deep experience in hackathons made her a credible founder for a hackathon platform
- The concept demonstrates that investors care about whether the founder is uniquely qualified to solve the problem they are addressing
- Jia's founder-market fit was demonstrated through: 21 hackathon wins, 70% win rate, deep understanding of hackathon strategy, and existing social media audience of hackathon-interested followers
