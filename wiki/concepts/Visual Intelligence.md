## Definition
Visual Intelligence is Black Forest Labs' long-term vision and strategic direction, representing the progression from image generation toward systems that understand, simulate, and interact with the visual world in real time. It encompasses multimodal generation, world models, and ultimately physical AI for robotics and automation.

## Key Information
- BFL's articulated trajectory: image generation → multimodal generation → world models → physical AI
- Key capabilities: real-time generation (sub-second), interactive editing, multi-reference editing, joint video+audio generation
- Enables: rendering mock-ups as fast as you think, interactive visual engines for gaming/films, real-time guided generation
- World models component: train models to understand and simulate geometry, relationships, and world interactions
- Ultimate goal: physical AI — training agents in generative worlds to scale self-driving and automate manufacturing
- Each FLUX model release represents a step toward visual intelligence
