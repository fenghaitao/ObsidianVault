## Definition
"Leaving your priors behind" is the mindset Gergely Orosz identifies as critical for getting value from AI tools. It means being open to new ways of working without being constrained by past experience, while still retaining your engineering judgment. It's about having a low ego and willingness to learn rather than clinging to established workflows.

## Key Information
- Gergely Orosz: "just leave your priors behind, just have an open mind"
- Does NOT mean leaving your experience behind — engineering judgment still matters
- Teams getting the most value from AI: low ego, open to learning, open to leaving priors behind
- Experienced engineers were initially skeptical because AI tools were only "mildly useful" on existing codebases
- Understanding AI theory (transformers, attention, etc.) does not make you better at using the tools — practical experience does
- Simon Willison: after 2 years, still figuring out what works — there's no manual
- "Once you figure out how you can be more productive, if you're inside a team, it kind of breaks and you have to relearn again"
