## Definition

The principle that the story of a product or company is not a marketing afterthought — it is the fundamental reason for building the product. The story comes first, and then the product provides evidence and proof that the story is real. Articulated by Jeetu Patel.

## Key Information

- Articulated by Jeetu Patel
- "The storytelling is not a marketing exercise after you built the product. The story is why you build the product to make the story come real."
- "Make sure that the story is there first and then that story has evidence and proof based on the products that you're building."
- If the leader can't tell the story well enough, there's no way 20,000 sellers or customers can understand it
- The story is a "foundational exercise" of the company, not a marketing exercise
