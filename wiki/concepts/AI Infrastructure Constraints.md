## Definition

The three primary constraints holding back AI progress, as articulated by Jeetu Patel: (1) Infrastructure (not enough power, compute, and network bandwidth), (2) Trust deficit (people don't trust AI systems), and (3) Data gap (running out of human-generated training data). Cisco positions itself to address all three.

## Key Information

- Articulated by Jeetu Patel as Cisco's strategic framework for AI
- **Infrastructure constraint**: "There's just not enough power, compute, and network bandwidth in the world to go out and satiate the needs of AI." GPUs need to be connected together — if they aren't, you don't have AI at scale. What used to train on a single GPU now requires servers with 8 GPUs connected together.
- **Trust deficit**: "If people don't trust these systems, they're not going to use them." Hallucination is a feature for poetry but a bug for predictable systems. Models are non-deterministic and unpredictable.
- **Data gap**: Training has relied on human-generated data publicly available on the internet, but we're running out of it. New sources of training data are needed.
- Cisco's role: connecting GPUs at scale (networking infrastructure), providing safety and security for AI systems, and enabling new data pipelines
