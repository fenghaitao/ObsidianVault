## Definition

A strategic framework from Jason Lemkin distinguishing between GTM "plays" (timeless tactics that still work) and GTM "playbooks" (prescriptive, dated methodologies that no longer apply in the AI era). All the plays still work — outbound, webinars, podcasts, events — but the old playbooks are broken because growth has decelerated and demand has evaporated for legacy B2B.

## Key Information

- Plays (timeless tactics): outbound, webinars, podcasts, events, roadshows — all still work and are used by the fastest-growing AI companies
- Playbooks (dated methodologies): prescriptive GTM books, courses, and frameworks from the 2021 era that no longer deliver ROI
- "The plays all work. It's just the playbooks are broken because growth has decelerated so much that nothing seems to work."
- Why plays still work: AI-native companies like 11 Labs (doing roadshows), Vercel, and Replit are running the same plays but with hyper-PLG focus and massive demand
- Why playbooks are broken: the old B2B SaaS world had 3-5% of prospects in-market per year; AI companies have 50%+ in-market at once — completely different dynamics
- Advice for readers: "Read all these go-to-market and sales books and take two or three things from them... but run from the playbooks. Embrace the plays."
- Jason Lemkin was asked to write a foreword for a GTM book but couldn't because "as good as it was, it was dated. It wasn't current enough in AI."
