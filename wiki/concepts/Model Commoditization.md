## Definition

Model Commoditization is the thesis that AI model providers are competing toward commoditization, with prices dropping as competition intensifies. Malte Ubl argues this is the more likely future (vs. model labs capturing all value), and that it benefits AI engineers by allowing value to accrue to the application layer.

## Key Information

- Two futures presented by Malte Ubl:
  1. Model labs win: AI stays expensive, all value accrues to the model company, AI engineers become "forward deployed engineers" for the winner
  2. Model commoditization: prices drop, AI engineers and the application layer capture value
- Malte believes the second future is more likely
- Evidence: Cloud (Anthropic) is amazing, Codex (OpenAI) is amazing, Google will catch up
- Google's role: has the cheapest infrastructure on the cost side, will always decide to make it cheaper, keeping prices low
- In the commoditized world: AI engineers are the powerful ones, agents create the business value, the application layer is where real innovation happens
- Examples of application layer innovation: OpenClaw, next paradigms of AI engineering
