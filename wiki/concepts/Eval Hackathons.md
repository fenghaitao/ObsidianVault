## Definition
Eval Hackathons are community-driven events hosted on Kaggle's platform that channel participants' energy and expertise toward creating open-source AI evaluation benchmarks. They provide guardrails while giving enough creative freedom for participants to flourish.

## Key Information

### Purpose
- Channel community energy toward solving specific eval problems
- Democratize benchmark creation — everyone has something unique to contribute that AI labs cannot provide themselves
- All results are open source for the benefit of everyone, not just a small group

### Current Example
- Hackathon with the Google DeepMind AGI team, focused on building benchmarks around five of the ten cognitive faculties of AGI identified in a recent DeepMind paper
- Goal: give everyone the chance to contribute to AI research

### Key Challenges

**Tool Provision**: For a thousand globally distributed online participants, must provide:
- Dataset hosting infrastructure
- Access to AI models — participants from poorer backgrounds may not have money for API keys to state-of-the-art models
- Write-up tools so others can understand, learn from, and build on participants' work

**Human Judging**: AI agents are not good at judging innovation and creativity. Human expert review is still required, and alignment among experts is itself difficult and non-trivial.

### Philosophy
- With the right energy, investment, and time, communities can do a lot with very little
- Similar to how the world galvanized over three years to make GenAI happen — hackathons are a microcosm of that process
