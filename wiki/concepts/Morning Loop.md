## Definition
The Morning Loop is one of Chris Parsons' specialized Ralph loops. Every morning at 6am, it produces a full briefing of the day — processing all overnight emails, calendar events, and information needed for the day ahead.

## Key Information
- Runs every morning at 6am via cron
- Produces a full daily briefing: overnight emails, calendar, all information needed
- Drafts replies to emails that came in overnight (Parsons typically has 15-16 draft emails waiting in the morning)
- Parsons always edits the drafts before sending — the loop prepares, the human reviews and sends
- Part of Parsons' layered loop system: morning loop (daily planning), worker loop (continuous execution), heartbeat loop (periodic check-ins)
- Demonstrates the "everything is a loop" philosophy applied to personal productivity
