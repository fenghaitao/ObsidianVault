## Definition

"Fast beats right" is Claire Vo's work motto. It reflects her philosophy that speed of execution is a real competitive advantage, and that being first out of the gate matters more than being perfect.

## Key Information

- Claire Vo's work motto
- "I optimize for efficiency. I like to go fast. First out of the gate is a real advantage."
- This philosophy is particularly resonant in the AI era: "My speed ambition is met by the capacity of the tools, and it feels very fun."
- Contrasts with her personal motto: "Most people you work with won't be at your funeral" — speed at work, perspective at home
