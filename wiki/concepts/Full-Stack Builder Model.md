## Definition

The Full-Stack Builder (FSB) model is a product development approach pioneered at LinkedIn that empowers individuals to take an idea from concept to launch across traditionally siloed functions (engineering, design, product management), supported by AI agents and tools. The goal is to collapse organizational complexity back to craftsmanship.

## Key Information

- Created by Tomer Cohen at LinkedIn as a response to the observation that the product development process had become overly complex through micro-specialization and process bloat.
- The model has three pillars: platform (re-architecting code for AI), tools/agents (specialized AI agents), and culture (change management, incentives, training).
- Five human traits remain core to the builder: vision, empathy, communication, creativity, and judgment — everything else is being automated.
- LinkedIn created a formal career ladder with the title "full-stack builder" and a dedicated FSB team.
- The model draws an analogy to Navy SEALs: cross-trained operators assembled in small, nimble pods focused on a mission.
- AI-native startups already operate as full-stack builders by default; established companies must invest in platform re-architecture and culture change.
- Key learning: AI tools cannot work off-the-shelf at scale; significant customization and platform investment is required.
- The model is not about individuals working alone but about smaller, more flexible teams (pods) where members can flex across functions.
- Top talent has adopted the model fastest, reinforcing that AI amplifies already-strong performers.
