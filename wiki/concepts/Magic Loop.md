## Definition

The Magic Loop (Ethan Evans) is a career partnership with your manager: you do everything they need done and over-deliver, and in exchange they do the one critical thing — make sure you are rewarded for it.

## Key Information

- The explicit deal: "I'll do everything you need done; you make sure I'm rewarded for it" — aligned expectations rather than hoping
- It takes time to build: Ethan's VP spent ~2.5 years before he was willing to stake his reputation on Ethan's promotion
- Most managers want to promote strong performers (a better team makes their job easier), but they cannot do it for you — you still have to make yourself promotable
- Caveat: know the process and the dates; a well-meaning but naive manager who misses the promotion cycle cannot honor the deal (Ethan's early "star engineer" mishap)
