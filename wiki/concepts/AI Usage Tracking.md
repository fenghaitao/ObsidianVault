## Definition
AI Usage Tracking is the practice of systematically categorizing and analyzing one's own AI interactions to understand how AI is being used. Priscila Andre de Oliveira had Claude analyze 116 of her AI sessions, classifying them into categories: Comprehension (67%), Modification, Process, Review, Generation (2%), and Other. This revealed that her primary use of AI was comprehension, not generation — a finding that surprised even her.

## Key Information
- Priscila Andre de Oliveira had Claude analyze 116 sessions across 6 categories
- Categories: Comprehension, Modification, Process, Review, Generation, Other
- The analysis revealed 67% comprehension vs. only 2% code generation
- "This impressed even me" — the finding was surprising to the user herself
- The analysis led to the creation of the [[Catch Me Up Skill]] to optimize comprehension workflows
- Recommendation: track your own AI usage to understand how you are actually using AI and improve your workflows
- Sentry built an internal tool called Abacus specifically to track AI usage across the company
