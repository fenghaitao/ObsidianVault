## Definition

Superposition is a phenomenon in neural networks where a single neuron corresponds to dozens of concepts, and when activated together with other neurons, represents more sophisticated concepts. This is a key concept in mechanistic interpretability.

## Key Information

- As models get bigger, it's not just a single neuron that corresponds to a single concept
- "A single neuron might correspond to a dozen concepts"
- "If it's activated together with other neurons, this is called superposition. And together it represents this more sophisticated concept"
- This is a key finding in mechanistic interpretability research at Anthropic
- Contrasts with earlier models where individual neurons more cleanly mapped to individual concepts
- This is something Anthropic is "learning about all the time"
