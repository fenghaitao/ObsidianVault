## Definition

Misophonia is a disorder where specific sounds trigger strong negative emotional and physiological reactions. It is a real neurological condition, not simply annoyance.

## Key Information

- Lenny Rachitsky has misophonia — he is extremely bothered by certain sounds
- His worst trigger is people chewing with their mouth open (rated 9-10 out of 10 on the distress scale)
- He finds it awkward to ask people to stop because it's such a normal activity
- Other triggers: loud gas blowers (rated 7/10), baby crying (rated 8/10)
- Nails on a chalkboard is less bad for him (rated 5-6/10)
- The best sounds for him: his son Jude saying "Papa" (rated 1/10) and Jude laughing
- Lenny describes it as "a real thing in the brain" — a neurological condition, not a preference
