## Definition
NPU (Neural Processing Unit) Acceleration refers to hardware-accelerated AI inference using dedicated neural processing chips on mobile and IoT devices, delivering 3-10x performance improvement and significant energy savings compared to CPU/GPU-only inference.

## Key Information
- Delivers 3-10x performance improvement for real-time AI applications (AR, VR, camera feeds)
- Significantly reduces energy consumption compared to CPU/GPU inference
- Google's Lite RT framework has completed NPU integration with Qualcomm and MediaTek
- Additional NPU integration partners are in progress for other platforms
- Game-changer for real-time applications: AR, TTS (text-to-speech), camera filters, video calls
- Enables use cases that were previously impractical on battery-powered devices
- Lite RT supports CPU, GPU, and NPU as selectable accelerators
