## Definition

A Public Benefit Corporation (PBC) is a corporate structure that legally allows a company to optimize for public benefit rather than maximizing shareholder value. Anthropic was structured as a PBC from the beginning, unlike most startups which form as Delaware C-corps with a fiduciary duty to maximize shareholder returns.

## Key Information

- Most startups form as Delaware C-corps with a fiduciary duty to maximize shareholder value
- Anthropic deliberately chose a PBC structure from the beginning
- A PBC "allows you to legally say that maximizing shareholder value is not the overarching umbrella goal of this company"
- "You can optimize for public benefit"
- This is foundational to Anthropic's mission: "Make sure the transition to powerful AI goes well and is net beneficial for humanity"
- "We are happy to take a significant commercial hit for" — and they've done so repeatedly (e.g., not releasing Claude before ChatGPT for safety reasons)
- The PBC structure is "deep in our corporate structure itself" — it's not just marketing
- Amol: "It starts from there and ladders down from there"
