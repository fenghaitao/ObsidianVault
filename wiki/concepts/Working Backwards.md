## Definition

Amazon's product development methodology (associated with Jeff Bezos) of writing the product's press release before starting the project; echoed by Tony Fadell's own advice in *Build*.

## Key Information

- Fadell's version: "you should really make the press release before you before you more or less start the project" — forcing clarity on the small number of key features and the customer-facing story before building.
- Lenny notes Amazon has "a whole book on working backwards that goes really deep on this approach."
- Fadell playfully interrogates the name itself: he argues it isn't really "backwards" — it's analogous to writing a movie's script/treatment and knowing your characters before shooting; treating it as backwards is, in his view, a symptom of being too technology-led rather than customer-led.
- Connected to Fadell's marketing philosophy: because a press release can only carry three or four key features before it becomes "gobbledygook" for a customer, the discipline of writing it early forces hard scoping decisions (what to cut, what to focus on) rather than leaving marketing as an afterthought.
- Extended playfully in the episode into the idea of writing an exaggerated "infomercial" first (setting up a "virus of doubt," dramatizing the pain point) as a lens to find the most persuasive true story, then dialing it back to something honest.
