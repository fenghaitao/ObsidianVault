## Definition
The Engineering AI Maturity Model describes the progression of engineers in adopting AI into their workflow, from basic usage to optimizing the entire environment for agents. Intercom developed this model to help engineers understand where they are and grow toward being highly effective at using agents in their work.

## Key Information
- Developed by Intercom to guide engineers through AI adoption
- Recognizes that AI adoption is unevenly distributed across the organization
- Similar to maturity ratings described by VAEGI
- **Level 1**: Use Claude Code for everything
- **Level 2**: Automate your work, then move that automation into a skill
- **Level 3**: Get really good at writing skills
- **Level 4**: Write skills and approve skills (reviewing others' skills)
- **Level 5**: Optimize the environment for agents — software architecture, documentation, approaches, ways of working that make agents more effective
- Ultimate goal: engineers drive agents less and less, moving higher up the value chain
- The model helps people understand where they're at and grow toward mastery
