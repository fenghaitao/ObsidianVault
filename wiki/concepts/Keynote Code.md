## Definition
"Keynote code" is a term used in the AI engineering industry for high-quality AI-generated code — the opposite of "slop code." Priscila Andre de Oliveira advocates shipping keynote code, especially when working on production codebases that pay your salary. The term implies code that is well-structured, well-understood, and production-ready.

## Key Information
- Term used by the AI engineering industry to describe high-quality AI-generated code
- Contrasts with "slop code" — poorly understood, low-quality AI output
- Priscila Andre de Oliveira's advice: "Don't ship slop code into the codebase that pays your salary. Ship keynote code."
- Implies that the developer understands the code, has verified it, and can stand behind it
- Particularly important for large, complex production codebases (like Sentry with 100k+ organizations depending on it)
- Requires comprehension of AI output before shipping
