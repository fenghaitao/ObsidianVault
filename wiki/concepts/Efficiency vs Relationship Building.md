## Definition

Efficiency and relationship building are often in opposition — you're doing one or the other, not both. For efficiency-oriented people, dropping into relationship mode takes real intention. If it feels awkward, slow, and "low stim," that's probably a sign you're doing it right.

## Key Information

- "One of the things I think about is that efficiency and relationship building are often in opposition. We're doing one or the other."
- Dr. Becky learned this "the very, very hard way" since working more intensely in recent years
- Efficiency is reinforced at work: "A lot of us who can be very efficient and get things done, we can have a little morality about it. Like we listen to someone's story, we're like, 'Come on, get to the point.'"
- When someone is telling a story, "the whole point is that I want to tell you the whole story"
- "Dropping into relationship mode takes real intention. And it's hard especially if a big part of your life the efficiency is reinforced."
- "If it feels awkward and slow and I always call it with my kids, my best time just feels low stim. It just feels so low stim. I'm like, 'What's is this right? Am I doing this right?' That's probably a sign I am because not much is happening and I'm not being efficient with my time."
- The best people at work are people who are good at this: "The best people I've worked with that are most effective at work are the people that are really good at this — that you feel like really listen, really care, just like aren't rushed."
- Lenny: "I could totally see the power here."
