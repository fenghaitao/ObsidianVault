## Definition

Scale refers to both the technical scale of systems serving billions of users and the career concept of "scaling yourself" — achieving impact beyond what you can personally deliver. At Meta, scale is the defining characteristic of engineering work.

## Key Information

- At Meta, "I work on my small thing and then I hit go and then within six hours that is affecting 3 billion people and every single post that comes into Facebook"
- The scale of Meta's infrastructure abstracts away complexity: engineers don't need to understand caching layers, databases, or deployment — they just use the abstractions
- Career scale: "scaling yourself" is the key feedback for going from senior to staff — you need to achieve impact through others
- The "snowflake" problem: skills at Meta's scale are not always transferable — "I had never set up a simple caching layer, I'd never configured my own database"
- The paradox: Meta's scale makes individual contributions hugely impactful, but also makes engineers dependent on infrastructure they don't understand
- The solution: combine Meta-scale impact with curiosity about how the infrastructure actually works
