## Definition

Workplace loneliness is the growing trend of people feeling alienated from one another at work, even as they build businesses together. Rachel Lockett identifies it as a default state that leaders must actively overcome by creating environments where human connection is inevitable, not optional.

## Key Information

- The world is getting more lonely — there's research on this, and it's obvious in Rachel's coaching sessions
- People feel more alienated from one another, even as they work together
- Building businesses is an inherently human endeavor, but the "default state" is blind grind and loneliness
- Leaders must actively overcome this default by connecting with the people around them, leading healthier teams, and creating environments where connection is inevitable
- The payoff: leaders will have more fun and build better businesses because of it
- The AI boom and technology, while valuable, can exacerbate this trend if not balanced with intentional human connection
- Rachel's closing call to action: "Think of yourselves as leaders who bring humans together to self-actualize"
