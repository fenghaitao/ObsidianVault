## Definition

A Commitment Mechanism (Ryan L. Peterman's framing) is a self-imposed constraint — like publishing something publicly — that forces you to do higher-effort, higher-quality work than you would otherwise choose.

## Key Information

- Ryan recorded Toastmasters speeches but never reviewed them; once he knew the podcast would be posted, he poured over every word
- The obligation to publish is what turns casual practice into a tight feedback loop
- Public commitment also filters for quality: you build something you'd actually want an audience to hear or see
