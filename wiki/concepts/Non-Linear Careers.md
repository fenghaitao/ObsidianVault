## Definition

Non-linear careers describe career progression that advances through step-function jumps (ups) rather than smooth linear growth, with downs along the way.

## Key Information

- Jay: "life is not linear for anyone," but when ups happen they tend to be step-function improvements rather than linear growth
- Serendipity and luck play an outsized role (e.g., a "number of islands" LeetCode problem appearing in his Facebook onsite)
- Surrounding yourself with smart people and high-growth environments raises the odds of step-function advancement
