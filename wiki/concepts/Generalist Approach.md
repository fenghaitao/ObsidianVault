## Definition

The Generalist Approach is the career strategy of crossing over multiple disciplines rather than specializing deeply in one. Boris Cherny argues this will be the most rewarded approach in the AI era, as AI handles the specialized execution while humans handle the broader problem-solving.

## Key Information

- Boris: "Some of the most effective engineers that I work with every day... they cross over disciplines"
- On the Claude Code team, everyone codes regardless of role: PM, EM, designer, finance person, data scientist
- The strongest engineers are often hybrids: product + infrastructure, product + design, product + business sense, product + user research
- Boris: "A lot of the people that will be rewarded most over the next few years, they won't just be AI native and they don't just know how to use these tools really well, but also they're curious and they're generalists and they cross over multiple disciplines"
- They "can think about the broader problem they're solving rather than just engineering part of it"
- This is connected to the prediction that titles like "software engineer" will be replaced by "builder"
