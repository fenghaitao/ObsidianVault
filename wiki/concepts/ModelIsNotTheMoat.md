## Definition
"Model Is Not The Moat" is the strategic insight that for almost all companies building LLM applications, the underlying AI model should not be treated as a competitive advantage. Instead, companies should focus on their product expertise, existing user base, and domain-specific value — their "zone of genius."

## Key Information
- Core thesis of the strategic section of the 2024 AI Engineer Summit keynote
- Model providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.) must build for all customers; application builders should build what the providers are not building
- Sam Altman's "steamrolling" concept: model providers will inevitably build features that commoditize the capabilities of downstream applications
- Models should be treated like interchangeable SaaS products — quickly drop one when a competitor is clearly better (e.g., Sonnet 3.5 as a competitive alternative to GPT-4)
- High benchmark scores (87% on Spider SQL, 67% on HumanEval) do not automatically translate to a product or automation of real user requests
- An excellent LLM-powered application is first and foremost an excellent product — well-designed, solving a job to be done, enhancing user experience
- The alternative to model-as-moat: build things that generalize to smarter and faster models, maintain product quality under uncertainty, and enable continuous improvement
