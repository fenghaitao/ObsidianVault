## Definition

A conversation and networking strategy: the goal of most interactions is to leave people in a position where they think "Wow, that was a really interesting person. I'd love to hear from them again." The opposite is telling your entire life story so they never need to talk to you again.

## Key Information

- Sam Lessin: "The real goal is to leave people in a position where they're like, 'Wow, that was a really interesting person. I'd love to hear from them again or meet with them again.'"
- The best outcome: someone walks across the room and says, "Hey, you really should talk to [you]."
- This is partially about knowing when to excuse yourself gracefully as much as when to enter
- It connects to the abundance mindset: "This isn't your one shot. You'll have other opportunities."
- People don't want to feel like you're "just on them"
