## Definition
Feedback as Moat is the principle that in an AI-recursive world where anything can be cloned, the only defensible competitive advantage is the speed and quality of feedback loops that compound learning faster than competitors. Every feature must either generate a feedback signal or deliver on what signals have already taught.

## Key Information
- Articulated by Jess Grogan-Avignon of Accenture: "Feedback is not the option. Feedback is the only moat."
- In a world where AI codes AI, anything shipped can be cloned the minute it goes viral
- The day you ship is not the finish line — it's when the race begins
- Competitive advantage comes from how quickly you can compound and iterate, turning signals into value
- The race is against yourself: engineering your own competitive edge recursively and constantly
- Two criteria for every feature: (1) generate a feedback signal, or (2) deliver on what the signal has already taught you
- If a feature does neither, you're building something anyone can copy
- Requires a fundamental shift in engineering vision toward feedback-first design
- Connects to living memory: feedback signals are how you build the unique knowledge that competitors cannot replicate
- Those who survive won't be the earliest adopters — they'll be the ones who learn to learn
