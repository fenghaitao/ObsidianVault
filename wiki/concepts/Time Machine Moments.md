## Definition

[[Zevi Arnovitz]]'s term for moments when he has multiple AI agents working in parallel across unrelated projects and, with nothing left to actively do, experiences a disorienting sense of having glimpsed the near future early.

## Key Information

- Named example: prepping for the podcast with a Claude project, fully localizing his app [[Studymate]] from Hebrew to English in two days (work he estimates would take a dev team weeks), and building a personal site from no domain to live in about ninety minutes — all three running in parallel, leaving him with nothing to do but wait for the agents to finish.
- His description of the feeling: "I feel like I was in the future and I just stick my head out of the time machine and whoever's next to me... I'll just say we live in the future" — framed as a recurring, almost surreal marker of how much unsupervised agentic work has quietly become possible.
- Functions in the episode as an emotional/experiential complement to his more mechanical [[Slash Command Development Workflow]] — the workflow describes *how* he works with agents; this concept describes *what it feels like* when several of them are running unattended at once.
