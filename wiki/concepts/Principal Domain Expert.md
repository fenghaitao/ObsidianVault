## Definition
A Principal Domain Expert is a single individual within an AI organization who is ultimately accountable for the quality of AI performance and has the authority to make decisions about it. This role is a key organizational principle advocated by Chris Lovejoy to avoid consensus-by-committee and ensure clear ownership of AI quality.

## Key Information
- **Single point of accountability**: One person ultimately responsible for AI quality, avoiding "everybody's responsible so nobody's truly responsible"
- **Decision-making authority**: Has the final say on quality-related decisions, not just an advisory role
- **Deep understanding**: With clear responsibility, the principal domain expert can invest time in deeply understanding how the AI performs
- **Speed benefit**: Avoids the slowness of consensus-by-committee in quality decisions
- **Ownership, not consulting**: Must be in the room for decisions, not treated as a consultant brought in for advice
- **Failure mode**: Two senior clinicians with ambiguous authority, treated as advisory, led to slow progress and both leaving within 12-18 months — loss of critical context
- **Hiring recommendation**: Hire early, with relevant domain expertise as base plus breadth of adjacent skills
- **Evolution**: Should start as Oracle and evolve role as the organization and product scale
