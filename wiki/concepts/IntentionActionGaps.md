## Definition
Intention-action gaps are discrepancies between what a person intends to do (expressed in task managers, journals, commitments) and what they actually do (revealed by browser history, email activity, and other digital exhaust). They are one of the three top insights revealed by cross-source analysis of cognitive exhaust fumes.

## Key Information
- One of three top use cases for read-only AI identified by Šimon Podhajský
- Revealed by comparing stated intentions (task manager, journal) with actual behavior (browser history, email patterns)
- Example: the AI observer shows "I've been avoiding my most important project for 2 weeks" — an intention-action gap
- Cannot be detected from any single data source — requires cross-source analysis
- The weekly reflection feature in Fulan specifically surfaces these gaps as "tensions and conflicts that I need to think about"
