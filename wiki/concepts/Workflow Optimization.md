## Definition

Workflow Optimization is the practice of dialing in your development environment, key bindings, tooling, and processes to maximize coding speed and efficiency. It is one of the two ways (along with working extra hours) to create a "budget of time" for additional scope.

## Key Information

- Ryan Peterman described being "so dialed in" that he could "fire off a diff in like 10 minutes" and submitted up to 10 diffs a day during his most coding-heavy period
- Having "everything memorized" and knowing "exactly where everything is in the codebase" was key to speed
- The goal: "someone says we need to make that tweak, and you know exactly where is the exact line of code, and you can just fire it off"
- Evan's approach: building a personal hosted search engine (variant of Lucene) where he stored markdown notes — "anytime I solved a problem, I would write it down, and then I never needed to struggle to solve a problem twice"
- This "speeds things up significantly" and prevents re-solving the same problems
- Combined with code search, workflow optimization is one of the primary ways to work faster without working more hours
