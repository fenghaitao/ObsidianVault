## Definition
Foundation models are large-scale AI models trained on broad data that can be adapted to a wide range of downstream tasks. Understanding how they work at a basic level is the first step in becoming an AI engineer, as they form the backbone of modern AI applications.

## Key Information
- Understanding foundation models includes knowing why they sometimes produce unexpected output (hallucinations)
- Knowledge of how to customize models is essential: prompt engineering, RAG, fine-tuning
- Function calling enables models to access additional external systems
- No single model works for all use cases — model evaluation and selection is critical
- Available through managed services like Amazon Bedrock which provides access to models from multiple providers including Anthropic, AI21 Labs, Cohere, Meta, Mistral, Stability AI, and Amazon Titan
- The "Generative AI with LLMs" course on deeplearning.ai/Coursera is recommended for learning fundamentals
