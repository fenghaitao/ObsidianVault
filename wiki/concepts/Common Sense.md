## Definition

Common Sense is Boris Cherny's life motto: think from first principles rather than blindly following processes or momentum. If something smells weird, it's probably not a good idea.

## Key Information

- Boris's life motto when asked: "Use common sense"
- Boris: "A lot of the failures that I see in especially in a work environment is people just failing to use common sense"
- Common failures: following a process without thinking, doing a thing without thinking, working on a product that's not a good idea and just following momentum
- Boris: "The best results that I see are people thinking from first principles and just developing their own common sense"
- "If something smells weird then it's probably not a good idea"
- This is the single advice Boris gives to co-workers more than anything else
