## Definition
Pricing iteration is the practice of rapidly and continuously adjusting pricing models as products evolve. In AI, where features move from premium to standard in months, frequent pricing changes are a signal of growth rather than instability.

## Key Information
- 84% of AI company leaders agree that fast pricing adaptation is a key competitive advantage
- The first price is a hypothesis, not a commitment
- Hypergrowth companies (100%+ YoY growth) changed pricing 3+ times in the last 2 years
- Only 22% of low-growth companies changed pricing 3+ times — suggesting static products
- Frequent pricing change is a signal of growth, not a problem
- Key practices:
  - Prioritize speed over perfection — don't wait a year for the "right" price point
  - Talk to churning customers to distinguish product-market fit issues from pricing issues
  - Talk to upgrading customers to understand what they value
  - Run A/B tests on pricing to find optimum points
  - Continuously realign to value as features evolve
- Under-the-hood iteration: change what credits mean while keeping customer-facing plans stable
- Grandfather existing customers on old pricing while new users pay updated rates
- Billing infrastructure choice determines iteration speed — if every change costs 3-4 months of engineering, it's not worth it
