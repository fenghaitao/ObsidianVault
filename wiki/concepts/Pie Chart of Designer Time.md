## Definition

The allocation of a designer's time has fundamentally shifted in the AI era. Mocking and prototyping has dropped from 60-70% to 30-40%, while pairing with engineers has grown to 30-40%, and a new slice — hands-on implementation — has emerged. The traditional design activities (user research, prototyping, mocking) still exist but in smaller proportions.

## Key Information

- **Before (a few years ago)**: 60-70% mocking and prototyping, ~20% jamming with engineers/consulting, ~10% coordination meetings
- **Now**: 30-40% mocking/prototyping, 30-40% jamming/pairing directly with engineers, plus a new slice for hands-on implementation (writing code, polishing)
- Traditional activities (user research, prototyping, mocking) still happen but proportionally less
- The change is driven by engineers' ability to ship rapidly with AI tools
- Designers now have a "wider set of tools" — including coding tools like Claude Code
- Jenny describes doing "last mile stuff" — implementing polish and working closely with engineers to get features across the line
