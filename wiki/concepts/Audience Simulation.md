## Definition
Audience Simulation is a technique where AI personas representing different audience segments review content in parallel, providing diverse feedback. Chris Parsons uses this to improve his newsletter and other content by running simulated readers with different perspectives.

## Key Information
- Technique used by Chris Parsons for content quality improvement
- Creates personas from transcripts and survey responses from his website
- Runs multiple personas as parallel sub-agents, each reviewing content from their unique perspective
- After all personas review, the system synthesizes feedback into actionable improvements
- Parsons describes it as "super experimental, but actually really effective"
- Often surfaces insights the human hadn't considered — e.g., "there's a lot of ideas in this, Chris. You just need to focus on one main point"
- Can be used to improve not just content but the skills that generate content
- Different models can be used for different personas to get genuinely diverse perspectives (avoiding confirmation bias)
