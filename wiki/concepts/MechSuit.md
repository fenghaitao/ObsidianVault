## Definition
The "mech suit" is DHH's metaphor for AI-augmented development: AI doesn't turn you into a manager of agents, it amplifies your capabilities like a powered exoskeleton. You can do seven things at once, faster, while staying in control.

## Key Information
- Coined by DHH (David Heinemeier Hansson, creator of Ruby on Rails)
- Contrasts with the "managing agents" framing of AI-assisted development
- Key attributes: you can do multiple things at once, a lot faster, and you're in control
- DHH is not excited about managing agents — prefers the mech suit framing
- Gergely Orosz agrees: managing agents is more like a tech lead role without people problems
- The feedback loop with agents is much faster than managing people (minutes vs. 6 months)
- Different people have different flavors: Mitchell Hashimoto runs exactly two agents, others run many
