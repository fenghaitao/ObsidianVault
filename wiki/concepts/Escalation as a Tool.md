## Definition

Escalation as a Tool is a management philosophy championed by Mark Zuckerberg and shared by Molly Graham. It reframes escalation from being seen as a failure or tattling to being recognized as a legitimate tool for unblocking decisions when two parties disagree and neither has the authority to decide.

## Key Principles

- Most people think of escalation as "I'm A and B and I are disagreeing, so I'm going to go up to C and tell on B" — this is not what escalation is
- True escalation: "We disagree. Neither one of us has enough power to make this decision. Let's go to someone who does."
- The key practice: "As soon as you are stuck, escalate. Go together. Go make your case to whoever it is. Go together up."
- Escalation is unlocking — it saves time and prevents deadlock
- "It's not a muscle that's very comfortable for people, but it's so smart"
- Escalation is not a failure: "It's a tool. It's what management is for. They're there to unblock you. Let them unblock you."
- Mark Zuckerberg was "very adamant" about this at Facebook and brought it to CZI — he believed people don't escalate enough
