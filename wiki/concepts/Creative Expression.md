## Definition

Creative Expression is the practice of pursuing ideas and projects that you care about, rather than only doing what you're told. Rahul Pandey wishes he had cultivated more creative interests earlier in his career, and believes this is the path to outlier outcomes.

## Key Information

### The Creative Shift
- Rahul described his earlier career as being "way more passive" — methodical and studious with what he was given, but not creative or exploratory
- The shift happened when he started publishing Android tutorials on YouTube, building Android apps, and running webinars
- "It felt like someone lifted off the blindfolds and you can do anything"
- He felt "so much joy" when people commented on his tutorials

### Creative Expression as a Career Accelerator
- The YouTube tutorials gave him signal that there was something worth pursuing, which contributed to his decision to leave Meta and start a company
- The debug tool he built at Meta was a form of creative expression — he saw a problem and built a solution without being told to
- Creative expression builds the kind of unique stories and projects that make networking natural and effective

### The Regret
- "I wish I had tried harder to cultivate more creative interests earlier on or at least allocated time to those creative interests in a much more deliberate way"
- The default path is to do what the instructor or manager tells you to do — but that's not how you get outlier outcomes
- Creative expression is about doing "something unique based off of what you care about rather than just doing what everyone else around you is doing"
