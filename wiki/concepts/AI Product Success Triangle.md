## Definition

[[Kiriti Badam]]'s three-dimensional framework for what distinguishes companies that build AI products successfully: "great leaders, good culture, and technical progress." The framing insists "it's never always technical — every technology problem is a people problem first."

## Key Information

- **Leaders**: leaders often carry intuitions built over 10-15 years that must be actively relearned in the AI era, which requires vulnerability ("you're probably the dumbest person in the room, and you want to learn from everyone"). Badam cites the [[Rackspace]] CEO ([[Gajen Kandiah]]), who blocks 4-6am daily for "catching up with AI" (no meetings) plus weekend vibe-coding sessions, as an exemplar of hands-on leadership. Badam argues successful AI adoption is almost always top-down — engineers can't get buy-in from a leader who doesn't trust the technology or has misaligned expectations about what it can do.
- **Culture**: companies bringing AI into non-AI-native workflows (often competitive pressure, not core mission) risk a FOMO/"you'll be replaced" culture that makes subject-matter experts unwilling to share the expertise the AI team needs to calibrate the system. The alternative is a culture of empowerment — augmenting employees' own workflows (10x their output) rather than threatening their jobs.
- **Technical progress**: successful technical teams are "incredibly obsessed about understanding their workflows very well," choosing the right tool per sub-task (a deterministic-code component, an ML model component, an LLM component) rather than reflexively reaching for an agent — see [[Problem First Approach]]. They also understand that the AI development life cycle iterates differently (fast, low-blast-radius cycles that still capture enough data to estimate behavior) and prioritize building the right flywheel over being first to ship an agent.
- Explicitly framed as inseparable from [[Dan Shipper]]'s independently-made observation (referenced by Lenny mid-episode) that the single best predictor of AI-adoption success is whether the CEO personally uses [[ChatGPT]]/[[Claude]] daily.
