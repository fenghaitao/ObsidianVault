## Definition

AI as Amplifier is the principle that AI tools amplify whatever you put into them. Good input and good judgment produce great output; bad input and bad judgment produce garbage faster. AI is not a leveler — it widens the gap between skilled and unskilled practitioners.

## Key Information

- Core maxim: "AI, regardless of your background, is an amplifier. If you don't know what you're doing, you're just going to produce garbage faster."
- In the old world, "good enough was good enough" because producing anything was hard. AI has made good enough the baseline.
- The gap between good enough and world-class has widened dramatically because everyone can produce good enough
- Implication: the skill to optimize for is judgment, clarity, and taste — not coding speed
- "We won't be rewarded in the world of AI for faster raw output. We will be rewarded for better judgment."
- AI amplifies the gap between top performers and the rest: "The best humans will get superpowers from AI" (Jason Lemkin)
- Counter-example: if you give a super-powerful tool a wrong input, the output will suck correspondingly more

- Simon Wilson's experience: "Using coding agents well is taking every inch of my 25 years of experience as a software engineer." AI amplifies existing expertise — senior engineers get the most benefit because they can use sophisticated engineering language that agents understand.
- Simon: "I get great results out of these things, because they are amplifiers of existing skills and experience. I have 25 years of existing pre-AI experience, which I can now amplify, because I can talk to the agent at a very high level"
- The flip side: mid-level engineers who lack deep expertise to amplify are in the most trouble (the "mid-level engineer squeeze")
- AI also amplifies ambition: Simon's New Year's resolution was "take on more stuff and be more ambitious" because the tools make everything possible
- "I constantly throw tasks to AI that I don't think it'll be able to do, because every now and then it does it" — the amplifier effect means you can attempt things you never would have before
