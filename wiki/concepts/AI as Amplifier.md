## Definition

AI as Amplifier is Steve Huynh's framework for understanding AI's impact on software engineering: AI can take a non-coder from 0 to 1 (building a basic app), and a 1x engineer to 10x (amplifying existing skills), but cannot yet take someone from 0 to 10x (create a senior/staff engineer from nothing).

## Key Information

- AI can take a non-coder from 0 to 1 easily — products like Replit can build apps soup-to-nuts
- AI can amplify a 1x engineer to a 10x engineer
- AI cannot yet go from 0 to 10x — if the app doesn't work exactly as intended, or needs to scale to "Meta scale," AI can't do it
- AI is an amplifier, not a replacement — it's similar to how IDEs previously helped with refactoring and boilerplate code
- Steve expressed skepticism about Mark Zuckerberg's prediction that AI would produce mid-level engineer code by end of 2025 — if it were already true, the prediction would target senior/staff levels
- CEO-level predictions tend to undershoot timelines and overshoot abilities
- Historical parallels: Lombok reduced Java boilerplate, IDEs automated refactoring — AI is the next step in this progression
- The pipeline for junior engineers is "busted" right now, but seniors come from mid-level and juniors
