## Definition

"Kind and Candid" is a leadership and communication philosophy developed by Alexander Imbiricos and his co-founders at their startup. It combines kindness with direct honesty, similar to radical candor.

## Key Information

- The number one company value from Alexander Imbiricos's startup, which still guides him
- The founders realized they would often be "nice" but not candid — delaying difficult conversations
- They put "kind" and "candid" together deliberately: kindness without candor isn't actually kind
- The process: every 6 months they'd realize they weren't candid enough 6 months ago, and push themselves to be even more candid
- The core insight: "think of being candid as an act of kindness"
- This applies both to willing yourself to be candid and to how you frame candid feedback to others
- Similar to the concept of radical candor (from Kim Scott's book): "challenge directly but care deeply"
- Alexander Imbiricos considers this a beautiful way to summarize how to lead well
