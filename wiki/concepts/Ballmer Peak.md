## Definition

Concept (originating from an XKCD comic, named for Steve Ballmer) describing a narrow window of alcohol consumption at which a person is at peak insight/creativity, before performance collapses past that point. [[Michelle Rial]] describes an analogous "peak" for caffeine: a single-shot latte and a roughly two-hour window (ideally with an external deadline) as her own optimal condition for generating chart ideas, with too much coffee producing scattered, "erratic" over-stimulation instead.
