## Definition

The Ballmer Peak is a concept from the webcomic XKCD that describes the optimal level of alcohol (or other stimulants) at which one is most insightful and creative. Too little and you're insufficiently stimulated; too much and productivity collapses.

## Key Information

- Lenny referenced the Ballmer Peak in the context of coffee consumption for creativity
- Michelle Rial applies a similar concept to coffee: a single shot latte is the sweet spot for creativity
- Too much coffee leads to frantic, tangential thinking — "like a spider web on LSD"
- The concept was originally about alcohol (named after Steve Ballmer), but Lenny and Michelle adapt it to caffeine
- Michelle: "I feel like a genius, and I'm having a panic attack" — describing the too-much-coffee state
