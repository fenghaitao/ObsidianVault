## Definition

The problem-first approach is the principle that when building AI products, you should focus obsessively on the problem you're solving rather than the technology. It's easy to get seduced by AI capabilities and solution complexity, forgetting the actual problem. Starting small forces clarity on what problem you're actually solving.

## Key Information

- **Counter to**: Tool-first approach and hype-first approach — both common pitfalls in AI product development
- "In all this advancement of AI, one easy slippery slope is to keep thinking about complexities of the solution and forget the problem that you're trying to solve."
- Starting with a small, minimalistic version with high human control forces you to think about what problem you're solving and how to break it down into levels of autonomy.
- **80% of successful AI engineers and PMs spend their time understanding workflows** — not building fancy models or cool workflows. They're "in the weeds understanding customer behavior and data."
- "Be obsessed with your customers. Be obsessed with the problem. AI is just a tool."
- Building is cheap today; design and problem understanding are more expensive and more valuable.
- The approach resonates strongly with practitioners because it's obvious in principle but easy to forget in the hype.
