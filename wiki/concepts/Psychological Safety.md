## Definition

Psychological Safety in the context of innovation is the environment where it's okay to fail. Boris Cherny describes it as essential for innovation: "You can't force it. There's no road map for innovation. You just have to give people space."

## Key Information

- Boris: "It's okay if 80% of the ideas are bad"
- Counterbalanced by accountability: "You also have to hold them accountable a bit. So, if the idea is bad, you cut your losses, move on to the next idea"
- Key to Claude Code's origin: Boris had the space to experiment for months without knowing if it would work
- "I had no idea that this thing would be useful at all"
- Innovation requires: space, psychological safety, and accountability
- Connected to the "underfunding" principle: constraint + safety = innovation
