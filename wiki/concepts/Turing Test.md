## Definition

The Turing Test is a test of machine intelligence by indistinguishability from a human. Judea Pearl invokes it to demand definitions before accepting claims such as "we can now program consciousness."

## Key Information

- In the transcript the term is transcribed "touring test."
- Pearl insists one first define what is meant by intelligence: by the definition "plays good chess," machines already pass; by more demanding definitions (including passing the Turing test), they have not.
- "Faking intelligence is intelligence" holds only where faking is super-exponentially hard (as for correct answers to causal queries); LLMs currently bypass faking by "stealing" human knowledge from the internet.
