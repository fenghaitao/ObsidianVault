## Definition

"Most people you work with won't be at your funeral" is Claire Vo's personal life motto. It serves as a clarifying lens for deciding what truly matters and how much mental energy to give to work-related stress.

## Key Information

- Claire Vo's personal motto
- "I have had colleagues who have had family members pass away or they pass away and so few of the people that they worked with on a day-to-day basis are at the funeral."
- Used as a clarifying lens: "When I think about how much mental energy I'm going to give to somebody at work or how much I'm going to allow somebody to keep me up at night... I think like literally none of the people in this doc will be at my funeral."
- Who will be at her funeral? "My children."
- "My vibe coding tools will probably not be at my funeral, so I will not give them sleepless nights."
- Related quote from Lenny: "The only people that will remember that you stayed late at work are your kids."
- Claire's concern: "I hate that my kids see me hunched over an 18-inch MacBook Air all the time." OpenClaw helps her be more present.
