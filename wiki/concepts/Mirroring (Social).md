## Definition

Mirroring is the social skill of matching your vocabulary, dress, and behavior to the person or room you're in, not to imitate them but to meet them where they're at in a way that makes them feel relaxed and good.

## Key Information

- Sam Lessin on matching vocabulary: "You're going to meet a lot of different people. You want to make people feel good and welcome."
- Example: speaking to a university professor vs. a 12-year-old — match their level of vocabulary
- "The point is to meet people where they're at in a way that makes them feel relaxed and good, not try to like mirror them"
- On dress: anticipate how the room is going to be dressed; don't massively overdress or underdress
- On emojis in communication: if someone sends you a smiley face, you can respond with it — match the tone
- "It goes back to this matching vocabulary and language"
