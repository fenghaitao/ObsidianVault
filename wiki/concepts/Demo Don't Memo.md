## Definition

"Demo Don't Memo" was Lovable's 2025 motto: instead of writing documents and sitting in meetings to communicate ideas, build a working prototype in 30 minutes and hand it over. It's a philosophy of replacing written communication with functional demonstrations.

## Key Information

- Lovable's 2025 motto: "Demo Don't Memo"
- Core idea: instead of writing PRDs, memos, and sitting in meetings trying to communicate your vision, build a prototype in Lovable in 30 minutes
- Enables non-technical people (marketers, sales, HR) to communicate ideas directly through functional prototypes
- Lazar's example: built a prototype in 4 hours that took engineers 6-7 months to replicate in production; describing it would have taken 1-2 weeks
- "Instead of writing all these documents and talking and sitting on meetings with your engineers trying to get your vision as a marketer or a sales guy in the office across, go into Lovable and build the prototype in 30 minutes and just hand it over"
- Even when the prototype can't go to production (regulatory, enterprise), it's vastly more efficient than written communication
