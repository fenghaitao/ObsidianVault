## Definition

"Demo don't memo" was [[Lovable]]'s internal company motto for 2025, per [[Lazar]]: instead of writing documents, holding meetings, and trying to convey a product vision in words, build a working prototype (often in Lovable itself) in minutes to hours and hand it over directly.

## Key Information

- Cited example: instead of a marketer or salesperson spending meetings trying to get an engineering vision across in writing, they go into Lovable and build a working prototype in about 30 minutes.
- Framed as broadly applicable beyond Lovable: Lazar notes many regulated-industry teams (healthcare, finance) can't push AI-built software to production, but building for the sake of prototyping/demoing is still one of the best use cases — marketers and non-engineers no longer need to wait on engineers to see if an idea works.
- Consistent with Lazar's broader "clarity through parallel prototyping" approach: building fast, disposable versions to clarify direction rather than spending that time writing up specs.
