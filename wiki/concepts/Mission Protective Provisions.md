## Definition

Legal/corporate-charter provisions, formalized by [[Eric Ries]] in *[[Incorruptible (book)|Incorruptible]]*, that structurally protect a company's mission — such as [[Public Benefit Corporation]] status — written into a company's founding documents from day one.

## Key Information

- [[Anthropic]] wrote Public Benefit Corporation status and the right to later establish a mission-oversight trust into its charter from its founding, before later standing up the [[Long-Term Benefit Trust]] at its Series C.
- [[Virgil]], a law firm Ries helped start, offers implementation help with these provisions for founders whose own lawyers are reluctant to assist — traditional hourly billing can create a disincentive to help founders quickly adopt them.
