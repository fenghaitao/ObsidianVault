## Definition
Relationship decay is the deterioration of personal and professional relationships when they are not actively maintained, detectable through cross-source analysis of digital exhaust — for example, noticing who you haven't communicated with recently despite shared interests or past interactions. It is one of the three top insights revealed by read-only AI systems.

## Key Information
- One of three top use cases for read-only AI identified by Šimon Podhajský
- Detected by cross-referencing communication patterns (email, messaging) with contact profiles (CRM) and shared interests (reading history)
- Example: Fulan identifies people in your network you haven't talked to yet about articles you've been reading — even finding the author of an article who is in your network
- The weekly reflection specifically covers "commitments and relationships, which is mostly notable by its omissions"
- Cannot be detected from any single data source — requires cross-source analysis
- All suggestions are read-only: nothing is sent, nothing is scheduled — just suggestions for the human to act on
