## Definition

Emotional Intelligence in the AI Era is the thesis that human-to-human skills — understanding human nature, emotional dynamics, and relationship layers — will become the most valuable and future-proof capabilities as AI automates deterministic work.

## Key Information

- Core thesis: "Emotional intelligence for sure. Just understanding human nature, real life stuff."
- In a world of AI-generated everything (fake images, posts, profiles, videos), humans will crave authentic human interaction
- "Anything human to human is going to be a big thing to scale up on"
- Deterministic work (clear X → Y) gets eaten by AI; human dynamics and relationships remain
- AI can't write good comedy: "AI is never going to be able to write a good joke. Never, never, never."
- AI can translate languages but can't understand what's funny
- AI amplifies great writers (7 books/year instead of 1) but replaces average writers and translators
- Design is emotional decision-making: "We're training these tools to make better technical decisions. I don't think we will train them just yet to make better emotional decisions."
- Copywriting will be a highly valued skill because people can detect AI-written copy in ~3 seconds
- "I don't even read AI output anymore. I want that raw human experience."
