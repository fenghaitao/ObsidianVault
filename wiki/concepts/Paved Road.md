## Definition

Paved Road is a platform engineering concept at Airbnb that defines the standard, supported way to build data pipelines — ensuring compatibility with the platform, high quality, and maintainability. Pipelines not on the "paved road" are considered non-standard, harder to maintain, and more prone to issues.

## Key Information

- At Airbnb, the Paved Road was the "way you're supposed to build a pipeline that is compatible with the platform, high quality"
- Zach's first major project at Airbnb was migrating the Pricing and Availability pipeline onto the Paved Road
- The project was originally scheduled to take four quarters (a full year); Zach completed it in one quarter
- The pipeline was described as "one of the most deadly freaking pipelines I have ever seen" — it was a mess before Zach's migration
- Successfully completing this migration quickly was Zach's first big win at Airbnb and established his internal brand as someone who could handle risky, complex infrastructure work
- The Paved Road concept represents the broader platform engineering philosophy of creating supported, standardized paths that reduce technical debt and operational burden
