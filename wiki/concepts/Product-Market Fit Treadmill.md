## Definition

The Product-Market Fit Treadmill describes the new reality for AI companies where product-market fit must be recaptured every ~3 months because both LLM capabilities and consumer expectations evolve that rapidly. It replaces the traditional model where PMF was a one-time achievement followed by years of scaling.

## Key Information

- Elena Verna: "Every 3 months, I feel like we have to recapture our product market fit — and not just on the same technology and with the same customers. Both pieces of the equation change every 3 months."
- Two drivers: (1) LLM providers release step-function capability changes every ~3 months, and (2) consumer expectations change faster than ever before
- Companies must build ahead of LLM releases to have functionality ready when the model catches up
- The team that finds PMF is different from the team that scales — yet AI companies need teams capable of doing both continuously
- AI companies are constantly focused on recapturing pioneers (early adopters excited by capabilities) rather than reaching adjacent users/latent majority
- Elena worries this creates a gap: pioneers get pulled too far into capabilities, and the latent majority may never catch up
- Even a $200M ARR company with 10% month-over-month growth is at risk of losing PMF in 3 months if it stops innovating
- Traditional scaling (marketing, sales, blitz growth) gets interrupted by the need to reinvent every few months
