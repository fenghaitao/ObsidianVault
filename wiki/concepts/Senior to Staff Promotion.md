## Definition

The senior-to-staff promotion is the transition where an engineer shifts from solving problems that others have identified to independently identifying, pitching, and solving problems end-to-end.

## Key Information

- Laurent: senior-to-staff is a bigger mindset shift than staff-to-senior-staff (and principal is simply the same behavior at higher scope).
- As a senior you typically work on other people's problems; as staff you must own the whole cycle: find a problem → pitch it → solve it → document it.
- To find problems: "channel your inner frustration" — log friction, use the product from first principles, and ask where you would fail or get stuck if you were very tired.
- Identify families of problems, rank them, then solve; this works for any role, not just developer productivity.
- Laurent coached Ryan L. Peterman out of being stuck as a senior (IC5) using this framing.
