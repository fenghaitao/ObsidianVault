## Definition

Notebook Channels are internal Slack channels at Anthropic where every employee can share their thoughts, provocative ideas, and updates — functioning like an internal Twitter feed. They serve as a mechanism for openness, scaling leadership beliefs, and providing training data for Claude.

## Key Information

- Everyone at Anthropic has a notebook channel — not just leadership
- Function: share internal thoughts, keep people updated on what's working, share provocative ideas
- Employees can join any notebook channel, including those of researchers and leadership
- Used for scaling leadership beliefs: as the org grows, leaders post about principles (e.g., "the importance of being comfortable leaving money on the table") so new hires absorb the culture
- "If you have a channel which is like here's my top of mind and you say these things — now all the new engineers on growth who've joined have seen that"
- Helps avoid "drift" in strategy and perception as the company adds many new people
- Encourages public disagreement: employees can challenge Dario in his notebook channel, and debates happen there
- "That sort of thing where it's encouraged — go to leadership and disagree with them, challenge them publicly"
- Also serves as training data for Claude: certain documents in onboarding warn "before editing, check with this person because this is a document Claude references"
- "More and more these types of things of how does the growth team think about this — you're arming Claude with that context"
- Amol: "This is something that every company's going to have to start doing — sharing their thoughts in a structured way so that all these agents have what they need to know"
