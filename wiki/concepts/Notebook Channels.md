## Definition

Internal Slack practice at [[Anthropic]] where every employee, including [[Dario Amodei]], maintains a running "notebook channel" — effectively a personal internal Twitter-style feed of top-of-mind thoughts, updates, and provocative ideas — visible to the rest of the company.

## Key Information

- Explicit purposes, per [[Amol]]: (1) organizational transparency/openness, letting anyone browse leadership's or colleagues' current thinking across any function (e.g., research); (2) a scaling mechanism for culture — as headcount grows quickly, notebook posts model desired behaviors and principles faster and more concretely than periodic all-hands meetings can, reducing "drift" in strategy and norms among new hires.
- Culture of open disagreement is explicitly encouraged: Amol describes an incident where an employee publicly challenged something Dario said at an all-hands directly in Dario's notebook channel, sparking a substantive debate — treated as a healthy sign of trust rather than a problem.
- Doubles as structured data for Claude: some onboarding documents and notebook content are explicitly written with the awareness that Claude will reference them (e.g., HR documents flagged "check with this person before editing, since Claude references this"), extending the transparency practice into a way of "arming" internal AI agents with organizational context.
