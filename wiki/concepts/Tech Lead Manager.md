## Definition

A Tech Lead Manager (TLM) is a hybrid role combining technical leadership with people management responsibilities. Ricky briefly served as a TLM at Google, managing a small team of four reports while continuing to contribute technically.

## Key Information

### Ricky's Motivation
- Ricky wanted to try management because he already found fulfillment in helping people grow and finding projects for others, even as an IC
- He enjoyed mentoring interns and watching them succeed: "it's almost like having a kid and watching them grow"
- His team was supportive of him trying the role

### The Awkwardness of Being a Young Manager
- Ricky felt immature at times — being in meetings with "a bunch of managers" who were older and more experienced felt awkward
- Social situations with reports outside work (e.g., running into them at a music festival while drunk) created unexpected discomfort
- He found the transition was less structured than expected: "I thought there'd be more of like a textbook on how to be a great manager, but I feel like a lot of people just kind of get thrown in"

### TL vs TLM Trade-offs
- As a TL, you get "all the fun parts" — helping people grow, guiding projects, mentoring
- As a manager, you add: difficult feedback conversations, writing performance reviews, attending calibration sessions, and other administrative overhead
- Ricky found management more rewarding when reports got promoted, but also more stressful

### Regret and Timing
- Ricky wished he had become a manager later in his career
- He only took the role because his team had 20 new hires and he wanted a formal role to limit his responsibilities to 4 people rather than informally helping all 20
- He would have preferred staying a senior IC for "a couple more years" to enjoy lower stress and more flexibility

### Rahul's TLM Experience at Meta
- Rahul served as a TLM at Meta as a transitional role between IC and full-time management
- He found the TLM role difficult because it's unclear whether you're being evaluated on IC work or people leadership
- TLM is "not a good place to be in terms of long-term sustainability"
- He described feeling that at the end of the day, he wondered "what did I actually achieve today?" — his time was spent in meetings, one-on-ones, and roadmap planning rather than coding
- If you approach your job with the mindset of "let me just crank out code and ship an experiment," you won't be satisfied with how your day or week goes as a TLM
- He ended up switching teams after the TLM experience
- TLM is best as a transitory role for deciding whether to go full-time manager or full-time IC, not as a long-term position
- He observed that he had never heard anyone say TLM is a great long-term idea — you become a "jack of all trades, master of none"
