## Definition

Social as Organic Marketing is the shift in organic growth strategy from SEO/search engine optimization to social media platforms (LinkedIn, X/Twitter, TikTok, Instagram). In the AI era, organic marketing is about what your CEO, team, users, and creators are posting on social platforms.

## Key Information

- Elena Verna: "If you asked me five years ago what organic marketing strategy is, I would have said SEO. Now it's all about social."
- Key channels for B2B: X (Twitter) and LinkedIn — where B2B audiences are
- Key channels for consumer: Instagram and TikTok
- Components: founder socials, employee socials, user-generated content, influencer/creator marketing
- Content must show personality and humanity — corporate-scrubbed AI-generated copy doesn't work
- People want to rally behind a team they want to win, which requires vulnerability and authenticity
- Influencer marketing is 10x bigger than paid social for Lovable
- Social works especially well for AI products because a 10-second video demo communicates the value proposition better than text
- "Nobody knows what vibe coding is, but you watch 10 seconds of it and you go — that's new, let me go give it a try"
