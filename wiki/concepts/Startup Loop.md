## Definition
The Startup Loop is an ambitious Ralph loop skill created by Chris Parsons that guides a product through an entire startup framework. Based on Ash Maurya's Running Lean and Scaling Lean methodologies, it aims to have AI figure out the next most important thing to work on and execute it in a continuous loop.

## Key Information
- Created by Chris Parsons as an experimental skill
- Based on Ash Maurya's startup frameworks (Running Lean, Scaling Lean)
- Encodes the entire startup methodology into an AI agent loop
- Designed to figure out what the next most important thing to work on is, then do it
- Has a big outer loop that says "what's the next most important thing to do? Let's do that"
- Parsons admits "it doesn't work" yet but is "getting somewhere"
- Produced an unsolicited investor memo deck for AirSkills — market sizing, problem statements, pricing analysis, competitive landscape — without being asked
- Parsons had to tell it "Hang on a minute. There's some serious thinking you need to do before you go to that"
- Represents the extreme end of the "everything is a loop" philosophy — applying Ralph loops to company-level strategy
