## Definition

Jeetu Patel's belief that innovation is not a function of company size but a deliberate choice made every hour of every day. Large companies can innovate just as much as small ones — they just need to choose to be creative.

## Key Information

- Articulated by Jeetu Patel
- "Innovation is a choice. I always find it interesting when people say, 'Well, you're a large company, you can't innovate. You're a small company, you can innovate.' It's like, no, it's just a choice."
- "Every day you come into work and you can choose to be thinking about being creative or you can choose to not be creative. It's a binary choice you can make every hour. Every minute of every day."
- Aligns with Chuck Robbins' vision: "I want Cisco not just to be an iconic company. I want Cisco to also be an iconic and innovative company."
- You must innovate "with the set of constraints that you are dealt with" — every company has constraints
