## Definition
Value-based pricing is a pricing approach that sets prices based on what the customer perceives the product to do for them, rather than what the product technically does. It is step one of Stripe's five-step AI pricing framework.

## Key Information
- Focuses on customer-perceived value, not technical product features
- Example: Gamma users care about decks/presentations produced, not API calls made
- 53% of hypergrowth companies offer clear value-based pricing vs only 26% of low-growth peers
- Four value frameworks for AI products:
  1. **Automation**: saving time and cost (e.g., automating workflows)
  2. **Augmentation**: same number of people producing better quality output (e.g., better campaigns, images)
  3. **Enhanced Service**: access to proprietary data or software not otherwise available (e.g., Stripe's fraud detection from payment volume)
  4. **Improved Results**: direct bottom-line impact (e.g., Intercom pricing by tickets resolved without humans)
- Understanding which framework applies determines the price you can command in the market
- Must be combined with the right charge metric (Step 2) to translate value into billable units
