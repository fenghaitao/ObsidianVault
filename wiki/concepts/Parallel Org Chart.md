## Definition

[[Dan Shipper]]'s term for the "everyone has their own personal AI agent" model of organizing work — each person's agent a small reflection of its owner, forming a shadow organizational structure that parallels the human org chart. He compares it to a daemon from *The Golden Compass*.

## Key Information

- Represented Dan's initial belief, formed after adopting [[Open Claw]] broadly at [[Every]], that the future of work was individual, personally-tailored agents rather than centralized ones.
- He later reversed this view: personal agents like Open Claw require real ongoing maintenance ("gardening") that most ordinary, non-technical users can't sustain — see [[Automation Is A Lie]].
- His revised prediction instead splits work into a company-wide shared "super agent" (accessed mostly via [[Slack]]) plus individual work happening inside computer-use surfaces like [[Codex]] or [[Claude Cowork]] — effectively replacing the parallel-org-chart model with centralized agent ownership plus a dedicated maintainer role ([[Forward Deployed Engineer]]).
