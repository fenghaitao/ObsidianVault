## Definition

One of [[Eric Ries]]'s named governance frameworks in *[[Incorruptible (book)|Incorruptible]]*: a designated role or body whose specific job is to protect an organization's mission against [[Financial Gravity]], distinct from ordinary management or board oversight.

## Key Information

- Concretely embodied in Patagonia's perpetual-purpose-trust structure by the "purpose protector" role, which can sue the trust's own trustees if they deviate from the mission — a checks-and-balances structure Ries compares to a government.
- Also embodied in Anthropic's [[Long-Term Benefit Trust]], which Ries credits with protecting the company's ability to refuse dangerous model releases and turn down lucrative but mission-incompatible contracts against investor pressure to prioritize growth.
