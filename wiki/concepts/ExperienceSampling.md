## Definition
Experience sampling is a metric collection method that captures developer experience data at the moment of activity, such as adding a field to a PR form asking whether AI was used. It complements telemetry and survey data in the DXAI Measurement Framework.

## Key Information
- One of three metric types in DX's measurement approach alongside telemetry (API data) and self-reported surveys
- Example: adding a field to a PR form like "I used AI to generate this PR" or "I enjoyed using AI to generate this PR"
- Addresses limitations of pure telemetry: accept vs. suggest metrics are unreliable because engineers may rewrite every suggested line after accepting
- Provides context that telemetry alone cannot capture
- Part of a multi-method approach to measuring AI impact accurately
