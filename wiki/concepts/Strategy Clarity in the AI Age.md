## Definition

Strategy clarity becomes exponentially more important in the AI age because when building happens faster, mistakes compound faster. Clear strategy anchors empowered teams and enables them to move at high velocity without building the wrong things.

## Key Information

- "When decisions are happening faster, building is happening faster. The flip side of that is mistakes compound faster as well"
- Strategy clarity is the mechanism for letting people go and run at as fast a velocity as they're capable of
- A corpus of shared beliefs, updated on a fast clip, enables teams to build the right stuff — not just whatever AI suggests
- Contrary to "PRDs are dead" takes, being very clear about strategy is now the most important thing because once you have clarity, you can fire off 100 agents to build and launch
- "That clarity of what you believe, what are the most important problems to invest time and energy into, where we want to invest compute and dollars — that is the mechanism for letting people go and run"
- Jessica Fain: "I am not in the Twitterverse. I find it exhausting partially because takes like 'PRDs are dead' or 'software is dead' is really dumb"
