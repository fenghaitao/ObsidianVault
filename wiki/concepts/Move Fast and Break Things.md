## Definition

Move Fast and Break Things is Facebook's early engineering motto, which Michael Novati reads as an instruction to break norms and think from first principles rather than to recklessly break systems.

## Key Information

- Michael was "the move fast and break things person at the time" and still likes the framing
- He interprets "break things" as breaking established norms and thinking differently/from scratch — closer to first-principles thinking than causing havoc
- The phrase's public removal was partly about Facebook not wanting to signal reckless breakage to the outside world
- As a company grows and matures, the calculus changes: even a small bug can have tens of millions of dollars of impact, so stability becomes a first-class concern
