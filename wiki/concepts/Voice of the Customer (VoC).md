## Definition

Voice of the Customer (VoC) is a business intelligence practice that aggregates and analyzes customer interaction data — including call reasons, sentiment, and resolution outcomes — to derive actionable insights for management. In contact center Voice AI, structured data from automated call processing feeds directly into VoC aggregation for dashboards and strategic decision-making.

## Key Information

- **Data Sources**: Structured JSON output from contact center Voice AI pipelines, including intent classification, sentiment analysis, and resolution status
- **Quality Improvement**: AI-driven categorization moves VoC data from subjective/operator-dependent to standardized/uniform, creating a highly consistent dataset
- **Downstream Uses**:
  - Management dashboards for operational visibility
  - Automated flagging of candidates for new FAQ entries
  - [[Predictive Staffing]] — time-series analytics on intent data to forecast call volume spikes
  - Identifying systemic customer pain points for product/service improvement
- **Shift in Enterprise Focus**: Mechanizing summarization and data extraction shifts the enterprise from merely handling calls to actually analyzing the voice of the customer for business growth
