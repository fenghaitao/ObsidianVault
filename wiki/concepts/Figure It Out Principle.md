## Definition

Leadership response coined by [[Eric Ries]] from a [[Cloudflare]] story: when a junior employee raises an idea that conflicts with short-term financial interest, a mission-driven leader says "let's figure it out" instead of shutting the idea down.

## Key Information

- Origin story: a junior [[Cloudflare]] engineer asked why the company charged for SSL/web encryption if "a better internet" should mean an encrypted internet by default. CEO [[Matthew Prince]] responded "let's figure it out" rather than dismissing the idea as a distraction from strategy or revenue.
- The team hand-rolled custom cryptographic software and renegotiated certificate-authority deals to make free universal SSL sustainable, rather than either abandoning the idea or bankrupting the company.
- Short-term, the free-SSL rollout depressed premium-tier conversion (reported to Cloudflare's board), but the company stuck with it; the resulting top-of-funnel growth increased by an order of magnitude, and Ries credits the resulting trust as central to Cloudflare becoming a $70 billion company and to today's default expectation of an encrypted internet.
- Cited by Ries as a concrete example of "[[Harder Is Easier]]": the harder, more expensive-looking short-term choice was the one that actually compounded into durable value.
