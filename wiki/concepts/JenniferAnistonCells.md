## Definition
Jennifer Aniston Cells are a neuroscience concept describing a small number of neurons that encode for a specific concept (person, place, or thing) and activate invariantly across all modalities — whether you hear the name, see a picture, watch a video, or hear the voice. This concept inspired the design of omnimodal embedding models like Gemini Embeddings 2.

## Key Information
- **Modality invariance**: The same neurons activate regardless of whether the concept is presented as text, image, video, or audio
- **Robustness**: These cells are very robust — they consistently activate for their specific concept across different presentations
- **Function**: Used by the brain for very fast retrieval, recognition, and comparison
- **AI inspiration**: Google DeepMind designed Gemini Embeddings 2 to achieve the same property — a single vector that represents a concept across text, video, audio, and PDFs
- **Training approach**: Uses contrastive losses to learn modality-invariant concept representations
- **Origin**: Discovered in neuroscience research on concept-selective neurons
