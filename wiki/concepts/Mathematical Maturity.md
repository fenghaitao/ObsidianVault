## Definition
Mathematical maturity is the ability to read and deeply understand research-level ML papers (and implement their ideas), which Vlad Feinberg identifies as a table-stakes skill for frontier-lab candidates.
## Key Information
- Without it, a back-end engineer's first gap in research is the literature: building a solid understanding of past work and learning to traverse a topic's citation tree.
- It includes judging which papers are high-value and whether a paper is worth reading without fully reading it.
- Prerequisites can include machine-learning background and domain-specific math and coursework.
- The bar: take an idea from a paper and implement it yourself, then iterate and improve it in the Google/DeepMind setting.
- Concrete evidence: completing the hand-written Scaling Book exercises signals this maturity.
