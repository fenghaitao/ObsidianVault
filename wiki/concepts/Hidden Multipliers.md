## Definition

Hidden Multipliers is Jason Cohen's book about small decisions and changes that have outsized, disproportionate impact on revenue, profit, and company success. The concept is that certain levers, when pulled correctly, multiply outcomes far beyond the effort invested.

## Core Idea

Small changes → huge impact. Examples:
- Moving cancellation rate from 5% to 4% (seems small, dramatically changes the company's maximum size)
- Focusing on onboarding over later-stage retention (front-loaded leverage)
- Repositioning a product from "save money" to "drive growth" (8x price increase for the same product)

## Book Details

- Title: Hidden Multipliers (note the plural "s" — there are many)
- Available for pre-order at hiddenmultipliers.com
- Jason Cohen's first "real" book (after 20 years of free blogging)
- Contains the kind of frameworks and insights discussed in the podcast episode, but with more depth, examples, and specific actions
- All of Cohen's blog content remains free with no ads or courses; the book is his only paid product
