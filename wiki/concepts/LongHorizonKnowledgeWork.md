# Long Horizon Knowledge Work

## Definition

The capability of AI agents to perform complex knowledge work tasks over extended time periods. Poolside envisions agents progressing from completing tasks in hours to completing tasks over days, representing a significant expansion of autonomous capability.

## Key Information

- **Current State**: Agents can run tasks for hours (asynchronous operation)
- **Near Future**: Agents completing tasks over days
- **Coming Years**: Further expansion of autonomous task duration
- **Application Domains**: Software development and other types of knowledge work
- **Interface Implications**: As task horizons lengthen, the interface between humans and agents will continue to evolve
