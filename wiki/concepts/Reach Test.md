## Definition

The "reach test" is [[Dan Shipper]]'s personal heuristic for whether a tool has achieved genuine product-market fit for him: whether he organically reaches for it each morning, unprompted.

## Key Information

- Introduced as one of the internal signals Dan Shipper uses at [[Every]] to notice which emerging AI tools are actually useful versus merely interesting — described alongside the practice of trying every new model/tool as it comes out and writing about what's noticed.
- Applied by Dan Shipper to his own adoption of Claude Code (a year before this episode) and later Codex — both passed the reach test for him before he publicly predicted their rise.
