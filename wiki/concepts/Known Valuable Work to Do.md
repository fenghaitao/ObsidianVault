## Definition

Known valuable work to do is work where you both know what it is and know that it's valuable. It's the antidote to hyperrealistic work-like activities, and supplying it is a core responsibility of leadership.

## Key Information

- **"Known" has two meanings**: you know what the work is, and you know it's going to be valuable
- At a startup's beginning, there's an enormous amount of known valuable work (open a bank account, create a users table, salt passwords, etc.)
- Over time, the relationship between supply of work to do and demand for doing work changes — more people get hired, the easy/obvious stuff gets done, and questions become more ambiguous (e.g., "Should we do FedRAMP High and make a GovSlack version?")
- **Leader's responsibility**: create clarity around what the known valuable work is, ensure alignment, and make sure everyone understands what they're supposed to be doing
- If you hire 17 product marketers, you'll have 17 product marketers' worth of demand for work — if there's not enough real product marketing work, they'll create hyperrealistic work-like activities
