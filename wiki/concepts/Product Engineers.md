## Definition

Product Engineers are engineers who also do product work — they own features from ideation through shipping and marketing/announcement. They combine engineering, product management, and marketing responsibilities, enabling high shipping velocity without needing enormous marketing teams.

## Key Information

- Lovable has a "very lean product organization" and leans on engineers to do product work
- Product engineers must announce the things they ship — it doesn't just funnel through marketing
- This requires high autonomy, high agency, and the ability to figure things out without specialists
- "I don't need a marketer to go launch something. I can go figure it out and I have high agency."
- The role is essential for maintaining shipping velocity without an enormous marketing team
- Product engineers own features from start to finish with very little supervision
- They need to understand what success looks like and what use cases they're building for
- Hiring criteria: passion (fire in the belly), high agency, high autonomy, ability to create clarity from chaos
