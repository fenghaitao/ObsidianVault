## Definition

Diminishing Returns is the economic principle that additional investment in one area yields progressively smaller gains. Applied to career and life, it suggests that a true maximizer should diversify across areas (career, relationships, health, hobbies) rather than over-optimizing any single one.

## Key Information

- Ryan framed life optimization using the diminishing returns curve: "if you put in a lot of extra stuff into some area, little by little the gains are going to go away"
- "If you're truly a maximizer, you go everywhere where you can get more returns before it starts to diminish" — diversify across multiple life areas
- This principle supports the argument for being a multifaceted person rather than making work your entire identity
- Both speakers emphasized that their happiest moments came from life outside work, consistent with the diminishing returns of career investment
