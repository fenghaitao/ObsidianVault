## Definition

"Low heart rate" is Sam Lessin's core framing for etiquette: the goal of learning good etiquette is to show up in any room with a low heart rate — calm, confident, and not in a state of transactional anxiety. It's the opposite of the "Energizer Bunny" who scares people off by projecting too much intensity.

## Key Information

- Sam Lessin: "Etiquette is a skill for how to show up in a room with a low heart rate"
- The opposite: showing up like a "little Energizer Bunny" — high intensity, transactional, projecting the wrong vibe
- Practical manifestations: being early (so you're not racing in with heart rate up), taking a beat before entering, not being flustered
- If you're late, just apologize simply — don't make it a "five alarm fire" that raises everyone's heart rate
- The goal is to project self-confidence and the calm of abundance
- "If you show up with too high a heart rate and you burn a bunch of mild relationship opportunities, I don't know why you wouldn't want these skills"
