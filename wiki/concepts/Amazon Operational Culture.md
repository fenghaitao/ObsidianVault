## Definition

Amazon's Operational Culture is the company's emphasis on operational excellence, including on-call rotations, understanding how systems break, and having ideas about how to fix operational issues. It is a core part of the engineering experience at Amazon.

## Key Information

- At Amazon, engineers are on call and expected to understand how things break
- Operational knowledge is part of the SDE2 ownership expectation: understanding the team's codebase weaknesses and strengths
- On-call experience is expected as part of the promotion process — Steve was challenged for not being on call recently during his principal promotion assessment
- Steve had been on call for "longer than most people's careers at Amazon — probably like four years continuously"
- The operational culture creates a "meat and potato" role at SDE2 where engineers understand both development and operations
