## Definition

Token Economics is the counterintuitive principle that using the most capable (and expensive per-token) AI model is often cheaper overall because less intelligent models consume more tokens through corrections, retries, and hand-holding.

## Key Information

- Boris: "Sometimes people try to use a less expensive model like Sonnet or something like this. But because it's less intelligent, it actually takes more tokens in the end to do the same task"
- "Often, it's actually cheaper and less token intensive if you use the most capable model cuz it can just do the same thing much faster with less correction, less hand-holding"
- Boris always uses Opus 4.6 with maximum effort enabled
- For individual engineers experimenting, token costs are "still probably relatively low relative to their salary"
- At Anthropic, some engineers are spending "hundreds of thousands a month in tokens"
- Boris's advice to CTOs: "Don't try to optimize. Don't try to cost cut at the beginning. Start by just giving engineers as many tokens as possible"
- Optimize only after an idea is proven to work at scale
