## Definition

The AI CTO Co-Founder is Zevi Arnovitz's approach of using a custom-prompted AI (originally a GPT Project, later Claude Code) as the complete technical owner of a project. The AI is instructed to challenge ideas, not be a people-pleaser, and own all technical decisions while the human owns the problem and user experience.

## Key Information

- Created by Zevi Arnovitz when he noticed that AI coding tools (Bolt, Lovable) were "super eager to write code" without proper planning
- The CTO prompt: "I own the problem. I own how we want the users to feel. You're the complete owner of how this is going to be built. I want you to challenge me. I don't want you to be a people pleaser."
- Originally implemented as a GPT Project with custom instructions and shared knowledge base
- Key anti-pattern addressed: ChatGPT's sycophantic tendency to agree with users (Zevi: "If regular ChatGPT was a CTO, that would be the CTO who goes along with your dumbest ideas")
- GPT Projects allowed compartmentalization — separate contexts for different facets of life (running, PM work, psychology studies)
- Zevi's CTO approach was the precursor to his full slash commands workflow
- The CTO approach puts you in "a chatbot and not writing code, so you take the time to converse and to learn"
- Zevi recommends starting with a GPT Project as CTO before graduating to more advanced tools
- The CTO concept has since been absorbed into Zevi's Claude Code workflow, where the same agent does exploration, planning, and execution
