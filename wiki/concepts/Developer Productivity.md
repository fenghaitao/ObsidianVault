## Definition

Developer Productivity is the discipline of making software engineers faster and more effective, which Laurent pursued as his specialization across Apple, Meta, Airbnb, Instagram, and Stripe.

## Key Information

- Laurent's core question: "how do you make people as productive as possible" when writing code.
- Lines of code or PR count are poor metrics because people contribute in many different ways.
- A better lens (used at Stripe): measure the "journey of a change" — the time and phases from branch to merge (thinking, writing, CI, code review) — which surfaces whether an org or individual is slow and why.
- Being slow relative to everyone else is useful signal (bad codebase, inefficient process), not an automatic productivity judgment.
- Friction logs ("channel your inner frustration") are the input for finding developer-productivity problems.
- Examples of his work: calendar-defrag tool for reports, test infrastructure, and leading build/test/tooling at Stripe.
