## Definition

Dynamic range is Ilya Grigorik's description of the core capability of principal+ and Distinguished Engineers: the ability to operate across the full technical stack (bare metal to business requirements) and across different execution modes (fast startup pivoting to slow, convergent design).

## Key Information

- "To be a really effective principal plus engineer, you need to have very good dynamic range"
- Technical axis: operate "all the way down to the bare metal" and also zoom out to business requirements and VP/product thinking, translating between them
- Execution axis: some projects are at the "frontier of knowledge" (fog of war, fire-aim-fire fast iteration); others are slow, high-leverage "pace layer" problems (API design with second/third-order repercussions years out) requiring slow, convergent thinking
- Distinguished Engineer is "proof of the dynamic range of the problems you can solve," a VP-equivalent role with no shared definition: "show me the volume and the versatility of your toolkit"
- Principal engineers can't be given a tick-box job description — the problems are ambiguous by definition ("people that have to ask that question by definition are not principal engineers")
