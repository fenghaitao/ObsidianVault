## Definition

Kindness in Engineering is the practice of leading software teams with empathy and grace, grounded in the awareness that everyone is going through things you cannot see. Fiona Fung makes it an explicit part of the culture she builds.

## Key Information

- Fiona's badge-post ended with the line "in a world where you can be anything, be kind"
- The value crystallized during COVID, when she was shipping Quest 2 and incubating Ray-Ban Stories while everyone, including her own team, was also dealing with a lot at home
- Personal origin: her grandmother was in an assisted-living facility in Canada during COVID, and FaceTime was the only way to connect; when a scarce FaceTime slot came up, a report she was supporting graciously let her reschedule their one-on-one at the last minute — an act of kindness with outsized impact
- The principle: "we never know what somebody is going through," and work is the collective pursuit of hard, "epic" things, which is easier when people are kind to each other
