## Definition

Model convergence is the observation that frontier AI models are becoming similarly capable, making the gap between them less significant over time. Brian Casel observed this when building the same app with Claude Opus 4.5 and Google Gemini 3 — both produced functional results with minor differences. The implication: the builder's skill at directing models matters more than model choice.

## Key Information

- Frontier models from different providers produce comparable results on the same task.
- The gap between models matters less over time.
- What matters more: the builder's product instincts, architectural decisions, taste, and skill at directing agents.
- Model evaluation should be personal — test on your own projects and workflows, not benchmarks.
- Different models may still excel at different phases (planning vs debugging vs greenfield).
