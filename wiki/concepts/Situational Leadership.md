## Definition

Situational Leadership is a coaching model (introduced to Laurent by Scott Renfro) holding that the right management/coaching style depends on where a learner sits across two axes: skill mastery and motivation.

## Key Information

- Learners progress along two axes at once — mastery of the skill and motivation/confidence — and both climb "up and to the right" over time.
- The model divides learning into four phases, each matched to a style: directing (early, low skill/high motivation) → selling/encouraging → supporting → delegating (once they know how, stay out of the way).
- Laurent's bootcamp failure: he assumed a new mentee needed directing, but the mentee had already studied the docs (higher knowledge, lower motivation) and needed supporting instead; switching styles unlocked their progress and they became the most effective of the four.
- The same framework applies universally — a parent helping with an iPhone or game controller vs. coaching a senior engineer.
- The common coaching mistake is picking one technique that worked and forgetting the other three; you must adapt to your audience.
