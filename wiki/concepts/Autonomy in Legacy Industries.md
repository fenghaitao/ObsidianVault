# Autonomy in Legacy Industries

The thesis that AI-driven autonomy is urgently needed in farming, mining, construction, and trucking — not to eliminate jobs, but to fill critical labor gaps.

## Core Idea

"These industries need autonomy and it couldn't come soon enough. This is not like people are not fighting for those trucking jobs."

## Key Points
- **Farming:** Average farmer age is late 50s (58). In 10-20 years, many will retire with no replacement workforce
- **Trucking:** Long-haul trucking jobs require family sacrifice (father absent for days/weeks). Today's working-class families choose Uber/DoorDash instead because they can turn off the app and pick up their kid
- **Mining:** Dangerous, remote jobs nobody clamors to do
- **Construction:** Similar labor shortages
- **Defense:** Military applications for autonomy
- The trade-off is no longer worth it for many workers — they prioritize family and flexibility
- "The productivity unlocked from teaming up with a machine... unlocks things that are very hard to think about"
- This is fundamentally different from the narrative of "AI will take all jobs" — these are jobs people don't want
