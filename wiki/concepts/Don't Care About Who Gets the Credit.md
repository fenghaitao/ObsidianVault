## Definition

A leadership principle Jeetu Patel learned from Cisco CEO Chuck Robbins: if you don't care about who gets the credit, you go a lot farther in life. It's about being confident enough to let go of credit and focus on outcomes.

## Key Information

- Learned by Jeetu Patel from Chuck Robbins (CEO of Cisco)
- Jeetu once apologized to Chuck for a quote being attributed to Jeetu that was actually Chuck's — Chuck's response: "Don't worry about it, man. What I've learned in life is if you don't care about who gets the credit, you just go a lot farther in life."
- Jeetu describes it as "profound" — Chuck is "way too confident to let anything [bother him]"
- Part of Chuck's broader leadership approach: knowing what you're good at and what you're not good at, and assembling teams around you
