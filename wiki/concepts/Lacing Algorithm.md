## Definition

A Lacing Algorithm is an algorithm that traces the provenance of digital art, tracking back through a tree of edits to determine if someone has altered an artist's NFT and identifying the original artist. Jia Chen's team built this at the Princeton Hackathon.

## Key Information

- Built at the Princeton Hackathon, winning the Verbar prize
- The algorithm traces back if someone has altered an artist's NFT, identifying the original artist through the edit history
- At some point in the tree of edits, the art "fans off" from the original — the algorithm tracks this lineage
- The project included a UI that visually showed the descendant tree of NFT edits
- This was an example of Jia's grand prize strategy: aiming high with technical complexity, but having thought through all steps in advance for flawless execution
