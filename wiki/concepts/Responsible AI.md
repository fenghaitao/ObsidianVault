## Definition
Responsible AI encompasses the policies, practices, and safeguards needed to ensure AI applications are developed with attention to data privacy, security, and ethical considerations, including implementing guardrails into applications throughout the development workflow.

## Key Information
- Must be incorporated across the entire AI development workflow
- Key aspects include ensuring data is private and secure
- Implementing guardrails into applications is a critical component
- Amazon Bedrock provides a secure environment where users are in full control of their data
- Model evaluation should include responsible AI considerations
