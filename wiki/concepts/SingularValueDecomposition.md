## Definition
Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) is a fundamental linear algebra algorithm that decomposes any matrix into three component matrices (U, Σ, V^T), underpinning Principal Component Analysis (PCA), data compression, recommendation systems, and many other applications across mathematics and computer science.

## Key Information
- Daniel Han describes SVD as "one of the most important algorithms in all of mathematics and computer science"
- One algorithm that spawns many other algorithms; used for diverse purposes
- Underpins PCA (Principal Component Analysis), widely used for data visualization
- Randomized SVD is an important variant for efficient computation on large matrices
- Related decompositions: QR decomposition, LU decomposition
- Despite its importance, SVD is less well-known than it should be, even among ML practitioners
