## Definition
Model Anxiety is the fear of missing out on capabilities by not using the latest or best AI model or platform. Brian Scanlan argues that this anxiety is counterproductive — it's analogous to being multi-cloud and missing the compounding benefits of optimizing on a single well-designed platform. Unless there are very specific, impactful reasons to spread across multiple agents, organizations should go all-in on one.

## Key Information
- Described by Brian Scanlan as a barrier to effective AI adoption
- Analogy: being multi-cloud prevents getting the full benefits of any single cloud provider
- Philosophy: unless there are very specific, impactful reasons, be all-in on one platform
- Exception: Intercom uses multimodal code reviews (Codex alongside Claude Code) for code review — but this is a deliberate, specific choice
- The compounding benefits of a well-designed platform are lost when work is spread across different tools
- Intercom chose Claude Code as their unified platform after evaluating Cursor, Augment, and GitHub Copilot
- The decision to consolidate was made in December 2024, with rollout in January 2025
