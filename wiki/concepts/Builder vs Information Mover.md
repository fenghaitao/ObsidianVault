## Definition

Builder vs Information Mover is a framework by Nikhyl Singhal dividing product managers into two camps: "builders" who love making things and are thriving in the AI era, and "information movers" whose primary skill was moving information between stakeholders and who are at risk of obsolescence.

## Key Information

- Builders: love sitting down and building, have direct connection between ideas and impact, comps at all-time high, more offers than ever, can now become founders/CEOs/CHROs
- Information movers: their day was "moving information from one to another," framing presentations for bosses, responsibility without authority -- this role is becoming a dinosaur
- Roughly half of PMs fall into each camp
- Even within builders, the key is not necessarily shipping code yourself, but having the builder mindset and judgment
- AI enables builders to have impact without dependencies on engineers/designers
- The "information mover" PM was already unhappy pre-AI (responsibility without authority is the greatest form of workplace stress)
