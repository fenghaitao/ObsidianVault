## Definition

Multimodal AI experiences go beyond text to incorporate multiple modes of communication — visual, audio, and behavioral signals — getting closer to the richness of human conversation. Aishwaria Raanti predicts this will be a major trend in 2026.

## Key Information

- **Predicted by**: [[Aishwaria Raanti]] as a key 2026 trend
- **Human richness**: "As humans, we are multimodal creatures. Language is probably one of our last forms of evolution." In conversation, we constantly get signals — nodding, boredom, engagement — and alter our communication accordingly.
- **Current gap**: "That dimension of expression is not explored as well." If we build better multimodal experiences, we get closer to human-like conversation richness.
- **Practical applications**: Better multimodal understanding could unlock handwritten documents, messy PDFs, and other unstructured data that even the best models can't parse today.
- **Technical convergence**: [[Demis Hassabis]] at DeepMind has talked about combining image models, LLMs, and world models (Genie) — multimodal AI as a major direction.
- **Beyond generation**: Progress is needed not just in generation but in understanding — reading visual, audio, and behavioral signals.
