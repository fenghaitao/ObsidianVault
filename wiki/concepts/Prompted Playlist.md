## Definition

Prompted Playlist is a Spotify feature that allows users to generate custom playlists by prompting Spotify's model with natural language descriptions. As of May 2026, it supports both music tracks and podcast episodes.

## Key Information

- **Natural Language Input**: Users describe what they want (mood, genre, activity, theme) and the model generates a matching playlist
- **Multi-Content Support**: Originally music-only, expanded to support podcast episode playlists as of the week of May 19, 2026
- **Generative Backend**: Powered by Spotify's unified generative recommender system that combines user embeddings, semantic IDs, and soft tokenization
- **Steerability**: Part of Spotify's broader vision of letting users steer recommendations through natural language rather than passive consumption
