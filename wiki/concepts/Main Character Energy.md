## Definition

Main Character Energy is the mindset of being the protagonist of your own career rather than a passive participant. Ryan Peterman coined this term during the interview to describe Rahul's philosophy of taking initiative and pursuing what you care about, contrasting it with "NPC energy" — the passive, follow-instructions approach.

## Key Information

### Origin of the Term
- Ryan Peterman suggested the framing: "main character energy versus the NPC energy"
- Rahul agreed with this framing, noting that you don't have to be the main character in every domain of your life
- "Those kind of people might be kind of annoying to be with in certain domains"
- But you should try to be the main character in the parts of your life you care about or where you have a unique take

### The Mindset Shift
- The default mode is NPC energy: doing what the instructor or manager tells you, following the standard path, waiting for permission
- Main character energy is: pursuing what you care about, building things without being told, putting yourself out there
- This is the difference between "let me wait until I'm ready" and "let me just go do it"

### Practical Application
- Main character energy means: building a keyboard as a side project, publishing Android tutorials, running webinars, launching a company
- It doesn't mean being the main character everywhere — it's about choosing specific domains where you take initiative
- The earlier you adopt this mindset, the better — even in middle school or high school
