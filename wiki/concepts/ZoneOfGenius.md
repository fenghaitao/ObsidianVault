## Definition
Zone of Genius is the strategic concept that companies building LLM applications should focus on their unique strengths — product expertise, existing user base, domain knowledge — rather than competing on model capabilities. It is where a company can create value that model providers cannot or will not build.

## Key Information
- Articulated by Bryan Bischof in the 2024 AI Engineer Summit keynote's strategic section
- "You should be building in your zone of genius — leveraging your product expertise or your existing product"
- Model providers have to build things for all their customers; application builders should find their niche and dig into it
- "Don't waste your calories" on building what model providers will inevitably build (the "steamrolling" concept)
- The zone of genius is about product quality, user experience, and solving a specific job to be done — not about having the best model
- Complements "Model Is Not The Moat": if the model isn't your advantage, your product expertise must be
