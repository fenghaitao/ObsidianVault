## Definition
Agent-as-Dishwasher is Thomas Dohmke's metaphor for defining AI agents: an agent is "like an AI dishwasher" — you fill it with dishes, let it do its thing, and then take the output and put it back on the shelves. It emphasizes autonomy within a bounded, well-defined task.

## Key Information
- Coined by Thomas Dohmke, CEO of GitHub, when asked to define what makes an agent
- Key characteristics of the dishwasher metaphor: input (fill it), autonomous execution (let it do its thing), output retrieval (put results back on shelves)
- Existing examples that already fit the definition: CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions), monitoring systems — they get a job done autonomously
- Contrast with security tooling today: "it's not a dishwasher, it's actually a tool that tells you the dishes are dirty and then you have to do the dishes yourself"
- The desired evolution: from tools that identify problems (security scanners adding to backlog) to agents that solve problems (autofix that burns down the backlog)
- Implies agents should handle the work developers don't want to do (boilerplate, security compliance, dependency updates), freeing them for creative work
- Developers are "artists and creators," not production workers; AI should take over the tedious parts
