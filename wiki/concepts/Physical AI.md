# Physical AI

AI applied to physical machines and systems — cars, tractors, planes, submarines, mining rigs, construction equipment, and military systems — rather than pure software. Coined/emphasized by [[Qasar Younis]] and [[Applied Intuition]].

## Core Idea

The real AI revolution in the next 5-10 years will be in physical industries (farming, mining, construction, trucking, defense), not just software. These industries face severe labor shortages and need autonomy urgently.

## Key Points
- Farming: average farmer age is late 50s — retiring in 10-20 years with no replacement
- Mining, trucking, construction: dangerous jobs people don't want — autonomy fills the gap
- Self-driving vehicles are essentially robots — we already accept them because they don't look humanoid
- Consumer-facing AI (chatbots, coding agents) is still a tiny part of society's actual daily experience
- [[Qasar Younis]]: "The real impact of AI in the next 5 to 10 years really is going to be in farming, mining, construction, in self-driving trucks"
- The big impact comes from putting intelligence into things that already exist (vehicles, heavy machinery) rather than building new humanoid robots
- Every car company is working on a Tesla FSD-like product; in 5-7 years, autonomy will be ubiquitous and near-free

## Contrast
- Software AI (chatbots, coding agents, LLMs) gets most of the media attention
- Physical AI gets less attention but will have more impact on the average person's life
- "My barometer of impact is you go to the Detroit airport and you sit in a gate and you look around... the impact of AI is going to be in this physical world"
