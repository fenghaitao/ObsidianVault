## Definition
Physical AI is Black Forest Labs' ultimate strategic direction — applying generative AI and world models to robotics and automation, including training agents in simulated generative worlds to scale self-driving and automate manufacturing.

## Key Information
- BFL's end-goal beyond image/video generation
- Uses world models trained to understand geometry, relationships, and physical interactions
- Applications: self-driving, manufacturing automation, robotics
- Self Flow model trained on action data can control robot arms to pick up objects
- Self Flow-trained robot outperforms baseline in accuracy for the same number of training steps
- Represents BFL's expansion from visual media into the physical world
