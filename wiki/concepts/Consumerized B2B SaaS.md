## Definition

Consumerized B2B SaaS refers to business software that adopts consumer-grade design principles — delight, experience, craft, and emotional connection — rather than the traditional enterprise approach of functional but unlovable software. Slack was famous for being one of the early pioneers of this approach.

## Key Information

- Slack was famous for being one of the early consumerized B2B SaaS products
- Key characteristics: leaning into delight, experience, craft, and great user experience
- Contrasted with traditional enterprise software that prioritized feature checklists over user experience
- Slack's consumerized approach drove word-of-mouth growth through emotional connections: users genuinely advocated for the product when switching companies
- Butterfield was known as a "taste forward, craft oriented leader" — rare in B2B at the time
