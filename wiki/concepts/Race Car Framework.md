## Definition

The Race Car Framework is a growth metaphor used by Elena Verna where the company is a race car: growth work involves putting oil into the engine (removing friction, greasing the wheels) and creating turbo boosts (big launches that create step-function changes in product-market fit).

## Key Information

- Elena Verna's framework for understanding growth at high-velocity companies
- "Oil" = day-to-day growth work: removing friction, greasing wheels, making sure the company doesn't hit a wall
- "Turbo boosts" = tier 1 launches that create step-function changes in product-market fit
- The daily shipping velocity maintains noise between the big turbo boost moments
- At Lovable, the company is moving so fast that the growth team is "just hanging on to it as fast as possible"
- The framework emphasizes that growth is about enabling velocity, not just optimizing conversions
