## Definition
The AI Engineer is an emerging role defined by swyx as a practitioner who builds AI-powered applications using foundation model APIs, without requiring deep ML research or data science expertise. The role focuses on integrating, customizing, and deploying AI models into production applications.

## Key Information
- Defined by swyx's viral post: "You don't need to be a full ML researcher anymore"
- AI projects that previously took months or years can now be accomplished with a few API calls
- Requires understanding of foundation model fundamentals, but not deep ML research
- Involves model evaluation, customization (prompt engineering, RAG, fine-tuning), and agent development
- Antje Barth outlines 5 practical steps: understand fundamentals, use AI developer tools, prototype with AI, build agents, engage with community
- The transition from software developer leverages existing engineering skills
- AI developer tools like Amazon Q Developer automate boilerplate tasks, freeing time for creative work
