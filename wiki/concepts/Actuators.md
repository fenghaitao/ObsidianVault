## Definition

Motorized components that produce movement in robots; cited by [[Caitlin Kalinowski]] as one of the key supply-chain bottlenecks (alongside magnets, silicon, and RAM) gating at-scale [[Humanoid Robots]] production.

## Key Information

- Part of her broader argument that physical-AI/robotics scaling is limited by manufacturing and supply chain, not by AI model capability.
- Connects to her advocacy for U.S. re-industrialization and independent domestic supply chains for both economic and military-security reasons.
