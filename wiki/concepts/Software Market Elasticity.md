## Definition

Software Market Elasticity is the thesis that the cheaper it is to make software, the more software will be made. Malte Ubl frames the current AI era as "speed running an experiment in economics" to test how elastic the software market truly is.

## Key Information

- Thesis: cheaper software creation → more software demand → higher demand for software engineers
- Malte Ubl: "We are speed running what's really an experiment in economics of how elastic the software market is"
- No signs of reaching an S-curve yet — as agents get better, there is still plenty of room for growth
- Evidence: demand for software engineers is going up, not down
- Connects to the Venn diagram concept: agents fill the gap between "all software that should exist" and what was economically viable with traditional methods
- Implication: fears about AI replacing engineers are unfounded because the market expands faster than automation reduces per-unit cost
