## Definition

"Computer errands," a term coined by Every COO [[Brandon Gall]], describes the emerging territory of using personal AI agents for everyday personal tasks (e.g., ordering groceries) rather than work tasks.

## Key Information

- Cited by [[Dan Shipper]] as a category he expects to be huge, distinct from the work-focused "super agent" (reached mostly via [[Slack]]) that he and [[Every]] focus most of their attention on.
- Dan Shipper notes people mentally like to keep their personal and work agents separate (referencing preferring "blue bubbles" personal-agent experiences on personal devices), which is part of why he expects work agents and personal-errand agents to remain distinct surfaces.
