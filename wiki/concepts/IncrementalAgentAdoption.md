## Definition
Incremental Agent Adoption is Radek Sienkiewicz's methodology for integrating a personal AI agent into daily life: start with one simple workflow, build trust incrementally, add capabilities one small step at a time, and always be able to take one step back when something breaks. This approach avoids catastrophic failures while producing sophisticated setups over time.

## Key Information
- Radek started with just WhatsApp messaging — "one channel, one ability to just chat"
- Each step adds a single capability: WhatsApp → Telegram → Discord, then one simple workflow at a time
- When something breaks: take one small step back, fix it, understand why it broke, add safeguards, then move forward again
- This is why Radek never experiences the catastrophic failures others report ("it broke my computer" or "completely bricked during update")
- The approach also applied to his path to becoming a maintainer: encountered an error → submitted a PR → submitted another → got involved in Discord → became a maintainer
- The result: a setup that surprised Radek with its sophistication — he thought it was simple until he compared it to what others had built
- Contrasts with the "install and expect everything" approach that leads to failure
