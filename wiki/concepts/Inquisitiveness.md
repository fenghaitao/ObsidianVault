## Definition

Inquisitiveness is the quality of being curious and eager to learn, especially about how things work beyond the surface level. Evan King identified this as the trait that his early managers praised and the trait he wishes he had exercised more at Meta.

## Key Information

- Evan's Zillow manager praised him for being "inquisitive and not feeling bounded by what I was told to do"
- At Zillow, Evan's inquisitiveness led him to hang out with the cyber security team, build side projects, and attend Defcon — all outside his assigned role
- As a junior engineer at Meta, Evan was "inquisitive" about problems and "didn't feel bounded" — he would finish his work quickly and then explore other areas
- Evan's regret: he was not as inquisitive at Meta as he could have been — "I wish I had been a bit more inquisitive about how things worked"
- He now applies inquisitiveness to his learning: reading engineering blogs, understanding how libraries and platforms work
- The shift from "just ship it" to "be inquisitive about why it actually worked" is a maturation in engineering mindset
- Inquisitiveness is what distinguishes someone who just executes tasks from someone who builds deep understanding
