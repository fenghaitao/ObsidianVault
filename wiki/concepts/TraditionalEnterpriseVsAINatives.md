## Definition

Traditional enterprise vs AI natives describes two contrasting organizational approaches to agentic development. Traditional enterprises delegate GenAI to existing ML/data science platform teams because "generative AI has AI in the name," while AI-native companies build cross-functional teams with closer proximity to the problem the agent is meant to solve.

## Key Information

### Traditional Enterprise Approach
- CEO/CIO reads about agents in a magazine, decides the company needs to build agents
- Delegates to existing ML or data science platform teams who already have tooling in place
- GenAI is handed to AI teams because it seems like a natural fit — it has "AI" in the name
- Risk: these teams may be isolated from the end problem and end users
- Tendency to treat agents like another predictive model, applying familiar ML workflows

### AI Native Approach
- Companies started building their entire offering around agents — nothing existed before GenAI
- No pre-existing AI/ML platform team; small, agile, cross-functional engineering teams
- Everyone is cross-functional across product engineering and AI engineering
- Each person has more proximity to the problem and better understanding of what the agent is meant to solve
- No rigid segmentation of responsibilities

### Key Difference
- Traditional enterprises isolate agent development within ML teams; AI natives make it a cross-functional product effort
- The mistake traditional companies make: saying "this is another predictive model" and handing it exclusively to data scientists
