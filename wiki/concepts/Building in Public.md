## Definition

Building in Public is a growth strategy where companies share their journey, metrics, lessons, and product updates openly on social media. It creates constant market noise, serves as both acquisition and re-engagement, and builds trust through authenticity and vulnerability.

## Key Information

- One of Lovable's biggest growth strategies, coupled with employee socials and founder socials
- Works as a "fantastic resurrection strategy" — people see new things and come back to check
- Also works as a re-engagement strategy — users check social media to see what shipped instead of relying on newsletters
- The short cycle from user feedback to delivery makes users feel heard and creates loyalty
- Requires trust within the organization that people will say the right things
- Works best for smaller companies with more narrative control
- Anton Osika (Lovable CEO) and Grant (Gamma CEO) are cited as examples of founders who build in public effectively
- Shipping velocity is key: daily or near-daily releases maintain the noise level
- Smaller changes are delegated to product/engineering teams to announce (not just marketing)
- Contrasts with traditional marketing where positioning and messaging stay stable for years
- Narrative changes every 3 months as the product evolves, making traditional marketing cycles obsolete
- Lazar's career was built on this: "It became a job by building in public." He shared projects on LinkedIn and YouTube, which got him noticed by Elena Verna.
- "Give away all the secrets, like there are no secrets whatsoever. If you're sitting on a good concept, you're missing out."
- Some Lovable hires got jobs by sending Lovable apps (not resumes) to demonstrate their fit

## Contrast: Building Quietly

- [[Qasar Younis]] and [[Applied Intuition]] represent the opposite approach: "Our best work is done alone and quietly"
- Applied Intuition stayed under the radar for ~10 years, focusing on product and customers without public presence
- Qasar: "Every minute you're writing something for public consumption, you're not focusing your very limited time on your customers and your product"
- He could do this because he was already known in the ecosystem (YC COO, personal network)
- Qasar only joined Twitter/X in 2026 at the urging of [[Marc Andreessen]] and [[Elad Gil]]
- Both approaches are valid — [[Radical Pragmatism]] means choosing the right approach for your situation
