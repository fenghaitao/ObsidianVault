## Definition

AI-powered voice input that goes beyond simple transcription — the AI understands context, distinguishes between dictation and commands, and can interact conversationally while transcribing. Marc Andreessen cites Whisper Flow as the prime example of this capability.

## Key Information

- Described by Marc Andreessen on Lenny's Podcast using Whisper Flow as the example
- Key capability: the AI distinguishes between content you want transcribed and instructions you're giving it
- Example: saying "I want bullet points over there" results in actual bullet points, not the text "I want bullet points"
- The AI "kind of understands when you're telling it, no, no, you know, I want bullet points over there"
- This represents a major leap from traditional voice-to-text which only transcribes literally
- Marc describes it as "a voice transcription function, but you can actually talk to the AI model while you're doing voice transcription"
- Part of the broader Voice AI Revolution that Marc predicts
