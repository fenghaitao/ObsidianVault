## Definition

Small Teams is the operating philosophy "if you want to go fast, go small" — that a small, high-talent-density team with full ownership moves faster and ships better than a large, heavily coordinated organization.

## Key Information

- Ryan Olson cut the Instagram Stories team down to a core of two iOS engineers (plus borrowed help) and shipped in 2–3 months; small teams give clear ownership ("that's my bug") and fewer decision-makers.
- White Out was easier to do when the app/codebase was smaller; the same project would be "massive" at Instagram's later size.
- Hidden costs of scale: more stakeholders, meetings, coordination, and code — Ryan was spending over 4 hours/day just waiting for the app to compile before leaving.
- Thought experiment: Thanos-snap half the org away (even randomly) and "frequently we felt like, yeah, maybe" it would be better.
- Echoed by Twitter cutting ~80% of staff under Elon and still running (and becoming profitable).
- IG Labs applied the same principle as a small "Delta Force" group spanning the org.
