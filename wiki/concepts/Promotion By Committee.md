## Definition

Promotion By Committee is the process at many big tech companies where Staff+ level promotions are decided by a panel of senior leaders rather than by an individual's direct manager. This shifts the manager's role from decision-maker to advocate and messenger.

## Key Information

- At Airbnb, all promotions before Staff were essentially controlled by the manager; Staff and beyond was by committee
- At Amazon, L6 promotions were decided by skip-level managers (senior manager for senior, director for principal)
- At lower levels (Junior to Senior), there is less ambiguity — what the manager says is likely what happens
- At higher levels, the manager becomes more of a messenger and middleman for the committee
- This is why it's even more important to express promotion interest early — so the manager can start getting committee feedback early
- You need sign-off from VPs and org leads at the highest levels
- The manager cannot "snap their fingers" and promote you at Staff+ levels — there are processes and other decision-makers
