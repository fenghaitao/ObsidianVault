## Definition

The Domain Specialist Archetype is one of several staff engineering archetypes. It describes engineers who achieve staff-level impact through deep expertise in a specific technical domain that no one else in the organization possesses, making them indispensable for certain types of work.

## Key Information

- Referenced by Rahul as one of the staff engineering archetypes alongside Tech Lead and Code Machine
- Domain specialists have expertise that "no one else can do their exact work"
- They may operate differently from the Tech Lead archetype — they might not be leading large teams but instead providing deep technical expertise
- Like the Code Machine archetype, Domain Specialists can achieve staff-level impact without necessarily spending most of their time on leadership and delegation
- They are still expected to share their knowledge and scale their expertise across the organization
- The existence of multiple archetypes demonstrates that there is no single path to staff — it depends on your strengths
- Some companies or roles specifically need domain specialists (e.g., distributed systems, AI/ML, security)
