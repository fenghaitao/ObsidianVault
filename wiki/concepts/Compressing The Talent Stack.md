## Definition

A framework credited by [[Alexander Imbiricos]] (Codex product lead, [[OpenAI]]) to Scott Belsky: as AI lets individuals do meaningfully more across traditionally separate disciplines (product, design, engineering), the boundaries between those roles matter less, and each person who "does more" removes a communication boundary that used to slow a team down.

## Key Information

- Illustrated at OpenAI's [[Codex]] org: designers vibe-code their own working prototypes rather than only specifying ideas in documents for engineers to build — and some, depending on comfort with the codebase, land their own PRs directly, with an engineer helping only to finish landing it.
- Imbiricos frames this as a natural extension of the deeper "[[Role Collapse]]" trend already discussed by [[Andrew Ambrosino]] in episode 3, applied specifically to the PM/design boundary via AI-assisted prototyping.
- Connected to his broader point that as coding agents improve, the gap between early-career and senior engineers narrows, since raw productivity with the latest tools now matters more than credentialed experience.
