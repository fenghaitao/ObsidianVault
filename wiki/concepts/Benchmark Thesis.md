## Definition
Benchmark Thesis is the first "art" axis of [[Benchmarking Agents]]. It means that great benchmarks have a research question about a subspace of capabilities and where the field is going. The most ambitious benchmarks are a statement about where the world is heading — they are bets on which capabilities will matter most.

## Key Information
- A benchmark should have a research question about a subspace of capabilities
- It should revisit and challenge previous capability assumptions
- The most ambitious benchmarks make a statement about where the world is going
- Exemplar: [[TerminalBench]] was a bet on the CLI as a core interface for general-purpose agent computer use — a bet that has proven largely correct as teams build enterprise capabilities on CLI-based tools
- This thesis-driven approach helps benchmarks shape the field rather than just measure it
