## Definition

Agentic Workflows in Growth Teams describes the shift where growth teams at AI companies move beyond traditional product surfaces (landing pages, onboarding flows, emails) to work on the AI agent itself — codifying agent instructions, improving agent behavior, and building agentic workflows to drive activation and engagement.

## Key Information

- Elena Verna: "All of a sudden growth team is not just doing product surfaces — now we're doing agentic workflows and codifying agent instructions in order for customers to activate better."
- The growth team's work has "gotten deeper into product and deeper into actual core product functionality as opposed to just being a smoothing surface on the outer layers"
- In AI products, the agent's quality directly determines the entire user lifecycle — better generations improve both first-time and returning user experiences
- This is a departure from traditional growth where the growth team optimizes the wrapper around the product (signup flow, onboarding, notifications)
- The agent team at Lovable "spends night and day thinking about activation, that first generation, reaching aha moment"
- Activation is "weaved into the DNA of the overall company" rather than being a growth team responsibility
- The growth team at Lovable started doing agent work because the core agent team's focus on activation made it natural for growth to contribute directly to agent improvements
