## Definition
The DXAI Measurement Framework is DX's structured approach to measuring generative AI impact in engineering organizations. It organizes metrics into three dimensions: Utilization, Impact, and Cost, and functions as a maturity curve for AI measurement.

## Key Information
- First-to-market AI measurement framework from DX
- Inspired by DORA, SPACE, and DevX frameworks
- Three dimensions:
  - **Utilization**: What's happening, who's using the tech, percentage of AI-assisted PRs, tasks assigned to agents
  - **Impact**: Correlation of utilization to velocity and quality outcomes — does AI actually move the needle?
  - **Cost**: Token consumption and financial cost optimization (acknowledged as the least mature dimension)
- Functions as a maturity curve: organizations typically start with utilization, mature to impact correlation, then (ideally) cost optimization
- Three metric types feed into the framework: telemetry (API data), experience sampling (PR form fields), and self-reported surveys
- Emphasizes that core developer productivity metrics (not just AI-specific metrics) still matter most for measuring real outcomes
