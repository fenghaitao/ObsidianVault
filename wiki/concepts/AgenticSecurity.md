## Definition

Agentic security is the practice of deploying governed, auditable AI agents into cybersecurity production workflows — moving AI adoption from sandbox pilots to operational use with the controls, audit evidence, and autonomy boundaries that CISOs and compliance teams require.

## Key Information

- **The problem**: without clear frameworks for controls, audit evidence, and autonomy boundaries, AI adoption for security often remains stuck in "pilot purgatory" — unable to move from sandbox to production.
- **PwC's approach**: PwC's Claude Native Cybersecurity offering addresses this via two tracks: (1) Secure AI Adoption, which moves enterprises from sandbox to production in weeks rather than quarters with deployment, governance, and audit evidence; (2) Scaled Frontier Defense, which integrates [[Claude4.7Opus|Claude Opus]]-powered agentic reasoning into existing vulnerability management, detection, security engineering, and GRC workflows, enabling autonomous execution within defined guardrails and auditability.
- **Key insight** (Morgan Adamski, U.S. Cyber, Data & Tech Leader, PwC): *"This is a defining moment for cybersecurity, where AI-driven transformation becomes essential to staying resilient and competitive."*
- **Relationship to the partner ecosystem**: this third pillar of the Claude Security partner strategy complements offensive testing (finding vulnerabilities) and remediation acceleration (fixing them) by addressing the organizational and compliance barriers that prevent AI from being deployed at all.
- **Core requirements**: defined guardrails for agent autonomy, auditable decision trails, integration with existing GRC (Governance, Risk, and Compliance) workflows, and evidence packages for CISO/CRO sign-off.
