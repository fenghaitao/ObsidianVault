## Definition
Attention drift is the pattern of where your focus actually goes over time, as revealed by digital exhaust analysis — distinct from where you think or intend your attention to be. It is one of the three top insights revealed by cross-source analysis of cognitive exhaust fumes.

## Key Information
- One of three top use cases for read-only AI identified by Šimon Podhajský
- Revealed by analyzing browser history, email patterns, and other digital activity over time
- Shows the gap between where you think your attention is and where it actually goes
- Cannot be detected from any single data source — requires cross-source analysis
- The weekly reflection surfaces "themes of the week" that reveal attention patterns
