## Definition

One of the three key constraints holding back AI adoption: people don't trust AI systems because they are unpredictable, non-deterministic, and prone to hallucination. Building trust requires safety and security factored into AI systems.

## Key Information

- Articulated by Jeetu Patel as one of three AI constraints Cisco addresses
- "If people don't trust these systems, they're not going to use them. And right now there's a lot of mistrust in these systems."
- Hallucination is contextual: "a feature when you're writing poetry, but when you're trying to run predictable systems, hallucination can be a bad thing"
- Models are "non-deterministic" and "unpredictable" — they don't always produce the same output for the same input
- "They have to make sure that they have safety and security kind of factored into them"
