## Definition

Race to the Top is Anthropic's strategy of open-sourcing safety tools and publishing research to encourage other AI labs to adopt safe practices. The goal is to make safety the default, not a competitive disadvantage.

## Key Information

- Internal Anthropic term for their safety-sharing strategy
- Example: Claude Code's sandbox was open-sourced and works with any agent, not just Claude Code
- Anthropic publishes mechanistic interpretability research openly
- Goal: "We wanted to make it really easy for others to do the same thing"
- Boris: "We want to make sure this thing goes well and this is just the lever that we have"
- Contrasts with a "race to the bottom" where labs might cut safety corners for speed
