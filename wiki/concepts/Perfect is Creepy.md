## Definition

"Perfect is creepy" is a mantra in the Kennedy household and at Good Inside that reframes the goal away from perfection. Only non-humans can be perfect; the human condition is defined by wanting to do well and messing up over and over again. The false belief that there's a goal to be perfect is what prevents people from doing repair.

## Key Information

- "In our family, we say something. We actually now say it at work: perfect is creepy."
- "I just think it encapsulates like you don't even want to be perfect. It's actually very creepy. Only non-humans can ever be perfect."
- "What defines the human condition is that we want to do well when we mess up over and over again."
- The false idea of perfection as a goal is what keeps people from repairing: "The thing that keeps us from repairing is actually this very false idea that there's a goal to be perfect."
- Dr. Becky extends this to the Good Inside community: "I really do believe we deserve access to tools... to close the gap between how I kind of want to show up and how I do show up. None of us close the gap completely."
- At work: "I'm never going to be perfect is creepy, but I can grow."
