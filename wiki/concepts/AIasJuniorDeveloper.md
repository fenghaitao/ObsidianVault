## Definition
AI as Junior Developer is the mental model proposed by Brendan O'Leary for thinking about AI coding agents. It frames the AI agent as an energetic, enthusiastic, extremely well-read, often confidently wrong junior developer — incredibly fast, tireless, ego-free, with astonishing breadth of knowledge, but lacking judgment and business context.

## Key Information
- **Strengths**: incredibly fast, doesn't get tired, no ego about code (will rewrite 6 times), astonishing breadth of knowledge across languages/frameworks/patterns, has effectively read every Stack Overflow answer
- **Weaknesses**: no judgment, no business context, doesn't understand why specific architectural decisions were made, will confidently write code that is technically correct but contextually wrong
- The analogy extends to management: the same skills needed to manage a junior engineer apply to managing AI agents — providing clear specs, reviewing work carefully, giving the right context about what's important and what's not
- Brendan's Balsamiq anecdote illustrates the analogy: he gave interns a wireframe with Comic Sans and emoji placeholders without clarifying what was placeholder vs. real, and they faithfully implemented it all — the fault was his for not providing the right context
- This mental model underpins the agentic engineering paradigm: you must direct the work, not just accept suggestions
- Armin Ronacher's 30% time savings come from knowing what to hand off and what to keep — exactly the skill of managing a junior developer effectively
