## Definition
AI tutors in education refers to the use of frontier AI models to provide personalized tutoring to students, with the goal of leveling the playing field and putting world-class tutors in front of every child regardless of socio-economic background. The UK government is working on safeguards and benchmarks rather than building products directly.

## Key Information
- UK government's role: producing safeguards and evaluating frontier models against benchmarks
- Not building competing products — setting standards for how schools can adopt AI tutors
- Key concern: safe interaction in classroom environments
- Benchmarks include measuring cognitive load placed on the student
- Initial testing done with 70 teachers role-playing their pupils
- Major challenge: student motivation — getting kids to actually engage with AI tutors
- Must be done carefully given the sensitivity of children interacting with AI
